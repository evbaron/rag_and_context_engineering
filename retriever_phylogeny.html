Retriever Phylogeny

Every checkpoint branched into approaches; RAGAS applied two selection pressures. Through Phase 1, retrieval recall kept climbing — .90 → .94 → 1.00 — while answer quality stayed flat, naming the real bottleneck early: reasoning over tables. Phase 2 moved quality — aligned JSON tables, then a table gateway that routes every table through a deterministic executor — lifting quality to .79 at perfect recall.

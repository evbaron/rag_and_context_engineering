Retriever Phylogeny

Every checkpoint branched into approaches; RAGAS applied two selection pressures. Through Phase 1, retrieval recall kept climbing — .90 → .94 → 1.00 — while answer quality stayed flat, naming the real bottleneck early: reasoning over tables. Phase 2 moved quality — aligned JSON tables, then a table gateway that routes every table through a deterministic executor — lifting quality to .79 at perfect recall. The held-out validation set (the right-hand chart and the far-right tiles) is the honest check: recall climbed to 1.00 while quality held near .86–.91 — the agent's win is recall, ~14× less context, and deterministic tables, not raw quality over a strong fixed baseline. In 6.1 the same survivor graph on gpt-5.4 reached .96, at 4.4× the billed cost. In 7.1 safeguards moved into the graph and the final survivor routed table questions' assessor and answer to gpt-5.4: a planted-source attack that fooled 5.1 on 5 of 20 chances fooled it on none (p = .047), while held-out quality rose to .93 over three runs.

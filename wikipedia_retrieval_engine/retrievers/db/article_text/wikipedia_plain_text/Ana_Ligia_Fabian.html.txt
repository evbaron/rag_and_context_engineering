Ana Ligia Fabian Hernández (born November 7, 1988) is a volleyball player from the Dominican Republic, who participated in the 2007 Junior World Championship[1] in Thailand. She competed as wing spiker, wearing the #4 jersey. At the 2006 U-20 NORCECA Women´s Junior Continental Championship, she took with her team the silver medal.[2]

She participated with Ingrid Carmona at the NORCECA Beach Volleyball Circuit in 2007[3] and 2009 with Ysaires Restituyo,[4] finishing in 11th and 9th position.

Cinthia Josefina Piñeiro Torres (born February 4, 1986, in Santo Domingo) is a volleyball and beach volleyball player from Dominican Republic, who participated in the NORCECA Beach Volleyball Circuit 2007 with Ysaires Restituyo finishing in 9th place.[1][2]

She won the gold medal of the Dominican Republic National Championship 2006, partnering Rosalin Angeles.[3]

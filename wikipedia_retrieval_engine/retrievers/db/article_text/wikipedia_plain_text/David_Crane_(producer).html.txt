David Crane (born August 13, 1957) is an American writer and producer. He is best known as one of the co-creators of the television sitcoms Friends and Episodes.

Early life

Crane was born[1][2] in West New York, New Jersey, the son of Joan Crane and veteran Philadelphia television personality Gene Crane in a traditional Jewish household. He attended Harriton High School in Pennsylvania and graduated from there in 1975.[3] He is a 1979 graduate of Brandeis University.[4]

Career

He and his husband, Jeffrey Klarik[5][6][7] (creator of Half & Half,[8] co-producer Mad About You[9]), created the 2006 ensemble sitcom The Class.[10]

In 2011, Crane and Klarik created a sitcom called Episodes for the UK-based BBC. Airing first in the US on Showtime on Sunday January 9, 2011[11] and then on BBC Two on Monday January 10, 2011,[12] it features Friends star Matt LeBlanc and Green Wing's Stephen Mangan and Tamsin Greig.

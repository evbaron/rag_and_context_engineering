Tony McCombs (born August 24, 1974) is an American former professional football player who was a linebacker in the National Football League (NFL). He played college football for the Eastern Kentucky Colonels and was selected in the sixth round of the 1997 NFL draft.[2] He played for the Arizona Cardinals from 1997 to 1998.[3][4]

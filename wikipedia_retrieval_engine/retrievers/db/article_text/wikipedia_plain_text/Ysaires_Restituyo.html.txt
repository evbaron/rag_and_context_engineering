Ysaires Restituyo is a female beach volleyball player from Dominican Republic, who played in the NORCECA Beach Volleyball Circuit 2007[1] with Cinthia Piñeiro, 2008,[2] with Ingrid Carmona and 2009[3] with Ana Ligia Fabian; at Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic.

***Friends & Relatives*** is a [compilation album](/wiki/Compilation_album) released by [Eagle Records](/wiki/Eagle_Records) and [Purple Pyramid](/wiki/Purple_Pyramid_Records) (US) in 1999.
The album features songs from various artists that have a common linkage to [Electric Light Orchestra](/wiki/Electric_Light_Orchestra), such as [The Move](/wiki/The_Move) and [Wizzard](/wiki/Wizzard).

# ŽAK Subotica

**ŽAK Subotica** ([Serbian Cyrillic](/wiki/Serbian_Cyrillic_alphabet): ЖАК Суботица) was a [football](/wiki/Association_football) club based in [Subotica](/wiki/Subotica), [Kingdom of Yugoslavia](/wiki/Kingdom_of_Yugoslavia).

The club was formed in 1921 and the name comes from the initials of *Železničarski atletski klub* derived from the fact that the club was backed by the [Yugoslav Railways](/wiki/Yugoslav_Railways) company.

It played in the [1935–36 Yugoslav Football Championship](/wiki/1935%E2%80%9336_Yugoslav_Football_Championship) where it was eliminated in the round of sixteen by [Slavija Osijek](/wiki/Slavija_Osijek). Afterwards, it played in the [1940–41 Serbian League](/wiki/1940%E2%80%9341_Serbian_League) where it finished 5th out of 10.

With the start of the [Second World War](/wiki/Second_World_War) and the [Axis invasion of Yugoslavia](/wiki/Axis_invasion_of_Yugoslavia), the city came under Hungarian occupation and the club competed in the [Hungarian Second League](/wiki/Nemzeti_Bajnoks%C3%A1g_II) under its Hungarian translated name, **Szabadkai Vasutas AC**. During this period the coach was Geza Takács and among its best known players was [István Nyers](/wiki/Istv%C3%A1n_Nyers) who begin his senior career at the club.

After the end of the Second World War, the club made a tour throughout Serbia in order to prepare for competition, however the new socialist authorities demanded that most of the pre-1945 clubs to be disbanded and new clubs to be formed. ŽAK was dissolved in 1945 and its players made a meeting where they decided to join the newly founded [FK Spartak Subotica](/wiki/FK_Spartak_Subotica) which beside the players, the stadium, the colors and the fans, it also inherited the tradition of being backed by the railways.

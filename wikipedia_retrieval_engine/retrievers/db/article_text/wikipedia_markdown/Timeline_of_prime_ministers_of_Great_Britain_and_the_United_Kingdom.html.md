## Career-based timeline

This timeline shows most of the early life, the political career and death of each prime minister of the United Kingdom from 1846. The first prime minister was [Robert Walpole](/wiki/Robert_Walpole) in the early 18th century ([Dickinson 1973](#CITEREFDickinson1973)).

Unlike countries where the leader is elected directly to the highest political office of a separate [executive](/wiki/Executive_(government)), the [prime minister](/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_the_United_Kingdom) must first establish a political career in the UK Parliament and typically serves many years in the [House of Commons](/wiki/House_of_Commons_of_the_United_Kingdom) before becoming prime minister, and in some cases for many years afterwards.

Since the [Marquess of Salisbury](/wiki/Robert_Gascoyne-Cecil,_3rd_Marquess_of_Salisbury) in 1895, all time in parliamentary service before being prime minister has been in the House of Commons, apart from Sir [Alec Douglas-Home](/wiki/Alec_Douglas-Home)'s period as a member of the Government while in the House of Lords (1951–63; though he was previously the elected member for [Lanark](/wiki/Lanark_(UK_Parliament_constituency)), 1931–1945). After becoming prime minister, Douglas-Home returned to the Commons by winning a by-election on the recess death of MP [Gilmour Leburn](/wiki/Gilmour_Leburn).

Uniquely, [Cameron](/wiki/David_Cameron)'s brief parliamentary activity as [Foreign Secretary](/wiki/Foreign_Secretary_(United_Kingdom)) from November 2023 to July 2024 was served while a member of the [House of Lords](/wiki/House_of_Lords).

### Key

- Each dark coloured bar denotes the time spent as prime minister
- A light colour denotes time spent in Parliament before or after serving as prime minister
- A grey colour bar denotes the time the prime minister spent outside the House of Commons or the entire Parliament, either before or after their political career

- *four separate periods in office:* [Gladstone](/wiki/William_Ewart_Gladstone)
- *[crossed the floor](/wiki/Crossing_the_floor):* [Palmerston](/wiki/Henry_John_Temple,_3rd_Viscount_Palmerston), [Derby](/wiki/Edward_Smith-Stanley,_14th_Earl_of_Derby), [Gladstone](/wiki/William_Ewart_Gladstone), [Churchill](/wiki/Winston_Churchill) (twice)
- *died in office:* [Canning](/wiki/George_Canning), [Palmerston](/wiki/Henry_John_Temple,_3rd_Viscount_Palmerston), [Perceval](/wiki/Spencer_Perceval) ([assassinated](/wiki/Assassination_of_Spencer_Perceval))
- *died less than six months after leaving office:* [Portland](/wiki/William_Cavendish-Bentinck,_3rd_Duke_of_Portland), [Campbell-Bannerman](/wiki/Henry_Campbell-Bannerman), [Law](/wiki/Bonar_Law), [Chamberlain](/wiki/Neville_Chamberlain)
- *left Parliament on leaving office:* [Russell](/wiki/John_Russell,_1st_Earl_Russell), [Baldwin](/wiki/Stanley_Baldwin), [Blair](/wiki/Tony_Blair)
- *lived for more than twenty years after leaving the House of Commons:* [Macmillan](/wiki/Harold_Macmillan), [Douglas-Home](/wiki/Alec_Douglas-Home), [Thatcher](/wiki/Margaret_Thatcher), [Major](/wiki/John_Major)
- *less than twenty years in Parliament before being prime minister:* [Baldwin](/wiki/Stanley_Baldwin), [Chamberlain](/wiki/Neville_Chamberlain), [Wilson](/wiki/Harold_Wilson), [Major](/wiki/John_Major), [Blair](/wiki/Tony_Blair), [Cameron](/wiki/David_Cameron), [May](/wiki/Theresa_May), [Johnson](/wiki/Boris_Johnson), [Truss](/wiki/Liz_Truss), [Sunak](/wiki/Rishi_Sunak), [Starmer](/wiki/Keir_Starmer)
- *more than twenty years in Parliament after being prime minister:* [Rosebery](/wiki/Archibald_Primrose,_5th_Earl_of_Rosebery), [Balfour](/wiki/Arthur_Balfour), [Lloyd George](/wiki/David_Lloyd_George), [Heath](/wiki/Edward_Heath)
- *served as prime minister after an interruption to their parliamentary career:* [MacDonald](/wiki/Ramsay_MacDonald), [Churchill](/wiki/Winston_Churchill), [Macmillan](/wiki/Harold_Macmillan) (twice), [Douglas-Home](/wiki/Alec_Douglas-Home), [Johnson](/wiki/Boris_Johnson)
- *served as prime minister for two monarchs:* [Walpole](/wiki/Robert_Walpole), [Newcastle](/wiki/Thomas_Pelham-Holles,_1st_Duke_of_Newcastle), [Liverpool](/wiki/Robert_Jenkinson,_2nd_Earl_of_Liverpool), [Wellington](/wiki/Arthur_Wellesley,_1st_Duke_of_Wellington), [Melbourne](/wiki/William_Lamb,_2nd_Viscount_Melbourne), [Peel](/wiki/Robert_Peel), [Salisbury](/wiki/Robert_Gascoyne-Cecil,_3rd_Marquess_of_Salisbury), [Asquith](/wiki/H._H._Asquith), [Churchill](/wiki/Winston_Churchill), [Truss](/wiki/Liz_Truss)

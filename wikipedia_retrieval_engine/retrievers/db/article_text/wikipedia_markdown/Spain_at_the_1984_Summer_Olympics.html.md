# Spain at the 1984 Summer Olympics

## Infobox

- **IOC code:** ESP
- **NOC:** Spanish Olympic Committee
- **Competitors:** 179 (163 men and 16 women) in 23 sports
- **Flag bearer:** Alejandro Abascal
- **Medals Ranked 20th:** Gold 1 Silver 2 Bronze 2 Total 5

Spain competed at the [1984 Summer Olympics](/wiki/1984_Summer_Olympics) in [Los Angeles](/wiki/Los_Angeles), United States. 179 competitors, 163 men and 16 women, took part in 104 events in 23 sports.

## Archery

Main article: [Archery at the 1984 Summer Olympics](/wiki/Archery_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics)

In its third Olympic archery competition, Spain was represented by two men and two women.

**Women's Individual Competition:**

- [Montserrat Martin](/wiki/Montserrat_Martin) – 2418 points (→ 28th place)

- [Manuel Rubio](/wiki/Manuel_Rubio) – 2390 points (→ 37th place)

## Rowing

Main article: [Rowing at the 1984 Summer Olympics](/wiki/Rowing_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics)

There were eight competitions for men and Spain qualified four boats, but had no women competing in 1984. Although he had competed at the [1980 Summer Olympics](/wiki/Spain_at_the_1980_Summer_Olympics), [Manuel Vera Vázquez](/wiki/Manuel_Vera_V%C3%A1zquez) was still the youngest competitor of the Spanish rowing team. The [coxless pair](/wiki/Rowing_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_coxless_pair) of [Fernando Climent](/wiki/Fernando_Climent) and [Luis María Lasúrtegui](/wiki/Luis_Mar%C3%ADa_Las%C3%BArtegui) was a surprise medallist, and as of [2016](/wiki/Spain_at_the_2016_Summer_Olympics#Rowing), the team's silver medal is the only Olympic rowing medal that the nation has ever won.

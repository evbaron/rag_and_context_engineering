*[Star Wars](/wiki/Star_Wars)* is a [media franchise](/wiki/Media_franchise) and [shared](/wiki/Shared_universe) [fictional universe](/wiki/Fictional_universe) that is the setting of [science fiction films](/wiki/Science_fiction_film) produced by [Lucasfilm](/wiki/Lucasfilm), based on [characters](/wiki/List_of_Star_Wars_characters) created by [George Lucas](/wiki/George_Lucas). The Skywalker Saga of the franchise includes nine films, featuring three [trilogies](/wiki/Trilogy); the original trilogy, the [prequel](/wiki/Prequel) trilogy, and the sequel trilogy. The original trilogy began with *[A New Hope](/wiki/Star_Wars_(film))* in 1977, followed by *[The Empire Strikes Back](/wiki/The_Empire_Strikes_Back)* (1980) and *[Return of the Jedi](/wiki/Return_of_the_Jedi)* (1983), while the prequel trilogy consists of *[The Phantom Menace](/wiki/Star_Wars:_Episode_I_%E2%80%93_The_Phantom_Menace)* (1999), *[Attack of the Clones](/wiki/Star_Wars:_Episode_II_%E2%80%93_Attack_of_the_Clones)* (2002), and *[Revenge of the Sith](/wiki/Star_Wars:_Episode_III_%E2%80%93_Revenge_of_the_Sith)* (2005). The sequel trilogy consists of *[The Force Awakens](/wiki/Star_Wars:_The_Force_Awakens)* (2015), *[The Last Jedi](/wiki/Star_Wars:_The_Last_Jedi)* (2017), and *[The Rise of Skywalker](/wiki/Star_Wars:_The_Rise_of_Skywalker)* (2019), and chronologically follows *Return of the Jedi* in the saga. Additionally, an [animated film](/wiki/Star_Wars:_The_Clone_Wars_(film)) was released in 2008, and two standalone anthology films were released in 2016 and 2018.

The films feature large, sometimes [ensemble](/wiki/Ensemble_cast), casts of actors and actresses, with multiple lead actors. [Mark Hamill](/wiki/Mark_Hamill), [Harrison Ford](/wiki/Harrison_Ford), and [Carrie Fisher](/wiki/Carrie_Fisher) star as [Luke Skywalker](/wiki/Luke_Skywalker), [Han Solo](/wiki/Han_Solo), and [Leia Organa](/wiki/Princess_Leia), respectively, in the original trilogy films. [Liam Neeson](/wiki/Liam_Neeson), [Ewan McGregor](/wiki/Ewan_McGregor), [Natalie Portman](/wiki/Natalie_Portman), and [Jake Lloyd](/wiki/Jake_Lloyd) respectively star as [Qui-Gon Jinn](/wiki/Qui-Gon_Jinn), [Obi-Wan Kenobi](/wiki/Obi-Wan_Kenobi), [Padmé Amidala](/wiki/Padm%C3%A9_Amidala) and [Anakin Skywalker](/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker) in *The Phantom Menace*, with McGregor and Portman returning for its two sequels and [Hayden Christensen](/wiki/Hayden_Christensen) playing the older Anakin in them. Ford, Hamill, Fisher, and other cast members from the original trilogy returned to reprise their roles and co-star alongside [Adam Driver](/wiki/Adam_Driver) as [Kylo Ren](/wiki/Kylo_Ren), [Daisy Ridley](/wiki/Daisy_Ridley) as [Rey](/wiki/Rey_(Star_Wars)) and [John Boyega](/wiki/John_Boyega) as [Finn](/wiki/Finn_(Star_Wars)) in the sequel trilogy films. [Matt Lanter](/wiki/Matt_Lanter) portrays Anakin in the animated film *The Clone Wars* (2008), [Felicity Jones](/wiki/Felicity_Jones) portrays [Jyn Erso](/wiki/Jyn_Erso) in *[Rogue One](/wiki/Rogue_One)* (2016), and [Alden Ehrenreich](/wiki/Alden_Ehrenreich) portrays a young Han Solo in *[Solo: A Star Wars Story](/wiki/Solo:_A_Star_Wars_Story)* (2018).

[Anthony Daniels](/wiki/Anthony_Daniels) is the only actor to have appeared in all films in the franchise, appearing as [C-3PO](/wiki/C-3PO) in all except *Solo*, in which he appeared as Tak. Multiple other cast members [recur](/wiki/Recurring_character) across multiple films and series within the franchise. The list below is sorted by film and the character's surname, as some characters have been portrayed by multiple actors. All characters that have made appearances in other *Star Wars* media, such as the [television series](/wiki/List_of_Star_Wars_television_series) or television films, are noted.

The American singer [Katy Perry](/wiki/Katy_Perry) has received hundreds of awards and nominations throughout her career. Among other accolades, she is the recipient of five [American Music Awards](/wiki/American_Music_Awards), 16 [ASCAP Pop Music Awards](/wiki/American_Society_of_Composers,_Authors_and_Publishers), five [*Billboard* Music Awards](/wiki/Billboard_Music_Awards), 20 [Guinness World Records](/wiki/Guinness_World_Records), seven [MTV Video Music Awards](/wiki/MTV_Video_Music_Awards) (including the [Michael Jackson Video Vanguard Award](/wiki/Michael_Jackson_Video_Vanguard_Award)), 14 [People's Choice Awards](/wiki/People%27s_Choice_Awards), a [Juno Award](/wiki/Juno_Award), a [Brit Award](/wiki/Brit_Award), two [Myx Music Awards](/wiki/Myx_Music_Awards), four [NRJ Music Awards](/wiki/NRJ_Music_Awards), two [Shorty Awards](/wiki/Shorty_Awards), and 12 [Webby Awards](/wiki/Webby_Awards). Perry has also been nominated for a [Swiss Music Award](/wiki/Swiss_Music_Awards), two [ARIA Music Awards](/wiki/ARIA_Music_Awards), 8 [MTV Italian Music Awards](/wiki/MTV_Italian_Music_Awards), 13 [Grammy Awards](/wiki/Grammy_Awards), two [Channel V Thailand Music Video Awards](/wiki/Channel_V_Thailand_Music_Video_Awards), two [*Q* Awards](/wiki/Q_Awards), and two [Danish Music Awards](/wiki/Danish_Music_Awards).

Perry's second studio album *[One of the Boys](/wiki/One_of_the_Boys)* was released in 2008 and received the 2009 [NRJ Music Award](/wiki/NRJ_Music_Award) for International Album of the Year. Two singles from the album—"[I Kissed a Girl](/wiki/I_Kissed_a_Girl)" and "[Hot n Cold](/wiki/Hot_n_Cold)"—were nominated for the [Grammy Award for Best Female Pop Vocal Performance](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Female_Pop_Vocal_Performance) in 2009 and 2010, respectively. The former's music video received five [MTV Video Music Award](/wiki/MTV_Video_Music_Award) nominations and the [MTV Video Music Award Japan for Best Pop Video](/wiki/MTV_Video_Music_Award_Japan_for_Best_Pop_Video). Perry received multiple Best New Artist nominations, including one at the [Los Premios MTV Latinoamérica 2008](/wiki/Los_Premios_MTV_Latinoam%C3%A9rica_2008), and won the 2008 [MTV Europe Music Award for Best New Act](/wiki/MTV_Europe_Music_Award_for_Best_New_Act) and also won the 2009 [Brit Award for International Female Solo Artist](/wiki/Brit_Award_for_International_Female_Solo_Artist). Her third album, *[Teenage Dream](/wiki/Teenage_Dream)*, became the first by a female artist to produce five number-one US [*Billboard* Hot 100](/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100) songs—"[California Gurls](/wiki/California_Gurls)", "[Teenage Dream](/wiki/Teenage_Dream_(Katy_Perry_song))", "[Firework](/wiki/Firework_(song))", "[E.T.](/wiki/E.T._(song))", and "[Last Friday Night (T.G.I.F.)](/wiki/Last_Friday_Night_(T.G.I.F.))". For the feat, she received a [Special Achievement Award](/wiki/American_Music_Award_for_Achievement) during the [2011 American Music Awards](/wiki/American_Music_Awards_of_2011) and an entry in the 2013 edition of *Guinness World Records*. The album was nominated for six Grammy Awards, including [Album of the Year](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Album_of_the_Year) and [Record of the Year](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Record_of_the_Year) for "Firework", and won the [Juno Award for International Album of the Year](/wiki/Juno_Award_for_International_Album_of_the_Year).

Perry received ten nominations at the [2011 MTV Video Music Awards](/wiki/2011_MTV_Video_Music_Awards), winning [Video of the Year](/wiki/MTV_Video_Music_Award_for_Video_of_the_Year) for "Firework" along with [Best Collaboration](/wiki/MTV_Video_Music_Award_for_Best_Collaboration) and [Best Visual Effects](/wiki/MTV_Video_Music_Award_for_Best_Visual_Effects) for "E.T.". She became the first artist to be nominated for four different videos at a single ceremony. Her single "[Wide Awake](/wiki/Wide_Awake_(Katy_Perry_song))" from *[Teenage Dream: The Complete Confection](/wiki/Teenage_Dream:_The_Complete_Confection)*—a reissue of *Teenage Dream*—was nominated for the [Grammy Award for Best Pop Solo Performance](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Pop_Solo_Performance). In 2012, *[Billboard](/wiki/Billboard_(magazine))* magazine declared her the "Woman of the Year" for her contributions to music. Perry's fourth album, *[Prism](/wiki/Prism_(Katy_Perry_album))*, released in 2013, contained the singles "[Roar](/wiki/Roar_(song))" and "[Dark Horse](/wiki/Dark_Horse_(Katy_Perry_song))". The former was nominated for [Song of the Year](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Song_of_the_Year) and Best Pop Solo Performance at the [56th Annual Grammy Awards](/wiki/56th_Annual_Grammy_Awards). At the [following ceremony](/wiki/57th_Annual_Grammy_Awards), the album received a [Best Pop Vocal Album](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Pop_Vocal_Album) nomination while "Dark Horse" was nominated for [Best Pop Duo/Group Performance](/wiki/Best_Pop_Duo/Group_Performance). "Dark Horse" won [Single of the Year](/wiki/American_Music_Award_for_Single_of_the_Year) at the 2014 American Music Awards.

Perry additionally received the [Audrey Hepburn](/wiki/Audrey_Hepburn) Humanitarian Award from [UNICEF](/wiki/UNICEF) in 2016, and won "Latin Pop/Urban Song of the Year" from the [2020 iHeartRadio Music Awards](/wiki/2020_iHeartRadio_Music_Awards) for a remix of "[Con Calma](/wiki/Con_Calma#Remix_version)" with [Daddy Yankee](/wiki/Daddy_Yankee) and [Snow](/wiki/Snow_(musician)). Her tenure as a judge alongside [Lionel Richie](/wiki/Lionel_Richie) and [Luke Bryan](/wiki/Luke_Bryan) on *[American Idol](/wiki/American_Idol)* won Outstanding Judging Panel at the [American Reality Television Awards](/wiki/American_Reality_Television_Awards) in 2021. During the 2023 [PETA's Libby Awards](/wiki/People_for_the_Ethical_Treatment_of_Animals), a shoe line of hers titled [Katy Perry Collections](/wiki/Katy_Perry_Collections) won Favorite Celeb Vegan Brand. At the [2024 MTV Video Music Awards](/wiki/2024_MTV_Video_Music_Awards), she received the [Michael Jackson Video Vanguard Award](/wiki/Michael_Jackson_Video_Vanguard_Award). The following year, Perry received the [Brit Billion Award](/wiki/Brit_Billion_Award) from the [British Phonographic Industry](/wiki/British_Phonographic_Industry) (BPI) for reaching one billion cumulative streams in the United Kingdom.

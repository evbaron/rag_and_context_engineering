**Cam Plante** (born March 12, 1964) is a Canadian former professional [ice hockey](/wiki/Ice_hockey) [defenceman](/wiki/Defenceman_(ice_hockey)). He was drafted in the seventh round, 128th overall, by the [Toronto Maple Leafs](/wiki/Toronto_Maple_Leafs) in the [1983 NHL Entry Draft](/wiki/1983_NHL_Entry_Draft). He played two games in the [National Hockey League](/wiki/National_Hockey_League) with the Maple Leafs in the [1984–85 season](/wiki/1984%E2%80%9385_NHL_season).

Plante holds the [Western Hockey League](/wiki/Western_Hockey_League) record for points in a season by a defenceman. He scored 140 points as a member of the [Brandon Wheat Kings](/wiki/Brandon_Wheat_Kings) in the [1983–84 WHL season](/wiki/1983%E2%80%9384_WHL_season). During 72 games that season he scored 22 goals and had 118 assists.

He finished his playing career with the [Wichita Thunder](/wiki/Wichita_Thunder) of the CHL, having returned to North America following four seasons on the United Kingdom. Most of that time was spent with the [Peterborough Pirates](/wiki/Peterborough_Pirates), but also encompassed short spells with the [Chelmsford Chieftains](/wiki/Chelmsford_Chieftains) and [Humberside Hawks](/wiki/Humberside_Hawks).

He is the father of former [Florida Panthers](/wiki/Florida_Panthers) prospect [Tyler Plante](/wiki/Tyler_Plante) and former [Edmonton Oilers](/wiki/Edmonton_Oilers) prospect [Alex Plante](/wiki/Alex_Plante).

**Robat-e Morad** ([Persian](/wiki/Persian_language): رباطمراد) is a village in, and the capital of, [Galehzan Rural District](/wiki/Galehzan_Rural_District) of the [Central District](/wiki/Central_District_(Khomeyn_County)) of [Khomeyn County](/wiki/Khomeyn_County), [Markazi](/wiki/Markazi_province) province, [Iran](/wiki/Iran).

## Demographics

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the village's population was 1,738 in 555 households. The following census in 2011 counted 1,688 people in 607 households. The 2016 census measured the population of the village as 1,415 people in 532 households. It was the most populous village in its rural district.

# Gwangsan Kim clan

## Infobox

- **Country:** North and South Korea

- **Website:** kwangsankim.co.kr

The **Gwangsan Kim clan** ([Korean](/wiki/Korean_language): 광산 김씨; [Hanja](/wiki/Hanja): 光山 金氏) is a Korean clan with its [bon-gwan](/wiki/Bon-gwan) located in Gwangsan, present-day [Gwangju](/wiki/Gwangju).

The members of the Gwangsan Kim clan are the descendants of Kim Hŭng-gwang ([Korean](/wiki/Korean_language): 김흥광; [Hanja](/wiki/Hanja): 金興光), the third son of [King Sinmu of Silla](/wiki/Sinmu_of_Silla), the 45th monarch of the [Silla](/wiki/Silla).

The family has produced eminent [Neo-Confucian](/wiki/Neo-Confucianism) scholars during the [Joseon Dynasty](/wiki/Joseon), including [Kim Jang-saeng](/wiki/Kim_Jang-saeng), [Kim Jip](/wiki/Kim_Jip), and [Kim Man-jung](/wiki/Kim_Man-jung).

**1901** ([MCMI](/wiki/Roman_numerals)) was a [common year starting on Tuesday](/wiki/Common_year_starting_on_Tuesday) of the [Gregorian calendar](/wiki/Gregorian_calendar) and a [common year starting on Monday](/wiki/Common_year_starting_on_Monday) of the [Julian calendar](/wiki/Julian_calendar), the 1901st year of the [Common Era](/wiki/Common_Era) (CE) and *[Anno Domini](/wiki/Anno_Domini)* (AD) designations, the 901st year of the [2nd millennium](/wiki/2nd_millennium), the 1st year of the [20th century](/wiki/20th_century), and the 2nd year of the [1900s](/wiki/1900s) decade. As of the start of 1901, the Gregorian calendar was 13 days ahead of the Julian calendar, which remained in localized use until 1923.

[December 13](/wiki/December_13) of this year is the beginning of signed [32-bit](/wiki/32-bit_computing) [Unix time](/wiki/Unix_time), and is scheduled to end in [January 19, 2038](/wiki/Year_2038_problem).

## Summary

### Political and military

1901 started with the [unification](/wiki/Federation_of_Australia) of multiple [British colonies](/wiki/Crown_colony) in Australia on January 1 to form the [Commonwealth of Australia](/wiki/Australia) after a [referendum](/wiki/1898%E2%80%931900_Australian_constitutional_referendums) in [1900](/wiki/1900), Subsequently, the [1901 Australian election](/wiki/1901_Australian_federal_election) would see the first [Australian prime minister](/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Australia), [Edmund Barton](/wiki/Edmund_Barton). On the same day, Nigeria became a [British protectorate](/wiki/Colonial_Nigeria).

Following this, the [Victorian Era](/wiki/Victorian_era) would come to an end after [Queen Victoria](/wiki/Queen_Victoria) died on January 22 after a reign of 63 years and 216 days, which was [longer than those of any of her predecessors](/wiki/List_of_monarchs_in_Britain_by_length_of_reign), Her son, [Edward VII](/wiki/Edward_VII), succeeded her to the throne.

## Events

### January

Main article: [January 1901](/wiki/January_1901)

January 1: The [Commonwealth of Australia](/wiki/Commonwealth_of_Australia) forms as [British colonies](/wiki/British_overseas_territories) [federate](/wiki/Federation_of_Australia).

January 22: King [Edward VII](/wiki/Edward_VII) ascends the [British throne](/wiki/British_monarchy).

- [January 1](/wiki/January_1)
  - The [British colonies](/wiki/Crown_colony) of [New South Wales](/wiki/New_South_Wales), [Queensland](/wiki/Queensland), [South Australia](/wiki/South_Australia), [Tasmania](/wiki/Tasmania), [Victoria](/wiki/Victoria_(Australia)) and [Western Australia](/wiki/Western_Australia) [federate](/wiki/Federation_of_Australia) as the [Commonwealth of Australia](/wiki/Australia); [Edmund Barton](/wiki/Edmund_Barton) becomes the first [Prime Minister of Australia](/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Australia).
  - [Nigeria](/wiki/Nigeria) becomes a British protectorate.
- [January 9](/wiki/January_9) – [Lord Kitchener](/wiki/Herbert_Kitchener,_1st_Earl_Kitchener) reports that [Christiaan de Wet](/wiki/Christiaan_de_Wet) has shot one of the "peace" envoys, and flogged two more, who had gone to his commando to ask the Burgher citizens of South Africa to halt fighting.
- [January 22](/wiki/January_22) – [Queen Victoria](/wiki/Queen_Victoria) of the United Kingdom dies at [Osborne House](/wiki/Osborne_House) on the [Isle of Wight](/wiki/Isle_of_Wight). She is 81 years old and, having ruled for nearly 64 years, will be the second longest-reigning monarch in [British history](/wiki/British_history). Her eldest son, Prince Albert Edward, "Bertie", the longest-serving [Prince of Wales](/wiki/Prince_of_Wales) to this time, succeeds his mother at the age of 59, reigning as King [Edward VII](/wiki/Edward_VII), of the [United Kingdom](/wiki/United_Kingdom) and in innovation the [British Dominions](/wiki/British_Dominions) and also becoming [Emperor of India](/wiki/Emperor_of_India).
- [January 31](/wiki/January_31) – [Anton Chekhov](/wiki/Anton_Chekhov)'s play *[Three Sisters](/wiki/Three_Sisters_(play))* (Три сeстры, *Tri sestry*) is premiered at the [Moscow Art Theatre](/wiki/Moscow_Art_Theatre).

## Significance of 1901 for software

The date of Friday December 13 20:45:52 1901 is significant for modern computers because it is the earliest date representable with a signed [32-bit](/wiki/32-bit) integer on systems that reference time in seconds since the [Unix epoch](/wiki/Unix_epoch). This corresponds to −2147483648 seconds from Thursday January 1 00:00:00 1970. Software that depends on this representation cannot represent an earlier date.

Similarly, many computer systems suffer from the [year 2038 problem](/wiki/Year_2038_problem), when the positive number of seconds since 1970 exceeds 2147483647 (01111111 11111111 11111111 11111111 in binary) and, if represented as a signed 32-bit integer, wraps to −2147483648, representing Friday December 13 20:45:52 1901. In a way, the [year 2038 problem](/wiki/Year_2038_problem) for using signed 32-bit integers with January 1, 1970, as the zero date is as the [year 2000 problem](/wiki/Year_2000_problem) was for software using two-digit decimal integers with January 1, 1900, as the zero date.

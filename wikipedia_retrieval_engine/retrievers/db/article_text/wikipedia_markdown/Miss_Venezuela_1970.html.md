# Miss Venezuela 1970

## Infobox

- **Date:** July 1, 1970
- **Venue:** Teatro Nacional, Caracas, Venezuela
- **Broadcaster:** RCTV
- **Entrants:** 16
- **Placements:** 5
- **Winner:** Bella La Rosa Carabobo

**Miss Venezuela 1970** was the 17th edition of [Miss Venezuela pageant](/wiki/Miss_Venezuela) held at Teatro Nacional in [Caracas](/wiki/Caracas), [Venezuela](/wiki/Venezuela), on July 1, 1970. The winner of the pageant was [Bella La Rosa](/wiki/Bella_La_Rosa), [Miss Carabobo](/wiki/Carabobo), who entered the contest by chance because her twin sister Linda La Rosa, who was originally competing, had to quit after having a horse fall.

The pageant was broadcast live by [RCTV](/wiki/RCTV).

## Results

### Placements

- *Miss Venezuela 1970* - [Bella La Rosa](/wiki/Bella_La_Rosa) ([Miss Carabobo](/wiki/Carabobo))
- *1st runner-up* - Tomasita de las Casas ([Miss Miranda](/wiki/Miranda_(state)))
- *2nd runner-up* - Sonia Ledezma ([Miss Monagas](/wiki/Monagas))
- *3rd runner-up* - Peggy Romero ([Miss Departamento Vargas](/wiki/Vargas_(state))): first Afro-descendant in the history of the competition
- *4th runner-up* - Marisela Berti ([Miss Nueva Esparta](/wiki/Nueva_Esparta))

### Special awards

- *Miss Fotogénica (Miss Photogenic)* - Marlene Agreda ([Miss Aragua](/wiki/Aragua_(state)))
- *Miss Amistad (Miss Friendship)* - Reyna Noguera ([Miss Guárico](/wiki/Gu%C3%A1rico))
- *Miss Cortesía (Miss Courtesy)* - Peggy Romero ([Miss Departamento Vargas](/wiki/Vargas_(state)))
- *Miss Sonrisa (Best Smile)* - Maigualida Leandro ([Miss Bolívar](/wiki/Bol%C3%ADvar_(state)))

**Lucille Frances Lawless** [MNZM](/wiki/Member_of_the_New_Zealand_Order_of_Merit) ([née](/wiki/Birth_name#Maiden_and_married_names) **Ryan**; born 29 March 1968) is a New Zealand actress, singer, and director. She is best known for her roles as [Xena](/wiki/Xena) in the television series *[Xena: Warrior Princess](/wiki/Xena:_Warrior_Princess)*, as [D'Anna Biers](/wiki/Number_Three_(Battlestar_Galactica)) on the reimagined *[Battlestar Galactica](/wiki/Battlestar_Galactica_(2004_TV_series))* series, and [Lucretia](/wiki/List_of_Spartacus_characters#Lucretia) in the television series *[Spartacus: Blood and Sand](/wiki/Spartacus:_Blood_and_Sand)* and associated series. Since 2019, she has starred as Alexa in the television series *[My Life Is Murder](/wiki/My_Life_Is_Murder)*.

Lawless had recurring roles as Diane Lewis-Swanson on the [NBC](/wiki/NBC) sitcom *[Parks and Recreation](/wiki/Parks_and_Recreation)* (2012–2014), and as [Countess Palatine Ingrid von Marburg](/wiki/Countess_Palatine_Ingrid_von_Marburg) on the [WGN America](/wiki/WGN_America) supernatural series *[Salem](/wiki/Salem_(TV_series))* (2015). She also starred as Ruby on the [Starz](/wiki/Starz) horror-comedy series *[Ash vs Evil Dead](/wiki/Ash_vs_Evil_Dead)* (2015–2018).

## Early life and education

Lawless was born in the [Auckland](/wiki/Auckland) suburb of [Mount Albert](/wiki/Mount_Albert,_New_Zealand) to teacher Julie Ryan (née Haynes) and Mount Albert's mayor, banker [Frank Ryan](/wiki/Frank_Ryan_(mayor)). She is the fifth of six siblings (four brothers and one sister). She has described her family as "this big, sprawling [Irish Catholic](/wiki/Irish_Catholics) family", and while filming in Ireland for the [Discovery Channel](/wiki/Discovery_Channel) in 2004, told *[Ireland on Sunday](/wiki/Ireland_on_Sunday)* that her father's family originated in [Quilty, County Clare](/wiki/Quilty,_County_Clare), Ireland.

Her first musical was at age 10 and she began acting in secondary school. She attended [Marist College, Auckland](/wiki/Marist_College,_Auckland), and began studies at [Auckland University](/wiki/Auckland_University) in languages. At 18, she went on her "[overseas experience](/wiki/Overseas_experience)", travelling through Europe and Australia with her future husband, Garth Lawless. At 21, she won the 1989 Mrs New Zealand competition.

Lawless has said she suffered from [bulimia](/wiki/Bulimia_nervosa) as a child, but was able to overcome the illness.

## Acting career

Lawless's television debut was in the New Zealand sketch-comedy series, *Funny Business*. Then she studied drama at the [William Davis Centre for Actors Study](/wiki/William_Davis_Centre_for_Actors_Study) in [Vancouver](/wiki/Vancouver), Canada.

In 1994, Lawless appeared in *[Hercules and the Amazon Women](/wiki/Hercules_and_the_Amazon_Women)*, that became the television pilot for *[Hercules: The Legendary Journeys](/wiki/Hercules:_The_Legendary_Journeys)*. In [that episode](/wiki/List_of_Hercules:_The_Legendary_Journeys_episodes), she played a man-hating Amazon named Lysia. She went on to play another character, Lyla, in the first-season episode "As Darkness Falls".

### *Xena: Warrior Princess*

Main article: [Xena: Warrior Princess](/wiki/Xena:_Warrior_Princess)

Lawless at [San Diego Comic-Con](/wiki/San_Diego_Comic-Con) in July 2010

Lawless received her best-known role as a heroic warrior woman named [Xena](/wiki/Xena) in the first season of *Hercules*. She first appeared in the episode "The Warrior Princess" which aired in March 1995. R. J. Stewart dramatised the teleplay from a story that [Robert G. "Rob" Tapert](/wiki/Robert_Tapert) commissioned John Schulian to write. The character became a fan favourite. [Vanessa Angel](/wiki/Vanessa_Angel_(English_actress)) was originally cast in the role, but she fell ill and was unable to travel to New Zealand for shooting. To differentiate between Xena and the similar Lysia, Lawless's hair, previously an ash blonde, was dyed black. She also wore a much darker costume. Lawless returned as Xena in two more episodes of the first season of *Hercules*, which portrayed her turn from villainess to a good, heroic character.

The character was popular enough that a [spin-off](/wiki/Spin-off_(media)) series was created: *[Xena: Warrior Princess](/wiki/Xena:_Warrior_Princess)* debuted on 4 September 1995. *Xena: Warrior Princess*, like its parent programme, was a huge hit and achieved high ratings and cultural significance, lasting six seasons. The series brought Lawless an immense amount of attention and she became an international celebrity.

While taping an appearance on *[The Tonight Show with Jay Leno](/wiki/The_Tonight_Show_with_Jay_Leno)* in October 1996, Lawless suffered a fractured pelvis when the horse she was riding lost its footing in the studio car park. She made a complete recovery, but several episodes of the second season of *Xena* were rewritten with *[body swaps](/wiki/Body_swap)* to focus on background characters to minimise the time Lawless was needed on set. According to Lawless, the media attention about the accident increased viewership of the show.

### Other acting work

Lawless first appeared on [Broadway](/wiki/Broadway_theatre) in September 1997 in the *[Grease](/wiki/Grease_(musical))* revival, as the "bad girl" character Betty Rizzo. She wanted to play the lead role of Sandy and later stated her belief that the producers typecast her to play "bad girls" following her success as Xena. She said the Sandy character was very similar to her sheltered childhood, growing up in New Zealand with many protective older brothers.

From 2005 to 2009, she had a recurring role in the television series *[Battlestar Galactica](/wiki/Battlestar_Galactica_(2004_TV_series))*. Lawless appeared as D'Anna Biers, a reporter with the Fleet News Service who worked on a critical documentary about the crew of the [*Galactica*](/wiki/Battlestar_Galactica_(ship)) and was later revealed to be [Humanoid Cylon](/wiki/Humanoid_Cylon) model Number Three.

Lawless has also performed as a [voice actor](/wiki/Voice_actor) in several animated features: In 2008, Lawless voiced the animated character of [Diana Prince](/wiki/Diana_Prince)/[Wonder Woman](/wiki/Wonder_Woman) in the direct-to-video superhero animated film *[Justice League: The New Frontier](/wiki/Justice_League:_The_New_Frontier)*, adapted from the [DC Comics](/wiki/DC_Comics) limited series. In 2014, Lawless voiced the militaristic "Queen of the Ants" in the American animated television series *[Adventure Time](/wiki/Adventure_Time)* on the [Cartoon Network](/wiki/Cartoon_Network).

In 2007, Lawless was to appear as one of the leads in the ensemble cast of the [ABC](/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company) television series, *[Football Wives](/wiki/Football_Wives)*, based on the popular British series *[Footballers' Wives](/wiki/Footballers%27_Wives)*. The series did not continue past the pilot episode, but the network extended the options on its contracts with Lawless and the other actors slated to star in the series, including [Gabrielle Union](/wiki/Gabrielle_Union), [Kiele Sanchez](/wiki/Kiele_Sanchez), [Ving Rhames](/wiki/Ving_Rhames), and [James Van Der Beek](/wiki/James_Van_Der_Beek).

Lawless returned to television on 10 November 2008 in a guest-starring role on the [CBS](/wiki/CBS) television series *[CSI: Miami](/wiki/CSI:_Miami)*, in a Season 7 episode, "Cheating Death", playing a madam with connections to a murder and helping [Horatio "H" Caine](/wiki/Horatio_Caine) with his inquiries.

In 2009, Lawless appeared in two episodes of the final season of *[The L Word](/wiki/The_L_Word)* as Sergeant Marybeth Duffy. She played a role in the [Adam Sandler](/wiki/Adam_Sandler) film *[Bedtime Stories](/wiki/Bedtime_Stories_(film))* released December 2008. Also in 2008, Lawless appeared with her former *Xena* stuntwoman [Zoë Bell](/wiki/Zo%C3%AB_Bell) in [Sony](/wiki/Sony_Pictures_Television) ([Crackle](/wiki/Crackle_(company)))'s new web series *[Angel of Death](/wiki/Angel_of_Death_(web_series))*, written by [Ed Brubaker](/wiki/Ed_Brubaker), which debuted online in early 2009. In 2009, Lawless guest-starred in the [HBO](/wiki/HBO) series *[Flight of the Conchords](/wiki/Flight_of_the_Conchords_(TV_series))* as Paula, assistant to the [Prime Minister of New Zealand](/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_New_Zealand).

Lawless co-starred in the [Starz](/wiki/Starz_(TV_channel)) original series *[Spartacus: Blood and Sand](/wiki/Spartacus:_Blood_and_Sand)*. The show was based on the life of [Spartacus](/wiki/Spartacus), the famous gladiator, and the slave revolt he led, and was produced by long-time *Xena* producers [Sam Raimi](/wiki/Sam_Raimi) and ["Rob" Tapert](/wiki/Robert_Tapert), her own husband. Lawless played the role of [Lucretia](/wiki/Lucretia_(Spartacus)), the wife of [Lentulus Batiatus](/wiki/Lentulus_Batiatus), who were both the owners of a gladiator [ludus](/wiki/Roman_Ludus), which also saw Lawless doing [nude scenes](/wiki/Nudity_in_film) for the first time. Lawless won the 2011 [Saturn Award](/wiki/Saturn_Award) as Best Supporting Actress for her role as Lucretia in *Spartacus: Blood and Sand*. Lawless reprised her role as Lucretia in *[Spartacus: Gods of the Arena](/wiki/Spartacus:_Gods_of_the_Arena)*, which chronicled life in the Ludus before Spartacus's arrival, and she also returned for the sequel *[Spartacus: Vengeance](/wiki/Spartacus:_Vengeance)*.

Lawless provided the voice of [Goldmoon](/wiki/Goldmoon) for *[Dragonlance: Dragons of Autumn Twilight](/wiki/Dragonlance:_Dragons_of_Autumn_Twilight)*, a direct-to-DVD animated film based on the [novel](/wiki/Dragons_of_Autumn_Twilight) of the same name. From 2012 to 2014, she had a recurring role on the NBC series *[Parks and Recreation](/wiki/Parks_and_Recreation)* as Diane, the love interest and eventual wife of [Ron Swanson](/wiki/Ron_Swanson).

In 2014, Lawless guest-starred in *[Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/wiki/Agents_of_S.H.I.E.L.D.)* as [Isabelle Hartley](/wiki/Isabelle_Hartley) in the [season two premiere](/wiki/Shadows_(Agents_of_S.H.I.E.L.D.)), and reprised the role later in episode 15 of season two, "[One Door Closes](/wiki/One_Door_Closes)". In 2015, Lawless landed the recurring guest role of [Countess Palatine Ingrid Von Marburg](/wiki/Countess_Palatine_Ingrid_Von_Marburg) in [WGN America](/wiki/WGN_America)'s *[Salem](/wiki/Salem_(TV_series))*. Countess Marburg is presented as one of the last remaining survivors of the legendary line of ancient German witches. The series ended in 2017. In March 2015, it was announced that she would be portraying the role of Ruby in [Starz](/wiki/Starz) horror-comedy series *[Ash vs Evil Dead](/wiki/Ash_vs_Evil_Dead)*. The series ended in 2018.

In 2019, Lawless began playing Alexa Crowe, a retired police officer turned police consultant investigator in *[My Life Is Murder](/wiki/My_Life_Is_Murder)*. The [Acorn TV](/wiki/Acorn_TV) series began in 2019 and reached its third season in 2022. A fifth season was announced on [February 10, 2025](https://www.amcnetworks.com/press-releases/lucy-lawless-returns-for-an-all-new-season-of-acorn-tvs-my-life-is-murder/)

## Filmmaking

In 2023, Lawless co-wrote and directed *[Never Look Away](/wiki/Never_Look_Away_(2024_film))*, a film based on the life of New Zealand-born [CNN](/wiki/CNN) photojournalist and camerawoman [Margaret Moth](/wiki/Margaret_Moth). The film had its world premiere in the World Cinema Documentary Competition section of the 40th [Sundance Film Festival](/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival) in [Park City](/wiki/Park_City,_Utah) and [Salt Lake City](/wiki/Salt_Lake_City), [Utah](/wiki/Utah), in January 2024, and has gone on to feature at several other film festivals in 2024.

## Music

Lawless performing at the [Roxy](/wiki/The_Roxy_Theatre), 2007

Lawless, who has a background in musical theatre, played Betty Rizzo on Broadway in a production of *Grease* in 1997. She continued to sing during the run of *Xena*, even contributing dirge music of her own composition; this made it into the episode "The Path Not Taken" as the dirge "Burial", which she sang for the first time in the episode. Two musically-oriented episodes, "The Bitter Suite" and "Lyre, Lyre, Heart's on Fire", also showcased not only her singing but also that of some of her castmates. (*See also* [List of *Xena: Warrior Princess* episodes](/wiki/List_of_Xena:_Warrior_Princess_episodes) *for details*.)

Lawless was encouraged to resume her singing career after being a contestant on *[Celebrity Duets](/wiki/Celebrity_Duets)*, which she finished as runner-up to [Alfonso Ribeiro](/wiki/Alfonso_Ribeiro). She made her solo debut at the [Roxy](/wiki/The_Roxy_Theatre) in Hollywood, a venue of 500 seats, on 13 January 2007, with a sold-out crowd for consecutive concerts.

## LGBT rights icon

See also: [Xena: Warrior Princess in popular culture](/wiki/Xena:_Warrior_Princess_in_popular_culture)

Xena's ambiguous romantic relationship with travelling companion [Gabrielle](/wiki/Gabrielle_(Xena)) ([Renee O'Connor](/wiki/Renee_O%27Connor)) led to Lawless becoming a lesbian icon, a role of which she has said she is proud. She has said that during the years she was playing the role, she had been undecided on the nature of the relationship, but in a 2003 interview with *Lesbian News* magazine, she said that after viewing the series finale, she had come to see Xena and Gabrielle's relationship as definitely gay, adding "they're married, man". She has appeared at [gay pride](/wiki/Gay_pride) events such as the [Sydney Gay and Lesbian Mardi Gras](/wiki/Sydney_Gay_and_Lesbian_Mardi_Gras).

For her support of [LGBT rights](/wiki/LGBT_rights), including her public support for same-sex marriage, Lawless was given the Star 100–Ally of the Year award at the Australian LGBTI Awards ceremony in 2017.

## Other activities

Lawless is a member of the board of trustees of the StarShip Foundation, the charity arm of the [Starship Children's Health](/wiki/Starship_Children%27s_Health) which is part of the Auckland District Health Board. It is set up to provide additional equipment, support and help to staff, patients and families. She helps fundraising for the organisation. In 2008, she sat for the New Zealand television series *The Sitting*, an arts series where celebrity portraits are produced during an interview session, with the portraits later auctioned for charity.

Since 2006, 21 September marks "Lucy Lawless Feel the Love Day/Week". The day, organised by the Official Lucy Lawless Fan Club, begins a week of charitable acts and donations by fans in honour and support of Lawless.

In May 2009 Lawless became a "climate ambassador" for the [Greenpeace](/wiki/Greenpeace) "Sign On" campaign.

In February 2012, she and six other Greenpeace activists boarded an oil drilling ship at [Port Taranaki](/wiki/Port_Taranaki), and remained on it for 77 hours to stop it leaving for the [Arctic](/wiki/Arctic) where it was going to take part in [oil exploration](/wiki/Petroleum_exploration_in_the_Arctic). She was subsequently arrested and charged with burglary, which carried an imprisonment term of up to 10 years if convicted. She pleaded guilty on 14 June 2012 to trespass charges regarding the February incident. Lawless said she intended for now to remain involved with Greenpeace. In February 2013, Lawless and the other six activists were each sentenced to pay a fine of NZ$651 and undertake 120 hours of [community service](/wiki/Community_service). The judge denied the NZ$545,000 in reparations that [Shell Todd Oil Services](/wiki/Shell_Todd_Oil_Services) had sought from the activists. Following the sentencing, Lawless said: "I consider it a great victory that the court has struck down the reparation demand from Shell, which I think was absolutely ludicrous."

## Personal life

Lawless with her husband [Robert Tapert](/wiki/Robert_Tapert) in 2018

In 1988, she became pregnant to her boyfriend Garth Lawless while working with him in the Australian [outback](/wiki/Outback). They married in [Kalgoorlie, Western Australia](/wiki/Kalgoorlie,_Western_Australia), that year, then returned to New Zealand where their daughter was born. They divorced in 1995.

On 28 March 1998, Lawless married *Xena's* executive producer, Pacific Renaissance Pictures CEO [Robert Tapert](/wiki/Robert_Tapert), at [St. Monica Catholic Church](/wiki/St._Monica_Catholic_Church_(Santa_Monica,_California)) in [Santa Monica, California](/wiki/Santa_Monica,_California), US. They have two sons.

## Recognition and awards

In the [2004 Queen's Birthday Honours](/wiki/2004_Birthday_Honours_(New_Zealand)), Lawless was appointed a [Member of the New Zealand Order of Merit](/wiki/Member_of_the_New_Zealand_Order_of_Merit), for services to entertainment and the community.

Astronomer [Michael E. Brown](/wiki/Michael_E._Brown) nicknamed his newly discovered [dwarf planet](/wiki/Dwarf_planet) "Xena" in 2003, its then-provisional designation being 2003 UB313. When this object was initially determined to be larger than [Pluto](/wiki/Pluto), it gained international attention and forced a year-long debate among astronomers as to the [definition of a planet](/wiki/Definition_of_planet). Observations made by [New Horizons](/wiki/New_Horizons) subsequently found Pluto to be marginally larger than the object, which was ultimately named [Eris](/wiki/Eris_(dwarf_planet)). The object's nickname "Xena" was used in the press. *[New Scientist](/wiki/New_Scientist)* magazine polled the public on their preferred final name for the so-called tenth planet; "Xena" ranked number 4. Lawless called Brown in December 2005 to thank him for his "senseless act of beauty", and claimed that she "never dared hope [the name] would stick". Eventually, Xena and Pluto were deemed not to be true planets, and were instead classified as dwarf planets. In 2006, the dwarf planet nicknamed Xena was officially named [Eris](/wiki/Eris_(dwarf_planet)). However, Brown made an indirect tribute to Lawless by naming Eris's moon [Dysnomia](/wiki/Dysnomia_(moon)) after [the Greek goddess of lawlessness](/wiki/Dysnomia_(deity)).

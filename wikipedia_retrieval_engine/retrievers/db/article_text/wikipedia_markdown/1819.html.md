[August 7](/wiki/August_7): General [Simon Bolivar](/wiki/Simon_Bolivar) leads Colombian nationalists to victory over Spain in the decisive [Battle of Boyacá](/wiki/Battle_of_Boyac%C3%A1).

**1819** ([MDCCCXIX](/wiki/Roman_numerals)) was a [common year starting on Friday](/wiki/Common_year_starting_on_Friday) of the [Gregorian calendar](/wiki/Gregorian_calendar) and a [common year starting on Wednesday](/wiki/Common_year_starting_on_Wednesday) of the [Julian calendar](/wiki/Julian_calendar), the 1819th year of the [Common Era](/wiki/Common_Era) (CE) and *[Anno Domini](/wiki/Anno_Domini)* (AD) designations, the 819th year of the [2nd millennium](/wiki/2nd_millennium), the 19th year of the [19th century](/wiki/19th_century), and the 10th and last year of the [1810s](/wiki/1810s) decade. As of the start of 1819, the Gregorian calendar was 12 days ahead of the Julian calendar, which remained in localized use until 1923.

## Events

### January–March

- [January 2](/wiki/January_2) – The [Panic of 1819](/wiki/Panic_of_1819), the first major peacetime [financial crisis](/wiki/Financial_crisis) in the [United States](/wiki/United_States), begins.
- [January 25](/wiki/January_25) – [Thomas Jefferson](/wiki/Thomas_Jefferson) founds the [University of Virginia](/wiki/University_of_Virginia).
- [January 29](/wiki/January_29) – Sir [Stamford Raffles](/wiki/Stamford_Raffles) lands on the island of [Singapore](/wiki/Singapore).
- [February 2](/wiki/February_2) – *[Dartmouth College v. Woodward](/wiki/Dartmouth_College_v._Woodward)*: The Supreme Court of the United States under [John Marshall](/wiki/John_Marshall) rules in favor of [Dartmouth College](/wiki/Dartmouth_College), allowing Dartmouth to keep its charter and remain a private institution.
- [February 6](/wiki/February_6) – The [Treaty of Singapore](/wiki/1819_Singapore_Treaty), is signed between [Hussein Shah of Johor](/wiki/Hussein_Shah_of_Johor) and Sir [Stamford Raffles](/wiki/Stamford_Raffles) of Britain, to create a trading settlement in [Singapore](/wiki/Singapore).[[1]](https://www.nlb.gov.sg/main/article-detail?cmsuuid=92bb56f0-e821-40d1-bd8c-cf8fa7e7f172)
- [February 15](/wiki/February_15) – The [United States House of Representatives](/wiki/United_States_House_of_Representatives) agrees to the [Tallmadge Amendment](/wiki/Tallmadge_Amendment), barring slaves from the new state of Missouri (the opening vote in a controversy that leads to the [Missouri Compromise](/wiki/Missouri_Compromise)).
- [February 19](/wiki/February_19) – Captain [William Smith](/wiki/William_Smith_(mariner)) sights [Williams Point](/wiki/Williams_Point), the northeast extremity of [Livingston Island](/wiki/Livingston_Island) in the [South Shetlands](/wiki/South_Shetland_Islands), the first land discovered south of [latitude](/wiki/Latitude) 60° S.
- [February 22](/wiki/February_22) – [Adams–Onís Treaty](/wiki/Adams%E2%80%93On%C3%ADs_Treaty): Agreement is reached for Spain to cede [Florida](/wiki/Florida) (already under USA's domination) to the United States in exchange for renunciation of American claims on [Texas](/wiki/Texas) arising from the [Louisiana Purchase](/wiki/Louisiana_Purchase), American payment of $5 million to settle citizens' claims regarding Florida, and definition of international borders. Following ratification, the treaty takes effect in 1821.
- [March 23](/wiki/March_23) – In [Mannheim](/wiki/Mannheim), [Duchy of Baden](/wiki/Duchy_of_Baden), German dramatist [August von Kotzebue](/wiki/August_von_Kotzebue) is assassinated by [Karl Ludwig Sand](/wiki/Karl_Ludwig_Sand).

### April–June

- [April 6](/wiki/April_6)–[June 21](/wiki/June_21) – The French [slave ship](/wiki/Slave_ship) *Le Rodeur* sails from [Bonny](/wiki/Bonny,_Rivers) in West Africa to [Guadeloupe](/wiki/Guadeloupe) in the West Indies. In the course of the transatlantic voyage many onboard become blind, and 30 [slaves](/wiki/Slave) are thrown overboard as a consequence.
- [April 7](/wiki/April_7) (N.S.) ([March 26](/wiki/March_26) O.S.) – The [Governorate of Livonia](/wiki/Governorate_of_Livonia) of the Russian Empire emancipates its peasants from [serfdom](/wiki/Serfdom).
- [April 21](/wiki/April_21) – In a period of about a month starting today during his "Living Year", English poet [John Keats](/wiki/John_Keats) (resident by [Hampstead Heath](/wiki/Hampstead_Heath), north of London) writes "[La Belle Dame sans Merci](/wiki/La_Belle_Dame_sans_Merci)" and most of his major odes.
- [May 22](/wiki/May_22)
  - The [SS *Savannah*](/wiki/SS_Savannah) leaves port at [Savannah, Georgia](/wiki/Savannah,_Georgia), on a voyage to become the first [steamship](/wiki/Steamship) to cross the Atlantic Ocean. The ship arrives at [Liverpool](/wiki/Liverpool), England, nearly a month later, on [June 20](/wiki/June_20), although only a fraction of the trip is made under steam.
  - The city of [Memphis, Tennessee](/wiki/Memphis,_Tennessee), is founded.
- [June 16](/wiki/June_16) – The [Rann of Kutch earthquake](/wiki/1819_Rann_of_Kutch_earthquake), estimated at at least 7.7 [Mw](/wiki/Seismic_magnitude_scales#Mw) kills at least 1,543 people in [India](/wiki/India) in what is now the state of [Gujarat](/wiki/Gujarat) at the [Arabian Sea](/wiki/Arabian_Sea), causing an 80–150 km (50–93 mi) stretch of land to be raised as much as 6 m (20 ft) and creating a natural dam, the Allahbund.

### July–September

- [July 1](/wiki/July_1) – German astronomer [Johann Georg Tralles](/wiki/Johann_Georg_Tralles) discovers what will be called the [Great Comet of 1819](/wiki/Great_Comet_of_1819).
- [July 21](/wiki/July_21) – Explorer [William Parry](/wiki/William_Parry_(explorer)), sailing in the Arctic in a quest for the [Northwest Passage](/wiki/Northwest_Passage) through North America, guides the ships [HMS *Hecla*](/wiki/HMS_Hecla_(1815)) and [HMS *Griper*](/wiki/HMS_Griper_(1813)) through an iceberg-laden passage that will later be named the [Parry Channel](/wiki/Parry_Channel).
- [July 24](/wiki/July_24) – A cabinet meeting is convened by British Prime Minister [Lord Liverpool](/wiki/Lord_Liverpool) to discuss a report of an adulterous affair involving the wife of [George, Prince of Wales](/wiki/George_IV), the regent for his ailing father King [George III](/wiki/George_III). Despite reports that [Princess Caroline](/wiki/Caroline_of_Brunswick) is involved with her servant, Bartolomeo Pergami, the cabinet concludes that the trial of Caroline for adultery would be an embarrassment to the nation.
- [August 2](/wiki/August_2) – The first [Hep-Hep riots](/wiki/Hep-Hep_riots), communal [pogroms](/wiki/Pogrom) against [Ashkenazi Jews](/wiki/Ashkenazi_Jews) in the German Federation begin at [Würzburg](/wiki/W%C3%BCrzburg) in Bavaria. They continue until October with many Jews killed.
- [August 6](/wiki/August_6) – [Norwich University](/wiki/Norwich_University) is founded by Captain [Alden Partridge](/wiki/Alden_Partridge) in [Vermont](/wiki/Vermont) as the first private military school in the United States.
- [August 7](/wiki/August_7) – [Battle of Boyacá](/wiki/Battle_of_Boyac%C3%A1): [Simón Bolívar](/wiki/Sim%C3%B3n_Bol%C3%ADvar) is victorious over the Royalist Army in [Colombia](/wiki/Colombia). Colombia acquires its definitive independence from [Spanish rule](/wiki/Viceroyalty_of_New_Granada).
- [August 16](/wiki/August_16) – [Peterloo Massacre](/wiki/Peterloo_Massacre): A [yeomanry](/wiki/Yeomanry) unit charges into a crowd of [radical](/wiki/Radicals_(UK)) protesters in [Manchester](/wiki/Manchester), England, resulting in 15 deaths and over 600 injuries.
- [August 25](/wiki/August_25) – [Salon of 1819](/wiki/Salon_of_1819) opens in [Paris](/wiki/Paris). Amongst the exhibits is *[The Raft of the Medusa](/wiki/The_Raft_of_the_Medusa)*.
- [September 20](/wiki/September_20) – The [Carlsbad Decrees](/wiki/Carlsbad_Decrees) are issued throughout the [German Confederation](/wiki/German_Confederation) in response to the March assassination of [August von Kotzebue](/wiki/August_von_Kotzebue), suppressing liberal and nationalist views.

### October–December

- [October 13](/wiki/October_13) – A treaty is entered between the Raja of Cutch [Deshalji II](/wiki/Deshalji_II), and [East India Company](/wiki/East_India_Company), placing the [Cutch State](/wiki/Cutch_State) under Company rule.
- [October 15](/wiki/October_15) – [Desolation Island](/wiki/Desolation_Island_(South_Shetland_Islands)), in the [South Shetland Islands](/wiki/South_Shetland_Islands) of the Antarctic, is discovered by Captain [William Smith](/wiki/William_Smith_(mariner)).
- [November 2](/wiki/November_2) – [Bagyidaw](/wiki/Bagyidaw) is crowned as [Emperor of Burma](/wiki/List_of_Burmese_monarchs), at the imperial capital of [Inwa](/wiki/Inwa).
- [November 3](/wiki/November_3) – The [USS *Congress*](/wiki/USS_Congress_(1799)) becomes the first American warship to visit [China](/wiki/China), landing at [Lintin Island](/wiki/Nei_Lingding_Island), off of the coast of [Canton](/wiki/Guangzhou).
- [November 19](/wiki/November_19) – The *[Museo del Prado](/wiki/Museo_del_Prado)* opens in [Madrid](/wiki/Madrid). Initially, it has 311 significant paintings.
- [November 25](/wiki/November_25) – A [British expeditionary force](/wiki/Persian_Gulf_campaign_of_1819) reaches [Ras Al Khaimah](/wiki/Ras_Al_Khaimah) in the [Persian Gulf](/wiki/Persian_Gulf), preparatory to the bombardment and invasion of the town, which led to the signing of the [General Maritime Treaty of 1820](/wiki/General_Maritime_Treaty_of_1820) between the British and what were to become known as the [Trucial States](/wiki/Trucial_States).
- [December 17](/wiki/December_17)
  - The [Republic of Gran Colombia](/wiki/Republic_of_Gran_Colombia) is formally established, with [Simón Bolívar](/wiki/Sim%C3%B3n_Bol%C3%ADvar) as its first president.
  - The new [astronomical observatory of Capodimonte](/wiki/Astronomical_Observatory_of_Capodimonte) in [Naples](/wiki/Naples) starts operating. The astronomer [Carlo Brioschi](/wiki/Carlo_Brioschi_(astronomer)) made the first observation by measuring the position of [⍺ Cassiopeiae](/wiki/Alpha_Cassiopeiae).

### Date unknown

- Early – [Denis Johnson](/wiki/Denis_Johnson_(inventor)) of London markets his "pedestrian curricle", a form of [dandy horse](/wiki/Dandy_horse), [velocipede](/wiki/Velocipede) or [kick scooter](/wiki/Kick_scooter).
- The city of Fernandina de Jagua (later [Cienfuegos](/wiki/Cienfuegos)) is founded in [Cuba](/wiki/Cuba).
- A British Arctic expedition under [William Parry](/wiki/William_Parry_(explorer)), comprising [HMS *Hecla*](/wiki/HMS_Hecla_(1815)) and [HMS *Griper*](/wiki/HMS_Griper_(1813)), reaches [longitude](/wiki/Longitude) 112°51' W in the [Northwest Passage](/wiki/Northwest_Passage), the furthest west which will be attained by any single-season voyage for 150 years.
- The United States [African Slave Trade Patrol](/wiki/African_Slave_Trade_Patrol) is founded to stop the slave trade on the coast of West Africa.

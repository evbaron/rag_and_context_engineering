**Casey Donovan** (born 13 May 1988) is an [Indigenous Australian](/wiki/Indigenous_Australian) singer and actress, best known for winning the [second season](/wiki/Australian_Idol_(season_2)) of the singing competition show *[Australian Idol](/wiki/Australian_Idol)* in 2004. She won the competition at the age of 16, becoming the series' youngest winner. In 2017, Donovan won the third series of *[I'm a Celebrity...Get Me Out of Here](/wiki/I%27m_a_Celebrity...Get_Me_Out_of_Here!_(Australia_season_3))*. Donovan also hosted the [NITV](/wiki/National_Indigenous_Television) music show *Fusion with Casey Donovan*.

## Early life

Casey Donovan was born in [Bankstown, New South Wales](/wiki/Bankstown,_New_South_Wales), to a family that included several relatives with musical careers, including her father, who along with his brothers are members of the country band [The Donovans](/wiki/The_Donovans). It was not until attending [Condell Park](/wiki/Condell_Park) High that her talent became noticed and was supported especially by both her mother and stepfather.

Donovan is a [Gumbaynggirr](/wiki/Gumbaynggirr) and Dungari woman.

## Career

### 2004–2006: *Australian Idol* and *For You*

Main article: [For You (Casey Donovan album)](/wiki/For_You_(Casey_Donovan_album))

In 2004, Donovan transferred to the [Australian Institute of Music](/wiki/Australian_Institute_of_Music) in Sydney and auditioned for the [second season](/wiki/Australian_Idol_(season_2)) of *[Australian Idol](/wiki/Australian_Idol)* singing [Kasey Chambers](/wiki/Kasey_Chambers)' "A Million Tears". During the series, Donovan won a [Deadly Award](/wiki/Deadly_Awards_2004) for Most Promising New Talent in Music. In November, Donovan was announced the winner of Idol over favourite [Anthony Callea](/wiki/Anthony_Callea). Her success was marred by a typographical mistake by Australian Idol sponsor [Telstra](/wiki/Telstra), in a half-page nationally run newspaper advertisement including what was assumed to be her website. The link was a memorial for the [late American pornography actor of the same name](/wiki/Casey_Donovan_(actor)). Telstra apologized for the error.

In February 2005, Donovan released "[What's Going On](/wiki/What%27s_Going_On_(Casey_Donovan_song))" which peaked at number 18 on the [ARIA Charts](/wiki/ARIA_Charts). At the [Deadly Awards 2005](/wiki/Deadly_Awards_2005), Donovan won Single Release of the Year for "Listen with Your Heart" and Artist of the Year. At the [ARIA Music Awards of 2005](/wiki/ARIA_Music_Awards_of_2005), Donovan was nominated for Highest Selling Single, losing to [Anthony Callea](/wiki/Anthony_Callea)'s "[The Prayer](/wiki/The_Prayer_(Celine_Dion_and_Andrea_Bocelli_song))". In 2006, Donovan was dropped from [Sony BMG](/wiki/Sony_BMG).

### 2007–2016: Post-*Idol* Career

In 2008, independently released the [extended play](/wiki/Extended_play) *[Eye 2 Eye](/wiki/Eye_2_Eye_(EP))*.

While starring in the successful concept show, *Women of Soul*, Donovan was approached by director [Rhoda Roberts](/wiki/Rhoda_Roberts) for the theatrical production of [Giorgio Battistelli](/wiki/Giorgio_Battistelli)'s opera *Miracle in Brisbane*, which was performed at the [Brisbane Festival](/wiki/Brisbane_Festival) in October 2009.

Donovan returned to music with the release of her single "Big, Beautiful & Sexy" written with songwriter Beau Golden, released in August 2010. A Facebook named for the song grew to over 10,000 fans shortly after release. Prior to the song's release, Donovan herself lost over 20 kg and continued her support larger men and women. Donovan went on to release another single in November 2010 titled "Last Regret," which gained some commercial airplay.

In December 2010, Donovan was nominated for two awards at the 2010 Sydney Theatre Awards for "Newcomer" and "Supporting Actress", continuing to receive praise and recognition from the industry for her work in *The Sapphires*. Although she auditioned for a role in the 2011 movie version, Donovan was not cast.

2011 saw Donovan reprise her role of Cynthia Macrae in another tour of *The Sapphires*. The tour ran during February in ACT and NSW, and was followed by two weeks in the UK in March.

In August/September 2011, Donovan created the role of [Mama Cass](/wiki/Mama_Cass) in the world premiere of new musical *Flowerchildren – [The Mamas & the Papas](/wiki/The_Mamas_%26_the_Papas) Story* written by Peter Fitzpatrick and produced by Australian company [Magnormos](/wiki/Magnormos). She was joined by [Matt Hetherington](/wiki/Matt_Hetherington) as [John Phillips](/wiki/John_Phillips_(musician)), Laura Fitzpatrick as [Michelle Phillips](/wiki/Michelle_Phillips), and Dan Humphris as [Denny Doherty](/wiki/Denny_Doherty).

Donovan also played several roles in Shakespeare's As You Like It at Sydney's Belvoir Street Theatre.

### 2017–2018: *I'm a Celebrity*, TV Advertisements and "Lonely"

In January 2017, Donovan was revealed as a celebrity contestant on the [third season](/wiki/I%27m_a_Celebrity...Get_Me_Out_of_Here!_(Australia_season_3)) of the Australian version of *[I'm a Celebrity...Get Me Out of Here!](/wiki/I%27m_a_Celebrity...Get_Me_Out_of_Here!_(Australian_TV_series))*. On 13 March 2017, Donovan won the series and was announced as the first ever Queen of the Jungle. In April 2017, Donovan became the face of the [Coles Supermarket](/wiki/Coles_Supermarket) "Down down" campaign. On 23 April 2017, Donovan performed an acoustic version of [David Bowie](/wiki/David_Bowie)'s "[Heroes](/wiki/Heroes_(David_Bowie_song))" at the [2017 Logie Awards](/wiki/2017_Logie_Awards) in which she received a standing ovation.
In May 2017 the *[Coles](/wiki/Coles_Supermarkets)*, "[Down Down](/wiki/Down_Down)" campaign was refreshed with Casey performing a disco version of the advertisement.
Donovan released her first single in seven years, "[Lonely](/wiki/Lonely_(Casey_Donovan_song))" on 19 May 2017 and *[Off the Grid & Somewhere in Between](/wiki/Off_the_Grid_%26_Somewhere_in_Between)* EP in August 2017.

She performed on New Year's Eve 2017 in Sydney, singing "[Lady Marmalade](/wiki/Lady_Marmalade)", "[Crazy Little Thing Called Love](/wiki/Crazy_Little_Thing_Called_Love)" and "[Nutbush City Limits](/wiki/Nutbush_City_Limits)".

### 2019–present: *Chicago*, *Australia Decides*, and *The Space* podcast

In 2019 Donovan starred as Mama Morton in the Australian revival of the musical, *[Chicago](/wiki/Chicago_(play))*, which received positive reviews.

In November 2019, Donovan was announced as a participant in *[Eurovision - Australia Decides](/wiki/Eurovision_-_Australia_Decides)*; in an attempt to represent [Australia in the Eurovision Song Contest 2020](/wiki/Australia_in_the_Eurovision_Song_Contest_2020). She performed the track "Proud" in the national final on 8 February 2020 and placed second.

In 2020, she became a host on *The Space* podcast, focused on mindfulness and wellbeing.

In 2021, Donovan appeared as Luna Keys in the third season of the comedy TV series *[How to Stay Married](/wiki/How_to_Stay_Married)*, alongside [Peter Helliar](/wiki/Peter_Helliar) and [Lisa McCune](/wiki/Lisa_McCune).

In 2022, she returned to the stage in the Australian production of the musical *[9 to Five](/wiki/9_to_5_(musical))*, playing the role of Judy Bernly which toured the [Capitol Theatre](/wiki/Capitol_Theatre,_Sydney) in Sydney, [Lyric Theatre](/wiki/Queensland_Performing_Arts_Centre) in Brisbane, [State Theatre](/wiki/State_Theatre_(Melbourne)) in Melbourne and [Festival Theatre](/wiki/Festival_Theatre,_Adelaide) in Adelaide.

In 2023, it was announced that Donovan would play the role of [Angelique/Nurse](/wiki/Nurse_(Romeo_and_Juliet)) in the Melbourne production of *[& Juliet](/wiki/%26_Juliet)* at the [Regent Theatre](/wiki/Regent_Theatre,_Melbourne). She then toured with the show to Singapore, Perth and Sydney.

Donovan next performed the role of Aunt Deborah in [Kimberly Akimbo](/wiki/Kimberly_Akimbo) in 2025, receiving praise for her "scene-stealer" performance.

Donovan announced that she would be touring Australia in 2026 with a show covering her entire career, *This Is Me*.

## Personal life

From 2004, Donovan was the victim of a six-year-long hoax telephone relationship with a persona named Campbell, [constructed](/wiki/Catfishing) by a woman named Olga.

In 2023, Donovan announced she was in a relationship with Renee Sharples, whom she met on a dating app during the COVID-19 lockdowns. The pair became engaged in June 2024. Donovan identifies as a queer woman.

**Brooks Wackerman** (born February 15, 1977) is an American musician. He is the current drummer of [metal](/wiki/Heavy_metal_music) band [Avenged Sevenfold](/wiki/Avenged_Sevenfold), which he joined in 2015. His first album with Avenged Sevenfold was *[The Stage](/wiki/The_Stage_(Avenged_Sevenfold_album))* (2016). He was previously the drummer for the punk rock band [Bad Religion](/wiki/Bad_Religion) (2001 to 2015). Wackerman has also performed, either as a member or a session or touring member, with [Blink-182](/wiki/Blink-182), [Bad4Good](/wiki/Bad4Good), [Infectious Grooves](/wiki/Infectious_Grooves), [Glenn Tipton](/wiki/Glenn_Tipton), [Mass Mental](/wiki/Mass_Mental), [Suicidal Tendencies](/wiki/Suicidal_Tendencies), [the Vandals](/wiki/The_Vandals), [Avril Lavigne](/wiki/Avril_Lavigne), [Korn](/wiki/Korn), Kidneys (in which he sang and played guitar, releasing two albums), [Fear and the Nervous System](/wiki/Fear_and_the_Nervous_System), [Tenacious D](/wiki/Tenacious_D), Farmikos, [Tom Delonge](/wiki/Tom_Delonge), and [Big Talk](/wiki/Big_Talk_(band)). His brothers [Chad Wackerman](/wiki/Chad_Wackerman) and John Wackerman are also drummers.

## Early life

Wackerman's father Chuck, also a drummer, started teaching him how to play drums as early as five years old and was also his jazz band teacher at [Los Alamitos High School](/wiki/Los_Alamitos_High_School), winning Most Outstanding Musician at the Reno Jazz Festival in 1993. Wackerman started his musical career as a teen with a group called [Bad4Good](/wiki/Bad4Good) in 1991. The band released one album in 1992 called *[Refugee](/wiki/Refugee_(Bad4Good_album))*, produced by [Steve Vai](/wiki/Steve_Vai).

## Career

### Infectious Grooves (1993–present)

From 1993 to 2000, Wackerman was a member of [Infectious Grooves](/wiki/Infectious_Grooves), playing drums on the albums *[Groove Family Cyco](/wiki/Groove_Family_Cyco)* in 1994 and *[Mas Borracho](/wiki/Mas_Borracho)* in 2000 as well as touring with the band. He returned to the band when they resumed activities and did a show in Brazil in November 2019.

### Suicidal Tendencies (1996–2001)

Wackerman was a member of [Suicidal Tendencies](/wiki/Suicidal_Tendencies) from 1996 to 2001, playing drums on *[Six the Hard Way](/wiki/Six_the_Hard_Way)* in 1998, *[Freedumb](/wiki/Freedumb)* in 1999, and *[Free Your Soul and Save My Mind](/wiki/Free_Your_Soul_and_Save_My_Mind)* in 2000. He left the band to join [Bad Religion](/wiki/Bad_Religion) in 2001.

### Bad Religion (2001–2015)

Wackerman with Bad Religion in 2008

In 2001, he became the drummer for [Bad Religion](/wiki/Bad_Religion), replacing [Bobby Schayer](/wiki/Bobby_Schayer) who left the band due to a shoulder injury. Wackerman appeared on five albums with the band (*[The Process of Belief](/wiki/The_Process_of_Belief)*, *[The Empire Strikes First](/wiki/The_Empire_Strikes_First)*, *[New Maps of Hell](/wiki/New_Maps_of_Hell_(Bad_Religion_album))*, *[The Dissent of Man](/wiki/The_Dissent_of_Man)* and *[True North](/wiki/True_North_(Bad_Religion_album))*), as well as their 2013 *[Christmas Songs](/wiki/Christmas_Songs_(Bad_Religion_album))* EP. On October 27, 2015, Brooks left Bad Religion, deciding it was time to pursue another path.

### Tenacious D (2006–2015)

In 2006, for [The Pick of Destiny tour](/wiki/The_Pick_of_Destiny_Tour_(2006-2007)), Wackerman was selected to feature as the drummer for Tenacious D due to his previous work on the Vandals' *Look What I Almost Stepped In...* album, which both [Jack Black](/wiki/Jack_Black) and [Kyle Gass](/wiki/Kyle_Gass) cameo on. He would go on to play for Tenacious D at various festivals as well as support act and benefit concerts throughout 2008 to 2011. in 2012, Wackerman played with Tenacious D again on their [Rize of the Fenix](/wiki/Rize_of_the_Fenix) tour where he got the nickname "Thunderskin" from Jack Black for comical remark, making appearances on the *[Late Show with David Letterman](/wiki/Late_Show_with_David_Letterman)*, *[Late Night with Jimmy Fallon](/wiki/Late_Night_with_Jimmy_Fallon)*, *[The Tonight Show with Jay Leno](/wiki/The_Tonight_Show_with_Jay_Leno)* and *[The Daily Show with Jon Stewart](/wiki/The_Daily_Show_with_Jon_Stewart)*. They also performed at the 2012 *[Rock am Ring](/wiki/Rock_am_Ring)* Festival. Wackerman also appeared in the music videos for their songs "Roadie" and "Rize of the Fenix". He would perform on their 2012 EP *Tenacious D Jazz* and their 2015 live album *[Tenacious D Live](/wiki/Tenacious_D_Live)*.

### Avenged Sevenfold (2015–present)

On November 4, 2015, it was revealed that Wackerman was the new drummer for [Avenged Sevenfold](/wiki/Avenged_Sevenfold). Brooks was working with the band one year prior to the official announcement because the band wanted to "fit in" with the new drummer. Like his predecessors [Mike Portnoy](/wiki/Mike_Portnoy) and Arin Ilejay, but unlike the rest of the band members, he doesn't have a stage name. His first full-length album with the band was their October 2016 release *[The Stage](/wiki/The_Stage_(Avenged_Sevenfold_album))*, which showcased his [progressive metal](/wiki/Progressive_metal) style of drumming, often using fast [double kicks](/wiki/Double_kick) and [blast beats](/wiki/Blast_beat).

### Other projects

Alongside [Terry Bozzio](/wiki/Terry_Bozzio), Wackerman contributed drums and percussion for [Korn](/wiki/Korn)'s [untitled album](/wiki/Untitled_Korn_album). Wackerman is also the drummer for the Innocent, a [thrash metal](/wiki/Thrash_metal) band featuring bassist [Ryan Sinn](/wiki/Ryan_Sinn) (formerly of [Angels & Airwaves](/wiki/Angels_%26_Airwaves) and [the Distillers](/wiki/The_Distillers)), vocalist [Brandan Schieppati](/wiki/Brandan_Schieppati) ([Bleeding Through](/wiki/Bleeding_Through)), and guitarist Dave Nassie ([No Use for a Name](/wiki/No_Use_for_a_Name)). In March 2008, Wackerman joined Korn guitarist [James "Munky" Shaffer](/wiki/James_Shaffer)'s newest solo project, [experimental](/wiki/Experimental_music) [industrial rock](/wiki/Industrial_rock) band [Fear and the Nervous System](/wiki/Fear_and_the_Nervous_System), alongside vocalist Steve Krolikowski ([Repeater](/wiki/Repeater_(band))), [Billy Gould](/wiki/Billy_Gould) ([Faith No More](/wiki/Faith_No_More)), Leopold Ross ([Error](/wiki/Error_(band))), and [Zac Baird](/wiki/Zac_Baird) (Korn, [Billy Goat](/wiki/Billy_Goat_(band))). Brooks also played drums for [Avril Lavigne](/wiki/Avril_Lavigne). Brooks filled in for [Travis Barker](/wiki/Travis_Barker) during Blink-182's 2013 tour of Australia in the [Soundwave](/wiki/Soundwave_(Australian_music_festival)) festival as well as their sideshows. Brooks was hired to record drums on Blink-182 member [Tom DeLonge](/wiki/Tom_DeLonge)'s debut solo album *[To the Stars... Demos, Odds and Ends](/wiki/To_the_Stars..._Demos,_Odds_and_Ends)*.

Wackerman formerly fronted Kidneys alongside fellow [Tenacious D](/wiki/Tenacious_D) band member [John Spiker](/wiki/John_Spiker). Initially, his brother, John Wackerman played with Kidneys, but he would later be replaced by Todd Hennig ([Death by Stereo](/wiki/Death_by_Stereo)). A full-length self-titled album was released in 2007, and another album in 2012, entitled *Hold Your Fire*, is available for digital download. The band parted ways in 2012.

Wackerman appeared in [the Suicide Machines](/wiki/The_Suicide_Machines)' "S.O.S." music video in place of drummer Derek Grant, due to Grant's refusal to appear in photos or videos.

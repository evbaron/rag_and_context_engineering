***Inside Out*** is the seventh studio album by [progressive metal](/wiki/Progressive_metal) band [Fates Warning](/wiki/Fates_Warning), released on July 26, 1994 through [Metal Blade Records](/wiki/Metal_Blade_Records). The album continues with the commercial sound that *[Parallels](/wiki/Parallels_(album))* began. It was the last to feature long-time bassist Joe DiBiase, who left after its release, as well as the last to feature guitarist [Frank Aresti](/wiki/Frank_Aresti) until his return to band in 2005. Aresti later appeared on their 2013 album *[Darkness in a Different Light](/wiki/Darkness_in_a_Different_Light)* as a full-time member and on the 2016 album *[Theories of Flight](/wiki/Theories_of_Flight)* as a guest. DiBiase would return in 2010 and 2016 for a number of shows, but did not officially rejoin.

This album was re-released in 2006 in Germany as part of a 2-CD Set with the album *[Disconnected](/wiki/Disconnected_(Fates_Warning_album))* featuring bonus tracks.

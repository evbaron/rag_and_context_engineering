# Ambyr Childers

**Ambyr Childers** is an American actress known for her portrayal of [Susan Atkins](/wiki/Susan_Atkins) in the [NBC](/wiki/NBC) crime drama *[Aquarius](/wiki/Aquarius_(U.S._TV_series))*, Ashley Rucker in the [Showtime](/wiki/Showtime_(TV_network)) crime drama *[Ray Donovan](/wiki/Ray_Donovan)*, and [Candace Stone](/wiki/Candace_Stone) in the [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) thriller series [*You*](/wiki/You_(TV_series)).

## Career

After appearing in the 2003 film *[Dickie Roberts: Former Child Star](/wiki/Dickie_Roberts:_Former_Child_Star)*, she played [Colby Chandler](/wiki/Colby_Chandler_(All_My_Children)) on the daytime soap opera *[All My Children](/wiki/All_My_Children)* from 2006 to 2008. She played Elizabeth "E" Dodd in *[The Master](/wiki/The_Master_(2012_film))*.

Childers and [Kate Bosworth](/wiki/Kate_Bosworth) co-created Ambyr Childers Jewelry.

Childers starred as [Candace Stone](/wiki/Candace_Stone) in [Lifetime](/wiki/Lifetime_(TV_network))'s television adaptation of *[You](/wiki/You_(TV_series))*, which premiered on September 9, 2018. On December 3, 2018, it was announced that *[You](/wiki/You_(TV_series))* would move to [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) as a "[Netflix Original](/wiki/Netflix_Original)" title, ahead of the premiere of the second season. On February 1, 2019, *[Deadline](/wiki/Deadline_Hollywood)* announced that Childers had been promoted to a series regular role, ahead of the second season's premiere. The second season was released on December 26, 2019.

## Personal life

Childers married film producer [Randall Emmett](/wiki/Randall_Emmett) in 2009. The couple had a daughter in 2010 and a second daughter in 2013. Emmett filed for separation from Childers in April 2015, but dismissed the petition the following year. Childers filed for divorce in January 2017. It was finalized in December 2017.

In this [Spanish name](/wiki/Spanish_name), the first or paternal [surname](/wiki/Surname) is  *Ollo* and the second or maternal family name is  *Hualde*.

**Ana Ollo Hualde** (born 24 January 1965) is a [Navarrese](/wiki/Navarrese) politician, [Minister of Citizen Relations of Navarre](/wiki/Government_of_Navarre) since July 2015 and Spokesperson of the Government of Navarre from July 2015 to September 2016.

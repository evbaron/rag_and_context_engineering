**Linus William Roache** (born 1 February 1964) is a British actor. He is best known to US audiences as Executive ADA [Michael Cutter](/wiki/List_of_Law_%26_Order_characters#Michael_Cutter) in the [NBC](/wiki/NBC) dramas *[Law & Order](/wiki/Law_%26_Order)* (2008–2010) and *[Law & Order: Special Victims Unit](/wiki/Law_%26_Order:_Special_Victims_Unit)* (2011–2012), and also played [Ecbert, King of Wessex](/wiki/Ecgberht,_King_of_Wessex) in *[Vikings](/wiki/Vikings_(2013_TV_series))* from 2014 to 2017.

He was nominated for a [Golden Globe Award](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actor_%E2%80%93_Miniseries_or_Television_Film) for playing [Robert F. Kennedy](/wiki/Robert_F._Kennedy) in *[RFK](/wiki/RFK_(film))* (2002), won a [Peabody Award](/wiki/Peabody_Award) for his role as Senator Wesley Smith in *[Fellow Travelers](/wiki/Fellow_Travelers_(miniseries))* (2023), and won a [Satellite Award for Best Supporting Actor (TV)](/wiki/Satellite_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor_%E2%80%93_Series,_Miniseries_or_Television_Film) for his role as [Ralph Wigram](/wiki/Ralph_Wigram) in *[The Gathering Storm](/wiki/The_Gathering_Storm_(2002_film))* (2002). His film appearances include *[Priest](/wiki/Priest_(1994_film))* (1994), *[The Wings of the Dove](/wiki/The_Wings_of_the_Dove_(1997_film))* (1997), (1990 BBC Omnibus film) Van Gogh *Pandaemonium* (2000), *[The Chronicles of Riddick](/wiki/The_Chronicles_of_Riddick)* (2004), *[Batman Begins](/wiki/Batman_Begins)* (2005), *[Non-Stop](/wiki/Non-Stop_(film))* (2014) and *[Mandy](/wiki/Mandy_(2018_film))* (2018). In 2018 and 2020, he had a recurring role in the final two seasons of *[Homeland](/wiki/Homeland_(TV_series))*.

## Early life

Roache was born in [Manchester](/wiki/Manchester), England, the son of *[Coronation Street](/wiki/Coronation_Street)* actor [William Roache](/wiki/William_Roache) and actress [Anna Cropper](/wiki/Anna_Cropper). Roache was educated at [Bishop Luffa School](/wiki/Bishop_Luffa_School) in [Chichester](/wiki/Chichester), West Sussex, and at [Rydal School](/wiki/Rydal_School) in [Colwyn Bay](/wiki/Colwyn_Bay) in [north Wales](/wiki/North_Wales). He studied acting at the [Central School of Speech and Drama](/wiki/Central_School_of_Speech_and_Drama).

## Career

From 1973 to 1975, Roache appeared in *Coronation Street* as [Peter Barlow](/wiki/Peter_Barlow_(Coronation_Street)), the son of his father's character [Ken Barlow](/wiki/Ken_Barlow). Roache is also a past member of the [Royal Shakespeare Company](/wiki/Royal_Shakespeare_Company).

In the 1990s, Roache began a career in films while remaining active in television and on stage. In 1994 he took a leading role in [Antonia Bird](/wiki/Antonia_Bird)'s *[Priest](/wiki/Priest_(1994_film))*. In 1997, he starred opposite [Helena Bonham Carter](/wiki/Helena_Bonham_Carter) in the film *[The Wings of the Dove](/wiki/The_Wings_of_the_Dove_(1997_film))*. In 2001, he won the [Evening Standard British Film Award for Best Actor](/wiki/Evening_Standard_British_Film_Awards), for his performance as [Samuel Taylor Coleridge](/wiki/Samuel_Taylor_Coleridge) in the [Julien Temple](/wiki/Julien_Temple) directed film *[Pandaemonium](/wiki/Pandaemonium_(film))* alongside [Samantha Morton](/wiki/Samantha_Morton). In 2005, he played [Thomas Wayne](/wiki/Thomas_Wayne), father of [Batman](/wiki/Batman), in *[Batman Begins](/wiki/Batman_Begins)*. On television, he played [Executive Assistant District Attorney](/wiki/District_attorney) [Michael Cutter](/wiki/List_of_Law_%26_Order_characters#Michael_Cutter) in two American series: *[Law & Order](/wiki/Law_%26_Order)* (2008–2010), and *[Law & Order: Special Victims Unit](/wiki/Law_%26_Order:_Special_Victims_Unit)* (2011–2012).

In June 2010, it was announced that he would return to play [Lawrence Cunningham](/wiki/Lawrence_Cunningham_(Coronation_Street)), the other son of Ken, in *Coronation Street*. The following month, Roache was cast in the [HBO](/wiki/HBO) pilot *The Miraculous Year*. In April 2011, Roache was cast as the lead in the [ITV](/wiki/ITV_(TV_network)) miniseries *[Titanic](/wiki/Titanic_(2012_TV_series))*, airing in March and April 2012 to coincide with the 100th anniversary of the sinking of [the ship](/wiki/Titanic). In February 2014, Roache debuted in season two of the TV series *[Vikings](/wiki/Vikings_(2013_TV_series))* as [Ecbert, King of Wessex](/wiki/Ecgberht,_King_of_Wessex). He starred in seasons seven and eight of the TV series *[Homeland](/wiki/Homeland_(TV_series))* as David Wellington in 2017–2019. Since then, he starred as Senator Wesley Smith on Showtime's [Peabody Award](/wiki/Peabody_Award)-winning miniseries *[Fellow Travelers](/wiki/Fellow_Travelers_(miniseries))*.

## Personal life

After appearing in 1994's *Priest* and *Seaforth*, Roache took a break from acting. He stayed in India for 18 months, where he discovered meditation. He married actress [Rosalind Bennett](/wiki/Rosalind_Bennett) in [Malvern Hills](/wiki/Malvern_Hills_(district)), [Worcestershire](/wiki/Worcestershire), in 2002.

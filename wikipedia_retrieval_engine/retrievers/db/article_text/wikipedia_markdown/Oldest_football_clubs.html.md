# Oldest football clubs

An 1881 [Australian rules football](/wiki/Australian_rules_football) match between [Melbourne](/wiki/Melbourne_Football_Club) and [Geelong](/wiki/Geelong_Football_Club). The clubs were founded in 1858 and 1859 respectively and currently compete in the [Australian Football League](/wiki/Australian_Football_League) (AFL), making them the world's oldest football clubs that are now professional.

The **oldest football clubs** trace their origins to the mid-19th century, a period when [football](/wiki/Football) evolved from being a casual pastime to an organised mainstream sport.

The identity of the oldest football clubs in the world, or even in a particular country, is often disputed or claimed by several clubs, across several codes of football. The [Foot-Ball Club](/wiki/Foot-Ball_Club) of Edinburgh is thought to be the earliest recorded football club in the world, with records going back to 1824. [Rugby](/wiki/Rugby_football) clubs also referred to themselves, or continue to refer to themselves, as simply a "football club", or as a "rugby football club". "[Club](/wiki/Sports_club)" has always meant an independent entity and, during the historical period in question, very few [high school](/wiki/High_school) or university *teams* were independent of the educational institutions concerned. Consequently, school and university football teams were seldom referred to as "clubs". That has always been the case, for example, in [American football](/wiki/American_football), which has always had ties to [college sport](/wiki/College_sport) in general. Conversely, however, the *oldest* still-existing "football club" with a well-documented, continuous history is [Dublin University Football Club](/wiki/Dublin_University_Football_Club), a rugby union club founded in 1854 at [Trinity College, Dublin](/wiki/Trinity_College,_Dublin), [Ireland](/wiki/Ireland). There exists some record of [Guy's Hospital Football Club](/wiki/Guy%27s_Hospital_Football_Club) being founded in [London](/wiki/London) in 1843, through an 1883 fixture card referring to Guy's 40th season.

The world's oldest extant *professional* football club of any code of football is the [Melbourne Football Club](/wiki/Melbourne_Football_Club), founded in 1858. Melbourne play [Australian Rules Football](/wiki/Australian_Rules_Football) (Aussie Rules) in the [Australian Football League](/wiki/Australian_Football_League) (AFL) and were [premiers](/wiki/List_of_VFL/AFL_premiers) as recently as 2021 after defeating the [Western Bulldogs](/wiki/Western_Bulldogs) in the [2021 AFL Grand Final](/wiki/2021_AFL_Grand_Final).

## Britain and Ireland

### Defunct clubs

Newspaper articles describing the game between the Body-guard Club and the Fear-nought Club, held on Christmas Day 1841

While the first clubs emerged in Britain, possibly as early as the fifteenth century, these are poorly-documented and defunct. For example, the records of the Brewers' Company of London between 1421 and 1423 mention the hiring out of their hall by the "football players" for "20 pence", under the heading "Trades and Fraternities". The listing of football players as a "fraternity" or a group of players meeting socially under this identity is the earliest allusion to what might be considered a football club. Other early sporting bodies dedicated to playing football include "[The Gymnastic Society](/wiki/The_Gymnastic_Society)" of London which met *regularly* during the second half of the eighteenth century to pursue two sports: football and [wrestling](/wiki/Wrestling). The club played its matches – for example between London-based natives of [Cumberland](/wiki/Cumberland) and [Westmorland](/wiki/Westmorland) – at the [Kennington](/wiki/Kennington) Common from well before 1789 until about 1800.

The [Foot-Ball Club](/wiki/Foot-Ball_Club) (active 1824–41) of [Edinburgh](/wiki/Edinburgh), Scotland, is the first documented club dedicated to football, and the first to describe itself as a football club. The only surviving club rules forbade tripping, but allowed pushing and holding and the picking up of the ball. Other documents describe a game involving 39 players and "such kicking of shins and such tumbling".

Other early clubs include the Great Leicestershire Cricket and Football Club present in 1840.

On Christmas Day 1841, an early documented match between two self-described "football clubs" took place. The Body-guard Club (of Rochdale) lost to the Fear-nought Club after using an ineligible player as a substitute. The complete rules used in this game are unknown, but they specified twelve players on each side, with each team providing its own umpire, and the game being started by the firing of a pistol.

A club for playing "[cricket](/wiki/Cricket), [quoits](/wiki/Quoits) and football" was established in [Newcastle upon Tyne](/wiki/Newcastle_upon_Tyne) in or before 1848. The Surrey Football Club was established in 1849 and published the first non-school football list of rules (which were probably based upon the eighteenth century Gymnastic Society cited above). [Windsor Home Park F.C.](/wiki/Windsor_Home_Park_F.C.) was in existence as early as 1854, and would go on to compete in early editions of the [FA Cup](/wiki/FA_Cup).

### Continuous clubs

Supported by the *Guinness Book of Records*, and founded by staff at [Guy's Hospital](/wiki/Guy%27s_Hospital) in London in 1843, the [Guy's, Kings and St Thomas' RFC](/wiki/Guy%27s,_Kings_and_St_Thomas%27_RFC) would be the oldest "football" club of any code. Nevertheless, the connection between the present club and the original "Guy's Hospital" formed in 1843 is still disputed, alleging that the present club is a modern amalgam of three formerly distinct hospital rugby clubs, starting with the [Guy's Hospital](/wiki/Guy%27s_Hospital) and [St Thomas' Hospital](/wiki/St_Thomas%27_Hospital) teams which were the first to merge following the union of their respective Medical Departments. The last department to merge was the [King's College Hospital](/wiki/King%27s_College_Hospital) in 1999, although [its club](/wiki/King%27s_College_Hospital_RFC) (founded in 1869) remained as a separate institution.

In [Northern Ireland](/wiki/Northern_Ireland), the oldest football club is [North of Ireland F.C.](/wiki/North_of_Ireland_F.C.), formed in 1868. It was the first rugby club formed in what is now Northern Ireland and only two other clubs - [Dublin University](/wiki/Dublin_University_Football_Club) and [Wanderers](/wiki/Wanderers_F.C._(rugby_union)) - were formed earlier anywhere else in all [Ireland](/wiki/Ireland).

It has been claimed that the present-day [Barnes Rugby Football Club](/wiki/Barnes_Rugby_Football_Club), from [Barnes](/wiki/Barnes,_London) in London, is a continuation of the nineteenth-century [Barnes Football Club](/wiki/Barnes_Football_Club), and moreover that the latter club was formed in 1839 and is thus the oldest club to have played football for its entire history. However, as of 2018, Barnes RFC's website claims only that the club was established in the 1920s, while alluding to "possibilities" that its history stretches back to 1862. In 2021, people with heritage and local enthusiasts revived the old [Barnes Football Club](/wiki/Barnes_Football_Club), established in 1862.

[Sheffield F.C.](/wiki/Sheffield_F.C.) (similar Sheffield representative side here pictured in 1875) is the oldest *independent* (that is, not related with a school or university) association football club in the world

[Sheffield F.C.](/wiki/Sheffield_F.C.) in England, is the world's oldest surviving *independent* open football club: that is, the oldest club not associated with an institution such as a school, hospital or university; and which was open to all to play. It was founded in 1857. Sheffield F.C. initially played [Sheffield rules](/wiki/Sheffield_rules), a code of its own devising, although the club's rules influenced those of the England [Football Association](/wiki/The_Football_Association) (FA) (1863) including handball, free kicks, corners and throw ins. While the international governing body of association football [FIFA](/wiki/FIFA) and the FA recognise Sheffield F.C. as the "world's oldest football club", and the club joined the FA in 1863, it continued to use the Sheffield rules. Sheffield F.C. did not officially adopt association football until 1877.

[Notts County F.C.](/wiki/Notts_County_F.C.) are officially the oldest professional football club in the world, formed in 1862, playing their first recorded match on 8 December 1864 at Nottingham's Meadows Cricket Ground against a team known as Trent Valley. Notts are thought to have mostly played under [Sheffield Rules](/wiki/Sheffield_Rules) in their early days, though certain matches are recorded as being played according to "Nottingham Rules". A team from the same city, [Nottingham Forest F.C.](/wiki/Nottingham_Forest_F.C.) (initially a group of [shinty](/wiki/Shinty) players) are recognised as the second oldest professional football club in the world, having been formed in 1865.

Setting its birth date in 1856, [Cambridge University A.F.C.](/wiki/Cambridge_University_A.F.C.) has been described by the university as the oldest club now playing [association football](/wiki/Association_football). For example, "[Salopians](/wiki/Shrewsbury_School) formed a club of their own in the late 1830s/early 1840s but that was presumably absorbed by the Cambridge University Football Club that they were so influential in creating in 1846". According to [Charles Astor Bristed](/wiki/Charles_Astor_Bristed), in the early 1840s at Cambridge, there were games played between clubs from colleges and houses. Football is documented as being played on the original club ground, [Parker's Piece](/wiki/Parker%27s_Piece), as early as 1838. The earliest existing evidence of the Cambridge University Football Club comes from "[The Laws of the University Football Club](/wiki/Cambridge_rules#Cambridge_Rules_of_1856)" dated 1856, and held at Shrewsbury School. The [Cambridge rules](/wiki/Cambridge_rules) of 1863 would provide the basis for the FA's original rules.

The only survivor among the FA's founding clubs still playing association football is [Civil Service F.C.](/wiki/Civil_Service_F.C.) Six of the 18 founding members later adopted rugby exclusively. [Cray Wanderers F.C.](/wiki/Cray_Wanderers_F.C.), originally of [St Mary Cray](/wiki/St_Mary_Cray) and currently playing in nearby [Chislehurst](/wiki/Chislehurst), founded in 1860, is the oldest club now playing association football in Greater London. The code played by Cray Wanderers in its earliest years is unknown.

[Liverpool Football Club](/wiki/Liverpool_St_Helens_F.C.) (not to be confused with [Liverpool F.C.](/wiki/Liverpool_F.C.) of the Premier League), later known as Liverpool St Helens F.C., were formed in 1857, which claims to be the oldest open rugby club in the world. The club adopted the Rugby Union rules in 1872, never playing association rules.

#### Dublin University Team

The first known record of the club appears under the heading 'Trinity College' in the [Dublin *Daily Express*](/wiki/Daily_Express_(Dublin)) of 1 December 1855 and is taken to show that it had then been in existence for at least a year:

> FOOTBALL. - A match will be played in the College Park today (Saturday) between original and new members of the club. Play to commence at two o'clock College time.

The club had thus been founded by about 1854, and it has a well-documented, continuous history since then, which gives it a strong claim to be considered the world's oldest extant football club of any code. ('College time' was a practice at Trinity of having the clocks set 15 minutes behind [local time](/wiki/Local_time) to reduce lateness at lectures.)

## Australia

### Australian rules football

See also: [List of Australian rules football clubs by date of establishment](/wiki/List_of_Australian_rules_football_clubs_by_date_of_establishment)

The oldest [Australian rules football](/wiki/Australian_rules_football) club is the [Melbourne Football Club](/wiki/Melbourne_Football_Club), founded in 1858, which still plays professionally, making it the oldest extant professional football club of any football code in the world.

The origins of the club date back to 10 July 1858 with a letter published in [Bell's Life in Victoria and Sporting Chronicle](/wiki/Bell%27s_Life) written by [Tom Wills](/wiki/Tom_Wills), captain of the [Melbourne Cricket Club](/wiki/Melbourne_Cricket_Club). He proposed a "foot-ball club" to keep Victorian cricketers fit in winter.

An informal and loosely organised Melbourne team, captained by [Wills](/wiki/Tom_Wills), played against other football enthusiasts in the winter and spring of 1858.

On August 7, 1858, in [Melbourne](/wiki/Melbourne), [Victoria](/wiki/Victoria_(Australia)), students from [Scotch College](/wiki/Scotch_College,_Melbourne) and [Melbourne Grammar](/wiki/Melbourne_Grammar) played the first recorded [Australian Rules](/wiki/Australian_Rules_Football) game against each other.

The [Melbourne Football Club](/wiki/Melbourne_Football_Club) was formally established the following year on 14 May 1859. Three days later (17 May), four members codified the first laws of [Australian Rules Football](/wiki/Australian_Rules_Football).

This makes [Australian Rules](/wiki/Australian_Rules_Football) the oldest of the football codes that are played today globally: [association football](/wiki/Association_football) was codified in 1863, [American football](/wiki/American_football) in 1869, and the two Rugby codes were written in 1871 (for [Rugby Union](/wiki/History_of_rugby_union#The_formation_of_the_first_rugby_union)) and 1895 (for [Rugby League](/wiki/Rugby_League)).

1859 also saw the formation of two other clubs, [Geelong](/wiki/Geelong_Football_Club), which still plays professionally today, and [University](/wiki/Melbourne_University_Football_Club), who played in the top level of Victorian senior football in 1885-1888 and 1908–1914.

Many more Australian Rules clubs were formed over the next few decades, including the following professional AFL clubs:

- [Carlton](/wiki/Carlton_Football_Club) in 1864.
- [North Melbourne](/wiki/North_Melbourne_Football_Club) in 1869.
- [Port Adelaide](/wiki/Port_Adelaide_Football_Club) in 1870.
- [Essendon](/wiki/Essendon_Football_Club) in 1872.
- [St Kilda](/wiki/St_Kilda_Football_Club) in 1873.
- [South Melbourne](/wiki/Sydney_Swans) in 1874 (now [Sydney](/wiki/Sydney_Swans) after relocation in 1982).
- [Western Bulldogs](/wiki/Western_Bulldogs) (originally named Footscray) in 1877.
- [Fitzroy](/wiki/Fitzroy_Football_Club) in 1883 (now the [Brisbane Lions](/wiki/Brisbane_Lions) due to the merger between Fitzroy and the [Brisbane Bears](/wiki/Brisbane_Bears) in 1996).
- [Richmond](/wiki/Richmond_Football_Club) in 1885.
- [Collingwood](/wiki/Collingwood_Football_Club) in 1892.
- [Hawthorn](/wiki/Hawthorn_Football_Club) in 1902.

Engraving of the first intercolonial football match between Victoria and South Australia, [East Melbourne Cricket Ground](/wiki/East_Melbourne_Cricket_Ground), 1879

The AFL was founded as the [Victorian Football League](/wiki/Victorian_Football_League) (VFL) in 1897, comprising eight teams based in the state of [Victoria](/wiki/Victoria_(state)) that had broken away from the Victorian Football Association.

Over the next 115 years, a series of expansions, along with the relocation of South Melbourne to Sydney, a merger and a club's withdrawal saw the league expand outside of Victoria to a national competition, leading to the 18 professional teams that exist today (19 from 2028).

The expansion saw the formation of the following clubs:

- [Brisbane Bears](/wiki/Brisbane_Bears) in 1986 (merged with Fitzroy in 1996).
- [West Coast Eagles](/wiki/West_Coast_Eagles) in 1986.
- [Adelaide](/wiki/Adelaide_Football_Club) in 1990.
- [Fremantle](/wiki/Fremantle_Football_Club) in 1994.
- [Brisbane Lions](/wiki/Brisbane_Lions) in 1996 (founded due to merger between Bisbane Bears and Fitzroy Lions).
- [Gold Coast Suns](/wiki/Gold_Coast_Suns) in 2009.
- [Greater Western Sydney](/wiki/Greater_Western_Sydney_Giants) in 2010.
- [Tasmania](/wiki/Tasmania_Football_Club) in 2023 (entering the league in 2028).

## Rest of the world

### Continental Europe

#### Albania

The first football club to be founded in Albania was the [Vllaznia Sport Club](/wiki/KF_Vllaznia_Shkod%C3%ABr) on 16 February 1919.

#### Austria

The oldest still active football club is the [First Vienna Football Club](/wiki/First_Vienna_FC) founded on 22 August 1894. Their early rivals, the [Vienna Cricket and Football-Club](/wiki/Vienna_Cricket_and_Football-Club), was founded only one day later.

#### Belgium

Football Club Spa was founded by the Hunter-Blair family of Blairquhan, [Ayrshire, Scotland](/wiki/Ayrshire), and was in operation in 1863. The fourth Baronet took his family there in order to economise. The oldest club still in existence today in Belgium is [Royal Antwerp FC](/wiki/Royal_Antwerp_FC), founded in 1880.

#### Bosnia and Herzegovina

The oldest football club in Bosnia and Herzegovina is [FK Sloboda](/wiki/FK_Sloboda_Novi_Grad) founded in [Novi Grad](/wiki/Novi_Grad,_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina) in 1910. The first club to be ever founded was [Zrinjski Mostar](/wiki/H%C5%A0K_Zrinjski_Mostar) in 1905, but it was inactive between 1945 and 1992.

#### Bulgaria

[Botev Plovdiv](/wiki/Botev_Plovdiv) is one of the oldest football club in Bulgaria founded in 1912.

#### Czech Republic

Oldest and second most successful Czech football club is [Slavia Prague](/wiki/SK_Slavia_Praha) founded in 1892. The club still exists and plays [Chance Liga](/wiki/Czech_First_League), first division of [Czech](/wiki/Czech_Republic) football.

#### Croatia

The oldest still active football club in [Croatia](/wiki/Croatia) is [NK Rijeka](/wiki/HNK_Rijeka), formed in 1904 and playing association football at the latest in 1906, currently competing in the first Croatian league. [HNK Segesta](/wiki/HNK_Segesta), that plays in the third division, was possibly founded in 1906, but its first recorded match was held in 1909.

#### Cyprus

[Anorthosis Famagusta FC](/wiki/Anorthosis_Famagusta_FC) founded in 1911 is the oldest club with a football team, however it was a reading club and the football division of the club started in 1929, the same year [Omonia Aradippou](/wiki/Omonia_Aradippou) was founded. The now defunct football division of [Enosis Neon Trust](/wiki/Enosis_Neon_Trust) founded in 1924 is therefore the oldest football club, however the club closed its football division in 1938. The oldest surviving football club is therefore [APOEL](/wiki/APOEL) founded in 1926. The same year the now defunct [Keravnos Strovolou FC](/wiki/Keravnos_Strovolou_FC) was founded. The now defunct [Pezoporikos Larnaca FC](/wiki/Pezoporikos_Larnaca_FC) was founded in 1927 first merging to form [EPA Larnaca FC](/wiki/EPA_Larnaca_FC) in 1930. [AEL Limassol](/wiki/AEL_Limassol) and [Aris Limassol](/wiki/Aris_Limassol) both founded in 1930 and one year after the Anorthosis football division are therefore old clubs as well. [Olympiakos Nicosia](/wiki/Olympiakos_Nicosia) and [Digenis Akritas Morphou FC](/wiki/Digenis_Akritas_Morphou_FC) were both subsequently founded in 1931.

#### Denmark

[Kjøbenhavns Boldklub](/wiki/Kj%C3%B8benhavns_Boldklub) is the oldest football club in Denmark and the oldest active club in continental Europe. The sports club was formed in 1876 and association football was first played two years later.

#### Estonia

[Estonian Sports Association Kalev](/wiki/Estonian_Sports_Association_Kalev) (Estonian: Eesti Spordiselts Kalev) is a sports association in Estonia, founded in 1901.

In nowadays Estonian state was the oldest formed club [Tallinna Jalgpalliselts Meteor](/wiki/FC_Meteor_Tallinn) in 1908. It's defunct since 1911, but restored after First World War and [Estonian War of Independence](/wiki/Estonian_War_of_Independence) in 1926 under the name Tallinna SK Meteor which took action until 1937.

#### Finland

[Helsingin Ponnistus](/wiki/Helsingin_Ponnistus) is one of the oldest continuously active sports clubs and the oldest football club in Finland. It was founded in 1887. It started as a gymnastic club but got its football program in 1903.

#### France

The first football club in France was established in Paris in 1863 by English expatriates, as the following excerpt from a contemporary newspaper shows: "A number of English gentlemen living in Paris have lately organised a football club.... The football contests take place in the Bois de Boulogne, by permission of the authorities and surprise the French amazingly".

[Le Havre AC](/wiki/Le_Havre_AC) was founded as an athletics and rugby club in 1872, making it the oldest surviving football club registered in France and continental Europe. They began playing association football on a regular basis in 1894. The English Taylors RFC, which was formed by British businessmen in Paris in 1877, was followed by Paris Football Club a year later. [Racing Club de France](/wiki/Racing_92) was formed in 1882 and their rivals [Stade Français](/wiki/Stade_Fran%C3%A7ais) in 1883. Technically [AS Strasbourg](/wiki/AS_Strasbourg) could be considered the first French association football team, being established in 1890; they were however a German team at the time.

#### Georgia

[FC Shevardeni-1906 Tbilisi](/wiki/FC_Shevardeni-1906_Tbilisi) was the first formal football club in Georgia, founded in 1906. Shevardeni started within the [Sokol](/wiki/Sokol_movement) society after Czech sports figures helped introduce the game to the country. They played their first recorded match in October 1911. In May 2022, the club was involved in a match-fixing scandal, causing them to get expelled from the [league](/wiki/2022_Erovnuli_Liga_2). As a result, the club has ceased operations and has not played since.

The first documented, officially established and legally organized Georgian club was Komet FC, founded in 1907.

The oldest, still active Georgian club is [FC Kolkheti-1913 Poti](/wiki/FC_Kolkheti-1913_Poti), founded in 1913. They currently play in the [Erovnuli Liga 2](/wiki/Erovnuli_Liga_2), the second-highest tier of Georgian football.

#### Germany

The oldest surviving rugby football club is [Heidelberger RK](/wiki/Heidelberger_RK), which was founded on 9 May 1872 as a rowing and rugby club. The HRK has won 14 national titles 1927, 1928, 1971, 1973, 1976, 1986, 2010–2015, 2017 and 2018. The biggest success of the club was the qualification for the Rugby European Challenge Cup 2018/19 - only to be denied participation, because their main sponsor owns [Stade Français](/wiki/Stade_Fran%C3%A7ais). The second oldest still surviving rugby club is [Hannover 1878](/wiki/DSV_78_Hannover) founded 1878 and winning 9 German championships. Both clubs still play in the 1. Bundesliga.

The first association football in Germany (and likely the first outside Great Britain) was [Dresden English](/wiki/Dresden_English_Football_Club), founded on 18 March 1874. The club had over 70 members by then, primarily Englishmen working in Dresden, with its matches being attended by hundreds of spectators. Nevertheless, Dresden English was dissolved in 1898.

The oldest still active association football club is [BFC Germania 1888](/wiki/BFC_Germania_1888) founded on 15 April 1888. They play in the 10th division as of season 2020/21 and never managed to win any national titles.

#### Greece

The oldest still active football club is [Messiniakos F.C.](/wiki/Messiniakos_F.C.) founded in 1888. This club was founded in the city of [Kalamata](/wiki/Kalamata).

#### Hungary

The oldest still active football club in Hungary is Újpest Torna Egylet, founded in 1885. There are other old teams like [Műegyetemi AFC](/wiki/M%C5%B1egyetemi_AFC) founded in autumn 1897 as a proper football club and [III. Kerületi TVE](/wiki/III._Ker%C3%BCleti_TVE), which whose football section was founded officially in 1899 but stemmed from the 1897-founded Budai Football Csapat. The first ever football club to be founded in Hungary was [Budapesti Torna Club](/wiki/Budapesti_TC) having founded its football section in February 1897, dissolved in 1945–46.

#### Italy

In Italy, [Genoa C.F.C.](/wiki/Genoa_C.F.C.) is the oldest active football club: it was founded by Charles De Grave Sells, S. Green, George Blake, W. Rilley, George Dormer Fawcus, H.M. Sandys, E. De Thierry, Johnathan Summerhill Sr., Johnathan Summerhill Jr. and [Sir Charles Alfred Payton](/wiki/Charles_Alfred_Payton) in Genoa on 7 September 1893. However, Genoa C.F.C. was not the first Italian football club, being Torino Football & Cricket Club (1887) but its history lasted only for 4 years. Founded by [Edoardo Bosio](/wiki/Edoardo_Bosio) (owner of Bosio & Caratsch, the earliest brewery in Italy), the team broke up in 1891. Older than Genoa and Torino is [Associazione Sportiva Dilettantistica Fanfulla](/wiki/A.S.D._Cavenago_Fanfulla), a sports club founded in [Lodi](/wiki/Lodi,_Lombardy) in 1873, but its football section was established thirty-five years later in 1908.

#### Latvia

Workers at the (British-owned) Salamandra metal factory in [Riga](/wiki/Riga) founded the first football team in 1907: the British Football Club (later named Britannia).

#### Lithuania

The first inter-city football games are dating back to 1911. Shortly after gaining the independence in 1918, a [Kybartai](/wiki/Kybartai) local of a German descent has purchased a [ball](/wiki/Football_(ball)), leveled out a football pitch, and gathered a team together. The team formed into a football club [FK Sveikata](/wiki/FK_Sveikata_Kybartai) in 1919. For the next three years the team was traveling to cities in [East Prussia](/wiki/East_Prussia) and [Klaipėda Region](/wiki/Klaip%C4%97da_Region) to play games, as there were no other teams in [Lithuania](/wiki/Lithuania) just yet. [FK Sveikata](/wiki/FK_Sveikata_Kybartai) is still a functioning club in Lithuania today.

#### Malta

The oldest football club in Malta is [St.George's FC](/wiki/St._George%27s_F.C.). Founded in 1890 in the city of Cospicua also known as Bormla.

#### Montenegro

The oldest football club in Montenegro is [FK Lovćen](/wiki/FK_Lov%C4%87en) founded in the town of Cetinje in 1913.

#### Netherlands

The oldest football (and oldest cricket) club in the Netherlands is Koninklijke UD from [Deventer](/wiki/Deventer), founded in 1875 as a cricket club. In 1894, the club founded an association football section.

[Koninklijke HFC](/wiki/Koninklijke_HFC) was the first Dutch rugby club, established on 15 September 1879 by the 14-year-old Pim Mulier, who first encountered the sport in 1870. However, HFC switched to association football in 1883.

[Utrechtse Sportvereniging Hercules](/wiki/USV_Hercules) (Utrecht Sports Association Hercules), also known as USV Hercules or Hercules Utrecht, is an amateur football club in [Utrecht](/wiki/Utrecht), established on 22 April 1882.

#### North Macedonia

The oldest football club in North Macedonia is [FK Ljuboten](/wiki/FK_Ljuboten), founded in 1919.

#### Norway

The first football team in Norway was probably started by a buekorps in Bergen, *Nygaards Bataljon*, in 1883. In 1885 the first Norwegian club however, *Idrætsforeningen Odd*, was founded in Skien. The footballing interest was very low, and was put on ice after a few months. However, the club [Odd Grenland](/wiki/Odd_Grenland) started up with football again in 1894, and are now Norway's oldest football club.

#### Poland

The oldest football club in Poland was either

- [Lechia Lwów](/wiki/Lechia_Lw%C3%B3w), established in 1903, or
- [Czarni Lwów](/wiki/Czarni_Lw%C3%B3w). This club is also believed to have been founded in 1903, but historical records are unclear as to the exact date of foundation.

When [Lviv](/wiki/Lviv) became a part of Ukraine (then part of the [USSR](/wiki/USSR)) the clubs ceased to exist.

The oldest currently existing football club within Poland's current borders is widely considered to be [Cracovia](/wiki/Cracovia_(football)), but this is disputed by their [Kraków](/wiki/Krak%C3%B3w) [arch-rivals](/wiki/Holy_War_(Krak%C3%B3w)) [Wisła Kraków](/wiki/Wis%C5%82a_Krak%C3%B3w); both clubs were founded in 1906, however there are multiple proofs that Wisła is younger. [Resovia](/wiki/Resovia_(football)) also lay claim to this distinction; the disputes centre around registration, continuity of a given team, and sources.

#### Portugal

In [Portugal](/wiki/Portugal), [Académica de Coimbra (football)](/wiki/Acad%C3%A9mica_de_Coimbra_(football)) is the oldest football club, founded in 1887.

#### Romania

The oldest football club in Romania is **Fotbal Club Baia Mare**, founded in 1896.

#### Russia

In the Russian Empire, the first football teams were established by British expatriates, who founded clubs such as the British Athletic Club (1878) and the Vasileostrovsky Footballers' Society (1891).

The first football team composed of Russian nationals, [KLS SPb](/wiki/FC_Sport_Saint_Petersburg) (1897), emerged from the Saint-Petersburg Circle of Sports Enthusiasts.

The longest continuous football history has [CSKA Moscow](/wiki/PFC_CSKA_Moscow), tracing its origins back to the founding of OLLS in 1911.

#### Serbia

The oldest club in Serbia is Subotičko Sportsko Društvo (*Szabadkai Sport Egylet*), founded in 1898.

#### Slovakia

The oldest football club in Slovakia is [1. FC Tatran Prešov](/wiki/1._FC_Tatran_Pre%C5%A1ov), founded in 1898, that after many name changes plays in the [Slovak first league](/wiki/1._Liga_(Slovakia)).

#### Slovenia

The first football club was founded in 1900 by the German minority in [Ljubljana](/wiki/Ljubljana), the Laibacher Sportverein and got discontinued in 1909, followed by the Hungarian minority [Lendevai FE](/wiki/NK_Nafta) in 1903, and the German minority in [Celje](/wiki/Celje) ([Athletik SK](/wiki/Athletik_SK) in 1906). In 1911, the first Slovenian football club of the Slovenian minority opened in Ljubljana, [Ilirija](/wiki/ND_Ilirija), followed by [Slovan](/wiki/ND_Slovan) two years later. Ilirija is nowadays considered by the Slovenian FA as the oldest running football club in the country, because the Lendava-based club went bankrupt in 2012, although it got recreated right away.

#### Spain

Founded on 23 December 1889, [Recreativo de Huelva](/wiki/Recreativo_de_Huelva) is considered the oldest football club in Spain. Two Scots, Alexander Mackay and Robert Russell Ross, overseas workers at the [Rio Tinto](/wiki/Rio_Tinto_(river)) mines, founded Huelva Recreation Club to provide their employees with physical recreation.

#### Sweden

The oldest active football club in Sweden is [Örgryte IS](/wiki/%C3%96rgryte_IS), founded on 4 December 1887.

#### Switzerland

[FC St. Gallen 1879](/wiki/FC_St._Gallen) is the oldest active football club in Switzerland and the second oldest in continental Europe, founded on 19 April 1879 as FC St. Gallen.

#### Turkey

The first club founded within the present-day borders of Turkey was [Panionios](/wiki/Panionios), founded in the city of [Izmir](/wiki/%C4%B0zmir) in 1890. Being the club of the Greek community, it relocated to modern day Greece in 1922 after the [population exchange between Greece and Turkey](/wiki/Population_exchange_between_Greece_and_Turkey). The first Turkish sports club ever is [Besiktas J.K.](/wiki/Be%C5%9Fikta%C5%9F_J.K.) founded in 1903. Besiktas established their football branch in 1911. The first registered football club in the country was [Galatasaray](/wiki/Galatasaray), founded in 1905.

### North America

Although football variants have been played in [North America](/wiki/North_America) since the 1820s, the claim of oldest continuous football club in North America is still a matter of debate.

[Oneida Football Club](/wiki/Oneida_Football_Club) of [Boston, Massachusetts](/wiki/Boston,_Massachusetts), established in 1862, was the first organised team to play any kind of [football](/wiki/Football) in the [United States](/wiki/United_States). The game played by the club, known as the "[Boston game](/wiki/Boston_game)", was an informal local variant that predated the codification of rules for either association or [American football](/wiki/American_football). The team, which consisted of graduates of Boston's elite preparatory schools, played on [Boston Common](/wiki/Boston_Common) from 1862 to 1865, during which time they reportedly never lost a game or even gave up a single point.

In terms of [gridiron football](/wiki/Gridiron_football), the [Hamilton Tiger-Cats](/wiki/Hamilton_Tiger-Cats) of the [Canadian Football League](/wiki/Canadian_Football_League) can trace their roots back to the [Hamilton Football Club](/wiki/Hamilton_Tigers_(football)) (nicknamed the *Tigers*) which formed in 1869, then later merged with the [Hamilton Wildcats](/wiki/Hamilton_Wildcats_(Canadian_football)) in 1950 to form the current franchise. Their rivals to the east, the [Toronto Argonauts](/wiki/Toronto_Argonauts), were founded four years later, in 1873, and have a mostly unchanged franchise history. Both clubs began as [rugby football](/wiki/Rugby_football) clubs and only later adapted to the gridiron-style of play which would become known as [Canadian football](/wiki/Canadian_football). The oldest continuous rugby club in North America which still plays rugby is the [McGill University](/wiki/McGill_University) Rugby Football Club, which was established in 1863, although their first recorded game was not until 1865. The oldest independent (non-university) rugby club is the [Westmount Rugby Club](/wiki/Westmount_Rugby_Club) of [Montreal](/wiki/Montreal), which formed in 1876.

In [1869](/wiki/1869_college_football_season), [Rutgers University](/wiki/Rutgers_Scarlet_Knights_football) and [Princeton University](/wiki/Princeton_Tigers_football) competed in the [first US intercollegiate football game](/wiki/1869_New_Jersey_vs._Rutgers_football_game). According to [U.S. Soccer](/wiki/U.S._Soccer), the rules of this game resembled rugby and association football more closely than gridiron football. However, university-affiliated teams competing in [intercollegiate championships](/wiki/College_football) are not typically classified as "clubs".

In the United States, gridiron-based variants of the game did not distinguish themselves from existing codes until 1871, when [Harvard University](/wiki/Harvard_Crimson_football) began playing the Boston Game invented by Oneida. Its rules allowed a player to pick up the ball and run with it if he were chased and it quickly spread, with innovations added by [Yale University](/wiki/Yale_University) student [Walter Camp](/wiki/Walter_Camp). The oldest existing non-university semiprofessional football club is the [Watertown Red & Black](/wiki/Watertown_Red_%26_Black), which was founded in 1896. The [Arizona Cardinals](/wiki/Arizona_Cardinals), founded as the Morgan Athletic Club in [Chicago](/wiki/Chicago) in 1898, are the oldest team in the [National Football League](/wiki/National_Football_League).

One of the first teams to have played football under the [association rules](/wiki/Laws_of_the_Game_(association_football)) in the US was [Fall River Rovers](/wiki/Fall_River_Rovers), founded in 1884. The club existed intermittently until 1921. The [Milwaukee Wave](/wiki/Milwaukee_Wave) of the [American Indoor Soccer Association](/wiki/American_Indoor_Soccer_Association), a professional [indoor soccer](/wiki/Indoor_soccer) team based in [Milwaukee, Wisconsin](/wiki/Milwaukee,_Wisconsin), was founded in 1984, and is the oldest continuously operating professional [soccer](/wiki/Football_(soccer)) team of any kind in the United States. Founded in 1993, both [Charleston Battery](/wiki/Charleston_Battery) and [Richmond Kickers](/wiki/Richmond_Kickers) are considered the oldest continuously operating professional soccer clubs in the United States to date. The ten charter members of [Major League Soccer](/wiki/Major_League_Soccer) were all new clubs created shortly before the league began play in [1996](/wiki/1996_Major_League_Soccer_season). Nine of those ten clubs are still in existence. In 2011, the league admitted the [Vancouver Whitecaps](/wiki/History_of_Vancouver_Whitecaps_FC), who had existed continuously since 1986, and who were a [phoenix club](/wiki/Phoenix_club_(sports)) of the [North American Soccer League side of the same name](/wiki/Vancouver_Whitecaps_(1974%E2%80%931984)) who were founded in 1974. Aside from the arguable exception of the Whitecaps, all of the other NASL clubs folded when the league folded in 1984. There is one American soccer club from an earlier first-division league still in existence: the [Kearny Scots](/wiki/Kearny_Scots) of the semi-pro [Eastern Premier Soccer League](/wiki/Eastern_Premier_Soccer_League) have been around since the 1930s, when they were a longtime member of the [American Soccer League](/wiki/American_Soccer_League_(1933%E2%80%931983)). They won the ASL league championship five years in a row from 1937 through 1941.

#### Mexico

In Mexico, the oldest Football club is [Club de Fútbol Pachuca](/wiki/C.F._Pachuca), based in [Pachuca](/wiki/Pachuca), [Hidalgo](/wiki/Hidalgo_(state)), that competes in [Liga MX](/wiki/Liga_MX). Founded by [Cornish miners](/wiki/Cornish_diaspora) from [Camborne](/wiki/Camborne) and [Redruth](/wiki/Redruth) in 1901, it is one of the oldest football clubs in the Americas, and was one of the founding members of the [Mexican Primera División](/wiki/Liga_MX).

### South America

#### Argentina

Argentine [Rosario A.C.](/wiki/Club_Atl%C3%A9tico_del_Rosario) rugby union squad in 1884. This is supposed to be the oldest photo of a rugby team in the country

[Buenos Aires Cricket & Rugby Club](/wiki/Buenos_Aires_Cricket_%26_Rugby_Club) claims to be the oldest club still in existence in Argentina. According to the club's website, the club was founded before 8 December 1864 as a [cricket](/wiki/Cricket) institution. The date of foundation has been recognised by the Buenos Aires Rugby Union. It is however believed that the club was founded in 1831, with existing documentary evidence about a [cricket](/wiki/Cricket) match played by Buenos Aires that same year. Nevertheless, the practise of any "football" code did not start until 1951 when the BACC merged with the [Buenos Aires F.C.](/wiki/Buenos_Aires_Football_Club_(1886)) and rugby union was added.

[Club Atlético del Rosario](/wiki/Club_Atl%C3%A9tico_del_Rosario) was officially established in 1867 as a cricket institution. The club soon added association football, being the first club from [Rosario](/wiki/Rosario,_Santa_Fe) playing in the [Primera División](/wiki/Argentine_Primera_Divisi%C3%B3n), the top division of Argentina. In rugby union, Rosario AC played the first inter-clubs match in the country on 28 June 1886, when the team faced [Buenos Aires Football Club](/wiki/Buenos_Aires_Football_Club_(1886)).

Having been established in 1875, [Club Mercedes](/wiki/Club_Mercedes) is considered the oldest association football club still in existence in Argentina. This places Mercedes above [Gimnasia y Esgrima La Plata](/wiki/Club_de_Gimnasia_y_Esgrima_La_Plata) and [Quilmes](/wiki/Quilmes_Atl%C3%A9tico_Club), both founded in 1887.

#### Bolivia

[Oruro Royal](/wiki/Oruro_Royal) was founded on 26 May 1896 by the English workers hired by the Bolivian Government to build the national railways, becoming the first Bolivian football squad.

#### Brazil

[São Paulo Athletic Club](/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Paulo_Athletic_Club), founded on 13 May 1888 by English immigrants as a cricket club, could be considered the oldest Brazilian club to have played any code of "football", starting to play association football in 1898. After football retired from competitions in 1912, rugby union became the main sport of SPAC.

The oldest club to have been in continuous activity in association football is [S.C. Rio Grande](/wiki/Sport_Club_Rio_Grande), founded in July 1900.
[Clube Náutico Capibaribe](/wiki/Clube_N%C3%A1utico_Capibaribe), founded in April 1901, is the oldest in [northeast region](/wiki/Northeast_Brazil).

São Paulo Railway Company and São Paulo Gaz Company, both established in 1895 (and then defunct) played the first football match in Brazil that same year.

#### Chile

[Santiago Wanderers](/wiki/Santiago_Wanderers) was founded on 15 August 1892 by Irish community and was the first football club in Chile.

#### Colombia

The defunct Barranquilla Football Club founded by British railway workers in 1908 was the first football club in Colombia.

Cali Football Club was formed in 1908 by students under the leadership of Nazario Lalinde, Juan Pablo Lalinde and Fidel Lalinde, who came back from Europe bringing football to the city of Cali, but in 1912 the students under the leadership of the three Lalinde brothers organized the team and renamed it as [Deportivo Cali](/wiki/Deportivo_Cali)

#### Uruguay

In [Uruguay](/wiki/Uruguay), the [Montevideo Cricket Club](/wiki/Montevideo_Cricket_Club), established in 1861, has however been ranked as the oldest rugby union club outside Europe by the [World Rugby Museum](/wiki/World_Rugby_Museum) of [Twickenham](/wiki/Twickenham_Stadium), although the first certain rugby match played by MVCC was in 1875.

### Oceania

#### New Zealand

Christchurch Football Club, established in 1863, is the oldest football club of any code in [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand). The club initially played using its own rules, before converting to [rugby football](/wiki/Rugby_football). Wellington Football Club, based in [Hataitai](/wiki/Hataitai), was established in 1870 and is the oldest continuous [rugby football](/wiki/Rugby_football) club in New Zealand. The honour of being the country's first organised association football club is likely to belong to Auckland's [North Shore United](/wiki/North_Shore_United), which was founded as North Shore in 1886. Two Dunedin-based clubs, [Northern](/wiki/Northern_AFC) and [Wakari](/wiki/Roslyn-Wakari), were officially founded in 1888, although it is possible that Northern had been playing as a team prior to this time.

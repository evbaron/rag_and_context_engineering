# List of current Major League Baseball stadiums

[Globe Life Field](/wiki/Globe_Life_Field), home of the [Texas Rangers](/wiki/Texas_Rangers_(baseball)), is the newest stadium in [Major League Baseball](/wiki/Major_League_Baseball). It opened in 2020.

There are 30 stadiums in use by [Major League Baseball](/wiki/Major_League_Baseball) (MLB) teams. The oldest [ballpark](/wiki/Ballpark) is [Fenway Park](/wiki/Fenway_Park) in [Boston](/wiki/Boston), home of the [Boston Red Sox](/wiki/Boston_Red_Sox), which opened in 1912. The newest stadium is [Globe Life Field](/wiki/Globe_Life_Field) in [Arlington, Texas](/wiki/Arlington,_Texas), home of the [Texas Rangers](/wiki/Texas_Rangers_(baseball)), which opened in 2020. Two ballparks were built in the 1910s, two in the 1960s, one in the 1970s, one in the 1980s, seven in the 1990s, thirteen in the 2000s, three in the 2010s, and one in the 2020s. Twenty-five ballparks have natural grass surfaces, while five have artificial turf. Eight ballparks do not have corporate [naming rights](/wiki/Naming_rights) deals: [Angel Stadium](/wiki/Angel_Stadium), [Dodger Stadium](/wiki/Dodger_Stadium), [Fenway Park](/wiki/Fenway_Park), [Kauffman Stadium](/wiki/Kauffman_Stadium), [Nationals Park](/wiki/Nationals_Park), [Oriole Park at Camden Yards](/wiki/Oriole_Park_at_Camden_Yards), [Wrigley Field](/wiki/Wrigley_Field), and [Yankee Stadium](/wiki/Yankee_Stadium). [Sutter Health Park](/wiki/Sutter_Health_Park) is being used temporarily by the [Athletics](/wiki/Oakland_Athletics) until [a new stadium in Las Vegas](/wiki/New_Las_Vegas_Stadium) is completed in 2028. Though the Dodgers sold naming rights of the field to [Uniqlo](/wiki/Uniqlo), the team retains stadium branding under the Dodger Stadium name.

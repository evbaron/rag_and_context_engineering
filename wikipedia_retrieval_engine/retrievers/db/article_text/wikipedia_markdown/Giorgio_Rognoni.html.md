**Giorgio Rognoni** (26 October 1946 in Modena, Italy – 20 March 1986 in [Pistoia](/wiki/Pistoia)) was an Italian professional [footballer](/wiki/Association_football) who played as a [midfielder](/wiki/Midfielder).

## Career

Born in [Modena](/wiki/Modena), Rognoni began playing football with local side Modena. In 1967, he signed with Milan, where he would make his [Serie A](/wiki/Serie_A) debut against Mantova on 11 February 1968. He played for 9 seasons (193 games, 13 goals) in the [Serie A](/wiki/Serie_A) for [A.C. Milan](/wiki/A.C._Milan), [U.S. Foggia](/wiki/U.S._Foggia), [A.C. Cesena](/wiki/A.C._Cesena) and [A.C. Pistoiese](/wiki/A.C._Pistoiese).

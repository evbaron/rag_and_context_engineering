# William III, Lord of Montpellier

**William III** (or in [Occitan](/wiki/Occitan_language): **Guilhem III**) was the [Lord of Montpellier](/wiki/Lord_of_Montpellier) from 1025 until his death in 1058. He was the son of [William II](/wiki/William_II_of_Montpellier) and husband of Beliardis. His son and successor was [William IV](/wiki/William_IV_of_Montpellier). He is the last of the "shadowy" lords of Montpellier, none of whose charters are conserved in the family [cartulary](/wiki/Cartulary), the *[Liber instrumentorum memorialium](/wiki/Liber_instrumentorum_memorialium)*.

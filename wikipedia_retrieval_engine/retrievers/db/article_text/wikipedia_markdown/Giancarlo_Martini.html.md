**Giancarlo Martini** (16 August 1947 – 26 March 2013) was a [racing driver](/wiki/Racing_driver) from [Italy](/wiki/Italy). He participated in two non-championship [Formula One](/wiki/Formula_One) Grands Prix driving a [Ferrari 312T](/wiki/Ferrari_312T) for [Giancarlo Minardi](/wiki/Giancarlo_Minardi). He was the uncle of the racing drivers [Pierluigi Martini](/wiki/Pierluigi_Martini) and [Oliver Martini](/wiki/Oliver_Martini).

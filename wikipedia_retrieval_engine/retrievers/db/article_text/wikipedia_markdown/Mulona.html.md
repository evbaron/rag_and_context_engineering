# Mulona

***Mulona*** is a genus of [moths](/wiki/Moth) in the subfamily [Arctiinae](/wiki/Arctiinae_(moth)). The genus was erected by [Francis Walker](/wiki/Francis_Walker_(entomologist)) in 1866.

## Species

- *[Mulona barnesi](/wiki/Mulona_barnesi)* Field, 1952
- *[Mulona grisea](/wiki/Mulona_grisea)* Hampson, 1900
- *[Mulona lapidaria](/wiki/Mulona_lapidaria)* Walker, 1866
- *[Mulona manni](/wiki/Mulona_manni)* Field, 1952
- *[Mulona phelina](/wiki/Mulona_phelina)* (Druce, 1885)
- *[Mulona piperita](/wiki/Mulona_piperita)* Reich, 1933
- *[Mulona schausi](/wiki/Mulona_schausi)* Field, 1952

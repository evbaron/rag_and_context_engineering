**Joel Oshiro Dyck** (born July 28, 1971) is a retired [Japanese](/wiki/Japanese_people)-[Canadian](/wiki/Canadians) professional [ice hockey](/wiki/Ice_hockey) [defenceman](/wiki/Defenceman). He played 15 seasons in the [Japan Ice Hockey League](/wiki/Japan_Ice_Hockey_League) and the [Asia League Ice Hockey](/wiki/Asia_League_Ice_Hockey) with the [Nippon Paper Cranes](/wiki/Nippon_Paper_Cranes), and competed at the [2002](/wiki/2002_IIHF_World_Championship), [2003](/wiki/2003_IIHF_World_Championship), and [2004 IIHF World Championships](/wiki/2004_IIHF_World_Championship) as a member of the [Japan men's national ice hockey team](/wiki/Japan_men%27s_national_ice_hockey_team).

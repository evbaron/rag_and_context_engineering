Main article: [2002 Wimbledon Championships](/wiki/2002_Wimbledon_Championships)

[Lleyton Hewitt](/wiki/Lleyton_Hewitt) defeated [David Nalbandian](/wiki/David_Nalbandian) in the final, 6–1, 6–3, 6–2 to win the gentlemen's singles tennis title at the [2002 Wimbledon Championships](/wiki/2002_Wimbledon_Championships). It was his second and last [major](/wiki/Grand_Slam_(tennis)#Tournaments) title, after the [2001 US Open](/wiki/2001_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_singles). Hewitt was the first Australian to win the title since [Pat Cash](/wiki/Pat_Cash) in [1987](/wiki/1987_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_singles).

[Goran Ivanišević](/wiki/Goran_Ivani%C5%A1evi%C4%87) was the reigning champion, but withdrew due to a shoulder surgery. He would not return to [Wimbledon](/wiki/The_Championships,_Wimbledon) until [2004](/wiki/2004_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_singles).

The tournament was notable for the poor results of the top players. With the exception of Hewitt and world No. 4 [Tim Henman](/wiki/Tim_Henman), the rest of the top 17 seeds were eliminated before the fourth round. In one of the biggest upsets in Wimbledon history, seven-time champion [Pete Sampras](/wiki/Pete_Sampras) was defeated by № 145-ranked [George Bastl](/wiki/George_Bastl) in the second round, in what would be Sampras' last appearance at Wimbledon. The [1992](/wiki/1992_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_singles) champion [Andre Agassi](/wiki/Andre_Agassi) suffered a second-round defeat to unseeded [Paradorn Srichaphan](/wiki/Paradorn_Srichaphan). Future eight-time champion [Roger Federer](/wiki/Roger_Federer) was defeated in the first round by qualifier [Mario Ančić](/wiki/Mario_An%C4%8Di%C4%87), his last defeat at Wimbledon until the [2008 final](/wiki/2008_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_singles_final), spanning a [record five straight Wimbledon titles](/wiki/Open_Era_tennis_records_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_singles) between 2003 and 2007.

This marked the last Wimbledon appearance of [1996](/wiki/1996_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_singles) champion [Richard Krajicek](/wiki/Richard_Krajicek), reaching the quarterfinals before losing to [Xavier Malisse](/wiki/Xavier_Malisse).

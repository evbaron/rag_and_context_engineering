**Kathleen Senior High School** is the second oldest of four [high schools](/wiki/Secondary_education_in_the_United_States) in [Lakeland](/wiki/Lakeland,_Florida), [Florida](/wiki/Florida), [United States](/wiki/United_States). It was originally housed north of its present location, at the site of the current Kathleen Middle School.

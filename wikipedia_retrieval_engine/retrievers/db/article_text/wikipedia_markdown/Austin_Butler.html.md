# Austin Butler

**Austin Robert Butler** (born August 17, 1991) is an American actor. Known for his performances on stage and screen, he has received a [British Academy Film Award](/wiki/British_Academy_Film_Award) and [Golden Globe Award](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award) as well as nominations for an [Academy Award](/wiki/Academy_Award), two [Critics' Choice Awards](/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Awards), and two [Screen Actors Guild Awards](/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Awards). He was named by *[Time](/wiki/Time_(magazine))* magazine as one of the [100 most influential people in the world](/wiki/Time_100) in 2023.

Butler began his career on television, appearing in sitcoms on [Disney Channel](/wiki/Disney_Channel) and [Nickelodeon](/wiki/Nickelodeon), most notably on *[Zoey 101](/wiki/Zoey_101)* (2007–2008), and later in teen dramas, including recurring parts on [The CW](/wiki/The_CW)'s *[Life Unexpected](/wiki/Life_Unexpected)* (2010–2011) and *[Switched at Birth](/wiki/Switched_at_Birth_(TV_series))* (2011–2012). He went on to star in *[The Carrie Diaries](/wiki/The_Carrie_Diaries_(TV_series))* (2013–2014) and *[The Shannara Chronicles](/wiki/The_Shannara_Chronicles)* (2016–2017).

Butler's film breakthrough came as [Tex Watson](/wiki/Tex_Watson) in [Quentin Tarantino](/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino)'s period dramedy *[Once Upon a Time in Hollywood](/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Hollywood)* (2019). He gained wider recognition for portraying [Elvis Presley](/wiki/Elvis_Presley) in [Baz Luhrmann](/wiki/Baz_Luhrmann)'s biopic *[Elvis](/wiki/Elvis_(2022_film))* (2022), which earned him a [BAFTA Award](/wiki/BAFTA_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Leading_Role) and [Golden Globe Award](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actor_%E2%80%93_Motion_Picture_Drama), as well as a nomination for the [Academy Award for Best Actor](/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actor). He has since starred in the [Apple TV+](/wiki/Apple_TV%2B) miniseries *[Masters of the Air](/wiki/Masters_of_the_Air)* (2024), [Denis Villeneuve](/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve)'s sci-fi epic *[Dune: Part Two](/wiki/Dune:_Part_Two)* (2024), and [Darren Aronofsky](/wiki/Darren_Aronofsky)'s crime thriller *[Caught Stealing](/wiki/Caught_Stealing_(film))* (2025).

On stage, Butler made his [Broadway](/wiki/Broadway_theatre) debut in the revival of [Eugene O'Neill](/wiki/Eugene_O%27Neill)'s *[The Iceman Cometh](/wiki/The_Iceman_Cometh)* (2018).

## Early life and education

Butler was born on August 17, 1991, in [Anaheim, California](/wiki/Anaheim,_California), the son of Lori Anne (née Howell), an aesthetician, and David Butler who is of Irish descent. The two divorced when he was seven. He has an older sister, Ashley (b. 1986), who worked as a background actress alongside him on *[Ned's Declassified School Survival Guide](/wiki/Ned%27s_Declassified_School_Survival_Guide)*. His maternal great-great-grandparents, Matti Matinpoika Sillanpää and Liisa Jaakontytär Järvelä, were [Finnish](/wiki/Finnish_Americans) immigrants from [Ostrobothnia](/wiki/Ostrobothnia_(historical_province)), Finland, who settled in Wisconsin.

When Butler was thirteen, he was approached by a representative from a background acting management company at the [Orange County Fair](/wiki/Orange_County_Fair_(California)) who helped him get started in the entertainment industry. He found that he enjoyed it and soon began taking acting classes. Butler attended public school until the seventh grade, when he left to be homeschooled to better accommodate his work schedule. He continued his [homeschooling](/wiki/Homeschooling) until the tenth grade and later passed the [CHSPE](/wiki/California_High_School_Proficiency_Exam), the state's high school equivalency diploma exam.

## Career

### 2005–2018: Career beginnings

In 2005, after working as an extra on several television series including *[Unfabulous](/wiki/Unfabulous)* and *[Drake & Josh](/wiki/Drake_%26_Josh)*, Butler landed his first regular job as a background actor playing the role of Zippy Brewster for two seasons on [Nickelodeon](/wiki/Nickelodeon)'s *[Ned's Declassified School Survival Guide](/wiki/Ned%27s_Declassified_School_Survival_Guide)*. His friend on the show, [Lindsey Shaw](/wiki/Lindsey_Shaw), introduced him to her manager, Pat Cutler, who signed him and launched his career. From that point on, Butler began taking acting seriously as a career. In May 2007, Butler landed a guest starring role on the [Disney Channel](/wiki/Disney_Channel) series *[Hannah Montana](/wiki/Hannah_Montana)* playing the role of Derek Hanson opposite actress [Miley Cyrus](/wiki/Miley_Cyrus), and in September of that same year, he portrayed Jake Krandle in the episode "iLike Jake" on the [Nickelodeon](/wiki/Nickelodeon) series *[iCarly](/wiki/ICarly)*.

Butler in 2008

In February 2008, Butler landed a main role on another [Nickelodeon](/wiki/Nickelodeon) series, *[Zoey 101](/wiki/Zoey_101)*, playing James Garrett, the love interest of [Jamie Lynn Spears](/wiki/Jamie_Lynn_Spears)'s title character Zoey in the fourth season; previously, he guest starred on the same show playing the role of Danifer on the third-season episode "Quarantine". In March of that year, he appeared in an episode on the short-lived [Cartoon Network](/wiki/Cartoon_Network) sitcom, *[Out of Jimmy's Head](/wiki/Out_of_Jimmy%27s_Head)*, playing the role of Lance in the episode titled "Princess".

In July 2009, Butler starred in 20th Century Fox's family adventure film *[Aliens in the Attic](/wiki/Aliens_in_the_Attic)* portraying Jake Pearson, alongside [Ashley Tisdale](/wiki/Ashley_Tisdale), [Carter Jenkins](/wiki/Carter_Jenkins), [Robert Hoffman](/wiki/Robert_Hoffman_(actor)), [Kevin Nealon](/wiki/Kevin_Nealon) and [Doris Roberts](/wiki/Doris_Roberts). In the film, his character, along with his family, battles to save their vacation home and the world from an alien invasion. That same summer, Butler appeared in the starring role of Jordan Gallagher on the popular, but short-lived [ABC Family](/wiki/Freeform_(TV_channel)) series *[Ruby & the Rockits](/wiki/Ruby_%26_the_Rockits)*, alongside [David Cassidy](/wiki/David_Cassidy), [Patrick Cassidy](/wiki/Patrick_Cassidy_(actor)) and his good friend, [Alexa Vega](/wiki/Alexa_PenaVega).

In February 2010, Butler landed a recurring role as Jones on the [CW](/wiki/The_CW) series *[Life Unexpected](/wiki/Life_Unexpected)*, Later that year, he guest-starred in episodes of the Disney Channel sitcoms *[Wizards of Waverly Place](/wiki/Wizards_of_Waverly_Place)* and *[Jonas](/wiki/Jonas_(TV_series))* as well as on *[CSI: Miami](/wiki/CSI:_Miami)* and *[The Defenders](/wiki/The_Defenders_(2010_TV_series))*. Also in 2010, Butler's *Aliens in the Attic* co-star [Ashley Tisdale](/wiki/Ashley_Tisdale) invited him to audition for a lead role in the film *[Sharpay's Fabulous Adventure](/wiki/Sharpay%27s_Fabulous_Adventure)*, a spin-off of [Disney](/wiki/The_Walt_Disney_Company)'s *[High School Musical](/wiki/High_School_Musical)* franchise that followed the adventures of [Sharpay Evans](/wiki/Sharpay_Evans) after high school as she tries to get her big break on [Broadway](/wiki/Broadway_theatre). In the film, Butler plays Peyton Leverette, the love interest of Sharpay. Regarding his casting, Butler told *Week In Rewind*, "I had worked with Ashley once, and then I heard about the movie, because she called me and said, 'Austin, I want you to come and read for the director for this—I think you'd be perfect.' So, I went and met with the director, and it went really well, and I ended up doing the movie." The film was released direct to DVD on April 19, 2011.

In 2011, he booked the recurring role of Wilke on the [ABC Family](/wiki/Freeform_(TV_channel)) series *[Switched at Birth](/wiki/Switched_at_Birth_(TV_series))*, debuting on June 27, 2011. In the same year, he signed on to play the lead role of Zack Garvey in the [Lifetime](/wiki/LMN_(TV_channel)) television film *The Bling Ring*, made by [Dick Clark Productions](/wiki/Dick_Clark_Productions) and directed by [Michael Lembeck](/wiki/Michael_Lembeck), based on the [burglary group of the same name](/wiki/Bling_Ring) who targeted the homes of Hollywood's celebrities. The film was released on September 26, 2011. In January 2012, Butler guest-starred on the [NBC](/wiki/NBC) comedy *[Are You There, Chelsea?](/wiki/Are_You_There,_Chelsea%3F)*

In his early twenties, Butler continued to act in teen-oriented television series, but gained greater recognition for his starring roles in *[The Carrie Diaries](/wiki/The_Carrie_Diaries_(TV_series))* and later *[The Shannara Chronicles](/wiki/The_Shannara_Chronicles)*; he also branched out into new stage and film projects. In March 2012, Butler was cast in The CW's *[Sex and the City](/wiki/Sex_and_the_City)* prequel series *[The Carrie Diaries](/wiki/The_Carrie_Diaries_(TV_series))* to play Sebastian Kydd, a brooding heartthrob attending the same high school as [Carrie Bradshaw](/wiki/Carrie_Bradshaw), played by [AnnaSophia Robb](/wiki/AnnaSophia_Robb). *The Carrie Diaries* is based on [Candace Bushnell](/wiki/Candace_Bushnell)'s [novel by the same name](/wiki/The_Carrie_Diaries) and follows Bradshaw's life in the 1980s as a teenager in New York City. The show was cancelled after two seasons.

In April 2014, it was announced that Butler had joined the cast of the play *Death of the Author* at the [Geffen Playhouse](/wiki/Geffen_Playhouse) in Los Angeles. He played the role of Bradley, a pre-law student with a double major in political science and math who is set to graduate from a wealthy university. The play was directed by [Bart DeLorenzo](/wiki/Bart_DeLorenzo) and written by [Steven Drukman](/wiki/Steven_Drukman). Following previews on May 20, 2014, the play ran from May 28 to June 29.

Butler then joined the cast of *[Arrow](/wiki/Arrow_(TV_series))* in the recurring role of Chase. His character was a DJ and a love interest of [Willa Holland](/wiki/Willa_Holland)'s character [Thea Queen](/wiki/Thea_Queen). He co-starred with [Miranda Cosgrove](/wiki/Miranda_Cosgrove) and [Tom Sizemore](/wiki/Tom_Sizemore) in the 2015 thriller film *[The Intruders](/wiki/The_Intruders_(2015_film))*, and appeared in Kevin Smith's 2016 horror-comedy film *[Yoga Hosers](/wiki/Yoga_Hosers)* about 15-year-old yoga nuts Colleen Collette and Colleen McKenzie. The film also stars [Johnny Depp](/wiki/Johnny_Depp), [Lily-Rose Depp](/wiki/Lily-Rose_Depp), [Harley Quinn Smith](/wiki/Harley_Quinn_Smith) and [Haley Joel Osment](/wiki/Haley_Joel_Osment). In 2016, he began playing the role of Wil Ohmsford in *[The Shannara Chronicles](/wiki/The_Shannara_Chronicles)*, [MTV](/wiki/MTV)'s television adaptation of the [Terry Brooks](/wiki/Terry_Brooks) novel *[The Elfstones of Shannara](/wiki/The_Elfstones_of_Shannara)*. The series was cancelled after two seasons.

Butler made his Broadway debut playing Don Parritt, the "lost boy" in *[The Iceman Cometh](/wiki/The_Iceman_Cometh)*, which starred [Denzel Washington](/wiki/Denzel_Washington) and [David Morse](/wiki/David_Morse). Previews for the limited run began in March 2018, and the play closed in July 2018. Per Hilton Als's review of the play in *[The New Yorker](/wiki/The_New_Yorker)*: "Although there are many performers in George C. Wolfe's staging of Eugene O'Neill's phenomenal [...] drama, *The Iceman Cometh*, [...] there is only one actor, and his name is Austin Butler."

### 2019–present: Breakthrough and stardom

Butler at the premiere of *[Once Upon a Time in Hollywood](/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Hollywood)* in 2019

In 2019, Butler appeared in the [Quentin Tarantino](/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino) film *[Once Upon a Time in Hollywood](/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Hollywood)* as a fictional version of [Manson Family](/wiki/Manson_Family) member [Tex Watson](/wiki/Tex_Watson). Though he had limited screen time, his performance has been described as "intense" and "brooding". He received numerous nominations as a member of the film's [ensemble cast](/wiki/Ensemble_cast). In that same year, Butler was cast as [Elvis Presley](/wiki/Elvis_Presley) in the biopic *[Elvis](/wiki/Elvis_(2022_film))*, directed by [Baz Luhrmann](/wiki/Baz_Luhrmann). The film premiered at the [2022 Cannes Film Festival](/wiki/2022_Cannes_Film_Festival). Butler's performance received critical acclaim, as well as praise from the Presley family. He described the part as "the most intimidating thing I've ever done. I honestly didn't sleep for about two years." The role earned him several nominations for Best Actor awards, including the [Academy Award](/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actor), [BAFTA](/wiki/BAFTA_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Leading_Role), [Critics' Choice](/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Movie_Award_for_Best_Actor), [Golden Globe](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Motion_Picture_-_Drama) and [SAG Awards](/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Male_Actor_in_a_Leading_Role), winning the BAFTA and Golden Globe. Butler hosted an episode of *[Saturday Night Live](/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live)* later that year.

In June 2023, Butler was invited to join the [Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences](/wiki/Academy_of_Motion_Picture_Arts_and_Sciences) as an actor. The [50th Telluride Film Festival](/wiki/50th_Telluride_Film_Festival) marked the premiere of [Jeff Nichols](/wiki/Jeff_Nichols)'s 1960s-set drama *[The Bikeriders](/wiki/The_Bikeriders)*, in which he played a member of a motorcycle club. The following year, he portrayed [Gale Cleven](/wiki/Gale_Cleven) in the [war drama](/wiki/War_drama) miniseries *[Masters of the Air](/wiki/Masters_of_the_Air)*. He next played the villainous [Feyd-Rautha](/wiki/Feyd-Rautha) in [Denis Villeneuve](/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve)'s science fiction epic *[Dune: Part Two](/wiki/Dune:_Part_Two)* (2024). Butler received praise for his commitment to the role, physical transformation, and complex character work.

Butler next starred as radical cult leader Vernon Jefferson Peak in [Ari Aster](/wiki/Ari_Aster)'s Western film *[Eddington](/wiki/Eddington_(film))*, alongside [Joaquin Phoenix](/wiki/Joaquin_Phoenix), [Emma Stone](/wiki/Emma_Stone) and [Pedro Pascal](/wiki/Pedro_Pascal), and in [Darren Aronofsky](/wiki/Darren_Aronofsky)'s crime thriller *[Caught Stealing](/wiki/Caught_Stealing)* as Henry "Hank" Thompson, a bartender caught up in a web of crime, alongside [Zoë Kravitz](/wiki/Zo%C3%AB_Kravitz), [Regina King](/wiki/Regina_King), and [Bad Bunny](/wiki/Bad_Bunny). In May 2025, It was announced that Butler would be joining [Jeremy Allen White](/wiki/Jeremy_Allen_White) in [A24](/wiki/A24)'s Henry Dunham Crime Pic *[Enemies](/wiki/Enemies_(upcoming_film)).*

In February 2026, it was announced that Butler would portray [Lance Armstrong](/wiki/Lance_Armstrong) in [Edward Berger](/wiki/Edward_Berger)'s biopic about Armstrong.

## Personal life

Butler enjoys creating and recording music. He taught himself to play guitar at the age of thirteen and piano at sixteen. In 2014, his mother died of [duodenal cancer](/wiki/Duodenal_cancer), which briefly made him consider retiring. From 2011 to 2019, Butler dated actress [Vanessa Hudgens](/wiki/Vanessa_Hudgens). In December 2021, he began dating actress and model [Kaia Gerber](/wiki/Kaia_Gerber). They broke up at the end of 2024.

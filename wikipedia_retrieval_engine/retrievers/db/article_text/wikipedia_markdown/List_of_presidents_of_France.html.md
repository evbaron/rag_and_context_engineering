# List of presidents of France

The [president of France](/wiki/President_of_France) is the head of state of [France](/wiki/France), elected by popular vote for five years.

The first officeholder is considered to be [Louis-Napoléon Bonaparte](/wiki/Napoleon_III), who was elected in [1848](/wiki/1848_French_presidential_election) but provoked the [1851 self-coup](/wiki/1851_French_coup_d%27%C3%A9tat) to later proclaim himself [emperor](/wiki/Emperor_of_the_French) as Napoleon III. His coup, which proved popular as he sought the restoration of [universal male suffrage](/wiki/Universal_manhood_suffrage) previously abolished by the [legislature](/wiki/French_Parliament), granted the newly-established [Second Empire](/wiki/Second_French_Empire) firm ground.

A republican regime was given way again in 1870 through the [Third Republic](/wiki/French_Third_Republic), after the [fall of Napoleon III](/wiki/Battle_of_Sedan). A [1962 referendum](/wiki/1962_French_presidential_election_referendum) held under the [Fifth Republic](/wiki/French_Fifth_Republic) at the request of President [Charles de Gaulle](/wiki/Charles_de_Gaulle) transferred the election of the president of France from an electoral college to a popular vote. Since then, ten presidential elections have taken place. The 25th and current officeholder has been [Emmanuel Macron](/wiki/Emmanuel_Macron) since 14 May 2017.

## First Republic (1792–1804)

Main article: [French First Republic](/wiki/French_First_Republic)

### National Convention

Main article: [National Convention](/wiki/National_Convention)

The National Convention (20 September 1792 – 26 October 1795) was led by the [President of the National Convention](/wiki/List_of_presidents_of_the_National_Convention); the presidency rotated fortnightly.

From 1793 the National Convention was dominated by its [Committee of Public Safety](/wiki/Committee_of_Public_Safety), in which the leading figures were [Georges Danton](/wiki/Georges_Danton) and then [Maximilien Robespierre](/wiki/Maximilien_Robespierre).

### Directory

Main article: [French Directory](/wiki/French_Directory)

The Directory was officially led by a president, as stipulated by Article 141 of the Constitution of the Year III. An entirely ceremonial post, the first president was [Jean-François Rewbell](/wiki/Jean-Fran%C3%A7ois_Rewbell), who was chosen by lot on 2 November 1795. The Directors conducted their elections privately, with the presidency rotating every three months. The last President was [Louis-Jérôme Gohier](/wiki/Louis-J%C3%A9r%C3%B4me_Gohier).

The leading figure of the Directory was [Paul Barras](/wiki/Paul_Barras), the only director to serve throughout the entirety of the Directory’s existence.

[Napoléon Bonaparte](/wiki/Napoleon) proclaimed himself [Emperor of the French](/wiki/Emperor_of_the_French) in 1804, reigning as Emperor Napoleon I 1804–1814 ([First French Empire](/wiki/First_French_Empire)) and 1815 ([Hundred Days](/wiki/Hundred_Days)).

The monarchy was restored 1814–1815 and 1815–1830 ([Bourbon Restoration](/wiki/Bourbon_Restoration_in_France)); again 1830–1848 ([July Monarchy](/wiki/July_Monarchy)).

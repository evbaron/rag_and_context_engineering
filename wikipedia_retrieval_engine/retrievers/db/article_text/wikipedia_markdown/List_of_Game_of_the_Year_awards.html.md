# List of Game of the Year awards

This article is about video game awards. For the board game equivalent, see [List of Game of the Year awards (board games)](/wiki/List_of_Game_of_the_Year_awards_(board_games)).

**Game of the Year** (**GotY**) is an award given to a [video game](/wiki/Video_game) by various award events and media publications that they feel represented the pinnacle of gaming that year.

Of these, five of these are considered the most significant, those of the Golden Joysticks, the Game Awards, the D.I.C.E. Awards, the Game Developer Choice Awards, and the BAFTA Games awards. Only two games have won all five in a given year: *[Baldur's Gate III](/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_III)* in 2023 and *[Clair Obscur: Expedition 33](/wiki/Clair_Obscur:_Expedition_33)* in 2025.

## Events and ceremonies

### British Academy Games Awards (BAFTA Games Awards)

Main article: [British Academy Games Award for Best Game](/wiki/British_Academy_Games_Award_for_Best_Game)

The [British Academy Games Awards](/wiki/British_Academy_Games_Awards) are an annual British awards ceremony honoring "outstanding creative achievement" in the video game industry. First presented in 2004 following the restructuring of the [BAFTA Interactive Entertainment Awards](/wiki/BAFTA_Interactive_Entertainment_Awards), the awards are presented by the British Academy of Film and Television Arts (BAFTA), and are thus commonly referred to as the BAFTA Games Awards.

### Electronic Gaming Awards (formerly Arcade Awards)

The Arcade Awards, also known as the [Arkie Awards](/wiki/Arkie_Awards), was one of the first video game awards, dating back to the [golden age of arcade video games](/wiki/Golden_age_of_arcade_video_games) and lasting up until the [video game crash of 1983](/wiki/Video_game_crash_of_1983). It was held since 1980 (for games released in 1979 and earlier) and were announced annually by *[Electronic Games](/wiki/Electronic_Games)* magazine since 1981, covering several platform categories. Following the magazine's revival in 1992, it published the Electronic Gaming Awards in January 1993 for the best video games released in 1992. The 1992 and 1993 award were voted on by readers.

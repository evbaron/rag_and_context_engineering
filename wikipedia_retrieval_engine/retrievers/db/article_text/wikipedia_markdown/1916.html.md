This article is about the year 1916. For the album by Motörhead, see [1916 (album)](/wiki/1916_(album)).

From top to bottom, left to right: The [Battle of the Somme](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Somme) begins as British and French forces attack German lines in northern France, causing over one million casualties; the [Battle of Verdun](/wiki/Battle_of_Verdun) between France and Germany leaves nearly 700,000 dead or wounded, symbolizing the Western Front stalemate; the [Brusilov Offensive](/wiki/Brusilov_Offensive) gives Russia major gains against Austria-Hungary but with heavy losses; the [Battle of Jutland](/wiki/Battle_of_Jutland) in the North Sea, the war’s largest naval battle, ends without a clear victor but secures British naval dominance; the [Arab Revolt](/wiki/Arab_Revolt) is launched against Ottoman rule with British support and T. E. Lawrence; and the [Easter Rising](/wiki/Easter_Rising) in [Dublin](/wiki/Dublin) sees Irish republicans stage a doomed revolt against British rule.

**1916** ([MCMXVI](/wiki/Roman_numerals)) was a [leap year starting on Saturday](/wiki/Leap_year_starting_on_Saturday) of the [Gregorian calendar](/wiki/Gregorian_calendar) and a [leap year starting on Friday](/wiki/Leap_year_starting_on_Friday) of the [Julian calendar](/wiki/Julian_calendar), the 1916th year of the [Common Era](/wiki/Common_Era) (CE) and *[Anno Domini](/wiki/Anno_Domini)* (AD) designations, the 916th year of the [2nd millennium](/wiki/2nd_millennium), the 16th year of the [20th century](/wiki/20th_century), and the 7th year of the [1910s](/wiki/1910s) decade. As of the start of 1916, the Gregorian calendar was 13 days ahead of the Julian calendar, which remained in localized use until 1923.

## Events

Below, the events of the [First World War](/wiki/First_World_War) have the "WWI" prefix.

### January

Main article: [January 1916](/wiki/January_1916)

- [January 1](/wiki/January_1) – The [British](/wiki/British_Empire) [Royal Army Medical Corps](/wiki/Royal_Army_Medical_Corps) carries out the first successful [blood transfusion](/wiki/Blood_transfusion), using blood that has been stored and cooled.
- [January 9](/wiki/January_9) – WWI: [Gallipoli Campaign](/wiki/Gallipoli_Campaign) – The last British troops are evacuated from [Gallipoli](/wiki/Gallipoli), as the [Ottoman Empire](/wiki/Ottoman_Empire) prevails over a joint British and French operation to capture [Constantinople](/wiki/Constantinople).
- [January 10](/wiki/January_10) – WWI: [Erzurum Offensive](/wiki/Erzurum_Offensive) – Russia defeats the Ottoman Empire.
- [January 12](/wiki/January_12) – The [Gilbert and Ellice Islands](/wiki/Gilbert_and_Ellice_Islands) Colony, part of the [British Empire](/wiki/British_Empire), is established in modern-day [Tuvalu](/wiki/Tuvalu) and [Kiribati](/wiki/Kiribati).
- [January 13](/wiki/January_13) – WWI: [Battle of Wadi](/wiki/Battle_of_Wadi_(1916)) – Ottoman Empire forces defeat the British, during the [Mesopotamian campaign](/wiki/Mesopotamian_campaign) in modern-day [Iraq](/wiki/Iraq).
- [January 29](/wiki/January_29) – WWI: Paris is bombed by [German](/wiki/German_Empire) [zeppelins](/wiki/Zeppelin).
- [January 31](/wiki/January_31) – WWI: An attack is planned on [Verdun](/wiki/Verdun), France.

### February

Main article: [February 1916](/wiki/February_1916)

- [February 9](/wiki/February_9) (6.00 p.m.) – [Tristan Tzara](/wiki/Tristan_Tzara) "founds" the art movement [Dadaism](/wiki/Dadaism) (according to [Hans Arp](/wiki/Hans_Arp)).
- [February 11](/wiki/February_11)
  - [Emma Goldman](/wiki/Emma_Goldman) is arrested for lecturing on [birth control](/wiki/Birth_control) in the United States.
  - The [Baltimore Symphony Orchestra](/wiki/Baltimore_Symphony_Orchestra) presents its first concert in the United States.
  - The [Romanian](/wiki/Romania) [Association football](/wiki/Association_football) club [Sportul Studențesc](/wiki/FC_Sportul_Studen%C8%9Besc_Bucure%C8%99ti) is founded in [Bucharest](/wiki/Bucharest).
- [February 12](/wiki/February_12) – WWI: [Battle of Salaita Hill](/wiki/Battle_of_Salaita_Hill) ([East African Campaign](/wiki/East_African_Campaign_(World_War_I))) – South African and other British Empire troops fail to take a [German East African](/wiki/German_East_Africa) defensive position.
- [February 21](/wiki/February_21) – WWI: The [Battle of Verdun](/wiki/Battle_of_Verdun) begins in [France](/wiki/French_Third_Republic).

### March

Main article: [March 1916](/wiki/March_1916)

- [March 8](/wiki/March_8)–[9](/wiki/March_9) – [Mexican Revolution](/wiki/Mexican_Revolution): [Pancho Villa](/wiki/Pancho_Villa) leads about 500 Mexican raiders in an [attack against Columbus, New Mexico](/wiki/Battle_of_Columbus_(1916)), killing 12 U.S. soldiers. A garrison of the U.S. [13th Cavalry Regiment](/wiki/13th_Cavalry_Regiment) fights back and drives them away.
- [March 10](/wiki/March_10) – The [McMahon–Hussein Correspondence](/wiki/McMahon%E2%80%93Hussein_Correspondence) concludes with an understanding that the United Kingdom would recognise Arab independence in return for [Hussein bin Ali, Sharif of Mecca](/wiki/Hussein_bin_Ali,_Sharif_of_Mecca), launching the [Arab Revolt](/wiki/Arab_Revolt) against the [Ottoman Empire](/wiki/Ottoman_Empire).
- [March 15](/wiki/March_15) – United States President [Woodrow Wilson](/wiki/Woodrow_Wilson) sends 12,000 United States troops over the [U.S.–Mexico border](/wiki/U.S.%E2%80%93Mexico_border) to pursue [Pancho Villa](/wiki/Pancho_Villa); the 13th Cavalry regiment enters Mexican territory.
- [March 16](/wiki/March_16) – [Mexican Revolution](/wiki/Mexican_Revolution): The U.S. [7th](/wiki/7th_Cavalry_Regiment) and [10th Cavalry](/wiki/10th_Cavalry_Regiment_(United_States)) regiments under [John J. Pershing](/wiki/John_J._Pershing) cross the border, to join the hunt for Villa.
- [March 22](/wiki/March_22) – The temporary [Emperor of China](/wiki/Emperor_of_China), [Yuan Shikai](/wiki/Yuan_Shikai), abdicates the throne, and the [Republic of China](/wiki/Republic_of_China_(1912%E2%80%9349)) is restored once again.
- [March 24](/wiki/March_24) – French ferry [SS *Sussex*](/wiki/SS_Sussex) is [torpedoed](/wiki/Torpedo) by [SM *UB-29*](/wiki/SM_UB-29) in the [English Channel](/wiki/English_Channel), with at least 50 killed (including the composer [Enrique Granados](/wiki/Enrique_Granados)), resulting on [May 4](/wiki/May_4) in the [*Sussex* Pledge](/wiki/Sussex_pledge) by Germany to the United States.

### April

[Proclamation of the Irish Republic](/wiki/Proclamation_of_the_Irish_Republic) distributed during the [Easter Rising](/wiki/Easter_Rising)

Main article: [April 1916](/wiki/April_1916)

- [April](/wiki/April)
  - The toggle [light switch](/wiki/Light_switch) is invented, by William J. Newton and Morris Goldberg.
  - [Korea Tungsten](/wiki/Korea_Tungsten_Company) is founded in [Daegu](/wiki/Daegu), predecessor of leading [steel](/wiki/Steel) producer in Asia, [POSCO](/wiki/POSCO) (Pohang Steel Company).
- [April 11](/wiki/April_11) – WWI: The [Egyptian Expeditionary Force](/wiki/Egyptian_Expeditionary_Force) begins the [occupation of](/wiki/Sinai_and_Palestine_Campaign#Raid_on_Jifjafa) the [Sinai Peninsula](/wiki/Sinai_Peninsula).
- [April 22](/wiki/April_22) – The [Chinese](/wiki/Republic_of_China_(1912%E2%80%9349)) troop transport [SS *Hsin-Yu*](/wiki/SS_Hsin-Yu) capsizes off the Chinese coast; at least 1,000 are killed.
- [April 24](/wiki/April_24)–[30](/wiki/April_30) – The [Easter Rising](/wiki/Easter_Rising) occurs in Ireland. Members of the [Irish Republican Brotherhood](/wiki/Irish_Republican_Brotherhood) [proclaim an Irish Republic](/wiki/Proclamation_of_the_Irish_Republic), and the [Irish Volunteers](/wiki/Irish_Volunteers) and [Irish Citizen Army](/wiki/Irish_Citizen_Army) occupy the [General Post Office](/wiki/General_Post_Office,_Dublin) and other buildings in [Dublin](/wiki/Dublin), before surrendering to the [British Army](/wiki/British_Army). A total of 485 [Irish Volunteers](/wiki/Irish_Volunteers), British soldiers and Irish civilians were killed as well as 2,200 wounded and 16 rebel leaders would be executed.
- [April 24](/wiki/April_24)–[May 10](/wiki/May_10) – [Voyage of the *James Caird*](/wiki/Voyage_of_the_James_Caird): An open boat journey from [Elephant Island](/wiki/Elephant_Island) in the [South Shetland Islands](/wiki/South_Shetland_Islands) to [South Georgia](/wiki/South_Georgia_Island) in the southern Atlantic Ocean (800 nautical miles (1,500 km; 920 mi)) is undertaken by Sir [Ernest Shackleton](/wiki/Ernest_Shackleton) and five companions, to obtain rescue for the main body of the [Imperial Trans-Antarctic Expedition](/wiki/Imperial_Trans-Antarctic_Expedition), following the loss of its ship [*Endurance*](/wiki/Endurance_(1912_ship)).
- [April 27](/wiki/April_27) – WWI: [Gas attack at Hulluch](/wiki/Gas_attack_at_Hulluch) in France: The 47th Brigade, [16th (Irish) Division](/wiki/16th_(Irish)_Division) is decimated, in one of the most heavily concentrated German gas attacks of the war.
- [April 29](/wiki/April_29) – WWI: [Mesopotamian campaign](/wiki/Mesopotamian_campaign) – The [Siege of Kut](/wiki/Siege_of_Kut) ends with the surrender of [British Indian Army](/wiki/British_Indian_Army) forces to the [Ottoman Empire](/wiki/Ottoman_Empire) at [Kut-al-Amara](/wiki/Kut-al-Amara) on the [Tigris](/wiki/Tigris) in [Basra Vilayet](/wiki/Basra_Vilayet).

### May

Main article: [May 1916](/wiki/May_1916)

[May 31](/wiki/May_31)–[June 1](/wiki/June_1):[Battle of Jutland](/wiki/Battle_of_Jutland) between Allies and Germany

- [May 3](/wiki/May_3)-[12](/wiki/May_12) - 15 Irish rebel leaders who took part in the [Easter Rising](/wiki/Easter_Rising) in [Dublin](/wiki/Dublin) in [April](/wiki/April_1916) are executed. Among them were [Patrick Pearse](/wiki/Patrick_Pearse), [James Connolly](/wiki/James_Connolly), [Thomas Clarke](/wiki/Thomas_Clarke_(professor)) and [William Pearse](/wiki/Willie_Pearse), brother of Patrick Pearse who had served as a captain rather than a leader.
- [May 16](/wiki/May_16)
  - [United States Marines invade the Dominican Republic](/wiki/United_States_occupation_of_the_Dominican_Republic_(1916%E2%80%9324)).
  - [Britain](/wiki/United_Kingdom_of_Great_Britain_and_Ireland) and [France](/wiki/French_Third_Republic) conclude the secret [Sykes–Picot Agreement](/wiki/Sykes%E2%80%93Picot_Agreement), which is to divide Arab areas of the [Ottoman Empire](/wiki/Ottoman_Empire), following the conclusion of WWI and the [partitioning of the Ottoman Empire](/wiki/Partitioning_of_the_Ottoman_Empire), into French and British [spheres of influence](/wiki/Sphere_of_influence).
- [May 31](/wiki/May_31)–[June 1](/wiki/June_1) – WWI: [Battle of Jutland](/wiki/Battle_of_Jutland), between the British [Royal Navy](/wiki/Royal_Navy)'s [Grand Fleet](/wiki/Grand_Fleet) and the [Imperial German Navy](/wiki/Imperial_German_Navy)'s [High Seas Fleet](/wiki/High_Seas_Fleet) in the [North Sea](/wiki/North_Sea), the war's only large-scale clash of battleships. The result is tactically inconclusive, but British dominance of the North Sea is maintained

### June

Main article: [June 1916](/wiki/June_1916)

- [June 4](/wiki/June_4) – WWI: The [Brusilov Offensive](/wiki/Brusilov_Offensive), the height of Russian operations in the war, begins with their breaking through Austro-Hungarian lines.
- [June 5](/wiki/June_5) – WWI: [HMS *Hampshire*](/wiki/HMS_Hampshire_(1903)) sinks, having hit a [mine](/wiki/Naval_mine) off the [Orkney Islands](/wiki/Orkney_Islands), Scotland, with [Lord Kitchener](/wiki/Herbert_Kitchener) aboard.
- [June 10](/wiki/June_10) – The [Arab Revolt](/wiki/Arab_Revolt) against the [Ottoman Empire](/wiki/Ottoman_Empire), to create a single unified Arab state spanning from [Aleppo](/wiki/Aleppo) to [Aden](/wiki/Aden), is formally declared by [Hussein bin Ali, Sharif of Mecca](/wiki/Hussein_bin_Ali,_Sharif_of_Mecca).
- [June 15](/wiki/June_15) – U.S. President [Woodrow Wilson](/wiki/Woodrow_Wilson) signs a bill incorporating the [Boy Scouts of America](/wiki/Boy_Scouts_of_America).
- [June 24](/wiki/June_24) – [Mary Pickford](/wiki/Mary_Pickford) becomes the first movie star to sign a million-dollar contract, making her one of the highest-paid people in the world.

### July

Main article: [July 1916](/wiki/July_1916)

[July 1](/wiki/July_1)–[November 18](/wiki/November_18):[Battle of the Somme](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Somme) between British and German.

- [July 1](/wiki/July_1)–[November 18](/wiki/November_18) – WWI: [Battle of the Somme](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Somme), opening with explosion of the British [Y Sap](/wiki/Y_Sap_mine) and [Lochnagar mines](/wiki/Lochnagar_mine) and the [Battle of Albert](/wiki/Battle_of_Albert_(1916)): More than one million soldiers die, with 57,470 [British Empire](/wiki/British_Empire) casualties on the [first day](/wiki/First_day_on_the_Somme), 19,240 of them killed, the British Army's bloodiest day. The immediate result is tactically inconclusive.
- [July 1](/wiki/July_1)–[12](/wiki/July_12) – [Jersey Shore shark attacks of 1916](/wiki/Jersey_Shore_shark_attacks_of_1916): At least one shark attacks 5 swimmers along 80 miles (130 km) of [New Jersey](/wiki/New_Jersey) coastline, resulting in 4 deaths and the survival of one youth, who requires limb amputation. This event is the inspiration for author [Peter Benchley](/wiki/Peter_Benchley), over half a century later, to write *[Jaws](/wiki/Jaws_(novel))*.
- [July 2](/wiki/July_2) – WWI: [Battle of Erzincan](/wiki/Battle_of_Erzincan) – [Russian](/wiki/Russian_Empire) forces defeat troops of the [Ottoman Empire](/wiki/Ottoman_Empire) in [Armenia](/wiki/Armenia).
- [July 6](/wiki/July_6) – WWI: The [Battle of Kostiuchnówka](/wiki/Battle_of_Kostiuchn%C3%B3wka) concludes in [Galicia](/wiki/Galicia_(Eastern_Europe)) (modern-day Ukraine) with Russian Imperial troops breaking through the line, forcing the [Polish Legions](/wiki/Polish_Legions_in_World_War_I) and supporting Hungarian troops to retreat, with the Poles enduring 2,000 casualties.
- [July 15](/wiki/July_15) – In [Seattle](/wiki/Seattle), [William Boeing](/wiki/William_Boeing) incorporates *Pacific Aero Products* (later renamed *[Boeing](/wiki/Boeing)*).
- [July 15](/wiki/July_15)–[19](/wiki/July_19) – WWI: [Battle of Delville Wood](/wiki/Battle_of_Delville_Wood) – 766 men from the South African Brigade are killed, in South Africa's biggest loss during the First World War.
- [July 16](/wiki/July_16) – [Max Reger](/wiki/Max_Reger)'s *[Hebbel Requiem](/wiki/Requiem_(Reger))* is first performed in a memorial concert for the composer, conducted by [Philipp Wolfrum](/wiki/Philipp_Wolfrum).
- [July 19](/wiki/July_19)–[20](/wiki/July_20) – WWI: [Battle of Fromelles](/wiki/Battle_of_Fromelles) – An attack by Australian and British troops is repulsed by the German army, with heavy casualties.
- [July 22](/wiki/July_22) – [Preparedness Day Bombing](/wiki/Preparedness_Day_Bombing): In San Francisco, a bomb explodes on Market Street during a Preparedness Day parade, killing 10 and injuring 40; [Warren Billings](/wiki/Warren_Billings) and [Tom Mooney](/wiki/Thomas_Mooney) are later wrongly convicted of it.
- [July 26](/wiki/July_26) – WWI: [East African Campaign](/wiki/East_African_Campaign_(World_War_I)) – The German armed ship [SMS *Graf von Goetzen*](/wiki/MV_Liemba) [scuttles](/wiki/Scuttling) herself on [Lake Tanganyika](/wiki/Lake_Tanganyika).
- [July 29](/wiki/July_29) – [Matheson Fire](/wiki/Matheson_Fire): In [Ontario](/wiki/Ontario), Canada, a lightning strike ignites a forest fire that destroys the towns of [Cochrane](/wiki/Cochrane,_Ontario) and [Matheson](/wiki/Matheson,_Ontario), killing 233.
- [July 30](/wiki/July_30) – German agents cause the [Black Tom explosion](/wiki/Black_Tom_explosion) in [Jersey City, New Jersey](/wiki/Jersey_City,_New_Jersey), an act of sabotage destroying an ammunition depot and killing at least 7 people.

- [August 27](/wiki/August_27) – WWI: The [Kingdom of Romania](/wiki/Kingdom_of_Romania) declares war on the [Central Powers](/wiki/Central_Powers), entering the war on the side of the [Allies](/wiki/Allies_of_World_War_I).

  - Germany declares war on Romania.

- [August 29](/wiki/August_29) – The United States passes the [Philippine Autonomy Act](/wiki/Jones_Law_(Philippines)).
- [August 30](/wiki/August_30) – The crew of the [Imperial Trans-Antarctic Expedition](/wiki/Imperial_Trans-Antarctic_Expedition)'s [*Endurance*](/wiki/Endurance_(1912_ship)) is rescued from [Elephant Island](/wiki/Elephant_Island).

Troops from New Zealand during WWI.

- [September 19](/wiki/September_19) – WWI: [East African Campaign](/wiki/East_African_Campaign_(World_War_I)) – Belgian troops occupy [Tabora](/wiki/Tabora) in [German East Africa](/wiki/German_East_Africa).

- [September 29](/wiki/September_29) – [John D. Rockefeller](/wiki/John_D._Rockefeller) becomes the first person to reach a nominal personal fortune of US$1 billion

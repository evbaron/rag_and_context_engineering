# List of countries and dependencies by population (United Nations)

Statistical subregions as [defined by](/wiki/United_Nations_geoscheme) the [United Nations Statistics Division](/wiki/United_Nations_Statistics_Division)

This is the list of [countries](/wiki/List_of_sovereign_states) and other [inhabited territories](/wiki/Dependent_territory) of the world by estimated total population. It is based on estimates published by the [United Nations](/wiki/United_Nations) in the 2024 revision of *[World Population Prospects](/wiki/World_Population_Prospects)*. It presents population estimates from 1950 to the present.

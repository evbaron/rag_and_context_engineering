**Mark O'Halloran** (born 6 March 1981) is a former professional [rugby league](/wiki/Rugby_league) footballer who played for the [Wests Tigers](/wiki/Wests_Tigers) and the [Penrith Panthers](/wiki/Penrith_Panthers) in the [NRL](/wiki/NRL), and the [London Broncos](/wiki/London_Broncos#1994–2005:_Broncos_and_Super_League) in the [ESL](/wiki/ESL). His junior club was Burwood United in the Balmain District Competition. O'Halloran's position of choice was as a [centre](/wiki/Rugby_league_positions#Centre) although he had also played as a [wing](/wiki/Rugby_league_positions#Wing), a defensive second rower, and briefly played five eighth in the English Super league.

## Background

O'Halloran was born in [Sydney, New South Wales, Australia](/wiki/Sydney,_New_South_Wales,_Australia).

## Playing career

O'Halloran made his NRL début for [Wests Tigers](/wiki/Wests_Tigers) in 2001 and played 28 first grade games, mostly as a centre. In 2004, O'Halloran moved to England, where he played in the [Super League](/wiki/Super_League) for the [London Broncos](/wiki/London_Broncos#1994–2005:_Broncos_and_Super_League).

O'Halloran returned to Australia from England and played for the Penrith Panthers in the NRL.

O'Halloran played in the [USA](/wiki/United_States_national_rugby_league_team) international team having played one game in 2007.

## Post playing

Since retiring, O'Halloran has become a PD/H/PE teacher and currently works at [St Patrick's College, Strathfield](/wiki/St_Patrick%27s_College,_Strathfield).

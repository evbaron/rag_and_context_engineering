# List of The Simpsons cast members

Four of the six main cast members attend a ceremony in 2009, left to right; [Nancy Cartwright](/wiki/Nancy_Cartwright) ([Bart](/wiki/Bart_Simpson)), [Yeardley Smith](/wiki/Yeardley_Smith) ([Lisa](/wiki/Lisa_Simpson)), [Dan Castellaneta](/wiki/Dan_Castellaneta) ([Homer](/wiki/Homer_Simpson)) and [Julie Kavner](/wiki/Julie_Kavner) ([Marge](/wiki/Marge_Simpson)).

*[The Simpsons](/wiki/The_Simpsons)* is an American [animated sitcom](/wiki/Animated_sitcom) that includes six main voice actors and numerous regular cast and recurring guest stars. The principal cast consists of [Dan Castellaneta](/wiki/Dan_Castellaneta), [Julie Kavner](/wiki/Julie_Kavner), [Nancy Cartwright](/wiki/Nancy_Cartwright), [Yeardley Smith](/wiki/Yeardley_Smith), [Hank Azaria](/wiki/Hank_Azaria) and [Harry Shearer](/wiki/Harry_Shearer). [Tress MacNeille](/wiki/Tress_MacNeille), [Maggie Roswell](/wiki/Maggie_Roswell), [Chris Edgerly](/wiki/Chris_Edgerly), Eric Lopez, [Alex Désert](/wiki/Alex_D%C3%A9sert), [Kevin Michael Richardson](/wiki/Kevin_Michael_Richardson), [Jenny Yokobori](/wiki/Jenny_Yokobori), [Kimberly D. Brooks](/wiki/Kimberly_Brooks), [Dawnn Lewis](/wiki/Dawnn_Lewis), Tony Rodriguez, Melanie Minichino, Jonathan Lipow, [Mo Collins](/wiki/Mo_Collins), Kelly Macleod and [Grey DeLisle](/wiki/Grey_DeLisle) have appeared as supporting cast members, along with former supporting cast members [Pamela Hayden](/wiki/Pamela_Hayden), [Russi Taylor](/wiki/Russi_Taylor), [Karl Wiedergott](/wiki/Karl_Wiedergott), [Marcia Mitzman Gaven](/wiki/Marcia_Mitzman_Gaven), [Doris Grau](/wiki/Doris_Grau), [Susan Blu](/wiki/Susan_Blu), [Jo Ann Harris](/wiki/Jo_Ann_Harris), and [Christopher Collins](/wiki/Christopher_Collins). Repeat guest cast members include [Marcia Wallace](/wiki/Marcia_Wallace), [Phil Hartman](/wiki/Phil_Hartman), [Jon Lovitz](/wiki/Jon_Lovitz), [Joe Mantegna](/wiki/Joe_Mantegna), [Frank Welker](/wiki/Frank_Welker), [Keegan-Michael Key](/wiki/Keegan-Michael_Key), [Nick Offerman](/wiki/Nick_Offerman), [Megan Mullally](/wiki/Megan_Mullally), [Pharrell Williams](/wiki/Pharrell_Williams),
[Kelsey Grammer](/wiki/Kelsey_Grammer), [Kiefer Sutherland](/wiki/Kiefer_Sutherland), [John C. Reilly](/wiki/John_C._Reilly), [Sarah Silverman](/wiki/Sarah_Silverman), [Tom Hanks](/wiki/Tom_Hanks) and [Albert Brooks](/wiki/Albert_Brooks). With the exception of "[Old Money](/wiki/Old_Money_(The_Simpsons))", episode credits list only the [voice actors](/wiki/Voice_actor), and not the characters they voice.

Both [Fox](/wiki/Fox_Broadcasting_Company) and the production crew wanted to keep their identities secret during the early seasons and closed most of the recording sessions while refusing to publish photos of the recording artists. The network eventually revealed which roles each actor performed in the episode "Old Money", because the producers said the voice actors should receive credit for their work. Every main cast member has won an Emmy for [Outstanding Voice-Over Performance](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Voice-Over_Performance). Shearer was the last cast member to win, receiving his award in 2014 for the episode "[Four Regrettings and a Funeral](/wiki/Four_Regrettings_and_a_Funeral)." Castellaneta and Azaria have won four, while Kavner, Cartwright, Smith, Shearer, Wallace, Grammer, and guest star [Jackie Mason](/wiki/Jackie_Mason) have each won one.

## Regular cast

### Background

[Hank Azaria](/wiki/Hank_Azaria) has been a part of the *Simpsons* regular voice cast since the second season.

[Harry Shearer](/wiki/Harry_Shearer) was the most recent principal cast member to win an [Emmy Award for Outstanding Voice-Over Performance](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Voice-Over_Performance).

[Marcia Wallace](/wiki/Marcia_Wallace) appeared regularly as [Edna Krabappel](/wiki/Edna_Krabappel) until her death in 2013.

[Kelsey Grammer](/wiki/Kelsey_Grammer) voices [Sideshow Bob](/wiki/Sideshow_Bob).

[Jan Hooks](/wiki/Jan_Hooks) played [Manjula Nahasapeemapetilon](/wiki/List_of_recurring_The_Simpsons_characters#Manjula_Nahasapeemapetilon).

[Maurice LaMarche](/wiki/Maurice_LaMarche) has appeared in several minor roles.

Castellaneta and Kavner were asked to voice the lead roles of [Homer](/wiki/Homer_Simpson) and [Marge Simpson](/wiki/Marge_Simpson) as they were regular cast members of *[The Tracey Ullman Show](/wiki/The_Tracey_Ullman_Show)* on which [*The Simpsons* shorts](/wiki/The_Simpsons_shorts) appeared. Cartwright auditioned for the part of [Lisa](/wiki/Lisa_Simpson), but found that Lisa was simply described as the "middle child" and at the time did not have much personality. She then became more interested in the role of [Bart](/wiki/Bart_Simpson), so *Simpsons* creator [Matt Groening](/wiki/Matt_Groening) let her try out for that part instead, and upon hearing her read, he gave her the job on the spot. Smith had initially been asked by casting director [Bonita Pietila](/wiki/Bonita_Pietila) to audition for the role of Bart, but Pietila then realised that Smith's voice was too high, Smith was given the role of Lisa instead. When the show was commissioned for a full half-hour series, Shearer joined the cast and performed multiple roles. Groening and [Sam Simon](/wiki/Sam_Simon) asked Shearer to join the cast as they were fans of his radio show. Azaria was only a guest actor in the [first season](/wiki/The_Simpsons_season_1), but became permanent in [season 2](/wiki/The_Simpsons_season_2). He first appeared in "[Some Enchanted Evening](/wiki/Some_Enchanted_Evening_(The_Simpsons))", rerecording [Christopher Collins](/wiki/Christopher_Collins)'s lines as [Moe Szyslak](/wiki/Moe_Szyslak). In 2001, Groening remarked that he still considered Azaria the "new guy."

Up until 1998, the six main actors were paid [$](/wiki/American_dollar)30,000 per episode. In 1998 they were then involved in a pay dispute in which Fox threatened to replace them with new actors and went as far as preparing for casting of new voices. However, the issue was soon resolved and from 1998 to 2004, they were paid $125,000 per episode. In 2004, the voice actors intentionally skipped several table reads, demanding they be paid $360,000 per episode. The strike was resolved a month later and until 2008 they earned something between $250,000 and $360,000 per episode. In 2008, production for the [twentieth season](/wiki/The_Simpsons_season_20) was put on hold due to new contract negotiations with the voice actors, who wanted a "healthy bump" in salary to an amount close to $500,000 per episode. The dispute was soon resolved, and the actors' salary was raised to $400,000 per episode.

In 2011, Fox announced that, due to financial difficulties, they were unable to continue to produce *The Simpsons* under its current contract and that unless there were pay cuts, the show could end. For the negotiations, the studio requested that the cast members accept a 45% cut of their salaries so that more seasons could be produced after season 23, or else that season would be the last. In the end, the studio and the actors reached a deal, in which the actors would take a pay cut of 30%, down to just over $300,000 per episode, prolonging the show to its 30th season. As well as the actors, everybody involved in the show took a pay cut.

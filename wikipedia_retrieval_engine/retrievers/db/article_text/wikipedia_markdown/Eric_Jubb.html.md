# Eric Jubb

## Infobox

- **Born:** ( 1931-01-17 ) 17 January 1931 (age 95) Toronto, Ontario, Canada
- **Sport:** Swimming

**Eric MacGregor Jubb** (born 17 January 1931), later known as **Eric Lamont**, is a Canadian former backstroke and freestyle [swimmer](/wiki/Swimming_(sport)). He competed in [three events](/wiki/Swimming_at_the_1948_Summer_Olympics) at the [1948 Summer Olympics](/wiki/1948_Summer_Olympics).

***Dreamspace*** is the third studio album by [power metal](/wiki/Power_metal) band [Stratovarius](/wiki/Stratovarius), released on 9 February 1994 through [Noise Records](/wiki/Noise_Records). It is the last Stratovarius album to feature guitarist [Timo Tolkki](/wiki/Timo_Tolkki) on vocals (after which [Timo Kotipelto](/wiki/Timo_Kotipelto) became the lead singer on 1995's *[Fourth Dimension](/wiki/Fourth_Dimension_(Stratovarius_album))*), as well as the first to feature bassist [Jari Kainulainen](/wiki/Jari_Kainulainen). Sami Kuoppamäki was asked to record drums for some of the songs after drummer Tuomo Lassila was unable to finish the sessions due to tendonitis.

Steve Huey at [AllMusic](/wiki/AllMusic) gave *Dreamspace* four stars out of five, saying that it "demonstrates the band coming into its own, honing its melodic Euro-metal attack to a razor-sharp point."

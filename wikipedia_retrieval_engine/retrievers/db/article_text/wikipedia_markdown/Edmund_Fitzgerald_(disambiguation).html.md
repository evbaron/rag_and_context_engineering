# Edmund Fitzgerald (disambiguation)

**[SS *Edmund Fitzgerald*](/wiki/SS_Edmund_Fitzgerald)** was an American freighter that sank in Lake Superior in 1975.

**Edmund Fitzgerald** may also refer to:

- [Edmund Fitzgerald Fredericks](/wiki/Edmund_Fitzgerald_Fredericks) (1874 or 1875–1935), Guyanese lawyer and Pan-African activist
- [Edmund B. Fitzgerald](/wiki/Edmund_B._Fitzgerald) (1926–2013), American businessman
- [The Edmund Fitzgerald (band)](/wiki/The_Edmund_Fitzgerald_(band)), a British math rock group
- "[The Wreck of the *Edmund Fitzgerald*](/wiki/The_Wreck_of_the_Edmund_Fitzgerald)", a 1976 song by Gordon Lightfoot

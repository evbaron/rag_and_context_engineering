# Jamie Miller (drummer)

**Jamie Miller** is an American musician, currently the drummer for rock bands [Bad Religion](/wiki/Bad_Religion), Vanishing Life and [Snot](/wiki/Snot_(band)). He is also the lead guitarist and the backing vocalist for [theStart](/wiki/TheStart_(band)), and was formerly the drummer for [Souls at Zero](/wiki/Souls_at_Zero_(band)) and the drummer and guitarist for [...And You Will Know Us By the Trail of Dead](/wiki/...And_You_Will_Know_Us_By_the_Trail_of_Dead).

## Career

### Souls at Zero (1994–1996)

Miller joined the band [Souls at Zero](/wiki/Souls_at_Zero_(band)), replacing drummer [Shannon Larkin](/wiki/Shannon_Larkin) after he left to join [Ugly Kid Joe](/wiki/Ugly_Kid_Joe) in 1994. He played on Souls at Zero's final album *A Taste for the Perverse*.

### Snot (1996–present)

Miller joined [Snot](/wiki/Snot_(band)) in 1996. Snot signed with [Geffen Records](/wiki/Geffen_Records), and began work on their debut album, *[Get Some](/wiki/Get_Some_(album))*, released on May 27, 1997. Although the album received positive critical notice, sales were disappointing.

The band performed on the 1998 [Ozzfest](/wiki/Ozzfest) tour. In December 1998, band member [Lynn Strait](/wiki/Lynn_Strait) died in a car accident when a truck struck his car.

In 2008, the band reformed, with former [Divine Heresy](/wiki/Divine_Heresy) singer [Tommy Vext](/wiki/Tommy_Vext) on vocals. Tommy Cummings and Sonny Mayo left the band, and a new band, Tons, was formed in 2009 with a new vocalist. They recorded three new songs titled "Ability & Control", "1000 Ways of Pain" and "Fan the Flames" before members of the group moved on to new projects.

On February 11, 2014, Snot reunited again at the [Whisky a Go Go](/wiki/Whisky_a_Go_Go) in Hollywood.

### theStart (1998–present)

Jamie Miller formed the [synthrock](/wiki/Synthrock) band theStart (originally named Hero) along with [Aimee Echo](/wiki/Aimee_Echo) as the band's lead guitarist, synthesizer player and backing vocalist. They released three full-length studio albums and an EP on [Geffen](/wiki/Geffen_Records), [Nitro](/wiki/Nitro_Records) and [Metropolis](/wiki/Metropolis_Records). The band toured extensively.

### …And You Will Know Us by the Trail of Dead (2011–2020)

Miller with […And You Will Know Us by the Trail of Dead](/wiki/...And_You_Will_Know_Us_by_the_Trail_of_Dead) in 2013

Miller was a drummer and guitarist of the band [...And You Will Know Us by the Trail of Dead](/wiki/...And_You_Will_Know_Us_by_the_Trail_of_Dead) from 2011 to 2020. He replaced [Kevin Allen](/wiki/Kevin_Allen_(guitarist)).

### Bad Religion (2015–present)

Miller with Bad Religion in 2022

In 2016, Miller became the drummer for [Bad Religion](/wiki/Bad_Religion) by replacing longtime drummer [Brooks Wackerman](/wiki/Brooks_Wackerman), who joined [Avenged Sevenfold](/wiki/Avenged_Sevenfold). Miller would not appear on a Bad Religion album until four years later, with *[Age of Unreason](/wiki/Age_of_Unreason_(album))*.

### The Offspring (2021, 2026)

In September 2021, Miller performed with [The Offspring](/wiki/The_Offspring) filling in for friend [Josh Freese](/wiki/Josh_Freese) due to Freese' prior commitments with [Devo](/wiki/Devo).

Miller filled in for the Offspring again during their SUPERCHARGED Worldwide in '26 tour while [Brandon Pertzborn](/wiki/Brandon_Pertzborn) was on paternity leave.

## Influences

Miller cited [Budgie](/wiki/Budgie_(musician)) of [Siouxsie and the Banshees](/wiki/Siouxsie_and_the_Banshees) as his favourite drummer for having "a signature feel" the Banshees as his favourite band. "Whenever I hear one of their songs, I can hear his drumming and know that it's him. I love drummers like that. It's not necessarily that they have a signature sound, but rather a signature feel".

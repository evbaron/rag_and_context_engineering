# Tim Allsop & Stewart Williams

## Infobox

- **Occupations:** Producers writers directors
- **Years active:** 2006–present

**Tim Allsop** and **Stewart Williams** are a British filmmaking duo.

They met in 2005 working for [Elisabeth Murdoch](/wiki/Elisabeth_Murdoch_(businesswoman))’s production company [Shine Limited](/wiki/Shine_Limited). Their first joint work was a [Comedy Lab](/wiki/Comedy_Lab) for [Channel 4](/wiki/Channel_4) starring Alex Zane which they wrote, produced, and directed. Since then they have written for a variety of comedy and entertainment shows for the [BBC](/wiki/BBC), [ITV](/wiki/ITV_(TV_network)) and [Channel 4](/wiki/Channel_4), as well directing [music videos](/wiki/Music_video) and [commercials](/wiki/Television_advertisement) – most recently for the [Guitar Hero](/wiki/Guitar_Hero) brand. In 2007 they began working on a [pilot](/wiki/Pilot_episode) for Plus One in the [Comedy Showcase](/wiki/Comedy_Showcase) strand for Channel 4 which they made with production company [Kudos](/wiki/Kudos_(production_company)).

They have previously written for the [Cartoon Network](/wiki/Cartoon_Network) animated series *[The Amazing World of Gumball](/wiki/The_Amazing_World_of_Gumball)*, as well as developing the show bible and writing for the [Disney EMEA](/wiki/Disney_Channel_(Europe,_Middle_East,_and_Africa)) series *[Space Chickens in Space](/wiki/Space_Chickens_in_Space)*.

From 2015 to 2022, Allsop served as Head of Comedy and Entertainment for [Spelthorne Community Television](/wiki/Spelthorne_Community_Television) and was a producer on the [Showtime](/wiki/Showtime_(TV_network)) television series *[Who Is America](/wiki/Who_Is_America)*. Williams wrote an episode for the [BBC](/wiki/BBC) series *[Famalam](/wiki/Famalam)* in 2020.

# 28th Tony Awards

## Infobox

- **Date:** April 21, 1974
- **Location:** Shubert Theatre, New York City, New York
- **Hosted by:** Peter Falk, Florence Henderson, Robert Preston and Cicely Tyson
- **Network:** ABC

The **28th Annual Tony Awards** ceremony was held on April 21, 1974, at the [Shubert Theatre](/wiki/Shubert_Theatre_(Broadway)) in New York City, and broadcast by [ABC](/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company) television. Hosts were [Peter Falk](/wiki/Peter_Falk), [Florence Henderson](/wiki/Florence_Henderson), [Robert Preston](/wiki/Robert_Preston_(actor)) and [Cicely Tyson](/wiki/Cicely_Tyson). The theme was "Homecoming", where stars from TV and film returned to Broadway to help present the awards or perform.

## Eligibility

Shows that opened on Broadway during the 1973–1974 season before March 11, 1974 are eligible.

## Special Tony awards

- [Liza Minnelli](/wiki/Liza_Minnelli), for adding lustre to the Broadway season
- [Bette Midler](/wiki/Bette_Midler), for adding lustre to the Broadway season
- [Peter Cook](/wiki/Peter_Cook) and [Dudley Moore](/wiki/Dudley_Moore), co-stars and authors of Good Evening
- *[A Moon for the Misbegotten](/wiki/A_Moon_for_the_Misbegotten)*, an outstanding dramatic revival of an American classic. Produced by Lester Osterman, Elliott Martin and Richard Hurner
- *[Candide](/wiki/Candide_(operetta))*, an outstanding contribution to the artistic development of the musical theatre. Produced by Chelsea Theatre Group, Harold Prince and Ruth Mitchell
- [Actors' Equity Association](/wiki/Actors%27_Equity_Association)
- [Theatre Development Fund](/wiki/Theatre_Development_Fund)
- [John F. Wharton](/wiki/John_F._Wharton_(lawyer)), veteran theatrical attorney
- Harold Friedlander, the industry's foremost printing expert

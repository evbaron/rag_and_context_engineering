The **1997 NFL draft** was the procedure by which [National Football League](/wiki/National_Football_League) teams selected amateur [college football](/wiki/College_football) players. It is officially known as the NFL Annual Player Selection Meeting. The [draft](/wiki/NFL_draft) was held April 19–20, [1997](/wiki/1997_NFL_season), at the [Paramount Theatre](/wiki/The_Theater_at_Madison_Square_Garden) at [Madison Square Garden](/wiki/Madison_Square_Garden) in [New York City](/wiki/New_York_City), New York. No teams chose to claim any players in the [supplemental draft](/wiki/NFL_draft#Supplemental_draft) that year.

This draft was notable for its high-profile [offensive linemen](/wiki/Offensive_linemen). The first overall selection was [Orlando Pace](/wiki/Orlando_Pace), who appeared in seven consecutive [Pro Bowls](/wiki/Pro_Bowl) from 2000 to 2006 and was inducted to the [Hall of Fame](/wiki/Pro_Football_Hall_of_Fame) in 2016. [Tarik Glenn](/wiki/Tarik_Glenn) was selected 19th overall and was also named to three Pro Bowls. [Walter Jones](/wiki/Walter_Jones_(American_football)), who made nine Pro Bowls (including eight consecutive from 2001 to 2008), was a seven time [All-Pro](/wiki/All-Pro) and was inducted to the [Hall of Fame](/wiki/Pro_Football_Hall_of_Fame) in 2014, was selected sixth overall. Others include [Chris Naeole](/wiki/Chris_Naeole), [Dan Neil](/wiki/Dan_Neil_(American_football)), [Ryan Tucker](/wiki/Ryan_Tucker), [Jeff Mitchell](/wiki/Jeff_Mitchell), [Mike Flynn](/wiki/Mike_Flynn_(American_football)) and [Joe Andruzzi](/wiki/Joe_Andruzzi).

The 1997 draft is also known for its running backs. [Warrick Dunn](/wiki/Warrick_Dunn), [Corey Dillon](/wiki/Corey_Dillon) and [Tiki Barber](/wiki/Tiki_Barber) each rushed for over 10,000 yards in their careers, and [Antowain Smith](/wiki/Antowain_Smith) and [Duce Staley](/wiki/Duce_Staley) all enjoyed productive seasons in the NFL. This draft is also well known for its undrafted Pro Bowl players, including [Jake Delhomme](/wiki/Jake_Delhomme), [Priest Holmes](/wiki/Priest_Holmes) and [Pat Williams](/wiki/Pat_Williams_(American_football)).

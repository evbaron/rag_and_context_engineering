**Hiraka Station** (平賀駅, *Hiraka-eki*) is a [railway station](/wiki/Railway_station) in [Hirakawa, Aomori](/wiki/Hirakawa,_Aomori), Japan, operated by the [private railway](/wiki/Private_railway) operator [Kōnan Railway Company](/wiki/K%C5%8Dnan_Railway_Company).

## Lines

Hiraka Station is served by the [Kōnan Railway Kōnan Line](/wiki/K%C5%8Dnan_Railway_K%C5%8Dnan_Line), and lies 7.5 kilometers from the northern terminus of the line at [Hirosaki](/wiki/Hirosaki_Station),

## Station layout

Hiraka Station has a two opposed [side platforms](/wiki/Side_platform) with an elevated station building; however, one of the platforms has not been in use since the discontinuation of the [Kuroishi Line](/wiki/K%C5%8Dnan_Railway_Kuroishi_Line) in 1998. The station is staffed and also serves as the operational headquarters for the Kōnan Line.

## History

Hiraka Station was opened on September 7, 1927. Freight operations were discontinued in 1984. Wicket gate operation was turned over to a separate company ([*kan'i itaku*](/wiki/Kan%27i_itaku_station)) in October 1985. A new station building was completed in December 1986. On June 12, 2007, an accident occurred at Hiraka Station when a Kuroishi-bound train derailed.

# 2017 Billboard Music Awards

## Infobox

- **Date:** May 21, 2017 ( 2017-05-21 )
- **Location:** T-Mobile Arena, Las Vegas, Nevada, United States
- **Hosted by:** Ludacris Vanessa Hudgens
- **Most wins:** Drake (13)
- **Most nominations:** The Chainsmokers and Drake (22 each)
- **Network:** ABC
- **Runtime:** 130 minutes
- **Viewership:** 8.70 million

The 2017 [*Billboard* Music Awards](/wiki/Billboard_Music_Awards) ceremony was held at the [T-Mobile Arena](/wiki/T-Mobile_Arena) in [Las Vegas](/wiki/Las_Vegas), [Nevada](/wiki/Nevada) on May 21, 2017. The list of nominees was announced on April 10, 2017. Non-televised awards were announced on *Billboard* website the same day as the main ceremony.

# Tati Gabrielle

**Tatiana Gabrielle Hobson** (born January 25, 1996), known professionally as **Tati Gabrielle**, is an American actress. She is known for her roles as Gaia on [The CW](/wiki/The_CW) science fiction television series *[The 100](/wiki/The_100_(TV_series))*, Prudence on the [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) original series  *[Chilling Adventures of Sabrina](/wiki/Chilling_Adventures_of_Sabrina_(TV_series))*, [Marienne Bellamy](/wiki/Marienne_Bellamy) on the Netflix series [*You*](/wiki/You_(TV_series)), and [Nora](/wiki/Characters_of_The_Last_of_Us_(TV_series)#Nora) in the [HBO](/wiki/HBO) post-apocalyptic drama series *[The Last of Us](/wiki/The_Last_of_Us_(TV_series))*. She also provided the voice of Willow Park on the [Disney Channel](/wiki/Disney_Channel) animated series *[The Owl House](/wiki/The_Owl_House)*, and played Jo Braddock in the 2022 film *[Uncharted](/wiki/Uncharted_(film))* and Hannah Kim on the Netflix series [*Kaleidoscope*](/wiki/Kaleidoscope_(American_TV_series)).

## Early life and education

Gabrielle was born on January 25, 1996, in San Francisco, California. She is one of three children born to Traci (née Hewitt) and Terry Hobson. Her father is African American, and her mother was born in South Korea to a Korean woman and African American soldier. At age four, her mother was adopted by an African American military family that was stationed in Japan at the time.

Gabrielle began acting in the third grade, in an adaptation of [Lemony Snicket](/wiki/Lemony_Snicket)'s *[A Series of Unfortunate Events](/wiki/A_Series_of_Unfortunate_Events)*, where she played the main role of Lemony Snicket. She auditioned for [Oakland School for the Arts](/wiki/Oakland_School_for_the_Arts) in middle school and was accepted into their theatre program. She has performed in and directed several productions and has won multiple awards for her work at various theatre festivals, including the [Edinburgh Festival Fringe](/wiki/Edinburgh_Festival_Fringe) in Scotland.

Gabrielle graduated from [Oakland School for the Arts](/wiki/Oakland_School_for_the_Arts) with a 3.7 GPA. She moved to Atlanta after high school to attend [Spelman College](/wiki/Spelman_College), where she majored in drama and French.

## Career

Gabrielle's first credit was under the name Tatiana Hobson, in the 2014 short film *To Stay the Sword*. In 2015, Gabrielle moved to Los Angeles. She starred in the 2015 short film *Tatterdemalion* and the 2016 television film *Just Jenna* as Monique.

Her first credit as Tati Gabrielle came in 2016 for her guest role as Wacky Jacky in the "[Tightrope of Doom](/wiki/List_of_K.C._Undercover_episodes#ep41)" episode of the [Disney Channel](/wiki/Disney_Channel) comedy series *[K.C. Undercover](/wiki/K.C._Undercover)*. Later that year, Gabrielle also starred in [Nickelodeon](/wiki/Nickelodeon)'s *[The Thundermans](/wiki/The_Thundermans)* in the episode "[Stealing Home](/wiki/List_of_The_Thundermans_episodes#ep67)" as Hacksaw.

In 2017, Gabrielle landed her first recurring role as [Gaia](/wiki/List_of_The_100_characters#Recurring_cast) in [The CW](/wiki/The_CW) apocalyptic drama *[The 100](/wiki/The_100_(TV_series))*. That same year, she was featured in an episode titled "Bob" of the [Hulu](/wiki/Hulu) anthology series *[Dimension 404](/wiki/Dimension_404)*, where she portrayed Amanda's sister. Gabrielle also voiced Addie in the 2017 film *[The Emoji Movie](/wiki/The_Emoji_Movie)*, her first major film role, and recurred in the second season of Hulu's *[Freakish](/wiki/Freakish_(TV_series))* as Birdie and on the [TBS](/wiki/TBS_(U.S._TV_channel)) animated series *[Tarantula](/wiki/Tarantula_(TV_series))*.

In March 2018, Gabrielle was cast as a series regular in the role of Prudence in the [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) original series, *[Chilling Adventures of Sabrina](/wiki/Chilling_Adventures_of_Sabrina)*. In 2020, Gabrielle was cast as [Marienne Bellamy](/wiki/Marienne_Bellamy) on the third season of the Netflix psychological thriller series *[You](/wiki/You_(TV_series))*, and reprised her role on the fourth and fifth seasons. In 2022, Gabrielle starred as the villain Jo Braddock in the film *[Uncharted](/wiki/Uncharted_(film))*. In May 2023, Gabrielle was cast as [Jade](/wiki/Jade_(Mortal_Kombat)) in [*Mortal Kombat II*](/wiki/Mortal_Kombat_II_(film)). In March 2024, Gabrielle was cast as [Nora](/wiki/Characters_of_The_Last_of_Us_(TV_series)#Nora) in the [second season](/wiki/The_Last_of_Us_season_2) of the [HBO](/wiki/HBO) post-apocalyptic drama series *[The Last of Us](/wiki/The_Last_of_Us_(TV_series))*. She was promoted to a series regular for [the third season](/wiki/The_Last_of_Us_season_3) in March 2026.

In 2024, it was announced that she will portray Jordan A. Mun in the upcoming video game *[Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet](/wiki/Intergalactic:_The_Heretic_Prophet)*, developed by [Naughty Dog](/wiki/Naughty_Dog).

**Enzo Nahuel Copetti** (born 16 January 1996) is an Argentine professional [footballer](/wiki/Association_football) who plays as a [winger](/wiki/Winger_(association_football)) or [second striker](/wiki/Second_striker) for [Rosario Central](/wiki/Rosario_Central).

## Career

Copetti joined [Atlético de Rafaela](/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_de_Rafaela)'s academy during 2012–13. He made the step into senior football in 2016, as he made his debut on 2 June in a [Copa Argentina](/wiki/Copa_Argentina) match against [Ferro Carril Oeste](/wiki/Ferro_Carril_Oeste). In April 2017, Copetti made his first appearance in the [Primera División](/wiki/Argentine_Primera_Divisi%C3%B3n) off the bench against [Huracán](/wiki/Club_Atl%C3%A9tico_Hurac%C3%A1n). Two further appearances - as a starter - followed in Rafaela's relegation season of [2016–17](/wiki/2016%E2%80%9317_Argentine_Primera_Divisi%C3%B3n). He scored his first senior goal in a cup victory over [Defensores de Belgrano](/wiki/Defensores_de_Belgrano) on 24 July 2018. Copetti left the club after the [2020](/wiki/2020_Primera_Nacional) campaign, which he ended with five goals in nine games; he notably scored twice over [Tigre](/wiki/Club_Atl%C3%A9tico_Tigre) on 7 December 2020.

On 16 February 2021, Copetti was loaned to Primera División side [Racing Club](/wiki/Racing_Club_de_Avellaneda); penning terms until the succeeding December, including a purchase option. He scored on his debut against [Aldosivi](/wiki/Club_Atl%C3%A9tico_Aldosivi) four days later, prior to netting again in his fourth overall appearance for them on 8 March versus [Rosario Central](/wiki/Rosario_Central). At the end of the year, Racing made use of the purchase option, with Copetti signing a permanently deal until the end of 2024.

On 6 November 2022, Copetti became champion of [Trofeo de Campeones de la Liga Profesional](/wiki/Trofeo_de_Campeones_de_la_Liga_Profesional) after beating Boca 2–1. He was the striker with most goals from Racing in all 2022.

On 11 January 2023, Copetti signed 3-year deal with [Major League Soccer](/wiki/Major_League_Soccer) side [Charlotte FC](/wiki/Charlotte_FC).

Copetti signed with [Argentine Primera División](/wiki/Argentine_Primera_Divisi%C3%B3n) side [Rosario Central](/wiki/Rosario_Central) on 16 May 2024.

## Style of play

Copetti is primarily a [winger](/wiki/Winger_(association_football)) or [second striker](/wiki/Second_striker), though is capable of playing as a [centre-forward](/wiki/Centre-forward) and [midfielder](/wiki/Midfielder).

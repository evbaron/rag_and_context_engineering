**Novake** (pronounced [[nɔˈʋaːkɛ]](/wiki/Help:IPA/Slovene)) is a [village](/wiki/Village) on the left bank of the [Dravinja River](/wiki/Dravinja) in the [Municipality of Poljčane](/wiki/Municipality_of_Polj%C4%8Dane) in northeastern [Slovenia](/wiki/Slovenia). The area belongs to the traditional region of [Styria](/wiki/Lower_Styria). It is now included with the rest of the municipality in the [Drava Statistical Region](/wiki/Drava_Statistical_Region).

**Albertville** (French pronunciation: [[albɛʁvil]](/wiki/Help:IPA/French) [ⓘ](/wiki/File:Fr-Albertville.ogg); [Arpitan](/wiki/Franco-Proven%C3%A7al): *Arbèrtvile*) is a [subprefecture](/wiki/Subprefectures_in_France) of the [Savoie](/wiki/Savoie) [department](/wiki/Departments_of_France) in the [Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes](/wiki/Auvergne-Rh%C3%B4ne-Alpes) [region](/wiki/Regions_of_France) in southeastern [France](/wiki/France).
It is best known for hosting the [1992 Winter Olympics](/wiki/1992_Winter_Olympics) and [Paralympics](/wiki/1992_Winter_Paralympics). In 2022, the [commune](/wiki/Communes_of_France) had a population of 19,706; its [urban area](/wiki/Urban_unit) had 40,715 inhabitants.

## Geography

Albertville is one of two [subprefectures](/wiki/Subprefectures_in_France) of the [Savoie](/wiki/Savoie) department, alongside [Saint-Jean-de-Maurienne](/wiki/Saint-Jean-de-Maurienne).

Albertville is situated on the river [Arly](/wiki/Arly), close to the confluence with the river [Isère](/wiki/Is%C3%A8re_(river)). Its altitude ranges from 345 to 2,037 metres (1,132 to 6,683 ft). Nearby [mountains](/wiki/Mountain) include: Belle Étoile, [Dent de Cons](/wiki/Dent_de_Cons), [Négresse](/wiki/N%C3%A9gresse), [Roche Pourrie](/wiki/Roche_Pourrie), Mirantin, [Pointe de la Grande Journée](/wiki/Pointe_de_la_Grande_Journ%C3%A9e), Chaîne du Grand Arc. Nearby [mountain ranges](/wiki/Mountain_range) include the [Bauges](/wiki/Bauges), the [Beaufortain](/wiki/Beaufortain) and the beginning of the [Vanoise](/wiki/Vanoise_Massif).

## History

Medieval town of Conflans, Albertville

The modern city of Albertville was formed in 1836 by King [Charles Albert of Sardinia](/wiki/Charles_Albert_of_Sardinia), who merged the medieval town of Conflans, which had buildings dating to the 14th century, with the town of L'Hôpital. Since then, Albertville has fostered trade between France, Italy and Switzerland. Industries such as [paper mills](/wiki/Paper_mills) and [hydroelectricity](/wiki/Hydroelectricity) are found along its river.

The [1992 Winter Olympics](/wiki/1992_Winter_Olympics) were organised in the Savoie region, with Albertville hosting it. Some of the sports venues were later adapted for other uses. Some sports venues still remain, such as the [ice rink](/wiki/Ice_rink), [La halle de glace Olympique](/wiki/La_halle_de_glace_Olympique), designed by the architect Jacques Kalisz. Despite this, the town remains more industrial than touristic.

In 2003, the town was labelled a "[Town of Art and History](/wiki/French_Towns_and_Lands_of_Art_and_History)".

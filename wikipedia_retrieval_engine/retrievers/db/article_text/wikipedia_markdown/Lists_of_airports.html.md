# Lists of airports

An **airport** is an [aerodrome](/wiki/Aerodrome) with facilities for flights to take off and land. Airports often have facilities to store and maintain aircraft, and a [control tower](/wiki/Air_traffic_control#Airport_traffic_control_tower). An airport consists of a [landing area](/wiki/Landing_area), which comprises an aerially accessible open space including at least one operationally active surface such as a [runway](/wiki/Runway) for a plane to take off or a [helipad](/wiki/Helipad), and often includes adjacent utility buildings such as control towers, [hangars](/wiki/Hangar) and [terminals](/wiki/Airport_terminal).

An airport with a helipad for rotorcraft but no runway is called a [heliport](/wiki/Heliport). An airport for use by [seaplanes](/wiki/Seaplane) and [amphibious aircraft](/wiki/Amphibious_aircraft) is called a [seaplane base](/wiki/Seaplane_base). Such a base typically includes a stretch of open water for [takeoffs](/wiki/Takeoff) and [landings](/wiki/Landing), and [seaplane](/wiki/Seaplane) docks for tying-up. An [international airport](/wiki/International_airport) has additional facilities for [customs](/wiki/Customs) and [immigration](/wiki/Immigration).

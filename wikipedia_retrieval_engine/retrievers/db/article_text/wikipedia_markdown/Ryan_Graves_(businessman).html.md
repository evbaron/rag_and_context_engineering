**Ryan Graves** (born 1983) is an American [billionaire](/wiki/Billionaire), a former CEO and member of the board of directors of [Uber](/wiki/Uber), and a current member of the board of directors of [Charity: Water](/wiki/Charity:_Water), Pachama, and [Metromile](/wiki/Metromile). He is also the CEO of Saltwater, his [family office](/wiki/Family_office).

## Early life and education

Graves was born in [San Diego](/wiki/San_Diego), California and grew up near the beach. He attended [Horizon Christian Academy](/wiki/Horizon_Christian_Academy), where he competed for the high school surf team. In 2006, he graduated with an economics degree from [Miami University](/wiki/Miami_University) in Ohio, where he was a member of the [water polo](/wiki/Water_polo) club team and the [Beta Theta Pi](/wiki/Beta_Theta_Pi) [fraternity](/wiki/Fraternity).

## Career

Graves worked for [General Electric](/wiki/General_Electric). He eventually got an [internship](/wiki/Internship) at [Foursquare](/wiki/Foursquare_(company)) by pretending to work for them after they turned him down for a job.

In February 2010, Graves became the first employee of Uber, receiving the job by responding "here's a tip. email me :)" to a post by [Travis Kalanick](/wiki/Travis_Kalanick) on [Twitter](/wiki/Twitter). Graves started out as general manager and was named [CEO](/wiki/CEO) shortly after the launch. In December 2010, Kalanick succeeded Graves as CEO and Graves became [chief operating officer](/wiki/Chief_operating_officer) (COO).

By 2019, Graves owned 31.9 million shares in Uber. That year, he resigned from the board of directors of Uber.

In 2018, Graves founded Saltwater, his [family office](/wiki/Family_office), focused on investments in technology businesses.

In February 2021, Graves made a $50 million investment in and became a member of the board of directors of [Metromile](/wiki/Metromile), a pay-per-mile auto insurer.

## Personal life

Graves is married to Molly Graves, his college sweetheart and a kindergarten teacher. They have four children, all sons, and live on the island of [Kauaʻi](/wiki/Kaua%CA%BBi), [Hawaii](/wiki/Hawaii). He is a supporter of the [San Diego Padres](/wiki/San_Diego_Padres) baseball team.

Graves serves on the board of directors of [Charity: Water](/wiki/Charity:_Water) and has pledged at least 1% of his net worth to the charity, which focuses on delivering clean water solutions to people in [developing nations](/wiki/Developing_nations).

# List of songs recorded by Christina Aguilera

Aguilera performing on the [Back to Basics Tour](/wiki/Back_to_Basics_Tour) in December 2006

American singer [Christina Aguilera](/wiki/Christina_Aguilera)'s recording career began in 1994 at the age of thirteen, following the cancellation of *[The Mickey Mouse Club](/wiki/The_Mickey_Mouse_Club)*. Between 1994 and 1995, Aguilera recorded a series of songs, which were eventually released as a demo album under the name *[Just Be Free](/wiki/Just_Be_Free)* (2001). She also moved to [Japan](/wiki/Japan) and recorded a [duet](/wiki/Duet) with Japanese singer [Keizo Nakanishi](/wiki/Keizo_Nakanishi) titled "All I Wanna Do". She returned to the [United States](/wiki/United_States) and in 1998, she signed a contract with [RCA Records](/wiki/RCA_Records) and was chosen to perform the song "[Reflection](/wiki/Reflection_(song))" for the [soundtrack](/wiki/Mulan_(1998_soundtrack)) of the 1998 [Disney](/wiki/Walt_Disney_Animation_Studios) animated feature *[Mulan](/wiki/Mulan_(1998_film))*. She followed this with albums *[Christina Aguilera](/wiki/Christina_Aguilera_(album))* (1999), *[Mi Reflejo](/wiki/Mi_Reflejo)* (2000), *[My Kind of Christmas](/wiki/My_Kind_of_Christmas)* (2000), *[Stripped](/wiki/Stripped_(Christina_Aguilera_album))* (2002), *[Back to Basics](/wiki/Back_to_Basics_(Christina_Aguilera_album))* (2006), *[Bionic](/wiki/Bionic_(Christina_Aguilera_album))* (2010), *[Lotus](/wiki/Lotus_(Christina_Aguilera_album))* (2012), *[Liberation](/wiki/Liberation_(Christina_Aguilera_album))* (2018) and *[Aguilera](/wiki/Aguilera_(album))* (2022).

She has recorded and released many songs throughout her career, topping the US [*Billboard* Hot 100](/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100) various times with the songs "[Genie in a Bottle](/wiki/Genie_in_a_Bottle)", "[What a Girl Wants](/wiki/What_a_Girl_Wants_(Christina_Aguilera_song))", "[Come on Over Baby (All I Want is You)](/wiki/Come_on_Over_Baby_(All_I_Want_is_You))", "[Lady Marmalade](/wiki/Lady_Marmalade)" and "[Moves Like Jagger](/wiki/Moves_Like_Jagger)". She has also had various songs reach the top ten on the same chart including "[I Turn to You](/wiki/I_Turn_to_You_(All-4-One_song))", "[Beautiful](/wiki/Beautiful_(Christina_Aguilera_song))", "[Ain't No Other Man](/wiki/Ain%27t_No_Other_Man)", "[Keeps Gettin' Better](/wiki/Keeps_Gettin%27_Better)", "[Feel This Moment](/wiki/Feel_This_Moment)" and "[Say Something](/wiki/Say_Something_(A_Great_Big_World_song))". Her songs have been inspired by a wide variety of [genres](/wiki/Music_genre) including [pop](/wiki/Pop_music), [R&B](/wiki/R%26B), [soul](/wiki/Soul_music), [hip hop](/wiki/Hip_hop_music), [rock](/wiki/Rock_music), [Latin](/wiki/Latin_music_(genre)), [jazz](/wiki/Jazz_music), [electronic](/wiki/Electronic_music), [dance-pop](/wiki/Dance-pop), amongst others. Her song themes have also been [diverse and controversial](/wiki/Cultural_impact_of_Christina_Aguilera#Controversial_themes) with her song "[Dirrty](/wiki/Dirrty)" facing controversy for its sexual nature. The song also reached number one in the [United Kingdom](/wiki/United_Kingdom). Other songs of Aguilera which have charted on the Hot 100 include, "[The Christmas Song](/wiki/The_Christmas_Song#Other_versions)", "[Fighter](/wiki/Fighter_(Christina_Aguilera_song))", "[Can't Hold Us Down](/wiki/Can%27t_Hold_Us_Down)", "[The Voice Within](/wiki/The_Voice_Within)", "[Car Wash](/wiki/Car_Wash_(song)#Christina_Aguilera_and_Missy_Elliott_version)", "[Hurt](/wiki/Hurt_(Christina_Aguilera_song))", "[Candyman](/wiki/Candyman_(Christina_Aguilera_song))", "[Not Myself Tonight](/wiki/Not_Myself_Tonight)" and "[Your Body](/wiki/Your_Body_(Christina_Aguilera_song))".

# Małgorzata Rożniecka

**Małgorzata Rożniecka** (born 1978, in [Szczecin](/wiki/Szczecin)) is a Polish model and beauty queen who won [Miss International 2001](/wiki/Miss_International_2001).

Her 2001 victory broke the four-year monopoly of [Latin American](/wiki/Latin_America) countries of the crown. She is 1.78 m tall.

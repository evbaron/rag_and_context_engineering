# List of Walt Disney Pictures films

For a broader list of Disney films, see [Lists of Walt Disney Studios films](/wiki/Lists_of_Walt_Disney_Studios_films).

This is a **list of films** produced by and released under the [Walt Disney Pictures](/wiki/Walt_Disney_Pictures) banner (known as that since 1983, with *[Never Cry Wolf](/wiki/Never_Cry_Wolf_(film))* as its first release) and films released before that under the former name of the [parent company](/wiki/Parent_company), [Walt Disney Productions](/wiki/Walt_Disney_Productions) (1929–1983). Most films listed here were distributed theatrically in the United States by the company's distribution division, [Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures](/wiki/Walt_Disney_Studios_Motion_Pictures), formerly known as Buena Vista Film Distribution Company (1953–1960), Buena Vista Distribution Company (1960–1987) and Buena Vista Pictures Distribution (1987–2007). The Disney features produced before *[The Living Desert](/wiki/The_Living_Desert)* (1953) were originally distributed by [United Artists](/wiki/United_Artists) and [RKO Radio Pictures](/wiki/RKO_Radio_Pictures), and are now distributed by Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures. Some films produced by Walt Disney Pictures are also released through the parent company's streaming service, [Disney+](/wiki/Disney%2B).

This list is organized by release date and includes live-action feature films (including theatrical, direct-to-video and streaming releases), animated feature films (including films developed and produced by [Walt Disney Animation Studios](/wiki/Walt_Disney_Animation_Studios) and [Pixar Animation Studios](/wiki/Pixar_Animation_Studios)) and documentary films (including titles from the *[True-Life Adventures](/wiki/True-Life_Adventures)* series and films produced by the [Disneynature](/wiki/Disneynature) label). For an exclusive list of animated films released by Walt Disney Pictures and its previous entities see *[List of Disney theatrical animated feature films](/wiki/List_of_Disney_theatrical_animated_feature_films)* and *[List of Disney feature-length home entertainment releases § Animated films](/wiki/List_of_Disney_feature-length_home_entertainment_releases#Animated_films_1994–Presents)*.

This list is only for films released under the main Disney banner. It does not include films produced or released by other existing, defunct or divested labels or subsidiaries owned by [Walt Disney Studios](/wiki/Walt_Disney_Studios_(division)) (i.e. [Marvel Studios](/wiki/Marvel_Studios), [Lucasfilm](/wiki/Lucasfilm), [20th Century Studios](/wiki/20th_Century_Studios), [Searchlight Pictures](/wiki/Searchlight_Pictures), [Fox 2000 Pictures](/wiki/Fox_2000_Pictures), [Touchstone Pictures](/wiki/Touchstone_Pictures), [Hollywood Pictures](/wiki/Hollywood_Pictures), [National Geographic Documentary Films](/wiki/National_Geographic_Society), [Miramax Films](/wiki/Miramax), [Dimension Films](/wiki/Dimension_Films), [ESPN Films](/wiki/ESPN_Films), etc.) unless they are credited as co-production partners, nor any direct-to-video releases unless they were produced under the main Disney banner, TV films, theatrical re-releases or films originally released by other non-Disney studios.

All films listed are [theatrical releases](/wiki/Art_release) and/or American-based films unless specified.

- A **‡** symbol signifies a [direct-to-video](/wiki/Direct-to-video) or [streaming](/wiki/Streaming_media) release exclusively through [Disney+](/wiki/Disney%2B).
- A **†** symbol signifies a [premium video on demand](/wiki/Video_on_demand#Premium) release through Disney+.
- A **§** symbol signifies a [simultaneous release](/wiki/Simultaneous_release) to theatres and on premium video on demand.
- A **\*** symbol signifies a film not produced in the United States, but rather in another country.

# United States presidential elections in Alaska

Since [Alaska](/wiki/Alaska)'s [admission to the Union](/wiki/Admission_to_the_Union) on January 3, 1959, it has participated in 17 [United States presidential elections](/wiki/United_States_presidential_election), always having 3 [electoral votes](/wiki/United_States_Electoral_College). In the [1960 presidential election](/wiki/1960_United_States_presidential_election), Alaska was narrowly won by the [Republican Party](/wiki/Republican_Party_(United_States))'s candidate and incumbent [vice president](/wiki/Vice_President_of_the_United_States) [Richard Nixon](/wiki/Richard_Nixon), defeating the [Democratic Party](/wiki/Democratic_Party_(United_States))'s candidate [John F. Kennedy](/wiki/John_F._Kennedy) by a margin of just 1.88% (1,144 votes). In the [1964 presidential election](/wiki/1964_United_States_presidential_election), the Democratic Party's candidate [Lyndon B. Johnson](/wiki/Lyndon_B._Johnson) won Alaska in a national Democratic [landslide victory](/wiki/Landslide_victory). Since the 1964 election, Alaska has been won by the Republican Party in every presidential election. However, no Republican candidate has gotten 55% of the statewide vote since 2008. [Donald Trump](/wiki/Donald_Trump) received 54.5% in 2024.

[Ronald Reagan](/wiki/Ronald_Reagan), the Republican candidate in the [1984 presidential election](/wiki/1984_United_States_presidential_election), won Alaska by 36.78%, which remains the largest margin of victory in the state's history. [Ross Perot](/wiki/Ross_Perot), the [independent candidate](/wiki/Independent_politician) in the [1992 presidential election](/wiki/1992_United_States_presidential_election), received the highest vote share (28.43%) ever won by a [third-party candidate](/wiki/List_of_third_party_and_independent_performances_in_United_States_elections) in Alaska. Various news organizations have characterized Alaska as a safe Republican state. No Republican has won the presidency without carrying Alaska since its statehood in 1959 due to [Lyndon B. Johnson](/wiki/Lyndon_B._Johnson) being the only Democratic candidate to ever carry the state. Alaska is tied with [Idaho](/wiki/Idaho), [Utah](/wiki/Utah), [Wyoming](/wiki/Wyoming), [North Dakota](/wiki/North_Dakota), [South Dakota](/wiki/South_Dakota), [Nebraska](/wiki/Nebraska), [Kansas](/wiki/Kansas) and [Oklahoma](/wiki/Oklahoma) for the longest Republican voting streak for any state in recent political history, from 1968 to present.

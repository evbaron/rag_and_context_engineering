**Roberto Cid Subervi** (Spanish pronunciation: [[roˈβeɾto ˈsið suˈβeɾβi]](/wiki/Help:IPA/Spanish); born 30 August 1993) is a [Dominican](/wiki/Dominican_Republic)–American tennis player.

Cid Subervi has a career high [ATP](/wiki/Association_of_Tennis_Professionals) singles ranking of 211 achieved on 14 September 2020. He also has a career high ATP doubles ranking of 262, achieved on 19 October 2020. Cid Subervi has won 4 [ITF](/wiki/International_Tennis_Federation) singles titles and 1 [ITF](/wiki/International_Tennis_Federation) doubles title.

Cid Subervi has represented [Dominican Republic](/wiki/Dominican_Republic_Davis_Cup_team) at [Davis Cup](/wiki/Davis_Cup), where he has a win–loss record of 4–11.

## College career

In 2016, Cid Subervi was ranked no. 2 in the nation in the NCAA Div 1 singles rankings in his last year at University of South Florida. Partnering with Sasha Gozun, he was ranked no. 6 in the nation in the NCAA Div 1 doubles rankings in 2015.

He reached the quarterfinals of the [2016 NCAA Division I Men's Tennis Championships](/wiki/2016_NCAA_Division_I_Tennis_Championships) in singles, losing to the eventual champion [Mackenzie McDonald](/wiki/Mackenzie_McDonald) from UCLA, and he had wins over [Dominik Kopfer](/wiki/Dominik_Koepfer) from Tulane University, [Cameron Norrie](/wiki/Cameron_Norrie) from Texas Christian University, and [Ryan Shane](/wiki/Ryan_Shane) from University of Virginia. In 2014, he made the quarterfinals of the [2014 NCAA Division I Men's Tennis Championships](/wiki/2014_NCAA_Division_I_Tennis_Championships) in singles, defeating No. 1 player in the nation [Clay Thompson](/wiki/Clay_Thompson) from UCLA.

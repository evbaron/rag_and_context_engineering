# Please Please Please (Sabrina Carpenter song)

"**Please Please Please**" is a song by American singer [Sabrina Carpenter](/wiki/Sabrina_Carpenter) from her sixth studio album, *[Short n' Sweet](/wiki/Short_n%27_Sweet)* (2024). It was released through [Island Records](/wiki/Island_Records) on June 6, 2024, as the second single from the album. Produced by [Jack Antonoff](/wiki/Jack_Antonoff), it was written by Antonoff, Carpenter, and [Amy Allen](/wiki/Amy_Allen_(songwriter)). It is a [yacht rock](/wiki/Yacht_rock), [country pop](/wiki/Country_pop), [alternative country](/wiki/Alternative_country), [disco-pop](/wiki/Disco-pop), and [synth-pop](/wiki/Synth-pop) song, and sees Carpenter discuss her fears that her boyfriend will let his bad-boy reputation overshadow and destroy both their relationship and her pride and embarrass her.

The song received acclaim from music critics upon release. "Please Please Please" peaked at the top of the [*Billboard* Hot 100](/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100), becoming her first number-one single on the chart. Outside of the United States, "Please Please Please" topped the charts in Australia, Ireland, New Zealand, Norway, and the United Kingdom. It peaked within the top ten in 18 other countries, including Canada, Denmark, the Netherlands, Portugal, and Sweden.

"Please Please Please" was nominated for [Song of the Year](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Song_of_the_Year) at the [67th Annual Grammy Awards](/wiki/67th_Annual_Grammy_Awards). A remix with [Dolly Parton](/wiki/Dolly_Parton) was released in February 2025, and promoted to US [country radio](/wiki/Country_radio).

## Background and release

Following the commercial success of "[Espresso](/wiki/Espresso_(song))", [Sabrina Carpenter](/wiki/Sabrina_Carpenter) announced her upcoming sixth studio album *Short n' Sweet* on June 3, 2024. She simultaneously promised an upcoming surprise for her fans, which would turn out to be the single. Only two days later, Carpenter took to social media to announce the song release and share a teaser of the accompanying music video featuring cinematic visuals set to showcase her acting skills. The clip shows her wearing a lacy red outfit in a robbery-like setting, as well as engaging in a romance with a criminal played by her then-boyfriend [Barry Keoghan](/wiki/Barry_Keoghan). "Please Please Please" has been labeled as a [pop](/wiki/Pop_music), [disco-pop](/wiki/Disco-pop), [synth-pop](/wiki/Synth-pop), [alternative country](/wiki/Alternative_country), [country pop](/wiki/Country_pop), and [yacht rock](/wiki/Yacht_rock) song.

In February 2025, Carpenter released a deluxe version of *Short n' Sweet* featuring a duet version of "Please Please Please" with American singer [Dolly Parton](/wiki/Dolly_Parton), which was promoted to [country radio](/wiki/Country_radio) in the US.

## Music video

Promotional poster for the music video

The music video for "Please Please Please" was filmed on May 23, 2024, in [Staten Island](/wiki/Staten_Island), [New York](/wiki/New_York_(state)), and serves as a sequel to the "[Espresso](/wiki/Espresso_(song))" music video released earlier in the year. The video explores a romantic dynamic between Carpenter and a convict, played by Irish actor [Barry Keoghan](/wiki/Barry_Keoghan) who was Carpenter's then-boyfriend. The prison scenes were shot in the [Arthur Kill Correctional Facility](/wiki/Arthur_Kill_Correctional_Facility), owned by [Broadway Stages](/wiki/Broadway_Stages).

The video continues from where "Espresso" ended and focuses on Carpenter's relationship with a chaotic love interest. The video's director, Bardia Zeinali, expanded on this idea, incorporating themes of role reversals and power dynamics, as well as recurring scenes such as Carpenter repeatedly picking up the love interest outside the same prison at different times. [Pamela Anderson](/wiki/Pamela_Anderson), [Tommy Lee](/wiki/Tommy_Lee), [Madonna](/wiki/Madonna), [Dennis Rodman](/wiki/Dennis_Rodman), [Quentin Tarantino](/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino), *[Natural Born Killers](/wiki/Natural_Born_Killers)* (1994), [Bonnie and Clyde](/wiki/Bonnie_and_Clyde), and *[Thelma & Louise](/wiki/Thelma_%26_Louise)* (1991) were cited as inspirations for the video. Cinematographer [Sean Price Williams](/wiki/Sean_Price_Williams) shot the video on film.

A second music video for the song, set to the duet version with Dolly Parton, was filmed on January 25, 2025, in front of a rear projection screen and co-directed by Carpenter alongside Williams. It was released on February 14, 2025, to coincide with the release of the deluxe edition of *Short n' Sweet*. The black and white vignette shows Carpenter and Parton driving a truck, having stolen a bag of money and kidnapped a man with the intention of burying him somewhere; evading the police while doing so.

## Commercial performance

On June 17, "Please Please Please" reached number one on the [*Billboard* Global 200](/wiki/Billboard_Global_200). On June 22, 2024, the song debuted at number two on the [*Billboard* Hot 100](/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100), making it Carpenter's highest debut on the chart and highest-charting single then. The following week, it reached the top spot of the chart, making it her first chart-topper in the United States.

In the United Kingdom, with "Please Please Please" at number one and her previous song "Espresso" at number two on the [UK Singles Chart](/wiki/UK_Singles_Chart), Carpenter became the first female artist to hold the top two positions of the chart for five weeks in a row and matched [Ed Sheeran](/wiki/Ed_Sheeran) as the only artist to hold this feat in general. On July 12, 2024 – for the week ending date July 18, 2024 – "Please Please Please" was dethroned from the top of the UK Singles Chart with "Espresso" returning to the summit. On July 26, 2024 – for the week ending date August 1, 2024 – "Please Please Please" dethroned "Espresso" from the top of the UK Singles Chart to return to the summit for a second time, with the former song being replaced at number two by "[Birds of a Feather](/wiki/Birds_of_a_Feather_(Billie_Eilish_song))" by [Billie Eilish](/wiki/Billie_Eilish), and the latter song falling to number nine on the chart.

## Live performances

Carpenter sang "Please Please Please" for the first time at the [Governors Ball Music Festival](/wiki/Governors_Ball_Music_Festival) on June 8, 2024. Later that month, she performed the song on the [Live Lounge](/wiki/Live_Lounge). At the [2024 MTV Video Music Awards](/wiki/2024_MTV_Video_Music_Awards), she performed the song as part of a medley with "[Taste](/wiki/Taste_(Sabrina_Carpenter_song))" and "[Espresso](/wiki/Espresso_(song))". *[Entertainment Weekly](/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly)*'s Lester Fabian Brathwaite and *[Vogue](/wiki/Vogue_(magazine))*'s Hannah Jackson thought Carpenter channeled [Britney Spears](/wiki/Britney_Spears), [Marilyn Monroe](/wiki/Marilyn_Monroe), and [Madonna](/wiki/Madonna) on her [Blond Ambition World Tour](/wiki/Blond_Ambition_World_Tour) (1990). "Please Please Please" was included on the permanent setlist of Carpenter's sixth headlining tour, the [Short n' Sweet Tour](/wiki/Short_n%27_Sweet_Tour) (2024–2025). Carpenter performed "Please Please Please" with American singer-songwriter [Taylor Swift](/wiki/Taylor_Swift) at the second [New Orleans](/wiki/New_Orleans) show of [the Eras Tour](/wiki/The_Eras_Tour) (2023–2024), as an acoustic mashup with "Espresso" and "[Is It Over Now?](/wiki/Is_It_Over_Now%3F)" (2023). On February 2, 2025, Carpenter performed "Please Please Please" at the [67th Annual Grammy Awards](/wiki/67th_Annual_Grammy_Awards).

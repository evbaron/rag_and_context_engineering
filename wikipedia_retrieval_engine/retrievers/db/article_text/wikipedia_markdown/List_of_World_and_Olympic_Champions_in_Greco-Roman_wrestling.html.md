# List of World and Olympic Champions in Greco-Roman wrestling

Listed are [wrestlers](/wiki/Wrestling) who were World or Olympic Champions in [Greco-Roman wrestling](/wiki/Greco-Roman_wrestling). Greco-Roman wrestling competition was held at the first modern [Olympics](/wiki/Wrestling_at_the_Summer_Olympics) in 1896. The Greco-Roman [World Championships](/wiki/World_Wrestling_Championships) was first held in 1904. The World Championships takes place during non Olympic years. At the World Championships team scoring is kept, while no official team statistics are kept for the Olympics.

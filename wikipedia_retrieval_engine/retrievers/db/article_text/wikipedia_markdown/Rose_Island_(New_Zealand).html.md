**Rose Island** is an uninhabited island and, with an area of 121 [hectares](/wiki/Hectare), the fifth largest of the [Auckland Islands](/wiki/Auckland_Islands) group, a subantarctic chain that forms part of the [New Zealand Outlying Islands](/wiki/New_Zealand_Outlying_Islands).

It is located in the northeast of the group, in the mouth of [Port Ross](/wiki/Port_Ross), [Auckland Island](/wiki/Auckland_Island) and south west of the larger [Enderby Island](/wiki/Enderby_Island,_New_Zealand). It was stocked with rabbits and provided with a [boatshed](/wiki/Castaway_depot) for the relief of castaways during the nineteenth century. The rabbits have since been eradicated by the [Department of Conservation](/wiki/Department_of_Conservation_(New_Zealand)), and provides a secure home for a population of [Auckland Islands teal](/wiki/Auckland_Islands_teal). The vegetation on Rose Island contains [southern rātā](/wiki/Metrosideros_umbellata), and while much original vegetation has been destroyed, it is less modified than much of the Auckland Islands.

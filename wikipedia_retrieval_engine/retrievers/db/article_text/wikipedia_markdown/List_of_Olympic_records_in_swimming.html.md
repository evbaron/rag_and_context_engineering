# List of Olympic records in swimming

[Léon Marchand](/wiki/L%C3%A9on_Marchand) set Olympic records in four individual events at the 2024 Summer Olympics.

The [International Olympic Committee](/wiki/International_Olympic_Committee) recognises the fastest performances in pool-based [swimming events](/wiki/Swimming_at_the_Summer_Olympics) at the [Olympic Games](/wiki/Olympic_Games). Men's swimming has been part of the official program of the Summer Olympics since the Games' modern inception in 1896; it was not until 1912 that women's events were held. The swimming events at the [1896 Olympic Games](/wiki/1896_Olympic_Games) were held in a bay in the [Aegean Sea](/wiki/Aegean_Sea) with swimmers being required to swim to the shore—Hungarian swimmer [Alfréd Hajós](/wiki/Alfr%C3%A9d_Haj%C3%B3s) won two gold medals that year, saying "My will to live completely overcame my desire to win." The [1900 Summer Olympic Games](/wiki/1900_Summer_Olympic_Games) in Paris had the swimming events take place in the [River Seine](/wiki/River_Seine), and the events at the [1908 Summer Olympics](/wiki/1908_Summer_Olympics) were held in a 100-metre pool surrounded by an athletics track in the [White City Stadium](/wiki/White_City_Stadium) in London.

Races are held in four swimming categories: [freestyle](/wiki/Freestyle_swimming), [backstroke](/wiki/Backstroke), [breaststroke](/wiki/Breaststroke) and [butterfly](/wiki/Butterfly_stroke), over varying distances and in either individual or [relay race](/wiki/Relay_race) events. [Medley swimming](/wiki/Medley_swimming) races are also held, both individually and in relays, in which all four swimming categories are used. In the [Swimming at the 2024 Summer Olympics](/wiki/Swimming_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics) in Paris, both men and women competed in eighteen events in the pool. Of the 35 pool-based events, swimmers from the United States hold fifteen records, Australia five, France four, China, Canada and South Africa two each, and one each to Ireland, Great Britain, [Russian Olympic Committee](/wiki/Russian_Olympic_Committee_athletes_at_the_2020_Summer_Olympics), Hungary, and Sweden. Nineteen of the current Olympic records were set at the 2024 Summer Olympics, seven in 2020, five in 2016, one in 2012, and three in 2008.

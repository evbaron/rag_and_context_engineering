**Phantom Ship** is a small [island](/wiki/Island) in [Crater Lake](/wiki/Crater_Lake) in the [U.S. state](/wiki/U.S._state) of [Oregon](/wiki/Oregon). It is a natural [rock formation](/wiki/Rock_formations_in_the_United_States) pillar which derives its name from its resemblance to a [ghost ship](/wiki/List_of_ghost_ships), especially in [foggy](/wiki/Fog) and low-light conditions.

## General

Phantom Ship from the Sun Notch viewpoint

Phantom Ship Island is mainly formed from [andesite](/wiki/Andesite) rock that dates from about 400,000 years ago, partly altered by [hydrothermal activity](/wiki/Hydrothermal_vent). The island is situated on the south east end of Crater Lake and projects more than 200 m (656 ft) out from the wall of the [caldera](/wiki/Caldera). The island is about 500 ft by 200 ft in size. The vegetation is similar to that on [Wizard Island](/wiki/Wizard_Island) except for the lodgepole pine (*[Pinus contorta](/wiki/Pinus_contorta)*) which is absent on Phantom Ship. The island forms the part of a submerged [stratovolcano](/wiki/Stratovolcano) cone that is jaggedly sticking out of the water.

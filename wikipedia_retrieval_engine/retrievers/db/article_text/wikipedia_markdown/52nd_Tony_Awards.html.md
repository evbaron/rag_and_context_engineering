# 52nd Tony Awards

## Infobox

- **Date:** June 7, 1998
- **Location:** Radio City Music Hall, New York City
- **Hosted by:** Rosie O'Donnell
- **Network:** CBS

The **52nd Annual Tony Awards** ceremony was held on June 7, 1998, at [Radio City Music Hall](/wiki/Radio_City_Music_Hall) and was broadcast by [CBS](/wiki/CBS) television. A documentaries segment was telecast on [PBS](/wiki/Public_Broadcasting_Service) television. The ceremony was hosted by [Rosie O'Donnell](/wiki/Rosie_O%27Donnell), who hosted a total of three times (1997, 1998, and 2000).

This ceremony is notable for its Best Direction of a Play and Best Direction of a Musical winners both being female, the first time a female has won either award. The writer of the Best Play winner was also female, the second female winner of the award.

*[Ragtime](/wiki/Ragtime_(musical))* had 13 nominations, the most of the night, and *[Ragtime](/wiki/Ragtime_(musical))*, *[The Beauty Queen of Leenane](/wiki/The_Beauty_Queen_of_Leenane)* and *[Cabaret](/wiki/Cabaret_(musical))* each won four Tonys.

## The ceremony

The opening number was "Broadway Divas", with [Rosie O'Donnell](/wiki/Rosie_O%27Donnell) and the *[Chicago](/wiki/Chicago_(musical))* dancers introducing: [Patti LuPone](/wiki/Patti_LuPone) ("[Don't Cry for Me Argentina](/wiki/Don%27t_Cry_for_Me_Argentina)" from *[Evita](/wiki/Evita_(musical))*); [Jennifer Holliday](/wiki/Jennifer_Holliday) ("[And I Am Telling You I'm Not Going](/wiki/And_I_Am_Telling_You_I%27m_Not_Going)" from *[Dreamgirls](/wiki/Dreamgirls_(musical))*); and [Betty Buckley](/wiki/Betty_Buckley) ("[Memory](/wiki/Memory_(Cats_song))" from *[Cats](/wiki/Cats_(musical))*).

Musicals represented:

- *[Ragtime](/wiki/Ragtime_(musical))* ("Ragtime" – [Marin Mazzie](/wiki/Marin_Mazzie), [Audra McDonald](/wiki/Audra_McDonald) and Company)
- *[The Sound of Music](/wiki/The_Sound_of_Music)* ("Wedding"/"Do-Re-Mi"/"The Sound of Music" Finale – [Rebecca Luker](/wiki/Rebecca_Luker) and Company)
- *[1776](/wiki/1776_(musical))* ("[Sit Down, John](/wiki/Sit_Down,_John)" – [Richard Poe](/wiki/Richard_Poe) and Company)
- *[The Lion King](/wiki/The_Lion_King_(musical))* ("[Circle of Life](/wiki/Circle_of_Life)" – [Tsidii Le Loka](/wiki/Tsidii_Le_Loka) and Company)
- *[Cabaret](/wiki/Cabaret_(musical))* ("Willkommen" – [Alan Cumming](/wiki/Alan_Cumming) and Company)
- *[Side Show](/wiki/Side_Show_(musical))* ("I Will Never Leave You" – [Alice Ripley](/wiki/Alice_Ripley) and [Emily Skinner](/wiki/Emily_Skinner_(actress,_born_1970)))
- *[The Scarlet Pimpernel](/wiki/The_Scarlet_Pimpernel_(musical))* ("Into the Fire" – [Douglas Sills](/wiki/Douglas_Sills) and Company)

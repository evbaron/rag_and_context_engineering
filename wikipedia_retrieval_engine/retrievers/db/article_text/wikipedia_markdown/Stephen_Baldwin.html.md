**Stephen Andrew Baldwin** (born May 12, 1966) is an American actor. He has appeared in the films *[Born on the Fourth of July](/wiki/Born_on_the_Fourth_of_July_(film))* (1989), *[Posse](/wiki/Posse_(1993_film))* (1993), *[8 Seconds](/wiki/8_Seconds)*, *[Threesome](/wiki/Threesome_(1994_film))* (both 1994), *[The Usual Suspects](/wiki/The_Usual_Suspects)* (1995), *[Bio-Dome](/wiki/Bio-Dome)* (1996) and *[The Flintstones in Viva Rock Vegas](/wiki/The_Flintstones_in_Viva_Rock_Vegas)* (2000). Baldwin also starred in the television series *[The Young Riders](/wiki/The_Young_Riders)* (1989–1992) and as himself in the reality shows *[Celebrity Big Brother 7](/wiki/Celebrity_Big_Brother_(British_TV_series)_series_7)*, in which he placed 9th in the United Kingdom, and *[The Celebrity Apprentice](/wiki/The_Celebrity_Apprentice)*. In 2004, he directed *[Livin' It](/wiki/Livin%27_It)*, a [Christian-themed](/wiki/Christian_film) [skateboarding](/wiki/Skateboarding) DVD. He is the youngest of the four [Baldwin brothers](/wiki/Baldwin_family).

## Early life

Stephen Andrew Baldwin was born on May 12, 1966 in [Massapequa, New York](/wiki/Massapequa,_New_York), the youngest son of Carol Newcomb ([née](/wiki/N%C3%A9e) Martineau; December 15, 1929 – May 26, 2022), founder of The Baldwin Fund, and Alexander Rae Baldwin Jr. (October 26, 1927 – April 15, 1983), a high school [social studies](/wiki/Social_studies) teacher and football coach. Baldwin's elder brothers are actors [Alec](/wiki/Alec_Baldwin), [Daniel](/wiki/Daniel_Baldwin) and [William](/wiki/William_Baldwin), they are collectively known as the "[Baldwin brothers](/wiki/Baldwin_family)". He was raised in the [Catholic](/wiki/Catholic_Church) faith. In high school, Baldwin participated on the varsity wrestling team, along with William. Baldwin has two elder sisters, Elizabeth (née Baldwin) Keuchler and Jane (née Baldwin) Sasso. Baldwin attended the [American Academy of Dramatic Arts](/wiki/American_Academy_of_Dramatic_Arts).

## Career

Baldwin began acting on television and made his film debut in *[The Beast](/wiki/The_Beast_(1988_film))*. He starred in the western television series *[The Young Riders](/wiki/The_Young_Riders)* and in *[Threesome](/wiki/Threesome_(1994_film))* (1994). Baldwin landed a breakthrough role in *[The Usual Suspects](/wiki/The_Usual_Suspects)* (1995) and played [Barney Rubble](/wiki/Barney_Rubble) in *[The Flintstones in Viva Rock Vegas](/wiki/The_Flintstones_in_Viva_Rock_Vegas)* (2000). He participated in *[Celebrity Mole Hawaii](/wiki/The_Mole_(American_season_3))*, the first celebrity edition of *[The Mole](/wiki/The_Mole_(American_TV_series))* in 2002. [ABC](/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company) broadcast the program in early 2003. Later that year, he returned for *[Celebrity Mole Yucatán](/wiki/The_Mole_(American_season_4))*, which ABC aired in early 2004. In 2006, Baldwin played a villainous thug in the television film *[Jesse Stone: Night Passage](/wiki/Jesse_Stone:_Night_Passage)*. In August 2007, Baldwin returned to television, when [CMT](/wiki/Country_Music_Television) cast him in *Ty Murray's Celebrity Bull Riding Challenge*, one of nine celebrities cast. In the first episode, Baldwin was injured in a bad fall from a wooden pontoon, breaking his [shoulder blade](/wiki/Scapula) and cracking a rib. Under doctor's orders, he left the show in the second episode. From January to March 2008, Baldwin appeared on [Donald Trump](/wiki/Donald_Trump)'s *[Celebrity Apprentice](/wiki/The_Apprentice_(American_TV_series)_season_7)* on [NBC](/wiki/NBC). He finished fifth out of the 14 celebrity contestants. He and [Trace Adkins](/wiki/Trace_Adkins) became friends while competing on the show. In October 2008, Baldwin appeared in Adkins's music video, "[Muddy Water](/wiki/Muddy_Water_(Trace_Adkins_song))". In March 2013, Baldwin returned to compete in *[All-Star Celebrity Apprentice](/wiki/All-Star_Celebrity_Apprentice)*. He was a contestant on the 2009 NBC reality show *[I'm a Celebrity…Get Me out of Here!](/wiki/I%27m_a_Celebrity%E2%80%A6Get_Me_out_of_Here!_(U.S._season_2))*. Baldwin quit the show mid-season. On the second episode, he baptized *[The Hills](/wiki/The_Hills_(TV_series))* actor [Spencer Pratt](/wiki/Spencer_Pratt). In 2019, Baldwin starred in a Filipino film, *Kaibigan.*

## Personal life

Baldwin reads to children at the 2007 White House [Easter Egg Roll](/wiki/Easter_Egg_Roll).

Baldwin resides in the village of [Nyack, New York](/wiki/Nyack,_New_York), with his wife, the Brazilian [graphic designer](/wiki/Graphic_designer) Kennya Baldwin (née Deodato), whom he met in 1987 and married in 1990. They have two daughters, Alaia Baldwin and [Hailey Bieber](/wiki/Hailey_Bieber); both are models. His father-in-law is Brazilian composer [Eumir Deodato](/wiki/Eumir_Deodato), and Canadian singer [Justin Bieber](/wiki/Justin_Bieber) is his son-in-law through marriage to his daughter Hailey. Through his daughters, Baldwin has two grandchildren.

In 2006, Baldwin launched a campaign to prevent an adult bookstore from opening in Nyack. He has a tattoo on his left shoulder of the initials "HM" for [Hannah Montana](/wiki/Hannah_Montana). He got the tattoo after making a pact with [Miley Cyrus](/wiki/Miley_Cyrus) that he would be allowed to cameo on the show if he had the initials tattooed on him. He revealed the tattoo to Cyrus at a book signing in [Nashville](/wiki/Nashville) on November 10, 2008. He was never given the opportunity to appear on the show and has since said that he regrets getting the tattoo.

In June 2009, Baldwin's foreclosed $515,000, 1.4-acre home in [Rockland County, New York](/wiki/Rockland_County,_New_York) was publicly auctioned after he and his wife defaulted on more than $824,000 in mortgage payments. In July, Baldwin filed for [Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection](/wiki/Chapter_11,_Title_11,_United_States_Code) claiming more than $2.3 million in debt. Court papers showed he owed $1.2 million on two mortgages on a [New York](/wiki/New_York_(state)) property valued at only $1.1 million, and more than $1 million in taxes and credit card debt.

Baldwin was the first person to enter the *[Celebrity Big Brother](/wiki/Celebrity_Big_Brother_(British_TV_series)_series_7)* house on January 3, 2010. The theme for this series was "Hell", with a devil head on the front door. He frequently taunted the head by saying "you're a loser". During his stay in the house, he frequently read passages from the Bible to his fellow housemates. He failed to win many fans and was evicted on January 22, 2010, becoming the fourth housemate to be evicted. In a three-way vote—against [Ivana Trump](/wiki/Ivana_Trump) and [Sisqó](/wiki/Sisq%C3%B3)—he received 50% of the public vote. After his eviction, the website restorestephenbaldwin.org, unsolicited by him, began soliciting cash donations to improve Baldwin's career.

In December 2010, Baldwin filed a $3.8 million lawsuit against [Kevin Costner](/wiki/Kevin_Costner) over oil-separating technology that was used to help solve the [BP oil spill](/wiki/Deepwater_Horizon_oil_spill) in the Gulf of Mexico. In June 2012, a jury sided with Costner and awarded Baldwin no damages. In March 2013, Baldwin pleaded guilty to failing to file income taxes for the years 2008, 2009, and 2010. He stated that he never intended to avoid paying taxes and that he had received bad advice from lawyers and accountants. Baldwin agreed to pay $300,000 within a year, or he would be sentenced to five years' probation and have five years to pay the money.

## Views

### Religion

Baldwin talks on the red carpet of a Children Uniting Nations party, 2009.

In September 2006, Baldwin released his book titled *The Unusual Suspect*, which details highlights from his personal life, career, days of [drug abuse](/wiki/Substance_abuse) and his turn to becoming a [born-again Evangelical](/wiki/Born_again) after the [September 11 attacks](/wiki/September_11_attacks) in 2001. In the same year, Baldwin, Mario D'Ortenzio, and [Bobby Brewer](/wiki/Bobby_Brewer) founded Breakthrough Ministry, which was designed to use [extreme sports](/wiki/Extreme_sports) as a ministry via arena tours, called AsSalt Tours. The tours featured extreme sports celebrities, including [Christian Hosoi](/wiki/Christian_Hosoi).

In late 2008, Baldwin formed a for-profit organization called Antioch Ministry, which exists "to facilitate the gifts and calling of Stephen Baldwin." In 2009, Baldwin launched a third ministry called Now More Than Ever, designed to reach enlisted men and women in the U.S. armed services around the world. In 2008, Baldwin teamed up with [conservative](/wiki/Conservatism_in_the_United_States) talk-radio host Kevin McCullough to put together a Saturday radio show called *Baldwin/McCullough Radio*. As of April 18, 2009, the show aired on 213 stations and in more than 400 cities across the US and worldwide on Sirius 161 and XM 227. Baldwin appears weekly on the show from the broadcast studios in New York City and from various locations around the country when he is traveling for other business purposes.

### Politics

Baldwin at the [Iowa Straw Poll](/wiki/Iowa_Straw_Poll_(1979%E2%80%932011)) in support of Brownback, 2007

In 2007, Baldwin endorsed [Sam Brownback](/wiki/Sam_Brownback) for U.S. president. After Brownback ended his campaign, Baldwin switched his support to [Mike Huckabee](/wiki/Mike_Huckabee). Baldwin was an outspoken advocate of the [John McCain](/wiki/John_McCain)–[Sarah Palin](/wiki/Sarah_Palin) presidential ticket after Huckabee dropped out of the [2008 presidential election](/wiki/2008_United_States_presidential_election).

In 2011, Baldwin mentioned the possibility of entering [New York City's 2013 mayoral election](/wiki/New_York_City_mayoral_election,_2013) as an opponent of his brother [Alec](/wiki/Alec_Baldwin), who had suggested that he might run himself.

Baldwin was among the first Hollywood celebrities to endorse [Donald Trump](/wiki/Donald_Trump) in the [2016 presidential election](/wiki/2016_United_States_presidential_election). He would later endorse Trump in [2020](/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election) and [2024](/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election).

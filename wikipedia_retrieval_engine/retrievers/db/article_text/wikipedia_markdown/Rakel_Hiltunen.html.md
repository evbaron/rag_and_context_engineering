**Lea Rakel Hiltunen** (née *Lyytikäinen*; born 2 July 1940 in [Keitele](/wiki/Keitele)) is a [Finnish](/wiki/Finland) social worker and politician. She was a member of the [Parliament of Finland](/wiki/Parliament_of_Finland) from 1999 to 2015, representing the [Social Democratic Party of Finland](/wiki/Social_Democratic_Party_of_Finland) (SDP).

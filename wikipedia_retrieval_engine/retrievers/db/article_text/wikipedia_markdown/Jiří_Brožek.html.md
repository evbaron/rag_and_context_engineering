# Jiří Brožek

## Infobox

- **Born:** ( 1947-03-11 ) 11 March 1947 (age 79) Roudnice nad Labem Czechoslovakia
- **Alma mater:** Film and TV School of the Academy of Performing Arts in Prague
- **Occupation:** film editor
- **Years active:** 1970–2020

**Jiří Brožek** (born 11 March 1947) is a Czech film editor.

## Biography

During 1967‒1973 he attended Editing and Directing at [FAMU](/wiki/Film_and_TV_School_of_the_Academy_of_Performing_Arts_in_Prague). Then he started to work in [Barrandov Movie Studios](/wiki/Barrandov_Studios), from 1976 Brožek is self-employed. During the career he edited more than 100 feature films, variety of the TV production and many TV series.

He cooperated with [Ladislav Smoljak](/wiki/Ladislav_Smoljak) (*[Ball Lightning](/wiki/Ball_Lightning_(film))*, *[Waiter, Scarper!](/wiki/Waiter,_Scarper!)*, *[Jára Cimrman Lying, Sleeping](/wiki/J%C3%A1ra_Cimrman_Lying,_Sleeping)*) [Karel Kachyňa](/wiki/Karel_Kachy%C5%88a) (*[Love Between the Raindrops](/wiki/Love_Between_the_Raindrops)*, *[Forbidden Dreams](/wiki/Forbidden_Dreams)*), [Jiří Menzel](/wiki/Ji%C5%99%C3%AD_Menzel) (*[Cutting It Short](/wiki/Cutting_It_Short)*, *[The Snowdrop Festival](/wiki/The_Snowdrop_Festival)*, *[My Sweet Little Village](/wiki/My_Sweet_Little_Village)*), [Věra Chytilová](/wiki/V%C4%9Bra_Chytilov%C3%A1) (*[Calamity](/wiki/Calamity_(film))*, *[Wolf's Hole](/wiki/Wolf%27s_Hole)*) or [Václav Havel](/wiki/V%C3%A1clav_Havel) (*[Leaving](/wiki/Leaving_(2011_film)))*.

Jiří Brožek was awarded nine [Czech Lions](/wiki/Czech_Lion_Awards) for films *[Krvavý román](/wiki/Krvav%C3%BD_rom%C3%A1n)* (1993), *[Sekal Has to Die](/wiki/Sekal_Has_to_Die)* (1998), *[Anděl Exit](/wiki/Andel_Exit)* (2000), *[Boredom in Brno](/wiki/Boredom_in_Brno)* (2003), *[The City of the Sun](/wiki/The_City_of_the_Sun_(film))* (2005), *[Pleasant Moments](/wiki/Pleasant_Moments)* (2006), *[...a bude hůř](/wiki/...a_bude_h%C5%AF%C5%99)* (2007), *[Leaving](/wiki/Leaving_(2011_film))* (2011) and *Filthy* (2017), twice he gained [Slovak](/wiki/Slovakia) film award [Slnko v sieti](/wiki/Slnko_v_sieti) for *[The City of the Sun](/wiki/The_City_of_the_Sun_(film))* (2006) and *[Gypsy](/wiki/Gypsy_(2011_film))* (2012). He is a member of [Czech Film and Television Academy](/wiki/Czech_Film_and_Television_Academy) (ČFTA) and honourable member of Slovak Film and Television Academy (SFTA).

He has three children.

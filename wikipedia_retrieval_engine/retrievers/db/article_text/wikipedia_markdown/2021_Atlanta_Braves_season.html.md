The **2021 Atlanta Braves season** was the 151st season of the [Atlanta Braves](/wiki/Atlanta_Braves) franchise, the 56th season in [Atlanta](/wiki/Atlanta), and the Braves' 5th season at [Truist Park](/wiki/Truist_Park). The Braves were managed by Brian Snitker, in his 6th season as the team's [manager](/wiki/Manager_(baseball)). The Braves clinched their fourth consecutive [National League East](/wiki/National_League_East) title. They defeated the [Milwaukee Brewers](/wiki/2021_Milwaukee_Brewers_season) in the [NLDS](/wiki/2021_National_League_Division_Series) and faced the [Los Angeles Dodgers](/wiki/2021_Los_Angeles_Dodgers_season) in the [NLCS](/wiki/2021_National_League_Championship_Series) for the second straight year. They defeated the Dodgers in six games to reach the [World Series](/wiki/2021_World_Series) for the first time since [1999](/wiki/1999_World_Series). They would go on to defeat the [Houston Astros](/wiki/2021_Houston_Astros_season) in six games, winning their first World Series since [1995](/wiki/1995_Atlanta_Braves_season), their second since moving to Atlanta, and their fourth in franchise history. [Jorge Soler](/wiki/Jorge_Soler) won the [Most Valuable Player](/wiki/World_Series_Most_Valuable_Player_Award) award in the World Series.

## Offseason

### November

- On November 11, 2020, the Braves re-signed right-handed relief pitcher [Josh Tomlin](/wiki/Josh_Tomlin) to a one-year, $1 million contract that includes a $1.25 million club option for 2022.
- On November 16, 2020, the Braves signed left-handed starting pitcher [Drew Smyly](/wiki/Drew_Smyly) to a one-year, $11 million contract.
- On November 24, 2020, the Braves signed right-handed starting pitcher [Charlie Morton](/wiki/Charlie_Morton_(pitcher)) to a one-year, $15 million contract.

### February

- On February 5, 2021, the Braves re-signed outfielder [Marcell Ozuna](/wiki/Marcell_Ozuna) to a four-year, $64 million contract. The contract includes a club option for a fifth year, worth $16 million, with a $1 million buyout.

## Regular season

The 2021 season wasn't a smooth sailing road for the [Atlanta Braves](/wiki/Atlanta_Braves). The Braves started off 0-4 and ended April with a 12–14 record. At the All-Star Break they were 44–45. After the All-Star Break in August they started ramping it up and went 18–8. On August 8, they climbed over .500 for the first time, at 57–56, and on Aug 15. they took over first place in the NL East, ending up winning the NL East with a 88–73 record.

The team's turnaround began after July 10 when [Ronald Acuña Jr.](/wiki/Ronald_Acu%C3%B1a_Jr.) suffered a season ending injury and the [trade deadline](/wiki/Trade_deadline) approached. After the Braves' star outfielder got injured, GM [Alex Anthopoulos](/wiki/Alex_Anthopoulos) remade his outfield by bringing in veterans [Adam Duvall](/wiki/Adam_Duvall), [Joc Pederson](/wiki/Joc_Pederson), [Eddie Rosario](/wiki/Eddie_Rosario), and [Jorge Soler](/wiki/Jorge_Soler). They made a huge impact on the team with Duvall finishing the season with a National League-leading 113 [RBIs](/wiki/Run_batted_in) (45 with the Braves); Pederson hit a game-winning home run in the [NLDS](/wiki/NLDS) against the [Brewers](/wiki/Milwaukee_Brewers); Rosario was the NLCS MVP; Soler was the [World Series](/wiki/World_Series) MVP.

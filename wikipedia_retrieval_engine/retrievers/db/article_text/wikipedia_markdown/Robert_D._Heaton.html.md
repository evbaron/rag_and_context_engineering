**Robert Douglas Heaton** (July 1, 1873 – June 11, 1933) was 20th-century American businessman and politician who served two terms as a [Republican](/wiki/Republican_Party_(United_States)) member of the [U.S. House of Representatives](/wiki/U.S._House_of_Representatives) from [Pennsylvania](/wiki/Pennsylvania) from 1915 to 1919.

## Biography

Robert D. Heaton was born in Raven Run, Pennsylvania. He moved to Ashland, Pennsylvania, with his parents in 1886. He attended the [Canandaigua Academy](/wiki/Canandaigua_Academy) in Canandaigua, New York, the [New York Military Academy](/wiki/New_York_Military_Academy) at [Cornwall, New York](/wiki/Cornwall,_New_York), and the [University of Pennsylvania](/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) at Philadelphia. He is identified with many business enterprises of the state and county.

### Congress

Heaton was an unsuccessful candidate for election in [1910](/wiki/United_States_House_election,_1910), and was elected as a Republican to the [Sixty-fourth](/wiki/64th_United_States_Congress) and [Sixty-fifth](/wiki/65th_United_States_Congress) Congresses. He did not seek renomination in [1918](/wiki/United_States_House_election,_1918), having become a candidate for the [Pennsylvania State Senate](/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_Senate).

### Later activities

He was a member of the State Senate from 1919 to 1932. He resumed his former business activities, and served as a member of the board of trustees of the Ashland State Hospital.

### Death and burial

He died in Ashland, Pennsylvania, aged 59, and is interred in the family cemetery in [Jim Thorpe, Pennsylvania](/wiki/Jim_Thorpe,_Pennsylvania).

# Pamela Fryman

## Infobox

- **Born:** Pamela Gail Fryman ( 1959-08-19 ) August 19, 1959 (age 66) Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, U.S.
- **Occupations:** Television director and producer
- **Years active:** 1988–present

**Pamela Gail Fryman** (born August 19, 1959) is an American [sitcom](/wiki/Sitcom) director and producer. She was the principal director of the television series *[How I Met Your Mother](/wiki/How_I_Met_Your_Mother)*, directing 196 of the show's 208 episodes.

## Biography

Born and raised in [Philadelphia, Pennsylvania](/wiki/Philadelphia,_Pennsylvania), Fryman got her first job on *The John Davidson Show* as an assistant to the talent coordinator, and went on to be a booth production assistant and secretary on *[Santa Barbara](/wiki/Santa_Barbara_(TV_series))*, eventually moving up to assistant director (AD), and director. In 1993, producer [Peter Noah](/wiki/Peter_Noah), with whom she had worked on the game show *[Dream House](/wiki/Dream_House_(game_show))*, gave Fryman a chance to direct an episode of the short-lived sitcom *[Café Americain](/wiki/Caf%C3%A9_Americain)*. These would be the first stepping stones toward a long and successful career.

Before her directing career blossomed, Fryman pursued stage directing. On the set of *[Frasier](/wiki/Frasier)*, rehearsal resembled a play staging, which is exactly what creator and executive producer [David Lee](/wiki/David_Lee_(screenwriter)) had in mind when he hired her. Fryman directed 34 episodes of the show from seasons four through eight.

She also became main director on *[Just Shoot Me!](/wiki/Just_Shoot_Me!)*, directing 94 out of the 148 episodes the show aired.

Fryman directed the majority of the episodes of *[How I Met Your Mother](/wiki/How_I_Met_Your_Mother)*. Show creator [Craig Thomas](/wiki/Craig_Thomas_(screenwriter)) praised her communication skills, saying, "She makes everyone feel they've been heard and respected and she can connect with anyone."
Though Fryman's original career plan did not include directing (she figured she would "follow in her father's footsteps in merchandising"), she has grown to realize that directing is her forte and passion. In [*Variety*](/wiki/Variety_(magazine)) magazine, Fryman said that continuing to direct *How I Met Your Mother* is her fantasy realized. In 2014, she officiated the wedding of *How I Met Your Mother* star [Neil Patrick Harris](/wiki/Neil_Patrick_Harris) and [David Burtka](/wiki/David_Burtka), who played a side role in the show.

She also directed 19 out of the 30 episodes of *[How I Met Your Father](/wiki/How_I_Met_Your_Father)*.

She achieved the milestone of directing her 500th episode of television in 2016 with the third episode of the third season of *[The Odd Couple](/wiki/The_Odd_Couple_(2015_TV_series))*.

## Awards

Fryman has received recognition from the [Academy of Television Arts & Sciences](/wiki/Academy_of_Television_Arts_%26_Sciences) also known as the (ATAS), the [National Academy of Television Arts and Sciences](/wiki/National_Academy_of_Television_Arts_and_Sciences), the [Directors Guild of America](/wiki/Directors_Guild_of_America), Goldderby.com, the Online Film & Television Association also known as the (OFTA), and the [Women in Film](/wiki/Women_in_Film_Crystal_%2B_Lucy_Awards) organization.

- 1990 Won - [Daytime Emmy Award for Outstanding Drama Series Directing Team](/wiki/Daytime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Drama_Series_Directing_Team) (*[Santa Barbara](/wiki/Santa_Barbara_(TV_series))*)
- 1991 Won - [Daytime Emmy Award for Outstanding Drama Series Directing Team](/wiki/Daytime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Drama_Series_Directing_Team) (*Santa Barbara*)
- 1998 Nominated - [Directors Guild Award for Outstanding Directorial Achievement in Comedy Series](/wiki/Directors_Guild_of_America_Award_for_Outstanding_Directing_%E2%80%93_Comedy_Series) ("[Halloween (Part 1)](/wiki/List_of_Frasier_episodes)") (*[Frasier](/wiki/Frasier)*)
- 1999 Nominated - [Directors Guild Award for Outstanding Directorial Achievement in Comedy Series](/wiki/Directors_Guild_of_America_Award_for_Outstanding_Directing_%E2%80%93_Comedy_Series) ("[Two Girls for Every Boy](/wiki/List_of_Just_Shoot_Me!_episodes)") (*[Just Shoot Me!](/wiki/Just_Shoot_Me!)*)
- 2000 Nominated - [Directors Guild Award for Outstanding Directorial Achievement in Comedy Series](/wiki/Directors_Guild_of_America_Award_for_Outstanding_Directing_%E2%80%93_Comedy_Series) ("[The Flight Before Christmas](/wiki/List_of_Frasier_episodes)") (*Frasier*)
- 2001 Nominated - [Directors Guild Award for Outstanding Directorial Achievement in Comedy Series](/wiki/Directors_Guild_of_America_Award_for_Outstanding_Directing_%E2%80%93_Comedy_Series) ("[And The Dish Ran Away With The Spoon (Part 1 & 2)](/wiki/List_of_Frasier_episodes)") (*Frasier*)
- 2009 Nominated - [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Comedy Series](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Comedy_Series) (*How I Met Your Mother*)
- 2011 Nominated - Online Film & Television Association Award for Best Direction in a Comedy Series (*How I Met Your Mother*)
- 2011 Nominated - [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Directing for a Comedy Series](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Directing_for_a_Comedy_Series) ("[Subway Wars](/wiki/List_of_How_I_Met_Your_Mother_episodes)") (*How I Met Your Mother*)
- 2011 Won - [Women in Film Crystal + Lucy Award, Dorothy Arzner Directors Award](/wiki/Women_in_Film_Crystal_%2B_Lucy_Awards#Dorothy_Arzner_Directors_award)
- 2020 Nominated - [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Directing for a Variety Special](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Directing_for_a_Variety_Special) (*[Live in Front of a Studio Audience: "All in the Family" and "Good Times"](/wiki/Live_in_Front_of_a_Studio_Audience)*) (shared with Andy Fisher)

# D.I.C.E. Award for Game of the Year

## Infobox

- **Country:** United States
- **Presented by:** Academy of Interactive Arts & Sciences
- **First award:** 1998
- **Currently held by:** Clair Obscur: Expedition 33
- **Website:** interactive.org

The **D.I.C.E. Award for Game of the Year** is an award presented annually by the [Academy of Interactive Arts & Sciences](/wiki/Academy_of_Interactive_Arts_%26_Sciences) during the [D.I.C.E. Awards](/wiki/D.I.C.E._Awards). It is given in honor of "the single game, without regard to system or delivery mechanism, voted by the membership of the Academy of Interactive Arts & Sciences that best utilizes the chosen medium to entertain users". All active creative/technical, business, and affiliate members of the Academy are qualified to vote for this category.

The most recent winner of the award is *[Clair Obscur: Expedition 33](/wiki/Clair_Obscur:_Expedition_33)*, developed by Sandfall Interactive and published by [Kepler Interactive](/wiki/Kepler_Interactive).

## History

### Category name changes

The [1st Annual Interactive Achievement Awards](/wiki/1st_Annual_Interactive_Achievement_Awards) ceremony was held on May 28, 1998, with the licensed adaptation of *[GoldenEye 007](/wiki/GoldenEye_007_(1997_video_game))*, developed by [Rare](/wiki/Rare_(company)) and published by [Nintendo](/wiki/Nintendo), receiving the first award. The award was originally known as **Interactive Title of the Year** for the 1998 ceremony. It would be renamed **Game of the Year** at the following year's awards ceremony. For a brief period between 2006 and 2009, the award was presented as **Overall Game of the Year**.

### Indie Games

See also: [D.I.C.E. Award for Outstanding Achievement for an Independent Game](/wiki/D.I.C.E._Award_for_Outstanding_Achievement_for_an_Independent_Game)

The first indie that was nominated for **Game of the Year** was *[Angry Birds HD](/wiki/Angry_Birds_HD)*, while *[Journey](/wiki/Journey_(2012_video_game))* was the first indie game winner. The only other [indie games](/wiki/Indie_game) that had won were *[Untitled Goose Game](/wiki/Untitled_Goose_Game)*, *[Hades](/wiki/Hades_(video_game))*, and *[Clair Obscur: Expedition 33](/wiki/Clair_Obscur:_Expedition_33)*. On a yearly basis since 2016, there had been at least one indie game nominee for **Game of the Year**:

- [2016](/wiki/19th_Annual_D.I.C.E._Awards): *[Ori and the Blind Forest](/wiki/Ori_and_the_Blind_Forest)*
- [2017](/wiki/20th_Annual_D.I.C.E._Awards): *[Inside](/wiki/Inside_(video_game))*
- [2018](/wiki/21st_Annual_D.I.C.E._Awards): *[Cuphead](/wiki/Cuphead)*
- [2019](/wiki/22nd_Annual_D.I.C.E._Awards): *[Into the Breach](/wiki/Into_the_Breach)*, *[Return of the Obra Dinn](/wiki/Return_of_the_Obra_Dinn)*
- [2020](/wiki/23rd_Annual_D.I.C.E._Awards): *[Disco Elysium](/wiki/Disco_Elysium)*, *[Outer Wilds](/wiki/Outer_Wilds)*, *[Untitled Goose Game](/wiki/Untitled_Goose_Game)*
- [2021](/wiki/24th_Annual_D.I.C.E._Awards): *[Hades](/wiki/Hades_(video_game))*
- [2022](/wiki/25th_Annual_D.I.C.E._Awards): *[Inscryption](/wiki/Inscryption)*
- [2023](/wiki/26th_Annual_D.I.C.E._Awards): *[Stray](/wiki/Stray_(video_game))*, *[Vampire Survivors](/wiki/Vampire_Survivors)*
- [2024](/wiki/27th_Annual_D.I.C.E._Awards): *[Cocoon](/wiki/Cocoon_(video_game))*
- [2025](/wiki/28th_Annual_D.I.C.E._Awards): *[Balatro](/wiki/Balatro_(video_game))*
- [2026](/wiki/29th_Annual_D.I.C.E._Awards): *[Blue Prince](/wiki/Blue_Prince)*, *[Clair Obscur: Expedition 33](/wiki/Clair_Obscur:_Expedition_33)*, *[Dispatch](/wiki/Dispatch_(video_game))*

2019, 2020, 2023 and 2026 were the only years that had more than one indie game nominee, with 2020 and 2026 being the years of indie games majority.

### Genres

The most frequently nominated and winning genres for **Game of the Year** had been [action](/wiki/Action_game), [adventure](/wiki/Adventure_game), and [role-playing](/wiki/Role-playing_video_game) games. No [strategy game](/wiki/Strategy_video_game) had ever won the award, but fourteen had been nominated: *[Age of Empires](/wiki/Age_of_Empires_(video_game))*, *[Sid Meier's Alpha Centauri](/wiki/Sid_Meier%27s_Alpha_Centauri)*, *[Age of Empires II: The Age of Kings](/wiki/Age_of_Empires_II)*, *[Age of Empires II: The Conquerors](/wiki/Age_of_Empires_II:_The_Conquerors)*, *[Command & Conquer: Red Alert 2](/wiki/Command_%26_Conquer:_Red_Alert_2)*, *[Sacrifice](/wiki/Sacrifice_(video_game))*, *[Sid Meier's Civilization III](/wiki/Sid_Meier%27s_Civilization_III)*, *[Command & Conquer: Generals](/wiki/Command_%26_Conquer:_Generals)*, *[Rise of Nations](/wiki/Rise_of_Nations)*, *[XCOM: Enemy Unknown](/wiki/XCOM:_Enemy_Unknown)*, *[Hearthstone](/wiki/Hearthstone)*, *[Into the Breach](/wiki/Into_the_Breach)*, *[Inscryption](/wiki/Inscryption)*, and *[Balatro](/wiki/Balatro_(video_game))*. *[The Sims](/wiki/The_Sims_(video_game))* was the only [simulation game](/wiki/Simulation_video_game) to win the top award, with six others also being nominated: *[MechWarrior 4: Vengeance](/wiki/MechWarrior_4:_Vengeance)*, *[RollerCoaster Tycoon: Loopy Landscapes](/wiki/RollerCoaster_Tycoon_(video_game)#Expansion_packs)*, *[Black & White](/wiki/Black_%26_White_(video_game))*, *[Animal Crossing](/wiki/Animal_Crossing_(video_game))*, *[Nintendogs](/wiki/Nintendogs)*, and *[Animal Crossing: New Horizons](/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_New_Horizons)*. Four [rhythm games](/wiki/Rhythm_game) had been nominated: *[PaRappa the Rapper](/wiki/PaRappa_the_Rapper)*, *[Guitar Hero](/wiki/Guitar_Hero_(video_game))*, *[Guitar Hero II](/wiki/Guitar_Hero_II)*, and *[Rock Band](/wiki/Rock_Band_(video_game))*. The only [fighting games](/wiki/Fighting_games) that had been nominated were *[Soulcalibur](/wiki/Soulcalibur_(video_game))*, *[Tekken Tag Tournament](/wiki/Tekken_Tag_Tournament)*, and *[WWF No Mercy](/wiki/WWF_No_Mercy_(video_game))*. The [awards ceremony in 2001](/wiki/4th_Annual_Interactive_Achievement_Awards) had six [sports game](/wiki/Sports_video_game) nominees: *[FIFA 2001](/wiki/FIFA_2001)*, *[Links 2001](/wiki/Links_2001)*, *[Madden NFL 2001](/wiki/Madden_NFL_2001)*, *[SSX](/wiki/SSX_(2000_video_game))*, *[Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 2](/wiki/Tony_Hawk%27s_Pro_Skater_2)*, and *[Virtua Tennis](/wiki/Virtua_Tennis_(video_game))*; *[SSX 3](/wiki/SSX_3)* and *[Wii Sports](/wiki/Wii_Sports)* were the only sports games nominated after 2001. Only three expansion packs had ever been nominated (all during [the awards ceremony in 2001](/wiki/4th_Annual_Interactive_Achievement_Awards)): *[Age of Empires II: The Conquerors](/wiki/Age_of_Empires_II:_The_Conquerors)*, *[EverQuest: The Ruins of Kunark](/wiki/EverQuest:_The_Ruins_of_Kunark)*, and *[RollerCoaster Tycoon: Loopy Landscapes](/wiki/RollerCoaster_Tycoon_(video_game)#Expansion_packs)*. The only compilation pack that received a nomination for **Game of the Year** was *[The Orange Box](/wiki/The_Orange_Box)* (which included *[Half-Life 2: Episode Two](/wiki/Half-Life_2:_Episode_Two)*, *[Portal](/wiki/Portal_(video_game))*, and *[Team Fortress 2](/wiki/Team_Fortress_2)*, as well as previously released titles of *[Half-Life 2](/wiki/Half-Life_2)* and *[Half-Life 2: Episode One](/wiki/Half-Life_2:_Episode_One)*).

### Platforms

Nearly every **Game of the Year** nominee had either been released for a [home video game console](/wiki/Home_video_game_console) or for [personal computer](/wiki/Personal_computer). *[Pokémon Yellow](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Yellow)*, *[Nintendogs](/wiki/Nintendogs)*, and *[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds](/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_A_Link_Between_Worlds)* were the only nominees that were released for [handheld game consoles](/wiki/Handheld_game_consoles). There had been six nominees that were released for [mobile devices](/wiki/Mobile_devices) at the time of their nomination: *[Angry Birds HD](/wiki/Angry_Birds_HD)*, *[The Walking Dead](/wiki/The_Walking_Dead_(video_game))*, *[Hearthstone](/wiki/Hearthstone)*, *[Pokémon Go](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Go)*, *[Vampire Survivors](/wiki/Vampire_Survivors)*, and *[Balatro](/wiki/Balatro_(video_game))*. *[Journey](/wiki/Journey_(2012_video_game))*, *[Borderlands 2](/wiki/Borderlands_2)*, *[XCOM: Enemy Unknown](/wiki/XCOM:_Enemy_Unknown)*, *[Inside](/wiki/Inside_(video_game))*, *[PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds](/wiki/PlayerUnknown%27s_Battlegrounds)*, *[Into the Breach](/wiki/Into_the_Breach)*, *[Disco Elysium](/wiki/Disco_Elysium)*, and *[Hades](/wiki/Hades_(video_game))* would eventually be released for mobile devices, but they were not at the time of their nomination.

### Other Game of the Year Awards

Usually the winner for **Game of the Year** would also win the award for their respective genre/platform category; the only exceptions had been *[The Sims](/wiki/The_Sims_(video_game))*, *[Battlefield 1942](/wiki/Battlefield_1942)*, *[Untitled Goose Game](/wiki/Untitled_Goose_Game)*, and *[It Takes Two](/wiki/It_Takes_Two_(video_game))*. Of the four exceptions, *Battlefield 1942* and *Untitled Goose Game* did not receive any nominations for their respective genre-related category (although the former did win the award for **[Online Gameplay of the Year](/wiki/D.I.C.E._Award_for_Online_Game_of_the_Year)**, while the latter won for **[Outstanding Achievement for an Independent Game](/wiki/D.I.C.E._Award_for_Outstanding_Achievement_for_an_Independent_Game)**). There had been seven winners for **[Family Game of the Year](/wiki/D.I.C.E._Award_for_Family_Game_of_the_Year)** that were also nominated for **Game of the Year**: *[Guitar Hero](/wiki/Guitar_Hero_(video_game))*, *[Guitar Hero II](/wiki/Guitar_Hero_II)*, *[Rock Band](/wiki/Rock_Band_(video_game))*, *[LittleBigPlanet](/wiki/LittleBigPlanet_(2008_video_game))*, *[Animal Crossing: New Horizons](/wiki/Animal_Crossing:_New_Horizons)*, *[Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart](/wiki/Ratchet_%26_Clank:_Rift_Apart)*, and *[Astro Bot](/wiki/Astro_Bot)*, with *LittleBigPlanet* and *Astro Bot* being the only winners for **Game of the Year**. *[Angry Birds HD](/wiki/Angry_Birds_(video_game))* and *[Journey](/wiki/Journey_(2012_video_game))* were the only winners for **Casual Game of the Year** that were also nominated for **Game of the Year**, the latter of which won both awards.

## Multiple nominations and wins

[Nintendo EAD/EPD](/wiki/Nintendo_EPD) has developed the most Game of the Year nominees with fourteen. Nintendo EAD/EPD is also tied with Infinity Ward, Santa Monica Studio, Naughty Dog, BioWare, and Bethesda Game Studios for each developing two Game of the Year winners, being the most of any developer. Developer [Insomniac Games](/wiki/Insomniac_Games) has the most nominations without a win.

Sony has published the most Game of the Year nominees and winners. Sony has twice had back-to-back wins for Game of the Year; the first for *[LittleBigPlanet](/wiki/LittleBigPlanet)* in 2009 and *[Uncharted 2: Among Thieves](/wiki/Uncharted_2:_Among_Thieves)* in 2010, and the second time for *[Journey](/wiki/Journey_(2012_video_game))* in 2013 and *[The Last of Us](/wiki/The_Last_of_Us_(video_game))* in 2014. The only other publisher with back-to-back wins is Nintendo with *[GoldenEye 007](/wiki/GoldenEye_007_(1997_video_game))* in [1998](/wiki/1st_Annual_Interactive_Achievement_Awards), and *[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time](/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Ocarina_of_Time)* in [1999](/wiki/2nd_Annual_Interactive_Achievement_Awards). [Rockstar Games](/wiki/Rockstar_Games) has published the most Game of the Year nominees without having a single winner.

The most nominated franchise is *[The Legend of Zelda](/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda)*, with eight nominations. *[Call of Duty](/wiki/Call_of_Duty)*, *[The Legend of Zelda](/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda)*, and
*[God of War](/wiki/God_of_War_(franchise))* are the only franchises to have won Game of the Year twice. The *[Grand Theft Auto](/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto)* franchise has garnered the most nominations without winning a single award in this category. *[Final Fantasy VII Remake](/wiki/Final_Fantasy_VII_Remake)* is the first and only remake that has been nominated, let alone a remake of a previous nominee.

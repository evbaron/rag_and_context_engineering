# List of current United States representatives

This is a list of individuals serving in the [United States House of Representatives](/wiki/United_States_House_of_Representatives) (as of April 22, 2026, the [119th Congress](/wiki/119th_United_States_Congress)). The membership of the House comprises 435 seats for representatives from the [50 states](/wiki/U.S._state), [apportioned](/wiki/United_States_congressional_apportionment) by [population](/wiki/List_of_U.S._states_and_territories_by_population), as well as six seats for non-voting delegates from [U.S. territories](/wiki/Territories_of_the_United_States) and the [District of Columbia](/wiki/District_of_Columbia). As of April 22, 2026, there are 430 representatives and 5 vacancies.

## Vacancies

- [California 1](/wiki/California%27s_1st_congressional_district): [Doug LaMalfa](/wiki/Doug_LaMalfa) (R) died on January 6, 2026. The [special election](/wiki/2026_California%27s_1st_congressional_district_special_election) will be held on June 2, 2026, with a runoff scheduled for August 4, 2026, if needed.
- [California 14](/wiki/California%27s_14th_congressional_district): [Eric Swalwell](/wiki/Eric_Swalwell) (D) resigned on April 14, 2026. The [special election](/wiki/2026_California%27s_14th_congressional_district_special_election) will be held on June 16, 2026, with a runoff scheduled for August 18, 2026, if needed.
- [Texas 23](/wiki/Texas%27s_23rd_congressional_district): [Tony Gonzales](/wiki/Tony_Gonzales) (R) resigned on April 14, 2026. The [special election](/wiki/2026_Texas%27s_23rd_congressional_district_special_election) will be held on a date TBD.
- [Florida 20](/wiki/Florida%27s_20th_congressional_district): [Sheila Cherfilus-McCormick](/wiki/Sheila_Cherfilus-McCormick) (D) resigned on April 21, 2026. The [special election](/wiki/2026_Florida%27s_20th_congressional_district_special_election) will be held on a date TBD.
- [Georgia 13](/wiki/Georgia%27s_13th_congressional_district): [David Scott](/wiki/David_Scott_(Georgia_politician)) (D) died on April 22, 2026. The [special election](/wiki/2026_Georgia%27s_13th_congressional_district_special_election) will be held on July 28, 2026.

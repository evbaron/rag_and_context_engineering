***Blue Collar Comedy Tour: The Movie*** is a 2003 American [stand-up comedy](/wiki/Stand-up_comedy) [concert film](/wiki/Concert_film) from [Warner Bros. Pictures](/wiki/Warner_Bros._Pictures). It stars comedians [Jeff Foxworthy](/wiki/Jeff_Foxworthy) and [Bill Engvall](/wiki/Bill_Engvall) and fellow Blue Collar comics [Ron White](/wiki/Ron_White) and [Larry the Cable Guy](/wiki/Larry_the_Cable_Guy). The movie is similar in nature to that of *[The Original Kings of Comedy](/wiki/The_Original_Kings_of_Comedy)*. It was followed by two [direct-to-video](/wiki/Direct-to-video) sequels, *[Blue Collar Comedy Tour Rides Again](/wiki/Blue_Collar_Comedy_Tour_Rides_Again)* (2004) and *[Blue Collar Comedy Tour: One for the Road](/wiki/Blue_Collar_Comedy_Tour:_One_for_the_Road)* (2006). The film received critical acclaim.

## Premise

The film features live stand-up performances filmed at the [Dodge Theater](/wiki/Arizona_Federal_Theatre) in [Phoenix, Arizona](/wiki/Phoenix,_Arizona) on July 21, 2002. It also features behind-the-scenes segments highlighting the individual comedians.

## Television broadcast

When aired on Comedy Central, in addition to editing of the stand-up material for time and content, [Heidi Klum](/wiki/Heidi_Klum)'s appearance is completely cut out.

## Reception

The film has received near-universal critical acclaim, praising the irreverent jokes and humor. On the [review aggregator](/wiki/Review_aggregator) website [Rotten Tomatoes](/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), 92% of 13 critics' reviews are positive, with an average rating of 6.8/10. [Metacritic](/wiki/Metacritic), which uses a [weighted average](/wiki/Weighted_arithmetic_mean), assigned the film a score of 70 out of 100, based on 6 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.

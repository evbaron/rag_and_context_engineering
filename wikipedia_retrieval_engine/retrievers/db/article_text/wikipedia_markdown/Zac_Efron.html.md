# Zac Efron

## Infobox

- **Born:** Zachary David Alexander Efron ( 1987-10-18 ) October 18, 1987 (age 38) San Luis Obispo, California, U.S.
- **Occupation:** Actor
- **Years active:** 2002–present
- **Relatives:** Dylan Efron (brother)

**Zachary David Alexander Efron** ([/ˈɛfrɒn/](/wiki/Help:IPA/English); born October 18, 1987) is an American actor. Efron began acting professionally in the early 2000s and rose to prominence as a [teen idol](/wiki/Teen_idol) for his leading role as [Troy Bolton](/wiki/Troy_Bolton) in the [*High School Musical* film series](/wiki/High_School_Musical_(franchise)) (2006–2008). During this time, he also starred in the musical film *[Hairspray](/wiki/Hairspray_(2007_film))* (2007) and the comedy film *[17 Again](/wiki/17_Again_(film))* (2009).

Efron had starring roles in *[New Year's Eve](/wiki/New_Year%27s_Eve_(2011_film))* (2011), *[The Lucky One](/wiki/The_Lucky_One_(film))* (2012), *[The Paperboy](/wiki/The_Paperboy_(2012_film))* (2012), *[Neighbors](/wiki/Neighbors_(2014_film))* (2014), *[That Awkward Moment](/wiki/That_Awkward_Moment)* (2014) *[Dirty Grandpa](/wiki/Dirty_Grandpa)* (2016), *[Baywatch](/wiki/Baywatch_(film))* (2017), and *[The Greatest Showman](/wiki/The_Greatest_Showman)* (2017). His roles have included [Ted Bundy](/wiki/Ted_Bundy) in *[Extremely Wicked, Shockingly Evil and Vile](/wiki/Extremely_Wicked,_Shockingly_Evil_and_Vile)* (2019) and [Kevin Von Erich](/wiki/Kevin_Von_Erich) in *[The Iron Claw](/wiki/The_Iron_Claw_(film))* (2023).

In 2021, he won a [Daytime Emmy Award](/wiki/Daytime_Emmy_Awards) for hosting the [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) travel show *[Down to Earth with Zac Efron](/wiki/Down_to_Earth_with_Zac_Efron)* (2020–2022). In 2023, he received [a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame](/wiki/List_of_actors_with_Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame_motion_picture_stars).

## Early life

Zachary David Alexander Efron was born on October 18, 1987, and grew up in [Arroyo Grande, California](/wiki/Arroyo_Grande,_California). His father, David Efron, is an electrical engineer at [Diablo Canyon Power Plant](/wiki/Diablo_Canyon_Power_Plant), and his mother, Starla Baskett, is an administrative assistant who also works at Diablo Canyon. Efron has a younger sister and two younger brothers, including [Dylan Efron](/wiki/Dylan_Efron), and had, as he has described, a "normal childhood" in a middle-class family. His surname originates from [Hebrew](/wiki/Hebrew). His paternal grandfather is [Jewish](/wiki/Jewish). Efron is Jewish, though he was raised [agnostically](/wiki/Agnosticism) and did not practice religion as a child.

He has said that he would "flip out" if he got a "B" and not an "A" in school, and was a class clown there. Efron subsequently worked at The Great American Melodrama and the Vaudeville theaters, and began taking singing lessons. He performed in shows such as *[Gypsy](/wiki/Gypsy:_A_Musical_Fable)*; *[Peter Pan, or The Boy Who Wouldn't Grow Up](/wiki/Peter_and_Wendy)*; *[Little Shop of Horrors](/wiki/Little_Shop_of_Horrors_(musical))*; and *[The Music Man](/wiki/The_Music_Man)*. He was recommended to an agent in Los Angeles by his drama teacher, Robyn Metchik, the mother of actor [Aaron Michael Metchik](/wiki/Aaron_Michael_Metchik). Efron was later signed to the [Creative Artists Agency](/wiki/Creative_Artists_Agency).

Efron graduated from [Arroyo Grande High School](/wiki/Arroyo_Grande_High_School) in 2006 and was then accepted into the [University of Southern California](/wiki/University_of_Southern_California) but did not enroll. He also attended [Pacific Conservatory of the Performing Arts](/wiki/Pacific_Conservatory_of_the_Performing_Arts), a theater company operating out of [Allan Hancock College](/wiki/Allan_Hancock_College), a [community college](/wiki/Community_college) located in [Santa Maria, California](/wiki/Santa_Maria,_California), where he performed in 2000 and 2001.

## Career

### 2002–2009: Early career and *High School Musical*

Efron at the [2007 Nickelodeon Australian Kids' Choice Awards](/wiki/Nickelodeon_Australian_Kids%27_Choice_Awards_2007)

Efron began acting in the early 2000s with guest roles on several television series including *[Firefly](/wiki/Firefly_(TV_series))*, *[ER](/wiki/ER_(TV_series))*, and *[The Guardian](/wiki/The_Guardian_(TV_series))*. In 2004, he began appearing as a recurring character in the first season of the [WB](/wiki/The_WB_Television_Network) series *[Summerland](/wiki/Summerland_(TV_series))*. For the show's second season, which aired in 2005, he was promoted to the main cast. He also appeared in some films, including the [Lifetime](/wiki/Lifetime_(TV_network)) television film *[Miracle Run](/wiki/Miracle_Run)* (2004), for which he earned a [Young Artist Award](/wiki/Young_Artist_Award) nomination for his performance as one of two autistic twins.

Efron's career reached a turning point with the teen musical television film *[High School Musical](/wiki/High_School_Musical)* (2006), which premiered on the [Disney Channel](/wiki/Disney_Channel) in January 2006. The film, which has been described as a modern adaptation of *[Romeo and Juliet](/wiki/Romeo_and_Juliet)*, saw Efron playing the male lead Troy Bolton, a high school basketball player who feels conflicted when he finds himself interested in participating in the school musical with Gabriella Montez ([Vanessa Hudgens](/wiki/Vanessa_Hudgens)), a girl from the scholastic decathlon team. The film's success helped Efron gain recognition among teenage audiences. The film's soundtrack was certified quadruple platinum by the [RIAA](/wiki/RIAA). However, Efron's recordings of the film's songs were not included in the final cut, and the majority of his parts were sung by [Drew Seeley](/wiki/Drew_Seeley); the Troy Bolton role had been intended for a tenor before Efron's casting and Efron was a baritone. In his subsequent musical films, Efron did his own singing.

Efron in 2008

Efron was next seen as Link Larkin in the musical comedy film *[Hairspray](/wiki/Hairspray_(2007_film))* (2007), based on the [2002 Broadway musical of the same name](/wiki/Hairspray_(musical)). Production conflicted with the [High School Musical concert tour](/wiki/High_School_Musical:_The_Concert), which prevented Efron from joining; Drew Seeley filled in. The film became a major commercial and critical success upon its release in July 2007. Later that year, he reprised his role of Troy Bolton in *[High School Musical 2](/wiki/High_School_Musical_2)* (2007), which aired on the Disney Channel in August 2007.

Efron again reprised his role in *[High School Musical 3: Senior Year](/wiki/High_School_Musical_3:_Senior_Year)* (2008), the first film in the *High School Musical* franchise to receive a theatrical release. The film succeeded at the box office, and received mixed to positive reviews from critics. He followed this with the commercially successful comedy *[17 Again](/wiki/17_Again_(film))* (2009) about a 37-year-old man who is transformed into his 17-year-old self (Efron) after a chance accident.

### 2009–present: Dramatic roles and mainstream projects

Efron in [2012 Cannes Film Festival](/wiki/2012_Cannes_Film_Festival)

Efron's next release was [Richard Linklater](/wiki/Richard_Linklater)'s period drama *[Me and Orson Welles](/wiki/Me_and_Orson_Welles)*, which premiered at the [Toronto International Film Festival](/wiki/2008_Toronto_International_Film_Festival) in September 2008 and received a wide release in late 2009. The film earned mostly positive reviews from critics. He then played the title role in the supernatural romantic drama *[Charlie St. Cloud](/wiki/Charlie_St._Cloud_(film))* (2010), which became a moderate success at the box office despite receiving mostly negative reviews from critics. Efron appeared as a part of the large ensemble cast in [Garry Marshall](/wiki/Garry_Marshall)'s *[New Year's Eve](/wiki/New_Year%27s_Eve_(2011_film))* (2011), which depicted a series of holiday vignettes of different groups of characters. The film received almost unanimously negative reviews from critics, but did well at the box office. He also played a supporting role in the critically successful *[Liberal Arts](/wiki/Liberal_Arts_(film))* (2012), which premiered at the [Sundance Film Festival](/wiki/2012_Sundance_Film_Festival) in January 2012 and received a limited release later that year. He also starred in *[The Paperboy](/wiki/The_Paperboy_(2012_film))* (2012), which premiered at the [Cannes Film Festival](/wiki/2012_Cannes_Film_Festival) in May 2012 and received a wider release later that year. The film received mixed to negative reviews from critics.

After lending his voice to the animated film *[The Lorax](/wiki/The_Lorax_(film))* (2012), he appeared as the male lead in the romantic drama *[The Lucky One](/wiki/The_Lucky_One_(film))* (2012), based on the [novel of the same name](/wiki/The_Lucky_One_(novel)) by [Nicholas Sparks](/wiki/Nicholas_Sparks). The film became a success financially despite negative reviews from critics. He also starred in the drama *[At Any Price](/wiki/At_Any_Price_(film))*, which premiered at the [2012 Venice International Film Festival](/wiki/69th_Venice_International_Film_Festival), and the historical drama *[Parkland](/wiki/Parkland_(film))*, which premiered at the [2013 Venice International Film Festival](/wiki/70th_Venice_International_Film_Festival). Both films received mixed reviews from critics. Efron's first release of 2014 was the romantic comedy *[That Awkward Moment](/wiki/That_Awkward_Moment)*, for which he also served as an executive producer. The film, which starred Efron as one of three bachelors in New York City, became a moderate commercial success despite receiving mostly negative reviews from critics.

Efron, 2012

Later in 2014, Efron was seen in the adult comedy *[Neighbors](/wiki/Neighbors_(2014_film))* (2014). The film revolved around a young couple who struggle to raise their baby daughter while living next to the house of a wild fraternity led by its president, played by Efron. The film was a commercial success and earned mostly positive reviews from critics, who also added that he had successfully shed his "Disney pretty boy" image.

Efron's only release in 2015 was the moderately successful *[We Are Your Friends](/wiki/We_Are_Your_Friends_(film))*, in which he played a struggling DJ. In January 2016, he starred in the adult comedy *[Dirty Grandpa](/wiki/Dirty_Grandpa)* as a straitlaced young man who begrudgingly indulges his grandfather's unhinged personality by taking him on a vacation to Florida. The film received mostly negative reviews from critics for its crude humor but became a commercial success. He subsequently starred in the comedy sequel *[Neighbors 2: Sorority Rising](/wiki/Neighbors_2:_Sorority_Rising)*, which became a commercial and critical success upon its release in May 2016. The film followed the same couple who team up with their former rival (Efron) to take down a hard-partying sorority led by a freshman. His third 2016 comedy, released in July, was *[Mike and Dave Need Wedding Dates](/wiki/Mike_and_Dave_Need_Wedding_Dates).*

Efron in 2017

In 2017, Efron starred as Matt Brody in *[Baywatch](/wiki/Baywatch_(film))*, an action comedy film version of the [television series of the same name](/wiki/Baywatch), released in May. Also in 2017, Efron had a supporting role in two biographical films released in December: *[The Disaster Artist](/wiki/The_Disaster_Artist_(film))*, a comedy-drama directed by and starring [James Franco](/wiki/James_Franco), and as Phillip in the musical *[The Greatest Showman](/wiki/The_Greatest_Showman)*. Both films were nominated for the [Golden Globe Award for Best Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Motion_Picture_%E2%80%93_Musical_or_Comedy).

In 2019, Efron played a drug-addled libertine in the [Harmony Korine](/wiki/Harmony_Korine) film *[The Beach Bum](/wiki/The_Beach_Bum)*. He also starred as serial killer [Ted Bundy](/wiki/Ted_Bundy) in *[Extremely Wicked, Shockingly Evil and Vile](/wiki/Extremely_Wicked,_Shockingly_Evil_and_Vile)*. The film premiered at Sundance in early 2019 and was released by Netflix on May 3.

Efron and *[Extremely Wicked, Shockingly Evil and Vile](/wiki/Extremely_Wicked,_Shockingly_Evil_and_Vile)* co-star [Lily Collins](/wiki/Lily_Collins) in 2019

In 2020, Efron voiced [Fred Jones](/wiki/Fred_Jones_(Scooby-Doo)) in Warner Bros.' *[Scooby-Doo](/wiki/Scooby-Doo)* animated film, *[Scoob!](/wiki/Scoob!)* In 2021, he won a [Daytime Emmy Award](/wiki/Daytime_Emmy_Awards) for Outstanding Daytime Program Host for the [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) [web documentary](/wiki/Web_documentary) series *[Down to Earth with Zac Efron](/wiki/Down_to_Earth_with_Zac_Efron)*.

In 2022, he starred in the survival thriller *[Gold](/wiki/Gold_(2022_Australian_film))*, directed by [Anthony Hayes](/wiki/Anthony_Hayes_(actor)) and headlined the horror film *[Firestarter](/wiki/Firestarter_(2022_film))*, a remake of the [1984 film](/wiki/Firestarter_(1984_film)) based upon the [novel of the same name](/wiki/Firestarter_(novel)) by [Stephen King](/wiki/Stephen_King), directed by Keith Thomas. Also that year, he starred in [Peter Farrelly](/wiki/Peter_Farrelly)'s Vietnam War–set comedy *[The Greatest Beer Run Ever](/wiki/The_Greatest_Beer_Run_Ever)*, which received mixed reviews.

In 2023, he starred as [Kevin Von Erich](/wiki/Kevin_Von_Erich) in [Sean Durkin](/wiki/Sean_Durkin)'s *[The Iron Claw](/wiki/The_Iron_Claw_(film))*, about the [Von Erich family](/wiki/Von_Erich_family) of wrestlers. That same year, Efron was awarded his own star on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame).

In 2024, Efron played Chris Cole, the role with [Joey King](/wiki/Joey_King) and [Nicole Kidman](/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) in the romantic comedy [*A Family Affair*](/wiki/A_Family_Affair_(2024_film)) of [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix). That same year, he also played a lead role in the comedy *[Ricky Stanicky](/wiki/Ricky_Stanicky)*.

In 2025, Efron played himself in an episode of the comedy series [*The Studio*](/wiki/The_Studio_(TV_series)).

In 2026, Efron is set to play a lead role with [Debby Ryan](/wiki/Debby_Ryan) and [Phoebe Dynevor](/wiki/Phoebe_Dynevor) in the thriller movie [*Famous*](/wiki/Famous_(film)). That same year, he is also expected to play a lead role with [Will Ferrell](/wiki/Will_Ferrell) and [Regina Hall](/wiki/Regina_Hall) in the comedy film [*Judgment Day*](/wiki/Judgment_Day_(upcoming_film)).

### Other work

In 2010, Efron started his own production company under [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.), Ninjas Runnin' Wild. The company played a part in the production of his films *Dirty Grandpa*, *That Awkward Moment*, and *Extremely Wicked, Shockingly Evil and Vile*. In 2019, Efron's brother [Dylan Efron](/wiki/Dylan_Efron), who also plays a role in the company, stated that Ninjas Runnin' Wild has started to produce more digital content in addition to their traditional film work.

Efron announced the creation of his [YouTube](/wiki/YouTube) channel in March 2019. The [video sharing platform](/wiki/Online_video_platform) hosts two weekly series. "Off the Grid" follows Efron and his brother Dylan as they participate in outdoor activities and trips without electronic devices—with the exception of a video camera in order to document their experiences. "Gym Time" spotlights fitness and nutrition, with Efron informing his viewers that he plans to "train with celebrities, athletes, and interesting people". YouTube received backlash for promoting Efron's new channel in a post made from their official Twitter account. Some YouTube users accused the platform of promoting a mainstream celebrity's account, which they feared would overshadow lesser known creators.

## Personal life

Efron was on the *[Forbes](/wiki/Forbes)* [Celebrity 100](/wiki/Celebrity_100) list in 2008 at number 92, with estimated earnings of $5.8 million from June 2007 to June 2008. In April 2009, his personal wealth equaled about $10 million. In May 2015, Efron's net worth was $18 million.

In March 2014, Efron fought with a [homeless](/wiki/Homeless) man on [Skid Row](/wiki/Skid_Row,_Los_Angeles). Law enforcement officials did not make any arrests because they viewed it as [mutual combat](/wiki/Mutual_combat).

Efron turned to [Transcendental Meditation](/wiki/Transcendental_Meditation) to help decompress after struggling to separate himself from his role as serial killer Ted Bundy. "I really love TM, transcendental meditation. I did TM on the way home. Not while driving, but when you're shooting a movie, one thing we can afford is a ride home, so on the way home I just do some TM and try to phase out of it."

Efron moved to Australia after selling his Los Angeles home in early 2021 and purchased a property in [Byron Bay](/wiki/Byron_Bay) while working on projects there.

### Relationships

Efron with Hudgens at [81st Academy Awards](/wiki/81st_Academy_Awards), 2009

From 2005 to 2010, Efron was in a relationship with actress [Vanessa Hudgens](/wiki/Vanessa_Hudgens), whom he met on the set of *[High School Musical](/wiki/High_School_Musical)*.

In 2011, he briefly dated actress [Lindsay Lohan](/wiki/Lindsay_Lohan).

In 2012–2013, he was in a relationship with actress [Lily Collins](/wiki/Lily_Collins).

In September 2014 to April 2016, he was in a relationship with model Sami Miro.

In 2017, he had a three-month relationship with actress [Alexandra Daddario](/wiki/Alexandra_Daddario), whom he met on the set of [*Baywatch*](/wiki/Baywatch_(film)).

From June 2020 to April 2021, Efron dated Australian model Vanessa Valladares.

### Health problems

Efron sought treatment in early 2013 after struggling with [alcoholism](/wiki/Alcoholism) and [substance abuse](/wiki/Substance_abuse). He got sober in June 2013. In November 2013, Efron had to have his [jaw wired shut](/wiki/Jaw_wiring) after breaking it in a fall at home. He revealed in 2022 that he almost died during this incident, and that it was responsible for his facial swelling, apparent in a viral 2021 video for [Bill Nye](/wiki/Bill_Nye)'s Earth Day Musical.

In December 2019, Efron contracted a serious and potentially life-threatening illness, a "form of [typhoid](/wiki/Typhoid) or similar [bacterial infection](/wiki/Bacterial_infection)", while filming his adventure series *Killing Zac Efron* in [Papua New Guinea](/wiki/Papua_New_Guinea). He was flown to [St Andrews War Memorial Hospital](/wiki/St_Andrews_War_Memorial_Hospital) in [Brisbane](/wiki/Brisbane), Australia, and was treated before flying back home on December 25. He recovered with little issue.

Efron was a [vegan](/wiki/Veganism) for two years until 2022, when he began [intermittent fasting](/wiki/Intermittent_fasting), and reintroduced meat into his diet after undergoing a series of [food sensitivity](/wiki/Food_sensitivity) tests.

In 2022, Efron opened up about his struggles with [insomnia](/wiki/Insomnia), [agoraphobia](/wiki/Agoraphobia), and [depression](/wiki/Depression_(mood)). He developed insomnia and depression after taking [diuretics](/wiki/Diuretics) for a long period to prepare for his role in *Baywatch*.

In August 2024, Efron was rushed to the hospital after being found unconscious in a swimming pool in [Ibiza](/wiki/Ibiza). According to TMZ, he was discovered by two villa employees who pulled him out of the water. The hospitalization was described as a "precautionary measure" and the incident as "minor". Efron aspirated a significant amount of water into his lungs and had x-rays taken to ensure his lungs were clear before being discharged the following day.

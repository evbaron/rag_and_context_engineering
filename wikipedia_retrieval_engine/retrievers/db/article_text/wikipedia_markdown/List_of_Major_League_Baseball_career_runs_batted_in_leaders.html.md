# List of Major League Baseball career runs batted in leaders

Hank Aaron, career leader in runs batted in (RBIs).

This is a list of [Major League Baseball](/wiki/Major_League_Baseball) players who have compiled 1,000 [runs batted in](/wiki/Runs_batted_in) (RBIs). RBIs are usually accumulated when a batter in [baseball](/wiki/Baseball) enables a runner on base (including himself, in the case of a [home run](/wiki/Home_run)) to score as a result of making contact at-bat (except in certain situations, such as when an error is made on the play or during a double play). A batter is also credited with an RBI if he reaches first base with the bases loaded via a [base on balls](/wiki/Base_on_balls) (walk), being [hit by a pitch](/wiki/Hit_by_pitch), or interference.

MLB's official list does not include RBIs accumulated before 1920 when runs batted in became an official statistic. The list on this page is compiled from [Baseball-Reference](/wiki/Baseball-Reference.com), which credits RBIs from 1907 to 1919 as recorded by baseball writer and historian [Ernest Lanigan](/wiki/Ernest_Lanigan). One difference between the lists is that [Babe Ruth](/wiki/Babe_Ruth) is ranked third by Baseball Reference, but seventh by MLB, which does not count Ruth's 224 RBI compiled before 1920.

As of May 7, 2026, [Freddie Freeman](/wiki/Freddie_Freeman) is the active leader in career RBIs and tied for 93rd overall with 1,341.

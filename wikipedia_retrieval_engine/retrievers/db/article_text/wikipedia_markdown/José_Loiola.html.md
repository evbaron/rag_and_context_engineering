# José Loiola

## Infobox

- **Full name:** José Geraldo Loiola
- **Born:** March 28, 1970 ( 1970-03-28 ) (age 56) Vitória, Brazil
- **Height:** 6 ft 4 in (193 cm)

**José Geraldo Loiola** (born March 28, 1970) is a Brazilian former [beach volleyball](/wiki/Beach_volleyball) player. He won the gold medal at the [1999 Beach Volleyball World Championships](/wiki/1999_Beach_Volleyball_World_Championships) in [Marseille](/wiki/Marseille), partnering with [Emanuel Rego](/wiki/Emanuel_Rego).

Loiola began playing beach volleyball in the [AVP](/wiki/Association_of_Volleyball_Professionals) in 1993 with partner [Eduardo "Anjinho" Bacil](/wiki/Anjinho_Bacil). He was selected as the AVP Rookie of the Year in 1993. He eventually won 11 tournaments partnering with [Adam Johnson](/wiki/Adam_Johnson_(volleyball)) and 17 partnering with [Kent Steffes](/wiki/Kent_Steffes). He was named MVP of the AVP tour in 1997, and was voted Best Offensive Player of the AVP four times. In total, he won 55 tournaments and $1,900,000 in prize money in the AVP and [FIVB](/wiki/International_Volleyball_Federation). Loiola was noted for his exceptional vertical jump.

Loiola represented his native country at the [2000 Summer Olympics](/wiki/2000_Summer_Olympics) in [Sydney](/wiki/Sydney), and added a silver medal to his tally a year later at the [2001 Beach Volleyball World Championships](/wiki/2001_Beach_Volleyball_World_Championships) in [Klagenfurt](/wiki/Klagenfurt), alongside [Ricardo Santos](/wiki/Ricardo_Santos_(beach_volleyball)).

## Hall of Fame

In 2014, Loiola was inducted into the [California Beach Volleyball Hall of Fame](/wiki/California_Beach_Volleyball_Association). In 2017, he was inducted into the [International Volleyball Hall of Fame](/wiki/International_Volleyball_Hall_of_Fame).

## Coaching

Loiola coached [Sara Hughes](/wiki/Sara_Hughes) and [Kelly Claes](/wiki/Kelly_Claes) of the [United States](/wiki/United_States) in preparation for the [2020 Olympic](/wiki/2020_Summer_Olympics) beach volleyball qualification. He currently works as a coach for the United States beach national team.

**David Crane** (born August 13, 1957) is an American writer and producer. He is best known as one of the co-creators of the television sitcoms *[Friends](/wiki/Friends)* and *[Episodes](/wiki/Episodes_(TV_series))*.

## Early life

Crane was born in [West New York, New Jersey](/wiki/West_New_York,_New_Jersey), the son of Joan Crane and veteran [Philadelphia](/wiki/Philadelphia) television personality Gene Crane in a traditional Jewish household. He attended [Harriton High School](/wiki/Harriton_High_School) in Pennsylvania and graduated from there in 1975. He is a 1979 graduate of [Brandeis University](/wiki/Brandeis_University).

## Career

He and his husband, [Jeffrey Klarik](/wiki/Jeffrey_Klarik) (creator of *[Half & Half](/wiki/Half_%26_Half)*, co-producer *[Mad About You](/wiki/Mad_About_You)*), created the 2006 [ensemble](/wiki/Ensemble_cast) sitcom *[The Class](/wiki/The_Class_(TV_series))*.

In 2011, Crane and Klarik created a sitcom called *[Episodes](/wiki/Episodes_(TV_series))* for the UK-based [BBC](/wiki/BBC). Airing first in the US on [Showtime](/wiki/Showtime_(TV_network)) on Sunday January 9, 2011 and then on [BBC Two](/wiki/BBC_Two) on Monday January 10, 2011, it features *Friends* star [Matt LeBlanc](/wiki/Matt_LeBlanc) and *[Green Wing](/wiki/Green_Wing)'*s [Stephen Mangan](/wiki/Stephen_Mangan) and [Tamsin Greig](/wiki/Tamsin_Greig).

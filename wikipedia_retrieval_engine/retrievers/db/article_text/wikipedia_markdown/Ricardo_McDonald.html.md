**Ricardo Milton McDonald** (born November 8, 1969) is a former [linebacker](/wiki/Linebacker) in the [National Football League](/wiki/National_Football_League) (NFL) that played for the [Cincinnati Bengals](/wiki/Cincinnati_Bengals), [Chicago Bears](/wiki/Chicago_Bears), and the [Denver Broncos](/wiki/Denver_Broncos). He was drafted by the Bengals in the fourth round of the 1992 NFL Draft with the 88th overall pick. He played [college football](/wiki/College_football) for the [University of Pittsburgh](/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh) under head coach [Mike Gottfried](/wiki/Mike_Gottfried) and [Paul Hackett](/wiki/Paul_Hackett_(American_football)).

He played for [Eastside High School](/wiki/Eastside_High_School_(Paterson,_New_Jersey)) in [Paterson, New Jersey](/wiki/Paterson,_New_Jersey) under Coach Barrett 'Barry' Rosser. [Joe Louis Clark](/wiki/Joe_Louis_Clark) was the school's Principal during his attendance there, as depicted in the film *[Lean on Me](/wiki/Lean_on_Me_(film))*.

## Family tree

Ricardo has a twin brother [Devon McDonald](/wiki/Devon_McDonald) who played linebacker for University of Notre Dame under Coach [Lou Holtz](/wiki/Lou_Holtz). He went on to play professionally for the [Indianapolis Colts](/wiki/Indianapolis_Colts) and the [Arizona Cardinals](/wiki/Arizona_Cardinals). Ricardo has a son named Maurice who played linebacker for [Tennessee State University](/wiki/Tennessee_State_University) under Head Coach [Eddie George](/wiki/Eddie_George) and also played linebacker for [Albany State University](/wiki/Albany_State_University) under head coach [Quinn Gray](/wiki/Quinn_Gray). His younger cousin [Lennox Gordon](/wiki/Lennox_Gordon) played collegiately for [University of New Mexico](/wiki/University_of_New_Mexico). He went on to play professionally for the [Indianapolis Colts](/wiki/Indianapolis_Colts) and the [Buffalo Bills](/wiki/Buffalo_Bills). Ricardo's Nephew Maurice McDonald played Wide receiver for [University of Maine](/wiki/University_of_Maine) from 2009 to 2012. McDonald's eldest daughter Brittany McDonald played basketball for [Tulane University](/wiki/Tulane_University) from 2010 to 2013. He also has a niece, Jazzmine McDonald who played for [Indiana University](/wiki/Indiana_University) from 2013 to 2016.

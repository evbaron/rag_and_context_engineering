**Kennebec County** is a [county](/wiki/County_(United_States)) located in the South-central portion of the [U.S. state](/wiki/U.S._state) of [Maine](/wiki/Maine). At the [2020 census](/wiki/2020_United_States_census), the population was 123,642. Its [county seat](/wiki/County_seat) is [Augusta](/wiki/Augusta,_Maine), the [state's capital](/wiki/List_of_capitals_in_the_United_States). The county was established on February 20, 1799, from portions of [Cumberland](/wiki/Cumberland_County,_Maine) and [Lincoln](/wiki/Lincoln_County,_Maine) Counties. The name "Kennebec" comes from the [Eastern Abenaki](/wiki/Eastern_Abnaki_language) */kínipekʷ/*, meaning "large body of still water, large bay".

Kennebec County comprises the *Augusta–[Waterville](/wiki/Waterville,_Maine), ME [Micropolitan Statistical Area](/wiki/Micropolitan_Statistical_Area)*.

In 2010, the [center of population](/wiki/Center_of_population) of Maine was in Kennebec County, in the city of Augusta.

## Geography

According to the [U.S. Census Bureau](/wiki/U.S._Census_Bureau), the county has a total area of 951 square miles (2,460 km2), of which 868 square miles (2,250 km2) is land and 84 square miles (220 km2) (8.8%) is water.

The county is nearly bisected by the [Kennebec River](/wiki/Kennebec_River), which also forms parts of the county line in the north and south of the county. The county is also dotted by many lakes and ponds, as well as the Kennebec Highlands in the northwest part of the county.

### 2020 census

As of the [2020 census](/wiki/2020_United_States_census), the county had a population of 123,642. Of the residents, 18.8% were under the age of 18 and 21.1% were 65 years of age or older; the median age was 44.9 years. For every 100 females there were 95.7 males, and for every 100 females age 18 and over there were 93.9 males. 37.8% of residents lived in urban areas and 62.2% lived in rural areas.

The racial makeup of the county was 91.9% White, 0.7% [Black or African American](/wiki/African_Americans), 0.4% [American Indian and Alaska Native](/wiki/Native_Americans_in_the_United_States), 1.0% [Asian](/wiki/Asian_Americans), 0.0% [Native Hawaiian](/wiki/Native_Hawaiians) and [Pacific Islander](/wiki/Pacific_Islander), 0.7% from some other race, and 5.2% from [two or more races](/wiki/Multiracial_Americans). [Hispanic or Latino](/wiki/Hispanic_and_Latino_Americans) residents of any race comprised 2.1% of the population.

There were 53,223 households in the county, of which 24.6% had children under the age of 18 living with them and 26.2% had a female householder with no spouse or partner present. About 30.9% of all households were made up of individuals and 13.7% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older.

There were 62,607 housing units, of which 15.0% were vacant. Among occupied housing units, 70.6% were owner-occupied and 29.4% were renter-occupied. The homeowner vacancy rate was 1.4% and the rental vacancy rate was 6.0%.

### 2010 census

As of the [2010 United States census](/wiki/2010_United_States_census), there were 122,151 people, 51,128 households, and 32,368 families living in the county. The population density was 140.8 inhabitants per square mile (54.4/km2). There were 60,972 housing units at an average density of 70.3 units per square mile (27.1 units/km2). The racial makeup of the county was % from other races, and % from two or more races. Those of Hispanic or Latino origin made up % of the population. In terms of ancestry, 24.2% were [English](/wiki/English_people), 17.0% were [Irish](/wiki/Irish_people), 8.3% were [German](/wiki/Germans), 7.7% were [French Canadian](/wiki/French_Canadian), 5.7% were [American](/wiki/Americans), and 5.4% were [Scottish](/wiki/Scottish_people).

The median income for a household in the county was $45,973 and the median income for a family was $56,853. Males had a median income of $42,176 versus $32,471 for females. The per capita income for the county was $24,656. About 7.8% of families and 12.5% of the population were below the [poverty line](/wiki/Poverty_line), including 16.9% of those under age 18 and 9.6% of those age 65 or over.

### 2000 census

As of the [census](/wiki/Census) of 2000, there were 117,114 people, 47,683 households, and 31,327 families living in the county. The [population density](/wiki/Population_density) was 135 people per square mile (52 people/km2). There were 56,364 housing units at an average density of 65 per square mile (25/km2). The racial makeup of the county was 97.45% [White](/wiki/Race_(United_States_Census)), 0.34% [Black](/wiki/Race_(United_States_Census)) or [African American](/wiki/Race_(United_States_Census)), 0.40% [Native American](/wiki/Race_(United_States_Census)), 0.59% [Asian](/wiki/Race_(United_States_Census)), 0.02% [Pacific Islander](/wiki/Race_(United_States_Census)), 0.18% from [other races](/wiki/Race_(United_States_Census)), and 1.02% from two or more races. 0.73% of the population were [Hispanic](/wiki/Race_(United_States_Census)) or [Latino](/wiki/Race_(United_States_Census)) of any race. 20.6% were of [English](/wiki/English_people), 17.4% [French](/wiki/French_people), 13.4% [French Canadian](/wiki/French_Canadian), 10.9% [United States](/wiki/United_States) or American and 9.9% [Irish](/wiki/Irish_people) ancestry. 92.5% spoke only [English](/wiki/English_language) at home, while 5.48% spoke [French](/wiki/French_language).

There were 47,683 households, out of which 31.20% had children under the age of 18 living with them, 51.60% were [married couples](/wiki/Marriage) living together, 10.00% had a female householder with no husband present, and 34.30% were non-families. 27.60% of all households were made up of individuals, and 10.60% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older. The average household size was 2.38 and the average family size was 2.89.

In the county, the population was spread out, with 23.80% under the age of 18, 8.50% from 18 to 24, 28.60% from 25 to 44, 24.90% from 45 to 64, and 14.20% who were 65 years of age or older. The median age was 39 years. For every 100 females there were 94.00 males. For every 100 females age 18 and over, there were 90.80 males.

The median income for a household in the county was $36,498, and the median income for a family was $43,814. Males had a median income of $32,279 versus $24,032 for females. The [per capita income](/wiki/Per_capita_income) for the county was $18,520. About 8.50% of families and 11.10% of the population were below the [poverty line](/wiki/Poverty_line), including 13.20% of those under age 18 and 10.20% of those age 65 or over.

## Micropolitan statistical area

See also: [List of micropolitan statistical areas](/wiki/List_of_micropolitan_statistical_areas)

Kennebec County has been designated as the **Augusta–Waterville, ME [Micropolitan Statistical Area](/wiki/Micropolitan_Statistical_Area) (μSA)** by the [United States Office of Management and Budget](/wiki/United_States_Office_of_Management_and_Budget). As of the [2010 U.S. census](/wiki/2010_United_States_census) the micropolitan area ranked the most populous in the State of Maine and also the only micropolitan area within the state. The area also ranks [14th most populous](/wiki/List_of_micropolitan_statistical_areas) in the United States with a population of 125,540 as of July 2022.

## Economy

Kennebec County's economy has traditionally been industrial. A large amount of paper and textiles were produced, and mills sprung up along the Kennebec river. The median household income is $36,498. The unemployment rate is 4.2%.

## Education

There are three colleges: [Colby College](/wiki/Colby_College) and [Thomas College](/wiki/Thomas_College) (both in [Waterville](/wiki/Waterville,_Maine)), and the [University of Maine at Augusta](/wiki/University_of_Maine_at_Augusta). There are 19 school districts (however, 4 are consolidated ones). In [China](/wiki/China,_Maine), there is no public high school, but a private school ([Erskine Academy](/wiki/Erskine_Academy)) exists, and the town pays for students' tuition if they choose to attend. Students from [Vassalboro](/wiki/Vassalboro,_Maine) can pick from one of four school districts (Cony in Augusta, Erskine, [Winslow](/wiki/Winslow,_Maine), or Waterville). However, many towns have local public high schools.

## Politics

Kennebec County is the only county in Maine that is split between Maine's two congressional districts. The larger, southern portion of the county is in the [1st Congressional District](/wiki/Maine%27s_1st_congressional_district), while the northern, smaller portion is in the [2nd Congressional District](/wiki/Maine%27s_2nd_congressional_district). While the more rural areas support the [Republican Party](/wiki/Republican_Party_(U.S.)), the urban areas are strongly [Democratic](/wiki/U.S._Democratic_Party).

Historically, the county was more Democratic-leaning than the rest of the state in presidential elections. Kennebec County had consistently voted for Democrats in recent elections, until [2016](/wiki/2016_United_States_presidential_election) when it voted for Republican [Donald Trump](/wiki/Donald_Trump), the first time it had voted Republican since [1988](/wiki/1988_United_States_presidential_election). The county voted for Democrat [Franklin D. Roosevelt](/wiki/Franklin_D._Roosevelt) in [1940](/wiki/1940_United_States_presidential_election) even as Republican [Wendell Willkie](/wiki/Wendell_Willkie) narrowly carried Maine, and it voted for Democrat [Jimmy Carter](/wiki/Jimmy_Carter) in [1976](/wiki/1976_United_States_presidential_election) as Republican [Gerald Ford](/wiki/Gerald_Ford) also narrowly won the state. In more recent elections, the county voted in-line with the state, voting for the statewide winner in every election from [1980](/wiki/1980_United_States_presidential_election) to [2012](/wiki/2012_United_States_presidential_election). In [2020](/wiki/2020_United_States_presidential_election), it was the closest county in the state, with Democrat [Joe Biden](/wiki/Joe_Biden) defeating Trump by just 181 votes. Kennebec swung back into the Republican column in [2024](/wiki/2024_United_States_presidential_election_in_Maine), with Trump carrying the county this time with a full majority.

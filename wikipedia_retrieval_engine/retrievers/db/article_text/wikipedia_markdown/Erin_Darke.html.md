**Erin Constance-Maja Darke** (born September 10, 1984) is an American actress. She is known for her role as Cindy Reston in the TV series *[Good Girls Revolt](/wiki/Good_Girls_Revolt)*. She also played Mary in *[The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel](/wiki/The_Marvelous_Mrs._Maisel)* and Leeta in the AMC series *[Dietland](/wiki/Dietland_(TV_series))*. She has appeared in the films *[Love & Mercy](/wiki/Love_%26_Mercy_(film))* (2014), *[Beside Still Waters](/wiki/Beside_Still_Waters_(film))* (2014), *[Still Alice](/wiki/Still_Alice)* (2014), and *[Don't Think Twice](/wiki/Don%27t_Think_Twice_(film))* (2016).

## Early life

Erin Constance-Maja Darke was born September 10, 1984, in [Flint, Michigan](/wiki/Flint,_Michigan) to Kristina Strand and Ian Darke.

At the age of 16, Darke enrolled in the [University of Michigan-Flint](/wiki/University_of_Michigan%E2%80%93Flint), where in 2005 she earned a [bachelor's degree](/wiki/Bachelor%27s_degree) of fine arts in theater performance. As Darke told her [alma mater](/wiki/Alma_mater), "[The teachers at the school] were patient and kind, but also constantly pushed me to challenge myself, explore, and work hard. These are all things that are essential to a career in this industry and I'm grateful to have learned them early."

## Career

Shortly after graduating, Darke moved to [New York City](/wiki/New_York_City) and starred in several plays before getting a job at a casting office. This was her full time job until she decided that "my heart was still in acting" and went on to star in *Obelisk Road*, a 2009 short film, and procured minor roles in the 2011 films [*We Need to Talk About Kevin*](/wiki/We_Need_to_Talk_About_Kevin_(film)) and [*Young Adult*](/wiki/Young_Adult_(film)). In 2012, Darke had a minor role in [*Disconnect*](/wiki/Disconnect_(2012_film)). Darke played a small role in the 2013 thriller [*Kill Your Darlings*](/wiki/Kill_Your_Darlings_(2013_film)). Along with *Kill Your Darlings*, Darke played a lead role in [*Beside Still Waters*](/wiki/Beside_Still_Waters_(film)). In 2014, Darke acted in *[Still Alice](/wiki/Still_Alice)* and *[Hunter&Game](/wiki/Hunter%26Game)*, along with several other films.

In 2015, she landed a lead role in the television series *[Good Girls Revolt](/wiki/Good_Girls_Revolt)*, which follows several young female news reporters who sue their male bosses for showing them [sexism](/wiki/Sexism). Darke starred in the 2016 film *Delinquent*. In 2017, Darke played Mary in *[The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel](/wiki/The_Marvelous_Mrs._Maisel)*. Darke acted in the 2018 TV series [*Dietland*](/wiki/Dietland_(TV_series)). Together with Radcliffe, Darke starred in *[Miracle Workers](/wiki/Miracle_Workers_(2019_TV_series))* in 2019. In 2023, Darke played an [AI](/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) in *[Molli and Max in the Future](/wiki/Molli_and_Max_in_the_Future)*.

## Personal life

Darke has been in a relationship with English actor [Daniel Radcliffe](/wiki/Daniel_Radcliffe) since 2012, having met on the set of the film *[Kill Your Darlings](/wiki/Kill_Your_Darlings_(2013_film))*. They have a son, who was born in April 2023.

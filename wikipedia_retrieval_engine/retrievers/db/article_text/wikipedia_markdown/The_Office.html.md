# The Office

For other uses, see [Office (disambiguation)](/wiki/Office_(disambiguation)). Not to be confused with [Office Space](/wiki/Office_Space).

[Title cards](/wiki/Title_card) for the British original (top) and American version (bottom)

***The Office*** is the title of several [mockumentary](/wiki/Mockumentary) sitcoms based on a British series originally created by [Ricky Gervais](/wiki/Ricky_Gervais) and [Stephen Merchant](/wiki/Stephen_Merchant) as *[The Office](/wiki/The_Office_(British_TV_series))* in 2001. The original series also starred Gervais as manager and primary character [David Brent](/wiki/David_Brent). The two series were broadcast on [BBC Two](/wiki/BBC_Two) in 2001 and 2002, totalling 12 episodes, with two special episodes concluding the series in 2003. A follow up movie (*[David Brent: Life on the Road](/wiki/David_Brent:_Life_on_the_Road)*) starring Gervais and featuring his David Brent character was released in 2016.

Versions of the original were subsequently made in Germany, the United States, and many other countries. The longest-running version of the series, the [American adaptation](/wiki/The_Office_(American_TV_series)), ran for nine seasons on the [NBC Television Network](/wiki/NBC_Television_Network) from 2005 to 2013, with a total of 201 episodes. According to [Nielsen Ratings](/wiki/Nielsen_Ratings) as of April 2019, the American version of *The Office* was the No. 1 streamed show on [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) in the United States. A [follow-up to the American version](/wiki/The_Paper_(2025_TV_series)) was announced in 2024 for release on [Peacock](/wiki/Peacock_(streaming_service)), and [Amazon Prime Video](/wiki/Amazon_Prime_Video) also debuted an [Australian version of *The Office*](/wiki/The_Office_(Australian_TV_series)), featuring the franchise's first female lead character, that same year.

On the Swedish series, *Kontoret*, it can be particularly noted that it was made as a combined remake as well as a spin-off, since the part as head of the office was given to the obnoxious character Ove Sundberg from the established Swedish TV series [Solsidan](/wiki/Solsidan_(TV_series)). Actor [Henrik Dorsin](/wiki/Henrik_Dorsin), who plays the character, stated in an interview, "it feels good to contribute with something of my own, Ove that is, when you're supposed to work with a well established material such as The office. So that it doesn't just become repetition. Furthermore, it's fun to take part in the first combined remake and spin-off ever in Sweden, or maybe even in the world? A spin-make. Or a re-off."

### Unproduced versions

Additional versions of the show have been planned but never released. A [Russian language](/wiki/Russian_language) version for [Channel One](/wiki/Channel_One_(Russia)) with an initial run of 24 episodes was announced in 2008, but had not begun production by 2018. Additionally, [Ricky Gervais](/wiki/Ricky_Gervais) announced plans to create a [Chinese language](/wiki/Chinese_language) version of the show in 2010, but the show also had not begun production as of 2018.

- American version: 2006 Golden Globe for Best Performance by an Actor in a Television Series – Musical or Comedy (Steve Carell); 2006 [Emmy Award](/wiki/Emmy_Award) for Outstanding Comedy Series; 2007 [Screen Actors Guild Award](/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award) for Outstanding Performance by an Ensemble in a Comedy Series; 2007 Emmy Award for Outstanding Writing for a Comedy Series (Greg Daniels); 2009 Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Directing for a Comedy Series (Jeffrey Blitz). For other awards and honors see *[List of awards and nominations received by The Office (American TV series)](/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_The_Office_(American_TV_series))*
- German version: 2006 [Grimme Award](/wiki/Adolf_Grimme_Awards) for Fiction/Entertainment – Series/Miniseries; 2007 [Deutscher Fernsehpreis](/wiki/Deutscher_Fernsehpreis) for Best Sitcom and Best Book; 2006, 2007, 2010 and 2012 [Deutscher Comedypreis](/wiki/Deutscher_Comedypreis) for Best Actor in a Comedy Series (Christoph Maria Herbst).

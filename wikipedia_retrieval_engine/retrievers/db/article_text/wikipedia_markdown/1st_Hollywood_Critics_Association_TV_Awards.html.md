# 1st Hollywood Critics Association TV Awards

## Infobox

- **Date:** August 29, 2021
- **Site:** Virtual presentation via YouTube
- **Most wins:** Ted Lasso (4)
- **Most nominations:** Ted Lasso (8)
- **Website:** hollywoodcriticsassociation.com
- **Network:** YouTube

The **1st Hollywood Critics Association TV Awards**, presented by the [Hollywood Critics Association](/wiki/Hollywood_Creative_Alliance), took place through a virtual ceremony on [YouTube](/wiki/YouTube) on August 29, 2021. However, due to the rising number of [COVID-19 cases](/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_cases) in [Los Angeles County](/wiki/Los_Angeles_County,_California) and the US, the ceremony was delayed a week and the broadcast was originally supposed to be held at the [Westin Bonaventure Hotel](/wiki/Westin_Bonaventure_Hotel) in [Los Angeles](/wiki/Los_Angeles) on August 22, 2021.

The nominations were announced via a [livestream](/wiki/Livestreaming) on July 8, 2021, by [Mckenna Grace](/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) and [Brooklynn Prince](/wiki/Brooklynn_Prince) on the organization's official [YouTube](/wiki/YouTube) channel. *[Ted Lasso](/wiki/Ted_Lasso)* led the nominations with 8, followed by *[The Handmaid's Tale](/wiki/The_Handmaid%27s_Tale_(TV_series))* and *[WandaVision](/wiki/WandaVision)* with 7 each. Channelwise, [HBO](/wiki/HBO) and [NBC](/wiki/NBC) led the nominations with 29 each, followed by [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) with 28.

The HCA TV Awards also became the first television awards organization to separate streaming programs from broadcast network/cable shows, electing to give the streaming platforms their own separate category in comedy and drama, with the exception of the limited series/tv movie field, which isn't divided by platform.

*[Ted Lasso](/wiki/Ted_Lasso)* won the most awards of the night with four total, followed by *[The Crown](/wiki/The_Crown_(TV_series))* with three.

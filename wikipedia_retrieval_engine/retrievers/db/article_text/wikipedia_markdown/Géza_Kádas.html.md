The native form of this [personal name](/wiki/Personal_name) is *Kádas Géza*. This article uses [Western name order](/wiki/Personal_name#Western_name_order) when mentioning individuals.

**Géza Kádas** (7 August 1926 – 6 March 1979) was a Hungarian [swimmer](/wiki/Swimming_(sport)) and Olympic medalist. He participated at the [1948](/wiki/1948_Summer_Olympics) and [1952](/wiki/1952_Summer_Olympics) Summer Olympics, winning a silver medal in [4 × 200 metre freestyle relay](/wiki/Swimming_at_the_1948_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_4_x_200_metre_freestyle_relay), and a bronze medal in [100 metre freestyle](/wiki/Swimming_at_the_1948_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_100_metre_freestyle) in 1948. Kádas died in [Budapest](/wiki/Budapest) on 6 March 1979, at the age of 52.

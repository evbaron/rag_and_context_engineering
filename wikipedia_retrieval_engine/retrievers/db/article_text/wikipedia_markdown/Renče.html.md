**Renče** (pronounced [[ˈɾeːntʃɛ]](/wiki/Help:IPA/Slovene); [Italian](/wiki/Italian_language): *Ranziano*) is a settlement in the lower [Vipava Valley](/wiki/Vipava_Valley) in the [Municipality of Renče–Vogrsko](/wiki/Municipality_of_Ren%C4%8De%E2%80%93Vogrsko) in the [Littoral](/wiki/Slovenian_Littoral) region of [Slovenia](/wiki/Slovenia).

The hamlets of [Arčoni](/wiki/Ar%C4%8Doni), Lukežiči, Martinuči, Mohorini, Merljaki (Mrljaki until 1993), Renški Podkraj, and Žigoni were independent settlements until 2000, when they were incorporated into Renče.

The [parish church](/wiki/Parish_church) in the settlement is dedicated to [Saints Hermagoras and Fortunatus](/wiki/Hermagoras_of_Aquileia) and belongs to the [Diocese of Koper](/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Koper).

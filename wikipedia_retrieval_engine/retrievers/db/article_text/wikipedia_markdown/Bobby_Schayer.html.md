**Bobby Schayer** (born December 23, 1966, in [Los Angeles](/wiki/Los_Angeles), California) is an American drummer. He was a member of [Bad Religion](/wiki/Bad_Religion) from 1991 to 2001. Schayer is from [Encino](/wiki/Encino,_Los_Angeles), a suburb in the [San Fernando Valley](/wiki/San_Fernando_Valley). He started drumming in 1976 at the age of 10, but it was not until 1980 that he became a student of original [Circle Jerks](/wiki/Circle_Jerks) drummer [Lucky Lehrer](/wiki/Lucky_Lehrer).

Schayer has cited [Tommy Ramone](/wiki/Tommy_Ramone), [Paul Cook](/wiki/Paul_Cook), [Clem Burke](/wiki/Clem_Burke), [Mick Tucker](/wiki/Mick_Tucker), [Bun E. Carlos](/wiki/Bun_E._Carlos), [Charlie Watts](/wiki/Charlie_Watts), and [Keith Moon](/wiki/Keith_Moon) as some of his earliest drumming influences. He officially joined Bad Religion on April 1, 1991, after previous drummer, Pete Finestone, left the band after their fifth LP, *[Against the Grain](/wiki/Against_the_Grain_(Bad_Religion_album))*. He was present on their sixth LP, *[Generator](/wiki/Generator_(Bad_Religion_album))*, in 1992. He also played drums on *[Recipe For Hate](/wiki/Recipe_For_Hate)* (1993), *[Stranger Than Fiction](/wiki/Stranger_than_Fiction_(Bad_Religion_album))* (1994), *[The Gray Race](/wiki/The_Gray_Race)* (1996), *[Tested](/wiki/Tested)* (1997), *[No Substance](/wiki/No_Substance)* (1998), and *[The New America](/wiki/The_New_America)* in 2000. In 2001, he suffered an injury to his shoulder, and deciding to venture in a new direction, left Bad Religion. Schayer was replaced by [Brooks Wackerman](/wiki/Brooks_Wackerman), formerly of [Infectious Grooves](/wiki/Infectious_Grooves) and [Suicidal Tendencies](/wiki/Suicidal_Tendencies).

For over ten years from 2001 to 2011, Schayer was the drum tech for [Sam Fogarino](/wiki/Sam_Fogarino) of the band [Interpol](/wiki/Interpol_(band)).

Schayer currently lives in Los Angeles, CA.

The **Miami Book Fair** is an annual street fair and literary festival organized by [Miami Dade College](/wiki/Miami_Dade_College).

## History

Miami Book Fair International, originally known as "Books by the Bay," was founded in 1984 by Miami Dade College President [Eduardo J. Padrón](/wiki/Eduardo_J._Padr%C3%B3n), Books & Books owner Mitchell Kaplan, Craig Pollock of BookWorks, and other local [bookstore](/wiki/Bookselling) owners in cooperation with the [Miami-Dade Public Library System](/wiki/Miami-Dade_Public_Library_System). The primary organizers of the inaugural 1984 Book Fair from the Miami-Dade Public Library System were Head of Community Relations [Margarita Cano](/wiki/Margarita_Cano_(artist)) and [Wolfsonian](/wiki/Wolfsonian-FIU) campus librarian Juanita Johnson.

## Community partners and sponsors

The Florida Center for the Literary Arts (FCLA) is affiliated with the Miami Book Fair International. A permanent endowment for the FCLA was established with a grant from the [John S. and James L. Knight Foundation](/wiki/John_S._and_James_L._Knight_Foundation). Full programming began in January 2002.

As a department of Miami Dade College, FCLA generates programs to support authors and writing, [journalism](/wiki/Journalism), [play](/wiki/Playwright) and [screen writing](/wiki/Screenwriting), reading and [literacy](/wiki/Literacy), as well as the Miami Book Fair International. Outreach consists of reading campaigns and book discussions, writing workshops, author presentations, [panel discussions](/wiki/Panel_discussion), and [master classes](/wiki/Master_class). The Center collaborates with Florida-based cultural institutions and other partners to advance literary initiatives.

## Events

### Festival of Authors

Typically more than 300 authors from around the world participate in the Fair's international Festival of Authors. Writers come from across the [United States](/wiki/United_States) and other countries including [Argentina](/wiki/Argentina), [Bosnia](/wiki/Bosnia_and_Herzegovina), [Brazil](/wiki/Brazil), [Canada](/wiki/Canada), [China](/wiki/China), [Cuba](/wiki/Cuba), [Dominican Republic](/wiki/Dominican_Republic), [England](/wiki/England), [France](/wiki/France), [Finland](/wiki/Finland), [Guatemala](/wiki/Guatemala), [Haiti](/wiki/Haiti), [Hong Kong](/wiki/Hong_Kong), [Israel](/wiki/Israel), [Ireland](/wiki/Ireland), [Jamaica](/wiki/Jamaica), [Mexico](/wiki/Mexico), [Nicaragua](/wiki/Nicaragua), [Philippines](/wiki/Philippines), [Russia](/wiki/Russia), [South Africa](/wiki/South_Africa), [Spain](/wiki/Spain), [Taiwan](/wiki/Taiwan), [Trinidad](/wiki/Trinidad_and_Tobago), among others.

### Evenings With... series

The "Evenings With... series" features readings by writers every evening for six consecutive festival days. Past guest authors have included recipients of the [Nobel Prize in Literature](/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Literature), [Pulitzer Prize](/wiki/Pulitzer_Prize), [National Book Award](/wiki/National_Book_Award), [Casa de las Americas Prize](/wiki/Casa_de_las_Am%C3%A9ricas_Prize), [Pushcart Prize](/wiki/Pushcart_Prize), [O'Henry Award](/wiki/O._Henry_Award), [National Magazine Award](/wiki/National_Magazine_Awards), [Commonwealth Prize](/wiki/Commonwealth_Writers), [MacArthur Fellowship](/wiki/MacArthur_Fellows_Program), and [Edgar Award](/wiki/Edgar_Awards).

### Street fair

The three-day outdoor festival gathers hundreds of booksellers and exhibitors from major [publishing](/wiki/Publishing) houses, small presses, scholarly imprints, and foreign publishers. Sellers of used books, including signed first editions, original [manuscripts](/wiki/Manuscript), and other collectibles, also participate. Millions of books in multiple languages are available, along with book signings and musical entertainment, during the weekend Fair activity.

### Comix Galaxy

Jonathan Case at the Comic Galaxy Program, MBFI 2011

Comic Galaxy is a Fair program offering information on [graphic novels](/wiki/Graphic_novel) and the comics world. It celebrates their place in United States literary life and their increasing popularity and integration into mainstream literature, culture, and education. The School of Comics, a prominent part of Comic Galaxy, is a day-long program with six sessions for teachers, librarians, parents, and others interested in the format, and special training for established comic book creators.

### Children's Alley

Children's Alley, designed for young readers, features [storytelling](/wiki/Storytelling), [puppets](/wiki/Puppet), theatrical performances, educational games, and hands-on activities. Generation Genius, a program within Children's Alley, hosts thousands of school-aged children annually. Miami Book Fair International also offers workshops and presentations to local teachers, librarians, and educators as part of Generation Genius. Programs are provided in cooperation with [Miami Art Museum](/wiki/Miami_Art_Museum), [HistoryMiami](/wiki/HistoryMiami), [Miami Children's Museum](/wiki/Miami_Children%27s_Museum), [Miami Science Museum](/wiki/Miami_Science_Museum), Early Learning Coalition of Miami Dade/Monroe, and Florida Blue.

### Ibero-American Authors

Cuban poet Néstor Díaz de Villegas at Spanish Language author program, MBFI 2011

Ibero-American Authors is Miami Book Fair International's Spanish and Portuguese language author program. More than 50 authors from various Latin American countries are featured during the eight days of the Fair. Readings take place nightly during the week and throughout the weekend.

### Twilight Tastings and the Kitchen

Miami Book Fair International also includes [hors d'oeuvre](/wiki/Hors_d%27oeuvre), complimentary cocktails, and nightly entertainment before weeknight author presentations. The Kitchen combines cooking demonstrations and author readings by featured [cookbook](/wiki/Cookbook) authors and chefs as they recreate [recipes](/wiki/Recipe) from their books.

### LGBTQIA+ topics

Authors writing on [LGBTQIA+](/wiki/LGBTQ) topics are represented throughout the festival with titles in [fiction](/wiki/Fiction), [non-fiction](/wiki/Non-fiction), [memoir](/wiki/Memoir), and [erotica](/wiki/Erotica). Many featured authors are [Lambda Award](/wiki/Lambda_Literary_Awards) winners or finalists.

### Miami Writer's Institute

Center for Literature and Theatre faculty, authors, and agents provide workshops related to fiction, nonfiction, [poetry](/wiki/Poetry), and publishing. The center offers one and three-day workshops, with several taking place in [Spanish](/wiki/Spanish_language).

# Tethytheria

**Tethytheria** is a [clade](/wiki/Clade) of [paenungulate](/wiki/Paenungulate) [mammals](/wiki/Mammal) that includes the [sirenians](/wiki/Sirenia), [proboscideans](/wiki/Proboscidea),
and the [extinct](/wiki/Extinct) order [Embrithopoda](/wiki/Embrithopoda).

Though there is strong anatomical and molecular support for the [monophyly](/wiki/Monophyly) of Tethytheria, the interrelationships between the included taxa remain disputed. The tethytheres are united by several characters, including anteriorly facing [orbits](/wiki/Orbit_(anatomy)) and more or less [bilophodont](/wiki/Bilophodont) cheek teeth (double transverse ridges on the crowns of the teeth). Proboscidea and Sirenia are linked together based on auditory characters in their [petrosal bones](/wiki/Petrosal_bone), but this link may be a [homoplasy](/wiki/Homoplasy). [Desmostylians](/wiki/Desmostylia), traditionally considered tethytheres, have been tentatively assigned to [Perissodactyla](/wiki/Odd-toed_ungulate), along with the Early Eocene family [Anthracobunidae](/wiki/Anthracobunidae), which was considered a sister group to Tethytheria.

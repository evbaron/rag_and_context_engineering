**Mette Solli** (born 14 September 1974) is a Norwegian [kickboxer](/wiki/Kickboxing).

## Biography

Solli was born in [Molde](/wiki/Molde_(town)) on 17 September 1974.

Her achievements include gold medal in light-contact at the [W.A.K.O. World Championships 2001 (Maribor)](/wiki/W.A.K.O._World_Championships_2001_(Maribor)), a gold medal in full-contact at the [W.A.K.O. European Championships 2004 (Budva)](/wiki/W.A.K.O._European_Championships_2004_(Budva)), and a gold medal in full-contact at the [W.A.K.O. World Championships 2007 (Coimbra)](/wiki/W.A.K.O._World_Championships_2007_(Coimbra)).

She was awarded the [Kongepokal](/wiki/Kongepokal) (King's Cup) trophy at the national championships in 2007.

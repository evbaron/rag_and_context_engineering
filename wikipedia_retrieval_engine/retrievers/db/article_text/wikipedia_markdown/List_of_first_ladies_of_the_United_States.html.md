# List of first ladies of the United States

The [White House](/wiki/White_House), official residence of the first lady of the United States

The [first lady of the United States](/wiki/First_lady_of_the_United_States) is the hostess of the [White House](/wiki/White_House). The position is traditionally filled by the wife of the [president of the United States](/wiki/President_of_the_United_States), but, on occasion, the title has been applied to women who were not presidents' wives, such as when the president was a bachelor or widower, or when the wife of the president was unable to fulfill the duties of the first lady. The first lady is not an elected position; it carries no official duties and receives no salary. Nonetheless, she attends many official ceremonies and functions of state either along with or in place of the president. Traditionally, the first lady does not hold outside employment while occupying the office, although [Eleanor Roosevelt](/wiki/Eleanor_Roosevelt) earned money writing and giving lectures, but gave most of it to charity, and [Jill Biden](/wiki/Jill_Biden) maintained her regular job as an educator during her time in the role. The first lady has [her own staff](/wiki/Office_of_the_First_Lady_of_the_United_States), including the [White House social secretary](/wiki/White_House_Social_Secretary), the [first lady's chief of staff](/wiki/Chief_of_Staff_to_the_First_Lady_of_the_United_States), the communications director, the [White House chief floral designer](/wiki/White_House_Chief_Floral_Designer), and the [executive chef](/wiki/White_House_Executive_Chef). The [Office of the First Lady](/wiki/Office_of_the_First_Lady) is also in charge of all social and ceremonial events of the White House, and is a branch of the [Executive Office of the President](/wiki/Executive_Office_of_the_President).

There have been total of 54 first ladies.

Following [Donald Trump](/wiki/Donald_Trump)'s [inauguration](/wiki/Second_inauguration_of_Donald_Trump) on January 20, 2025, his wife, [Melania Trump](/wiki/Melania_Trump), became the 44th official first lady. There are four living former first ladies: [Hillary Clinton](/wiki/Hillary_Clinton); [Laura Bush](/wiki/Laura_Bush); [Michelle Obama](/wiki/Michelle_Obama); and [Jill Biden](/wiki/Jill_Biden). The most recent first lady to die was [Rosalynn Carter](/wiki/Rosalynn_Carter). The first first lady was [Martha Washington](/wiki/Martha_Washington). Presidents [John Tyler](/wiki/John_Tyler) and [Woodrow Wilson](/wiki/Woodrow_Wilson) had two official first ladies; both remarried during their presidential tenures. The wives of four presidents died before their husbands were sworn into office but are still considered first ladies by the White House and National First Ladies' Library: [Martha Jefferson](/wiki/Martha_Jefferson); [Rachel Jackson](/wiki/Rachel_Jackson); [Hannah Van Buren](/wiki/Hannah_Van_Buren); and [Nell Arthur](/wiki/Nell_Arthur).

One woman who was not married to a president is still considered an official first lady: [Harriet Lane](/wiki/Harriet_Lane), niece of bachelor [James Buchanan](/wiki/James_Buchanan). The other non-spousal relatives who served as White House hostesses are not recognized by the First Ladies' Library.

In 2007, the [United States Mint](/wiki/United_States_Mint) began releasing a set of half-ounce $10 gold coins under the [First Spouse Program](/wiki/First_Spouse_Program) with engravings of portraits of the first ladies on the [obverse](/wiki/Obverse). When a president served without a spouse, a gold coin was issued that bears an obverse image emblematic of [Liberty](/wiki/Liberty_(goddess)) as depicted on a circulating coin of that era and a reverse image emblematic of themes of that president's life. This is true for the coins for Thomas Jefferson, Andrew Jackson, Martin Van Buren, and James Buchanan's first ladies, but not the coin for Chester A. Arthur's first lady, which instead depicts [suffragette](/wiki/Suffragette) [Alice Paul](/wiki/Alice_Paul).

## Other spouses of presidents of the United States

Certain spouses of presidents of the United States are not considered first ladies of the United States.

Four presidents were widowed prior to their presidencies:

- [Thomas Jefferson](/wiki/Thomas_Jefferson) was married to [Martha Wayles](/wiki/Martha_Wayles) from 1772 until her death in 1782.
- [Andrew Jackson](/wiki/Andrew_Jackson) was married to [Rachel Donelson](/wiki/Rachel_Donelson) from 1794 until her death in 1828.
- [Martin Van Buren](/wiki/Martin_Van_Buren) was married to [Hannah Hoes](/wiki/Hannah_Hoes) from 1807 until her death in 1819.
- [Chester A. Arthur](/wiki/Chester_A._Arthur) was married to [Ellen Lewis Herndon](/wiki/Nell_Arthur) from 1859 until her death in 1880.

Two presidents were widowed and remarried prior to their presidencies:

- [Theodore Roosevelt](/wiki/Theodore_Roosevelt) was married to [Alice Lee](/wiki/Alice_Hathaway_Lee_Roosevelt) from 1880 until her death in 1884. He was subsequently married to [Edith Carow](/wiki/Edith_Carow) from 1886 to his death in 1919.
- [Joe Biden](/wiki/Joe_Biden) was married to [Neilia Hunter](/wiki/Neilia_Hunter) from 1966 until her death in 1972. He has subsequently been married to [Jill Jacobs](/wiki/Jill_Jacobs) since 1977.

Two presidents were divorced and remarried prior to their presidencies:

- [Ronald Reagan](/wiki/Ronald_Reagan) was married to [Jane Wyman](/wiki/Jane_Wyman) from 1940 until 1949. He was subsequently married to [Nancy Davis](/wiki/Nancy_Davis) from 1952 to his death in 2004.
- [Donald Trump](/wiki/Donald_Trump) was married to [Ivana Zelníčková](/wiki/Ivana_Zeln%C3%AD%C4%8Dkov%C3%A1) from 1977 until 1992 and to [Marla Maples](/wiki/Marla_Maples) from 1993 until 1999. He has subsequently been married to [Melania Knauss](/wiki/Melania_Knauss) since 2005.

Two presidents remarried after their presidencies:

- [Millard Fillmore](/wiki/Millard_Fillmore) was married to [Caroline Carmichael](/wiki/Caroline_Carmichael) from 1858 until his death in 1874.
- [Benjamin Harrison](/wiki/Benjamin_Harrison) was married to [Mary Dimmick](/wiki/Mary_Dimmick), the niece of his first wife, from 1896 until his death in 1901.

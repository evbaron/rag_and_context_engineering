# Andrea Riseborough

## Infobox

- **Born:** Andrea Louise Riseborough ( 1981-11-20 ) 20 November 1981 (age 44) Newcastle upon Tyne, England
- **Alma mater:** Royal Academy of Dramatic Art
- **Occupation:** Actress
- **Years active:** 2001–present

**Andrea Louise Riseborough** (born 20 November 1981) is an English actress. She made her film debut with a small part in *[Venus](/wiki/Venus_(2006_film))* (2006), and has since appeared in more prominent roles in *[Brighton Rock](/wiki/Brighton_Rock_(2010_film))* (2010), *[W.E.](/wiki/W.E.)* (2011), *[Shadow Dancer](/wiki/Shadow_Dancer_(2012_film))* (2012), *[Oblivion](/wiki/Oblivion_(2013_film))* (2013), *[Birdman](/wiki/Birdman_(film))* (2014), *[Nocturnal Animals](/wiki/Nocturnal_Animals)* (2016), *[Battle of the Sexes](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Sexes_(2017_film))*, *[The Death of Stalin](/wiki/The_Death_of_Stalin)* (both 2017), *[Mandy](/wiki/Mandy_(2018_film))*, *[Nancy](/wiki/Nancy_(film))* (both 2018), *[The Grudge](/wiki/The_Grudge_(2020_film))*, and *[Possessor](/wiki/Possessor_(film))* (both 2020). For playing an alcoholic in *[To Leslie](/wiki/To_Leslie)* (2022), she was nominated for the [Academy Award for Best Actress](/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actress).

Riseborough was nominated for a [BAFTA TV](/wiki/British_Academy_Television_Award_for_Best_Actress) for her portrayal of [Margaret Thatcher](/wiki/Margaret_Thatcher) in the television film *[The Long Walk to Finchley](/wiki/The_Long_Walk_to_Finchley)* (2008). She won critical acclaim for her performances in the [Channel 4](/wiki/Channel_4) miniseries *[The Devil's Whore](/wiki/The_Devil%27s_Whore)* (2008) and *[National Treasure](/wiki/National_Treasure_(British_TV_series))* (2016), as well as the [BBC One](/wiki/BBC_One) miniseries *[The Witness for the Prosecution](/wiki/The_Witness_for_the_Prosecution_(TV_series))* (2016). Her stage credits include *[Miss Julie](/wiki/Miss_Julie)*, *[Measure for Measure](/wiki/Measure_for_Measure)* (both 2006), and [Anton Chekhov](/wiki/Anton_Chekhov)'s *[Ivanov](/wiki/Ivanov_(play))* (2008).

## Early life and education

Riseborough was born on 20 November 1981 in [Newcastle upon Tyne](/wiki/Newcastle_upon_Tyne), the daughter of Isabel, a secretary and beautician, and George, a car dealer. She grew up in [Whitley Bay](/wiki/Whitley_Bay). In reference to *[The Long Walk to Finchley](/wiki/The_Long_Walk_to_Finchley)*, she has described her parents as "working-class [Thatcherites](/wiki/Thatcherite)."

She appeared at the [People's Theatre](/wiki/People%27s_Theatre,_Newcastle_upon_Tyne), Newcastle upon Tyne, in the play *Riding England Sidesaddle* by Christopher Goulding, as [Celia Fiennes](/wiki/Celia_Fiennes), and was a member of the Young People's Theatre for five years. Riseborough spent her schooldays at the independent school, [Newcastle upon Tyne Church High School](/wiki/Newcastle_upon_Tyne_Church_High_School). She was a student of the [Royal Academy of Dramatic Art](/wiki/Royal_Academy_of_Dramatic_Art) (RADA), graduating in 2005 with a BA in Acting Degree (H Level).

## Career

Riseborough at the premiere of *[The Death of Stalin](/wiki/The_Death_of_Stalin)*, 2017 Toronto Film Festival

Riseborough portrayed [Margaret Thatcher](/wiki/Margaret_Thatcher) in the [BBC Four](/wiki/BBC_Four) film *[The Long Walk to Finchley](/wiki/The_Long_Walk_to_Finchley)* (2008). She appeared in the 2010 films *[Made in Dagenham](/wiki/Made_in_Dagenham)* and [Mark Romanek](/wiki/Mark_Romanek)'s adaptation of *[Never Let Me Go](/wiki/Never_Let_Me_Go_(2010_film))*. She starred in the American premiere of Alexi Kaye Campbell's *The Pride* at the [Lucille Lortel Theatre](/wiki/Lucille_Lortel_Theatre) in January 2010. The production was directed by [Joe Mantello](/wiki/Joe_Mantello). She appears in [Rowan Joffé](/wiki/Rowan_Joff%C3%A9)'s film adaptation of *[Brighton Rock](/wiki/Brighton_Rock_(2011_film))*. She worked with *The Devil's Mistress* author [Peter Flannery](/wiki/Peter_Flannery) on his screenplay based on the life of Angelica Fanshawe. She played the role of [Wallis Simpson](/wiki/Wallis_Simpson) in *[W.E.](/wiki/W.E.)*, a film directed by [Madonna](/wiki/Madonna). She stars in *[Resistance](/wiki/Resistance_(2011_film))*, an adaptation of an [Owen Sheers](/wiki/Owen_Sheers) novel. The film was released on 25 November 2011.

She writes with her creative partner, actor [Tom Burke](/wiki/Tom_Burke_(actor)), and with [Mike Leigh](/wiki/Mike_Leigh). Riseborough starred in the thriller *[Hidden](/wiki/Hidden_(2015_film))*, a [low-budget film](/wiki/Low-budget_film) directed by [the Duffer Brothers](/wiki/The_Duffer_Brothers). *Hidden* was released for streaming September 2015. She appeared in *[Oblivion](/wiki/Oblivion_(2013_film))* (2013), in a supporting role. She co-starred in the acting ensemble of the [Alejandro González Iñárritu](/wiki/Alejandro_Gonz%C3%A1lez_I%C3%B1%C3%A1rritu)'s showbusiness drama film *[Birdman (or The Unexpected Virtue of Ignorance)](/wiki/Birdman_(film))*, which won the Oscar for Best Picture at the [87th Academy Awards](/wiki/87th_Academy_Awards). Riseborough shared the [Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Performance by a Cast in a Motion Picture](/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Cast_in_a_Motion_Picture) for the film.

In 2016 she co-starred in apartheid drama *[Shepherds and Butchers](/wiki/Shepherds_and_Butchers)* and the [Tom Ford](/wiki/Tom_Ford) directed psychological thriller *[Nocturnal Animals](/wiki/Nocturnal_Animals)*. She joined the cast of Netflix's *[Bloodline](/wiki/Bloodline_(TV_series))* for season 2, as a series regular character, Evangeline. She acted as [Emma Stone](/wiki/Emma_Stone)'s love interest in the biographical sports film *[Battle of the Sexes](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Sexes_(2017_film))*, based on the 1973 tennis match between [Billie Jean King](/wiki/Billie_Jean_King) and [Bobby Riggs](/wiki/Bobby_Riggs). She portrayed [Joseph Stalin](/wiki/Joseph_Stalin)'s daughter [Svetlana Stalina](/wiki/Svetlana_Stalina) in the 2017 comedy-drama film *[The Death of Stalin](/wiki/The_Death_of_Stalin)* and was praised by *[Variety](/wiki/Variety_(magazine))* for the "shrewd, multi-layered complexity" of her performance.

[Jonathan Dayton](/wiki/Jonathan_Dayton_and_Valerie_Faris), [Elisabeth Shue](/wiki/Elisabeth_Shue), Riseborough, [Emma Stone](/wiki/Emma_Stone) and [Billie Jean King](/wiki/Billie_Jean_King) at the London premiere of *[Battle of the Sexes](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Sexes_(2017_film))* (2017)

She was cast in *[Waco](/wiki/Waco_(miniseries))*, a six-part television series about the [Waco siege](/wiki/Waco_siege). The first episode was released on 24 January 2018. That same year she acted in three films, the horror film *[Mandy](/wiki/Mandy_(2018_film))*, the mystery drama *[Nancy](/wiki/Nancy_(film))* (which she also produced), and the historical drama *[Burden](/wiki/Burden_(2018_film))*. Riseborough has been cast in [Lone Scherfig](/wiki/Lone_Scherfig)'s *[The Kindness of Strangers](/wiki/The_Kindness_of_Strangers_(film))* as an ER nurse who runs an eclectic therapy group. The film started shooting at the [Russian Tea Room](/wiki/Russian_Tea_Room) in the spring of 2018. She starred in a Sony remake of *[The Grudge](/wiki/The_Grudge)*. The film was released on 3 January 2020. Riseborough starred in the international cocaine trade drama *[ZeroZeroZero](/wiki/ZeroZeroZero)*, an eight-part series adapted from the book by [Roberto Saviano](/wiki/Roberto_Saviano), which had its debut on Sky in the UK and Amazon Prime in the US in 2020. She starred in *[Possessor](/wiki/Possessor_(film))*—written and directed by [Brandon Cronenberg](/wiki/Brandon_Cronenberg)—as Tasya Vos, an agent for a secretive organization who uses brain-implant technology to inhabit other people's bodies, driving them to commit assassinations for the benefit of high-paying clients. The film had its world premiere at the [Sundance Film Festival](/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival) in January 2020.

Riseborough in 2013

In 2022, Riseborough appeared in the film *[To Leslie](/wiki/To_Leslie)*, for which she received critical acclaim and a nomination for the [Academy Award for Best Actress](/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actress). Numerous celebrities praised her performance publicly and on social media, and hosted screenings during the voting period for the [Academy Award nominations](/wiki/95th_Academy_Awards) in January 2023. Her unexpected *To Leslie* nomination generated some questions and, without referring to her, the Board of Governors pledged "review of the campaign procedures around this year's nominees, to ensure that no guidelines were violated, and to inform us whether changes to the guidelines may be needed in a new era of social media and digital communication."

Also that year she had supporting roles as Mrs. Wormwood in the musical *[Matilda the Musical](/wiki/Matilda_the_Musical_(film))* and Beatrice Vandenheuvel in [David O. Russell](/wiki/David_O._Russell)'s *[Amsterdam](/wiki/Amsterdam_(2022_film))*. In 2023, she acted opposite [Kate Winslet](/wiki/Kate_Winslet) portraying *[Vogue](/wiki/British_Vogue)* editor [Audrey Withers](/wiki/Audrey_Withers) in the biographical war drama *[Lee](/wiki/Lee_(2023_film))* which premiered at the [Toronto International Film Festival](/wiki/Toronto_International_Film_Festival). In 2024, Riseborough acted opposite Winslet again, portraying the latter's character's right-hand woman, in the HBO [political satire](/wiki/Political_satire) miniseries *[The Regime](/wiki/The_Regime_(miniseries))*, for which she earned a nomination at the inaugural [Gotham TV Awards](/wiki/The_Gotham_Film_%26_Media_Institute) for Outstanding Performance in a Limited Series.

## Personal life

Riseborough was in a relationship with Joe Appel, an American [street artist](/wiki/Street_artist), from 2009 to 2016.

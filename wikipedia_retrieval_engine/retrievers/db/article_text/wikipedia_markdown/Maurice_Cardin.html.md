**Maurice Cardin** (July 19, 1909 – March 23, 2009) was an American politician who served in the [Maryland House of Delegates](/wiki/Maryland_House_of_Delegates) from [Baltimore](/wiki/Baltimore) City's 5th district from 1951 to 1966. His nephew is former Maryland [U.S. Senator](/wiki/United_States_Senator) [Ben Cardin](/wiki/Ben_Cardin), who took over his seat in the Maryland House of Delegates when he retired from politics.

He died of heart failure on March 23, 2009, in [Lake Worth Beach, Florida](/wiki/Lake_Worth_Beach,_Florida) at age 99.

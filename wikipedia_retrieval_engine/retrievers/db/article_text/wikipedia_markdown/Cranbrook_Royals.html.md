# Cranbrook Royals

The **Cranbrook Royals** were a senior men's AAA level [ice hockey](/wiki/Ice_hockey) team that played in the [Western International Hockey League](/wiki/Western_International_Hockey_League) from 1965 to 1987.

The Cranbrook Royals won the [Allan Cup](/wiki/Allan_Cup) as senior ice hockey champions of Canada in 1982.

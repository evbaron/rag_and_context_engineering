**Alexander Guy Cook** (born 23 August 1990) is an English record producer. He first became known as founder of the influential record label [PC Music](/wiki/PC_Music) in 2013; its signees included [Hannah Diamond](/wiki/Hannah_Diamond), [GFOTY](/wiki/GFOTY), [Danny L Harle](/wiki/Danny_L_Harle) and [felicita](/wiki/Felicita_(musician)). The label's style of exaggerated pop tropes grew to serve as the foundation of the [hyperpop](/wiki/Hyperpop) genre, which Cook is credited for developing and popularising. He later became more widely known as executive producer for [Charli XCX](/wiki/Charli_XCX). He has released solo music under both his name and aliases like **DJ Lifeline** and **DJ Warlord**.

Cook formed the one-off project, QT, with musician [Sophie](/wiki/Sophie_(musician)) and performance artist [Hayden Dunham](/wiki/Hayden_Dunham), producing the 2014 single "[Hey QT](/wiki/Hey_QT)". He released his first solo singles in that same year, and was named number 12 in the *[Dazed](/wiki/Dazed)* 100 for "redefining style and youth culture in 2015 and beyond". He served as executive producer on Charli XCX's mixtapes *[Number 1 Angel](/wiki/Number_1_Angel)* and *[Pop 2](/wiki/Pop_2_(mixtape))* (both 2017), and albums *[Charli](/wiki/Charli_(album))* (2019), *[How I'm Feeling Now](/wiki/How_I%27m_Feeling_Now)* (2020), and *[Brat](/wiki/Brat_(album))* (2024).

Cook's first two albums, *[7G](/wiki/7G_(album))* and *[Apple](/wiki/Apple_(A._G._Cook_album))* (both 2020), both explored hyperpop and were met with critical praise. That same year, he won the *[Variety](/wiki/Variety_(magazine))* Hitmakers Innovator of The Year Award in 2020. In 2022, he co-produced the song "All Up in Your Mind" from [Beyoncé](/wiki/Beyonc%C3%A9)'s album *[Renaissance](/wiki/Renaissance_(Beyonc%C3%A9_album))*, which earned him a Grammy nomination for the [Album of the Year](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Album_of_the_Year), and he released his third album, *[Britpop](/wiki/Britpop_(A._G._Cook_album))*, in 2024. He continued to produce for XCX with *[Brat](/wiki/Brat_(Charli_XCX_album))* (2024) and its [remix album](/wiki/Brat_and_It%27s_Completely_Different_but_Also_Still_Brat), winning the [Grammy Award for Best Dance/Electronic Album](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Dance/Electronic_Album) for the former. He made [his scoring debut](/wiki/The_Moment_(The_Score)) in 2026 with the film *[The Moment](/wiki/The_Moment_(2026_film))*.

## Early life

Alexander Guy Cook is the son of English architect [Sir Peter Cook](/wiki/Peter_Cook_(architect)) and Israeli architect Yael Reisner.
Cook was privately educated; he attended the [King Alfred School](/wiki/King_Alfred_School,_London) in [Hampstead](/wiki/Hampstead), where he met future collaborator [Danny L Harle](/wiki/Danny_L_Harle).
He then attended [Goldsmiths, University of London](/wiki/Goldsmiths,_University_of_London), where he studied music, and reconnected with Harle. The two bonded over their shared musical tastes for artists including [Captain Beefheart](/wiki/Captain_Beefheart) and [Archie Shepp](/wiki/Archie_Shepp), as well as interest in comedy duo [Tim & Eric](/wiki/Tim_%26_Eric). This grew into a musical project called Dux Content.

## Career

### 2011–2013: Career beginnings

Since they did not have a vocalist, Dux Content focused on musical experiments like [compound metres](/wiki/Compound_metre) and changes in [tempo](/wiki/Tempo). One of their earlier works was a collection of compositions for the [Disklavier](/wiki/Disklavier), released with Spencer Noble and Tim Phillips under the name "Dux Consort". The pair released their songs with renderings of digital avatars for promotional artwork.

Cook has worked on graphics for PC Music acts including [Finn Keane](/wiki/Finn_Keane) (formerly Easyfun) and Maxo.

Cook created Gamsonite, a "pseudo-label" collecting his early collaborations. Dux Content released its songs with renderings of digital avatars for promotional artwork. They contributed to the score for Alicia Norman's animated film *Heart of Death* and began considering a children's television show titled *Dux Content's Jungle Jam*. Cook and Harle explored how to build rhythms out of a vocalist's natural singing tempo and released the results as "Dux Kidz". The project was noticed by producer [Sophie](/wiki/Sophie_(musician)), who later worked with PC Music's acts. Cook began working on building flashy websites with [Hannah Diamond](/wiki/Hannah_Diamond) and decided to focus on using websites to promote music.

### 2013–2016: Foundation of PC Music

In August 2013, Cook founded PC Music as a way of embracing an [A&R](/wiki/A%26R) role, with the aim of "recording people who don't normally make music and treating them as if they're a major label artist." In January 2014, Cook released "[Keri Baby](/wiki/Keri_Baby)" as his first solo single, with vocals by [Diamond](/wiki/Hannah_Diamond). The track uses pop clichés and glitchy vocals to depict Diamond as a digital entity on a screen. His follow-up single "[Beautiful](/wiki/Beautiful_(A._G._Cook_song))" was released in June. "Beautiful" is a pastiche of [Eurodance](/wiki/Eurodance), featuring high, pitch-shifted vocals and [donk](/wiki/UK_hard_house) sounds. *[Fact](/wiki/Fact_(UK_magazine))* magazine called it PC Music's "de-facto anthem", and the song received a remix from Scottish producer [Rustie](/wiki/Rustie).

Sophie (left) and Cook (right) produced QT's single "Hey QT"

Cook worked with Sophie to produce a song for [QT](/wiki/QT_(musician)), a pop singer portrayed by American performance artist Hayden Dunham. She found Cook through his work online and wanted to use a song to market a QT energy drink. Their resulting collaboration "[Hey QT](/wiki/Hey_QT)" was released in August 2014 on [XL Recordings](/wiki/XL_Recordings).

On 22 December 2014, A. G. Cook released "What I Mean" from his "Personal Computer Music" mix as a single. The single was made available as a free download via radio presenter [Annie Mac](/wiki/Annie_Mac)'s "Free Music Monday" SoundCloud channel. Opening with muffled dialogue, the song incorporates robotic vocals and a sample of [R&B](/wiki/R%26B) artist [Chuckii Booker](/wiki/Chuckii_Booker). Its organ-based arrangement was a more soulful take on Cook's usual style of dance-pop. After discussing a collaboration on a [Charli XCX](/wiki/Charli_XCX) album, Cook contributed an official remix of her single "[Doing It](/wiki/Doing_It_(Charli_XCX_song))" featuring [Rita Ora](/wiki/Rita_Ora).

Cook's work received recognition on year-end lists for 2014. "Keri Baby" was listed at number 5 of *Dummy* magazine's "20 Best Tracks of 2014", and [BuzzFeed](/wiki/BuzzFeed)'s "13 Obscure Tracks of 2014", number 1 on [Gorilla vs. Bear](/wiki/Gorilla_vs._Bear)'s, "Favourite Tracks of 2014", number 2 on *[Dazed & Confused](/wiki/Dazed)*'s "Top 20 Tracks of 2014". *[Pitchfork](/wiki/Pitchfork_(website))* ranked "Beautiful" number 30 on its list of "The 100 Best Tracks of 2014".

March 2015 saw Cook's [PC Music](/wiki/PC_Music) head to the US to showcase all 11 of his label's talent at the Empire Garage in Austin, Texas as part of [SXSW](/wiki/SXSW). The showcase received positive reviews, with *[The Guardian](/wiki/The_Guardian)* stating that his "entire thundering set [shows] this is a label refusing to be confined by definitions of genre or good taste." On 8 May 2015, Cook performed as part of a PC Music show at [BRIC House](/wiki/BRIC_House) in Brooklyn, New York as part of the [Red Bull Music Academy](/wiki/Red_Bull_Music_Academy) Festival. The show was billed as the premiere of Pop Cube, "a multimedia reality network".

In January 2016, Chinese singer [Li Yuchun](/wiki/Li_Yuchun), also known as Chris Lee, released a single consisting of songs "Real Love" and "Only You", both of which were produced by Cook. That April, experimental music producer [Oneohtrix Point Never](/wiki/Oneohtrix_Point_Never) posted a cryptic video to his Instagram that appeared to show Cook working on a remix of "Sticky Drama", a single from his 2015 album *[Garden of Delete](/wiki/Garden_of_Delete)*. The remix was later surprise-released on 16 December. Also in April, Cook made a cameo in Charli XCX's music video for "[Vroom Vroom](/wiki/Vroom_Vroom_(song))". "[Superstar](/wiki/Superstar_(A._G._Cook_song))", Cook's fifth single, was released via PC Music on 13 July. On the day of its release, Cook revealed via Twitter that "Superstar" had been in the works for over two years prior, originally beginning as a "[topline pitch](/wiki/Sales_presentation)" for [electro house](/wiki/Electro_house) DJ [Zedd](/wiki/Zedd).

### 2017–2020: Charli XCX, Jónsi, *7G* and *Apple*

In March 2017, Charli XCX's mixtape *[Number 1 Angel](/wiki/Number_1_Angel)* was released, prominently featuring production by Cook and others, including PC Music artists and affiliates Sophie, Danny L Harle, Life Sim and Finn Keane, who created the project EasyFX with Cook. This was followed by the mixtape *[Pop 2](/wiki/Pop_2_(mixtape))*, also featuring production by Cook and others. [Pitchfork Media](/wiki/Pitchfork_(website)) gave *[Pop 2](/wiki/Pop_2_(mixtape))* a rating of 8.4 out of 10, calling it "a vision of what pop music could be" and "the best full-length work of both Charli and PC Music's respective careers". In July he covered Aphex Twin’s 1999 single "[Windowlicker](/wiki/Windowlicker)" for his set at [Field Day](/wiki/Field_Day_(festival)) which directly clashed with Aphex Twin's headline show at the festival, spending an "intense 48 hours inside a windowless room" to do so.

Cook released a remix of [Phoenix](/wiki/Phoenix_(French_band))'s song "Fior Di Latte" in February 2018. In May, he released a 57-minute mix titled *Hollywood Ambient*, remixing tracks by artists including [Rihanna](/wiki/Rihanna), [Sigur Rós](/wiki/Sigur_R%C3%B3s), Charli XCX, and [Katy Perry](/wiki/Katy_Perry), for Sigur Rós' “Liminal” ambient playlist. It also remixed Grimes' song "[Flesh Without Blood](/wiki/Flesh_Without_Blood)"; Cook called it "Flesh Without Musk" in reference to her then partner [Elon Musk](/wiki/Elon_Musk). Cook produced the Charli XCX single track [*Focus*](/wiki/Focus_/_No_Angel) with [Jack & Coke](/wiki/Jack_%26_Coke_(songwriters)) in June. In November, he contributed to [Tommy Cash](/wiki/Tommy_Cash_(rapper))'s second studio album *[¥€$](/wiki/%C2%A5%E2%82%AC$)*. He is credited as the producer on 5 tracks on the record, including lead single "X-RAY" which he co-produced with Danny L Harle.

Cook was announced as the co-executive producer for Charli XCX's third studio album *[Charli](/wiki/Charli_(album))*, which was released on 13 September 2019. Cook produced six of the album's seven singles, including "[Gone](/wiki/Gone_(Charli_XCX_and_Christine_and_the_Queens_song))", the third single from the album, which features [Christine and the Queens](/wiki/Christine_and_the_Queens). The song was awarded "Best New Track" by *[Pitchfork](/wiki/Pitchfork_(website))* and best song of the week by *[Stereogum](/wiki/Stereogum)*. In October, [100 gecs](/wiki/100_gecs) released Cook's remix of their single "[Money Machine](/wiki/Money_Machine_(song))" to announce their remix album *[1000 gecs and the Tree of Clues](/wiki/1000_gecs_and_the_Tree_of_Clues)*. *[The Fader](/wiki/The_Fader)* called the remix "a disorienting exercise in [maximalism](/wiki/Maximalism) and rapid tonal shifts."

On 6 April 2020, Cook and [BJ Burton](/wiki/BJ_Burton) were announced as co-executive producers for Charli XCX's [quarantine](/wiki/COVID-19_lockdowns) album *[how i'm feeling now](/wiki/How_I%27m_Feeling_Now)*, which was written in an open-source style, sharing the production process online and utilizing fan input and content. Cook also produced [Jónsi](/wiki/J%C3%B3nsi)'s first solo music in a decade, the song "Exhale", released on 23 April, and followed this by serving as executive producer on Jónsi's album *[Shiver](/wiki/Shiver_(J%C3%B3nsi_album))*, released on 2 October.

On 30 July 2020, Cook announced an upcoming studio album [*7G*](/wiki/7G_(album)), and held a [virtual concert](/wiki/Virtual_concert) over [Zoom](/wiki/Zoom_(software)) featuring [Caroline Polachek](/wiki/Caroline_Polachek), GRRL and [Thy Slaughter](/wiki/Thy_Slaughter) titled *7 by 7* on 7 August. Thy Slaughter is a collaboration project between Cook and labelmate Finn Keane. *7G* was released through PC Music on 12 August, comprising 49 tracks split over seven discs.

On 20 August 2020, Cook released the single "Oh Yeah" and announced another studio album, *[Apple](/wiki/Apple_(A._G._Cook_album)).* Leading up to its release, Cook hosted another free livestream festival across [Zoom](/wiki/Zoom_Video_Communications), [Bandcamp](/wiki/Bandcamp) and [Twitch](/wiki/Twitch_(service)) entitled 'Appleville', featuring performers including 100 gecs, [Alaska Reid](/wiki/Alaska_Reid), Charli XCX, [Clairo](/wiki/Clairo), [Dorian Electra](/wiki/Dorian_Electra), [Hannah Diamond](/wiki/Hannah_Diamond), [Kero Kero Bonito](/wiki/Kero_Kero_Bonito), [Namasenda](/wiki/Namasenda) and [Oklou](/wiki/Oklou). All proceeds from VIP ticket sales were donated to [Mermaids](/wiki/Mermaids_(charity)) and [Black Cultural Archives](/wiki/Black_Cultural_Archives). The album was released through PC Music on 18 September 2020.

After 5 years of collaborating with Charli XCX, in November 2020 the pair was awarded the [Variety](/wiki/Variety_(magazine)) Hitmakers Innovator of The Year Award. In December 2020, Cook took part in [LuckyMe Records](/wiki/LuckyMe_(record_label))' 12 Alternative Futures Advent Calendar Project. Cook's remix of [Baauer](/wiki/Baauer)'s song "[Planet's Mad](/wiki/Planet%27s_Mad)" was released on 10 December.

### 2021–2022: Hikaru Utada, *Apple vs. 7G*, Lady Gaga and Beyoncé

Cook collaborated with Japanese American singer-songwriter [Hikaru Utada](/wiki/Hikaru_Utada) in March 2021, co-producing the song "[One Last Kiss](/wiki/One_Last_Kiss_(EP))", the theme song for the Japanese animated science fiction film *[Evangelion: 3.0+1.0 Thrice Upon a Time](/wiki/Evangelion:_3.0%2B1.0_Thrice_Upon_a_Time)*. On 9 March, he released a cover of [The Smashing Pumpkins](/wiki/The_Smashing_Pumpkins)' song "[Today](/wiki/Today_(The_Smashing_Pumpkins_song))", announcing his [SoundCloud](/wiki/SoundCloud)-exclusive album *Dream Logic*, featuring reworks of *7G* and *Apple* tracks as well as collaborations with and remixes of Charli XCX, Oneohtrix Point Never, [The 1975](/wiki/The_1975), [Jónsi](/wiki/J%C3%B3nsi), [No Rome](/wiki/No_Rome), [Oklou](/wiki/Oklou), [Alex Somers](/wiki/Alex_Somers), and Alaska Reid. This was released on 12 March. In April, Cook penned a tribute to Sophie who had died earlier that year.

Cook later announced that further remixes from his would be included on a 21-track joint remix album titled *[Apple vs. 7G](/wiki/Apple_vs._7G).* The album was released on 28 May and features remixes from Keane and Hannah Diamond as well as Polachek, Charli XCX and No Rome. Cook released the remixed version of No Rome's "[Spinning](/wiki/Spinning_(No_Rome,_Charli_XCX_and_The_1975_song))", featuring Charli XCX and [the 1975](/wiki/The_1975), in May 2021. In June, Cook hosted and curated his first show in a 4 part residency on [BBC Radio 6 music](/wiki/BBC_Radio_6_Music) as part of the 'Lose Yourself with...' series. He contributed to [Lady Gaga](/wiki/Lady_Gaga)'s *[Dawn of Chromatica](/wiki/Dawn_of_Chromatica)* remix album, released in September 2021, providing a remix for the track "[911](/wiki/911_(Lady_Gaga_song))" alongside Charli XCX. That October, Cook partnered with [Apple Inc.](/wiki/Apple_Inc.) to release "Start Up", a song that incorporates sounds from Apple products from the past 45 years. It was used as the intro music for an Apple media event that month.

Cook performing in 2022

Cook co-produced another song with Utada in 2022, called "[Kimi ni Muchū](/wiki/Kimi_ni_Much%C5%AB)", and remixed Utada's song "[Face My Fears](/wiki/Face_My_Fears)" for her album *[Bad Mode](/wiki/Bad_Mode)*. In 2022, he co-wrote and co-produced the song "All Up in Your Mind" with [Beyoncé](/wiki/Beyonc%C3%A9), which was included on her seventh studio album *[Renaissance](/wiki/Renaissance_(Beyonc%C3%A9_album))*. It earned his first [Grammy Award](/wiki/Grammy_Awards) nomination for [Album of the Year](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Album_of_the_Year).

### 2023–present: Closure of PC Music, Thy Slaughter, *Britpop* and *Brat*

Cook lent additional production to "Lick the light out", featuring [Madonna](/wiki/Madonna), on Christine and the Queens' *[Paranoïa, Angels, True Love](/wiki/Paranoia,_Angels,_True_Love)*. Cook also produced the majority of Alaska Reid's album *Disenchanter*, which released in July 2023. He worked again with Utada, in a new song called "Gold -Mata Au Hi Made- (Gold – Till the day we see each other again)", the theme song of the movie [KINGDOM: The Flame of Destiny](/wiki/Kingdom_(2019_film)), released July 28. Cook also contributed to the score of 2023 film *[Bottoms](/wiki/Bottoms_(film)),* and produced "How to Stay With You" from [Troye Sivan](/wiki/Troye_Sivan)'s *[Something to Give Each Other](/wiki/Something_to_Give_Each_Other)* album.

In July 2023, Cook announced that PC Music would cease to release any further new material after the end of 2023. In August, the label released the album *Another Pobbles Christmas* under the name Pobbles, a project which largely involved Cook with visual art by George Michael Brower. Some of the label's final releases include Felicita's *Spalarkle*, Hannah Diamond's *[Perfect Picture](/wiki/Perfect_Picture_(album))*, and Thy Slaughter's debut album *Soft Rock* which included features from Charli XCX, [Alaska Reid](/wiki/Alaska_Reid), Caroline Polachek, and [Ellie Rowsell](/wiki/Ellie_Rowsell).

On January 1, 2024, Cook released the single "Silver Thread Golden Needle", his first release following the dissolution of [PC Music](/wiki/PC_Music). He announced his next album *[Britpop](/wiki/Britpop_(A._G._Cook_album))* on 23 February with the release of its title track featuring vocals by Charli XCX. He released the *Britpop* single "Soulbreaker" on 17 April. *Britpop* was released on 10 May 2024 under Cook's newly set up record label New Alias, consisting of 24 songs which were split into three discs representing the past, present and future respectively.

Cook was a significant producer on the Charli XCX album *[Brat](/wiki/Brat_(album))*, working on singles "[Von Dutch](/wiki/Von_Dutch_(song))", "[Club Classics](/wiki/Club_Classics)", and "[B2B](/wiki/B2B_(song))". He further produced "Hello Goodbye" and "Spring Breakers" on the deluxe version, *Brat and It’s the Same but There’s Three More Songs So It’s Not*, sampling [Britney Spears](/wiki/Britney_Spears)' "[Everytime](/wiki/Everytime)" in the production of the latter. Charli XCX followed the album with several remixes, including a remix of "Von Dutch" by Cook which featured [Addison Rae](/wiki/Addison_Rae). This remix was praised by *[The Line of Best Fit](/wiki/The_Line_of_Best_Fit)*, which called Cook's production "[idiosyncratic](/wiki/Idiosyncrasy)". His "Von Dutch" remix was then nominated for the [Grammy Award for Best Remixed Recording](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Remixed_Recording), while his production credits on *Brat* earned him his first Grammy Award win for [Best Dance/Electronic Album](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Dance/Electronic_Album).

In May 2024, Utada released a re-recorded version of "[Simple and Clean](/wiki/Simple_and_Clean)" with Cook credited as a producer. He co-produced [Kesha](/wiki/Kesha)'s "[Joyride](/wiki/Joyride_(Kesha_song))" for her album [Period](/wiki/Period_(Kesha_album)), and was also a co-producer on [F5ve](/wiki/F5ve)'s [Sequence 01](/wiki/Sequence_01) single "UFO" and "Magic Clock" which released on October 17, 2024 and March 5, 2025. He additionally co-produced F5ve's song, Snowman, part of Sequence 01.5 (Dreaming of the 2nd 1st Impact - Consequences of Fate Redux)

## Artistry

Cook's style of music amplifies the clichés of mainstream pop music from the 1990s and 2000s. He follows the work of "mega-producers" such as [Max Martin](/wiki/Max_Martin) and [Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis](/wiki/Jimmy_Jam_and_Terry_Lewis). Cook references [Scritti Politti](/wiki/Scritti_Politti)'s album *[Cupid & Psyche 85](/wiki/Cupid_%26_Psyche_85)* for its "conscious decision to take pop music and make it as shiny and detailed as possible". He cites Korean and Japanese pop music as influences, as well as [gyaru](/wiki/Gyaru) subculture.

Cook begins constructing tracks by constructing chords and melodies note by note. He prefers the sounds of virtual instruments and avoids sound design early in the process, giving his music a deadpan simplicity. He experiments with combining dissonant sounds, and the resulting dense, multi-layered arrangements are influenced by the [Black MIDI](/wiki/Black_MIDI) techniques. Cook's arrangements are inspired by the mechanized music of composer [Conlon Nancarrow](/wiki/Conlon_Nancarrow). When collaborating with other artists, he prepares an extensive demo so that they can complete lyrics and record vocals straight away. Cook thoroughly processes the vocals, [chopping](/wiki/Chopping_(sampling_technique)) them to use as a rhythmic element atop the melody.

In contrast to most of the artists on PC Music, Cook wears plain clothing. [GFOTY](/wiki/GFOTY) jokingly characterised his style as [normcore](/wiki/Normcore).

## PC Music

Main article: [PC Music](/wiki/PC_Music)

PC Music is a record label founded by A. G. Cook in 2013. Its first song was made available on [SoundCloud](/wiki/SoundCloud) the same year. PC Music is known for its surreal or exaggerated take on [pop music](/wiki/Pop_music), often featuring pitch-shifted, feminine vocals and bright, synthetic textures. Artists on its roster include [Hannah Diamond](/wiki/Hannah_Diamond), [Finn Keane](/wiki/Finn_Keane), [Namasenda](/wiki/Namasenda) and [Danny L Harle](/wiki/Danny_L_Harle). The label has been characterized as embracing the aesthetics of [advertising](/wiki/Advertising), [consumerism](/wiki/Consumerism) and corporate branding. Its artists often present devised personas inspired by [cyberculture](/wiki/Cyberculture). The label has inspired both praise and criticism from journalists and has been called "polarizing". In 2019 it was described by [Dazed](/wiki/Dazed) as one of the 'most exhilarating record labels of the 2010s.' In more recent years it has been noted for its influence on mainstream pop due to the production work by PC Music signees for artists such as [Kim Petras](/wiki/Kim_Petras), [Charli XCX](/wiki/Charli_XCX) and [Jónsi](/wiki/J%C3%B3nsi).

## Personal life

Cook moved to Los Angeles in 2019. He is in a relationship with fellow musician and collaborator [Alaska Reid](/wiki/Alaska_Reid).

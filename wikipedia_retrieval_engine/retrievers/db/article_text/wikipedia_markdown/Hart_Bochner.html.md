# Hart Bochner

## Infobox

- **Born:** Hart Matthew Bochner ( 1956-10-03 ) October 3, 1956 (age 69) Toronto, Ontario, Canada

- **Years active:** 1977–present
- **Parent:** Lloyd Bochner (father)

**Hart Matthew Bochner** (born October 3, 1956) is a Canadian TV, film and voice actor, film director, screenwriter and producer. He has appeared in films such as *[Breaking Away](/wiki/Breaking_Away)* (1979), *[Terror Train](/wiki/Terror_Train)* (1980), *[Rich and Famous](/wiki/Rich_and_Famous_(1981_film))* (1981), *[The Wild Life](/wiki/The_Wild_Life_(film))* (1984), *[Making Mr. Right](/wiki/Making_Mr._Right)* (1987), *[Die Hard](/wiki/Die_Hard)* (1988), *[Apartment Zero](/wiki/Apartment_Zero)* (1988), *[Mr. Destiny](/wiki/Mr._Destiny)* (1990), *[Mad at the Moon](/wiki/Mad_at_the_Moon)* (1992), *[Break Up](/wiki/Break_Up_(1998_film))* (1998), *[Liberty Stands Still](/wiki/Liberty_Stands_Still)* (2002) and *[Spread](/wiki/Spread_(film))* (2009). On television, he has starred in *[War and Remembrance](/wiki/War_and_Remembrance_(miniseries))* (1988–89), *[Children of the Dust](/wiki/Children_of_the_Dust_(miniseries))* (1995), *[Baby for Sale](/wiki/Baby_for_Sale)* (2004), *[The Starter Wife](/wiki/The_Starter_Wife_(TV_series))* (2008) and *[Scandal](/wiki/Scandal_(American_TV_series))* (2015).

## Early life

Bochner was born in [Toronto](/wiki/Toronto), [Ontario](/wiki/Ontario), the son of Ruth (née Roher), a concert pianist, and the late actor [Lloyd Bochner](/wiki/Lloyd_Bochner). His family is [Jewish](/wiki/Jewish). Bochner is a board member of the [Environmental Media Awards](/wiki/Environmental_Media_Awards). His grand-uncle was journalist, lawyer and philanthropist [Isaiah L. Kenen](/wiki/Isaiah_L._Kenen).

## Career

Bochner appeared in such films as *[Islands in the Stream](/wiki/Islands_in_the_Stream_(film))* (1977), *[Breaking Away](/wiki/Breaking_Away)* (1979), *[Terror Train](/wiki/Terror_Train)* (1980), *[Rich and Famous](/wiki/Rich_and_Famous_(1981_film))* (1981), *[Supergirl](/wiki/Supergirl_(1984_film))* (1984), *[Apartment Zero](/wiki/Apartment_Zero)* (1988), *[Die Hard](/wiki/Die_Hard)* (1988), *[Mr. Destiny](/wiki/Mr._Destiny)* and *Fellow Traveller* (1989). In the late 1990s, Bochner had a voice role in *[Batman: Mask of the Phantasm](/wiki/Batman:_Mask_of_the_Phantasm)* (1993) and starred opposite [Susan Sarandon](/wiki/Susan_Sarandon) in *[Anywhere But Here](/wiki/Anywhere_but_Here_(film))* (1999). He also appeared in the films *[Urban Legends: Final Cut](/wiki/Urban_Legends:_Final_Cut)* (2000), and *[Say Nothing](/wiki/Say_Nothing_(film))* (2001). Bochner had an uncredited role in the 2013 remake of *[Carrie](/wiki/Carrie_(2013_film))*.

On television, Bochner had a key role as Byron Henry in the 1988 ABC miniseries *[War and Remembrance](/wiki/War_and_Remembrance_(miniseries))*. Later, he starred as Zach, the boyfriend of Molly Kagan ([Debra Messing](/wiki/Debra_Messing)), on [USA Network](/wiki/USA_Network)'s short-lived series *[The Starter Wife](/wiki/The_Starter_Wife_(TV_series))* (2008).

Among the films he has directed are *[PCU](/wiki/PCU_(film))* (1994), *[High School High](/wiki/High_School_High)* (1996), and *[Just Add Water](/wiki/Just_Add_Water_(film))* (2008).

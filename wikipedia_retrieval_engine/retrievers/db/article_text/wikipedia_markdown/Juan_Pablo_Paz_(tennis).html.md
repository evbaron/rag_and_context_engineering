**Juan Pablo Paz** (Latin American Spanish: [[ˈxwam ˈpaβlo ˈpas]](/wiki/Help:IPA/Spanish); born 4 January 1995) is an [Argentine](/wiki/Argentina) [tennis](/wiki/Tennis) player. Paz has a career-high [ATP](/wiki/Association_of_Tennis_Professionals) singles ranking of No. 284 achieved on 8 May 2017 and a career-high ATP doubles ranking of No. 231 achieved on 6 May 2024.

Paz made his ATP main draw debut at the [2017 Ecuador Open Quito](/wiki/2017_Ecuador_Open_Quito) in the doubles draw partnering [Gonzalo Escobar](/wiki/Gonzalo_Escobar).

# William VIII of Montpellier

**William VIII** (in [Occitan](/wiki/Occitan_language): Guilhem; died 1202) was [Lord of Montpellier](/wiki/Lord_of_Montpellier), the son of [William VII](/wiki/William_VII_of_Montpellier) and Matilda of Burgundy.

William VIII married [Eudokia Komnene](/wiki/Eudokia_Komnene,_Lady_of_Montpellier), grand-niece of the Byzantine emperor [Manuel I Komnenos](/wiki/Manuel_I_Komnenos). They had one daughter, [Marie of Montpellier](/wiki/Marie_of_Montpellier).

Lacking a male heir, William separated from Eudokia, sending her to a monastery in Ariane.
 William then married Agnes of Castile and sired eight more children:

- [William IX of Montpellier](/wiki/William_IX_of_Montpellier)
- Aymard, d. 1199
- [Bernat William](/wiki/Bernat_Guillem_de_Montpeller), married Jussiana d’Entença, daughter of Ponç Hug d’Entença
- Tortoseta (Thomas)
- Bergunyo, a priest
- Gui, a priest
- Agnes, married in 1203 [Raymond Roger Trencavel](/wiki/Raymond_Roger_Trencavel), viscount of Carcassonne, Béziers, Albi and Razès.
- Adalaïs.

The Pope ruled William's marriage to Agnes as illegitimate and Marie was given the throne.

William VIII was a patron of [troubadours](/wiki/Troubadour). [Arnaut de Mareuil](/wiki/Arnaut_de_Mareuil) came to his court after fleeing from the entourage of [Azalais of Toulouse](/wiki/Azalais_of_Toulouse), and at least one of Arnaut's poems is addressed to him.

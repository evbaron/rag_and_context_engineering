***General der Luftwaffe*** ([English](/wiki/English_language): General of the air force) was a [General of the branch](/wiki/General_of_the_branch) rank of the [Deutsche Luftwaffe](/wiki/Luftwaffe) (English: German Air Force) in Nazi Germany. Until the end of [World War II](/wiki/World_War_II) in 1945, this particular [general officer](/wiki/General_officer) rank was on [three-star](/wiki/Three-star_rank) level ([OF-8](/wiki/OF-8)), equivalent to a US [Lieutenant general](/wiki/Lieutenant_general).

The "General of the branch" ranks of the Luftwaffe were in 1945:

- [General of parachute troops](/wiki/General_der_Fallschirmtruppe)
- [General of anti-aircraft artillery](/wiki/General_der_Flakartillerie)
- [General of the aviators](/wiki/General_der_Flieger)
- [General of air force communications troops](/wiki/General_der_Luftnachrichtentruppe)
- General of the air force

The rank was equivalent to the [General of the branch](/wiki/General_of_the_branch) ranks of the *[Heer](/wiki/German_Army_(Wehrmacht))* (army) as follows:

Heer

- [General of artillery](/wiki/General_of_the_Artillery_(Germany))
- [General of mountain troops](/wiki/General_der_Gebirgstruppe)
- [General of infantry](/wiki/General_of_the_Infantry_(Germany))
- [General of cavalry](/wiki/General_of_the_Cavalry_(Germany))
- [General of the communications troops](/wiki/General_der_Nachrichtentruppe)
- [General of panzer troops](/wiki/General_der_Panzertruppe) (armoured troops)
- [General of engineers](/wiki/General_der_Pioniere)
- [General of the medical corps](/wiki/Generaloberstabsarzt)
- [General of the veterinary corps](/wiki/Generaloberstabveterin%C3%A4r)

The rank was also equivalent to the German three-star ranks:

- [*Admiral*](/wiki/Admiral_(Germany)) of the [Kriegsmarine](/wiki/Kriegsmarine), equivalent to (US [Vice admiral](/wiki/Vice_admiral)) and

***ID; Peace B*** is the debut [studio album](/wiki/Studio_album) by South Korean singer [BoA](/wiki/BoA), released on August 25, 2000 by [SM Entertainment](/wiki/SM_Entertainment). It saw moderate levels of success in South Korea upon its release, peaking at number 16 on the Recording Industry Association of Korea's monthly album chart in October 2000. The album sold 156,354 copies in 2000 and was the 59th best-selling album of the year. The album was released on May 29, 2002 in Japan, where it reached number 30 on the [Oricon Albums Chart](/wiki/Oricon_Albums_Chart).

## Production and lyrics

The song "Sara" is about a cat, "I'm Sorry" is about the tragedy of loving the lover of her older sister, and "[ID; Peace B](/wiki/ID;_Peace_B_(song))" is about the generation gap due to the use of the internet.

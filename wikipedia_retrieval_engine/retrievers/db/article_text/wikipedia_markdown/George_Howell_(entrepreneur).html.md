# George Howell (entrepreneur)

**George Howell** (born 1945) is an American [entrepreneur](/wiki/Entrepreneurship) and one of the pioneers of the [specialty-coffee](/wiki/Specialty_coffee) movement in the early 1970s.

## Early life

Born and raised in [New Jersey](/wiki/New_Jersey), Howell's family moved to [Mexico City](/wiki/Mexico_City) when he was 13. He studied art history, French, and Spanish literature at [Yale University](/wiki/Yale_University) from 1964 to 1967, when he dropped out.

## Career

In 1974, Howell and his wife Laurie moved to [Boston](/wiki/Boston). After having lived in [Berkeley, California](/wiki/Berkeley,_California), for a time, Howell said, "In 1974 we [decided] to leave the [West Coast](/wiki/West_Coast_of_the_United_States), I already had two kids with another on the way. We decided to move east to Boston. We drove cross-country. I took with me some whole bean coffee and a grinder. We stopped at the various [Howard Johnson’s](/wiki/Howard_Johnson%27s) that were on the [interstates](/wiki/Interstate_Highway_System) on our way and I would go into men’s room, grind the coffee there, leaving it smelling a whole lot better than when I walked in, and then I would take out my [French press](/wiki/French_press) on the counter, ask for hot water for 35 cents and make my French press at the Howard Johnson’s counter. And within minutes I would always have several people around me wondering what that was, and this is before I even had any clue that I would get into a coffee business."

### Coffee Connection

In 1975, Howell founded **Coffee Connection**, a [coffee shop](/wiki/Coffeehouse) and retailer in [Cambridge, Massachusetts](/wiki/Cambridge,_Massachusetts) which was a pioneer in [third wave coffee](/wiki/Third-wave_coffee). It expanded to 23 locations in the Northeast. It was acquired by [Starbucks Corporation](/wiki/Starbucks) for $23 million in 1994 (equivalent to $50 million in 2025), and formed the nexus of their expansion into the Boston area. With the acquisition, Starbucks acquired the rights to use the name [Frappuccino](/wiki/Frappuccino) for a popular blended iced coffee drink, which Starbucks reformulated.

In 2012, Howell founded **George Howell Coffee**. Its first location was in [Newtonville, Massachusetts](/wiki/Newtonville,_Massachusetts), and as of 2026 it is a mini-chain with five locations in the Boston area.

### Professional activities

In 1997, Howell was invited to become a quality consultant to [Brazil](/wiki/Brazil) as part of the Gourmet Project, a 2-year initiative set up by the [United Nations](/wiki/United_Nations)’ International Trade Council (ITC) and the [International Coffee Organization](/wiki/International_Coffee_Organization) (ICO), which influenced the creation of the [Cup of Excellence](/wiki/Cup_of_Excellence) competition.

In 1996, Howell received the [Specialty Coffee Association of America](/wiki/Specialty_Coffee_Association_of_America)'s Lifetime Achievement Award.

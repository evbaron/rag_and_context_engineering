**January 13** is the 13th day of the year in the [Gregorian calendar](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorian_calendar); 352 days remain until the end of the year (353 in [leap years](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leap_year)).

## Events

### Pre-1600

- [27 BC](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/27_BC) – [Octavian](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustus) transfers the state to the free disposal of the [Roman Senate](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Senate) and the people. He receives [Spain](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispania), [Gaul](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Gaul), and [Syria](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Syria) as his province for ten years.
- [532](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/532) – The [Nika riots](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nika_riots) break out, during the racing season at the [Hippodrome](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hippodrome) in [Constantinople](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constantinople), as a result of discontent with the rule of the Emperor [Justinian I](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justinian_I).
- [1435](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1435) – *[Sicut Dudum](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sicut_Dudum)*, forbidding the enslavement by the Spanish of the [Guanche](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guanches) natives in [Canary Islands](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canary_Islands) who had converted, or were converting to, Christianity, is promulgated by [Pope Eugene IV](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Eugene_IV).
- [1547](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1547) – [Henry Howard, Earl of Surrey](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Howard,_Earl_of_Surrey), is sentenced to death for treason, on the grounds of having quartered his arms to make them similar to those of the King, [Henry VIII of England](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_VIII_of_England).

### 1601–1900

- [1793](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1793) – [Nicolas Jean Hugon de Bassville](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Jean_Hugon_de_Bassville), representative of [Revolutionary France](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolutionary_France), is lynched by a mob in Rome.
- [1797](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1797) – [French Revolutionary Wars](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Revolutionary_Wars): A [naval battle](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_of_13_January_1797) between a French [ship of the line](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_of_the_line) and two British frigates off the coast of [Brittany](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brittany) ends with the French vessel running aground, resulting in over 900 deaths.
- [1815](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1815) – [War of 1812](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_1812): [British](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Empire) troops capture [Fort Peter](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Fort_Peter) in [St. Marys, Georgia](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Marys,_Georgia), the only battle of the war to take place in the state.
- [1822](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1822) – The design of the [Greek flag](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Greece) is adopted by the [First National Assembly at Epidaurus](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_National_Assembly_at_Epidaurus).
- [1833](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1833) – United States President [Andrew Jackson](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Jackson) writes to [Vice President](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice_President_of_the_United_States) elect [Martin Van Buren](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Van_Buren) expressing his opposition to [South Carolina](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Carolina)'s defiance of federal authority in the [Nullification Crisis](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nullification_Crisis).
- [1840](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1840) – The steamship *[Lexington](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lexington_(steamship))* burns and sinks four miles off the coast of [Long Island](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Island) with the loss of 139 lives.
- [1842](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1842) – Dr. [William Brydon](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Brydon), an assistant surgeon in the British [East India Company](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_India_Company) Army during the [First Anglo-Afghan War](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Anglo-Afghan_War), becomes famous for being the [sole survivor of an army](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1842_retreat_from_Kabul) of 4,500 men and 12,000 [camp followers](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_follower) when he reaches the safety of a garrison in [Jalalabad](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jalalabad), Afghanistan.
- [1847](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1847) – The [Treaty of Cahuenga](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Cahuenga) ends the [Mexican–American War](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican%E2%80%93American_War) in [California](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California).
- [1849](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1849) – Establishment of the [Colony of Vancouver Island](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colony_of_Vancouver_Island).
- 1849 – Second Anglo-Sikh War: [Battle of Chillianwala](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Chillianwala): British forces retreat from the Sikhs.
- [1888](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1888) – The [National Geographic Society](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Geographic_Society) is founded in Washington, D.C.
- [1893](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1893) – The [Independent Labour Party](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_Labour_Party) of the United Kingdom holds its first meeting.
- 1893 – [U.S. Marines](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Marine_Corps) land in [Honolulu, Hawaii](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honolulu) from the [USS *Boston*](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Boston_(1884)) to prevent the queen from abrogating the [Bayonet Constitution](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1887_Constitution_of_the_Kingdom_of_Hawaii).
- [1895](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1895) – [First Italo-Ethiopian War](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Italo-Ethiopian_War): The war's opening battle, the [Battle of Coatit](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Coatit), occurs; it is an Italian victory.
- [1898](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1898) – [Émile Zola](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89mile_Zola)'s *[J'accuse…!](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%27accuse%E2%80%A6!)* exposes the [Dreyfus affair](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dreyfus_affair).
- [1900](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1900) – To combat [Czech nationalism](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_nationalism), Emperor [Franz Joseph](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Joseph) decrees [German](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language) will be language of the [Austro-Hungarian Armed Forces](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austro-Hungarian_Armed_Forces).

### 1901–present

- [1908](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1908) – The [Rhoads Opera House fire](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhoads_Opera_House_fire) in [Boyertown, Pennsylvania](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boyertown,_Pennsylvania) kills 171 people.
- [1915](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1915) – The 6.7 Mw  [Avezzano earthquake](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1915_Avezzano_earthquake) shakes the [Province of L'Aquila](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_L%27Aquila) in Italy with a maximum [Mercalli intensity](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercalli_intensity_scale) of XI (*Extreme*), killing between 29,978 and 32,610.
- [1920](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1920) – The [Reichstag Bloodbath of January 13, 1920](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reichstag_Bloodbath), the bloodiest demonstration in German history.
- [1935](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1935) – A [plebiscite](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1935_Saar_status_referendum) in [Saarland](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saar_(League_of_Nations)) shows that 90.3% of those voting wish to no more [being a "region occupied and governed by the United Kingdom and France"](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Territory_of_the_Saar_Basin).
- [1939](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1939) – The [Black Friday bushfires](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Friday_bushfires) burn 20,000 square kilometres (7,700 sq mi) of land in Australia, claiming the lives of 71 people.
- [1942](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1942) – [Henry Ford](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Ford) patents a [soybean car](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soybean_car), which is 30% lighter than a regular car.
- 1942 – [World War II](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II): First use of an aircraft [ejection seat](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ejection_seat) by a German test pilot in a [Heinkel He 280](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinkel_He_280) jet fighter.
- [1950](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950) – British submarine [HMS *Truculent*](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Truculent_(P315)) collides with an oil tanker in the [Thames Estuary](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thames_Estuary), killing 64 men.
- 1950 – Finland forms [diplomatic relations](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China%E2%80%93Finland_relations) with the People's Republic of China.
- [1951](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1951) – [First Indochina War](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Indochina_War): The [Battle of Vĩnh Yên](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_V%C4%A9nh_Y%C3%AAn) begins.
- [1953](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1953) – An article appears in *[Pravda](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pravda)* accusing some of the most prestigious and prominent doctors, mostly Jews, in the Soviet Union of taking part in a [vast plot](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctors%27_plot) to poison members of the top Soviet political and military leadership.
- [1958](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1958) – The [Moroccan Army of Liberation](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moroccan_Army_of_Liberation) ambushes a Spanish patrol in the [Battle of Edchera](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ifni_War).
- [1963](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1963) – [Coup d'état](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1963_Togolese_coup_d%27%C3%A9tat) in [Togo](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Togo) results in the assassination of president [Sylvanus Olympio](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvanus_Olympio).
- [1964](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964) – [Anti-Muslim](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violence_against_Muslims_in_India) riots break out in [Calcutta](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolkata), in response to [anti-Hindu riots](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964_East_Pakistan_riots) in [East Pakistan](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Pakistan). About one hundred people are killed.
- 1964 – In [Manchester, New Hampshire](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester,_New_Hampshire), fourteen-year-old Pamela Mason is murdered. Edward Coolidge is tried and convicted of the crime, but the conviction is set aside by the landmark [Fourth Amendment](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_Amendment_to_the_United_States_Constitution) case [Coolidge v. New Hampshire](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coolidge_v._New_Hampshire) (1971).
- [1966](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1966) – [Robert C. Weaver](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_C._Weaver) becomes the first [African American](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_American) Cabinet member when he is appointed [United States Secretary of Housing and Urban Development](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Secretary_of_Housing_and_Urban_Development).
- [1968](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968) – [Johnny Cash](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Cash) [performs live](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/At_Folsom_Prison) at [Folsom State Prison](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folsom_State_Prison).
- [1972](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972) – Prime Minister [Kofi Abrefa Busia](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kofi_Abrefa_Busia) and President [Edward Akufo-Addo](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Akufo-Addo) of [Ghana](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghana) are ousted in a bloodless [military coup](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coup_d%27%C3%A9tat) by Colonel [Ignatius Kutu Acheampong](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ignatius_Kutu_Acheampong).
- [1977](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1977) – [Japan Air Lines Cargo Flight 1045](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Air_Lines_Cargo_Flight_1045), a [Douglas DC-8](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_DC-8) jet, crashes onto the runway during takeoff from [Ted Stevens Anchorage International Airport](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Stevens_Anchorage_International_Airport), killing five.
- [1978](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1978) – United States [Food and Drug Administration](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_and_Drug_Administration) requires all [blood donations](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_donation) to be labeled "paid" or "volunteer" donors.
- [1982](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1982) – Shortly after takeoff, [Air Florida Flight 90](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Florida_Flight_90), a [Boeing 737](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_737) jet, crashes into [Washington, D.C.](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.)'s [14th Street Bridge](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/14th_Street_bridges) and falls into the [Potomac River](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potomac_River), killing 78 including four motorists.
- [1985](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985) – A passenger train [plunges into a ravine](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awash_rail_disaster) in [Ethiopia](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethiopia), killing 428 in the worst railroad disaster in Africa.
- [1986](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986) – A month-long violent struggle begins in [Aden, South Yemen](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aden) between supporters of [Ali Nasir Muhammad](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Nasir_Muhammad) and [Abdul Fattah Ismail](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Fattah_Ismail), resulting in thousands of casualties.
- [1988](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1988) – [Lee Teng-hui](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Teng-hui) becomes the first [native Taiwanese](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese_people) [President of the Republic of China](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Republic_of_China).
- [1990](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990) – [Douglas Wilder](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_Wilder) becomes the first elected African American governor as he takes office as [Governor of Virginia](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_Virginia) in [Richmond, Virginia](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond,_Virginia).
- [1991](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991) – Soviet Union troops [attack](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_Events_(Lithuania)) [Lithuanian](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuania) independence supporters in [Vilnius](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilnius), killing 14 people and wounding around 1,000 others.
- [1993](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993) – [Space Shuttle program](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Shuttle_program): [*Endeavour*](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Shuttle_Endeavour) heads for space for the third time as [STS-54](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STS-54) launches from the [Kennedy Space Center](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennedy_Space_Center).
- 1993 – The [Chemical Weapons Convention](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_Weapons_Convention) (CWC) is signed.
- 1993 – [Operation Southern Watch](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Southern_Watch): [U.S.A.F., U.S.N., R.A.F. and French Air Force jets attack AAA and SAM sites in Southern Iraq.](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_1993_airstrikes_on_Iraq)
- [1998](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998) – [Alfredo Ormando](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfredo_Ormando) sets himself on fire in [St. Peter's Square](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Peter%27s_Square), protesting against [homophobia](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homophobia).
- [2000](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000) – A [Short 360](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_360) aircraft chartered by the [Sirte Oil Company](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirte_Oil_Company) [crashes](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000_Marsa_Brega_Short_360_crash) off the coast of [Brega](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brega), Libya, killing 21.
- [2001](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001) – An [earthquake](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_2001_El_Salvador_earthquake#The_January_13_earthquake) hits [El Salvador](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Salvador), killing more than 800.
- [2003](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003) – [208996 Achlys](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/208996_Achlys) was discovered by [Chad Trujillo](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chad_Trujillo) and [Michael E. Brown](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_E._Brown) at [Palomar Observatory](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palomar_Observatory).
- [2004](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004) – [Uzbekistan Airways Flight 1154](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbekistan_Airways_Flight_1154) crashes while landing at [Tashkent International Airport](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tashkent_International_Airport), killing 37.
- [2012](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012) – The passenger [cruise ship](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruise_ship) *[Costa Concordia](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Concordia)* [sinks](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Concordia_disaster) off the coast of Italy due to the captain [Francesco Schettino](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Schettino)'s negligence and irresponsibility. There are 32 confirmed deaths.
- [2018](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018) – A [false emergency alert](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Hawaii_false_missile_alert) warning of an impending missile strike in [Hawaii](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaii) causes widespread panic in the state.
- [2020](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020) – The Thai [Ministry of Public Health](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Public_Health_(Thailand)) confirms the first case of [COVID-19](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19) outside China.
- [2021](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021) – Outgoing [U.S. President](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._President) [Donald Trump](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump) is [impeached for a second time](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_impeachment_of_Donald_Trump) on a charge of incitement of insurrection following the [January 6 United States Capitol attack](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_6_United_States_Capitol_attack) one week prior.

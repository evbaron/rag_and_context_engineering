# Victoria Pedretti

Not to be confused with [Vittoria Ceretti](/wiki/Vittoria_Ceretti).

**Victoria Pedretti** (born March 23, 1995) is an American actress. She gained wide recognition for starring in the [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) horror series [*The Haunting of Hill House*](/wiki/The_Haunting_of_Hill_House_(TV_series)) (2018) and *[The Haunting of Bly Manor](/wiki/The_Haunting_of_Bly_Manor)* (2020), and in the Netflix thriller series *[You](/wiki/You_(TV_series))* (2019–2021; 2023), emerging as a [scream queen](/wiki/Scream_queen). For these roles, she received a [MTV Award](/wiki/MTV_Movie_%26_TV_Awards) and nominations at the [Critics' Choice](/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Super_Awards) and [Saturn Awards](/wiki/Saturn_Awards).

Pedretti's made her feature film debut as [Leslie Van Houten](/wiki/Leslie_Van_Houten) in the comedy drama *[Once Upon a Time in Hollywood](/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Hollywood)* (2019). She has since appeared in the biographical drama [*Origin*](/wiki/Origin_(film)) (2023), the thriller *[Ponyboi](/wiki/Ponyboi)* (2024) and the comedy horror *[Forbidden Fruits](/wiki/Forbidden_Fruits)* (2026). She made her stage debut in a [Broadway](/wiki/Broadway_theatre) revival of *[An Enemy of the People](/wiki/An_Enemy_of_the_People)* (2024).

## Early life and education

Victoria Pedretti was born on March 23, 1995, in [Philadelphia](/wiki/Philadelphia). She is of part Italian ancestry. She was raised Jewish, and had a [bat mitzvah](/wiki/Bat_mitzvah). She was diagnosed with [ADHD](/wiki/Attention_deficit_hyperactivity_disorder) when she was seven years old. She first expressed an interest in musical theatre while attending [Pennsbury High School](/wiki/Pennsbury_High_School) in [Fairless Hills](/wiki/Fairless_Hills,_Pennsylvania). She received her [Bachelor of Fine Arts](/wiki/Bachelor_of_Fine_Arts) at the [Carnegie Mellon School of Drama](/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_School_of_Drama) in [Pittsburgh](/wiki/Pittsburgh), where she said her professors advised her not to pursue acting.

## Career

Pedretti began acting in 2014, having roles in two [short films](/wiki/Short_film): *Sole* where she played the female protagonist, and *Uncovering Eden* where she played Edie.

In 2017, Pedretti was cast in [Mike Flanagan](/wiki/Mike_Flanagan_(filmmaker))'s [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) supernatural horror drama series [*The Haunting of Hill House*](/wiki/The_Haunting_of_Hill_House_(TV_series)), in which she played Eleanor "Nell" Crain. It was released in 2018 to critical acclaim, and became Pedretti's [breakthrough role](/wiki/Breakthrough_role). Chad Collins of *[Dread Central](/wiki/Dread_Central)* called her the "soul of *The Haunting of Hill House*", and [Lucy Mangan](/wiki/Lucy_Mangan) of *[The Guardian](/wiki/The_Guardian)* called her "scene-stealing". Dakota Moss of *[MovieWeb](/wiki/MovieWeb)* wrote: "Pedretti's emotions to the role add a horrifying realism to the character, and viewers can feel all of Nell's pain and anguish to the very end. This is one of the defining roles for Victoria Pedretti, and she's only getting started." She received [Saturn Award](/wiki/Saturn_Awards) and [MTV Award](/wiki/MTV_Movie_%26_TV_Awards) nominations for the role and was established as a [scream queen](/wiki/Scream_queen).

Pedretti auditioned for the role of [Guinevere Beck](/wiki/Guinevere_Beck) on the Netflix thriller series *[You](/wiki/You_(TV_series))* but did not land the part. The directors later cast her as [Love Quinn](/wiki/Love_Quinn) in the show's [second](/wiki/You_season_2) and [third](/wiki/You_season_3) seasons after seeing her previous acting work and chemistry with her co-star [Penn Badgley](/wiki/Penn_Badgley). While filming during the [COVID-19 pandemic](/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic), the intimate scenes posed a challenge for Pedretti to film as "it took months to figure out how to do it safely. [...] We played a couple before, but it didn't just immediately come back after being in a global pandemic." The second and third seasons were released in 2019 and 2021, respectively, to critical acclaim. For season two, writers at [*Elle*](/wiki/Elle_(magazine)) said Pedretti "delivered the perfect amount of spook and smolder to keep us on the edge of our seats for all ten episodes." She was nominated for the [MTV Movie Award for Best Villain](/wiki/MTV_Movie_Award_for_Best_Villain).

In 2019, Pedretti played [Leslie Van Houten](/wiki/Leslie_Van_Houten) in [Quentin Tarantino](/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino)'s comedy-drama film *[Once Upon a Time in Hollywood](/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Hollywood)*, which was her first role in a feature film and a commercial success. She followed this starring as Evelyn Porter in the 2020 [pilot episode](/wiki/Television_pilot) of the anthology series *[Amazing Stories](/wiki/Amazing_Stories_(2020_TV_series))*. She also appeared in the short film *This is Not a Love Letter* and played Katherine in the biographical-drama film *[Shirley](/wiki/Shirley_(2020_film)).*

A follow up series to *The Haunting of Hill House* was announced in 2019 with Pedretti cast in the leading role; it was released as *[The Haunting of Bly Manor](/wiki/The_Haunting_of_Bly_Manor)* in 2020 and saw Pedretti playing Danielle "Dani" Clayton. [Alan Sepinwall](/wiki/Alan_Sepinwall) of *[The Hollywood Reporter](/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter)* wrote that "Her appealing vulnerability, and her talent for making the supernatural feel like something painfully real to Dani, do a lot of the heavy lifting this time around. Katerina Daley of *[Screen Rant](/wiki/Screen_Rant)* said that her performance "has absolutely been a star-making one..." [Angelica Jade Bastién](/wiki/Angelica_Jade_Basti%C3%A9n) of [*Vulture* magazine](/wiki/Vulture_(website)) said Pedretti "has proven herself to be one of the most exciting young actors to watch". She was nominated for a [Critics' Choice Super Award](/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Super_Awards) and won [Best Frightened Performance](/wiki/MTV_Movie_Award_for_Best_Scared-As-Shit_Performance) at the [2021 MTV Movie & TV Awards](/wiki/2021_MTV_Movie_%26_TV_Awards).

In 2021, Pedretti appeared in singer-songwriter [Kacey Musgraves](/wiki/Kacey_Musgraves)' *[Star-Crossed: The Film](/wiki/Star-Crossed:_The_Film)*, a companion feature to [Musgraves' album of the same name](/wiki/Star-Crossed_(album)), in a [cameo role](/wiki/Cameo_role) as a woman participating in a heist. A clip from the film featuring Pedretti later served as the music video for Musgraves' song "Simple Times". Also in 2021, she was announced to star in the film *Lucky*, based on the [memoir of the same name](/wiki/Lucky_(memoir)), in which she would be portraying writer [Alice Sebold](/wiki/Alice_Sebold) during her freshman year at Syracuse University. However, the film was cancelled after losing its financing.

Pedretti was announced in 2022 to be headlining the [Hulu](/wiki/Hulu) [original series](/wiki/Hulu_original_series) *[Saint X](/wiki/Saint_X)*, but she reportedly left the series due to creative differences, with [Alycia Debnam-Carey](/wiki/Alycia_Debnam-Carey) replacing her in the lead role.

In 2023, Pedretti played Irma Eckler in [Ava DuVernay](/wiki/Ava_DuVernay)'s acclaimed biographical drama film *[Origin](/wiki/Origin_(film))*. She also reprised the role of Love Quinn in the [fourth season](/wiki/You_season_4) of *You* as a [cameo appearance](/wiki/Cameo_appearance). Michelle Boyar of *[Screen Rant](/wiki/Screen_Rant)* opined that, because she appears briefly and has few lines, "*You* season 4 wasted Love's cameo."

In 2024, Pedretti starred as Angel in the thriller-drama film *[Ponyboi](/wiki/Ponyboi)*, which was described as the "queer breakout" of that year's [Sundance Film Festival](/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival). On accepting the role, Pedretti said that "I think the most important films to be made are the ones that deal with subject matters that people aren't well-versed in and with characters they haven't seen before. This was an incredible opportunity for that to happen." She also starred in the short films *Fall Risk* and *Merci, Poppy* as Dylan and Poppy respectively. That same year, she made her [Broadway](/wiki/Broadway_(theatre)) debut in the [Sam Gold](/wiki/Sam_Gold) directed adaptation of the [Henrik Ibsen](/wiki/Henrik_Ibsen) play, *[An Enemy of the People](/wiki/An_Enemy_of_the_People)*, at the [Circle in the Square Theatre](/wiki/Circle_in_the_Square_Theatre). Gold said of her casting that "She worked really hard in auditions and won the part for proving herself a really strong state actor who could take direction and take [it] all on her shoulders..." Greg Evans of *[Deadline Hollywood](/wiki/Deadline_Hollywood)* believed Pedretti "is ready-made for her strong-willed, occasionally fretful, role". She next headlined an [Off-Broadway](/wiki/Off-Broadway) production of the [Philip Ridley](/wiki/Philip_Ridley) play *[Tender Napalm](/wiki/Tender_Napalm)*.

Pedretti's first release of 2026 was the comedy horror film *[Forbidden Fruits](/wiki/Forbidden_Fruits)*, in which she plays Cherry, the "earnest heart" of a [cult](/wiki/Cult). A writer from the *[Los Angeles Times](/wiki/Los_Angeles_Times)* believed the role expanded her scream queen status, and her performance earned additional praise from writers at *[The New York Times](/wiki/The_New_York_Times)* and *[The A.V. Club](/wiki/The_A.V._Club)*.

Pedretti will next star in *If She Burns* written and directed by [Alex Wolff](/wiki/Alex_Wolff), *The Non-Actor* written and directed by Eliza Callahan, and *The Last Day* directed by [Rachel Rose](/wiki/Rachel_Rose). The lattermost is inspired by [Virginia Woolf](/wiki/Virginia_Woolf)'s 1925 novel *[Mrs Dalloway](/wiki/Mrs_Dalloway)*.

## Artistry

Pedretti has said her experience with *The Haunting of Hill House* made her comfortable with playing darker roles in the horror and thriller genres, and that she has "got a knack for" them. Laura Studarus of *[Shondaland](/wiki/Shondaland)* says that Pedretti "carved out a niche in the horror and suspense genre" and also opined that she can be versatile. For her performance in *The Haunting of Bly Manor*, she cites [Timothée Chalamet](/wiki/Timoth%C3%A9e_Chalamet)'s acting in the film [*Call Me by Your Name*](/wiki/Call_Me_by_Your_Name_(film)) (2017) as an inspiration. After her Broadway debut, Pedretti expressed an interest in continuing stage work, telling [*Interview*](/wiki/Interview_(magazine)) that "doing theater, it's very ideal. I'd love to keep doing this."

## Personal life

Pedretti currently resides in New York. She briefly dated her *You* co-star [Dylan Arnold](/wiki/Dylan_Arnold) in 2021, and [Fred Hechinger](/wiki/Fred_Hechinger) in 2024. In July 2025, she announced that she was engaged to photographer Ethan Delorenzo, who has a son (b. 2016) with his former *fiancée*, actress [Jena Malone](/wiki/Jena_Malone).

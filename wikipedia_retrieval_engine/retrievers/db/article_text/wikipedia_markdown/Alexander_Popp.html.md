**Alexander Popp** (born 4 November 1976) is a former German professional [tennis](/wiki/Tennis) player. He reached the quarterfinals of [Wimbledon](/wiki/The_Championships,_Wimbledon) in [2000](/wiki/2000_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_singles) and [2003](/wiki/2003_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_singles).

## Personal life

Popp was born in [Heidelberg](/wiki/Heidelberg) to parents Rainer and Jennifer, and started playing tennis at the age of 8. He was coached by Helmut Luthy, from 1994 until retirement. He holds a [British](/wiki/United_Kingdom) passport through his mother, who was born in [Wolverhampton](/wiki/Wolverhampton).

## Tennis career

Popp turned professional in 1997 at the age of 21.

Popp's career highlights are making the quarterfinals of Wimbledon (by far his most successful tournament) in [2000](/wiki/2000_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_singles) (defeating [Gustavo Kuerten](/wiki/Gustavo_Kuerten) and [Michael Chang](/wiki/Michael_Chang) en route), and in [2003](/wiki/2003_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_singles) (defeating [Jiří Novák](/wiki/Ji%C5%99%C3%AD_Nov%C3%A1k)). He also reached the fourth round in [2004](/wiki/2004_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_singles), losing to the eventual runner-up in each of these three runs ([Patrick Rafter](/wiki/Patrick_Rafter), [Mark Philippoussis](/wiki/Mark_Philippoussis) and [Andy Roddick](/wiki/Andy_Roddick) respectively), and the third round in [2005](/wiki/2005_Wimbledon_Championships_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_singles). Popp also reached the final of [Newport in 2004](/wiki/2004_Hall_of_Fame_Tennis_Championships) and achieved a career-high singles ranking of World No. 74.

In doubles, Popp made the final of [Newport](/wiki/Hall_of_Fame_Tennis_Championships) in 2002 (partnering [Jürgen Melzer](/wiki/J%C3%BCrgen_Melzer)) and the semifinals of the [Ho Chi Minh City](/wiki/Ho_Chi_Minh_City) championships in 2005 (partnering [Jiří Vaněk](/wiki/Ji%C5%99%C3%AD_Van%C4%9Bk_(tennis_player))).

# The New England Magazine

For the unrelated magazine published 1831–1835, see [The New-England Magazine](/wiki/The_New-England_Magazine).

***The New England Magazine*** was a monthly [literary magazine](/wiki/Literary_magazine) published in [Boston, Massachusetts](/wiki/Boston,_Massachusetts), from 1884 to 1917. It was known as ***The Bay State Monthly*** from 1884 to 1886.

The magazine was published by J. N. McClinctock and Company.

The magazine has no connection to the 1830s publication *[The New-England Magazine](/wiki/The_New-England_Magazine)*.

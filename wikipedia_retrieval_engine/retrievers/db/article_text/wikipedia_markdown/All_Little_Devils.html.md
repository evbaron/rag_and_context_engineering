***All Little Devils*** is the third studio album by the Norwegian [gothic metal](/wiki/Gothic_metal) band [Dismal Euphony](/wiki/Dismal_Euphony). It was released in 1999, and was the band's first album with [Nuclear Blast](/wiki/Nuclear_Blast).

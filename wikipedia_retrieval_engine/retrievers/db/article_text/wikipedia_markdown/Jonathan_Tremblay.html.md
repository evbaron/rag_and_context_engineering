**Jonathan Tremblay** (born November 15, 1984) is a [Canadian](/wiki/Canadians) politician, who was elected to the [House of Commons of Canada](/wiki/House_of_Commons_of_Canada) in the [2011 election](/wiki/2011_Canadian_federal_election). He represented the electoral district of [Montmorency—Charlevoix—Haute-Côte-Nord](/wiki/Montmorency%E2%80%94Charlevoix%E2%80%94Haute-C%C3%B4te-Nord) as a member of the [New Democratic Party](/wiki/New_Democratic_Party). Tremblay was defeated when he ran for re-election in [2015](/wiki/2015_Canadian_federal_election).

Shortly after his defeat, Tremblay announced he would seek a city council seat in [Beaupré](/wiki/Beaupr%C3%A9,_Quebec) in a by-election.

Prior to being elected, Tremblay was a bricklayer and mason. He has three children.

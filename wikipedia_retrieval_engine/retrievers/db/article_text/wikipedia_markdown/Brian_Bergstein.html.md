# Brian Bergstein

**Brian Bergstein** is the National [Technology](/wiki/Technology) Editor for the American [Associated Press](/wiki/Associated_Press) news agency, based in [Boston, Massachusetts](/wiki/Boston,_Massachusetts). His work focuses mainly on the economic, legal, and social implications of upcoming technologies.

## Personal

He is a graduate of [Northwestern University](/wiki/Northwestern_University). Bergstein was raised in [Los Angeles, California](/wiki/Los_Angeles,_California). He, his wife, and two children currently live in [Brookline, Massachusetts](/wiki/Brookline,_Massachusetts).

## Awards and honors

From 2004 to 2005, he held one of the [Knight Science Journalism Fellowships](/wiki/Knight_Science_Journalism_Fellowships).

## Career

Bergstein has been a technology journalist for 13 years. He has worked on the Web, computing, telecom, and in the business of technology from Silicon Valley, [New York](/wiki/New_York_(state)), and [Boston](/wiki/Boston). Previously a technology writer for the Associated Press's New York bureau, Bergstein was promoted to Technology Editor in mid-2008 when the AP reorganised to cover stories by topic rather than geographical areas. He has worked as a journalism instructor at [Boston University](/wiki/Boston_University). He is also deputy editor of MIT Technology Review.

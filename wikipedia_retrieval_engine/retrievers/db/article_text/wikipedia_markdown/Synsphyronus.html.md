# Synsphyronus

***Synsphyronus*** is a [genus](/wiki/Genus) of [pseudoscorpions](/wiki/Pseudoscorpions) in the [Garypidae](/wiki/Garypidae) [family](/wiki/Family_(biology)). It was described in 1930 by American [arachnologist](/wiki/Arachnology) [Joseph Conrad Chamberlin](/wiki/Joseph_Conrad_Chamberlin). Its distribution is mainly in [Australia](/wiki/Australia), but also extends to [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand) and [New Caledonia](/wiki/New_Caledonia).

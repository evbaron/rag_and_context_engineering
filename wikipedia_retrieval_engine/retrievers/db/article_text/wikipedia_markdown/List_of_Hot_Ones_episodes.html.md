# List of Hot Ones episodes

*[Hot Ones](/wiki/Hot_Ones)* is an American [YouTube](/wiki/YouTube) [talk show](/wiki/Talk_show), created by Christopher Schonberger and [Sean Evans](/wiki/Sean_Evans_(interviewer)) and produced by [First We Feast](/wiki/First_We_Feast) and [Complex Media](/wiki/Complex_(magazine)). Its basic premise involves celebrities being interviewed by Evans over a platter of increasingly spicy [chicken wings](/wiki/Buffalo_wing).

399 episodes including seven bonus episodes and one removed episode have been released as of February 19, 2026.

# 2001 Miami Dolphins season

The **2001 Miami Dolphins season** was the franchise's 32nd season in the [National Football League](/wiki/National_Football_League) and the 36th season in the AFC division, and their 2nd under the guidance of head coach Dave Wannstedt. The [Miami Dolphins](/wiki/Miami_Dolphins) finished the season 2nd in the [AFC East](/wiki/AFC_East) with a record of 11–5. Their season ended with a resounding 17-point home loss to the defending Super Bowl champions, the [Baltimore Ravens](/wiki/2001_Baltimore_Ravens_season), in the Wild Card round of the playoffs.

The Miami Dolphins ranked 8th in points scored and 11th in points allowed.

While the team finished with a respectable 11–5 record, they engineered embarrassing regular season losses to the [New York Jets](/wiki/New_York_Jets) 24–0 in Miami and 21–17 in New Jersey after blowing a 17-point halftime lead; and blow out losses to the [San Francisco 49ers](/wiki/2001_San_Francisco_49ers) 21–0 and [St. Louis Rams](/wiki/2001_St._Louis_Rams) 42–10. The Dolphins finished the season with a 20–3-home loss to the [Baltimore Ravens](/wiki/2001_Baltimore_Ravens). In these five losses, the Dolphins were -19 in turnover differential, turning the football over 20 times. For the season, the Dolphins gave away the football 41 times for a -13 total turnover differential. Starting quarterback [Jay Fiedler](/wiki/Jay_Fiedler) led the team in turnovers by throwing 19 interceptions and fumbling six times.

The Dolphins did not return to the playoffs until [2008](/wiki/2008_Miami_Dolphins_season), and would not make back-to-back playoff appearances until [2022](/wiki/2022_Miami_Dolphins_season) and [2023](/wiki/2023_Miami_Dolphins_season). After the season, [Harry Swayne](/wiki/Harry_Swayne) retired.

#### AFC: Baltimore Ravens 20, Miami Dolphins 3

**Game summary**

- **Game time**: 4:00 p.m. [EST](/wiki/Eastern_Time_Zone)

The Ravens recorded 222 rushing yards, while limiting the Dolphins to 151 total yards and nine first downs, while forcing three turnovers and three sacks. Baltimore running back [Terry Allen](/wiki/Terry_Allen_(running_back)) ran for 109 yards and a touchdown, while quarterback [Elvis Grbac](/wiki/Elvis_Grbac) completed 12 of 18 passes for 133 yards and a touchdown. Throughout the day, the Dolphins were unable to move the ball on the ground. Running backs [Travis Minor](/wiki/Travis_Minor) and [Lamar Smith](/wiki/Lamar_Smith_(American_football)) were held to a combined total of 20 yards on 11 carries, while quarterback [Jay Fiedler](/wiki/Jay_Fiedler) ended up as the leading rusher with 16 yards. In contrast, the Ravens called 50 running plays, gained 222 rushing yards, and held the ball for 38 minutes.

The Dolphins' only score was [Olindo Mare](/wiki/Olindo_Mare)'s 33-yard field goal just two minutes into the game, after linebacker [Tommy Hendricks](/wiki/Tommy_Hendricks) recovered a fumble from Baltimore's [Jermaine Lewis](/wiki/Jermaine_Lewis_(American_football,_born_1974)) on the opening kickoff. In the second quarter, the Ravens finished a 17-play, 90-yard drive with a 4-yard touchdown run from Allen to take a 7–3 lead. Baltimore later had a chance to increase their lead before halftime when linebacker [Peter Boulware](/wiki/Peter_Boulware) recovered Minor's fumble on the Dolphins 41-yard line, but their ensuing drive ended without points when [Matt Stover](/wiki/Matt_Stover) missed a 40-yard field goal attempt on the last play of the half.

Later in the game, Grbac led the Ravens on a 99-yard scoring drive, featuring a 45-yard completion to [Travis Taylor](/wiki/Travis_Taylor_(American_football)) on third down and 1. Taylor finished the drive with a 4-yard touchdown catch to give the Ravens a 14–3 lead with 1:20 left in the third quarter.

Early in the final quarter, Boulware forced a fumble while sacking Fiedler that Ravens lineman [Sam Adams](/wiki/Sam_Adams_(American_football)) recovered on the Dolphins 37-yard line, leading to Stover's 35-yard field goal with 11:26 left in the game. The Dolphins responded with a drive to the Ravens 41. On first and 10, Fiedler's 40-yard pass to [James McKnight](/wiki/James_McKnight_(American_football)) at the Ravens 5-yard line bounced off the receiver's shoulder and was intercepted by defensive back [Duane Starks](/wiki/Duane_Starks), who returned the ball 26 yards to the 28-yard line. Baltimore's offense subsequently drove 50 yards and took 6:30 off the clock, including five carries by [Jason Brookins](/wiki/Jason_Brookins) for 36 yards, setting up Stover's second field goal to put the game away.

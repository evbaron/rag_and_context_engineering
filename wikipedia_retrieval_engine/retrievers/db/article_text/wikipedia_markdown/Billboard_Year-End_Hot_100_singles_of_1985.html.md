# Billboard Year-End Hot 100 singles of 1985

[Wham!](/wiki/Wham!) (*pictured*) had four songs on the Year-End Hot 100, including the year's biggest hit, *[Careless Whisper](/wiki/Careless_Whisper)*.

[Madonna](/wiki/Madonna) (*pictured*) had five songs on the Year-End Hot 100, tied with Phil Collins with the most of any artist in 1985.

This is a list of *[Billboard](/wiki/Billboard_(magazine))* magazine's Top **[Hot 100](/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100)** [singles](/wiki/Single_(music)) of [1985](/wiki/1985_in_music).

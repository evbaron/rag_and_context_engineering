**James Christopher Carmack** (born December 22, 1980) is an American actor, singer, and former fashion model. He is known for his roles in popular television shows—the teen drama series *[The O.C.](/wiki/The_O.C.)* (2003–2004) as [Luke Ward](/wiki/Luke_Ward), the country music drama *[Nashville](/wiki/Nashville_(2012_TV_series))* as Will Lexington (2012–2018), and the medical drama *[Grey's Anatomy](/wiki/Grey%27s_Anatomy)* (2018–present) as Dr. Atticus "Link" Lincoln.

Carmack has also appeared in films, including *[The Butterfly Effect 3: Revelations](/wiki/The_Butterfly_Effect_3:_Revelations)*, *[Into the Blue 2: The Reef](/wiki/Into_the_Blue_2:_The_Reef)*, *[Love Wrecked](/wiki/Love_Wrecked)*, *[Beauty & the Briefcase](/wiki/Beauty_%26_the_Briefcase)* and *[Alpha and Omega](/wiki/Alpha_and_Omega_(film))*.

## Early life and education

Carmack was born on December 22, 1980 in Washington D.C. He was born James Christopher Carmack and grew up in [Derwood, Maryland](/wiki/Derwood,_Maryland). He has a brother and a sister. As a young boy, he played [baseball](/wiki/Baseball), [basketball](/wiki/Basketball), and [football](/wiki/American_football) at [Magruder High School](/wiki/Colonel_Zadok_A._Magruder_High_School). However, his attentions turned to theater and he began working on three productions per year, on-stage and off, in addition to competing in numerous drama festivals.

## Career

After graduation, Carmack attended [New York University](/wiki/New_York_University) to pursue an arts degree at the [Tisch School](/wiki/Tisch_School). He left NYU after he was discovered by John Yannella, a modeling scout, and decided to have a go at the profession. His looks were exactly what [Bruce Weber](/wiki/Bruce_Weber_(photographer)) was seeking for [Abercrombie & Fitch](/wiki/Abercrombie_%26_Fitch). The other catalogs and editorials that he has worked for include: [Lord and Taylor](/wiki/Lord_and_Taylor), [Macy's](/wiki/Macy%27s), [Target](/wiki/Target_Corporation), [Who.A.U](/wiki/Who.A.U), *[Elle](/wiki/Elle_(magazine))* France, [Nautica](/wiki/Nautica), [Guess?](/wiki/Guess%3F), and *[Cosmogirl](/wiki/Cosmogirl)*.

He played [Luke](/wiki/Luke_Ward) on the first season of *[The O.C.](/wiki/The_O.C.)*, but did not continue because producers could not guarantee him airtime in the upcoming year. In 2005, Carmack became a model for [Ezra Fitch](/wiki/Ezra_Fitch), a limited upscale label sold at Abercrombie & Fitch.

In 2006, Carmack starred in the film *[Lovewrecked](/wiki/Lovewrecked)* with [Amanda Bynes](/wiki/Amanda_Bynes) and [Jonathan Bennett](/wiki/Jonathan_Bennett_(actor)) and honed his acting skills in the theaters of New York and London.

From March 16 to May 21, 2006 Carmack starred in the Off-Broadway production of *[Entertaining Mr. Sloane](/wiki/Entertaining_Mr._Sloane)* at the Laura Pels Theatre. The play, directed by [Scott Ellis](/wiki/Scott_Ellis), also featured Tony Award nominees [Alec Baldwin](/wiki/Alec_Baldwin) and [Jan Maxwell](/wiki/Jan_Maxwell).

In 2006, Carmack made his London stage debut in a rare revival of [Tennessee Williams](/wiki/Tennessee_Williams)' *[Summer and Smoke](/wiki/Summer_and_Smoke)*, a play that had not been performed in London for 55 years. Carmack starred opposite [Rosamund Pike](/wiki/Rosamund_Pike) and was directed by former Royal Shakespeare Company artistic director [Adrian Noble](/wiki/Adrian_Noble). It played the Apollo Theatre from October 21 – November 25, 2006, failing to complete its scheduled run.

Carmack portrayed [Susan Delfino](/wiki/Susan_Delfino)'s cousin Tim in the television show *[Desperate Housewives](/wiki/Desperate_Housewives)* for one episode.

Carmack portrayed Sam Reide, the main character of the film *[The Butterfly Effect 3: Revelations](/wiki/The_Butterfly_Effect_3:_Revelations)*, the third film in *[The Butterfly Effect](/wiki/The_Butterfly_Effect)* film franchise, released in theaters in January 2009. He also made his first voice role in *[Alpha and Omega](/wiki/Alpha_and_Omega_(film))*, which was released in theaters in September 17, 2010, and reprised his role as Garth in *[Alpha and Omega 2: A Howl-iday Adventure](/wiki/Alpha_and_Omega_2:_A_Howl-iday_Adventure)*, being one of the few actors from the original to appear in at least one sequel.

In 2013, Carmack was cast in the ABC drama series *[Nashville](/wiki/Nashville_(2012_TV_series))* as Scarlett and Gunnar's neighbor Will, a sexy and charming country singer and guitar player. Carmack's character initially appeared to be a womanizer and a potential threat to Scarlett and Gunnar's relationship, but it was revealed that the character is gay, and trying to protect his image as a heterosexual country music heartthrob. Carmack appeared in the final six episodes of the season, and was promoted from a recurring role to a series regular for season 2.

In 2018, Carmack was cast as Dr. [Atticus "Link" Lincoln](/wiki/Atticus_Lincoln) on the ABC hit [medical drama](/wiki/Medical_drama) *[Grey's Anatomy](/wiki/Grey%27s_Anatomy)*.

In 2019, Carmack made his [Hollywood Bowl](/wiki/Hollywood_Bowl) debut playing the role of [Rapunzel's Prince](/wiki/Prince_Charming) in the show *[Into the Woods](/wiki/Into_the_Woods)*.

## Personal life

Carmack announced his engagement to Erin Slaver on March 2, 2016. They married on October 20, 2018. Their first daughter was born in August 2016, and a second daughter was born in May 2022.

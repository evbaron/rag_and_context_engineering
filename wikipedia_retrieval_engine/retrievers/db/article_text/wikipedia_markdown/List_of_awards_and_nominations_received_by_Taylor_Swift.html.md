The American singer-songwriter [Taylor Swift](/wiki/Taylor_Swift) has received numerous industry awards and honorary accolades. She is the most-awarded artist of the [American Music Awards](/wiki/American_Music_Awards) (40), the [*Billboard* Music Awards](/wiki/Billboard_Music_Awards) (49), the [MTV Video Music Awards](/wiki/MTV_Video_Music_Awards) (30), and the [iHeartRadio Music Awards](/wiki/IHeartRadio_Music_Awards) (41). She has the most [Album of the Year](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Album_of_the_Year) wins at the [Grammy Awards](/wiki/Grammy_Awards) (4) and the most [Video of the Year](/wiki/MTV_Video_Music_Award_for_Video_of_the_Year) wins at the MTV Video Music Awards (5), and she has been recognized as the [Global Recording Artist of the Year](/wiki/Global_Recording_Artist_of_the_Year) by the [International Federation of the Phonographic Industry](/wiki/International_Federation_of_the_Phonographic_Industry) more times than any other artist (6).

Swift began her career in [country music](/wiki/Country_music). She was nominated for [Best New Artist](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_New_Artist) at the [50th Annual Grammy Awards](/wiki/50th_Annual_Grammy_Awards) in 2008. Her second studio album, *[Fearless](/wiki/Fearless_(Taylor_Swift_album))* (2008), won Album of the Year at the [Country Music Association Awards](/wiki/Country_Music_Association_Awards), [Academy of Country Music Awards](/wiki/Academy_of_Country_Music_Awards), and Grammy Awards; it further won the [Grammy Award for Best Country Album](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Country_Album). Her singles "[White Horse](/wiki/White_Horse_(Taylor_Swift_song))" (2008) and "[Mean](/wiki/Mean_(song))" (2010) both won the [Grammy Award for Best Country Song](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Country_Song); the former also won [Best Female Country Vocal Performance](/wiki/Best_Female_Country_Vocal_Performance), and the latter [Best Country Solo Performance](/wiki/Best_Country_Solo_Performance). Her soundtrack single "[Safe & Sound](/wiki/Safe_%26_Sound_(Taylor_Swift_song))" for [*The Hunger Games*](/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Songs_from_District_12_and_Beyond) (2011) won the [Grammy Award for Best Song Written for Visual Media](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Song_Written_for_Visual_Media).

Swift transitioned from country to [pop](/wiki/Pop_music) stardom with her fifth studio album, *[1989](/wiki/1989_(album))* (2014), which won Album of the Year and [Best Pop Vocal Album](/wiki/Best_Pop_Vocal_Album) at the [58th Annual Grammy Awards](/wiki/58th_Annual_Grammy_Awards) in 2016, where the music video for its single "[Bad Blood](/wiki/Bad_Blood_(Taylor_Swift_song))" won [Best Music Video](/wiki/Best_Music_Video). At the 2016 [BMI Pop Awards](/wiki/BMI_Awards), Swift was honored with the Taylor Swift Award, becoming the second artist after [Michael Jackson](/wiki/Michael_Jackson) to have an award named after its recipient. In 2019, Swift was honored as the Woman of the Decade by [*Billboard*](/wiki/Billboard_(magazine)) and the Artist of the Decade at the [American Music Awards](/wiki/American_Music_Awards_of_2019). Her eighth studio album, *[Folklore](/wiki/Folklore_(Taylor_Swift_album))* (2020), won Album of the Year at the [63rd Annual Grammy Awards](/wiki/63rd_Annual_Grammy_Awards) in 2021. She became the first woman honored with the [Global Icon Award](/wiki/Brit_Award_for_Outstanding_Contribution_to_Music) at the [Brit Awards](/wiki/Brit_Awards), in [2021](/wiki/Brit_Awards_2021).

In 2023, Swift became the first entertainer to be honored as [*Time*'s Person of the Year](/wiki/Time_Person_of_the_Year). She achieved her fourth Grammy Award for Album of the Year with her tenth studio album, *[Midnights](/wiki/Midnights)* (2022), which also won Best Pop Vocal Album, at the [66th Annual Grammy Awards](/wiki/66th_Annual_Grammy_Awards) in 2024. The Grammy nomination for [Song of the Year](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Song_of_the_Year) of "[Anti-Hero](/wiki/Anti-Hero_(song))" made her the first songwriter to have seven nominations in this category. Swift's eleventh album, *[The Tortured Poets Department](/wiki/The_Tortured_Poets_Department)* (2024), made her the first woman to earn seven Album of the Year nominations at the Grammys. In January 2025, she was named the Top Artist of the 21st Century by *Billboard*. In 2026, she was inducted into the [Songwriters Hall of Fame](/wiki/Songwriters_Hall_of_Fame).

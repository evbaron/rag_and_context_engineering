# Wilhelm Zander

- **Born:** 22 April 1911 Saarbrücken, Kingdom of Bavaria, German Empire (now Saarland, Germany )
- **Died:** 27 September 1974 (1974-09-27) (aged 63) Munich, Bavaria, West Germany

- **Service years:** 1933 to 1945

**Wilhelm Zander** (22 April 1911 – 27 September 1974) was an [adjutant](/wiki/Adjutant) to [Martin Bormann](/wiki/Martin_Bormann) during [World War II](/wiki/World_War_II). He died in Munich in 1974.

## Biography

He was born in [Saarbrücken](/wiki/Saarbr%C3%BCcken). Although he received only minimal education, Zander built up business interests in [Italy](/wiki/Italy). He abandoned these interests to take up a full-time post as a [Nazi Party](/wiki/Nazi_Party) worker. In 1933, he joined the *[Schutzstaffel](/wiki/Schutzstaffel)* (SS) and later achieved the rank of SS-*[Standartenführer](/wiki/Standartenf%C3%BChrer)*.

In early 1945, he accompanied [Martin Bormann](/wiki/Martin_Bormann) and German leader [Adolf Hitler](/wiki/Adolf_Hitler) to the *[Führerbunker](/wiki/F%C3%BChrerbunker)* in [Berlin](/wiki/Berlin). On 29 April 1945, during the [Battle of Berlin](/wiki/Battle_of_Berlin), Hitler dictated his [last will and political testament](/wiki/Last_will_and_testament_of_Adolf_Hitler). Hitler then ordered that three copies be made and hand-delivered to [Field Marshal](/wiki/Feldmarschall) [Ferdinand Schörner](/wiki/Ferdinand_Sch%C3%B6rner) in [Czechoslovakia](/wiki/Czechoslovakia), the [Brown House, Munich](/wiki/Brown_House,_Munich) and [Karl Dönitz](/wiki/Karl_D%C3%B6nitz) in [Schleswig-Holstein](/wiki/Schleswig-Holstein). Three messengers were assigned to take the will and political testament out of the besieged city of Berlin to ensure their presence for posterity. The couriers were Hitler's army adjutant, [Willy Johannmeyer](/wiki/Willy_Johannmeyer), deputy press attaché, [Heinz Lorenz](/wiki/Heinz_Lorenz) and Zander, respectively. The three men said their farewell to Hitler and were handed a white dossier with the testament by Bormann at approximately 4.00 am. Armed with automatic weapons and wearing helmets to break through Soviet lines, the men left the bunker around 8.00 am.

On 30 April 1945 as the Soviet [Red Army](/wiki/Red_Army) closed in on the bunker complex, Hitler [committed suicide](/wiki/Death_of_Adolf_Hitler). The three couriers continued their journey and made it through the Soviet Army encirclement of Berlin to the Elbe River.

## Post-war

Zander changed his identity, adopted the surname Paustin and worked as a gardener after the war in Europe ended. He hid his copy of the documents in a suitcase near Lake Tegernsee. Zander was arrested in the American occupation zone, and as a consequence, the copies of Hitler's will, testament, and wedding documents fell into the hands of the American and British forces. Thereafter, by January 1946, the texts of the documents were published in the American and British press.

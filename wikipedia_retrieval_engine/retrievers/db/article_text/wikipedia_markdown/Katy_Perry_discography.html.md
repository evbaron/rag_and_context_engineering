The American singer [Katy Perry](/wiki/Katy_Perry) has released seven [studio albums](/wiki/Studio_album), one [reissue](/wiki/Reissue), seven [extended plays](/wiki/Extended_play) (EP), 42 [singles](/wiki/Single_(music)) (including four as featured artist), and 13 [promotional singles](/wiki/Promotional_recording#Promotional_single). According to the [Recording Industry Association of America](/wiki/Recording_Industry_Association_of_America) (RIAA), Perry is the fourteenth [top digital singles artist](/wiki/List_of_highest-certified_music_artists_in_the_United_States#Top_50_certified_digital_singles_artists) in the United States, with 121.5 million digital singles and 19 million album units. As of July 2024, she has a total of seven RIAA [Diamond-certified](/wiki/List_of_highest-certified_digital_singles_in_the_United_States) records. *[Billboard](/wiki/Billboard_(magazine))* listed Perry as the fifth top female artist of the [21st century](/wiki/21st_century) (15th overall) and the 61st greatest artist of all time. She has scored three number one albums on US [*Billboard* 200](/wiki/Billboard_200) and nine number one songs on US [*Billboard* Hot 100](/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100). With over 151 million [pure sales](/wiki/Record_sales) worldwide, Perry is one of the [best-selling music artists](/wiki/List_of_best-selling_music_artists) of all time. Her discography has garnered more than 125 billion cumulative streams from various digital and streaming platforms as of November 2025.

At age 16, Perry released a [self-titled gospel album](/wiki/Katy_Hudson_(album)) in March 2001 under her real name Katy Hudson, which failed to chart in any music market. After signing with [Capitol Records](/wiki/Capitol_Records) in April 2007, she released her second album, *[One of the Boys](/wiki/One_of_the_Boys)*, in June 2008. Its singles "[I Kissed a Girl](/wiki/I_Kissed_a_Girl)" and "Hot n Cold" both topped charts in Austria, Canada, Germany, and Switzerland. The album peaked within the top 10 in the US, Austria, Canada, France, Germany, and Switzerland, and has sold more than seven million copies worldwide. Throughout 2009 and 2010, Perry was featured on two singles. The first was on [Colorado](/wiki/Colorado)-based band [3OH!3](/wiki/3OH!3)'s song "[Starstrukk](/wiki/Starstrukk)", and the second was a collaboration with [Timbaland](/wiki/Timbaland) on "[If We Ever Meet Again](/wiki/If_We_Ever_Meet_Again)", from his album *[Shock Value II](/wiki/Shock_Value_II)*. Both singles reached the top 10 in Australia, Ireland, and the United Kingdom, while the latter topped the charts in New Zealand. Perry also performed for *[MTV Unplugged](/wiki/MTV_Unplugged)* and a [live album of the performance](/wiki/MTV_Unplugged_(Katy_Perry_album)) was released in November 2009.

Perry's third studio album *[Teenage Dream](/wiki/Teenage_Dream)* was released in August 2010 and topped the charts in the US, Australia, Austria, Canada, New Zealand, and the UK. It has sold more than 12 million copies worldwide, becoming Perry's highest-selling album to date. When its singles "California Gurls", "[Teenage Dream](/wiki/Teenage_Dream_(Katy_Perry_song))", "Firework", "E.T.", and "[Last Friday Night (T.G.I.F.)](/wiki/Last_Friday_Night_(T.G.I.F.))" topped the Hot 100, she became the first woman and only the second artist after [Michael Jackson](/wiki/Michael_Jackson) to attain five number-one singles in the US from one album. The album was re-released in March 2012 as *[Teenage Dream: The Complete Confection](/wiki/Teenage_Dream:_The_Complete_Confection)*. Both of its singles, "[Part of Me](/wiki/Part_of_Me_(Katy_Perry_song))" and "[Wide Awake](/wiki/Wide_Awake_(Katy_Perry_song))", charted at number one in Canada and New Zealand. Her fourth studio album *[Prism](/wiki/Prism_(Katy_Perry_album))* was released in October 2013 and reached number one in Australia, Canada, New Zealand, the UK, and the US. It has sold more than eight million copies worldwide. The album's lead single, "[Roar](/wiki/Roar_(song))", topped the charts in the US, Australia, Austria, Canada, New Zealand, and the UK. The song has become the [fourth most-certified single of all time in Australia](/wiki/List_of_highest-certified_singles_in_Australia) and Perry's most-certified single in the US and the UK. "[Dark Horse](/wiki/Dark_Horse_(Katy_Perry_song))", the third single from *Prism*, peaked at number one in the US and Canada and became the second best-selling single worldwide in 2014, according to the [International Federation of the Phonographic Industry](/wiki/International_Federation_of_the_Phonographic_Industry) (IFPI). She subsequently released an anthem for the [2016 Summer Olympics](/wiki/2016_Summer_Olympics) titled "[Rise](/wiki/Rise_(Katy_Perry_song))", which debuted at number one in Australia.

Perry's fifth album *[Witness](/wiki/Witness_(Katy_Perry_album))* followed in June 2017. It topped the charts in the US and Canada. That year, she was also featured on [Calvin Harris](/wiki/Calvin_Harris)'s song "[Feels](/wiki/Feels_(song))" from his album *[Funk Wav Bounces Vol. 1](/wiki/Funk_Wav_Bounces_Vol._1)* along with [Big Sean](/wiki/Big_Sean) and [Pharrell Williams](/wiki/Pharrell_Williams), which reached number one in the UK. In 2018, she released the [Christmas](/wiki/Christmas_music) single "[Cozy Little Christmas](/wiki/Cozy_Little_Christmas)". This was followed by the release of the collaborations "[365](/wiki/365_(Zedd_and_Katy_Perry_song))" (with [Zedd](/wiki/Zedd)) and "[Con Calma](/wiki/Con_Calma#Remix_version)" (remix, with [Daddy Yankee](/wiki/Daddy_Yankee) and [Snow](/wiki/Snow_(singer))). Perry released the non-album singles "[Never Really Over](/wiki/Never_Really_Over)", "[Small Talk](/wiki/Small_Talk_(song))" and "[Harleys in Hawaii](/wiki/Harleys_in_Hawaii)" in 2019. *[Smile](/wiki/Smile_(Katy_Perry_album))* and *[143](/wiki/143_(Katy_Perry_album))* respectively followed as her sixth and seventh albums in August 2020 and September 2024. Both reached the top 10 of the album charts in the US, Australia, Austria, Italy, New Zealand, and the UK.

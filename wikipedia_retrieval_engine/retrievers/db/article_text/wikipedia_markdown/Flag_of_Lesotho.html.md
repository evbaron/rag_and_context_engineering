# Flag of Lesotho

## Infobox

- **Proportion:** 2:3
- **Adopted:** 4 October 2006; 19 years ago ( 2006-10-04 )
- **Design:** A horizontal triband of blue, white and green in proportions 3:4:3; [ 1 ] charged with a black mokorotlo (a Basotho hat) centred on the white band.
- **Use:** Royal Standard

A [mokorotlo](/wiki/Mokorotlo) is a type of straw hat widely used for traditional Sotho clothing

Lesotho flag at the [Sani Pass](/wiki/Sani_Pass) border post

The mokorotlo hat in black, which appears on the flag of Lesotho

The **flag of Lesotho**, adopted on the 40th anniversary of [Lesotho](/wiki/Lesotho)'s independence on 4 October 2006, features a horizontal [blue](/wiki/Blue), [white](/wiki/White), and [green](/wiki/Green) [tricolour](/wiki/Tricolour_(flag)) with a black [mokorotlo](/wiki/Mokorotlo) (a [Basotho](/wiki/Basotho) hat) in the center. The design is intended to reflect a state that is both at peace internally and with its only neighbour [South Africa](/wiki/South_Africa), replacing the [old flag design](#1987–2006) that featured a military emblem of a shield, spear and [knobkerrie](/wiki/Knobkerrie).

## Colours

The symbolism of the colours is as such:

| Colour | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Blue | Represents the sky or [rain](/wiki/Rain). |
| White | Represents [peace](/wiki/Peace). Lesotho has had the [ideology](/wiki/Ideology) of peace since the ages of King [Moshoeshoe I](/wiki/Moshoeshoe_I). |
| Green | Represents prosperity. |

## History

### 1966–1987

The first flag of Lesotho was introduced on 4 October 1966, the day of Lesotho's full independence from the [United Kingdom](/wiki/United_Kingdom). It featured a prominent white *[mokorotlo](/wiki/Mokorotlo)*. The blue stood for [sky](/wiki/Sky) and [rain](/wiki/Rain), the white for [peace](/wiki/Peace), the green for [land](/wiki/Landform), and the red for [faith](/wiki/Faith).

- National flag from 1966–1987
- Royal standard from 1966–1987

### 1987–2006

A new flag, designed by Sergeant Retšelisitsoe Matete, was adopted on 20 January 1987, following a military [coup](/wiki/Coup) which ousted the [Basotho National Party](/wiki/Basotho_National_Party) after 20 years in power. A light brown traditional Basotho [shield](/wiki/Shield) along with an [assegai](/wiki/Assegai) (lance) and [knobkierrie](/wiki/Knobkierrie) (club) replaced the mokorotlo as the primary emblem. The colour scheme and pattern changed as well, with a triangular white field standing for peace. The bottom diagonal contained a blue strip for rain and a green triangle for prosperity.

- National flag from 1987–2006
- Royal standard from 1987–2006

### 2006–present

In 2006, a new flag was chosen from four proposed designs; all of these designs included a brown Basotho hat instead of the shield. This was subsequently changed to a black Basotho hat in order to represent Lesotho as a [black](/wiki/Black_people) nation. The bill changing the flag was approved by the [National Assembly](/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Lesotho) on 18 September 2006, with 84 members of parliament voting in favour of it, 18 against it, and two abstaining. It was subsequently approved by the [Senate](/wiki/Senate_of_Lesotho) as well.

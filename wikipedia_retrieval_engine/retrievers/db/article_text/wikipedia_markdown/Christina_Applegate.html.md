**Christina Applegate** (born November 25, 1971) is an American actress. She gained recognition in the late 1980s for playing Kelly Bundy in the [Fox](/wiki/Fox_Broadcasting_Company) sitcom *[Married... with Children](/wiki/Married..._with_Children)* (1987–1997). Her titular role in the sitcom *[Jesse](/wiki/Jesse_(TV_series))* (1998–2000), earned her first nomination for the [Golden Globe Award](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award) for [Best Actress – Television Series Musical or Comedy](/wiki/Best_Actress_%E2%80%93_Television_Series_Musical_or_Comedy). For her guest role in the [NBC](/wiki/NBC) sitcom *[Friends](/wiki/Friends)* (2002–2003), she won the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Guest Actress in a Comedy Series](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Guest_Actress_in_a_Comedy_Series). She received additional Emmy and Golden Globe nominations for her roles in the television sitcom *[Samantha Who?](/wiki/Samantha_Who%3F)* (2007–2009), starred in a short-lived sitcom *[Up All Night](/wiki/Up_All_Night_(TV_series))* (2011–2012), and the dark tragicomedy series *[Dead to Me](/wiki/Dead_to_Me_(TV_series))* (2019–2022). Applegate served as a producer of all three aforementioned projects.

Applegate has achieved success in films of varying genres, particularly in [comedies](/wiki/Comedy_film) such as *[Don't Tell Mom the Babysitter's Dead](/wiki/Don%27t_Tell_Mom_the_Babysitter%27s_Dead)* (1991), *[The Big Hit](/wiki/The_Big_Hit)* (1998), *[The Sweetest Thing](/wiki/The_Sweetest_Thing_(film))* (2002), *[Anchorman: The Legend of Ron Burgundy](/wiki/Anchorman:_The_Legend_of_Ron_Burgundy)* (2004) and *[Anchorman 2: The Legend Continues](/wiki/Anchorman_2:_The_Legend_Continues)* (2013), *[Hall Pass](/wiki/Hall_Pass)* (2011), *[Vacation](/wiki/Vacation_(2015_film))* (2015), *[Bad Moms](/wiki/Bad_Moms)* (2016), and *[Crash Pad](/wiki/Crash_Pad)* (2017). On stage, Applegate received a nomination for the [Tony Award for Best Actress in a Musical](/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Musical) for her debut performance as the [title character](/wiki/Title_character) in the [Broadway](/wiki/Broadway_theatre) musical revival *[Sweet Charity](/wiki/Sweet_Charity)* (2005).

After being diagnosed with [multiple sclerosis](/wiki/Multiple_sclerosis) in August 2021, Applegate announced she would step away from appearing on-screen, but would continue doing voice work.

She published a memoir in 2026, an instant [*New York Times* Best Seller](/wiki/The_New_York_Times_Best_Seller_list), where she focused on resilience and self-acceptance.

## Early and family life

Applegate was born on November 25, 1971, in [Hollywood, Los Angeles](/wiki/Hollywood,_Los_Angeles), California. Her father, Robert William "Bobby" Applegate (1942–2025), was a staff producer at [Dot Records](/wiki/Dot_Records); her mother, [Nancy Priddy](/wiki/Nancy_Priddy), is a singer and actress. She was named after the [Andrew Wyeth](/wiki/Andrew_Wyeth) painting *[Christina's World](/wiki/Christina%27s_World)*, for which her mother also named a song on her album *[You've Come This Way Before](/wiki/You%27ve_Come_This_Way_Before)*. Her parents separated shortly after her birth and Christina was raised by her mother. She has two half-siblings, Alisa and Kyle, from her father's second marriage. As a child, Applegate trained as a dancer in various styles, including [jazz](/wiki/Jazz_dance) and [ballet](/wiki/Ballet).

## Career

### 1972–1986: Early projects

Newspaper clipping, November 2, 1986

Applegate made her television debut in 1972 alongside her mother in the soap opera *[Days of Our Lives](/wiki/Days_of_Our_Lives)* and starred in a commercial for [Playtex](/wiki/Playtex) baby bottles at 3 months old. She made her film debut in the 1981 horror film *[Jaws of Satan](/wiki/Jaws_of_Satan)* (or *King Cobra*) and appeared in the 1981 movie *Beatlemania*. She debuted as a young [Grace Kelly](/wiki/Grace_Kelly) in the television biopic *[Grace Kelly](/wiki/Grace_Kelly_(film))* (1983) and appeared in her first television series in [Showtime](/wiki/Showtime_(network))'s political comedy *Washingtoon* (1985), in which she played a congressman's daughter.

Applegate was a guest in the series *[Father Murphy](/wiki/Father_Murphy)* (1981), *[Charles in Charge](/wiki/Charles_in_Charge)* (1984–1985), and *[Silver Spoons](/wiki/Silver_Spoons)* (1986). In 1986, she won the role of Robin Kennedy, a policeman's daughter, in the police drama series *[Heart of the City](/wiki/Heart_of_the_City_(TV_series))* (1986–1987). For her performance, she received a [Young Artist Award](/wiki/Young_Artist_Award). She guest-starred in several television series, including *All Is Forgiven*, *[Still the Beaver](/wiki/Still_the_Beaver)*, *[Amazing Stories](/wiki/Amazing_Stories_(1985_TV_series))*, and *[Family Ties](/wiki/Family_Ties)*.

### 1987–2001: Breakthrough and *Married... with Children*

Applegate at the Governor's Ball following the [41st Primetime Emmy Awards](/wiki/41st_Primetime_Emmy_Awards), September 1989

From 1987 to 1997, Applegate played the ditzy, sexually [promiscuous](/wiki/Promiscuous) daughter, [Kelly Bundy](/wiki/Kelly_Bundy), on [Fox](/wiki/Fox_Broadcasting_Company)'s first sitcom, *[Married... with Children](/wiki/Married..._with_Children)*. While working on the series, Applegate was seen in *[Dance 'til Dawn](/wiki/Dance_%27til_Dawn)* (1988) and *[Streets](/wiki/Streets_(film))* (1990), in which a teenage drug addict is stalked by a psychotic police officer. She guest-starred in *[21 Jump Street](/wiki/21_Jump_Street)* (1988) and *Top of the Heap* (1991) and hosted *[Saturday Night Live](/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live)* (1993) and *[MADtv](/wiki/MADtv)* (1996). The character of Sue Ellen Crandell in the black comedy feature *[Don't Tell Mom the Babysitter's Dead](/wiki/Don%27t_Tell_Mom_the_Babysitter%27s_Dead)* (1991) was Applegate's first starring role in a mainstream film, playing a rebellious teenager who is forced to take care of siblings after their summer babysitter dies. She had roles in films such as *[Vibrations](/wiki/Vibrations_(film))* (1995), *[Across the Moon](/wiki/Across_the_Moon)* (1995), *[Wild Bill](/wiki/Wild_Bill_(1995_film))* (1995), [Tim Burton](/wiki/Tim_Burton)'s *[Mars Attacks!](/wiki/Mars_Attacks!)* (1996), and [Gregg Araki](/wiki/Gregg_Araki)'s *[Nowhere](/wiki/Nowhere_(1997_film))* (1997). Contrary to reports, she did not audition for Titanic, as she told Vanity Fair in their May 2023 edition: "No. Who said that?…That would not have come across my desk, if I had a desk." [Shaw, Vanity Fair, May 4, 2023.] When *Married... with Children* was cancelled in 1997, producers pitched a spinoff centered on Kelly Bundy, but Applegate declined.

In 1998, Applegate starred as Claudine Van Doozen in the independent feature *[Claudine's Return](/wiki/Claudine%27s_Return)* (or *Kiss of Fire*), appeared in the action-comedy *[The Big Hit](/wiki/The_Big_Hit)* and played the fiancée of a mob boss in the Mafia satire *[Jane Austen's Mafia](/wiki/Jane_Austen%27s_Mafia)*.

Applegate was one of the founding members of [The Pussycat Dolls](/wiki/The_Pussycat_Dolls) that debuted at [Johnny Depp](/wiki/Johnny_Depp)'s [Viper Room](/wiki/Viper_Room) on the [Sunset Strip](/wiki/Sunset_Strip) in 1995. She emceed for the group when they moved to [The Roxy Theatre](/wiki/The_Roxy_Theatre) in 2002.

### 2002–2009: *Anchorman*, stage, and television

Applegate in 2004

Applegate played the dual role of a 12th-century noblewoman, Princess Rosalind, and her 21st-century descendant, Julia Malfete, in the time-travel comedy *[Just Visiting](/wiki/Just_Visiting_(film))* (2001). She was Princess Gwendolyn and Kate in the movie *Prince Charming* (2001). After playing [Cameron Diaz](/wiki/Cameron_Diaz)'s level-headed best friend, Courtney Rockcliffe, in *[The Sweetest Thing](/wiki/The_Sweetest_Thing_(film))* (2002), she appeared in *Heroes* (2002), the romantic airplane comedy *[View from the Top](/wiki/View_from_the_Top)* (2003), the true-crime film *[Wonderland](/wiki/Wonderland_(2003_film))* (2003) based on the [Wonderland murders](/wiki/Wonderland_murders), and the [Gram Parsons](/wiki/Gram_Parsons) biopic *[Grand Theft Parsons](/wiki/Grand_Theft_Parsons)* (2003). In 2004, she starred with [Ben Affleck](/wiki/Ben_Affleck) in the holiday comedy *[Surviving Christmas](/wiki/Surviving_Christmas)* and with [Matt Dillon](/wiki/Matt_Dillon) in *[Employee of the Month](/wiki/Employee_of_the_Month_(2004_film))*. She was the executive producer of *Comforters, Miserable* (2001).

Applegate guest-starred on *[Friends](/wiki/Friends)* in the ninth (2002) and tenth (2003) seasons in episodes titled "[The One with Rachel's Other Sister](/wiki/The_One_with_Rachel%27s_Other_Sister)" and "[The One Where Rachel's Sister Babysits](/wiki/The_One_Where_Rachel%27s_Sister_Babysits)" as [Amy Green](/wiki/Amy_Green), [Rachel Green](/wiki/Rachel_Green)'s ([Jennifer Aniston](/wiki/Jennifer_Aniston)) sister. She won the [Primetime Emmy Award](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award) from two nominations for [Outstanding Guest Actress in a Comedy Series](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Guest_Actress_in_a_Comedy_Series) for her performance in "The One with Rachel's Other Sister."

Applegate at the [66th Golden Globe Awards](/wiki/66th_Golden_Globe_Awards) in January 2009

Applegate received recognition for her portrayal of anchorwoman Veronica Corningstone in the 2004 comedy films *[Anchorman: The Legend of Ron Burgundy](/wiki/Anchorman:_The_Legend_of_Ron_Burgundy)* and *[Wake Up, Ron Burgundy: The Lost Movie](/wiki/Wake_Up,_Ron_Burgundy:_The_Lost_Movie)*, an alternative film comprising alternate takes and deleted scenes and story elements.

Applegate has performed on stage in *The Axeman's Jazz*, *Nobody Leaves Empty Handed*, *The Runthrough*, and [John Cassavetes](/wiki/John_Cassavetes)' *The Third Day* (co-starring [Gena Rowlands](/wiki/Gena_Rowlands)). In 2004, she debuted on Broadway as Charity Hope Valentine in a revival of the 1966 musical *[Sweet Charity](/wiki/Sweet_Charity)*. In late April 2005, she took part in the annual Broadway Cares' Easter Bonnet Competition, being sawed in half by a magician in their Clearly Impossible sawing illusion. *Sweet Charity* ended its Broadway run on December 31, 2005. Applegate won the 2005 Theatre World Award and was nominated for a 2005 [Tony Award for Best Actress in a Musical](/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Musical) for her role in the musical.

While appearing in *Sweet Charity*, Applegate broke her foot and it was announced that the musical would close during previews. She persuaded the producers to change their minds. Because of her injury, she had to wear special shoes to prevent another accident. In a 2013 interview, she said that because of what happened, she "actually can't dance anymore. And that is sad for me because I always wanted to go back. But I probably won't be able to." She does dance whenever the opportunity presents, but cannot perform in strenuous roles.

In 2006, Applegate appeared in [Jessica Simpson](/wiki/Jessica_Simpson)'s music video for "[A Public Affair](/wiki/A_Public_Affair_(song))" with [Eva Longoria](/wiki/Eva_Longoria), [Ryan Seacrest](/wiki/Ryan_Seacrest), and [Christina Milian](/wiki/Christina_Milian). She starred in the ABC comedy *[Samantha Who?](/wiki/Samantha_Who%3F)* from October 15, 2007, until it was cancelled on May 18, 2009; the finale aired on July 23, 2009. The series co-starred [Jean Smart](/wiki/Jean_Smart), [Jennifer Esposito](/wiki/Jennifer_Esposito), and [Melissa McCarthy](/wiki/Melissa_McCarthy) and focused on a 30-year-old who, after a hit-and-run accident, develops [amnesia](/wiki/Amnesia) and has to rediscover her life, her relationships, and herself. She received two nominations for the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Comedy Series](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actress_in_a_Comedy_Series) and two nominations for the [Golden Globe Award for Best Actress – Television Series Musical or Comedy](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actress_%E2%80%93_Television_Series_Musical_or_Comedy). Shortly after the cancellation was announced, she began a campaign to get the show back into production, which failed. She topped the list of [*People*](/wiki/People_Magazine)'s Most Beautiful People in 2009. She appeared with her *[Married... with Children](/wiki/Married..._with_Children)* co-star [David Faustino](/wiki/David_Faustino) in an episode of Faustino's comedy series *[Star-ving](/wiki/Star-ving)*.

Applegate in June 2012

### 2010–present: *Dead to Me* and further acclaim

Applegate voiced Catherine the Cat in the three-dimensional talking animal sequel *[Cats & Dogs: The Revenge of Kitty Galore](/wiki/Cats_%26_Dogs:_The_Revenge_of_Kitty_Galore)* (2010). She said her mother wanted her to be involved in the film. Prior to *Cats & Dogs 2*, she voiced Brittany, one of the [Chipettes](/wiki/Chipettes), in *[Alvin and the Chipmunks: The Squeakquel](/wiki/Alvin_and_the_Chipmunks:_The_Squeakquel)* (2009), *[Alvin and the Chipmunks: Chipwrecked](/wiki/Alvin_and_the_Chipmunks:_Chipwrecked)* (2011), and *[Alvin and the Chipmunks: The Road Chip](/wiki/Alvin_and_the_Chipmunks:_The_Road_Chip)* (2015).

In 2011, Applegate starred in the [Farrelly brothers](/wiki/Farrelly_brothers) comedy *[Hall Pass](/wiki/Hall_Pass)*. Applegate also starred in the NBC [sitcom](/wiki/Sitcom) *[Up All Night](/wiki/Up_All_Night_(TV_series))* with [Maya Rudolph](/wiki/Maya_Rudolph) and [Will Arnett](/wiki/Will_Arnett), which debuted on September 14, 2011. On February 8, 2013, she left the series after its second-season hiatus, which was leading into a planned format change. The series was ultimately cancelled.

On July 31, 2013, Applegate was featured on the second episode of the fourth season of the revived American version of the [TLC](/wiki/TLC_(TV_channel)) series *[Who Do You Think You Are?](/wiki/Who_Do_You_Think_You_Are%3F_(American_TV_series))*. The episode centered on Applegate trying to find information about her paternal grandmother, Lavina Applegate Walton, who was absent for most of Applegate's father's life and died when he was young. Applegate learned that Walton died in 1955 from tuberculosis and alcohol-related [cirrhosis](/wiki/Cirrhosis).

In 2013, Applegate reprised her role as Veronica Corningstone in the comedy sequel film *[Anchorman 2: The Legend Continues](/wiki/Anchorman_2:_The_Legend_Continues)*. The film received positive reviews from critics and was a box office success. In 2014, she had a starring voice role as Mary Beth in the animated musical fantasy film *[The Book of Life](/wiki/The_Book_of_Life_(2014_film))*. In 2015, she starred with [Ed Helms](/wiki/Ed_Helms) in the *National Lampoon* sequel *[Vacation](/wiki/Vacation_(2015_film))*, the fifth full-length movie episode of the road-trip comedies. They played Rusty Griswold and his wife, Debbie, who with their two sons take a trip to Walley World, just as Rusty did with his parents in the 1983 original film, *[National Lampoon's Vacation](/wiki/National_Lampoon%27s_Vacation)*. The film was poorly reviewed, but was a box office success. In 2016, she starred as Gwendolyn James in the comedy film *[Bad Moms](/wiki/Bad_Moms)* with [Mila Kunis](/wiki/Mila_Kunis), [Kristen Bell](/wiki/Kristen_Bell), and [Kathryn Hahn](/wiki/Kathryn_Hahn). The film earned mixed reviews from critics and was a box office success.

In July 2018, Applegate co-starred with [Linda Cardellini](/wiki/Linda_Cardellini) in the [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) dark comedy series *[Dead to Me](/wiki/Dead_to_Me_(TV_series))* and executive produced the series with [Will Ferrell](/wiki/Will_Ferrell), [Adam McKay](/wiki/Adam_McKay), and [Jessica Elbaum](/wiki/Jessica_Elbaum). The series received critical acclaim. Applegate received two nominations for the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Comedy Series](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actress_in_a_Comedy_Series) for her performance in seasons one and two.

On November 14, 2022, she received a star on [Hollywood Walk of Fame](/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame). She was accompanied by her *Married... with Children* co-stars [Katey Sagal](/wiki/Katey_Sagal) and [David Faustino](/wiki/David_Faustino). Her star was placed adjacent to Sagal and [Ed O'Neill](/wiki/Ed_O%27Neill)'s stars.

In 2024, Applegate began a [podcast](/wiki/Podcast) with fellow actress with multiple sclerosis [Jamie-Lynn Sigler](/wiki/Jamie-Lynn_Sigler) called *MeSsy* about their friendship based on the condition. In January 2026, Sigler credited Applegate with inspiring her to be more public about her own MS condition.

Applegate released her memoir, *You with the Sad Eyes*, in March 2026, topping [*The New York Times* Best Seller](/wiki/The_New_York_Times_Best_Sellers_List) list in its first week of sales.

## Personal life

Applegate attending the *[Up All Night](/wiki/Up_All_Night_(TV_series))* cast panel at [PaleyFest](/wiki/Paley_Center_for_Media) in 2012

Applegate married actor [Johnathon Schaech](/wiki/Johnathon_Schaech) in [Palm Springs](/wiki/Palm_Springs,_California), on October 20, 2001. Schaech filed for divorce in December 2005, citing irreconcilable differences, and the divorce was finalized in August 2007. She began dating Dutch musician [Martyn LeNoble](/wiki/Martyn_LeNoble) in 2009. The couple became engaged on [Valentine's Day](/wiki/Valentine%27s_Day) 2010 and married on February 23, 2013, at their Los Angeles home. It was the second marriage for both. They have one daughter, Sadie, born in January 2011.

### Health

*People* reported on August 3, 2008 that Applegate had been diagnosed with [breast cancer](/wiki/Breast_cancer). A representative stated, "Christina Applegate was diagnosed with an early stage of breast cancer. Detected early through doctor-ordered [MRI](/wiki/Magnetic_resonance_imaging), the cancer was not life-threatening. Christina is following the recommended treatment of her doctors and will have a full recovery." It was announced that she was cancer-free after a double [mastectomy](/wiki/Mastectomy), although cancer had been found in only one breast. She inherited a genetic trait, *[BRCA1](/wiki/BRCA1)*, which can trigger breast and [ovarian cancer](/wiki/Ovarian_cancer). Her mother is also a breast cancer survivor. Applegate said when she was diagnosed, "I was just shaking and then also immediately, I had to go into 'take care of business mode'."

Applegate announced on August 10, 2021 that she had received a diagnosis of [multiple sclerosis](/wiki/Multiple_sclerosis) a few months before. The disease significantly affected her ability to perform in the final season of *Dead to Me*, for whose success she credits her co-star [Linda Cardellini](/wiki/Linda_Cardellini) and showrunner [Liz Feldman](/wiki/Liz_Feldman)'s support and openness in making [reasonable adjustments](/wiki/Reasonable_adjustments) to her filming schedule.

Applegate said in February 2023 that, because of her illness, she would probably no longer act on camera, but would be open to [voice-over](/wiki/Voice-over) work, such as a planned animated revival of *Married... with Children*, as well as working behind the scenes.

During an appearance on *[GMA](/wiki/Good_Morning_America)* in March 2024, Applegate acknowledged now having "a [tremor](/wiki/Tremor)" from her MS condition.

In March 2026, *[The New York Times](/wiki/The_New_York_Times)* reported that Applegate had retired. On an episode of *Wild Card with Rachel Martin* the same month, Applegate informed [NPR](/wiki/NPR) journalist [Rachel Martin](/wiki/Rachel_Martin) that her death was "looming" and that she had bought a burial plot.

## Philanthropy

Applegate has supported [EIF](/wiki/Entertainment_Industry_Foundation), [Adopt-A-Classroom](/wiki/Adopt-A-Classroom), [MPTF](/wiki/Motion_Picture_%26_Television_Fund), [WSPA](/wiki/World_Animal_Protection), and [the Trevor Project](/wiki/The_Trevor_Project). In 2003, she was the spokesman for the [Lee National Denim Day](/wiki/Lee_National_Denim_Day), which raises millions of dollars for breast cancer education and [research](/wiki/Cancer_research). Following her breast cancer diagnosis, she appeared on a TV special, *[Stand Up to Cancer](/wiki/Stand_Up_to_Cancer)*, designed to raise funds for breast cancer research. The one-hour special was broadcast on CBS, NBC, and ABC on September 5, 2008.

In 2009, Applegate announced plans to return as the ambassador for Lee National Denim Day. Also in 2009, she founded Right Action for Women, a charitable foundation dedicated to breast-cancer screening for women and focused on the type of [MRI](/wiki/MRI) scan that saved her life. In February 2015, she was awarded the [Saint Vintage Love Cures Award](/wiki/Paige_Jansen-Nichols) at the 2nd annual [unite4:humanity](/wiki/Unite4:humanity) event hosted by [*Variety* magazine](/wiki/Variety_(magazine)) for her dedication to and work with Right Action for Women.

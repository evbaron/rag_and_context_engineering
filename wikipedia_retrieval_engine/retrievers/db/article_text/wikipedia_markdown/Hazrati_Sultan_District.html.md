# Hazrati Sultan District

## Infobox

- **Country:** Afghanistan
- **Province:** Samangan
- **Time zone:** + 4.30

**Hazrati Sultan District** is a [district](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Afghanistan) in [Samangan Province](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samangan_Province), [Afghanistan](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghanistan). It is also locally known as Azrat Sultan. In 2019 the estimated population was 45,962.

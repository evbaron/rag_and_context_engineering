**Unnecessary Mountain**, also called **Mount Unnecessary**, is a peak in the [North Shore Mountains](/wiki/North_Shore_Mountains) near [Vancouver](/wiki/Vancouver), [British Columbia](/wiki/British_Columbia), [Canada](/wiki/Canada) and a popular hiking destination. Its two summits are within [Cypress Provincial Park](/wiki/Cypress_Provincial_Park), accessible via the [Howe Sound Crest Trail](/wiki/Howe_Sound_Crest_Trail) and the Unnecessary Mountain Trail from [Lions Bay](/wiki/Lions_Bay,_British_Columbia). The taller south summit has an elevation of 1,548 m (5,079 ft) and its north summit is slightly lower at 1,543 m (5,062 ft).

The mountain is so named because of an "unnecessary" ascent over this mountain to reach [The Lions](/wiki/The_Lions_(peaks)).

In December 2014, a [rockslide](/wiki/Rockslide) killed 7-year-old Erin Kate Moore as she was hiking with a group on Unnecessary Mountain.

A second peak, also named Unnecessary Mountain, is located in [Alberta](/wiki/Alberta) northwest of the town of [Pincher Creek](/wiki/Pincher_Creek,_Alberta).

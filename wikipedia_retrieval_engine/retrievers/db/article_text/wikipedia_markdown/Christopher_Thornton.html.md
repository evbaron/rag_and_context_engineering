**Christopher Thornton** (born March 29, 1967) is an American actor. He is known for his role as Kenny "Shammy" Shamberg in *[Magnum P.I.](/wiki/Magnum_P.I._(2018_TV_series))*, and has had numerous other film and television roles from 1990 to the present.

## Early life and education

Thornton was born in [New Orleans](/wiki/New_Orleans), Louisiana. His father was an amateur opera singer who appeared in local theater. Christopher appeared in several plays while majoring in theater at the [University of New Orleans](/wiki/University_of_New_Orleans), but quit college after two years to relocate to Los Angeles and train at the [Stella Adler Conservatory](/wiki/Stella_Adler_Studio_of_Acting).

## Career

Thornton made his first feature film appearance in the 1990 horror sequel *[Watchers II](/wiki/Watchers_II)*, playing a young man who gets his eyes gouged out by the mutant antagonist. Only two years later, at age 25, Thornton suffered an accident while [rock climbing](/wiki/Rock_climbing) which fractured two [vertebrae](/wiki/Vertebra) and left him paralyzed from the waist down. He returned to mostly theater roles for the remainder of the 1990s. In 2000, he was cast to play [Hamlet](/wiki/Hamlet) at the Lillian Theater in Los Angeles. Believed to be the first actor to ever play the [iconic Shakespeare character](/wiki/Prince_Hamlet) in a wheelchair, Thornton's performance won rave reviews.

Thornton has gone on to appear in numerous film and television roles. He has recurred in TV series such as *[Any Day Now](/wiki/Any_Day_Now_(TV_series))*, *[Rules of Engagement](/wiki/Rules_of_Engagement_(TV_series))* and *[Vice Principals](/wiki/Vice_Principals)*, and had guest roles in *[Curb Your Enthusiasm](/wiki/Curb_Your_Enthusiasm)*, *[Alias](/wiki/Alias_(TV_series))*, *[Brothers & Sisters](/wiki/Brothers_%26_Sisters_(2006_TV_series))*, *[My Name Is Earl](/wiki/My_Name_Is_Earl)*, *[Grey's Anatomy](/wiki/Grey%27s_Anatomy)*, and *[Will & Grace](/wiki/Will_%26_Grace)*. From 2018 to 2024, he played recurring character Kenny "Shammy" Shamberg, a Marine veteran paralyzed during a tour of duty in Iraq, on the [CBS](/wiki/CBS) reboot of *[Magnum P.I.](/wiki/Magnum_P.I._(2018_TV_series))*

Thornton was both writer and lead actor for the 2010 film *[Sympathy for Delicious](/wiki/Sympathy_for_Delicious)*, playing [disc jockey](/wiki/Disc_jockey) "Delicious" Dean O'Dwyer. The film was directed by close friend [Mark Ruffalo](/wiki/Mark_Ruffalo), who also stars in it alongside [Orlando Bloom](/wiki/Orlando_Bloom), [Laura Linney](/wiki/Laura_Linney), and [Juliette Lewis](/wiki/Juliette_Lewis).

He will appear in the upcoming [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) limited series adaptation *[All the Sinners Bleed](/wiki/All_the_Sinners_Bleed_(TV_series))*, based on the [S. A. Cosby](/wiki/S._A._Cosby) novel of the [same name](/wiki/All_the_Sinners_Bleed).

# Academy Award for Best Actor

## Infobox

- **Awarded for:** Best Performance by an Actor in a Leading Role
- **Country:** United States
- **Presented by:** Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences (AMPAS)
- **First award:** May 16, 1929; 96 years ago ( 1929-05-16 ) (for films released during the 1927 / 1928 film season)
- **Most recent winner:** Michael B. Jordan, Sinners ( 2025 )
- **Most awards:** Daniel Day-Lewis (3)
- **Most nominations:** Laurence Olivier and Spencer Tracy (9)
- **Website:** oscars.org

The **Academy Award for Best Actor** is an award presented annually by the [Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences](/wiki/Academy_of_Motion_Picture_Arts_and_Sciences) (AMPAS). It has been awarded since the [1st Academy Awards](/wiki/1st_Academy_Awards) to an actor who has delivered an outstanding performance in a [leading role](/wiki/Leading_actor) in a film released that year. The award was traditionally presented by the previous year's [Best Actress](/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actress) winner. However, in recent years, it has shifted towards being presented by previous years' Best Actor winners instead.

The Best Actor award has been presented 98 times, to 87 actors. The first winner was [German actor](/wiki/List_of_German_Academy_Award_winners_and_nominees) [Emil Jannings](/wiki/Emil_Jannings) for his roles in *[The Last Command](/wiki/The_Last_Command_(1928_film))* (1928) and *[The Way of All Flesh](/wiki/The_Way_of_All_Flesh_(1927_film))* (1927). The most recent winner is [Michael B. Jordan](/wiki/Michael_B._Jordan) for his dual role as twin brothers Elijah and Elias Moore in *[Sinners](/wiki/Sinners_(2025_film))* (2025). The record for [most wins](/wiki/List_of_actors_with_two_or_more_Academy_Awards_in_acting_categories) is three, held by [Daniel Day-Lewis](/wiki/Daniel_Day-Lewis), and ten other actors have won twice. The record for [most nominations](/wiki/List_of_actors_with_three_or_more_Academy_Award_nominations_in_acting_categories) is nine, held jointly by [Spencer Tracy](/wiki/Spencer_Tracy) and [Laurence Olivier](/wiki/Laurence_Olivier). At the [5th Academy Awards](/wiki/5th_Academy_Awards) in 1932, [Fredric March](/wiki/Fredric_March) finished one vote ahead of [Wallace Beery](/wiki/Wallace_Beery); under the rules of the time this resulted in them sharing the award, the only time this has occurred.

## Nominations process

Nominees are currently determined by [single transferable vote](/wiki/Single_transferable_vote) within the actors branch of AMPAS; winners are selected by a [plurality vote](/wiki/Plurality_(voting)) from the entire [eligible voting members](/wiki/Film_industry) of the Academy.

In the first three years of the awards, actors and actresses were nominated as the best individuals in their categories. At that time, all of their work during the qualifying period (as many as three films, in some cases) was listed after the award. Despite this, at the [3rd Academy Awards](/wiki/3rd_Academy_Awards), held in 1930, only one film was cited in each winner's award regardless of how many they were eligible to be considered for during that span. The current system, in which an actor is nominated for a specific performance in a single film, was introduced for the [4th Academy Awards](/wiki/4th_Academy_Awards). Starting with the [9th Academy Awards](/wiki/9th_Academy_Awards), held in 1937, the category was limited to a maximum five nominations per year.

## Winners and nominees

In the following table, the years are listed as per Academy convention, and generally correspond to the year of film release in [Los Angeles County](/wiki/Los_Angeles_County); the ceremonies are always held the following year. For the first five ceremonies, the eligibility period spanned twelve months, from August 1 to July 31. For the 6th ceremony held in 1934, the eligibility period lasted from August 1, 1932, to December 31, 1933. Since the 7th ceremony held in 1935, the period of eligibility became the full previous calendar year from January 1 to December 31.

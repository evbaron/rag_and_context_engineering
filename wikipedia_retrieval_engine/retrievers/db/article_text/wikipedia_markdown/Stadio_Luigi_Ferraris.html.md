The **Stadio Luigi Ferraris**, also known as the **Marassi** from the name of the neighbourhood where it is located, is a multi-use stadium in [Genoa](/wiki/Genoa), Italy. The home of [Genoa CFC](/wiki/Genoa_CFC) and [UC Sampdoria](/wiki/UC_Sampdoria) football clubs, it opened in 1911 and is the oldest stadium still in use for football and other sports in Italy. Aside from football, the stadium has hosted meetings of rugby in the [Italian national rugby team](/wiki/Italy_national_rugby_union_team) and, more rarely, some concerts.

The stadium is named after [Luigi Ferraris](/wiki/Luigi_Ferraris_(footballer)) (1887–1915), an Italian footballer, engineer and soldier who died during [WWI](/wiki/WWI).

## Notable matches

The stadium was inaugurated on 22 January 1911 with a football match between Genoa and [Internazionale](/wiki/Internazionale), and had a capacity of 20,000. On 22 December 1912, it hosted its first international, in which [Italy](/wiki/Italy_national_football_team) lost 3–1 to [Austria](/wiki/Austria_national_football_team) in a friendly.

It also hosted the [1934 World Cup](/wiki/1934_FIFA_World_Cup) round-of-16 match between [Spain](/wiki/Spain_national_football_team) and [Brazil](/wiki/Brazil_national_football_team), and by then its capacity had been expanded to 30,000.

The stadium was dismantled and rebuilt before the [1990 FIFA World Cup](/wiki/1990_FIFA_World_Cup), for which it hosted three Group C matches (between [Costa Rica](/wiki/Costa_Rica_national_football_team), [Scotland](/wiki/Scotland_national_football_team) and [Sweden](/wiki/Sweden_men%27s_national_football_team)) and a round-of-16 match between the [Republic of Ireland](/wiki/Republic_of_Ireland_national_football_team) and [Romania](/wiki/Romania_national_football_team).

The highest attendance at the Luigi Ferraris was 60,000 on 27 February 1949, for a match between [Italy](/wiki/Italy_national_football_team) and [Portugal](/wiki/Portugal_national_football_team).

On 12 October 2010, a [Euro 2012](/wiki/Euro_2012) [qualifier](/wiki/UEFA_Euro_2012_qualifying_Group_C) between [Italy](/wiki/Italy_national_football_team) and [Serbia](/wiki/Serbia_national_football_team) was abandoned after Serbia fans continued to throw flares onto the pitch and light fireworks. When the game finally began, more flares and fireworks were thrown onto the field and the referee stopped the match after only six minutes of play.

On 29 February 2012, the [United States](/wiki/United_States_men%27s_national_soccer_team) defeated Italy 1–0 in a friendly played at the stadium. It was the first time in almost 100 years that Italy had been defeated in Genoa after 22 December 1912 defeat against Austria, and the first time that the US had ever defeated Italy.

On 14 November 2014, it hosted [Italy's](/wiki/Italy_national_rugby_union_team) end-of-year rugby union international against [Argentina](/wiki/Argentina_national_rugby_union_team) who won 20–18.

# 2007 NHL entry draft

## Infobox

- **Date:** June 22–23, 2007
- **Location:** Nationwide Arena Columbus, Ohio, U.S.
- **First selection:** Patrick Kane ( Chicago Blackhawks )

The **2007 NHL entry draft** was the 45th [draft](/wiki/NHL_entry_draft) for the [National Hockey League](/wiki/National_Hockey_League). It was held at [Nationwide Arena](/wiki/Nationwide_Arena) in [Columbus, Ohio](/wiki/Columbus,_Ohio), on June 22, 2007. The draft consisted of seven rounds, with rounds two through seven taking place on June 23. The draft was televised on [TSN](/wiki/The_Sports_Network) and [RDS](/wiki/R%C3%A9seau_des_sports), with the first round simulcasted in the United States on [Versus](/wiki/Versus_(TV_channel)) and in Europe on [NASN](/wiki/North_American_Sports_Network).

[Columbus Blue Jackets](/wiki/Columbus_Blue_Jackets)' president and general manager [Doug MacLean](/wiki/Doug_MacLean) and the NHL announced the event on March 21, 2006. On March 13, 2007, it was reported that NHL owners had voted in favor of changes to the team ranking system which would begin at the 2007 draft. This draft marked the first time in NHL history in which American players were selected with the top two picks, with [Patrick Kane](/wiki/Patrick_Kane) and [James van Riemsdyk](/wiki/James_van_Riemsdyk) being selected by the [Chicago Blackhawks](/wiki/Chicago_Blackhawks) and [Philadelphia Flyers](/wiki/Philadelphia_Flyers), respectively. It also tied the record of the most Americans being selected in the first round with ten players.

As of 2026, there are 11 active NHL players from this draft year.

## Lottery system

Starting with the 2007 NHL entry draft, the [Stanley Cup](/wiki/Stanley_Cup) champion and [runner-up](/wiki/Runner-up) will receive the 30th and 29th picks, respectively. The conference finalists will get the 28th and 27th picks, and all other playoff teams will get picks based on their regular season point totals, but with the division winners getting the latest picks even if they had fewer points during the regular season than a non-division winner.

The draft order of the first 14 picks was determined by a lottery involving the non-playoff teams on April 10.

Under the weighted lottery system, the club with the fewest regular-season points had the greatest chance (25%) of winning the Draft Drawing, have a 48.2% chance to pick first overall, and could pick no lower than second at the 2007 entry draft. The winner of the drawing would move up a maximum of four places, with all other clubs' draft order being adjusted accordingly (no team moving down more than one spot).

The [Chicago Blackhawks](/wiki/Chicago_Blackhawks), originally slated to draft in fifth place, won the lottery and as a result had the first overall pick in the draft.

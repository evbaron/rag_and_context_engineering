**Alexander Kostritsyn** (born 1986) is a [Russian](/wiki/Russians) professional [poker](/wiki/Poker) player considered among the best online [cash game](/wiki/Cash_game) players in the world. Kostritsyn plays under the alias **joiso** on [PokerStars](/wiki/PokerStars) and **PostflopAction** on [Full Tilt Poker](/wiki/Full_Tilt_Poker).

In 2010, Kostritsyn won Event No. 9 at the PokerStars World Championship of Online Poker for $269,284. Kostritsyn described winning $2.7 million online in 2013 as not a big winning streak for the stakes being played.

Kostritsyn won the 2008 [Aussie Millions](/wiki/Aussie_Millions) [Main Event](/wiki/Crown_Australian_Poker_Championship#2008_Crown_Australian_Poker_Championships) for $1,450,396 defeating WSOP bracelet winner [Erik Seidel](/wiki/Erik_Seidel) heads up. As of 2015, his total live tournament winnings exceed $3,000,000.

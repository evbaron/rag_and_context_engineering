# List of Apple products

"Apple devices" redirects here. For the software for Windows, see [Apple Devices](/wiki/Apple_Devices).

This **list of Apple products** is a list of all computers, phones, tablets, [wearables](/wiki/Wearable_computer), and other products made by [Apple Inc.](/wiki/Apple_Inc.) This list is ordered by the release date of the products. [Macintosh Performa](/wiki/Macintosh_Performa) models were often physically identical to other models, in which case they are omitted in favor of the identical twin.

**Markazi province** ([Persian](/wiki/Persian_language): استان مرکزی) is one of the 31 [provinces](/wiki/Provinces_of_Iran) of Iran. Its capital is the city of [Arak](/wiki/Arak,_Iran). The present borders of the province date to 1977, when the province was split into the current Markazi and [Tehran](/wiki/Tehran_province) provinces, with portions being annexed by [Isfahan](/wiki/Isfahan_province), [Semnan](/wiki/Semnan_province), and [Zanjan](/wiki/Zanjan_province) Provinces. In 2014, the province was placed in [Region 4](/wiki/Regions_of_Iran).

## History

The Congregation Mosque of [Narāgh](/wiki/Naraq)

Markazi province was part of the [Median Empire](/wiki/Median_Empire) in the first millennium BC, which included all of the central and western parts of modern-day [Iran](/wiki/Iran). The region is considered to be one of the ancient settlements on the [Iranian plateau](/wiki/Iranian_plateau). Numerous remaining ruins testify to the antiquity of this area.

In the early centuries of Islam, the name of the area was changed to *[Jibal](/wiki/Jibal)* or *Kuhestan*. By the early 10th century, *Khorheh* had become a famous city of Jibal province, followed by [Tafresh](/wiki/Tafresh) and [Khomein](/wiki/Khomein).

In recent times, the expansion of the north–south railroad (commonly known as the *[Persian Corridor](/wiki/Persian_Corridor)*) and the establishment of major industries have helped boost development in the area.

## Demographics

### Ethnicity

[Persians](/wiki/Persians) make up the vast majority of the province, but there are also smaller minorities of [Azerbaijanis](/wiki/Iranian_Azerbaijanis), [Kurds](/wiki/Kurds_in_Iran), [Lurs](/wiki/Lurs), etc.

### Population

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the province's population was 1,326,826 in 364,155 households. The following census in 2011 counted 1,413,959 people in 426,613 households. The 2016 census measured the population of the province as 1,429,475 in 455,866 households.

## Notable people

Many figures in Iranian history trace their origins to this province. namely: [Mirza Abu'l-Qasem Qa'em-Maqam](/wiki/Mirza_Abu%27l-Qasem_Qa%27em-Maqam), [Abbas Eqbal Ashtiani](/wiki/Abbas_Eqbal_Ashtiani), [Mirza Taqi Khan Amir Kabir](/wiki/Amir_Kabir), [Mirza Bozorg Qa'em-Maqam](/wiki/Mirza_Bozorg_Qa%27em-Maqam), [Mahmoud Hessabi](/wiki/Mahmoud_Hessabi), [Ayatollah Khomeini](/wiki/Ayatollah_Khomeini), Ayatollah Araki, and many others.

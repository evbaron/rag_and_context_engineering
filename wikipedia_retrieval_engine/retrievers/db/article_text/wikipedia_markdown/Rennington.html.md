**Rennington** is a village and [civil parish](/wiki/Civil_parish) in [Northumberland](/wiki/Northumberland), England about 4 miles (6 km) north of [Alnwick](/wiki/Alnwick). The parish includes the village of [Rock](/wiki/Rock,_Northumberland) and the hamlets of [Broxfield](/wiki/Broxfield) and Stamford. In 2011 the parish had a population of 366.

## Governance

**Rennington** is in the [parliamentary](/wiki/British_House_of_Commons) constituency of [Berwick-upon-Tweed](/wiki/Berwick-upon-Tweed_(UK_Parliament_constituency)). From 1974 to 2009 it was in [Alnwick](/wiki/Alnwick_District) district.

Rennington was formerly a [township](/wiki/Township_(England)) and [chapelry](/wiki/Chapelry) in the parish of Embleton, in 1866 Rennington became a separate civil parish, on 1 April 1955 the parishes of Broxfield, Rock and Stamford were abolished and merged with Rennington.

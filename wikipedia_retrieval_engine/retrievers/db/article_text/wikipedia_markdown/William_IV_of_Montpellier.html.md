# William IV of Montpellier

**William IV** (or **Guillem IV**) was the [Lord of Montpellier](/wiki/Lord_of_Montpellier) from 1058 until his death in 1068. He was the son of [William III](/wiki/William_III_of_Montpellier) and Beliardis. He was married to Ermengarde, daughter of Raymond I, [Count of Melgueil](/wiki/Count_of_Melgueil). He is the first of his dynasty with charters preserved in the family [cartulary](/wiki/Cartulary), the *[Liber instrumentorum memorialium](/wiki/Liber_instrumentorum_memorialium)*. They record agreements concerning some local castles in 1059. He was succeeded by his son, [William V](/wiki/William_V_of_Montpellier).

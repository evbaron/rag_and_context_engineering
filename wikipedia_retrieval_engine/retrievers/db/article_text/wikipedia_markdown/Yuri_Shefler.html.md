**Yuri Shefler** (Russian: Юрий Викторович Шефлер; born 10 September 1967) is a Russian-born businessman. He is the owner of SPI Group, an international consortium that sells alcohol in 160 countries, most notably the [Stolichnaya](/wiki/Stolichnaya) vodka brand. As of July 2025, his net worth was estimated at US$1.1 billion.

## Business career

Shefler left the [Soviet Army](/wiki/Soviet_Army) in September 1987 and embarked on a career in business, which saw him running one of Moscow's leading shopping malls and [Vnukovo Airlines](/wiki/Vnukovo_Airlines).

He used to own one of the largest yachts in the world, [*Serene*](/wiki/Serene_(yacht)), with a length of 133.9 m (439 ft 4 in). In 2015 he sold *Serene* to Saudi Crown Prince [Mohammed bin Salman](/wiki/Mohammad_bin_Salman) for a reported $550 million. In March 2017 he bought Tulchan Estate on [Speyside](/wiki/Strathspey,_Scotland), Scotland's most expensive sporting estate.

On 17 February 2022, [Brad Pitt](/wiki/Brad_Pitt) said that [Angelina Jolie](/wiki/Angelina_Jolie) sold to Shefler's wine group Tenute del Mondo, which is a subsidiary of Shefler's Stoli Group, her interest in the [Château Miraval](/wiki/Ch%C3%A2teau_Miraval,_Correns-Var) at [Correns](/wiki/Correns) in [Provence](/wiki/Provence), France, which Pitt and Jolie purchased in 2008 for $28.4 million.

### SPI Group

SPI Group produces and sells alcohol under 380 brands in 160 countries, most notably [Stolichnaya](/wiki/Stolichnaya) vodka. SPI Group also owns Louisiana Spirits Company, Fabrica de Tequilas Finos in Mexico and Achaval Ferrer in Argentina. A subsidiary of SPI Group, Luxembourg-based Amber Beverage Group Holding S.à r.l. owns Latvian-based SIA Amber Beverage Group, which in turn owns [Latvijas Balzams](/wiki/Latvijas_Balzams) which manufactures [Riga Black Balsam](/wiki/Riga_Black_Balsam).

Shefler's SPI Group has been involved in a decades-long legal dispute with the Russian state-owned company FKP [Sojuzplodoimport](/wiki/Soyuzplodoimport) (FKP) over ownership of the Stolichnaya and [Moskovskaya](/wiki/Moskovskaya_(brand)) trademarks. In 2020, the [Dutch Supreme Court](/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_the_Netherlands) ruled that the rights belonged to Russia and ordered SPI Group to stop selling its products in [Benelux](/wiki/Benelux).

In 2022, Shefler was ordered to pay a former UK executive over £1.6 million after sacking him when he complained about pay cuts.

## Personal life

Shefler was born to a Russian Jewish family in [Oryol](/wiki/Oryol). He has citizenship in the United Kingdom and Israel.

He was formerly married to [Tatiana Kovylina](/wiki/Tatiana_Kovylina), a Russian model who previously worked for [Victoria's Secret](/wiki/Victoria%27s_Secret); they separated in 2021. They have four children together, and lived together in [Geneva](/wiki/Geneva), Switzerland.
He has since been linked to [Ksenia Sukhinova](/wiki/Ksenia_Sukhinova), who was [Miss Russia 2007](/wiki/Miss_Russia_2007), and [Miss World 2008](/wiki/Miss_World_2008).

As reported by Forbes in March 2022, Shefler had an estimated net worth of $1.5 billion.

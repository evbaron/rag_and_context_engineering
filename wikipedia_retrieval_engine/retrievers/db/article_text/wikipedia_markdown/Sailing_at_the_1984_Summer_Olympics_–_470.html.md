# Sailing at the 1984 Summer Olympics – 470

## Infobox

- **Venue:** Long Beach
- **Dates:** 31 July to 8 August
- **Competitors:** 56 from 28 nations
- **Teams:** 28

The **[470](/wiki/470_(dinghy))** was a sailing event on the [Sailing at the 1984 Summer Olympics](/wiki/Sailing_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics) program in [Long Beach](/wiki/Long_Beach,_California), [Los Angeles County, California](/wiki/Los_Angeles_County,_California) . Seven races were scheduled. 56 sailors, on 28 boats, from 28 nations competed.

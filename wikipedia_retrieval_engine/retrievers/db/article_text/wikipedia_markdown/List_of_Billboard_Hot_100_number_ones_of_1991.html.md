# List of Billboard Hot 100 number ones of 1991

[Bryan Adams](/wiki/Bryan_Adams) (pictured) earned his second Hot 100 number-one single with "[(Everything I Do) I Do It for You](/wiki/(Everything_I_Do)_I_Do_It_for_You)", which stayed at the top position for seven straight weeks.

This is a list of the U.S. *[Billboard](/wiki/Billboard_(magazine))* magazine **[Hot 100](/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100)** number-ones of 1991. The longest running number-one single of 1991 is "[(Everything I Do) I Do It for You](/wiki/(Everything_I_Do)_I_Do_It_for_You)" by [Bryan Adams](/wiki/Bryan_Adams), which attained seven weeks at number-one. ("[Black or White](/wiki/Black_or_White)" by [Michael Jackson](/wiki/Michael_Jackson) would also spend a total of seven weeks at #1, but only four of those weeks were in the 1991 calendar year.)

That year, 14 acts earn their first number one song, such as [Surface](/wiki/Surface_(band)), [C+C Music Factory](/wiki/C%2BC_Music_Factory), [Freedom Williams](/wiki/Freedom_Williams), [Timmy T](/wiki/Timmy_T), [Londonbeat](/wiki/Londonbeat), [Hi-Five](/wiki/Hi-Five), [Extreme](/wiki/Extreme_(band)), [EMF](/wiki/EMF_(band)), [Color Me Badd](/wiki/Color_Me_Badd), [Marky Mark and the Funky Bunch](/wiki/Marky_Mark_and_the_Funky_Bunch), [Loleatta Holloway](/wiki/Loleatta_Holloway), [Karyn White](/wiki/Karyn_White), [The New Power Generation](/wiki/The_New_Power_Generation), and [P.M. Dawn](/wiki/P.M._Dawn). [Mariah Carey](/wiki/Mariah_Carey) and [Paula Abdul](/wiki/Paula_Abdul) were the only acts to hit number one more than once, with Mariah Carey having the most with three and Paula Abdul having two.

The November 30 chart ("Set Adrift on Memory Bliss") was the first Hot 100 to be compiled with [Soundscan](/wiki/Soundscan) data.

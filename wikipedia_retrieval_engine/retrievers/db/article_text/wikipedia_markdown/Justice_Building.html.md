# Justice Building

The **Justice Building** is an office hill on [Parliament Hill](/wiki/Parliament_Hill) in [Ottawa](/wiki/Ottawa). The building was designed by [Thomas W. Fuller](/wiki/Thomas_W._Fuller) and is called the 'Justice Building' because it was previously home to the [Department of Justice](/wiki/Department_of_Justice_(Canada)).

Originally called Block D, it was built from 1935 to 1938 for the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police](/wiki/Royal_Canadian_Mounted_Police) (RCMP). It was renovated in 1998–2001 and now houses some of the offices of members of Parliament.

The building is part of [Gothic Revival architecture](/wiki/Gothic_Revival_architecture). Its design is similar to the [Confederation Building](/wiki/Confederation_Building_(Ottawa)) located just east of it, which was also designed by Thomas W. Fuller.

In [Ian Fleming](/wiki/Ian_Fleming)'s short story "[For Your Eyes Only](/wiki/For_Your_Eyes_Only_(short_story_collection)#For_Your_Eyes_Only)", [James Bond](/wiki/James_Bond_(literary_character)) visits the RCMP headquarters when it was located in this building, and the book contains a description of its structure.

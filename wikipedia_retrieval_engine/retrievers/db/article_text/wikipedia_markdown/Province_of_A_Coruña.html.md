The **province of A Coruña** ([Galician](/wiki/Galician_language): *provincia da Coruña* [[pɾoˈβinθjɐ ðɐ koˈɾuɲɐ]](/wiki/Help:IPA/Galician); [Spanish](/wiki/Spanish_language): *provincia de La Coruña* [[la koˈɾuɲa]](/wiki/Help:IPA/Spanish) [ⓘ](/wiki/File:Pronunciation_of_A_Coru%C3%B1a_in_Spanish.ogg); historical English: Corunna) is the northwesternmost province of [Spain](/wiki/Spain), and one of the four provinces which constitute the [autonomous community](/wiki/Nationalities_and_regions_of_Spain) of [Galicia](/wiki/Galicia_(Spain)). This province is surrounded by the [Atlantic Ocean](/wiki/Atlantic_Ocean) to the west and north, [Pontevedra Province](/wiki/Pontevedra_Province) to the south and [Lugo Province](/wiki/Lugo_Province) to the east. It has a population of 1,128,449 across its 93 municipalities.

## History

The history of this province starts at the end of the [Middle Ages](/wiki/Middle_Ages) during the reign of the [Catholic Monarchs of Spain](/wiki/Catholic_Monarchs). During those years this province was far smaller than today. This is because in the [1833 territorial division of Spain](/wiki/1833_territorial_division_of_Spain) the entire [Province of Betanzos](/wiki/Betanzos) together with half of the [Mondoñedo](/wiki/Mondonedo) were amalgamated into one single province with its capital [city in A Coruña](/wiki/A_Coru%C3%B1a).
Since 1833, the province has always been the one with the largest population and largest coast. Until the second half of the 20th century, this province was both the religious and cultural centre of the entire region. The [University of Santiago de Compostela](/wiki/University_of_Santiago_de_Compostela) was the only university in North-western Spain until the arrival of democracy after the death of General [Francisco Franco](/wiki/Francisco_Franco).

## Main sights

The [cathedral of Santiago de Compostela](/wiki/Cathedral_of_Santiago_de_Compostela) is the destination of the [Way of St. James](/wiki/Way_of_St._James), a major historical [pilgrimage](/wiki/Pilgrimage) route since the [Middle Ages](/wiki/Middle_Ages) which still gathers thousands of pilgrims each year from all over the world.

## Parks

- [Atlantic Islands of Galicia National Park](/wiki/Atlantic_Islands_of_Galicia_National_Park) is the only [national park](/wiki/National_park) in Galicia. It is shared between the provinces of A Coruña and [Pontevedra](/wiki/Pontevedra_(province)).
- [The "Fragas" of the River Eume Natural Park](/wiki/The_%22Fragas%22_of_the_River_Eume_Natural_Park) extends itself throughout the [Eume](/wiki/O_Eume) and [Ferrol](/wiki/Ferrol_Region) regions of [Ferrolterra](/wiki/Ferrolterra).
- [The Dunes of Corrubedo Natural Park](/wiki/The_Dunes_of_Corrubedo_Natural_Park) (*Parque Natural das Dunas de Corrubedo e lagoas de Carregal e Vixán*) is a beach park at the very end of the [Barbanza Peninsula](/wiki/A_Barbanza).

Events from the year **1944 in [Argentina](/wiki/Argentina)**.

## Incumbents

- [President](/wiki/President_of_Argentina): [Pedro Pablo Ramírez](/wiki/Pedro_Pablo_Ram%C3%ADrez) (until 25 February); [Edelmiro Julián Farrell](/wiki/Edelmiro_Juli%C3%A1n_Farrell) (from 25 February)
- Vice president: vacant (until 8 July); [Juan Perón](/wiki/Juan_Per%C3%B3n) (from 8 July)

### Governors

- [Buenos Aires Province](/wiki/Governor_of_Buenos_Aires_Province):
  - until 5 January: Faustino J. Legón
  - 5 January-5 May: Julio O. Ojea
  - 5 May-19 July: Luis García Mata
  - 19 July-27 December: Juan Carlos Sanguinetti
  - from 27 December: Roberto M. Vanetta
- [Mendoza Province](/wiki/Governor_of_Mendoza_Province): Aristóbulo Vargas Belmonte

### Vice Governors

- Buenos Aires Province: vacant

## Events

### January

- [15 January](/wiki/15_January) – [1944 San Juan earthquake](/wiki/1944_San_Juan_earthquake), produces nearly 7000 deaths, 12000 injured and destroyed the 90% of building in the city
- [22 January](/wiki/22_January) – [Juan Perón](/wiki/Juan_Per%C3%B3n) and [Eva Perón](/wiki/Eva_Per%C3%B3n) met during a fundraising for the aforementioned earthquake.

### February

- [Pedro Ramírez](/wiki/Pedro_Pablo_Ram%C3%ADrez) resigns as president, being replaced by [Edelmiro Farrell](/wiki/Edelmiro_Farrell)

### March

- [Bernardo Houssay](/wiki/Bernardo_Houssay), [Juan Lewis](/wiki/Juan_Lewis) and Eduardo Menendez establish the *[Instituto de Biología y Medicina Experimental](/wiki/Instituto_de_Biolog%C3%ADa_y_Medicina_Experimental)*

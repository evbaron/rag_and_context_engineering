**Ian David "Shoey" Schuback** [OAM](/wiki/Medal_of_the_Order_of_Australia) (born 4 September 1952) is an Australian former lawn and indoor bowler, and the only player from outside the United Kingdom to ever win the World Indoor Bowls Championship.

## Biography

Schuback bowled for the Coolangatta club in Queensland and was also a former professional [tennis](/wiki/Tennis) coach.

He started bowling in 1980 after watching the [Melbourne World Outdoor Championships](/wiki/World_Bowls_Events). Schuback claimed a Men's Singles silver medal at the [1986 Commonwealth Games](/wiki/Lawn_bowls_at_the_1986_Commonwealth_Games) and then won the [1988 World Indoor Bowls Championship](/wiki/1988_World_Indoor_Bowls_Championship) and the [1990 Commonwealth Games](/wiki/Lawn_bowls_at_the_1990_Commonwealth_Games) Pairs titles.

He won the 1992 [World Indoor Bowls Championships](/wiki/World_Indoor_Bowls_Championships) Singles, the [1994 World Indoor Bowls Championship](/wiki/1994_World_Indoor_Bowls_Championship) Pairs and the [1996 World Indoor Bowls Championship](/wiki/1996_World_Indoor_Bowls_Championship) Pairs.

He won two silver medals at the 1991 [Asia Pacific Bowls Championships](/wiki/Asia_Pacific_Bowls_Championships), in [Kowloon](/wiki/Kowloon), Hong Kong.

In the [2009 Australia Day Honours](/wiki/2009_Australia_Day_Honours) he was awarded the [Medal of the Order of Australia](/wiki/Medal_of_the_Order_of_Australia) (OAM) for "service to lawn bowls as a coach, competitor and commentator". He was also awarded the [Australian Sports Medal](/wiki/Australian_Sports_Medal) on 23 August 2000.

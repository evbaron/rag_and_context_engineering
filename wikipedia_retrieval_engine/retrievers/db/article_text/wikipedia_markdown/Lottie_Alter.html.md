**Charlotte Alice Alter** (January 16, 1871 – December 25, 1924) was an American actress on stage and in silent films.

## Early life

Alter was born in [La Crosse, Wisconsin](/wiki/La_Crosse,_Wisconsin) on January 16, 1871, the daughter of Frederick Pernal Alter and Ida Alter (née Soplitt).

## Career

Arthur Vezin and Lottie Alter in *Mrs. Wiggs of the Cabbage Patch* (1908).

Alter began acting in the American midwest by 1890, playing [soubrette](/wiki/Soubrette) roles in touring companies managed by [Henrietta Crosman](/wiki/Henrietta_Crosman), [Joseph Jefferson](/wiki/Joseph_Jefferson), and [Charles Frohman](/wiki/Charles_Frohman), in such shows as *[The Cricket on the Hearth](/wiki/The_Cricket_on_the_Hearth)*, *Beside the Bonnie Brier Bush*, *The Country Circus*, *Fifi*, *[The Shadows of a Great City](/wiki/The_Shadows_of_a_Great_City_(play))*, *The Girl I Left Behind Me*, and *Hearts are Trumps*. On Broadway, she acted in *To Have and to Hold* (1901), *The Vinegar Buyer* (1903), *The Trifler* (1905) with [Esme Beringer](/wiki/Esme_Beringer), *[Charley's Aunt](/wiki/Charley%27s_Aunt)* (1906), and *Excuse Me* (1911) by [Rupert Hughes](/wiki/Rupert_Hughes). Of her work in *Excuse Me*, critic [George Jean Nathan](/wiki/George_Jean_Nathan) wrote that she was among "the best in a generally capable cast."

She toured Australia and Great Britain in *[Mrs. Wiggs of the Cabbage Patch](/wiki/Mrs._Wiggs_of_the_Cabbage_Patch)*. In 1916 she was leading her own company in [vaudeville](/wiki/Vaudeville).

Silent film appearances by Alter included roles in the film shorts *Advertising for a Wife* (1910) and *An Arizona Romance* (1910), and the feature films *[The Eternal City](/wiki/The_Eternal_City_(1915_film))* (1915) alongside [Pauline Frederick](/wiki/Pauline_Frederick) and [Thomas Holding](/wiki/Thomas_Holding), and *[The Lottery Man](/wiki/The_Lottery_Man_(1916_film))* (1916) with [Oliver Hardy](/wiki/Oliver_Hardy) and [Thurlow Bergen](/wiki/Thurlow_Bergen).

## Personal life

Alter married a fellow actor, [Harry C. Bradley](/wiki/Harry_C._Bradley_(actor)), in 1923.

She died in [Beechhurst, Queens](/wiki/Beechhurst,_Queens), New York, in 1924 of [pneumonitis](/wiki/Pneumonitis). She was buried at [Flushing Cemetery](/wiki/Flushing_Cemetery).

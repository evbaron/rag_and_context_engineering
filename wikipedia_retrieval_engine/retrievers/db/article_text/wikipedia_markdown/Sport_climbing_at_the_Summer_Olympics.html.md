# Sport climbing at the Summer Olympics

## Infobox

- **IOC Code:** CLB
- **Governing body:** IFSC
- **Events:** 4 (men: 2; women: 2)

[Competition climbing](/wiki/Competition_climbing) has been held at two editions of the [Summer Olympic Games](/wiki/Summer_Olympic_Games). First selected as one of the discretionary sports at the [2020](/wiki/Sport_climbing_at_the_2020_Summer_Olympics) and [2024](/wiki/Sport_climbing_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics) games, sport climbing will be inducted as one of the mandatory sports at the [2028](/wiki/2028_Summer_Olympics) games. Athletes compete in the disciplines of [bouldering](/wiki/Bouldering), [lead climbing](/wiki/Lead_climbing), and [speed climbing](/wiki/Speed_climbing). All three were contested as a single event in the 2020 programme, while speed climbing was spun off into its own event in the 2024 programme. [Slovenia](/wiki/Slovenia_at_the_Olympics) have won the most gold medals (2), while [Austria](/wiki/Austria_at_the_Olympics), [Japan](/wiki/Japan_at_the_Olympics), and the [United States](/wiki/United_States_at_the_Summer_Olympics) have won the most medals overall (3 each).

## Bid for inclusion

The inclusion was proposed by the [International Federation of Sport Climbing (IFSC)](/wiki/International_Federation_of_Sport_Climbing) in 2015. In September 2015, competition climbing was included in a shortlist along with [baseball](/wiki/Baseball_at_the_Summer_Olympics), [softball](/wiki/Softball), [skateboarding](/wiki/Skateboarding_at_the_Summer_Olympics), [surfing](/wiki/Surfing_at_the_Summer_Olympics), and [karate](/wiki/Karate_at_the_Summer_Olympics) to be considered for inclusion in the [2020 Summer Olympics](/wiki/2020_Summer_Olympics); and in June 2016, the executive board of the [International Olympic Committee](/wiki/International_Olympic_Committee) (IOC) announced that they would support the proposal to include all of the shortlisted sports in the 2020 Games. Finally, on August 3, 2016, all five sports (counting baseball and softball together as one sport) were approved for inclusion in the 2020 Olympic program.

## Competition format

At the [2020 Summer Olympics](/wiki/2020_Summer_Olympics), two climbing events were contested: men's combined and women's combined. The competition format combined three disciplines of competition climbing: [competition speed climbing](/wiki/Competition_speed_climbing), [competition bouldering](/wiki/Competition_bouldering), and [competition lead climbing](/wiki/Competition_lead_climbing). This decision caused widespread criticism in the climbing community.

Members of the IFSC explained that they were only granted one gold medal per gender by the Olympic committee, and they did not want to exclude speed climbing. The IFSC's goal for the 2020 Olympics was primarily to establish climbing and its three disciplines as Olympic sports; changes to the format could follow later. This tactic proved to be successful; the [2024 Summer Olympics](/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics) would expand sport climbing to separate boulder-and-lead and speed climbing events.

Beginning at the [2028 Summer Olympics](/wiki/2028_Summer_Olympics), the boulder and lead disciplines will be split into separate medal events.

## Technical information

For Olympic level sports climbing, [route setters](/wiki/Route_setter) are instructed to set lead climbs at a [grade](/wiki/Grade_(climbing)#Free_climbing) of between [8c](/wiki/Grade_(climbing)#Free_climbing) (5.14b) and [9a](/wiki/Grade_(climbing)#Free_climbing) (5.14d) for both the men's and women's events. For boulder, men's routes are set between a [grade](/wiki/Grade_(climbing)#Bouldering) of [V10](/wiki/Grade_(climbing)#Bouldering) (7C+) and [V14](/wiki/Grade_(climbing)#Bouldering) (8B+), and women's routes between [V9](/wiki/Grade_(climbing)#Bouldering) (7C) and [V12](/wiki/Grade_(climbing)#Bouldering) (8A+).

*NB*:

- Lead grades are given in [French Numerical](/wiki/Grade_(climbing)#French_numerical_grade) and grades in brackets () are in [American YDS](/wiki/Grade_(climbing)#American_YDS_grade).
- Boulder grade are given in [V-Grades](/wiki/Grade_(climbing)#V-grade) and grades in brackets () are in [Fb-Grades](/wiki/Grade_(climbing)#Font_grade).
- Due to the climbing routes being at the highest level, routes cannot be sufficiently tested, therefore the exact grade of each route is unknown, and may not reflect the target range.

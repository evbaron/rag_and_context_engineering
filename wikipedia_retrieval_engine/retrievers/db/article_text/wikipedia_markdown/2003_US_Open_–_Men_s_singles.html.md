Main article: [2003 US Open (tennis)](/wiki/2003_US_Open_(tennis))

[Andy Roddick](/wiki/Andy_Roddick) defeated [Juan Carlos Ferrero](/wiki/Juan_Carlos_Ferrero) in the final, 6–3, 7–6(7–2), 6–3 to win the men's singles tennis title at the [2003 US Open](/wiki/2003_US_Open_(tennis)). It was his first and only [major](/wiki/Grand_Slam_(tennis)#Tournaments) title. Roddick remains the most recent American to win a major men's singles title. Roddick [saved a match point](/wiki/List_of_Grand_Slam%E2%80%93related_tennis_records#Won_a_title_after_saving_match_points) en route to the title, in the semifinals against [David Nalbandian](/wiki/David_Nalbandian). He also completed the [Summer Slam](/wiki/List_of_ATP_Tour_top-level_tournament_singles_champions#Calendar_sweeps), having won the [Canada Masters](/wiki/2003_Canada_Masters_%E2%80%93_Singles) and [Cincinnati Masters](/wiki/2003_Western_%26_Southern_Financial_Group_Masters_%E2%80%93_Singles).

[Pete Sampras](/wiki/Pete_Sampras) was the reigning champion, but he retired from professional tennis in August 2003.

This tournament marked the first US Open main draw appearance of future four-time champion [Rafael Nadal](/wiki/Rafael_Nadal). It also marked the final major appearances of former major champions [Michael Chang](/wiki/Michael_Chang) and [Yevgeny Kafelnikov](/wiki/Yevgeny_Kafelnikov).

# 1988 World Indoor Bowls Championship

## Infobox

- **Location:** Alexandra Palace, & Bournemouth
- **Date(s):** 5-13 March 1988 & 5-13 Dec 1987+.
- **Category:** World Indoor Championships

The **1988 Embassy World Indoor Bowls Championship**  was held at [Alexandra Palace](/wiki/Alexandra_Palace), [London](/wiki/London), [England](/wiki/England), from 5 to 13 March 1988.
The event moved to the newly rebuilt [Alexandra Palace](/wiki/Alexandra_Palace) from the [Coatbridge](/wiki/Coatbridge) indoor bowling club. Seeds were introduced for the first time.

[Hugh Duff](/wiki/Hugh_Duff_(bowls)) won the title beating [Wynne Richards](/wiki/Wynne_Richards_(bowls)) in the final five games to one (7-0, 7-1, 2-7, 7-6, 7-4, 7-4).

The **1988 Midland Bank World Indoor Pairs Championship** was held at the [Bournemouth](/wiki/Bournemouth) International Centre from 5 to 13 December 1987+.(+ Held in Dec 1987 to avoid a clash with the 1988 [World Outdoor Championships](/wiki/World_Bowls_Events)).
[Ian Schuback](/wiki/Ian_Schuback) & Jim Yates won the title defeating [Andy Thomson](/wiki/Andy_Thomson_(bowls)) & Gary Smith 5-1 in the final (6-3, 7-5, 5-7, 8-2, 7-5, 7-5).

The **1988 Inaugural Women's Championship** was held at the Llanelli Indoor Bowling Club from April 29 to 1 May 1. [Margaret Johnston](/wiki/Margaret_Johnston_(bowls)) won the title beating Edna Bessell in the final 7-3 5-7 7-6 7-2.

# Richard Burton (comics)

**Richard Burton** is a British [comic](/wiki/Comics) publisher and [editor](/wiki/Editing) who had a lengthy career at [IPC Magazines](/wiki/IPC_Magazines). While an assistant editor at *[2000 AD](/wiki/2000_AD_(comic))*, he became known to readers as [Tharg the Mighty](/wiki/Tharg_the_Mighty)'s bumbling assistant Burt, who appeared in a number of strips with him. (Burton was later top editor at *2000 AD*.) Earlier in his career, Burton published the popular [fanzine](/wiki/Fanzine) *Comic Media News*, and was a co-founder of the [Eagle Awards](/wiki/Eagle_Award_(comics)).

## Career

### *Comic Media News*

In 1972, Burton and fellow enthusiast [Nick Landau](/wiki/Nick_Landau) took over a [fanzine](/wiki/Fanzine) called *Comic Catalog*, renaming it *Comic Media* and establishing the Comic Media brand. Burton and Landau produced a few issues of *Comic Media*, assisted by Dennis Lee and Tom Downes. In 1973, receiving permission to reprint U.S. comics industry news items from the long-running American comic fanzine *[The Comic Reader](/wiki/The_Comic_Reader)*, Burton launched the four-page *Comic Media and The Comic Reader Newsletter* #1, which eventually came to be known as *Comic Media News* (*CMN*).

Meanwhile, Landau produced the 1972 [UK Comicon](/wiki/British_Comic_Art_Convention) and then branched out to one-day [London Comic Marts](/wiki/London_Comic_Mart) and [direct market](/wiki/Direct_market) distribution. Landau's **Comic Media Distribution Service** imported American comics from the "big two" publishers [DC](/wiki/DC_Comics) and [Marvel](/wiki/Marvel_Comics); it later became known as [Titan Distributors](/wiki/Titan_Distributors).

Burton produced *Comic Media News* from 1973 to 1980, eventually publishing 40 issues. Continuing the fanzine's relationship with *[The Comic Reader](/wiki/The_Comic_Reader)*, the first seven issues were titled *Comic Media & The Comic Reader UK Edition Newsletter* before shortening to *Comic Media News*. [Martin Lock](/wiki/Martin_Lock) was features editor for *CMN* from 1973 to 1977, while he was publishing his own comics fanzine, *[Bemusing Magazine](/wiki/Bemusing_Magazine)* (*BEM*). *CMN* had a circulation of close to 2,000 in late 1977, and began to feature art contributions from such established creators as [Brian Bolland](/wiki/Brian_Bolland). With issue #34, the magazine expanded its title to *Comic Media News International*, reflecting its readership in more than 20 different countries.

In [1977](/wiki/1977_in_comics), along with fellow British comics enthusiast [Mike Conroy](/wiki/Mike_Conroy_(writer)), Burton started the [Eagle Awards](/wiki/Eagle_Awards) (voted on by British comics enthusiasts). (*Comic Media News* won the Eagle Award for Favourite Specialist Comics Publication/Trade Publication in both 1977 and 1978.)

### *2000 AD*

After turning down a job offer with [DC Comics](/wiki/DC_Comics), Burton was hired as an assistant editor at [Marvel UK](/wiki/Marvel_UK) in March 1978, working for editor [Dez Skinn](/wiki/Dez_Skinn) (whom Burton knew from the early days of UK conventions and fanzine publishing). With Skinn, he created the popular character [Night Raven](/wiki/Night_Raven), who first appeared in [Marvel UK](/wiki/Marvel_UK)'s *[Hulk Comic](/wiki/Hulk_Comic)* #1 (March 7, 1979).

After less than a year at Marvel UK, in March 1979 Burton left for a new job at the weekly comic magazine *[Tornado](/wiki/Tornado_(comics))*, published by [IPC Magazines](/wiki/IPC_Magazines). *Tornado* only lasted through August 1979, at which point Burton moved to *[2000 AD](/wiki/2000_AD_(comics))* (also published by IPC). At this point, Burton retired *Comic Media News*, though he continued to co-produce the [Eagle Awards](/wiki/Eagle_Award_(comics)) for another couple of years with [Mike Conroy](/wiki/Mike_Conroy_(writer)).

Burton stayed with *2000 AD* throughout the 1980s and into the mid-1990s. In 1986 he became editor of *[Battle](/wiki/Battle_(comics))*. In 1987, IPC's comics line was sold to [Robert Maxwell](/wiki/Robert_Maxwell) and renamed [Fleetway Publications](/wiki/Fleetway_Publications); Burton was at that point promoted to editor of *2000 AD*, succeeding [Steve MacManus](/wiki/Steve_MacManus). [Egmont UK](/wiki/Egmont_UK) bought Fleetway from Maxwell in 1991, merging it with their own comics publishing operation, London Editions, to form [Fleetway Editions](/wiki/Fleetway_Editions). At that point, Burton took the opportunity to launch the very successful [videogame](/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog_(1991_video_game)) tie-in *[Sonic the Comic](/wiki/Sonic_the_Comic)* with Fleetway, which lasted nine years (three with him as editor). Notable *2000 AD* strips and storylines published under Burton's editorship as [Tharg the Mighty](/wiki/Tharg_the_Mighty) include:

- "Democracy," a [Judge Dredd](/wiki/Judge_Dredd) storyline inherited from MacManus and published through 1991
- [Zenith](/wiki/Zenith_(comics)) ([Grant Morrison](/wiki/Grant_Morrison) and [Steve Yeowell](/wiki/Steve_Yeowell)), launched in 1987
- [Friday](/wiki/Friday_(2000_AD)) ([Dave Gibbons](/wiki/Dave_Gibbons) and [Will Simpson](/wiki/Will_Simpson_(comics))), launched in 1989
- *[The Dead Man](/wiki/The_Dead_Man)* ([John Wagner](/wiki/John_Wagner) and [John Ridgway](/wiki/John_Ridgway_(comics))), published in 1989–1990
- "[Necropolis](/wiki/Necropolis_(Judge_Dredd_story))" ([John Wagner](/wiki/John_Wagner) and [Carlos Ezquerra](/wiki/Carlos_Ezquerra)), 26-part [Judge Dredd](/wiki/Judge_Dredd) storyline published in 1990
- *[Time Flies](/wiki/Time_Flies_(comics))* ([Garth Ennis](/wiki/Garth_Ennis) and [Philip Bond](/wiki/Philip_Bond)), launched in 1990
- [Finn](/wiki/Finn_(comics)) ([Pat Mills](/wiki/Pat_Mills)), acquired in 1991
- "[Judgement Day](/wiki/Judgement_Day_(Judge_Dredd))" ([Garth Ennis](/wiki/Garth_Ennis)), published in 1992
- *[Firekind](/wiki/Firekind)* ([John Smith](/wiki/John_Smith_(comics_writer)) and Paul Marshall), published in 1993

Burton held the editor position at *2000 AD* from 1987 to 1994. He then worked on the computer magazine *Fun Online*.

Burton has since made a career in computers and digital cameras.

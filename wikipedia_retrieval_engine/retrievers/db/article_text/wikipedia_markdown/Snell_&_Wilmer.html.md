**Snell & Wilmer** is an American law firm based in [Phoenix, Arizona](/wiki/Phoenix,_Arizona). Founded in 1938, the firm represents clients ranging from large, publicly traded corporations, to small businesses, individuals and entrepreneurs. Barbara J. Dawson is the chair.

# 2019 European Parliament election in Lombardy

The **[2019 European Parliament election](/wiki/2019_European_Parliament_election_in_Italy)** took place in [Italy](/wiki/Italy) on 26 May 2019.

In [Lombardy](/wiki/Lombardy) [Lega Nord](/wiki/Lega_Nord) came first with 43.4% of the vote (country-level result 34.3%) and more than 20pp than the [Democratic Party](/wiki/Democratic_Party_(Italy)), which came second with 23.1%. The [Five Star Movement](/wiki/Five_Star_Movement) came third with 9.3%, ahead of [Forza Italia](/wiki/Forza_Italia_(2013)) (8.7%), [Brothers of Italy](/wiki/Brothers_of_Italy) (5.5%), [More Europe](/wiki/More_Europe) (3.1%), [Green Europe](/wiki/Green_Europe) (2.5%) and [The Left](/wiki/The_Left_(Italy)) (1.3%).

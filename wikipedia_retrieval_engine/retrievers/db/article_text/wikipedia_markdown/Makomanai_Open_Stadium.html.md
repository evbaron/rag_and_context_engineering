The **Makomanai Sekisui Heim Stadium** is a multi-purpose [stadium](/wiki/Stadium) in [Sapporo](/wiki/Sapporo), [Japan](/wiki/Japan). During the [1972 Winter Olympics](/wiki/1972_Winter_Olympics), it hosted the opening ceremonies and the [speed skating](/wiki/Speed_skating_at_the_1972_Winter_Olympics) events. Located inside the [Makomanai Park](/wiki/Makomanai_Park), the stadium holds 17,324 people.

Currently the stadium holds the [speed skating](/wiki/Speed_skating) Winter Annual National Competition, and until 2011 the [Toyota Big Air](/wiki/Toyota_Big_Air) snowboarding contest.

During summer it is used as [tennis](/wiki/Tennis) courts (up to eight at the same time) or [futsal](/wiki/Futsal) fields (up to two at the same time).

As of 2021, the Olympic cauldron is still seemingly mounted on the original 1972 plinth, just within the stadium's south east perimeter.

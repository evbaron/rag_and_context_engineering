**Courteney Bass Cox** (born June 15, 1964) is an American actress and producer. She rose to international prominence by playing [Monica Geller](/wiki/Monica_Geller) in the [NBC](/wiki/NBC) sitcom *[Friends](/wiki/Friends)* (1994–2004) and [Gale Weathers](/wiki/Gale_Weathers) in the horror film franchise *[Scream](/wiki/Scream_(franchise))* (1996–present). Her accolades include a [Screen Actors Guild Award](/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Awards), nominations for two [Emmy Awards](/wiki/Emmy_Awards) and a [Golden Globe Award](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Awards), and a star on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame).

Cox had a recurring role in the [NBC](/wiki/NBC) sitcom *[Family Ties](/wiki/Family_Ties)* (1987–1989), and starred in the [FX](/wiki/FX_(TV_channel)) drama series *[Dirt](/wiki/Dirt_(TV_series))* (2007–2008), the [ABC](/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company)/[TBS](/wiki/TBS_(American_TV_channel)) sitcom *[Cougar Town](/wiki/Cougar_Town)* (2009–2015), and the [Starz](/wiki/Starz) horror comedy series *[Shining Vale](/wiki/Shining_Vale)* (2022–2023). Her film credits include the action fantasy *[Masters of the Universe](/wiki/Masters_of_the_Universe_(1987_film))* (1987), the comedy *[Ace Ventura: Pet Detective](/wiki/Ace_Ventura:_Pet_Detective)* (1994), the animated comedy *[Barnyard](/wiki/Barnyard_(film))* (2006), the fantasy comedy *[Bedtime Stories](/wiki/Bedtime_Stories_(film))* (2008), and the [independent](/wiki/Independent_film) drama *[Mothers and Daughters](/wiki/Mothers_and_Daughters_(2016_film))* (2016).

Cox owned the production company [Coquette Productions](/wiki/Coquette_Productions), which she founded with her then-husband, [David Arquette](/wiki/David_Arquette). She has directed the television drama film *[TalhotBlond](/wiki/TalhotBlond_(2012_film))* (2012), the black comedy drama film *[Just Before I Go](/wiki/Just_Before_I_Go)* (2014), and executive produced the game show *[Celebrity Name Game](/wiki/Celebrity_Name_Game)* (2014–2017).

## Early life

Courteney Bass Cox was born June 15, 1964, in [Birmingham, Alabama](/wiki/Birmingham,_Alabama), and raised there. She is a daughter of businessman Richard Lewis Cox (1931–2001) and Courteney Copeland (née Bass; 1934–2020). Cox has two older sisters, Virginia and Dorothy, and an older brother, Richard Jr. Her parents divorced in 1974 and her mother then married businessman Hunter Copeland (uncle to music promoter and business manager [Ian Copeland](/wiki/Ian_Copeland) and [the Police](/wiki/The_Police) drummer [Stewart Copeland](/wiki/Stewart_Copeland)).

After graduating from [Mountain Brook High School](/wiki/Mountain_Brook_High_School), Cox left for [Mount Vernon College](/wiki/Mount_Vernon_College_for_Women) in [Washington, D.C.](/wiki/Washington,_D.C.) (later part of [George Washington University](/wiki/George_Washington_University)), but did not complete her architecture course, opting instead to pursue a career in modeling and acting. Cox’s father is of British (English and Scottish and Welsh) descent, and her mother had Norman and Irish ancestry; researching her family tree for the series *Who Do You Think You Are?*, Cox discovered she is a direct descendant of [William the Conqueror](/wiki/William_the_Conqueror) and [Edward I of England](/wiki/Edward_I_of_England).

## Career

### Early work

Cox was chosen from a [casting call](/wiki/Casting_call) by director [Brian De Palma](/wiki/Brian_De_Palma) to appear in the 1984 music video for [Bruce Springsteen](/wiki/Bruce_Springsteen)'s "[Dancing in the Dark](/wiki/Dancing_in_the_Dark_(Bruce_Springsteen_song))", as the young woman pulled onstage at the [St. Paul Civic Center](/wiki/St._Paul_Civic_Center) to dance with Springsteen. Her early television work includes a starring role as Gloria Dinallo in the short-lived [NBC](/wiki/NBC) science fiction series *[Misfits of Science](/wiki/Misfits_of_Science)* (1985), and guest-starring roles in the [ABC](/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company) comedy-drama series *[The Love Boat](/wiki/The_Love_Boat)* (1986) and the [CBS](/wiki/CBS) crime drama series *[Murder, She Wrote](/wiki/Murder,_She_Wrote)* (1986). She also appeared in a 1985 [Tampax](/wiki/Tampax) ad notable for making her the first person to use the word "period" in an American television commercial. She later had a recurring role as Lauren Miller, the girlfriend of [Michael J. Fox](/wiki/Michael_J._Fox)'s character [Alex P. Keaton](/wiki/Alex_P._Keaton) in the NBC comedy series *[Family Ties](/wiki/Family_Ties)* (1987–1989). Cox's early film roles include *[Masters of the Universe](/wiki/Masters_of_the_Universe_(1987_film))* (1987), *[Cocoon: The Return](/wiki/Cocoon:_The_Return)* (1988), and *[I'll Be Home for Christmas](/wiki/I%27ll_Be_Home_for_Christmas_(1988_film))* (1988). She also played Jewel Jagger, the tough-as-nails assistant of Larry Burrows ([James Belushi](/wiki/James_Belushi)), in *[Mr. Destiny](/wiki/Mr._Destiny)* (1990).

### 1990s: International breakthrough

Cox in 1995

In 1993, Cox co-starred in the short-lived CBS sitcom *[The Trouble with Larry](/wiki/The_Trouble_with_Larry)*, alongside [Bronson Pinchot](/wiki/Bronson_Pinchot) and [Perry King](/wiki/Perry_King). The following year, shortly before the debut of the sitcom *Friends*, Cox starred alongside [Jim Carrey](/wiki/Jim_Carrey) in the comedy film *[Ace Ventura: Pet Detective](/wiki/Ace_Ventura:_Pet_Detective)*, and as [Jerry Seinfeld](/wiki/Jerry_Seinfeld_(character))'s girlfriend, Meryl, in the *[Seinfeld](/wiki/Seinfeld)* episode "[The Wife](/wiki/The_Wife_(Seinfeld))".

In 1994, Cox was asked to audition for the starring role of [Rachel Green](/wiki/Rachel_Green) on a new sitcom, *[Friends](/wiki/Friends)*; she was cast as [Monica Geller](/wiki/Monica_Geller) instead. At first the most famous cast member of the new show, Cox joined [Jennifer Aniston](/wiki/Jennifer_Aniston) ([Rachel Green](/wiki/Rachel_Green)), [Lisa Kudrow](/wiki/Lisa_Kudrow) ([Phoebe Buffay](/wiki/Phoebe_Buffay)), [Matt LeBlanc](/wiki/Matt_LeBlanc) ([Joey Tribbiani](/wiki/Joey_Tribbiani)), [Matthew Perry](/wiki/Matthew_Perry) ([Chandler Bing](/wiki/Chandler_Bing)) and [David Schwimmer](/wiki/David_Schwimmer) ([Ross Geller](/wiki/Ross_Geller)) for what became her most famous role, lasting for ten seasons until 2004. The series is commonly referred to as one of the greatest of all sitcoms. According to the *[Guinness Book of World Records](/wiki/Guinness_Book_of_World_Records)* (2005), Cox (along with her female co-stars) became the highest-paid TV actress of all time, with her US$1 million-per-episode fee for the final two seasons of *Friends*. Syndication of the series earned Cox and her co-stars an estimated $20 million in annual residuals. In 1995, she was cast in [Toad the Wet Sprocket](/wiki/Toad_the_Wet_Sprocket)'s music video "Good Intentions". The song was also featured on the [*Friends* soundtrack](/wiki/Friends_Soundtrack).

Between seasons five and six, she married [David Arquette](/wiki/David_Arquette) and consequently changed her name to Courteney Cox Arquette. A joke reference to this is made in the opening credits of the episode "[The One After Vegas](/wiki/The_One_After_Vegas)", where the rest of the cast has "Arquette" added to their names. The dedication "For Courteney and David, who did get married" – a reference to Monica and Chandler's decision not to marry in the episode – appears during the fade out to the tag scene.

Cox received further recognition and critical acclaim for her starring role as reporter [Gale Weathers](/wiki/Gale_Weathers) in the high-profile slasher horror film *[Scream](/wiki/Scream_(1996_film))* (1996), and its sequels *[Scream 2](/wiki/Scream_2)* (1997) and *[Scream 3](/wiki/Scream_3)* (2000). The series is one of the [highest grossing](/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_horror_films) and critically acclaimed horror franchises of all time. Cox's character was well known for her "snappy remarks and being brilliantly bossy". She met her future ex-husband, David Arquette, who played her on-screen love interest, Dwight "Dewey" Riley, while filming the first *Scream* film. He also was in an episode of the TV Show Friends  Cox also hosted an episode of the variety sketch series *[Saturday Night Live](/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live)* in April 1995, and appeared in the crime thriller film *[The Runner](/wiki/The_Runner_(1999_film))* (1999).

### 2000s: Continued success

Cox's major films during this period include the crime drama *[3000 Miles to Graceland](/wiki/3000_Miles_to_Graceland)* (2001) and the comedy *[The Shrink Is In](/wiki/The_Shrink_Is_In)* (2001). In late 2003, Cox and Arquette produced one season of the reality television series *Mix It Up.* The lifestyle series, which aired on the WE cable channel, struggled with low ratings and was not renewed for a second season. After the conclusion of *Friends*, Cox was producer [Marc Cherry](/wiki/Marc_Cherry)'s first choice to be offered a starring role as [Susan Mayer](/wiki/Susan_Mayer) on *[Desperate Housewives](/wiki/Desperate_Housewives)*, but Cox was unavailable due to her pregnancy and the role later went to [Teri Hatcher](/wiki/Teri_Hatcher). A few years later, Cox signed a deal with [ABC Studios](/wiki/ABC_Studios) (formerly Touchstone Television) to star in her own series.

She starred in the [independent](/wiki/Independent_film) drama film *[November](/wiki/November_(2004_film))* (2005), which had a limited theatrical release. She had a [cameo appearance](/wiki/Cameo_appearance) in the big-budget remake *[The Longest Yard](/wiki/The_Longest_Yard_(2005_film))* (2005) as Lena, the girlfriend of Paul Crewe ([Adam Sandler](/wiki/Adam_Sandler)), and co-starred with [Tim Allen](/wiki/Tim_Allen) in the critically derided *[Zoom](/wiki/Zoom_(2006_film))* (2006). Cox voiced Daisy the Cow in the animated film *[Barnyard](/wiki/Barnyard_(film))* (2006). A *Friends* reunion film was rumored to be in production following the success of *[Sex and the City](/wiki/Sex_and_the_City_(film))* (2008), but this was later [denied](/wiki/Friends#Film_rumors) by [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.) and others.
Cox starred as Lucy Spiller, a cynical [tabloid](/wiki/Tabloid_(newspaper_format)) editor, in the [FX](/wiki/FX_(TV_channel)) television drama series *[Dirt](/wiki/Dirt_(TV_series))*, which premiered in 2007. Cox and her then-husband David Arquette were the executive producers of the series. The series was eventually canceled after the second season in 2008. In July 2008, *[Entertainment Weekly](/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly)* announced that Cox signed on to star in a three-episode arc for the television medical comedy series *[Scrubs](/wiki/Scrubs_(TV_series))*. Also that year, she starred in the fantasy comedy film *[Bedtime Stories](/wiki/Bedtime_Stories_(film))*, reuniting with co-star Adam Sandler. She went to executive produce the short drama film *[The Butler's in Love](/wiki/The_Butler%27s_in_Love)*, directed by David Arquette.

Cox in 2009

Cox guest-starred in a three-episode [story arc](/wiki/Story_arc) on former *Friends* co-star [Lisa Kudrow](/wiki/Lisa_Kudrow)'s web comedy series *[Web Therapy](/wiki/Web_Therapy)* (2009). Also in 2009, she began her role as the star of the [single-camera](/wiki/Single-camera) ABC comedy series *[Cougar Town](/wiki/Cougar_Town)*, playing a newly single 40-year-old mother on the hunt for new experiences. It is notably Cox's most successful work since *Friends*, earning her a nomination for the [Golden Globe Award for Best Actress – Television Series Musical or Comedy](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actress_%E2%80%93_Television_Series_Musical_or_Comedy). The series' third season was set to premiere in November 2011 but moved to February 14, 2012. Cox directed two episodes of the series' fifteen episodes that season. The fourth season premiered on January 8, 2013. The series came to an end on March 31, 2015, after six seasons.

### 2010s: Directorial debut and expansion

Cox reprised her role as Gale Weathers from the *Scream* trilogy for the sequel *[Scream 4](/wiki/Scream_4)*. The film was released in theaters on April 15, 2011. She made her directorial debut and had a cameo appearance in a [Lifetime](/wiki/Lifetime_(TV_network)) television drama film *[TalhotBlond](/wiki/TalhotBlond_(2012_film))*, which premiered on the network on June 23, 2012.

In 2014, Cox directed and produced the black comedy film *[Just Before I Go](/wiki/Just_Before_I_Go)*, starring [Seann William Scott](/wiki/Seann_William_Scott) and [Elisha Cuthbert](/wiki/Elisha_Cuthbert). The film premiered at the [Tribeca Film Festival](/wiki/Tribeca_Film_Festival) on April 24, 2014, and it was released in select theaters on April 24, 2015. From 2014 to 2017, she executive produced the syndicated game show *[Celebrity Name Game](/wiki/Celebrity_Name_Game_(American_game_show))*, hosted by [Craig Ferguson](/wiki/Craig_Ferguson). It ended after three seasons. The series earned her a nomination for the [Daytime Emmy Award for Outstanding Game Show](/wiki/Daytime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Game_Show).

In 2016, Cox starred in the independent drama film *[Mothers and Daughters](/wiki/Mothers_and_Daughters_(2016_film))*, alongside [Susan Sarandon](/wiki/Susan_Sarandon), [Mira Sorvino](/wiki/Mira_Sorvino), and [Sharon Stone](/wiki/Sharon_Stone). The film was released to generally negative reviews on May 6, 2016. In 2019, she created and executive produced the [Facebook Watch](/wiki/Facebook_Watch) documentary series *9 Months with Courteney Cox*, which focuses on "people from across the country of various race, religion, and class as they self-document their 9-month journey of pregnancy". It lasted for three seasons until 2021. In 2019, it was reported she would executive produce and star as Brittany Wagner in the [Spectrum Originals](/wiki/Spectrum_Originals) adaptation of the documentary series *[Last Chance U](/wiki/Last_Chance_U)*.

### 2020s: Current work

In 2020, she guest starred in the ABC sitcom *[Modern Family](/wiki/Modern_Family)*. Cox reunited with her *Friends* co-stars for a reunion special titled *[Friends: The Reunion](/wiki/Friends:_The_Reunion)*, which was released on May 27, 2021, on [HBO Max](/wiki/HBO_Max). The special earned Cox a nomination for the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Variety Special (Pre-Recorded)](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Variety_Special_(Pre-Recorded)). In the same year she launched her line of home products, named Homecourt. She also participated in the *Celebrity Escape Room* special to raise $150,000 for Red Nose Day.

Cox reprised her role as Gale Weathers for the [fifth *Scream* film](/wiki/Scream_(2022_film)), which was directed by [Matt Bettinelli-Olpin](/wiki/Matt_Bettinelli-Olpin) and [Tyler Gillett](/wiki/Tyler_Gillett). The film was released on January 14, 2022, to box office success and positive reviews. Also in 2022, she signed on to star alongside [Greg Kinnear](/wiki/Greg_Kinnear) in the [Starz](/wiki/Starz) horror comedy series *[Shining Vale](/wiki/Shining_Vale)*, from creators [Sharon Horgan](/wiki/Sharon_Horgan) and Jeff Astrof; she plays Patricia "Pat" Phelps, who moves her family "from the 'crazy' of the city to a large, old house in the suburbs where evil and humor collide."

Star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame

Cox again reprised her role as Gale Weathers for the [sixth *Scream* film](/wiki/Scream_VI), which she also executive produced. The film was released on March 9, 2023, to box office success and positive reviews. Also in 2023, she received a star on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame).

## Personal life

Cox dated actor [Michael Keaton](/wiki/Michael_Keaton) from 1989 to 1995. Cox married her *Scream* co-star [David Arquette](/wiki/David_Arquette) on June 12, 1999, at [Grace Cathedral](/wiki/Grace_Cathedral,_San_Francisco) in [San Francisco](/wiki/San_Francisco). The couple have a daughter, born in June 2004. [Jennifer Aniston](/wiki/Jennifer_Aniston) is her [godmother](/wiki/Godparent). Cox revealed that she suffered from [postpartum depression](/wiki/Postpartum_depression) six months after the birth of her child. On October 11, 2010, Cox and Arquette announced that they had separated, although they still maintain a close friendship and ongoing business relationship in [Coquette Productions](/wiki/Coquette_Productions). In June 2012, Arquette filed for divorce after nearly two years of separation from Cox. The divorce became final in May 2014. Cox began dating [Snow Patrol](/wiki/Snow_Patrol) band member [Johnny McDaid](/wiki/Johnny_McDaid) in late 2013. The couple announced their engagement in June 2014. Soon afterward they called off the engagement, but they have remained a couple.

In 2017, Cox was the subject of an episode of the television programme *[Who Do You Think You Are?](/wiki/Who_Do_You_Think_You_Are%3F_(American_TV_series)#Season_8_(2017))*, during which she learned that she is a direct descendant of [William the Conqueror](/wiki/William_the_Conqueror).

In 2020, Cox said that she did not remember much about the episode plots of *Friends* and that she had begun binge-watching the show. Cox is a practitioner of [Budokan karate](/wiki/Budokan_karate).

**Freddie James Prinze Jr.** (born March 8, 1976) is an American actor. He has starred in films such as *[I Know What You Did Last Summer](/wiki/I_Know_What_You_Did_Last_Summer)* (1997) and its sequels *[I Still Know What You Did Last Summer](/wiki/I_Still_Know_What_You_Did_Last_Summer)* (1998) and *[I Know What You Did Last Summer](/wiki/I_Know_What_You_Did_Last_Summer_(2025_film))* (2025), *[She's All That](/wiki/She%27s_All_That)* (1999), *[Down to You](/wiki/Down_to_You)*, *[Boys and Girls](/wiki/Boys_and_Girls_(2000_film))* (both 2000), *[Summer Catch](/wiki/Summer_Catch)* (2001), *[Scooby-Doo](/wiki/Scooby-Doo_(film))* (2002), and *[Scooby-Doo 2: Monsters Unleashed](/wiki/Scooby-Doo_2:_Monsters_Unleashed)* (2004). Alongside recurring roles on *[Boston Legal](/wiki/Boston_Legal)* (2004) and *[24](/wiki/24_(TV_series))* (2010), Prinze starred on the self-titled [ABC](/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company) sitcom *[Freddie](/wiki/Freddie_(TV_series))* (2005–2006)—which he co-created and executive produced—and voiced [Kanan Jarrus](/wiki/Kanan_Jarrus) in the [Disney XD](/wiki/Disney_XD) series *[Star Wars Rebels](/wiki/Star_Wars_Rebels)* (2014–2018) and the film *[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker](/wiki/Star_Wars:_The_Rise_of_Skywalker)* (2019). Prinze also worked for [WWE](/wiki/WWE) as a writer from 2007–2009 and then as a producer from 2010–2012. He is the only child of actor and comedian [Freddie Prinze](/wiki/Freddie_Prinze).

## Early life

Freddie James Prinze Jr. was born in Los Angeles on March 8, 1976, the only child of Katherine Elaine Prinze (*née* Cochran) and actor and [stand-up comedian](/wiki/Stand-up_comedian) [Freddie Prinze](/wiki/Freddie_Prinze).

On January 29, 1977, Freddie Prinze died of a self-inflicted gunshot wound, after which Prinze Jr. relocated with his mother to [Albuquerque, New Mexico](/wiki/Albuquerque,_New_Mexico). Prinze was raised Catholic. His paternal grandmother was from Puerto Rico and he is fluent in Spanish. Prinze had enjoyed acting in school productions as a child and participated in Albuquerque Children's Theatre, though he originally planned to become a civil engineer before getting roles on *[Family Matters](/wiki/Family_Matters)* and *The Watcher*. Soon after that, he saw [Neil Patrick Harris](/wiki/Neil_Patrick_Harris) at his high school getting kids excited about acting, and decided to become an actor. After graduating from [La Cueva High School](/wiki/La_Cueva_High_School) in 1994, Prinze moved to Los Angeles to audition for television roles.

## Career

### Television and film

Prinze was cast in a guest role on the ABC TV series *Family Matters* in 1995. He then appeared in a few programs and made-for-TV movies, before making his motion picture debut in *[To Gillian on Her 37th Birthday](/wiki/To_Gillian_on_Her_37th_Birthday)* in 1996. In subsequent years, Prinze's appearances in youth-oriented movies *[I Know What You Did Last Summer](/wiki/I_Know_What_You_Did_Last_Summer)* (1997) and its sequel *[I Still Know What You Did Last Summer](/wiki/I_Still_Know_What_You_Did_Last_Summer)* (1998) made him known to teenage audiences. His first leading role was in the romantic comedy *[She's All That](/wiki/She%27s_All_That)* (1999), which grossed $63 million in the United States.

Subsequently, he had leading roles in *[Wing Commander](/wiki/Wing_Commander_(film))* (1999), *[Down to You](/wiki/Down_to_You)* (2000), *[Boys and Girls](/wiki/Boys_and_Girls_(2000_film))* (2000), *[Head over Heels](/wiki/Head_over_Heels_(2001_film))* (2001), and *[Summer Catch](/wiki/Summer_Catch)* (2001), most of which were disliked by critics and had moderate box office success.

Prinze Jr. at the 2009 [San Diego Comic-Con](/wiki/San_Diego_Comic-Con)

He played [Fred Jones](/wiki/Fred_Jones_(Scooby-Doo)) in the 2002 [live-action film version](/wiki/Scooby-Doo_(film)) of the popular cartoon *[Scooby-Doo](/wiki/Scooby-Doo)*, and reprised the role in the 2004 sequel, *[Scooby-Doo 2: Monsters Unleashed](/wiki/Scooby-Doo_2:_Monsters_Unleashed)*, both alongside his wife [Sarah Michelle Gellar](/wiki/Sarah_Michelle_Gellar) and along with [Matthew Lillard](/wiki/Matthew_Lillard) and [Linda Cardellini](/wiki/Linda_Cardellini). He guest starred on the popular NBC show *[Friends](/wiki/Friends)* as a sensitive male nanny named Sandy on the series' 200th episode. He took the role of Sandy after [Tom Hanks](/wiki/Tom_Hanks) could not do it due to scheduling. He also appeared as [Donny Crane](/wiki/List_of_Boston_Legal_characters), a character believed to be [Denny Crane](/wiki/List_of_Boston_Legal_characters)'s son in the ABC legal drama-comedy *[Boston Legal](/wiki/Boston_Legal)*. Prinze starred in his own television [sitcom](/wiki/Sitcom), titled *[Freddie](/wiki/Freddie_(TV_series))*. The sitcom is said to depict some actual events from his life, and was cancelled after one season in May 2006. He guest starred on *[George Lopez](/wiki/George_Lopez_(TV_series))* for a crossover with *Freddie*. In 2004, Prinze accepted a special award from [TV Land](/wiki/TV_Land) on behalf of his late father. He thanked his father's former co-star [Della Reese](/wiki/Della_Reese) for her continued advice and support. In 2006, he lent his voice to the character of Pi in the animated film *[Shark Bait](/wiki/Shark_Bait_(2006_film))*. Also in 2007, he lent his voice to the character of Rick in *[Happily N'Ever After](/wiki/Happily_N%27Ever_After)* along with his wife Gellar, [Wallace Shawn](/wiki/Wallace_Shawn), [Andy Dick](/wiki/Andy_Dick), [George Carlin](/wiki/George_Carlin), and [Sigourney Weaver](/wiki/Sigourney_Weaver). In 2008, he auditioned for [Jigsaw](/wiki/Jigsaw_(Marvel_Comics)) in *[Punisher: War Zone](/wiki/Punisher:_War_Zone)*, but was not given the part at the decision of [Lionsgate Studios](/wiki/Lionsgate_Studios). He later was the voice of the titular character in the animated movie *[Delgo](/wiki/Delgo_(film))*. In 2010, Prinze guest starred on *[Psych](/wiki/Psych)* as Dennis, a grade school friend of [Shawn Spencer](/wiki/Shawn_Spencer) and [Burton Guster](/wiki/Gus_(Psych)). In March 2019, Prinze was cast as [Nancy Drew](/wiki/Nancy_Drew)'s father, Carson Drew, in [The CW](/wiki/The_CW) mystery [pilot](/wiki/Television_pilot) *[Nancy Drew](/wiki/Nancy_Drew_(2019_TV_series))*, but was later replaced by [Scott Wolf](/wiki/Scott_Wolf). He also voiced the future version of Tim and Jim Possible in the *[Kim Possible](/wiki/Kim_Possible)* movie *[Kim Possible: A Sitch in Time](/wiki/Kim_Possible:_A_Sitch_in_Time)*.

In March 2009, it was announced that Prinze had signed on to star as Bradley, aka Ultimatum, in the ABC show *No Heroics*, an American remake of the [British show of the same name](/wiki/No_Heroics). The show was not picked up, but Prinze was cast as a series regular for [the eighth season](/wiki/24_(season_8)) of the television show *[24](/wiki/24_(TV_series))*; he played [Cole Ortiz](/wiki/Cole_Ortiz), a new [CTU](/wiki/CTU_Los_Angeles) operative. In 2021, Prinze joined the [reboot](/wiki/Punky_Brewster_(2021_TV_series)) of *[Punky Brewster](/wiki/Punky_Brewster_(2021_TV_series))* on [Peacock](/wiki/Peacock_(streaming_service)), playing the title character's ex-husband.

### Other work

Prinze and [Chris Klein](/wiki/Chris_Klein_(actor)) appeared in the 2002-03 [West End](/wiki/West_End,_London) production of [Kenneth Lonergan's](/wiki/Kenneth_Lonergan) play *[This Is Our Youth](/wiki/This_Is_Our_Youth)*, replacing original cast members [Jake Gyllenhaal](/wiki/Jake_Gyllenhaal) and [Hayden Christensen](/wiki/Hayden_Christensen).

He voiced a pilot in a *[Vatta's War: Trading in Danger](/wiki/Vatta%27s_War)* graphic audio book. Prinze voiced different characters in [BioWare](/wiki/BioWare) video games: [Lieutenant](/wiki/Lieutenant) [James Vega](/wiki/James_Vega) in *[Mass Effect 3](/wiki/Mass_Effect_3)* and [The Iron Bull](/wiki/Iron_Bull) in *[Dragon Age: Inquisition](/wiki/Dragon_Age:_Inquisition)*. He returned to the role of James Vega again for the animated feature film *[Mass Effect: Paragon Lost](/wiki/Mass_Effect:_Paragon_Lost)*, dubbed by [FUNimation](/wiki/FUNimation). From 2014 until 2018, he was the voice of Kanan Jarrus, one of the last surviving [Jedi Knights](/wiki/Jedi), on the [Disney XD](/wiki/Disney_XD) series *[Star Wars Rebels](/wiki/Star_Wars_Rebels)*. He reprised the role for the opening scene of the pilot episode of the [Disney+](/wiki/Disney%2B) series *The Bad Batch* in May 2021. In March 2023, he launched a new podcast, *That Was Pretty Scary*, with co-host Jon Lee Brody.

### Professional wrestling

#### Work with WWE (2008–2009, 2010–2012)

Prinze is a [WWE](/wiki/WWE) fan and was seen on television in attendance at the March 2008 [WrestleMania XXIV](/wiki/WrestleMania_XXIV) [pay-per-view](/wiki/Pay-per-view) and its preceding [Hall of Fame](/wiki/WWE_Hall_of_Fame) ceremony. He also made a [cameo](/wiki/Cameo_appearance) on an episode of *The Dirt Sheet*, an online program hosted by wrestlers [John Morrison and The Miz](/wiki/John_Morrison_and_The_Miz). He had created an official profile on WWE's "Universe" blog community where he would regularly write his thoughts about the current goings-on in the world of the WWE. His relationship with the company was furthered when he was hired as a member of the creative staff to contribute to weekly television and pay-per-view programming for the [SmackDown](/wiki/WWE_Friday_Night_SmackDown) brand. It was reported on February 22, 2009, that Prinze and WWE had parted ways, but in January 2021, Prinze explained that he had actually chosen to leave the company at that time.

On August 17, 2009, Prinze appeared on *[Raw](/wiki/WWE_Raw)* as a [special guest host](/wiki/List_of_WWE_Raw_guest_stars), where he was assaulted by then [WWE Champion](/wiki/WWE_Championship) [Randy Orton](/wiki/Randy_Orton) after refusing to bail Orton out of his [tag team match](/wiki/Tag_team_match) with [John Cena](/wiki/John_Cena), who was due to challenge him in the upcoming [SummerSlam](/wiki/SummerSlam_(2009)). Prinze later returned to the program and got his payback during Orton's match with Cena against [Big Show](/wiki/Big_Show) and [Chris Jericho](/wiki/Chris_Jericho), by setting up a [lumberjack match](/wiki/Professional_wrestling_match_types#Lumberjack_match) involving [Mark Henry](/wiki/Mark_Henry), [Primo](/wiki/Eddie_Col%C3%B3n), [Evan Bourne](/wiki/Evan_Bourne), [Kofi Kingston](/wiki/Kofi_Kingston), [MVP](/wiki/Montel_Vontavious_Porter), and [Jamie Noble](/wiki/Jamie_Noble).

Prinze returned to WWE on October 1, 2010, in a role as a producer and director. On the November 1, 2010 episode of *Monday Night Raw*, Prinze made an on-screen appearance as [Vince McMahon](/wiki/Vince_McMahon)'s doctor, a [dream sequence](/wiki/Dream_sequence) scene that coincided with [Linda McMahon](/wiki/Linda_McMahon)'s attempt to win a seat in the Senate for the state of Connecticut. Prinze left WWE for a second time due to a comment made by [Stone Cold Steve Austin](/wiki/Stone_Cold_Steve_Austin) to a contestant while taping an episode of the fifth season of *[WWE Tough Enough](/wiki/WWE_Tough_Enough)*.

#### Podcast (since 2021)

In November 2021, Prinze started his own podcast *Wrestling With Freddie* with cohost [Jeff Dye](/wiki/Jeff_Dye).

### Premier Streaming Network

In April 2023, Freddie Prinze Jr. joined the ownership team of Premier Streaming Network, a streaming platform focused on wrestling, sports, and entertainment content.

### MyFandom Sports App

In June 2024, Freddie Prinze Jr. was announced as part of the ownership team behind MyFandom, an iOS app co-founded by brothers Fred and Josh Shernoff. MyFandom allows sports fans to contribute personal media to a collective archive of sporting events, creating a user-generated historical record while enabling individuals to curate their own attendance history.

## Personal life

Prinze Jr. with his wife [Sarah Michelle Gellar](/wiki/Sarah_Michelle_Gellar) at the 2007 [Tribeca Film Festival](/wiki/Tribeca_Film_Festival)

Prinze dated actress [Kimberly McCullough](/wiki/Kimberly_McCullough) from 1996 until 1999. He married actress [Sarah Michelle Gellar](/wiki/Sarah_Michelle_Gellar) on September 1, 2002, in [Puerto Vallarta, Jalisco](/wiki/Puerto_Vallarta,_Jalisco), Mexico. The couple met several years before, while filming *[I Know What You Did Last Summer](/wiki/I_Know_What_You_Did_Last_Summer)* (1997), started dating in 2000 and were engaged in April 2001. They co-starred in *Scooby-Doo* (2002), *Scooby-Doo 2: Monsters Unleashed* (2004), *Happily N'Ever After* (2006), *Star Wars Rebels* (2014–2018), and [the 2025 sequel](/wiki/I_Know_What_You_Did_Last_Summer_(2025_film)) to *I Know What You Did Last Summer* that bore the same title. Gellar made a non-speaking cameo in Prinze's film *[She's All That](/wiki/She%27s_All_That)* (1999). They have two children together: a daughter born September 19, 2009, and a son born September 21, 2012.

Prinze is an avid practitioner of [Brazilian jiu-jitsu](/wiki/Brazilian_jiu-jitsu) and has reached the rank of purple belt in the sport, having been promoted under [Jean Jacques Machado](/wiki/Jean_Jacques_Machado).

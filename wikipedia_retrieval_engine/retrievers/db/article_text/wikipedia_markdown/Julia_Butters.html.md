# Julia Butters

**Julia Butters** (born April 15, 2009) is an American actress. She began her career at the age of five, and starred as Trudi Fraser in the film *[Once Upon a Time in Hollywood](/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Hollywood)* (2019), for which she was nominated for the [Critics' Choice Movie Award for Best Young Actor/Actress](/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Movie_Award_for_Best_Young_Performer), and as Reggie Fabelman in *[The Fabelmans](/wiki/The_Fabelmans)* (2022). She rose to prominence for playing Anna-Kat Otto in *[American Housewife](/wiki/American_Housewife)* (2016–2020), and for playing Harper Coleman in *[Freakier Friday](/wiki/Freakier_Friday)* (2025).

## Early life

Butters was born on April 15, 2009, in Los Angeles. Her father Darrin Butters is a [Disney animator](/wiki/Walt_Disney_Animation_Studios) who has worked on films such as *[Frozen](/wiki/Frozen_(2013_film))* and *[Ralph Breaks the Internet](/wiki/Ralph_Breaks_the_Internet)*.

## Career

Julia Butters began her career doing commercials. Her first speaking role was the role of Gabby in *[Criminal Minds](/wiki/Criminal_Minds)*. In 2016, Butters portrayed the recurring role of Ella in the [Amazon Prime Video](/wiki/Amazon_Prime_Video) series *[Transparent](/wiki/Transparent_(TV_series))*. Later that year, she began starring in the [ABC](/wiki/ABC_Studios) sitcom *[American Housewife](/wiki/American_Housewife)* as Anna-Kat Otto. While [Quentin Tarantino](/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino) was writing the script of his ninth film *[Once Upon a Time in Hollywood](/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Hollywood)*, he spotted Butters on television, and later cast her in the role of precocious child actress Trudi Fraser. After she received critical acclaim for this role, Butters decided to pursue similar opportunities, leading her to leave *American Housewife* after four seasons. In December 2020, Butters was cast in [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix)'s *[The Gray Man](/wiki/The_Gray_Man_(2022_film))*.

In June 2021, it was announced that Butters was cast in *[The Fabelmans](/wiki/The_Fabelmans)*, [Steven Spielberg](/wiki/Steven_Spielberg)'s semi-autobiographical coming-of-age film as Reggie Fabelman, a character based on Spielberg's sister [Anne](/wiki/Anne_Spielberg). In August 2022, Butters was cast as Lily in the film *Queen of Bones*. In 2024, she was cast in *[Freakier Friday](/wiki/Freakier_Friday)* (2025), the sequel to the 2003 film *[Freaky Friday](/wiki/Freaky_Friday_(2003_film))*. She played Harper Coleman, the daughter of Anna Coleman ([Lindsay Lohan](/wiki/Lindsay_Lohan)) and granddaughter of Tess Coleman ([Jamie Lee Curtis](/wiki/Jamie_Lee_Curtis)).

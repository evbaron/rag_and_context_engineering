**USS *Mizpah* (PY-29)** was a [United States Navy](/wiki/United_States_Navy) [patrol yacht](/wiki/Armed_yacht). Constructed in 1926, the vessel was constructed as the [pleasure yacht](/wiki/Yacht) *Savarona*. In 1929 it was renamed *Allegro* and then *Mizpah* for use on the [Great Lakes](/wiki/Great_Lakes). The vessel was acquired by the United States Navy in 1942 and converted to a warship and [commissioned](/wiki/Ship_commissioning) the same year. *Mizpah* served as a [convoy](/wiki/Convoy) escort along the United States East Coast before becoming a [school ship](/wiki/School_ship) in 1944. Following the end of the war, the vessel returned to private operation in 1946 until 1967 when *Mizpah* was laid up with a broken [crankshaft](/wiki/Crankshaft) at [Tampa](/wiki/Tampa), Florida. An attempt to save the ship proved futile and *Mizpah* was [scuttled](/wiki/Scuttling) off the coast of Florida as an [artificial reef](/wiki/Artificial_reef) in 1968. The wreck is now a popular [dive site](/wiki/Scuba_diving).

## Service history

The 185-foot (56 m) ship was berthed in 1926 from the parts of an abandoned new [destroyer](/wiki/Destroyer), as the [pleasure yacht](/wiki/Yacht) *Savanarola* by the [Newport News Shipbuilding and Drydock Company](/wiki/Newport_News_Shipbuilding_and_Drydock_Company) of [Newport News](/wiki/Newport_News,_Virginia), Virginia. The yacht, originally constructed for the United States Navy but due to naval treaties was prevented from being completed. The still unbuilt vessel was sold to Mrs. Cadwalader of [Philadelphia](/wiki/Philadelphia), Pennsylvania. After completion the vessel was given the name *Sequoia* before being sold to James Elverson who renamed it *Allegro*. In 1929 it was sold to [Eugene F. McDonald](/wiki/Eugene_F._McDonald) of [Philadelphia](/wiki/Philadelphia), Pennsylvania, founder and president of the [Zenith Radio Corporation](/wiki/Zenith_Radio_Corporation), who used it both as a [Chicago](/wiki/Chicago) residence and a floating laboratory on which to test his electronics company's new products. One of the largest yachts on the [Great Lakes](/wiki/Great_Lakes) in its Jazz Age heyday, the ship was renamed *Mizpah* in 1929.

### United States Navy service

USS *Mizpah* in drydock, undergoing conversion

*Mizpah* was acquired by the [United States Navy](/wiki/United_States_Navy) on 16 March 1942 and converted to a warship at [Sturgeon Bay Shipbuilding Company](/wiki/Sturgeon_Bay_Shipbuilding_Company), [Sturgeon Bay](/wiki/Sturgeon_Bay,_Wisconsin), Wisconsin, with Lieutenant [Stephen M. Etnier](/wiki/Stephen_M._Etnier) in command. It was [commissioned](/wiki/Ship_commissioning) USS *Mizpah* (PY-29) on 26 October 1942. *Mizpah* departed Sturgeon Bay on 16 November 1942 for service as a [convoy](/wiki/Convoy) escort along the eastern coast of the United States between [New York City](/wiki/New_York_City) and [Key West](/wiki/Key_West,_Florida), Florida until July 1944. From August 1944 until April 1945, *Mizpah* served as a navigation [school ship](/wiki/School_ship) from the Amphibious Training Base at [Little Creek](/wiki/Little_Creek,_Virginia), Virginia, training officers to sail amphibious vessels in the [Chesapeake Bay](/wiki/Chesapeake_Bay) region.

*Mizpah* underwent conversion to a [flagship](/wiki/Flagship) at [Boston](/wiki/Boston), Massachusetts and was employed as such by Destroyer Force, [Atlantic Fleet](/wiki/United_States_Fleet_Forces_Command). *Mizpah* became the flagship of Rear Admiral Oliver M. Hustvedt at [Portland](/wiki/Portland,_Maine), Maine, on 28 May 1945 under the command of Lt. Stephen M. Etnier. Hustvedt was succeeded by Rear Admiral Frank E. Beatty, Jr., on 4 September 1945. In mid-October, 1945, Lt. Etnier was succeeded by Lieutenant D. Dudley Bloom, who sailed *Mizpah* from Portland, Maine, south to [Charleston](/wiki/Charleston,_South_Carolina), South Carolina, arriving on 10 December 1945. She was [decommissioned](/wiki/Ship_decommissioning) on 16 January 1946 and transferred to the United States War Shipping Administration (WSA) on 25 September 1946 for disposal.

### Final years

The WSA then sold her to a private Honduran corporation for transporting bananas out of South America. While sailing to [Tampa](/wiki/Tampa,_Florida), Florida, the vessel was caught in a storm, suffered a broken [crankshaft](/wiki/Crankshaft), and was laid up in Tampa for repair. At that time, Eugene Kinney, McDonald's nephew and Zenith Corporation vice president who had grown up on *Mizpah* and served as a naval officer in the [South Pacific](/wiki/Australasia) during [World War II](/wiki/World_War_II), learned of her plight and purchased her. Finding *Mizpah* irreparably damaged, however, Kinney [scuttled](/wiki/Scuttling) her off the coast of Palm Beach, Florida, on 9 April 1968, along with another ship, USS *PC-1174*, to serve as an [artificial](/wiki/Artificial) reef to prevent [beach erosion](/wiki/Beach_erosion).

Sitting in 90–110 feet (27–34 m) of water with her hatches left ajar, *Mizpah* is now one of the chief attractions of an offshore scuba diving area known as "The Mizpah Corridor".

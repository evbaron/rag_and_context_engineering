# South Korea at the 2006 Winter Olympics

## Infobox

- **IOC code:** KOR
- **NOC:** Korean Olympic Committee
- **Website:** www.sports.or.kr (in Korean and English)
- **Competitors:** 40 in 9 sports
- **Flag bearers:** Lee Bo-ra (opening) Jeon Da-hye (closing) [ 1 ] [ 2 ]
- **Medals Ranked 7th:** Gold 6 Silver 3 Bronze 2 Total 11

Athletes from South Korea marched with athletes from North Korea in the opening ceremonies under the [Korean Unification Flag](/wiki/Korean_Unification_Flag).

**[South Korea](/wiki/South_Korea)** competed as *Republic of Korea* at the **[2006 Winter Olympics](/wiki/2006_Winter_Olympics)**, while [North Korea](/wiki/North_Korea) competed as the *[Democratic People's Republic of Korea](/wiki/North_Korea_at_the_2006_Winter_Olympics)*. At the opening ceremony, the athletes of both North and South Korea entered the stadium together behind the [Korean Unification Flag](/wiki/Korean_Unification_Flag).

## Medalists

- In terms of medal count, South Korea is the only Asian country in the top ten.
- Ten of the eleven medals were won in short track speed skating. Short track speedskaters [Jin Sun-yu](/wiki/Jin_Sun-yu) and [Ahn Hyun-soo](/wiki/Viktor_An) were the second and third people to win three gold medals each in Turin. Ahn also won a bronze.

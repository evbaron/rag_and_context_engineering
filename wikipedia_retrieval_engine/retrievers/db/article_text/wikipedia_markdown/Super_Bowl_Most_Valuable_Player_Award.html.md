# Super Bowl Most Valuable Player

## Infobox

- **Awarded for:** Most valuable player of the Super Bowl
- **Presented by:** SPORT (1967–1989) NFL (1990–present)
- **First award:** 1967
- **Most wins:** Tom Brady (5)
- **Most recent:** Kenneth Walker III

"Pete Rozelle Trophy" redirects here; not to be confused with [Pete Rozelle Radio-Television Award](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pete_Rozelle_Radio-Television_Award).

The **Super Bowl Most Valuable Player** (**MVP**) award is presented annually to the [most valuable player](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Most_valuable_player) of the [Super Bowl](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl), the [National Football League](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Football_League)'s (NFL) championship game. The winner is chosen by a panel of 16 [football](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_football) writers and broadcasters, and, since [Super Bowl XXXV](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XXXV) in 2001, fans voting electronically. The media panel's ballots count for 80 percent of the vote tally, while the viewers' ballots make up the other 20 percent. The game's viewing audience can vote on the Internet, or by using [cell phones](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_phone). Media voters are asked to vote with about five minutes remaining in the game, but are allowed to change their mind when the game ends. They can nominate one player from each team, with instructions to count their vote for the player on the winning team. Voters cannot select an entire unit.

The Super Bowl MVP has been awarded annually since the game's inception in 1967. Through 1989, the award was presented by *[Sport](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sport_(American_magazine))* magazine. [Bart Starr](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bart_Starr) was the MVP of the first two Super Bowls. Since 1990, the award has been presented by the NFL. At [Super Bowl XXV](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XXV), the league first awarded the **Pete Rozelle Trophy**, named after former NFL commissioner [Pete Rozelle](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pete_Rozelle), to the Super Bowl MVP. [Ottis Anderson](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottis_Anderson) was the first to win the trophy. The most recent Super Bowl MVP, from [Super Bowl LX](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LX), is [Seattle Seahawks](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Seahawks) [running back](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Running_back) [Kenneth Walker III](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Walker_III).

[Tom Brady](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Brady) is the only player to have won five Super Bowl MVP awards (four with the [New England Patriots](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_England_Patriots) and one with the [Tampa Bay Buccaneers](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tampa_Bay_Buccaneers)); [Joe Montana](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Montana) and [Patrick Mahomes](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Mahomes) won three times and three other players—[Bart Starr](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bart_Starr), [Terry Bradshaw](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terry_Bradshaw) and [Eli Manning](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eli_Manning)—have won the award twice. Starr, Bradshaw, and Mahomes are the only ones to have won it in back-to-back years. The MVP has come from the winning team every year except 1971, when [Dallas Cowboys](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallas_Cowboys) [linebacker](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linebacker) [Chuck Howley](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuck_Howley) won the award, despite the Cowboys' loss in [Super Bowl V](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_V) to the [Baltimore Colts](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore_Colts). [Harvey Martin](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Martin) and [Randy White](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randy_White_(American_football)) were named co-MVPs of [Super Bowl XII](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XII), the only time co-MVPs have been chosen. Including the Super Bowl XII co-MVPs, seven Cowboys players have won Super Bowl MVP awards, the most of any [NFL team](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NFL_team). Quarterbacks have earned the honor 34 times in 59 games (and 60 awards).

From [Super Bowl I](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_I) to [Super Bowl XLIX](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XLIX) the Super Bowl MVP won a new car as a part of their MVP award. However, since [Hyundai](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyundai_Motor_Company) became the official vehicle partner of the NFL, from the [2015 NFL season](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_NFL_season) onward no new car has been awarded to the Super Bowl MVP since [Super Bowl 50](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_50).

## Winners

[Bart Starr](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bart_Starr) was the MVP of [Super Bowls](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl) [I](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_I) and [II](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_II) while [quarterback](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quarterback) for the [Green Bay Packers](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Bay_Packers)

[Joe Montana](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Montana) won three [Super Bowl](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl) MVP awards as [quarterback](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quarterback) for the [San Francisco 49ers](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_49ers)

[Jerry Rice](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerry_Rice), the MVP of [Super Bowl XXIII](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XXIII), played [wide receiver](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wide_receiver) for the [San Francisco 49ers](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_49ers)

[Desmond Howard](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desmond_Howard), MVP of [Super Bowl XXXI](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XXXI) with the [Green Bay Packers](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Bay_Packers), is the only [special teams](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_teams) player – a [kick](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kick_returner)/[punt returner](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punt_returner) – to win the award

[Tom Brady](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Brady), a [quarterback](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quarterback), has the most MVP awards with five, winning in [Super Bowls](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl) [XXXVI](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XXXVI), [XXXVIII](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XXXVIII), [XLIX](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_XLIX), and [LI](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LI) while playing for the [New England Patriots](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_England_Patriots) and [Super Bowl LV](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LV) playing for the [Tampa Bay Buccaneers](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tampa_Bay_Buccaneers)

[Von Miller](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Miller), MVP of [Super Bowl 50](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_50) with the [Denver Broncos](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denver_Broncos), is the most recent defensive player – a [linebacker](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linebacker) – to win the award

[Patrick Mahomes](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Mahomes), a [quarterback](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quarterback), won three MVP awards in [Super Bowls LIV](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LIV), [LVII](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LVII), and [LVIII](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LVIII) while playing for the [Kansas City Chiefs](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_City_Chiefs)

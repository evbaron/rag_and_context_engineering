**Michel Frank Kratochvil** (born 7 April 1979) is a former [tennis player](/wiki/Tennis_player) from Switzerland. He was a member of the [Swiss Davis Cup Team](/wiki/Swiss_Davis_Cup_Team) between 2000 and 2004 with a singles record 3–9, winning the decider in the first round of the 2003 World Group against [Martin Verkerk](/wiki/Martin_Verkerk) of Netherlands 1–6, 7–6, 7–6, 6–1. He played doubles alongside [Roger Federer](/wiki/Roger_Federer) in the [2002 Gstaad Open](/wiki/2002_Allianz_Suisse_Open_Gstaad_%E2%80%93_Doubles) who was the defending champion but lost in the first round this time. He also defeated reigning champion [Andre Agassi](/wiki/Andre_Agassi) in the first round [2002 Indian Wells Masters](/wiki/2002_Pacific_Life_Open_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_singles).

The **Eggstock** (3,554 m), is a minor prominence of the [Uri Alps](/wiki/Uri_Alps), forming the northern pillar of the [Dammastock](/wiki/Dammastock) on the border between the cantons of [Valais](/wiki/Valais) and [Uri](/wiki/Canton_of_Uri). The [tripoint](/wiki/Tripoint) between the cantons of [Bern](/wiki/Canton_of_Bern), Valais and Uri (3,500 m) is located 600 m west of the summit.

**Vostochnaya Niva** ([Russian](/wiki/Russian_language): Восточная Нива) is a [rural locality](/wiki/Types_of_inhabited_localities_in_Russia) (a [selo](/wiki/Village#Russia)) in Amaransky Selsoviet of [Romnensky District](/wiki/Romnensky_District), [Amur Oblast](/wiki/Amur_Oblast), Russia. The population was 6 as of 2018. There are 2 streets.

## Geography

Vostochnaya Niva is located on the left bank of the Amaranka River, 39 km southeast of [Romny](/wiki/Romny,_Amur_Oblast) (the district's administrative centre) by road. Amaranka is the nearest rural locality.

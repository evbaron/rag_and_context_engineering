# Condor (mountain)

**Condor** (possibly from [Quechua](/wiki/Quechua_language) for [condor](/wiki/Condor)) is a mountain in the [Andes](/wiki/Andes) of [Peru](/wiki/Peru), about 5,200 metres (17,060 ft) high. It is located in the [Arequipa Region](/wiki/Arequipa_Region), [Caylloma Province](/wiki/Caylloma_Province), [Choco District](/wiki/Choco_District). It lies in the [Chila](/wiki/Chila_mountain_range) mountain range north of the [Colca](/wiki/Colca_River) River. Condor is situated at the Umaranra valley. Its stream flows to the Collpamayo (possibly from Quechua for "salpeter river") whose waters feed the Colca River.

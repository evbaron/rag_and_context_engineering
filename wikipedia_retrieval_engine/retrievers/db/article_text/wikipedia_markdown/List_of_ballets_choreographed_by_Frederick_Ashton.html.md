# List of ballets choreographed by Frederick Ashton

The following is a list, by decade, of ballets created by the English [choreographer](/wiki/Choreographer) [Frederick Ashton](/wiki/Frederick_Ashton).

## 1920s

- *[A Tragedy of Fashion](/wiki/A_Tragedy_of_Fashion)* (music by [Eugene Goossens](/wiki/Eugene_Aynsley_Goossens), arranged by [Ernest Irving](/wiki/Ernest_Irving)) (1926)
- *Various dances* for a Purcell Opera Society production of *[The Fairy-Queen](/wiki/The_Fairy-Queen)*: (music by [Henry Purcell](/wiki/Henry_Purcell)) (1927)
- *Pas de deux* (music by [Fritz Kreisler](/wiki/Fritz_Kreisler)) (1927)
- *Suite de danses* (music by [Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart](/wiki/Wolfgang_Amadeus_Mozart)) (1927)
- *Argentine Dance* (music by Artello) (1927)
- *Nymphs and Shepherds* (music by Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart) (1928)
- *Leda* (music by [Christoph Willibald Gluck](/wiki/Christoph_Willibald_Gluck)) (1928)
- *Various dances* for *Jew Süss* (incidental music arranged by [Constant Lambert](/wiki/Constant_Lambert)) (1929)

## 1930s

- *Capriol Suite* (music by [Peter Warlock](/wiki/Peter_Warlock)) (1930)
- *Pomona* (music by Constant Lambert) (1930)
- *Regatta* (music by [Gavin Gordon](/wiki/Gavin_Gordon_(composer))) (1931)
- **La Péri** (music by [Léo Delibes](/wiki/L%C3%A9o_Delibes)) (1931)
- **[Façade](/wiki/Fa%C3%A7ade_(ballet))** (music by [William Walton](/wiki/William_Walton)) (1931)
- *The Lady of Shalott* (music by [Jean Sibelius](/wiki/Jean_Sibelius)) (1931)
- **Foyer de danse** (music by [Lord Berners](/wiki/Gerald_Tyrwhitt-Wilson,_14th_Baron_Berners)) (1932)
- *[Les Masques](/wiki/Les_Masques)* (music by Francis Poulenc) (1933)
- **[Les Rendezvous](/wiki/Les_Rendezvous)** (music by [Daniel Auber](/wiki/Daniel_Auber), arranged by Constant Lambert) (1933)
- **Pavane pour une enfante défunte** (music by [Maurice Ravel](/wiki/Maurice_Ravel)) (1933)
- **Mephisto Valse** (music by [Franz Liszt](/wiki/Franz_Liszt)) (1934)
- **Le Baisier de la fée** (music by [Igor Stravinsky](/wiki/Igor_Stravinsky)) (1935)
- *Apparitions* (music by Franz Liszt, arranged by Constant Lambert and orchestrated by [Gordon Jacob](/wiki/Gordon_Jacob)) (1936)
- *Nocturne* (music by [Frederick Delius](/wiki/Frederick_Delius)) (1936)
- **[Les Patineurs](/wiki/Les_Patineurs_(ballet))** (music by [Giacomo Meyerbeer](/wiki/Giacomo_Meyerbeer), arranged by Constant Lambert) (1937 )
- *A Wedding Bouquet* (music by Lord Berners) (1937)
- *[Horoscope](/wiki/Horoscope_(ballet))* (music by Constant Lambert) (1938)
- *The Judgement of Paris* (music by [Lennox Berkeley](/wiki/Lennox_Berkeley)) (1938)
- *Cupid and Psyche* (music by Lord Berners) (1939)

## 1940s

- *Dante Sonata* (music by Franz Liszt, orchestrated by Constant Lambert) (1940)
- *[The Wise Virgins](/wiki/The_Wise_Virgins)* (music by [J S Bach](/wiki/Johann_Sebastian_Bach), orchestrated by William Walton), (1940)
- *The Wanderer* (music by [Franz Schubert](/wiki/Franz_Schubert)) (1941)
- *The Quest* (music by William Walton) (1943)
- *[Symphonic Variations](/wiki/Symphonic_Variations_(Franck))* (music by [César Franck](/wiki/C%C3%A9sar_Franck)) (1946)
- *Les Sirènes* (music by Lord Berners, orchestrated by [Roy Douglas](/wiki/Roy_Douglas)) (1946)
- various dances for a [Royal Opera](/wiki/The_Royal_Opera_House) production of *[The Fairy-Queen](/wiki/The_Fairy-Queen)* (music by [Henry Purcell](/wiki/Henry_Purcell), arranged by Constant Lambert) (1946)
- *Valses nobles et sentimentales* (music by Maurice Ravel) (1947)
- *[Scènes de ballet](/wiki/Sc%C3%A8nes_de_ballet_(Ashton))* (music by Igor Stravinsky) (1948)
- *Don Juan* (music by [Richard Strauss](/wiki/Richard_Strauss)) (1948)
- *[Cinderella](/wiki/Cinderella_(Ashton))* (music by [Sergei Prokofiev](/wiki/Sergei_Prokofiev)) (1948)
- *Le Rêve de Léonor* (music by [Benjamin Britten](/wiki/Benjamin_Britten), orchestrated by [Arthur Oldham](/wiki/Arthur_Oldham)) (1949)

## 1950s

- *Illuminations* (music by Benjamin Britten) (1950)
- **[Daphnis et Chloé](/wiki/Daphnis_et_Chlo%C3%A9)** (music by Maurice Ravel) (1951), created for [Margot Fonteyn](/wiki/Margot_Fonteyn) and [Michael Somes](/wiki/Michael_Somes)
- *[Tiresias](/wiki/Tiresias_(ballet))* (music by [Constant Lambert](/wiki/Constant_Lambert)) (1951)
- *[Sylvia](/wiki/Sylvia_(Ashton))* (music by [Léo Delibes](/wiki/L%C3%A9o_Delibes)) (1952)
- *Picnic at Tintagel* (music by [Arnold Bax](/wiki/Arnold_Bax)) (1952)
- *[Homage to the Queen](/wiki/Homage_to_the_Queen)* (music by [Malcolm Arnold](/wiki/Malcolm_Arnold)) (1953)
- *Madame Chrysanthème* (music by [Alan Rawsthorne](/wiki/Alan_Rawsthorne)) (1955)
- *[Romeo and Juliet](/wiki/Romeo_and_Juliet_(Prokofiev))* (music by Sergei Prokofiev) (1955)
- *Rinaldo and Armida* (music by Malcolm Arnold) (1955)
- **[La Péri](/wiki/La_P%C3%A9ri_(Dukas))** (music by [Paul Dukas](/wiki/Paul_Dukas)) (1956)
- *[Birthday Offering](/wiki/Birthday_Offering)* (music by [Alexander Glazunov](/wiki/Alexander_Glazunov), arranged by [Robert Irving](/wiki/Robert_Irving_(conductor))) (1956)
- **[Ondine](/wiki/Ondine_(Ashton))** (music by [Hans Werner Henze](/wiki/Hans_Werner_Henze)) (1958), created for Dame Margot Fonteyn
- **La Valse** (music by Maurice Ravel) (1958)

## 1960s

- **[La fille mal gardée](/wiki/La_fille_mal_gard%C3%A9e_(Ashton))** (music by [Ferdinand Hérold](/wiki/Ferdinand_H%C3%A9rold) - [John Lanchbery](/wiki/John_Lanchbery) (1960)
- *Persephone* (music by Igor Stravinsky) (1961)
- *[The Two Pigeons](/wiki/Les_Deux_Pigeons_(ballet))* (music by [André Messager](/wiki/Andr%C3%A9_Messager), arranged by [John Lanchbery](/wiki/John_Lanchbery)) (1961)
- *[Marguerite and Armand](/wiki/Marguerite_and_Armand)* (music by [Franz Liszt](/wiki/Franz_Liszt), orchestrated by [Humphrey Searle](/wiki/Humphrey_Searle)) 1963). Soloists: Margot Fonteyn and [Rudolf Nureyev](/wiki/Rudolf_Nureyev)
- *[The Dream](/wiki/The_Dream_(ballet))* after [Shakespeare](/wiki/Shakespeare)'s *[A Midsummer Night's Dream](/wiki/A_Midsummer_Night%27s_Dream)* (music by [Felix Mendelssohn](/wiki/Felix_Mendelssohn), some music arranged by John Lanchbery) (1964)
- *[Monotones](/wiki/Monotones_(ballet))* (music by [Erik Satie](/wiki/Erik_Satie), orchestrated by [Claude Debussy](/wiki/Claude_Debussy)) (1965)
- *[Jazz Calendar](/wiki/Jazz_Calendar)* (music by [Richard Rodney Bennett](/wiki/Richard_Rodney_Bennett)) (1968)
- *[Enigma Variations](/wiki/Enigma_Variations_(ballet))* (music by [Edward Elgar](/wiki/Edward_Elgar)) (1968)

## 1970s

- **Die Geschöpfe des Prometheus** ([The Creatures of Prometheus](/wiki/The_Creatures_of_Prometheus)) (music by [Ludwig van Beethoven](/wiki/Ludwig_van_Beethoven)) (1970)
- *Lament of the Waves* (music by [Gérard Masson](/wiki/G%C3%A9rard_Masson)) (1970)
- *The Walk to the Paradise Garden* (music by Frederick Delius) (1972)
- *[Five Brahms Waltzes in the Manner of Isadora Duncan](/wiki/Five_Brahms_Waltzes_in_the_Manner_of_Isadora_Duncan)* (music by [Johannes Brahms](/wiki/Johannes_Brahms)) (1976) (expanded from *Brahms Waltz*, 1975)
- *[A Month in the Country](/wiki/A_Month_in_the_Country_(ballet))* (music by [Frédéric Chopin](/wiki/Fr%C3%A9d%C3%A9ric_Chopin), arranged by John Lanchbery) (1976)
- *[Voices of Spring](/wiki/Voices_of_Spring_(Ashton))* (music by [Johann Strauss II](/wiki/Johann_Strauss_II)) (1977)

## 1980s

- *[Rhapsody](/wiki/Rhapsody_(Ashton))* (music by [Sergei Rachmaninoff](/wiki/Sergei_Rachmaninoff)) (1980)
- **[Pas de légumes](/wiki/Pas_de_l%C3%A9gumes)** (music by [Gioachino Rossini](/wiki/Gioachino_Rossini)) (1982)
- *La chatte métamorphosée en femme* (music by [Jacques Offenbach](/wiki/Jacques_Offenbach)) (1985)

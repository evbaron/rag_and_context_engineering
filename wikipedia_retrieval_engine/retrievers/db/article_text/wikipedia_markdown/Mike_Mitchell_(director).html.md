# Mike Mitchell (director)

## Infobox

- **Born:** Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, U.S.
- **Occupations:** Film director writer producer actor animator
- **Years active:** 1993–present

Not to be confused with [Mike Mitchell (actor)](/wiki/Mike_Mitchell_(actor)) or Canadian film director [Mike Kanentakeron Mitchell](/wiki/Mike_Kanentakeron_Mitchell).

**Mike Mitchell** is an American filmmaker, actor and animator. He is known for directing *[Deuce Bigalow: Male Gigolo](/wiki/Deuce_Bigalow:_Male_Gigolo)* (1999), *[Sky High](/wiki/Sky_High_(2005_film))* (2005), *[Shrek Forever After](/wiki/Shrek_Forever_After)* (2010), *[Alvin and the Chipmunks: Chipwrecked](/wiki/Alvin_and_the_Chipmunks:_Chipwrecked)* (2011), *[Trolls](/wiki/Trolls_(film))* (2016), *[The Lego Movie 2: The Second Part](/wiki/The_Lego_Movie_2:_The_Second_Part)* (2019), and *[Kung Fu Panda 4](/wiki/Kung_Fu_Panda_4)* (2024).

## Early life and education

Mike Mitchell was born in [Oklahoma City](/wiki/Oklahoma_City), [Oklahoma](/wiki/Oklahoma).

He graduated from [Putnam City North High School](/wiki/Putnam_City_North_High_School) in 1988, then subsequently moved to [Los Angeles](/wiki/Los_Angeles) to attend the [California Institute of the Arts](/wiki/California_Institute_of_the_Arts).

## Career

He started his career as director for [Matt O'Callaghan's](/wiki/Matthew_O%27Callaghan) *[The Itsy Bitsy Spider](/wiki/The_Itsy_Bitsy_Spider)*. He first gained critical attention when he co-wrote, produced and directed the short film *Herd*, which won several film festival awards, including the Spirit of Slamdance Award at the 1999 Slamdance Festival. The same year, he made his feature film directorial debut with the comedy *[Deuce Bigalow: Male Gigolo](/wiki/Deuce_Bigalow:_Male_Gigolo)*, starring [Rob Schneider](/wiki/Rob_Schneider). He worked for various companies including [Disney](/wiki/Disney), [DreamWorks](/wiki/DreamWorks_Pictures), [MTV](/wiki/MTV), [Nickelodeon](/wiki/Nickelodeon), [Universal](/wiki/Universal_Pictures), and [Sony](/wiki/Sony).

He directed the films *[Alvin and the Chipmunks: Chipwrecked](/wiki/Alvin_and_the_Chipmunks:_Chipwrecked)*, *[Surviving Christmas](/wiki/Surviving_Christmas)* and *[Sky High](/wiki/Sky_High_(2005_film))*. The first two received negative reviews, while the third has received positive reviews. In 2006, he joined [DreamWorks Animation](/wiki/DreamWorks_Animation), and was a story artist for *[Shrek the Third](/wiki/Shrek_the_Third)* and Creative Consultant for *[Kung Fu Panda](/wiki/Kung_Fu_Panda)*. He later directed the movie *[Shrek Forever After](/wiki/Shrek_Forever_After)* and voiced the character Butterpants.

Mitchell's short film *Frannie's Christmas* was preserved by the [Academy Film Archive](/wiki/Academy_Film_Archive) in 2012.

He also voiced Advisor Wedgie (known as "Nerd" for most of the film) in *[Monsters vs. Aliens](/wiki/Monsters_vs._Aliens)*, and Andy "Jack" Beanstalk in *[Puss in Boots](/wiki/Puss_in_Boots_(2011_film))*. He also directed live action sequences on *[The SpongeBob Movie: Sponge Out of Water](/wiki/The_SpongeBob_Movie:_Sponge_Out_of_Water)*. He not only works on movies but in television as well. He directed and wrote several episodes of *[SpongeBob SquarePants](/wiki/SpongeBob_SquarePants)*. Mitchell will also direct *Nightcrawlers*, a supernatural action comedy, a sequel to *Sky High*, a live-action-animated adaptation of the book *We're Not From Here*, and a live-action/animated adaptation of the song *[Puff, the Magic Dragon](/wiki/Puff,_the_Magic_Dragon)*.

On April 26, 2023, it was announced that Mitchell was set to direct [the fourth installment](/wiki/Kung_Fu_Panda_4) in the *[Kung Fu Panda](/wiki/Kung_Fu_Panda)* franchise, which was released on March 8, 2024.

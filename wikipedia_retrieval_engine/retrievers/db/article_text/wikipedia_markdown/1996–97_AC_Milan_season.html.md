During the 1996–97 season Milan Associazione Calcio competed in Serie A, Coppa Italia, UEFA Champions League and Supercoppa.

## Summary

**[Milan Associazione Calcio](/wiki/AC_Milan)** fell into pieces once [Fabio Capello](/wiki/Fabio_Capello) left his job to join [Real Madrid](/wiki/Real_Madrid_C.F.). The new organisation with [Óscar Tabárez](/wiki/%C3%93scar_Tab%C3%A1rez) as technical director completely miscued its signings, with neither [Jesper Blomqvist](/wiki/Jesper_Blomqvist), [Edgar Davids](/wiki/Edgar_Davids), [Christophe Dugarry](/wiki/Christophe_Dugarry) or [Michael Reiziger](/wiki/Michael_Reiziger) being successful. After a [Supercoppa Italiana](/wiki/Supercoppa_Italiana) defeat against [Fiorentina](/wiki/ACF_Fiorentina) at home at the [San Siro](/wiki/San_Siro), a 2–3 loss at [Piacenza](/wiki/Piacenza_Calcio) for [the league](/wiki/1996%E2%80%9397_Serie_A) cost the Uruguayan his position. Tabarez was replaced by former championship winning coach [Arrigo Sacchi](/wiki/Arrigo_Sacchi), the change did not happen and days later due to a 2–1 defeat to [Rosenborg](/wiki/Rosenborg_BK) at home in the Champions League the team was not qualified to Quarterfinals. The *Rossoneri* eventually finished 11th managed to save the reigning champions' contract in Serie A by just six points.

The miserable season also prompted captain [Franco Baresi](/wiki/Franco_Baresi) to end his active career, with Milan retiring the #6 shirt in his honour.

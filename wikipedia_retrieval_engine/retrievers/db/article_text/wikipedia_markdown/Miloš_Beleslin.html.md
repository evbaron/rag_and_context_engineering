**Miloš Beleslin** ([Serbian Cyrillic](/wiki/Serbian_Cyrillic_alphabet): Милош Белеслин; 8 September 1901 – 7 March 1984) was a [Yugoslavian](/wiki/Yugoslavia) [footballer](/wiki/Association_football).

## Club career

Born in Sereg, [Kingdom of Hungary](/wiki/Kingdom_of_Hungary), [Austro-Hungarian Empire](/wiki/Austro-Hungarian_Empire), he started playing in [Szeged](/wiki/Szeged) in [Szegedi AK](/wiki/Szegedi_AK). His played his first official match on May 5, 1917. In 1919 his parents moved to the city of [Novi Sad](/wiki/Novi_Sad) in the [Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes](/wiki/Kingdom_of_Yugoslavia) and he joined [NAK Novi Sad](/wiki/NAK_Novi_Sad). Later he would play with other Yugoslav top-league clubs, [SAND Subotica](/wiki/SAND_Subotica) and [ŽAK Subotica](/wiki/%C5%BDAK_Subotica). He played his last official match in 1939 on the day of his anniversary.

## International career

Miloš Beleslin played 8 matches for the [Yugoslavia national team](/wiki/Yugoslavia_national_football_team) having scored once. He was part of Yugoslavia's team at the [1928 Summer Olympics](/wiki/1928_Summer_Olympics), but he did not play in any matches. He received a call to represent Yugoslavia at the [1930 FIFA World Cup](/wiki/1930_FIFA_World_Cup) however as he was playing in Subotica, his representative subassociation, the Subotica Football Subassociation, boycotted the national team, not allowing him to travel to Uruguay. His final international was a May 1930 [King Alexander's Cup](/wiki/King_Alexander%27s_Cup) against [Romania](/wiki/Romania_national_football_team).

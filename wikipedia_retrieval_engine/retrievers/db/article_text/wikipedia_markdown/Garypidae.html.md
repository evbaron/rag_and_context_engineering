# Garypidae

**Garypidae** is a family of [pseudoscorpions](/wiki/Pseudoscorpion), first described by [Eugène Simon](/wiki/Eug%C3%A8ne_Simon) in 1879.

## Genera

As of October 2023, the *World Pseudoscorpiones Catalog* accepts the following eleven genera:

- *Ammogarypus* Beier, 1962
- *[Anagarypus](/wiki/Anagarypus)* Chamberlin, 1930
- *[Anchigarypus](/wiki/Anchigarypus)* Harvey, 2020
- *Elattogarypus* Beier, 1964
- *Eremogarypus* Beier, 1955
- *[Garypus](/wiki/Garypus)* L. Koch, 1873
- *Meiogarypus* Beier, 1955
- *Neogarypus* Vachon, 1937
- *Paragarypus* Vachon, 1937
- *[Synsphyronus](/wiki/Synsphyronus)* Chamberlin, 1930
- *Thaumastogarypus* Beier, 1947

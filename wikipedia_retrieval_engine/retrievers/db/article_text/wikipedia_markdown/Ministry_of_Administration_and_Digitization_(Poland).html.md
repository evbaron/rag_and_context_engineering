# Ministry of Administration and Digitization (Poland)

- **Website:** www.mac.gov.pl

The **Ministry of Administration and Digitization** ([Polish](/wiki/Polish_language): *Ministerstwo Administracji i Cyfryzacji*) was formed on 21 November 2011, from a reorganisation of the *[Ministry of Infrastructure](/wiki/Ministry_of_Infrastructure_(Poland))* and the *[Ministry of Interior and Administration](/wiki/Ministry_of_Interior_and_Administration_(Poland))*.

The Ministry was disbanded on 16 November 2015 based on the regulation of the Council of Ministers and partially superseded by the [Ministry of Digital Affairs](/wiki/Ministry_of_Digital_Affairs).

The ministry was concerned with various aspects of [administration](/wiki/Administration_(government)), [Internet](/wiki/Internet) and [telecommunication](/wiki/Telecommunication) in Poland.

The last minister was Andrzej Halicki.

The Ministry used to oversee:

- the Office of Electronic Communication (*Urząd Komunikacji Elektronicznej*)
- Chief Country Geodesist (*Główny Geodeta Kraju*)

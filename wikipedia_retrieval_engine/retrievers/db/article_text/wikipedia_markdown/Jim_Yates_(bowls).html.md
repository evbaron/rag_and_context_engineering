# Jim Yates (bowls)

## Infobox

- **Nationality:** Australia
- **Born:** ( 1934-10-11 ) 11 October 1934 (age 91)

**Jim Yates** is a former Australian lawn and indoor bowler and coach, born in [Corowa](/wiki/Corowa), [New South Wales](/wiki/New_South_Wales) on 11 October 1934.

## Bowls career

Yates partnered [Ian Schuback](/wiki/Ian_Schuback) when he won the [1988 World Indoor Bowls Championship](/wiki/1988_World_Indoor_Bowls_Championship) Pairs. He won the [Australian Singles Championship](/wiki/Australian_National_Bowls_Championships) in 1979 and the Adelaide Masters in 1983, 1984 and 1987.

Additionally he has won ten Club Singles Championships and played 211 games for the Victorian State team.

August 21, 1942: Japanese soldiers killed during the [Battle of the Tenaru](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Tenaru).

The following events occurred in **August 1942**:

## [August 1](/wiki/August_1), 1942 (Saturday)

- The Germans cut the railway line linking [Stalingrad](/wiki/Volgograd) to [Krasnodar](/wiki/Krasnodar).
- [1942–1944 musicians' strike](/wiki/1942%E2%80%931944_musicians%27_strike): The [American Federation of Musicians](/wiki/American_Federation_of_Musicians) went on strike against the major U.S. recording companies because of disagreements over royalty payments.
- [Andrey Yeryomenko](/wiki/Andrey_Yeryomenko) met with [Joseph Stalin](/wiki/Joseph_Stalin) at the Kremlin and accepted command of one of Stalingrad's southern fronts.
- German submarines *[U-226](/wiki/German_submarine_U-226)* and *[U-448](/wiki/German_submarine_U-448)* were commissioned.
- **Born:**
  - [Jerry Garcia](/wiki/Jerry_Garcia), singer, songwriter and guitarist for the [Grateful Dead](/wiki/Grateful_Dead), in [San Francisco](/wiki/San_Francisco), [California](/wiki/California) (d. 1995);
  - [Giancarlo Giannini](/wiki/Giancarlo_Giannini), actor and dubber, in [La Spezia](/wiki/La_Spezia), Italy
  - [Brianda Domecq](/wiki/Brianda_Domecq), writer and environmentalist, in [New York](/wiki/New_York_City), USA.

## [August 2](/wiki/August_2), 1942 (Sunday)

- The German [4th Panzer Army](/wiki/4th_Panzer_Army) captured [Kotelnikovo](/wiki/Kotelnikovo,_Volgograd_Oblast).
- After spending most of the day studying maps of Stalingrad and the surrounding area, Andrey Yeryomenko had a second conference with Stalin. Yeryomenko protested that two Russian fronts in the same area meant that trying to co-ordinate Stalingrad's defence with another commander would be "utterly confusing, if not tragically impossible," and asked to command the [Stalingrad Front](/wiki/Stalingrad_Front) in the north rather than the [Southeastern Front](/wiki/Southeastern_Front). Stalin firmly said that everything would be left as it was already outlined.
- A man named José Gallardo Díaz was found unconscious and dying on a road near a swimming hole in [Commerce, California](/wiki/Commerce,_California). He was rushed to the hospital but died shortly after. 17 Mexican-American youths were soon arrested in a case that came to be known as the [Sleepy Lagoon murder](/wiki/Sleepy_Lagoon_murder).
- **Born:**
  - [Isabel Allende](/wiki/Isabel_Allende), writer, in [Lima](/wiki/Lima), [Peru](/wiki/Peru)

## [August 3](/wiki/August_3), 1942 (Monday)

- The British launched [Operation Pedestal](/wiki/Operation_Pedestal), an effort to get desperately needed supplies to Malta.
- German submarine *[U-335](/wiki/German_submarine_U-335)* was torpedoed and sunk southeast of the [Faroe Islands](/wiki/Faroe_Islands) by the British submarine *[Saracen](/wiki/HMS_Saracen_(P247))*.
- American destroyer [USS *Tucker*](/wiki/USS_Tucker_(DD-374)) struck a mine off [Espiritu Santo](/wiki/Espiritu_Santo) late in the day and sank early on August 4.
- **Died:**
  - [James Cruze](/wiki/James_Cruze), 58, American film actor and director;
  - [Richard Willstätter](/wiki/Richard_Willst%C3%A4tter), 69, German organic chemist

## [August 4](/wiki/August_4), 1942 (Tuesday)

- The German [4th Panzer Army](/wiki/4th_Panzer_Army) crossed the Aksay River in its drive on Stalingrad.
- Yeremenko flew down to Stalingrad in a Douglas transport aircraft. Commissar [Nikita Khrushchev](/wiki/Nikita_Khrushchev) met him at the airport with a car and they drove to the city's headquarters.
- The [Bracero program](/wiki/Bracero_program) was initiated when the United States signed the Mexican Farm Labor Agreement with [Mexico](/wiki/Mexico).
- German submarine *[U-372](/wiki/German_submarine_U-372)* was depth charged and sunk off [Haifa](/wiki/Haifa) by a [Vickers Wellington](/wiki/Vickers_Wellington) bomber.
- Citing documents seized in a raid on [Indian National Congress](/wiki/Indian_National_Congress) headquarters in [Allahabad](/wiki/Allahabad), the British government accused [Mahatma Gandhi](/wiki/Mahatma_Gandhi) and the majority of his party of working toward "appeasement" of Japan.
- The musical film *[Holiday Inn](/wiki/Holiday_Inn_(film))* starring [Bing Crosby](/wiki/Bing_Crosby) and [Fred Astaire](/wiki/Fred_Astaire) with music by [Irving Berlin](/wiki/Irving_Berlin) premiered in New York City.
- **Born:** [David Lange](/wiki/David_Lange), 32nd [Prime Minister of New Zealand](/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_New_Zealand), in [Otahuhu](/wiki/Otahuhu), [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand) (d. 2005)

## [August 5](/wiki/August_5), 1942 (Wednesday)

- German forces took [Voroshilovsk](/wiki/Stavropol).
- [Anthony Eden](/wiki/Anthony_Eden) announced in the House of Commons that the [Munich Agreement](/wiki/Munich_Agreement) of 1938 would play no part in the postwar settlement of Czechoslovakia's borders, because the British government no longer considered itself bound to that agreement since the Germans destroyed it.
- Queen [Wilhelmina of the Netherlands](/wiki/Wilhelmina_of_the_Netherlands) visited the [White House](/wiki/White_House) and addressed U.S. Congress.
- German submarines *[U-188](/wiki/German_submarine_U-188)*, *[U-304](/wiki/German_submarine_U-304)* and *[U-415](/wiki/German_submarine_U-415)* were commissioned.

## [August 6](/wiki/August_6), 1942 (Thursday)

- German forces on the Eastern Front captured [Tikhoretsk](/wiki/Tikhoretsk) and [Armavir](/wiki/Armavir,_Russia).
- [Dzyatlava massacre](/wiki/Dzyatlava_massacre): During the liquidation of the [Zdzięcioł Ghetto](/wiki/Zdzi%C4%99cio%C5%82_Ghetto) several thousand Jews were murdered at the local Jewish cemetery.
- For aiding an escaped German prisoner of war, Detroit restaurant owner Max Stephan became the first American sentenced to execution for treason since the [Whiskey Rebellion](/wiki/Whiskey_Rebellion) in 1794.
- The British submarine [HMS *Thorn*](/wiki/HMS_Thorn_(N11)) went missing off southern Crete, probably sunk by the Italian torpedo boat [*Pegaso*](/wiki/Italian_torpedo_boat_Pegaso_(1936)).
- The Germans lost three submarines in one day. *[U-210](/wiki/German_submarine_U-210)* was rammed and sunk in the Atlantic Ocean by the Canadian destroyer *[Assiniboine](/wiki/HMS_Kempenfelt_(I18))*, *[U-612](/wiki/German_submarine_U-612)* sank off [Gotenhafen](/wiki/Gdynia), Germany after colliding with *[U-444](/wiki/German_submarine_U-444)*, and *[U-578](/wiki/German_submarine_U-578)* went missing in the [Bay of Biscay](/wiki/Bay_of_Biscay). Her fate remains unknown.
- German submarine *[U-634](/wiki/German_submarine_U-634)* was commissioned.
- **Born:** [Evelyn Hamann](/wiki/Evelyn_Hamann), actress, in [Hamburg](/wiki/Hamburg), Germany (d. 2007)

## [August 7](/wiki/August_7), 1942 (Friday)

- The [Guadalcanal Campaign](/wiki/Guadalcanal_Campaign) and the [Battle of Tulagi and Gavutu–Tanambogo](/wiki/Battle_of_Tulagi_and_Gavutu%E2%80%93Tanambogo) began. Allied forces consisting mostly of U.S. Marines made amphibious landings in the southern [Solomon Islands](/wiki/Solomon_Islands) in the first major offensive by the Allies against Japan.
- The German [6th Army](/wiki/6th_Army_(Wehrmacht)) crossed the [Don](/wiki/Don_River_(Russia)) at [Kalach](/wiki/Kalach,_Kalacheyevsky_District,_Voronezh_Oblast).
- [Winston Churchill](/wiki/Winston_Churchill) visited the British troops at [El Alamein](/wiki/El_Alamein).
- [Alfonso López Pumarejo](/wiki/Alfonso_L%C3%B3pez_Pumarejo) became the 16th [President of Colombia](/wiki/President_of_Colombia).
- **Born:**
  - [Tobin Bell](/wiki/Tobin_Bell), actor, in [Queens](/wiki/Queens), [New York City](/wiki/New_York_City);
  - [Garrison Keillor](/wiki/Garrison_Keillor), author and radio personality, in [Anoka, Minnesota](/wiki/Anoka,_Minnesota)
  - Sir [Richard Sykes](/wiki/Richard_Sykes_(microbiologist)), British microbiologist, chair of the [Vaccine Taskforce](/wiki/Vaccine_Taskforce), in [Huddersfield](/wiki/Huddersfield), Yorkshire, England
  - [Caetano Veloso](/wiki/Caetano_Veloso), Brazilian musician, in [Santo Amaro, Bahia](/wiki/Santo_Amaro,_Bahia)
- **Died:**
  - [William Gott](/wiki/William_Gott), 44, British Army officer (plane shot down in North Africa);
  - [Janusz Korczak](/wiki/Janusz_Korczak), 63 or 64, Polish-Jewish educator, children's author and pediatrician (killed at Treblinka)

## [August 8](/wiki/August_8), 1942 (Saturday)

- U.S. Marines captured the unfinished Japanese airbase on Guadalcanal. The base was named [Henderson Field](/wiki/Henderson_Field_(Guadalcanal)) after the [Battle of Midway](/wiki/Battle_of_Midway) hero [Lofton R. Henderson](/wiki/Lofton_R._Henderson).
- The [Battle of Savo Island](/wiki/Battle_of_Savo_Island), the first major naval engagement of the Guadalcanal Campaign, began.
- German [Army Group B](/wiki/Army_Group_B) captured [Surovikino](/wiki/Surovikino).
- The American attack transport [USS *George F. Elliott*](/wiki/USS_George_F._Elliott_(AP-13)) was bombed and sunk in [Ironbottom Sound](/wiki/Ironbottom_Sound) by a [Mitsubishi G4M](/wiki/Mitsubishi_G4M).
- German submarine *[U-379](/wiki/German_submarine_U-379)* was depth charged and sunk southeast of [Cape Farewell, Greenland](/wiki/Cape_Farewell,_Greenland) by the Royal Navy corvette *[Dianthus](/wiki/HMS_Dianthus_(K95))*.
- [Mahatma Gandhi](/wiki/Mahatma_Gandhi) made the [Quit India speech](/wiki/Quit_India_speech).
- **Died:** [Edward H. Ahrens](/wiki/Edward_H._Ahrens), 22, [United States Marine Raider](/wiki/Marine_Raiders) (killed in action during the [Guadalcanal campaign](/wiki/Guadalcanal_campaign))

## [August 9](/wiki/August_9), 1942 (Sunday)

- The [Battle of Tulagi and Gavutu–Tanambogo](/wiki/Battle_of_Tulagi_and_Gavutu%E2%80%93Tanambogo) ended in Allied victory.
- The [Battle of Savo Island](/wiki/Battle_of_Savo_Island) ended in tactical Japanese victory. American cruisers *[Astoria](/wiki/USS_Astoria_(CA-34))*, *[Quincy](/wiki/USS_Quincy_(CA-39))* and *[Vincennes](/wiki/USS_Vincennes_(CA-44))* and the Australian cruiser *[Canberra](/wiki/HMAS_Canberra_(D33))* were all sunk while three Japanese cruisers were damaged.
- American destroyer *[Jarvis](/wiki/USS_Jarvis_(DD-393))* was sunk by Japanese aircraft off Guadalcanal.
- German [Army Group A](/wiki/Army_Group_A) captured [Krasnodar](/wiki/Krasnodar) and the Soviet oil centre of [Maykop](/wiki/Maykop).
- The [Leningrad première of Shostakovich's Symphony No. 7](/wiki/Leningrad_premi%C3%A8re_of_Shostakovich%27s_Symphony_No._7) occurred while the city was still under siege by German forces.
- Bombay police arrested Mahatma Gandhi and fifty other members of the [Indian National Congress](/wiki/Indian_National_Congress) a few hours before a massive [civil disobedience](/wiki/Civil_disobedience) campaign was to begin. Five people were killed later in the day when police fired on crowds of people who were jeering and throwing stones.
- [Ahmad Qavam](/wiki/Ahmad_Qavam) became [Prime Minister of Iran](/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Iran) for the third time.
- The [Walt Disney](/wiki/The_Walt_Disney_Company) animated film *[Bambi](/wiki/Bambi)* had its world premiere in London.
- [*The New York Times* Best Seller list](/wiki/The_New_York_Times_Best_Seller_list) switched from a local survey to a national one compiled from booksellers in 22 cities. The first [Fiction Best Seller](/wiki/The_New_York_Times_Fiction_Best_Sellers_of_1942) under the new system was *And Now Tomorrow* by [Rachel Field](/wiki/Rachel_Field).
- **Died:** [Edith Stein](/wiki/Edith_Stein), 50, German Jewish philosopher and Catholic saint (murdered at Auschwitz)

## [August 10](/wiki/August_10), 1942 (Monday)

- The German [6th Army](/wiki/6th_Army_(Wehrmacht)) crossed the lower [Don River](/wiki/Don_River_(Russia)) and reached the outskirts of [Stalingrad](/wiki/Volgograd).
- Forward elements of German Army Group A reached [Pyatigorsk](/wiki/Pyatigorsk).
- In the second phase of the [Battle of Kokoda](/wiki/Battle_of_Kokoda), Australian troops which had briefly recaptured Kokoda on the 8th are now forced to withdraw, ending the engagement.
- Japanese cruiser *[Kako](/wiki/Japanese_cruiser_Kako)* was torpedoed and sunk off Simbari Island, [New Ireland](/wiki/New_Ireland_Province) by the American submarine [USS *S-44*](/wiki/USS_S-44_(SS-155)).
- The Italian submarine *[Scirè](/wiki/Italian_submarine_Scir%C3%A8_(1938))* was sunk off [Haifa](/wiki/Haifa) by the British armed trawler *[Islay](/wiki/HMT_Islay_(T172))*.
- While on his way to [Moscow](/wiki/Moscow), [Winston Churchill](/wiki/Winston_Churchill) stopped at [Tehran](/wiki/Tehran) and had lunch with the [Shah of Iran](/wiki/Mohammad_Reza_Pahlavi).

## [August 11](/wiki/August_11), 1942 (Tuesday)

- [Pierre Laval](/wiki/Pierre_Laval) reached an agreement with the Germans that 150,000 French workers would go to Germany in exchange for 50,000 French prisoners of war.
- The British aircraft carrier HMS *[Eagle](/wiki/HMS_Eagle_(1918))* was torpedoed and sunk by the German submarine *[U-73](/wiki/German_submarine_U-73_(1940))* during Operation Pedestal.
- Sixty-one people died in riots in [New Delhi](/wiki/New_Delhi).
- [Al Milnar](/wiki/Al_Milnar) of the [Cleveland Indians](/wiki/Cleveland_Indians) and [Tommy Bridges](/wiki/Tommy_Bridges) of the [Detroit Tigers](/wiki/Detroit_Tigers) had one of the most epic pitchers' duels in baseball history. With the game locked in a scoreless tie in the top of the ninth inning, Milnar lost a [no-hitter](/wiki/No-hitter) with two out when [Doc Cramer](/wiki/Doc_Cramer) singled to right field. Both pitchers maintained their shutouts until the fifteenth inning when the game was finally called in a 0–0 tie.
- **Died:** [Sabina Spielrein](/wiki/Sabina_Spielrein), 56, Russian physician and psychoanalyst (murdered by an SS [death squad](/wiki/Einsatzgruppen))

## [August 12](/wiki/August_12), 1942 (Wednesday)

- The [Second Moscow Conference](/wiki/Moscow_Conference_(1942)) began. In attendance were [Joseph Stalin](/wiki/Joseph_Stalin), [Winston Churchill](/wiki/Winston_Churchill), and U.S. representative [W. Averell Harriman](/wiki/W._Averell_Harriman).
- Elements of German Army Group A reached [Slavyansk](/wiki/Slavyansk-na-Kubani).
- During [Operation Pedestal](/wiki/Operation_Pedestal) the British cruiser *[Cairo](/wiki/HMS_Cairo_(D87))* and British destroyer *[Foresight](/wiki/HMS_Foresight_(H68))* were sunk, and the tanker *[Ohio](/wiki/SS_Ohio)* was severely damaged and had to be taken under tow.
- Movie star [Clark Gable](/wiki/Clark_Gable) entered a U.S. Army recruiting station in Los Angeles and enlisted as a private at the age of 41.
- German submarines *[U-468](/wiki/German_submarine_U-468)*, *[U-526](/wiki/German_submarine_U-526)*, *[U-527](/wiki/German_submarine_U-527)* and *[U-709](/wiki/German_submarine_U-709)* were commissioned.
- **Died:** [Phillips Holmes](/wiki/Phillips_Holmes), 35, American film actor (mid-air plane collision)

## [August 13](/wiki/August_13), 1942 (Thursday)

- [Bernard Montgomery](/wiki/Bernard_Montgomery,_1st_Viscount_Montgomery_of_Alamein) took over command of the British [Eighth Army](/wiki/Eighth_Army_(United_Kingdom)) following the death of [William Gott](/wiki/William_Gott).
- On the Eastern Front, German forces captured [Elista](/wiki/Elista).
- During Operation Pedestal the British cruiser *[Manchester](/wiki/HMS_Manchester_(15))* was torpedoed and heavily damaged by two Italian motor torpedo boats and then scuttled.
- Japan passed the [Enemy Airmen's Act](/wiki/Enemy_Airmen%27s_Act), stating that Allied airmen participating in bombing raids against Japanese-held territory would be treated as "violators of the [law of war](/wiki/Law_of_war)" and subject to trial and punishment if captured.
- German submarine *[U-635](/wiki/German_submarine_U-635)* was commissioned.
- The play *[Flare Path](/wiki/Flare_Path)* by [Terence Rattigan](/wiki/Terence_Rattigan) premiered at the [Apollo Theatre](/wiki/Apollo_Theatre) in London.
- **Born:** [Arthur K. Cebrowski](/wiki/Arthur_K._Cebrowski), admiral, in [Passaic, New Jersey](/wiki/Passaic,_New_Jersey) (d. 2005)

## [August 14](/wiki/August_14), 1942 (Friday)

- [Dwight D. Eisenhower](/wiki/Dwight_D._Eisenhower) was named Anglo-American commander for [Operation Torch](/wiki/Operation_Torch).
- [British Commandos](/wiki/British_Commandos) carried out [Operation Barricade](/wiki/Operation_Barricade), an overnight raid on an anti-aircraft gun and radar site northwest of [Pointe de Saire](/wiki/Saint-Saire), France.
- **Born:** [Molefi Kete Asante](/wiki/Molefi_Kete_Asante), leading scholar of African-American Studies, in [Valdosta, Georgia](/wiki/Valdosta,_Georgia)
- **Died:** [Rafaela Ottiano](/wiki/Rafaela_Ottiano), Italian-American actress (b. 1888)

## [August 15](/wiki/August_15), 1942 (Saturday)

- [Operation Pedestal](/wiki/Operation_Pedestal) ended in tactical disaster but strategic victory for the British.
- German troops captured [Georgiyevsk](/wiki/Georgiyevsk) and reached the foothills of the [Caucasus Mountains](/wiki/Caucasus_Mountains).
- 5,000 Jews were arrested in Vichy France.
- German submarines *[U-189](/wiki/German_submarine_U-189)*, *[U-358](/wiki/German_submarine_U-358)* and *[U-759](/wiki/German_submarine_U-759)* were commissioned.
- **Born:** [Friede Springer](/wiki/Friede_Springer), publisher and spouse of [Axel Springer](/wiki/Axel_Springer), in [Oldsum](/wiki/Oldsum), Germany

## [August 16](/wiki/August_16), 1942 (Sunday)

- The *[Kriegsmarine](/wiki/Kriegsmarine)* began [Operation Wunderland](/wiki/Operation_Wunderland) with the objective of entering the [Kara Sea](/wiki/Kara_Sea) and destroying as many Russian vessels as possible.
- The Russians evacuated [Maykop](/wiki/Maykop).
- Axis positions in Egypt were bombed by American warplanes for the first time.
- In [Bilbao](/wiki/Bilbao), [Spain](/wiki/Spain), a mass was held at the [Basilica of Begoña](/wiki/Basilica_of_Bego%C3%B1a) to commemorate members of the Begoña Regiment who died in the [Civil War](/wiki/Spanish_Civil_War). After the service there was some shouting between the [Falangist](/wiki/Falangism) and [Carlist](/wiki/Carlism) factions, and during the ensuing scuffle a Falangist threw two hand grenades and wounded 30 people.

## [August 17](/wiki/August_17), 1942 (Monday)

- U.S. Marines conducted the [Makin Island raid](/wiki/Makin_Island_raid).
- The Second Moscow Conference ended.
- 1,700 Jews were massacred in the Polish village of Łomazy by [Reserve Police Battalion 101](/wiki/Reserve_Police_Battalion_101).
- German Army Group A established bridgeheads across the [Kuban River](/wiki/Kuban_River).
- The [USAAF](/wiki/United_States_Army_Air_Forces) made its first air raid on occupied Europe, bombing railroad marshaling yards at [Sotteville-lès-Rouen](/wiki/Sotteville-l%C3%A8s-Rouen).
- **Died:** [Clyde A. Thomason](/wiki/Clyde_A._Thomason), 28, United States Marine and posthumous recipient of the Medal of Honor (killed in action during the Makin Island raid)

## [August 18](/wiki/August_18), 1942 (Tuesday)

- 900 Japanese troops landed at Taivu Point on Guadalcanal, while another 500 landed at [Kokumbona](/wiki/Kokumbona). These landings were the first run of what the U.S. Marines nicknamed the [Tokyo Express](/wiki/Tokyo_Express).
- In [Stalingrad Oblast](/wiki/Volgograd_Oblast), Soviet forces withdrew southwest of Kletskaya while the Germans renewed their offensive northeast of [Kotelnikovo](/wiki/Kotelnikovo,_Volgograd_Oblast).
- Hitler issued [Directive No. 46](/wiki/List_of_Adolf_Hitler%27s_directives), Instructions for Intensified Action Against Banditry in the East.
- **Born:** [Judith Keppel](/wiki/Judith_Keppel), £1,000,000 winner on the UK edition of *[Who Wants to Be a Millionaire?](/wiki/Who_Wants_to_Be_a_Millionaire%3F_(UK_game_show))* in [Wolverhampton](/wiki/Wolverhampton), [Staffordshire](/wiki/Staffordshire), England; [Wu Ma](/wiki/Wu_Ma), filmmaker, in [Tianjin](/wiki/Tianjin), [China](/wiki/China) (d. 2014)

## [August 19](/wiki/August_19), 1942 (Wednesday)

- The [Dieppe Raid](/wiki/Dieppe_Raid) took place on the northern coast of France. The operation was virtually a complete failure and almost 60% of the 6,086 men who made it ashore were killed, wounded or captured. The British destroyer *[Berkeley](/wiki/HMS_Berkeley_(L17))* was crippled by [Focke-Wulf Fw 190s](/wiki/Focke-Wulf_Fw_190) and scuttled.
- The Soviets began the [Sinyavino Offensive](/wiki/Sinyavino_Offensive_(1942)) on the southern shore of [Lake Ladoga](/wiki/Lake_Ladoga).
- The [Battle of Kupres](/wiki/Battle_of_Kupres_(1942)) ended with the forces of the [Independent State of Croatia](/wiki/Independent_State_of_Croatia) successfully defending the town against the [Yugoslav Partisans](/wiki/Yugoslav_Partisans).
- Mahatma Gandhi's son [Devdas](/wiki/Devdas_Gandhi) was arrested in New Delhi for publishing an article on the Indian civil disobedience campaign in violation of the [Defence of India Act](/wiki/Defence_of_India_Act,_1939).
- German submarine *[U-269](/wiki/German_submarine_U-269)* was commissioned.
- **Born:** [Fred Thompson](/wiki/Fred_Thompson), politician and actor, in [Sheffield, Alabama](/wiki/Sheffield,_Alabama) (d. 2015)
- **Died:** [Heinrich Rauchinger](/wiki/Heinrich_Rauchinger), 84, Kraków-born painter, in [Theresienstadt](/wiki/Theresienstadt) (b. 1858)

## [August 20](/wiki/August_20), 1942 (Thursday)

- Chinese forces recaptured [Guangfeng](/wiki/Guangfeng_District) and [Shangrao](/wiki/Shangrao) in [Jiangxi](/wiki/Jiangxi) Province from the Japanese.
- German submarine *[U-464](/wiki/German_submarine_U-464)* was depth charged and crippled in the Atlantic Ocean by a [Consolidated PBY Catalina](/wiki/Consolidated_PBY_Catalina) and then scuttled.
- U.S. [Twelfth Air Force](/wiki/Twelfth_Air_Force) was established.
- German submarine *[U-636](/wiki/German_submarine_U-636)* was commissioned.
- The comedy-drama film *[The Talk of the Town](/wiki/The_Talk_of_the_Town_(1942_film))* starring [Cary Grant](/wiki/Cary_Grant), [Jean Arthur](/wiki/Jean_Arthur) and [Ronald Colman](/wiki/Ronald_Colman) was released.
- **Born:** [Isaac Hayes](/wiki/Isaac_Hayes), soul singer, songwriter and actor, in [Covington, Tennessee](/wiki/Covington,_Tennessee) (d. 2008)
- **Died:** [István Horthy](/wiki/Istv%C3%A1n_Horthy), 37, Hungarian politician, fighter pilot and son of Hungarian Regent [Miklós Horthy](/wiki/Mikl%C3%B3s_Horthy) (plane crash)

## [August 21](/wiki/August_21), 1942 (Friday)

- The [Battle of the Tenaru](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Tenaru) was fought on Guadalcanal, resulting in Allied victory.
- German infantry companies of [LI Army Corps](/wiki/LI_Army_Corps_(Wehrmacht)) crossed the [Don](/wiki/Don_River_(Russia)) in inflatable boats and quickly established a bridgehead near the village of Luchinsky.
- Chinese forces recaptured [Yingtan](/wiki/Yingtan).

## [August 22](/wiki/August_22), 1942 (Saturday)

- [Brazil](/wiki/Brazil) declared war on Germany and Italy after the sinking of several Brazilian ships.
- The German [16th Panzer Division](/wiki/16th_Infantry_Division_(Wehrmacht)) began crossing the Don as soon as the bridgehead was ready.
- The American destroyer [USS *Blue*](/wiki/USS_Blue_(DD-387)) was torpedoed and crippled at [Ironbottom Sound](/wiki/Ironbottom_Sound) during the Battle of Guadalcanal by the Japanese destroyer *[Kawakaze](/wiki/Japanese_destroyer_Kawakaze_(1936))*. She was scuttled the next day after salvage attempts failed.
- Chinese forces captured [Yujiang](/wiki/Yujiang_County).
- German submarine *[U-654](/wiki/German_submarine_U-654)* was depth charged and sunk in the Caribbean by an American [Douglas B-18 Bolo](/wiki/Douglas_B-18_Bolo) bomber.
- The American destroyer [USS *Ingraham*](/wiki/USS_Ingraham_(DD-444)) sank off the coast of Nova Scotia after colliding in heavy fog with the oil tanker *[Chemung](/wiki/USS_Chemung_(AO-30))*.
- German submarines *[U-227](/wiki/German_submarine_U-227)* and *[U-449](/wiki/German_submarine_U-449)* were commissioned.
- **Died:** [Michel Fokine](/wiki/Michel_Fokine), 62, Russian choreographer and dancer

## [August 23](/wiki/August_23), 1942 (Sunday)

- The [Battle of Stalingrad](/wiki/Battle_of_Stalingrad) began.
- The Luftwaffe conducted the first major bombing raid on Stalingrad. A rain of incendiary and explosive bombs killed more than 40,000 civilians and reduced most of the city to rubble.
- The German 16th Panzer Division came within striking distance of the [Stalingrad Tractor Factory](/wiki/Volgograd_Tractor_Plant), the Soviet Union's largest producer of [T-34](/wiki/T-34) tanks.
- At about 1:45 am, 45-year-old Martha Hansen of [Twin Falls, Idaho](/wiki/Twin_Falls,_Idaho), was fatally mauled by a [brown bear](/wiki/Brown_bear) or [grizzly bear](/wiki/Grizzly_bear) she encountered while walking from her cabin at [Yellowstone National Park](/wiki/Yellowstone_National_Park) to the ladies' restroom. Hansen would die of her injuries on August 27.
- **Died:** [Heinrich-Wilhelm Ahnert](/wiki/Heinrich-Wilhelm_Ahnert), 27, German flying ace (shot down over [Koptevo](/wiki/Northern_Administrative_Okrug), USSR)

## [August 24](/wiki/August_24), 1942 (Monday)

- The German [6th Army](/wiki/6th_Army_(Wehrmacht)) attacked the northern suburbs of Stalingrad.
- The [Battle of the Eastern Solomons](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Eastern_Solomons) began. The Japanese aircraft carrier *[Ryūjō](/wiki/Japanese_aircraft_carrier_Ry%C5%ABj%C5%8D)* was sunk by aircraft from [USS *Saratoga*](/wiki/USS_Saratoga_(CV-3)).
- The [Charge of the Savoia Cavalleria at Isbuscenskij](/wiki/Charge_of_the_Savoia_Cavalleria_at_Isbuscenskij) occurred, one of the last cavalry charges in history.
- [An earthquake in Peru](/wiki/1942_Peru_earthquake) killed 30 people.
- Winston Churchill returned to England from Moscow.
- German submarine *[U-193](/wiki/German_submarine_U-193)* was commissioned.
- The war film *[Wake Island](/wiki/Wake_Island_(film))*, recounting the December 1941 [Battle of Wake Island](/wiki/Battle_of_Wake_Island), premiered at the U.S. Marine base at Camp Elliott in [San Diego](/wiki/San_Diego), [California](/wiki/California).
- **Born:** [Hans Peter Korff](/wiki/Hans_Peter_Korff), actor, in [Hamburg](/wiki/Hamburg), [Germany](/wiki/Germany) (d. 2025)

## [August 25](/wiki/August_25), 1942 (Tuesday)

- The [Battle of the Eastern Solomons](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Eastern_Solomons) ended in a draw, but strategic Allied success. The Japanese destroyer *[Mutsuki](/wiki/Japanese_destroyer_Mutsuki)* was crippled by four [B-17s](/wiki/Boeing_B-17_Flying_Fortress) and had to be scuttled.
- The [Battle of Milne Bay](/wiki/Battle_of_Milne_Bay) began in eastern New Guinea.
- A citywide evacuation effort began in Stalingrad. First priority went to specialists and workers whose factories had been destroyed.
- German submarine *[U-339](/wiki/German_submarine_U-339)* was commissioned.
- **Born:** [Ivan Koloff](/wiki/Ivan_Koloff), born Oreal Perras, professional wrestler, in [Montreal, Quebec](/wiki/Montreal), [Canada](/wiki/Canada) (d. 2017)
- **Died:** [Prince George, Duke of Kent](/wiki/Prince_George,_Duke_of_Kent), 39, British royal (plane crash)

## [August 26](/wiki/August_26), 1942 (Wednesday)

- The [Battle of Isurava](/wiki/Battle_of_Isurava), part of the [Kokoda Track Campaign](/wiki/Kokoda_Track_Campaign), begins, with Japanese forces attacking the Australian positions there.
- A round-up of Jews began in [Nazi-occupied France](/wiki/German_military_administration_in_occupied_France_during_World_War_II).
- The British government lifted the ban on the communist newspaper *[The Daily Worker](/wiki/Morning_Star_(British_newspaper))*.
- **Died:** [Junichi Sasai](/wiki/Junichi_Sasai), 24, Japanese fighter ace (killed during the Battle of Guadalcanal)

## [August 27](/wiki/August_27), 1942 (Thursday)

- German Army Group B captured [Prokhladny](/wiki/Prokhladny,_Kabardino-Balkar_Republic), pushing closer to the oil prize of [Grozny](/wiki/Grozny).
- British and American bombers raided [Rotterdam](/wiki/Rotterdam).
- German submarine *[U-637](/wiki/German_submarine_U-637)* was commissioned.
- **Born:** "Captain" [Daryl Dragon](/wiki/Daryl_Dragon), keyboardist and one-half of the pop music duo [Captain & Tennille](/wiki/Captain_%26_Tennille), in [Los Angeles](/wiki/Los_Angeles), [California](/wiki/California) (d. 2019)

## [August 28](/wiki/August_28), 1942 (Friday)

- Japanese destroyer *[Asagiri](/wiki/Japanese_destroyer_Asagiri_(1929))* was sunk en route to Guadalcanal by American [SBD Dauntless](/wiki/Douglas_SBD_Dauntless) dive bombers.
- German submarine *[U-94](/wiki/German_submarine_U-94_(1940))* was sunk off [Haiti](/wiki/Haiti) by depth charges from an American [PBY Catalina](/wiki/Consolidated_PBY_Catalina) flying boat and ramming by the Canadian corvette *[Oakville](/wiki/HMCS_Oakville_(K178))*.
- A German fighter-bomber flying at 20,000 feet dropped a 500lb bomb into the centre of the English city of [Bristol](/wiki/Bristol), killing 45 and wounding 56 people.
- 1942 Suō-nada Typhoon, a violence 935 to 942 hectopascal level, violence wind and [tidal wave](/wiki/Storm_surge) hit around [Suō-nada](/wiki/Su%C5%8D-nada), [Yatsushiro Sea](/wiki/Yatsushiro_Sea), [Ariake Sea](/wiki/Ariake_Sea), southwestern [Honshu](/wiki/Honshu) and [Kyushu Island](/wiki/Kyushu_Island), Japan. According to [Japanese government](/wiki/Japanese_government) official document figures, killing 1,158 persons, injures 1,438 persons.
- **Born:** [José Eduardo dos Santos](/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Eduardo_dos_Santos), President of Angola, in [Luanda](/wiki/Luanda), [Angola](/wiki/Angola) (d. [2022](/wiki/2022))

## [August 29](/wiki/August_29), 1942 (Saturday)

- British destroyer *[Eridge](/wiki/HMS_Eridge_(L68))* was permanently disabled off [El Daba](/wiki/RAF_El_Daba), Egypt by an Italian torpedo boat.
- The German [Tiger I](/wiki/Tiger_I) tank made its battlefield debut southeast of Leningrad.
- Japanese submarine *[Ro-33](/wiki/Japanese_submarine_Ro-33)* was depth charged and sunk near [Port Moresby](/wiki/Port_Moresby) by the Australian destroyer *[Arunta](/wiki/HMAS_Arunta_(I30))*.
- The [Red Cross](/wiki/International_Red_Cross_and_Red_Crescent_Movement) announced that Japan had refused free passage of ships carrying food, medicine and other necessities for American prisoners of war.
- German submarine *[U-385](/wiki/German_submarine_U-385)* was commissioned.
- **Died:** [Charles Urban](/wiki/Charles_Urban), 75, American-born British film producer and distributor

## [August 30](/wiki/August_30), 1942 (Sunday)

- [Operation Wunderland](/wiki/Operation_Wunderland) ended in German success.
- The [Battle of Alam el Halfa](/wiki/Battle_of_Alam_el_Halfa) began south of [El Alamein](/wiki/El_Alamein).
- The U.S. Army occupied [Adak Island](/wiki/Adak_Island). Runways would be constructed there over the next two weeks allowing for air strikes against the nearby Japanese-held islands of [Attu](/wiki/Attu_Island) and [Kiska](/wiki/Kiska).
- **Born:** [Jonathan Aitken](/wiki/Jonathan_Aitken), politician, in [Dublin](/wiki/Dublin), Ireland

## [August 31](/wiki/August_31), 1942 (Monday)

- The [Battle of Isurava](/wiki/Battle_of_Isurava) ends in a Japanese victory, as Australian forces conduct a strategic withdrawal under Japanese pressure.
- The American aircraft carrier [USS *Saratoga*](/wiki/USS_Saratoga_(CV-3)) was torpedoed by the Japanese submarine *[I-26](/wiki/Japanese_submarine_I-26)* and had to undergo three months of repairs.
- [Harro](/wiki/Harro_Schulze-Boysen) and [Libertas Schulze-Boysen](/wiki/Libertas_Schulze-Boysen) of the anti-Nazi resistance group known as the [Red Orchestra](/wiki/Red_Orchestra_(espionage)) were arrested by the [Gestapo](/wiki/Gestapo).
- [British Commandos](/wiki/British_Commandos) began [Operation Anglo](/wiki/Operation_Anglo), a raid on the island of [Rhodes](/wiki/Rhodes).
- **Born:** [Isao Aoki](/wiki/Isao_Aoki), golfer, in [Abiko, Chiba](/wiki/Abiko,_Chiba), [Japan](/wiki/Japan)
- **Died:** [Georg von Bismarck](/wiki/Georg_von_Bismarck), 51, German commander of [21st Panzer Division](/wiki/21st_Panzer_Division) (killed in action during the [Battle of Alam el Halfa](/wiki/Battle_of_Alam_el_Halfa))

**Sachin Nag** ([Bengali](/wiki/Bengali_language): শচীন নাগ; 5 July 1920 – 19 August 1987) was an Indian [swimmer](/wiki/Swimming_(sport)). He won the gold medal in [men's 100 m freestyle](/wiki/Swimming_at_the_1951_Asian_Games) at the [1951 Asian Games](/wiki/1951_Asian_Games), the first ever gold medal for India at the [Asian Games](/wiki/Asian_Games). It remains the only gold medal won by India in [Asian Games swimming](/wiki/Swimming_at_the_Asian_Games) as of the conclusion of the [2022 Asian Games](/wiki/2022_Asian_Games). He was also a member of the bronze medal-winning Indian teams in the [men's 4 × 100 m freestyle relay](/wiki/Swimming_at_the_1951_Asian_Games) and the [men's 3x100 m medley relay](/wiki/Swimming_at_the_1951_Asian_Games) at the 1951 Asian Games.

He competed in the [men's 100 m freestyle](/wiki/Swimming_at_the_1948_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_100_metre_freestyle) at the [1948 Summer Olympics](/wiki/1948_Summer_Olympics). He also competed in the [water polo](/wiki/Water_polo) at the 1948 and [1952 Summer Olympics](/wiki/1952_Summer_Olympics). He scored four goals on behalf of India.

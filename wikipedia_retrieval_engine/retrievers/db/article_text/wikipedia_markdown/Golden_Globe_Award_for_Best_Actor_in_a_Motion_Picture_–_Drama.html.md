# Golden Globe Award for Best Actor in a Motion Picture – Drama

## Infobox

- **Awarded for:** Best Performance by a Leading Actor in a Drama
- **Location:** United States
- **Presented by:** Dick Clark Productions
- **Currently held by:** Wagner Moura for The Secret Agent (2025)
- **Website:** goldenglobes.com

**The Golden Globe Award for Best Actor in a Motion Picture – Drama** is a [Golden Globe Award](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Awards) that was first awarded by the [Hollywood Foreign Press Association](/wiki/Hollywood_Foreign_Press_Association) as a separate category in 1951. Previously, there was a single award for "Best Actor in a Motion Picture" but the splitting allowed for recognition of it and the [Best Actor – Musical or Comedy](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actor_%E2%80%93_Motion_Picture_Musical_or_Comedy).

The formal title has varied since its inception. In 2005, it was officially called: "Best Performance by an Actor in a Motion Picture – Drama". As of 2013, the wording is "Best Actor in a Motion Picture – Drama".

## Firsts

- [José Ferrer](/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Ferrer) became the first Latino as well as the first Puerto Rican actor to win in [1951](/wiki/8th_Golden_Globe_Awards).
- [Sidney Poitier](/wiki/Sidney_Poitier) became the first actor of African descent as well as the first Bahamian actor to win in [1963](/wiki/20th_Golden_Globe_Awards).
- [Omar Sharif](/wiki/Omar_Sharif) became the first actor of Middle Eastern descent as well as the first Egyptian actor to win in [1966](/wiki/23rd_Golden_Globe_Awards).
- [Peter Finch](/wiki/Peter_Finch) became the first actor to win posthumously in [1977](/wiki/34th_Golden_Globe_Awards).
- [Ben Kingsley](/wiki/Ben_Kingsley) became the first actor of Asian descent to win in [1983](/wiki/40th_Golden_Globe_Awards).
- [Wagner Moura](/wiki/Wagner_Moura) became the first actor to win for a foreign language/non-English language performance in [2025](/wiki/83rd_Golden_Globe_Awards).

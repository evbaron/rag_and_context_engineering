# Haribomo

**Haribomo**  is a [rural commune](/wiki/Communes_of_Mali) of the [Cercle of Gourma-Rharous](/wiki/Gourma-Rharous_Cercle) in the [Tombouctou Region](/wiki/Tombouctou_Region) of [Mali](/wiki/Mali). The commune contains 29 villages and in the 2009 census had a population of 7,389. The principal village (*[chef-lieu](/wiki/Chef-lieu)*) is [Daka Fifo](/wiki/Daka_Fifo).

**Kristen Anne Bell** (born July 18, 1980) is an American actress and singer. [Her work](/wiki/List_of_Kristen_Bell_performances) includes both film and television, and [her accolades](/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Kristen_Bell) include two [Emmy Awards](/wiki/Emmy_Awards), in addition to nominations for three [Golden Globe Awards](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Awards). In 2025, *[Time](/wiki/Time_(magazine))* included her in their selection of [the 100 most influential people in the world](/wiki/Time_100).

Bell began her acting career starring in stage productions, while attending the [Tisch School of the Arts](/wiki/Tisch_School_of_the_Arts) at [New York University](/wiki/New_York_University). She made her [Broadway](/wiki/Broadway_theatre) stage debut as [Becky Thatcher](/wiki/Becky_Thatcher) in the comedy musical *[The Adventures of Tom Sawyer](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Sawyer_(musical))* (2001) and appeared in a Broadway revival of *[The Crucible](/wiki/The_Crucible)* (2002). She then appeared in the action film *[Spartan](/wiki/Spartan_(film))* and received praise for her performance in the television drama film *[Gracie's Choice](/wiki/Gracie%27s_Choice)* (both 2004).

Bell had her [breakout performance](/wiki/Breakthrough_role) as [the title character](/wiki/Veronica_Mars_(character)) in the television series *[Veronica Mars](/wiki/Veronica_Mars)* (2004–2007), a role she reprised in the [2014 spin-off film](/wiki/Veronica_Mars_(film)) and the [2019 revival](/wiki/Veronica_Mars_(season_4)). She also starred as [Elle Bishop](/wiki/Elle_Bishop) in the superhero drama series *[Heroes](/wiki/Heroes_(American_TV_series))* (2007–2008), voiced the titular narrator in the teen drama series *[Gossip Girl](/wiki/Gossip_Girl)* (2007–2012) and its [2021 standalone sequel](/wiki/Gossip_Girl_(2021_TV_series)), and starred in the [Showtime](/wiki/Showtime_(TV_network)) comedy series *[House of Lies](/wiki/House_of_Lies)* (2012–2016). She received nominations for the [Golden Globe Award for Best Actress](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actress_-_Television_Series_Musical_or_Comedy) for her leading roles in the [NBC](/wiki/NBC) comedy series *[The Good Place](/wiki/The_Good_Place)* (2016–2020) and the [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) romantic comedy series *[Nobody Wants This](/wiki/Nobody_Wants_This)* (2024); the latter also earned her two [Primetime Emmy Award](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award) nominations, for [Outstanding Lead Actress in a Comedy Series](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actress_in_a_Comedy_Series) and [Outstanding Comedy Series](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Comedy_Series).

During her time on *Veronica Mars*, Bell starred in the television musical film *[Reefer Madness: The Movie Musical](/wiki/Reefer_Madness:_The_Movie_Musical)* (2005) and the horror film *[Pulse](/wiki/Pulse_(2006_film))* (2006). She has since appeared in a number of comedy films, including *[Forgetting Sarah Marshall](/wiki/Forgetting_Sarah_Marshall)* (2008), *[Couples Retreat](/wiki/Couples_Retreat)* (2009), *[When in Rome](/wiki/When_in_Rome_(2010_film))* (2010), *[You Again](/wiki/You_Again)* (2010), *[Burlesque](/wiki/Burlesque_(2010_American_film))* (2010), *[Safety Not Guaranteed](/wiki/Safety_Not_Guaranteed)* (2012), *[Stuck in Love](/wiki/Stuck_in_Love_(film))* (2012), *[The Lifeguard](/wiki/The_Lifeguard)* (2013), *[The Boss](/wiki/The_Boss_(2016_film))* (2016), *[Bad Moms](/wiki/Bad_Moms)* (2016), *[A Bad Moms Christmas](/wiki/A_Bad_Moms_Christmas)* (2017), *[How to Be a Latin Lover](/wiki/How_to_Be_a_Latin_Lover)* (2017), *[CHiPs](/wiki/CHiPs_(film))* (2017), *[Like Father](/wiki/Like_Father)* (2018), *[Queenpins](/wiki/Queenpins)* (2021), *[The People We Hate at the Wedding](/wiki/The_People_We_Hate_at_the_Wedding)* (2022), and *[Paw Patrol: The Mighty Movie](/wiki/Paw_Patrol:_The_Mighty_Movie)* (2023). She received further recognition for voicing [Princess Anna](/wiki/Anna_(Frozen)) in the Disney animated films *[Frozen](/wiki/Frozen_(2013_film))* (2013) and *[Frozen 2](/wiki/Frozen_2)* (2019). She is also the voice of [Amy Rose](/wiki/Amy_Rose) from the upcoming *[Sonic the Hedgehog 4](/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog_4_(film))*.

## Early life and education

Kristen Anne Bell was born on July 18, 1980, in [Huntington Woods](/wiki/Huntington_Woods,_Michigan), a suburb of [Detroit](/wiki/Detroit). Her mother, Lorelei Jo ([née](/wiki/Birth_name#Maiden_and_married_names) Frygier), was a registered nurse, and her father, Thomas Michael "Tom" Bell, was a television news director.

Kristen described her mother as "very religious," which put a strain on their relationship. Her mother is of Polish descent, and her father has German, Scottish, and Irish ancestry. Her parents divorced when she was six months old; both have remarried, and Bell has two half-sisters from her father's second marriage and four step-siblings from her mother's second marriage.

At the age of four, Bell said she did not like her first name, so her mother encouraged her to use her middle name, Anne, which she continued to use until high school. Just before her first year of high school, Bell's parents removed her from the public school system. She attended [Shrine Catholic High School](/wiki/Shrine_Catholic_High_School) in nearby [Royal Oak](/wiki/Royal_Oak,_Michigan), where she participated in the drama and music clubs.

Before Shrine Catholic, she was a student at Burton Elementary School in Huntington Woods. At Shrine, she won the starring role in the school's 1997 production of *[The Wizard of Oz](/wiki/The_Wizard_of_Oz_(1987_musical))* as [Dorothy Gale](/wiki/Dorothy_Gale) and appeared in productions of *[Fiddler on the Roof](/wiki/Fiddler_on_the_Roof)* (1995), *[Lady, Be Good](/wiki/Lady,_Be_Good_(musical))* (1996), and *[Li'l Abner](/wiki/Li%27l_Abner_(musical))* (1998). In 1998, the year she graduated, she was named the yearbook's "Best Looking Lil' Lady" via a senior class vote.

Shortly after high school, Bell moved to [Manhattan](/wiki/Manhattan) to attend [New York University](/wiki/New_York_University)'s [Tisch School of the Arts](/wiki/Tisch_School_of_the_Arts), studying [musical theater](/wiki/Musical_theater). She left about six credits shy of graduating to play a role in a Broadway musical, saying in an interview with [iVillage](/wiki/IVillage), "You get to the point where you don't need a degree to be an actor."

## Career

### 1992–2003: Early work

In 1992, Bell went to her first audition and won a dual role as a banana and a tree in a suburban Detroit theater's production of *[Raggedy Ann and Andy](/wiki/Raggedy_Ann_and_Andy)*. Her mother had established her with an agent before Bell was 13, which allowed her to appear in newspaper advertisements for several Detroit retailers and television commercials. She began private acting lessons. In 1998, she had an uncredited role in the film *[Polish Wedding](/wiki/Polish_Wedding)*. In 2001, Bell left New York University to play [Becky Thatcher](/wiki/List_of_Tom_Sawyer_characters#Becky_Thatcher) in the short-lived Broadway musical of *[The Adventures of Tom Sawyer](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Sawyer_(musical))*. That year, she made her credited film debut in *[Pootie Tang](/wiki/Pootie_Tang)*. Her single line of dialogue was cut, and she appears only during the credits.

In 2002, she appeared in the Broadway revival of *[The Crucible](/wiki/The_Crucible)* with [Liam Neeson](/wiki/Liam_Neeson), [Angela Bettis](/wiki/Angela_Bettis) and [Laura Linney](/wiki/Laura_Linney). Bell moved to Los Angeles in 2002 because of her friendship with writers [Kevin Murphy](/wiki/Kevin_Murphy_(screenwriter)) and Dan Studney, and appeared in a handful of television shows as a special guest, finding trouble gaining a recurring role in a television series. Bell said she "tested like eight times and booked nothing and every show [she] tested for got picked up", including auditions for *[Skin](/wiki/Skin_(American_TV_series))* and what she called ["that Norm Macdonald show"](/wiki/A_Minute_with_Stan_Hooper). In 2003, Bell appeared in FX's *The Shield*, season 2, episode 1, which aired on January 7, 2003. She appeared in *Everwood*, season 2 episode 2 as a cheerleader. She co-starred in the film *The King and Queen of Moonlight Bay* as Alison, a 17-year-old girl who travels to Arizona to reconnect with the father who abandoned her family.

### 2004–2006: *Veronica Mars* and other roles

In 2004, Bell earned acclaim for her starring role in the [Lifetime](/wiki/Lifetime_(TV_network)) television film *[Gracie's Choice](/wiki/Gracie%27s_Choice)*, which received one of the network's highest ratings. She made her debut in a theatrically released film with [David Mamet](/wiki/David_Mamet)'s action thriller *[Spartan](/wiki/Spartan_(film))*, as Laura Newton, the kidnapped daughter of the U.S. president, acting alongside [Val Kilmer](/wiki/Val_Kilmer). Bell guest-starred on the [HBO](/wiki/HBO) period drama series *[Deadwood](/wiki/Deadwood_(TV_series))*, in a two-episode story arc ("[Bullock Returns to the Camp](/wiki/Bullock_Returns_to_the_Camp_(Deadwood_episode))" and "[Suffer the Little Children](/wiki/Suffer_the_Little_Children_(Deadwood_episode))").

Bell on the set of *Veronica Mars* in 2004

At 24, she won the role of the title character in the [UPN](/wiki/UPN) noir drama series *[Veronica Mars](/wiki/Veronica_Mars)*, which was launched in the fall of 2004. Created by [Rob Thomas](/wiki/Rob_Thomas_(writer)), the series starred Bell as the seventeen-year-old anti-establishment high school student and private detective. Bell drew on the parallels between the character of Veronica and her own life, since Bell's parents had divorced and her best friend had also died. The series earned acclaim from critics. Bell's performance earned her the [Saturn Award for Best Actress on Television](/wiki/Saturn_Award_for_Best_Actress_on_Television) and a nomination for the [TCA Award for Individual Achievement in Drama](/wiki/TCA_Award_for_Individual_Achievement_in_Drama), among numerous others. Some critics asserted that her performance was overlooked, and deserved consideration for a [Primetime Emmy Award](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award).

Aside from working on *Veronica Mars*, Bell starred in *[Reefer Madness: The Movie Musical](/wiki/Reefer_Madness:_The_Movie_Musical)*, reprising the role she played in the short-lived 2001 [Off-Broadway](/wiki/Off-Broadway) musical. The musical was a spoof of *[Reefer Madness](/wiki/Reefer_Madness)*, the 1936 exploitation film. *Reefer Madness: The Movie Musical* debuted on the [Showtime](/wiki/Showtime_(TV_network)) network on April 16, 2005. Also in April, Bell starred as Gracie in *[Fifty Pills](/wiki/Fifty_Pills)*, an entry for the [Tribeca Film Festival](/wiki/Tribeca_Film_Festival). She appeared in a short independent film called *The Receipt* and the horror film *Roman*, which was directed by her *Crucible* co-star Angela Bettis. Released on August 11, 2006, *[Pulse](/wiki/Pulse_(2006_film))* starred Bell as the lead Mattie. A remake of the Japanese horror film *[Kairo](/wiki/Pulse_(2001_film))*, the film grossed US$27.9 million worldwide but garnered negative response from critics. Frank Scheck of *[The Hollywood Reporter](/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter)* commented, "despite the starring presence of Kristen Bell, [the] young actress has far less interesting material to work with here than she does as [the character] 'Veronica Mars.'"

### 2007–2011: Film breakthrough and *Gossip Girl*

*Veronica Mars* continued on UPN for a second season; for the third season, the show was renewed and appeared on the newly created [The CW](/wiki/The_CW). On January 19, 2007, CW Entertainment President [Dawn Ostroff](/wiki/Dawn_Ostroff) announced that while she was pleased with the gradual improvement of *Veronica Mars*'s ratings, the series would be put on hiatus after February sweeps to air a new reality series, *[Pussycat Dolls Present](/wiki/Pussycat_Dolls_Present:_The_Search_for_the_Next_Doll)*. On May 17, 2007, Ostroff announced the cancelation of the series. A two-hour series finale aired in the United States on May 22, 2007, and on June 11, 2007, Thomas officially announced in an email to *[TV Guide](/wiki/TV_Guide)*'s Michael Ausiello that *Veronica Mars* had been canceled by The CW. A *Veronica Mars* feature film and comic book series continuation had been discussed, and for a short time there was talk of another collaboration between Bell and creator Thomas that would be unrelated to the *Veronica Mars* series.

Following the cancellation of *Veronica Mars*, Bell voiced interest in appearing on *Heroes* because she was a fan. On July 29, 2007, during a train ride back to Los Angeles from [San Diego Comic-Con](/wiki/San_Diego_Comic-Con) with *Heroes* actors [Zachary Quinto](/wiki/Zachary_Quinto) and [Masi Oka](/wiki/Masi_Oka), and writers from the series, the writers had mentioned that if she "ever want[ed] to come on *Heroes*, give us [writers] a call," to which Bell said she would "love to". Meanwhile, there were discussions about a role on *[Lost](/wiki/Lost_(2004_TV_series))*, but Bell turned down the role of [Charlotte Staples Lewis](/wiki/Charlotte_Staples_Lewis). Bell portrayed [Elle Bishop](/wiki/Elle_Bishop) on *Heroes*, a "mysterious young lady" with an "awesome power". She did not have to audition for the role of Elle, who made her first appearance in an October 2007 episode, and appeared in twelve episodes during the run of the series. The casting of Bell, *Heroes* creator [Tim Kring](/wiki/Tim_Kring) explained, "was not easy to pull off", but because of the large ensemble cast of the series and multiple [story arcs](/wiki/Story_arc), "we found a way to jump into a small window in [Bell's] schedule." Bell lent her voice to The CW series *[Gossip Girl](/wiki/Gossip_Girl)*; she voiced the titular character in every episode of the series, appearing in person only for a surprise cameo in the final episode, portraying herself.

Bell at the 2008 [Tribeca Film Festival](/wiki/Tribeca_Film_Festival)

Shortly after the cancelation of *Veronica Mars* in early 2007, Bell filmed on location in [Hawaii](/wiki/Hawaii) for a starring role as the title character in the [Judd Apatow](/wiki/Judd_Apatow) comedy *[Forgetting Sarah Marshall](/wiki/Forgetting_Sarah_Marshall)*. She regarded the [improvisational comedy](/wiki/Improvisational_comedy) in the film as "a lovely experience". The film, written by and also starring [Jason Segel](/wiki/Jason_Segel), was released theatrically on April 18, 2008, and greatly increased Bell's profile. Bell lent her voice and likeness to the video game *[Assassin's Creed](/wiki/Assassin%27s_Creed_(video_game))*, which was released in November 2007 for consoles and April 2008 for the PC. Bell reprised her role of Lucy in *[Assassin's Creed II](/wiki/Assassin%27s_Creed_II)*, released in November 2009, and again in *[Assassin's Creed: Brotherhood](/wiki/Assassin%27s_Creed:_Brotherhood)*, released in November 2010. In the spring of 2006, she finished filming the *[Star Wars](/wiki/Star_Wars)*-themed comedy *[Fanboys](/wiki/Fanboys_(film))*. Director [Kyle Newman](/wiki/Kyle_Newman) received additional funding to shoot new scenes, but the busy schedules of the actors only allowed for filming in September 2007. As a result, the release was delayed until January 14, 2008. Bell starred in the 2009 comedies *[Serious Moonlight](/wiki/Serious_Moonlight_(2009_film))*, alongside [Meg Ryan](/wiki/Meg_Ryan), and *[Couples Retreat](/wiki/Couples_Retreat)*, which chronicles four couples who partake in therapy sessions at a tropical island resort. [Jason Bateman](/wiki/Jason_Bateman) played her husband. She provided the voice for Cora in *[Astro Boy](/wiki/Astro_Boy_(film))*. On March 31, 2008, Bell began shooting the [Mark Steven Johnson](/wiki/Mark_Steven_Johnson)-written [Disney](/wiki/Disney) film *[When in Rome](/wiki/When_in_Rome_(2010_film))* on location in Rome and New York; the film was released in 2010. Bell reprised her role as Sarah Marshall for a cameo appearance in the film *[Get Him to the Greek](/wiki/Get_Him_to_the_Greek)*, a spin-off sequel from *Forgetting Sarah Marshall*, released June 4, 2010.

Bell co-starred alongside singers [Christina Aguilera](/wiki/Christina_Aguilera) and [Cher](/wiki/Cher) in the backstage musical film *[Burlesque](/wiki/Burlesque_(2010_American_film))*, which was released in November 2010. She had a cameo appearance alongside [Anna Paquin](/wiki/Anna_Paquin) in the slasher horror film *[Scream 4](/wiki/Scream_4)*, which was released on April 15, 2011.

### 2012–2019: *Frozen* and *The Good Place*

In 2012, Bell starred in the family drama film *[Big Miracle](/wiki/Big_Miracle)*. She appeared in the music video for "Madder Red" by Brooklyn [experimental rock](/wiki/Experimental_rock) band [Yeasayer](/wiki/Yeasayer). Bell portrayed [Mary Magdalene](/wiki/Mary_Magdalene) in *The Truth & Life Dramatized Audio New Testament Bible*, a 22-hour, celebrity-voiced, dramatized audio adaptation of the [New Testament](/wiki/New_Testament) that uses the [RSV-CE](/wiki/Revised_Standard_Version_Catholic_Edition) translation.

Bell at the premiere of *[Frozen](/wiki/Frozen_(2013_film))* in 2013 at the El Capitan Theatre

Bell starred as Jeannie van der Hooven, the female lead on the [Showtime](/wiki/Showtime_(TV_network)) comedy series *[House of Lies](/wiki/House_of_Lies)*, which premiered on January 8, 2012. The series ended on June 12, 2016. Bell appeared in a supporting role in the science-fiction comedy *[Safety Not Guaranteed](/wiki/Safety_Not_Guaranteed)* (2012). She starred in the drama film *[The Lifeguard](/wiki/The_Lifeguard)*, written and directed by [Liz W. Garcia](/wiki/Liz_W._Garcia), which began filming in July 2012 and was released in August 2013. She voiced [Anna](/wiki/Anna_(Frozen)) in *[Frozen](/wiki/Frozen_(2013_film))*, which was released on November 27, 2013. In 2013, for multiple episodes, Bell played Ingrid de Forest, an Eagleton City Councilwoman, on *[Parks and Recreation](/wiki/Parks_and_Recreation)*.

On March 13, 2013, it was confirmed that a *[Veronica Mars](/wiki/Veronica_Mars_(film))* film would be coming to fruition. Bell and series creator [Rob Thomas](/wiki/Rob_Thomas_(writer)) launched a fundraising campaign to produce the film through [Kickstarter](/wiki/Kickstarter) and attained the $2 million goal in less than ten hours. The main cast members of the series all reprised their roles in the feature film. Production of the film took place during summer 2013, and it was released theatrically and on video-on-demand on March 14, 2014.

In September 2014, Bell starred with her husband [Dax Shepard](/wiki/Dax_Shepard) in a commercial for the [Samsung Galaxy Tab S](/wiki/Samsung_Galaxy). It was so popular (with over 20 million [YouTube](/wiki/YouTube) views) that they did another for the holiday season. The ad agency [McKinney](/wiki/McKinney_(advertising_agency)) was behind both. In 2016, Bell voiced the [sloth](/wiki/Sloth) Priscilla in the animated comedy film *[Zootopia](/wiki/Zootopia)*, and starred as Claire in the comedy film *[The Boss](/wiki/The_Boss_(2016_film))*. Bell starred as Kiki in the 2016 comedy film *[Bad Moms](/wiki/Bad_Moms)*, a role she reprised in the 2017 sequel, *[A Bad Moms Christmas](/wiki/A_Bad_Moms_Christmas)*. In 2016, Bell began starring as Eleanor Shellstrop in the [NBC](/wiki/NBC) comedy series *[The Good Place](/wiki/The_Good_Place)*. For her performance, she received a nomination for the [Golden Globe Award for Best Actress – Television Series Musical or Comedy](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actress_%E2%80%93_Television_Series_Musical_or_Comedy), the [TCA Award for Individual Achievement in Comedy](/wiki/TCA_Award_for_Individual_Achievement_in_Comedy), and the [Critics' Choice Television Award for Best Actress in a Comedy Series](/wiki/Critics%27_Choice_Television_Award_for_Best_Actress_in_a_Comedy_Series).

Also in 2017, she appeared in the biographical comedy-drama *[The Disaster Artist](/wiki/The_Disaster_Artist_(film))*, the action comedy *[CHiPs](/wiki/Chips_(film))* and the comedy *[How to Be a Latin Lover](/wiki/How_to_Be_a_Latin_Lover)*. In November 2017, she played a housewife (with [Dax Shepard](/wiki/Dax_Shepard) as her husband) preparing for a Christmas party in [Sia](/wiki/Sia)'s music video for "[Santa's Coming for Us](/wiki/Santa%27s_Coming_for_Us)".

In 2018, she began hosting the web series *Momsplaining with Kristen Bell*, with episodes airing on the [Ellen DeGeneres](/wiki/Ellen_DeGeneres) video platform Ellentube. In the series, Bell gives new and expecting mothers tips about motherhood. The title of the series is a pun on the commonly used term "[mansplaining](/wiki/Mansplaining)". Bell later had the leading role in the [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) comedy-drama film *[Like Father](/wiki/Like_Father)*. She voiced the character of Jade Wilson in the animated comedy film *[Teen Titans Go! To the Movies](/wiki/Teen_Titans_Go!_To_the_Movies)* and reprised her voice role as Princess Anna in the animated comedy sequel film *[Ralph Breaks the Internet](/wiki/Ralph_Breaks_the_Internet)*.

On September 20, 2018, [Hulu](/wiki/Hulu) confirmed that Bell would reprise her role of Veronica Mars in an eight-episode [fourth season](/wiki/Veronica_Mars_(season_4)) of the drama series *Veronica Mars*, which would premiere in July 2019. In 2019, she again reprised her role of Princess Anna in the video game *[Kingdom Hearts III](/wiki/Kingdom_Hearts_III)* and the sequel *[Frozen 2](/wiki/Frozen_2)* which was released on November 22, 2019. Bell served as host and executive producer on the [Disney+](/wiki/Disney%2B) docuseries *[Encore!](/wiki/Encore!_(TV_series))*, which premiered in November 2019. She made her directorial debut with the eighth episode of the fourth season of *The Good Place*. The series concluded after its fourth season, airing its final episode in January 2020. At the [25th Critics' Choice Awards](/wiki/25th_Critics%27_Choice_Awards), Bell received the #SeeHer Award, which recognizes women who "push boundaries on changing stereotypes and acknowledge the importance of authentic portrayals of women across the entertainment landscape".

### 2020–present

In 2020, Bell published the children's book *The World Needs More Purple People*, which she co-wrote with [Benjamin Hart](/wiki/Benjamin_Hart). Also in 2020, Bell voiced Molly Tillerman in the [Apple TV+](/wiki/Apple_TV%2B) animated musical comedy series *[Central Park](/wiki/Central_Park_(TV_series))*, which reunited her with *Frozen* co-star [Josh Gad](/wiki/Josh_Gad) and *Bad Moms* co-star [Kathryn Hahn](/wiki/Kathryn_Hahn). *Central Park* received a two-season order from Apple and the series premiered on May 29, 2020. In June 2020, it was announced that Bell, who is white, would no longer voice the biracial character of Molly. The role would be re-cast with a black or mixed race actress, with Bell voicing a new role.

In 2021, Bell produced and voiced a lead character in the [Amazon Prime Video](/wiki/Amazon_Prime_Video) animated musical pre-school series *Do, Re, & Mi*. She returned to narrate as the titular character of the [HBO Max](/wiki/HBO_Max) teen drama series *[Gossip Girl](/wiki/Gossip_Girl_(2021_TV_series))*, a soft reboot and sequel to the 2007–2012 series *[Gossip Girl](/wiki/Gossip_Girl)*. Bell starred alongside [Kirby Howell-Baptiste](/wiki/Kirby_Howell-Baptiste) as a pair of housewives who created a multi-million dollar coupon scam in the comedy film *[Queenpins](/wiki/Queenpins)*. The film was released to [Paramount+](/wiki/Paramount%2B) on September 10, 2021.

Her miniseries *[The Woman in the House Across the Street from the Girl in the Window](/wiki/The_Woman_in_the_House_Across_the_Street_from_the_Girl_in_the_Window)* was released on [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) in January 2022. In November 2022, Bell starred in the Amazon Prime movie *[The People We Hate at the Wedding](/wiki/The_People_We_Hate_at_the_Wedding)*. Bell appeared in three episodes of *[The Tiny Chef Show](/wiki/The_Tiny_Chef_Show)* and serves as the show's executive producer.

Bell also played the main character, an agnostic sex podcaster by the name of Joanne, in *[Nobody Wants This](/wiki/Nobody_Wants_This)* (2024). Her performance earned her another Golden Globe nomination for Best Actress as well her first nomination for [Primetime Emmy Award](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award) in her career for [Outstanding Lead Actress in a Comedy Series](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actress_in_a_Comedy_Series) and [Outstanding Comedy Series](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Comedy_Series) as series executive producer.

In February 2026, Bell was announced to have been cast as the voice of [Amy Rose](/wiki/Amy_Rose) in the upcoming live-action/animated film *[Sonic the Hedgehog 4](/wiki/Sonic_the_Hedgehog_4_(film))*.

## Public image

In 2006 and again in 2013, Bell was selected "World's Sexiest [Vegetarian](/wiki/Vegetarianism)" on [PETA](/wiki/People_for_the_Ethical_Treatment_of_Animals)'s yearly poll. She was placed 68 on [*Maxim*](/wiki/Maxim_(magazine))'s 2005 "Hot 100" list, 11 in *Maxim*'s 2006 "Hot 100" list, and 46 in *Maxim*'s 2007 "Hot 100" list in which she was stated to have "single-handedly saved [The CW](/wiki/The_CW) from becoming the worst network ever". In 2006, *Maxim* also placed Bell at the top of the "Fall TV's Criminally Sexy Investigators" List. In 2008, she was ranked 59 on [AskMen](/wiki/AskMen)'s Top 99 Women of 2008 List. Reflecting on her admitted popularity with "geeks", Bell was voted the fourth-sexiest woman on TV by the staff at [*Wizard* magazine](/wiki/Wizard_(magazine)).

Bell stated she never thought of herself as womanly because "I always play and look and act 10 years younger than I am." However, she said, "Something magical happened when I turned 25 — I looked in the mirror and was like, 'You might not get carded for an R-rated movie anymore.' Like I didn't have a little stick figure anymore." Bell has said that many of her characters are [tomboys](/wiki/Tomboy) because she was "not homely enough to play the [nerdy](/wiki/Nerd) girl and not nearly pretty enough to play the pretty girl".

Bell signing autographs in 2006 at the [Metreon](/wiki/Metreon) in [San Francisco](/wiki/San_Francisco)

Bell has been associated with the idea that "[nerdy](/wiki/Nerd) is the new cool", and she explained, "what was previously perceived as nerdy is now viewed as original. What I like about nerdiness, [geekiness](/wiki/Geek), is it doesn't really matter what you're into—it just means you're not a follower." She has also said, "I love nerds. [Comic-Con](/wiki/Comic-Con_International) junkies are the tastemakers of tomorrow. Isn't that funny? The tables have turned." Vanessa Juarez of *[Entertainment Weekly](/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly)* commented that Bell's roles on *Veronica Mars*, *Heroes* and as a *[Star Wars](/wiki/Star_Wars)* fanatic in *[Fanboys](/wiki/Fanboys_(film))* have "solidif[ied] her placement at the center of the geek universe," while [Rodney Rothman](/wiki/Rodney_Rothman) stated, "I guess she's cornered the market on losers." Bell's work is often compared to [Sarah Michelle Gellar](/wiki/Sarah_Michelle_Gellar)'s portrayal of the [title character](/wiki/Buffy_Summers) on the television series *[Buffy the Vampire Slayer](/wiki/Buffy_the_Vampire_Slayer)*. Frank Scheck of *[The Hollywood Reporter](/wiki/The_Hollywood_Reporter)* stated that Bell was "arguably the television successor [to Gellar's portrayal of Buffy] when it comes to fighting bad guys." Bell is sometimes confused with [Lauren Conrad](/wiki/Lauren_Conrad) from *[The Hills](/wiki/The_Hills_(TV_series))*. "Yeah, sometimes fans yell, 'Hey, Lauren' to me, but usually from a distance," said Bell.

Despite "new celebrity" status, Bell claimed that she was not concerned because "no one ever recognizes me anyway". She has said that her friend [Hayden Panettiere](/wiki/Hayden_Panettiere) is more famous than she is and attracts more attention; as Bell explained, "I hang out with Hayden quite a bit—they never take pictures of me. I just step to the side, and I push myself in front of her when she wants to get out of it, or put her in the car."

Bell was a recurring guest on *[The Late Late Show with Craig Ferguson](/wiki/The_Late_Late_Show_with_Craig_Ferguson)*, appearing in interviews as well as sketches. On *The Late Late Show*, she repeatedly showed a humorous hostility towards [Craig Ferguson](/wiki/Craig_Ferguson)'s robot skeleton sidekick [Geoff Peterson](/wiki/Geoff_Peterson), claiming that she had wanted to be Ferguson's sidekick on his show and taking it upon herself to cut Peterson down every chance she got. Both Bell and Peterson appeared with Ferguson during the five *Late Late Show* episodes filmed in France in 2011.

Bell's star on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame).

In January 2011, it was announced that Bell would be the new face of [Neutrogena](/wiki/Neutrogena). In 2014, Bell posed nude for the May issue of *[Allure](/wiki/Allure_(magazine))* magazine – alongside [Jenna Dewan](/wiki/Jenna_Dewan), [Minnie Driver](/wiki/Minnie_Driver), and [Nia Long](/wiki/Nia_Long). In April 2019, Bell was featured on the cover of *[Entrepreneur](/wiki/Entrepreneur_(magazine))* magazine's April–May issue. In the article, she discussed her snack bar company, This Bar Saves Lives, which donates its sales to help feed malnourished children around the globe. In November 2019, Bell and [Idina Menzel](/wiki/Idina_Menzel), who play sisters in [Disney's](/wiki/The_Walt_Disney_Company) [*Frozen* franchise](/wiki/Frozen_(franchise)), received neighboring stars—Bell's was the 2681st and Menzel's was the 2682nd—on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame).

## Personal life

### Relationships and family

[Dax Shepard](/wiki/Dax_Shepard) and Bell at the 2012 [DoSomething](/wiki/DoSomething) Awards

In 2007, Bell ended a five-year relationship with former fiancé Kevin Mann. She later told *[Complex](/wiki/Complex_(magazine))* magazine that dating "makes me want to vomit. And not out of grossness—OK, a little bit out of grossness, but just nerves... I've always been a [serial monogamist](/wiki/Serial_monogamy)."

In late 2007, Bell began dating actor [Dax Shepard](/wiki/Dax_Shepard), who is also from the [Detroit metropolitan area](/wiki/Metro_Detroit). They announced their engagement in January 2010. They decided to delay marriage until the state of California passed legislation legalizing [same-sex marriage](/wiki/Same-sex_marriage). They co-starred in the 2010 film *[When in Rome](/wiki/When_in_Rome_(2010_film))*, the 2012 film *[Hit and Run](/wiki/Hit_and_Run_(2012_film))*, and the 2017 film *[CHiPs](/wiki/CHiPs_(film))*. After section 3 of the [Defense of Marriage Act](/wiki/Defense_of_Marriage_Act) was ruled unconstitutional by the [Supreme Court](/wiki/U.S._Supreme_Court) on June 26, 2013, Bell asked Shepard to marry her through Twitter, which he accepted. They were married at the [Beverly Hills](/wiki/Beverly_Hills,_California) County Clerk's Office on October 16, 2013. They have two daughters, born in March 2013 and December 2014.

### Mental health

In May 2016, Bell said that she has received treatment for [depression](/wiki/Depression_(mood)) and [anxiety](/wiki/Anxiety_disorder). She said, "It's important for me to be candid about this so people in a similar situation can realize that they are not worthless and that they do have something to offer." In a March 2020 blog post, Bell wrote, "Don't be fooled by this game of perfection that humans play. Because Instagram and magazines and TV shows, they strive for a certain aesthetic, and everything looks so beautiful, and people seem like they don't have any problems, but everyone's human. Everyone has problems. Everyone feels yucky on the inside sometimes."

In an interview with [Jimmy Kimmel](/wiki/Jimmy_Kimmel) in August 2018, Bell discussed why she wears scuba gloves while swimming. She stated that she fears the sensation of touching things with water-immersed, wrinkled fingers, otherwise known as "[prune fingers](/wiki/Prune_fingers)". The gloves have allowed her to swim with her children without fearing touching them.

### Beliefs and interests

Bell with the Dancing Merengue Dog at the [2012 Bonnaroo Music Festival](/wiki/2012_Bonnaroo_Music_Festival)

At age 11, Bell became a [vegetarian](/wiki/Vegetarianism). In an interview with [PETA](/wiki/PETA), Bell discussed her pets, "I have always been an animal lover. I had a hard time disassociating the animals I cuddled with—dogs and cats, for example—from the animals on my plate, and I never really cared for the taste of meat. I always loved my [Brussels sprouts](/wiki/Brussels_sprout)!" By 2012, Bell and Shepard had become [vegan](/wiki/Veganism) after watching the documentary *[Forks Over Knives](/wiki/Forks_Over_Knives)*. However, during her first pregnancy she went back to eating dairy products and eggs. She started eating meat in 2022.

Bell has said that she is [non-religious](/wiki/Non-religious) and identifies as a [humanist](/wiki/Humanism). She and her husband Dax Shepard are pro-[vaccination](/wiki/Vaccination). Bell supported and campaigned for [Barack Obama](/wiki/Barack_Obama) during the [2008 United States presidential election](/wiki/2008_United_States_presidential_election). Along with [Rashida Jones](/wiki/Rashida_Jones), she visited college campuses in [Missouri](/wiki/Missouri) to discuss the candidates and encourage voter registration. Bell showed support for the [Writers Guild of America](/wiki/Writers_Guild_of_America) in the [writers' strike](/wiki/2007%E2%80%9308_Writers_Guild_of_America_strike), appearing in the picket lines in December 2007, stating, "the writers are just looking for some fairness".

In 2023, Bell revealed that she had taken up [Brazilian jiu-jitsu](/wiki/Brazilian_jiu-jitsu). She was training under Cesalina Gracie in mother-daughter classes alongside her daughter.

### Charity work

During her time in Michigan, Bell fostered animals from the [Michigan Humane Society](/wiki/Michigan_Humane_Society), and she now supports the San Diego–based [Helen Woodward Animal Center](/wiki/Helen_Woodward_Animal_Center). Bell often attends fundraisers for the [American Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals](/wiki/American_Society_for_the_Prevention_of_Cruelty_to_Animals) and other non-profit organizations dedicated to protecting animals. She has had several dogs, including a [Welsh Corgi](/wiki/Welsh_Corgi)-[Chow Chow](/wiki/Chow_Chow) mix named Lola, a Welsh Corgi-[Chihuahua](/wiki/Chihuahua_(dog_breed)) mix named Shakey and a black [Labrador Retriever](/wiki/Labrador_Retriever) named Sadie, who was 11-years-old when she was rescued from [Hurricane Katrina](/wiki/Hurricane_Katrina) and adopted by Bell in 2005.

Bell and other *Veronica Mars* cast members, including [Ryan Hansen](/wiki/Ryan_Hansen), are involved with the charity [Invisible Children, Inc.](/wiki/Invisible_Children,_Inc.) The goal of the organization is to create awareness of the plight of [Northern Ugandans](/wiki/Northern_Uganda) who are caught in the midst of a [civil war](/wiki/Civil_war) between the government and [Joseph Kony](/wiki/Joseph_Kony)'s [Lord's Resistance Army](/wiki/Lord%27s_Resistance_Army). In 2014, Bell launched a [Prizeo](/wiki/Prizeo) campaign offering fans a chance to win a date with her in return for donating to Invisible Children, Inc. In 2018, Bell participated in a television advertisement for bone marrow donations, specifically the Gift of Life Marrow Registry. Her actions resulted in several matches that are documented on the Gift of Life website. Bell won a [2020 Webby Special Achievement Award](/wiki/2020_Webby_Award).

In 2013, Kristen Bell, along with actors [Ryan Devlin](/wiki/Ryan_Devlin), [Todd Grinnell](/wiki/Todd_Grinnell), and [Ravi Patel](/wiki/Ravi_Patel_(actor)), founded the granola bar company This Bar Saves Lives to help fight malnutrition. The business donates food aid in the form of nutrition packets to children in [Haiti](/wiki/Haiti), [South Sudan](/wiki/South_Sudan), [Ethiopia](/wiki/Ethiopia), and [Somalia](/wiki/Somalia). Since its launch, the company has donated more than 10 million packets as of February 2019.

### Entrepreneurship

In 2019, Bell and her husband founded the company Hello Bello that markets plant-based baby care products said to be environmentally friendly and affordable. As of 2021, Hello Bello's estimated annual revenue was $26.1 million a year with 104 employees. In 2020, she partnered to start Happy Dance, a line of vegan CBD products such as eye cream and hand moisturizers. In 2023, Hello Bello filed for [Chapter 11 bankruptcy](/wiki/Chapter_11_bankruptcy), blaming years of declining sales and the financial startup crisis. The company is set to be acquired by private equity firm Hildred Capital Management.

# Albert Einstein Medal

This article is about the medal. For the similarly named award, see [Albert Einstein Award](/wiki/Albert_Einstein_Award).

The **Albert Einstein Medal** is an award presented by the [Albert Einstein Society](/wiki/Albert_Einstein_Society) in [Bern](/wiki/Bern). First given in 1979, the award is presented to people for "scientific findings, works, or publications related to [Albert Einstein](/wiki/Albert_Einstein)" each year.

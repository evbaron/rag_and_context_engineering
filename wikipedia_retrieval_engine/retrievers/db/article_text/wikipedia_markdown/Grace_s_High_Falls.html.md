# Grace's High Falls

**Grace's High Falls** is a seasonal waterfall in the US state of [Alabama](/wiki/Alabama), in [Little River Canyon National Preserve](/wiki/Little_River_Canyon_National_Preserve). The waterfall is Alabama's highest at 133 feet.

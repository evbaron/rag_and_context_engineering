# Solomon Sea

The **Solomon Sea** is a [sea](/wiki/Sea) located within the [Pacific Ocean](/wiki/Pacific_Ocean). It lies between [Papua New Guinea](/wiki/Papua_New_Guinea) and [Solomon Islands](/wiki/Solomon_Islands). Many major battles were fought there during [World War II](/wiki/World_War_II).

## Extent

The [International Hydrographic Organization](/wiki/International_Hydrographic_Organization) defines the limits of the Solomon Sea as follows:

> *On the Northwest.* By the Southeast limit of [Bismarck Sea](/wiki/Bismarck_Sea) [A line from the Southern point of [New Ireland](/wiki/New_Ireland_(island)) along the parallel of 4°50' South to the coast of [New Britain](/wiki/New_Britain), along its Northern coast and thence a line from its Western extreme through the Northern point of [Umboi Island](/wiki/Umboi_Island) to Teliata Point, [New Guinea](/wiki/New_Guinea) ([5°55′S 147°24′E / 5.917°S 147.400°E / -5.917; 147.400](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Solomon_Sea&params=5_55_S_147_24_E_))].
>
> *On the Northeast.* By a line from the Southern point of New Ireland to the North point of [Buka Island](/wiki/Buka_Island), through this island to the Northwest point of [Bougainville Island](/wiki/Bougainville_Island), along the Southern coasts of Bougainville, Choisel [[Choiseul](/wiki/Choiseul_Island)], Ysabel [[Santa Isabel](/wiki/Santa_Isabel_Island)], [Malaita](/wiki/Malaita) and San Cristobal [[Makira](/wiki/Makira)] Islands.
>
> *On the South.* The Northern limit of the Coral Sea between San Cristobal Island, [Solomon Islands](/wiki/Solomon_Islands), and Gado-Gadoa Island, off the Southeast extreme of [New Guinea](/wiki/New_Guinea) [Gado-Gadoa Island near its Southeastern extreme ([10°38′S 150°34′E / 10.633°S 150.567°E / -10.633; 150.567](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Solomon_Sea&params=10_38_S_150_34_E_)), down this meridian to the 100-fathom [600 ft; 183 m] line and thence along the Southern edges of Uluma (Suckling) Reef and those extending to the Eastward as far as the Southeast point of Lawik Reef ([11°43.5′S 153°56.5′E / 11.7250°S 153.9417°E / -11.7250; 153.9417](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Solomon_Sea&params=11_43.5_S_153_56.5_E_)) off Tagula Island [[Vanatinai](/wiki/Vanatinai)], thence a line to the Southern extreme of [Rennell Island](/wiki/Rennell_Island) and from its Eastern point to Cape Surville, the Eastern extreme of San Cristobal Island].
>
> *On the Southwest.* By the coast of New Guinea and a line from its Southeasternmost point through the [Louisiade Archipelago](/wiki/Louisiade_Archipelago) to [Rossel Island](/wiki/Rossel_Island_(Yela)).

## Etymology

The Solomon Sea is named after the [Solomon Islands archipelago](/wiki/Solomon_Islands_(archipelago)). These islands received their name "Islas Salomón" from the legend of the biblical land of [Ophir](/wiki/Ophir) — fabled as the source of King [Solomon](/wiki/Solomon)'s wealth which was hoped to be discovered, in the first 1568 voyage by their discoverer Spanish navigator [Álvaro de Mendaña de Neira](/wiki/%C3%81lvaro_de_Menda%C3%B1a_de_Neira). They were so named after his voyage when it was mapped.

## Deepest point

The Solomon Sea roughly corresponds with the [Solomon Sea plate](/wiki/Solomon_Sea_plate), a tectonic feature, and includes the [New Britain Trench](/wiki/New_Britain_Trench), in the [New Britain subduction zone](/wiki/New_Britain_subduction_zone), which reaches its maximum depth at 29,988 feet (9,140 m) below sea level in the Planet Deep.

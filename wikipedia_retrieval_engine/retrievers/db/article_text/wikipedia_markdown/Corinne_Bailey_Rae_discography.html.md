English singer and songwriter [Corinne Bailey Rae](/wiki/Corinne_Bailey_Rae) has released four [studio albums](/wiki/Studio_album), one [live album](/wiki/Live_album), four [extended plays](/wiki/Extended_play), 13 [singles](/wiki/Single_(music)) (including five as a featured artist), one [promotional single](/wiki/Promotional_single) and 13 [music videos](/wiki/Music_video).

Rae released her [eponymous debut album](/wiki/Corinne_Bailey_Rae_(album)) in February 2006. It debuted atop the [UK Albums Chart](/wiki/UK_Albums_Chart), while reaching the top five in Ireland and the United States, and the top 10 in Canada, New Zealand and Switzerland. *Corinne Bailey Rae* was certified triple platinum by the [British Phonographic Industry](/wiki/British_Phonographic_Industry) (BPI) and platinum by the [Recording Industry Association of America](/wiki/Recording_Industry_Association_of_America) (RIAA), selling over four million copies worldwide. The album spawned four singles, including "[Put Your Records On](/wiki/Put_Your_Records_On)", which peaked at number two on the [UK Singles Chart](/wiki/UK_Singles_Chart).

Rae's second studio album, *[The Sea](/wiki/The_Sea_(Corinne_Bailey_Rae_album))*, was released in January 2010, reaching number five on the UK Albums Chart, number seven on the US [*Billboard* 200](/wiki/Billboard_200) and number 13 on the [Canadian Albums Chart](/wiki/Canadian_Albums_Chart). It was later certified gold by the BPI. The album was promoted by three singles: "[I'd Do It All Again](/wiki/I%27d_Do_It_All_Again)", "[Paris Nights/New York Mornings](/wiki/Paris_Nights/New_York_Mornings)" and "[Closer](/wiki/Closer_(Corinne_Bailey_Rae_song))". In May 2016, Rae released her third studio album, *[The Heart Speaks in Whispers](/wiki/The_Heart_Speaks_in_Whispers)*, which peaked at number 14 on the UK Albums Chart.

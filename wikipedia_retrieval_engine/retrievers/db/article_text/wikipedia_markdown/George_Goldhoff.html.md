**George Goldhoff** (born January 14, 1995) is an American tennis player who specializes in doubles.
He has a career high [ATP](/wiki/Association_of_Tennis_Professionals) doubles ranking of No. 101 achieved on 4 August 2025.
He has won five [ATP Challenger](/wiki/ATP_Challenger_Tour) doubles titles in his career.

Goldhoff played college tennis at [Texas](/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin).

## Career

He won his first two ATP Challenger titles at the [2024 Cleveland Open](/wiki/2024_Cleveland_Open) and at the [2024 Challenger La Manche](/wiki/2024_Challenger_La_Manche) in Cherbourg, France with [James Trotter](/wiki/James_Trotter_(tennis)). Following a third Challenger title in [Modena](/wiki/2024_Modena_Challenger), Italy partnering [Jonathan Eysseric](/wiki/Jonathan_Eysseric) he reached the top 150 in the rankings on 15 July 2024.

**Ben Darwin** (born 17 October 1976) is an Australian former [rugby union](/wiki/Rugby_union) player and coach. He played 28 times for the [Wallabies](/wiki/Wallabies_(rugby_union)), Australia's national team, from 2001 to 2003. During this time, Darwin played games against the [British and Irish Lions](/wiki/British_and_Irish_Lions) and was part of the Wallabies' [2003 Rugby World Cup](/wiki/2003_Rugby_World_Cup) team. His usual position was [tighthead prop](/wiki/Rugby_union_positions#1._Loosehead_prop_.26_3._Tighthead_prop), although on occasion he played on the loosehead side.

## Playing career

Darwin made his international debut for Australia in June 2001, coming off the bench against the touring [Lions](/wiki/Great_Britain_national_rugby_league_team) in [Brisbane](/wiki/Brisbane). He also played in the [2001 Tri Nations Series](/wiki/2001_Tri_Nations_Series) later that year, and was capped against [Spain](/wiki/Spain_rugby_union_team), [England](/wiki/England_national_rugby_union_team), [France](/wiki/France_national_rugby_union_team) and [Wales](/wiki/Wales_national_rugby_union_team). He played in the [2002 Tri Nations Series](/wiki/2002_Tri_Nations_Series), as well as being capped another four times during the end of year tour. He played 11 times for Australia in 2003, and was included in the [2003 World Cup squad](/wiki/2003_Rugby_World_Cup_squads#.C2.A0Australia). It was during the Wallabies' World Cup [semi-final](/wiki/2003_Rugby_World_Cup#Semi-finals) win against the [All Blacks](/wiki/All_Blacks) that Darwin sustained a neck injury that forced him to retire from rugby.

## Coaching career

After retirement, Darwin made the switch from player to coach, starting at club rugby level with Sydney's [Northern Suburbs](/wiki/Norths_rugby) where he was appointed head coach in 2005. In 2006, Darwin was involved with the start up of the Super 14 club, the [Western Force](/wiki/Western_Force), as the Forwards Coach until mid-2006. In 2007, Darwin was selected to be a part of the commentary team covering the [2007 Rugby World Cup](/wiki/2007_Rugby_World_Cup) in France for the Australian television network, Channel 10. While he France, Darwin was also in training for his first [Iron Man](/wiki/Iron_Man) triathlon, which he completed in Port Macquarie in April 2008. He finished his second Iron Man, again at Port Macquarie, the following year.

At the beginning of June 2008, Darwin moved to [Tokyo](/wiki/Tokyo) where he served as the Forwards Coach for the Japanese Top League Rugby team, the [NTT Shining Arcs](/wiki/NTT_Shining_Arcs), a team owned by Japanese telecommunications giant NTT. Darwin was integral to the club's promotion from the 2nd Division Top East competition to Top League at the end of the 09/10 season.
At the beginning of 2010, Darwin married a writer/journalist from [Melbourne](/wiki/Melbourne). Shortly after, Darwin and his wife returned to Australia in order for Darwin to take up the Scrum Coach/Video Analyst position for the [Melbourne Rebels](/wiki/Melbourne_Rebels) [Super Rugby](/wiki/Super_Rugby) franchise team.

Darwin writes regular columns on rugby matters for www.rugbyzone.com.
Darwin began Gain Line Analytics in 2013 and works with leading sporting clubs, national teams and corporate clients.

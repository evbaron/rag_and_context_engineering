**Hirosaki Station** (弘前駅, *Hirosaki-eki*) is a railway station in the city of [Hirosaki](/wiki/Hirosaki,_Aomori), [Aomori Prefecture](/wiki/Aomori_Prefecture), Japan, jointly operated by [East Japan Railway Company](/wiki/East_Japan_Railway_Company) (JR East) and the private railway operator [Kōnan Railway](/wiki/K%C5%8Dnan_Railway). It is also a freight terminal for the [Japan Freight Railway Company](/wiki/Japan_Freight_Railway_Company).

## Lines

Hirosaki Station is served by the [Ōu Main Line](/wiki/%C5%8Cu_Main_Line), and is located 447.1 km from the southern starting point of the Ōu Main Line at [Fukushima](/wiki/Fukushima_Station_(Fukushima)). Although the terminus of the [Gonō Line](/wiki/Gon%C5%8D_Line) is at [Kawabe](/wiki/Kawabe_Station_(Aomori)), most trains continue on to terminate at Hirosaki for ease of connections. It also forms the terminus of the 16.8 km private [Kōnan Railway Kōnan Line](/wiki/K%C5%8Dnan_Railway_K%C5%8Dnan_Line) to [Kuroishi](/wiki/Kuroishi_Station_(Aomori)).

## Station layout

Hirosaki Station is an elevated station. The JR portion has a single [side platform](/wiki/Side_platform) and an [island platform](/wiki/Island_platform), serving three tracks, and the Kōnan Railway has a [bay platform](/wiki/Bay_platform) serving another two tracks. The station has a JR East *[Midori no Madoguchi](/wiki/Midori_no_Madoguchi)* staffed ticket office and View Plaza travel agency.

## History

Hirosaki Station opened on December 1, 1894. On September 7, 1927, the Konan Railway also began operations at Hirosaki Station. The station building was again reconstructed in 1981 to incorporate a shopping center. With the [privatization](/wiki/Privatization) of [JNR](/wiki/JNR) on April 1, 1987, the station came under the operational control of JR East. A new station building was completed in December 2004.

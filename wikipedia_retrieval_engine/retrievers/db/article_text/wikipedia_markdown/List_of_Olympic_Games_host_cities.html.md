# List of Olympic Games host cities

For individual summer and winter lists, see [List of Summer Olympic Games](/wiki/Summer_Olympic_Games#List_of_Summer_Olympic_Games) and [List of Winter Olympic Games](/wiki/Winter_Olympic_Games#List_of_Winter_Olympic_Games)

For villages that house Olympic athletes, see [List of Olympic Villages](/wiki/List_of_Olympic_Villages).

In [the SVG file,](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/bc/Olympic_games_host_cities.svg) tap or hover over a city to show its name (only on the desktop).

The [modern Olympics](/wiki/Modern_Olympics) began in 1896. Since then, summer and winter games have usually celebrated a four-year period known as an Olympiad. From the inaugural Winter Games in 1924 until 1992, winter and summer Games were held in the same year. Since 1994, summer and winter Games have been held in staggered even years. The last Olympic games were held in both Milan and Cortina, the first to ever be hosted by two cities, in February 2026. Through 2026, there have been 30 [Summer Olympic Games](/wiki/Summer_Olympic_Games), held in 23 cities, and 25 [Winter Olympic Games](/wiki/Winter_Olympic_Games), held in 22 cities. In addition, three summer and two winter editions of the games were scheduled to take place but were later cancelled due to war: [Berlin](/wiki/Berlin) (summer) in [1916](/wiki/1916_Summer_Olympics); [Sapporo](/wiki/Sapporo)–[Garmisch-Partenkirchen](/wiki/Garmisch-Partenkirchen) ([winter](/wiki/1940_Winter_Olympics)) and [Tokyo](/wiki/Tokyo)–[Helsinki](/wiki/Helsinki) ([summer](/wiki/1940_Summer_Olympics)) in 1940; and [Cortina d'Ampezzo](/wiki/Cortina_d%27Ampezzo) ([winter](/wiki/1944_Winter_Olympics)) and [London](/wiki/London) ([summer](/wiki/1944_Summer_Olympics)) in 1944. The [1906 Intercalated Olympics](/wiki/1906_Intercalated_Games) were officially sanctioned and held in [Athens](/wiki/Athens). However, in 1949, the [International Olympic Committee](/wiki/International_Olympic_Committee) (IOC) decided to unrecognize the 1906 Games. The [2020 Summer Olympics](/wiki/2020_Summer_Olympics) in Tokyo were postponed for the first time in the Olympics history to summer 2021 due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic), with the [2022 Winter Olympics](/wiki/2022_Winter_Olympics) being held roughly six months later in [Beijing](/wiki/Beijing) which also hosted the [2008 Summer Olympics](/wiki/2008_Summer_Olympics).

Four cities and regions have been chosen by the [IOC](/wiki/International_Olympic_Committee) to host upcoming Olympic Games: [Los Angeles](/wiki/Los_Angeles) for the [2028 Summer Olympics](/wiki/2028_Summer_Olympics); the [2030 Winter Olympics](/wiki/2030_Winter_Olympics) will be the first Olympic Games to be hosted by a region (the [French Alps](/wiki/French_Alps)—comprising 7 cities and towns); [Brisbane](/wiki/Brisbane) will host the [2032 Summer Olympics](/wiki/2032_Summer_Olympics); and [Utah](/wiki/Utah) will host the [2034 Winter Olympics](/wiki/2034_Winter_Olympics).

In 2022, Beijing became the first city to have hosted both the Summer and Winter Olympics. By 2034, eleven cities will have hosted the Olympic Games more than once: Athens ([1896](/wiki/1896_Summer_Olympics) and [2004 Summer Olympics](/wiki/2004_Summer_Olympics)), [Paris](/wiki/Paris) ([1900](/wiki/1900_Summer_Olympics), [1924](/wiki/1924_Summer_Olympics) and [2024 Summer Olympics](/wiki/2024_Summer_Olympics)), London ([1908](/wiki/1908_Summer_Olympics), [1948](/wiki/1948_Summer_Olympics) and [2012 Summer Olympics](/wiki/2012_Summer_Olympics)), [St. Moritz](/wiki/St._Moritz) ([1928](/wiki/1928_Winter_Olympics) and [1948 Winter Olympics](/wiki/1948_Winter_Olympics)), [Lake Placid](/wiki/Lake_Placid,_New_York) ([1932](/wiki/1932_Winter_Olympics) and [1980 Winter Olympics](/wiki/1980_Winter_Olympics)), Los Angeles ([1932](/wiki/1932_Summer_Olympics), [1984](/wiki/1984_Summer_Olympics) and [2028 Summer Olympics](/wiki/2028_Summer_Olympics)), Cortina d'Ampezzo ([1956](/wiki/1956_Winter_Olympics) and [2026 Winter Olympics](/wiki/2026_Winter_Olympics)), [Innsbruck](/wiki/Innsbruck) ([1964](/wiki/1964_Winter_Olympics) and [1976 Winter Olympics](/wiki/1976_Winter_Olympics)), Tokyo ([1964](/wiki/1964_Summer_Olympics) and [2020 Summer Olympics](/wiki/2020_Summer_Olympics)), Beijing ([2008 Summer Olympics](/wiki/2008_Summer_Olympics) and [2022 Winter Olympics](/wiki/2022_Winter_Olympics)) and Salt Lake City ([2002](/wiki/2002_Winter_Olympics) and [2034 Winter Olympics](/wiki/2034_Winter_Olympics)).
[Stockholm](/wiki/Stockholm) hosted the [1912 Summer Olympics](/wiki/1912_Summer_Olympics) and the equestrian portion of the [1956 Summer Olympics](/wiki/1956_Summer_Olympics). London and Paris have both hosted three Games, while Los Angeles will become the third city to gain that distinction in 2028.

As of 2026, a large majority of the Games (42 out of 55) have been hosted in Western Europe, the United States, Canada, or Australia. Eight Games have been hosted in Asia (all in East Asia), three in Eastern Europe, and two in Latin America. Africa has yet to host an Olympic Games. Other major geographic regions and subcontinents that have never hosted the Olympics include the Middle East, Central Asia, the [Indian subcontinent](/wiki/Indian_subcontinent), Southeast Asia, and the [Caribbean](/wiki/Caribbean). Between the first Winter Olympics in 1924 and the last ones to be held in the same year as the Summer Olympics in 1992, the Summer and Winter Games took place in the same country three times.

Usually, the Games' host cities are selected by the IOC members six to seven years in advance.

Until the 2022 Winter Olympics, the selection process lasts approximately two years. In the first stage, any city in the world may apply to become a host city. After ten months, the Executive Board of the IOC decides which applicant cities will become official candidates based on the recommendation of a working group that reviews the applications. In the second stage, the candidate cities are investigated thoroughly by an Evaluation Commission, which then submits a final short list of cities for selection. The host city is then chosen by vote of the [IOC session](/wiki/List_of_IOC_meetings), a general meeting of IOC members. There was a change in host selection process in the late 2010s to address several problems – including the costs of hosting and the disappointment felt by unsuccessful applicants. Called [Olympic Agenda 2020](/wiki/Olympic_Agenda_2020), this new process is focused on reducing the cost of Games, minimising wasteful single-use construction projects and increasing the benefits felt by host nations. Bids are now easier and less expensive to prepare. The 2032 Summer Games host city was the first to be fully selected under this process, but other elements and rules were introduced later.

Africa has never hosted any Olympics. Egypt, South Africa, and Morocco have been acknowledged as future possibilities, although it is noted that increased dialogue and developments are needed.

In addition, the Middle East, though not a continent (with most of the region situated in Asia), has never hosted an Olympic Games. Several nations have been in talks as potential hosts, but the only city to enter a formal bid was [Doha](/wiki/Doha) (see also [List of bids for the Summer Olympics](/wiki/List_of_bids_for_the_Summer_Olympics)).

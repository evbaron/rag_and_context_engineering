**Preston Neville Brown** (born March 2, 1958) is an American former professional [football](/wiki/American_football) player who was a [wide receiver](/wiki/Wide_receiver) for the [New England Patriots](/wiki/New_England_Patriots), [New York Jets](/wiki/New_York_Jets), and [Cleveland Browns](/wiki/Cleveland_Browns) of the [National Football League](/wiki/National_Football_League) (NFL). He played [college football](/wiki/College_football) for the [Vanderbilt Commodores](/wiki/Vanderbilt_Commodores_football).

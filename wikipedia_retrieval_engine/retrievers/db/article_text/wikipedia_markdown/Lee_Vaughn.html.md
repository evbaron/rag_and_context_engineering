**Lee Vaughn** (born November 27, 1974) is an American former [football](/wiki/American_football) [cornerback](/wiki/Cornerback) in the [National Football League](/wiki/National_Football_League) (NFL) for the [Dallas Cowboys](/wiki/Dallas_Cowboys). He also was a member of the [BC Lions](/wiki/BC_Lions) in the [Canadian Football League](/wiki/Canadian_Football_League) (CFL). He played [college football](/wiki/College_football) at the [University of Wyoming](/wiki/University_of_Wyoming).

## Early years

Vaughn attended East High School in [Cheyenne, Wyoming](/wiki/Cheyenne,_Wyoming), where he practiced football, [basketball](/wiki/Basketball) and [track](/wiki/Track_%26_field). As a senior, he received All-state honors at [cornerback](/wiki/Cornerback) and [return specialist](/wiki/Return_specialist). He also was named the Class 4A Back of the Year.

In track as a senior, he broke the state records in the [100 metres](/wiki/100_metres), [200 metres](/wiki/200_metres) and [long jump](/wiki/Long_jump) (originally set in 1963). He also received the [Milward Simpson](/wiki/Milward_Simpson) award, given annually to the state's outstanding prep athlete.

## College career

Vaughn accepted a football scholarship from the [University of Wyoming](/wiki/University_of_Wyoming). As a freshman, he was a backup player, making 10 tackles. As a sophomore, he started 11 games at [free safety](/wiki/Free_safety), tallying 65 tackles, one interception and 3 pass deflections. He also contributed to the team winning the WAC title with an 8–4 record.

As a junior, he was moved to the starting [right cornerback](/wiki/Cornerback) position, registering 70 tackles (3 for loss), 3 interceptions, 14 pass deflections, one sack and one quarterback pressure.

As a senior, he registered 75 tackles (51 solo), one interception, 10 pass deflections and 2 fumble recoveries. He contributed to the team achieving a 10–1 regular season record, the WAC's Pacific title and a 22 ranking in the [AP Poll](/wiki/AP_Poll). He finished his college career with 220 tackles (4 for loss), 5 interceptions and 27 pass deflections.

## Professional career

### Dallas Cowboys

Vaughn was selected by the [Dallas Cowboys](/wiki/Dallas_Cowboys) in the sixth round (187th overall) of the [1997 NFL draft](/wiki/1997_NFL_draft). On July 23, he tore the anterior cruciate ligament in his right knee and was placed on the [injured reserve list](/wiki/Injured_reserve_list) on August 14. He was waived on August 24, [1998](/wiki/1998_NFL_season).

### Amsterdam Admirals (NFLEL)

Vaughn was selected by the [Amsterdam Admirals](/wiki/Amsterdam_Admirals) in the 19th round (111th overall) of the 1999 [NFL Europe](/wiki/NFL_Europe) Draft. He was released on March 29.

### Grand Rapids Rampage (AFL)

On July 3, [2001](/wiki/2001_Arena_Football_League_season), he was signed by the [Grand Rapids Rampage](/wiki/Grand_Rapids_Rampage) of the [Arena Football League](/wiki/Arena_Football_League). He was released on July 6.

### BC Lions (CFL)

On May 24, [2001](/wiki/2001_CFL_season), he was signed by the [BC Lions](/wiki/BC_Lions) of the [Canadian Football League](/wiki/Canadian_Football_League). He posted 38 defensive tackles, 2 [special teams](/wiki/Special_teams) tackles, one interception and 2 fumble recoveries. He was released on October 1.

### Wichita Stealth (AF2)

In [2003](/wiki/2003_AF2_season), he was signed by the [Wichita Stealth](/wiki/Wichita_Stealth) of the [AF2 League](/wiki/AF2). In June, he suffered a torn anterior cruciate ligament and was placed on the [injured reserve list](/wiki/Injured_reserve_list).

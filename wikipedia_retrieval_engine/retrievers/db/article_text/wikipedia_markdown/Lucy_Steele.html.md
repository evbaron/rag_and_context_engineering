**Lucy Steele** (born 12 May 1969) is a [Canadian](/wiki/Canadians) former [cross-country skier](/wiki/Cross-country_skiing_(sport)) who competed in the [1992 Winter Olympics](/wiki/1992_Winter_Olympics).

# 19th People's Choice Awards

## Infobox

- **Date:** March 9, 1993
- **Location:** Universal Studios Hollywood, Universal City, California
- **Hosted by:** John Ritter and Jane Seymour
- **Network:** CBS

The **19th [People's Choice Awards](/wiki/People%27s_Choice_Awards)**, honoring the best in [popular culture](/wiki/Popular_culture) for 1992, were held on March 9, 1993, at [Universal Studios Hollywood](/wiki/Universal_Studios_Hollywood), in [Universal City, California](/wiki/Universal_City,_California). They were hosted by [John Ritter](/wiki/John_Ritter) and [Jane Seymour](/wiki/Jane_Seymour_(actress)), and broadcast on [CBS](/wiki/CBS).

Special tributes were paid to both [Knots Landing](/wiki/Knots_Landing) and [Cheers](/wiki/Cheers) for their long runs on television.

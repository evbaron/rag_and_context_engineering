The following is a list of awards and nominations received by [Steven Spielberg](/wiki/Steven_Spielberg).

Steven Spielberg is an American filmmaker. He began his career in the [New Hollywood](/wiki/New_Hollywood) era and is currently the most [commercially successful director](/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_film_directors). Spielberg is recipient of various awards including three [Academy Awards](/wiki/Academy_Awards), four [BAFTA Awards](/wiki/British_Academy_Film_Awards), thirteen [Emmy Awards](/wiki/Emmy_Awards), five [Golden Globe Awards](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Awards), three [Directors Guild of America Awards](/wiki/Directors_Guild_of_America_Awards), seven [Producers Guild of America Awards](/wiki/Producers_Guild_of_America_Awards), a [Tony Award](/wiki/Tony_Awards), and a [Grammy Award](/wiki/Grammy_Awards).

Spielberg has received nine nominations for the [Academy Award for Best Director](/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Director) making him the third most-nominated director behind [Martin Scorsese](/wiki/Martin_Scorsese) (10) and [William Wyler](/wiki/William_Wyler) (12). Spielberg won this category twice for his [Holocaust](/wiki/Holocaust) epic *[Schindler's List](/wiki/Schindler%27s_List)* (1993) and the [World War II](/wiki/World_War_II) drama *[Saving Private Ryan](/wiki/Saving_Private_Ryan)* (1998). He holds the record for most nominations for the [Academy Award for Best Picture](/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Picture) with fourteen nominations for the [science-fiction](/wiki/Science-fiction) drama *[E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial](/wiki/E.T._the_Extra-Terrestrial)* (1982), the period drama *[The Color Purple](/wiki/The_Color_Purple_(1985_film))* (1985), *Schindler's List* (1993), *Saving Private Ryan* (1998), the war dramas *[Munich](/wiki/Munich_(2005_film))* (2005), *[Letters from Iwo Jima](/wiki/Letters_from_Iwo_Jima)* (2006), and *[War Horse](/wiki/War_Horse_(film))* (2011), the historical epics *[Lincoln](/wiki/Lincoln_(film))* (2012), *[Bridge of Spies](/wiki/Bridge_of_Spies_(film))* (2015), and *[The Post](/wiki/The_Post_(film))* (2017), the musical *[West Side Story](/wiki/West_Side_Story_(2021_film))* (2021), the [coming-of-age](/wiki/Coming-of-age) drama *[The Fabelmans](/wiki/The_Fabelmans)* (2022), the biopic *[Maestro](/wiki/Maestro_(2023_film))* (2023), and the [historical](/wiki/Historical_drama_film) [tragedy](/wiki/Tragedy) *[Hamnet](/wiki/Hamnet_(film))* (2025).

For his work on television, he won the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Animated Program](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Animated_Program) for *[A Pinky and the Brain Christmas](/wiki/A_Pinky_and_the_Brain_Christmas)* (1995), and three [Primetime Emmy Awards for Outstanding Limited or Anthology Series](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Limited_or_Anthology_Series) for the [HBO](/wiki/HBO) war drama series *[Band of Brothers](/wiki/Band_of_Brothers_(miniseries))* (2001), the science-fiction series *[Taken](/wiki/Taken_(miniseries))* (2003), and the HBO war drama series *[The Pacific](/wiki/The_Pacific_(miniseries))* (2010). He also won seven [Daytime Emmy Awards](/wiki/Daytime_Emmy_Awards) for producing the animated series *[Tiny Toon Adventures](/wiki/Tiny_Toon_Adventures)*, *[Animaniacs](/wiki/Animaniacs)*, *[Freakazoid!](/wiki/Freakazoid!)*, *[Pinky and the Brain](/wiki/Pinky_and_the_Brain)*, and *[Pinky, Elmyra & the Brain](/wiki/Pinky,_Elmyra_%26_the_Brain)*. As a producer for [Broadway](/wiki/Broadway_(theater)) productions he has received three nominations for the [Tony Award for Best Musical](/wiki/Tony_Award_for_Best_Musical). He was awarded in 2022 for producing *[A Strange Loop](/wiki/A_Strange_Loop)*, before receiving further nominations for producing both *[Water for Elephants](/wiki/Water_for_Elephants_(musical))* (2024), and *[Death Becomes Her](/wiki/Death_Becomes_Her_(musical))* (2025). He won the [Grammy Award for Best Music Film](/wiki/Grammy_Award_for_Best_Music_Film) for *[Music by John Williams](/wiki/Music_by_John_Williams)* (2026), achieving [EGOT](/wiki/EGOT) status.

Over his career, Spielberg has received various honors and honorary awards including the [BAFTA Fellowship](/wiki/BAFTA_Fellowship) in 1986, the [Irving G. Thalberg Memorial Award](/wiki/Irving_G._Thalberg_Memorial_Award) in 1987, a [Career Golden Lion](/wiki/Career_Golden_Lion) in 1993, the [AFI Life Achievement Award](/wiki/AFI_Life_Achievement_Award) in 1995, the [Honorary César](/wiki/Honorary_C%C3%A9sar) in 1995, the [Directors Guild of America's Lifetime Achievement Award](/wiki/Directors_Guild_of_America_Lifetime_Achievement_Award_%E2%80%93_Feature_Film) in 2000, a Motion Picture star on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame) in 2003, the [Kennedy Center Honors](/wiki/Kennedy_Center_Honors) in 2006, and the [Cecil B. DeMille Award](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Cecil_B._DeMille_Award) in 2009, and the [Honorary Golden Bear](/wiki/Honorary_Golden_Bear) in 2023. He was presented with the [Presidential Medal of Freedom](/wiki/Presidential_Medal_of_Freedom) from President [Barack Obama](/wiki/Barack_Obama) in 2015 and the [National Medal of Arts](/wiki/National_Medal_of_Arts) in 2024 from President [Joe Biden](/wiki/Joe_Biden). He was made an Honorary [Knight Commander of the Order of the British Empire](/wiki/Knight_Commander_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire) in 2001, and was made both a Knight and Officer of the France's [Legion of Honour](/wiki/Legion_of_Honour) in 2004 and 2008, respectively.

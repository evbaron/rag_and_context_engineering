# Monteiro-Mor Palace

## Infobox

- **Location:** Lisbon, Portugal

- **Type:** Property of Interest

The **Monteiro-Mor Palace** ([Portuguese](/wiki/Portuguese_language): Palácio do Monteiro-Mor) is a Portuguese palace located in [Lisbon](/wiki/Lisbon), [Portugal](/wiki/Portugal).

## History

Monteiro-Mor Palace is located in the [Lumiar](/wiki/Lumiar) parish in Lisbon.

The name of the palace dates back to the 18th century when the High Huntsman of the Royal House ([Portuguese](/wiki/Portuguese_language): Monteiro-Mor) D. Henrique de Noronha and his wife D. Fernao Telles da Silva occupied the palace.

The Portuguese [National Museum of Costume and Fashion](/wiki/National_Museum_of_Costume_and_Fashion) is currently installed in the palace.

The palace is surrounded by an eleven hectare botanical garden.

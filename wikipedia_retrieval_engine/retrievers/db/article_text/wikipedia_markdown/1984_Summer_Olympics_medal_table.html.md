The [1984 Summer Olympics](/wiki/1984_Summer_Olympics), officially known as the Games of the *XXIII Olympiad*, were a summer [multi-sport event](/wiki/Multi-sport_event) held in [Los Angeles](/wiki/Los_Angeles), [California](/wiki/California), United States, from July 28 to August 12. A total of 6,829 athletes from 140 nations participated in 221 events in 21 sports.

Overall, 47 nations received at least one medal, and 25 of them won at least one gold medal. Athletes from host nation [United States](/wiki/United_States_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics) won the most medals overall, with 174, and the most gold medals, with 83. The former record was the largest overall medal haul for the nation since the [1904 edition](/wiki/1904_Summer_Olympics); the latter record was the highest gold medal tally at a single Games in Olympic history and the most for a host nation. It marked the first time the United States led the medal count in both gold and overall medals since [1968](/wiki/1968_Summer_Olympics). Sports commentators noted that the absence of the [Soviet Union](/wiki/Soviet_Union_at_the_Olympics) and various other [Eastern Bloc](/wiki/Eastern_Bloc) nations stemming from a [boycott](/wiki/1984_Summer_Olympics_boycott) contributed to the highly skewed medal results benefitting the United States and other countries. [Romania](/wiki/Romania_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics) won the second most gold medals (20) and the third most total medals (53), marking its highest medal tally in history. [West Germany](/wiki/West_Germany_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics) won the third most gold medals, with 17, and the second most total medals, with 59.

Runner [Carl Lewis](/wiki/Carl_Lewis) and gymnast [Ecaterina Szabo](/wiki/Ecaterina_Szabo) won the most gold medals at the games with four each. Gymnast [Li Ning](/wiki/Li_Ning) won the greatest number of medals overall, winning six in total. [Morocco](/wiki/Morocco_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics) and [Portugal](/wiki/Portugal_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics) won their first Olympic gold medals. [Algeria](/wiki/Algeria_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics), [Dominican Republic](/wiki/Dominican_Republic_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics), [Ivory Coast](/wiki/Ivory_Coast_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics), [Syria](/wiki/Syria_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics), and [Zambia](/wiki/Zambia_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics) won their nation's first Olympic medals.

## Medal table

[Joan Benoit](/wiki/Joan_Benoit) won the inaugural [women's marathon](/wiki/Athletics_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_marathon).

[Greg Louganis](/wiki/Greg_Louganis) won the [men's 3 metre springboard](/wiki/Diving_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_3_metre_springboard) and [10 platform diving](/wiki/Diving_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_10_metre_platform) competitions.

[Michael Gross](/wiki/Michael_Gross_(swimmer)) won gold medals in the [men's 100 metre butterfly](/wiki/Swimming_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_100_metre_butterfly) and [men's 200 metre freestyle](/wiki/Swimming_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_200_metre_freestyle) swimming events.

[Sebastian Coe](/wiki/Sebastian_Coe) became the first person to successfully defend the [men's 1500 metre title](/wiki/Athletics_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_1500_metres).

[Kōji Gushiken](/wiki/K%C5%8Dji_Gushiken) won gold medals in the [men's all-around individual](/wiki/Gymnastics_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_artistic_individual_all-around) and [rings gymnastics competitions](/wiki/Gymnastics_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_rings).

The medal table is based on information provided by the [International Olympic Committee](/wiki/International_Olympic_Committee) (IOC) and is consistent with IOC convention in its published medal tables. The table uses the [Olympic medal table](/wiki/Olympic_medal_table) sorting method. By default, the table is ordered by the number of gold medals the athletes from a nation have won, where a nation is an entity represented by a [National Olympic Committee](/wiki/National_Olympic_Committee) (NOC). The number of silver medals is taken into consideration next, and then the number of bronze medals. If teams are still tied, equal ranking is given and they are listed alphabetically by their [IOC country code](/wiki/List_of_IOC_country_codes).

In the [boxing](/wiki/Boxing_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics) and [judo](/wiki/Judo_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics) events, two bronze medals are awarded in each weight class. Two gold medals (and no silver medals) were awarded for first place ties in the [women's uneven bars](/wiki/Gymnastics_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_uneven_bars), [women's balance beam](/wiki/Gymnastics_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_balance_beam), and [men's rings](/wiki/Gymnastics_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_rings) gymnastics events. Four silver medals (and no bronze) were awarded in the [men's vault](/wiki/Gymnastics_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_vault) gymnastics competition. Two bronze medals were awarded for third-place ties in both the [women's 100 metre hurdles](/wiki/Athletics_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_100_metres_hurdles) and the [men's pole vault](/wiki/Athletics_at_the_1984_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_pole_vault) competitions.

# List of Major League Baseball career home run leaders

Barry Bonds holds the record for most career home runs, hitting 762 over his 22-year career.

Giancarlo Stanton is the active home run leader with 456 and is 40th all-time.

This is a list of the 300 [Major League Baseball](/wiki/Major_League_Baseball) players who have hit the most career [home runs](/wiki/Home_runs) in regular season play (i.e., excluding playoffs or exhibition games).

In the sport of [baseball](/wiki/Baseball), a home run is a hit in which the batter scores by circling all the bases and reaching [home plate](/wiki/Home_plate) in one play, without the benefit of a fielding error. This can be accomplished either by hitting the ball out of play while it is still in fair territory (a conventional home run) or by an [inside-the-park home run](/wiki/Inside-the-park_home_run).

[Barry Bonds](/wiki/Barry_Bonds) holds the Major League Baseball home run record with 762. He passed [Hank Aaron](/wiki/Hank_Aaron), who hit 755, on August 7, 2007. The only other players to have hit 700 or more are [Babe Ruth](/wiki/Babe_Ruth) with 714, and [Albert Pujols](/wiki/Albert_Pujols) with 703. [Alex Rodriguez](/wiki/Alex_Rodriguez) (696), [Willie Mays](/wiki/Willie_Mays) (660), [Ken Griffey Jr.](/wiki/Ken_Griffey_Jr.) (630), [Jim Thome](/wiki/Jim_Thome) (612), and [Sammy Sosa](/wiki/Sammy_Sosa) (609) are the only other players to have hit [600 or more](/wiki/600_home_run_club).

[Giancarlo Stanton](/wiki/Giancarlo_Stanton) is the active home run leader and currently 40th all-time with 456.

Players in **bold face** are active as of the [2026 Major League Baseball season](/wiki/2026_Major_League_Baseball_season) (including [free agents](/wiki/Free_agent)), with the number in parentheses designating the number of home runs they have hit during the [2026 season](/wiki/2026_Major_League_Baseball_season). The last change in the cutoff for the top 300 occurred on March 29, 2026 when [Christian Yelich](/wiki/Christian_Yelich) hit his 234th career home run, tying [Carlos González](/wiki/Carlos_Gonz%C3%A1lez_(baseball)), [Gary Matthews](/wiki/Gary_Matthews), [Kevin Mitchell](/wiki/Kevin_Mitchell_(baseball)), and [Paul Molitor](/wiki/Paul_Molitor).

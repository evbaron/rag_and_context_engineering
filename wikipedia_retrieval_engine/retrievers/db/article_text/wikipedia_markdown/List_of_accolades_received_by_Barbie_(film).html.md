*[Barbie](/wiki/Barbie_(film))* is a 2023 [fantasy comedy](/wiki/Fantasy_comedy) film directed by [Greta Gerwig](/wiki/Greta_Gerwig) from a screenplay she wrote with [Noah Baumbach](/wiki/Noah_Baumbach). It is the first live-action film based on the [eponymous fashion dolls](/wiki/Barbie) by [Mattel](/wiki/Mattel), following numerous [animated films and specials](/wiki/List_of_Barbie_films). The film stars [Margot Robbie](/wiki/Margot_Robbie) as the titular character and [Ryan Gosling](/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) as [Ken](/wiki/Ken_(doll)), and follows the duo on a journey of self-discovery after the former experiences an [existential crisis](/wiki/Existential_crisis); Robbie also serves as one of the film's producers. The [ensemble supporting cast](/wiki/Ensemble_cast) includes [America Ferrera](/wiki/America_Ferrera), [Kate McKinnon](/wiki/Kate_McKinnon), [Issa Rae](/wiki/Issa_Rae), [Rhea Perlman](/wiki/Rhea_Perlman), and [Will Ferrell](/wiki/Will_Ferrell).

*Barbie* had its world premiere at the [Shrine Auditorium](/wiki/Shrine_Auditorium_and_Expo_Hall) in [Los Angeles](/wiki/Los_Angeles) on July 9, 2023, followed by its European premiere at [Empire Leicester Square](/wiki/Empire_Leicester_Square) in [London](/wiki/London) on July 12. The film was released theatrically in the United States, the United Kingdom, and most other territories on July 21 through [Warner Bros. Pictures](/wiki/Warner_Bros._Pictures). Its concurrent release with [Universal Pictures](/wiki/Universal_Pictures)'s *[Oppenheimer](/wiki/Oppenheimer_(film))* was the catalyst for the "[Barbenheimer](/wiki/Barbenheimer)" phenomenon, encouraging audiences to see both films as a [double feature](/wiki/Double_feature). Produced on a budget of $128–145 million, *Barbie* grossed $1.447 billion worldwide. It became the [highest-grossing film of 2023](/wiki/2023_in_film#Highest-grossing_films), the [highest-grossing comedy film](/wiki/List_of_highest-grossing_comedy_films) of all time, the highest-grossing film by a solo female director (and the first to cross the $1 billion mark), and the highest-grossing release in Warner Bros. Pictures history. *Barbie* received widespread critical acclaim, with particular praise towards its direction, screenplay, cast performances, cinematography, costume design, production design, and music. On the [review aggregator](/wiki/Review_aggregator) website [Rotten Tomatoes](/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), the film holds an approval rating of 88% based on 507 reviews.

*Barbie* garnered awards and nominations in a wide assortment of categories, with recognition for its direction, screenplay, performances, music, and production values. At the [96th Academy Awards](/wiki/96th_Academy_Awards), the film received eight nominations including [Best Picture](/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Picture), winning [Best Original Song](/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Original_Song) for "[What Was I Made For?](/wiki/What_Was_I_Made_For%3F)". Its [accompanying soundtrack album](/wiki/Barbie_the_Album) and [score](/wiki/Barbie_(score)) were further recognized at the [66th Annual Grammy Awards](/wiki/66th_Annual_Grammy_Awards), earning twelve nominations and winning in three categories. *Barbie* received a leading nine nominations at the [81st Golden Globe Awards](/wiki/81st_Golden_Globe_Awards), including [Best Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Motion_Picture_%E2%80%93_Musical_or_Comedy), and won the inaugural [Cinematic and Box Office Achievement award](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Cinematic_and_Box_Office_Achievement). At the [29th Critics' Choice Awards](/wiki/29th_Critics%27_Choice_Awards), *Barbie's* eighteen nominations (leading to six award wins) were the most for any film in the organization's history. The film also received five nominations at the [77th British Academy Film Awards](/wiki/77th_British_Academy_Film_Awards). Both the [National Board of Review](/wiki/National_Board_of_Review) and the [American Film Institute](/wiki/American_Film_Institute) named *Barbie* one of the Top Ten Films of 2023. It received 24 nominations from the Online Film and Television Association awards, the most nominations ever for a film from those awards.

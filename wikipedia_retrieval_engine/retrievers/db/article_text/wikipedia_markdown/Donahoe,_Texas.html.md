**Donahoe** is a [ghost town](/wiki/Ghost_town) in [Bell County](/wiki/Bell_County,_Texas), in the [U.S. state](/wiki/U.S._state) of [Texas](/wiki/Texas). It is located within the [Killeen-Temple-Fort Hood metropolitan area](/wiki/Killeen-Temple-Fort_Hood_metropolitan_area).

## Geography

Donahoe was located on Donahoe Creek, 16 mi (26 km) southeast of [Belton](/wiki/Belton,_Texas) in the southeastern corner of Bell County.

## Education

Today, Donahoe is located within the [Bartlett Independent School District](/wiki/Bartlett_Independent_School_District).

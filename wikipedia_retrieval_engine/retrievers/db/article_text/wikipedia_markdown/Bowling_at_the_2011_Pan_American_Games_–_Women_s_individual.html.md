# Bowling at the 2011 Pan American Games – Women's individual

## Infobox

- **Venue:** Bolearmo Tapatio
- **Dates:** October 24 – 27

The **women's [individual](/wiki/Individual)** competition of the [bowling](/wiki/Bowling) events at the **[2011 Pan American Games](/wiki/2011_Pan_American_Games)** will take place between the 24 and 27 of October at the Bolearmo Tapatio. The [defending](/wiki/Bowling_at_the_2007_Pan_American_Games#Events) [Pan American Games](/wiki/Pan_American_Games) champion is Tennelle Milligan of the [United States](/wiki/United_States), while the defending Pan American Championship champion is [Karen Marcano](/wiki/Karen_Marcano) of [Venezuela](/wiki/Venezuela).

## Qualification round

In the qualification round, the bowlers are ranked according to their performance across twelve bowling games. The top sixteen bowlers advance to the knockout round.

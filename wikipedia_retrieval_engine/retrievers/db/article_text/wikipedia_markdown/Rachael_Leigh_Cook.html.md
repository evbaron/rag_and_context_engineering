# Rachael Leigh Cook

**Rachael Leigh Cook** (born October 4, 1979) is an American actress and model. She has starred in the films *[The Baby-Sitters Club](/wiki/The_Baby-Sitters_Club_(film))* (1995), *[She's All That](/wiki/She%27s_All_That)* (1999), and *[Josie and the Pussycats](/wiki/Josie_and_the_Pussycats_(film))* (2001), and in the television series *[Into the West](/wiki/Into_the_West_(miniseries))* and *[Perception](/wiki/Perception_(TV_series))*. She is also the voice behind various characters in *[Robot Chicken](/wiki/Robot_Chicken)* and [Tifa Lockhart](/wiki/Tifa_Lockhart) in the *[Final Fantasy](/wiki/Final_Fantasy)* series, starting with the English version of the film *[Final Fantasy VII: Advent Children](/wiki/Final_Fantasy_VII:_Advent_Children)*. Since 2016, her television appearances have primarily been [made-for-TV movies](/wiki/Television_film) on the [Hallmark Channel](/wiki/Hallmark_Channel).

## Early life

Rachael Leigh Cook was born on October 4, 1979, in [Minneapolis](/wiki/Minneapolis), Minnesota, the daughter of Thomas Howard Cook, a [social worker](/wiki/Social_work) and former [stand-up comedian](/wiki/Stand-up_comedian), and JoAnn, a cooking instructor and [weaver](/wiki/Weaving). She is of part English and Italian descent. Cook first appeared in a [public service announcement](/wiki/Public_service_announcement) for [foster care](/wiki/Foster_care) at seven years of age and began working as a [child](/wiki/Child_modeling) [print model](/wiki/Model_(person)#Commercial_print_models) at the age of 10, including nationwide advertisements for [Target](/wiki/Target_Corporation) and appearing on the boxes of [Milk-Bone](/wiki/Milk-Bone) dog biscuits. She attended [Clara Barton Open School](/wiki/Clara_Barton#Places_named_for_Clara_Barton), [Laurel Springs School](/wiki/Laurel_Springs_School), and [Minneapolis South High School](/wiki/South_High_School_(Minneapolis)).

## Career

Cook at the Toronto International Film Festival in 2007

Cook began auditioning for acting work at age 14. She made her screen debut as an actress in the 1995 film *[The Baby-Sitters Club](/wiki/The_Baby-Sitters_Club_(film))*. She also played a role in the adventure film *[Tom and Huck](/wiki/Tom_and_Huck)*, released in December 1995. In 1996, her modeling agency sent her to star in a short film, *26 Summer Street*. In 1997, Cook appeared in a leading role in the film *[Country Justice](/wiki/Country_Justice)* as a 15-year-old [rape](/wiki/Rape) victim who is impregnated by her rapist.

In 1999, Cook starred in her breakout role in the [romantic comedy](/wiki/Romantic_comedy) film *[She's All That](/wiki/She%27s_All_That)*, the most financially successful film of her career. In 2000, she starred opposite [Elijah Wood](/wiki/Elijah_Wood) in the well-received *[The Bumblebee Flies Anyway](/wiki/The_Bumblebee_Flies_Anyway)*. She took the lead role in 2001's *[Josie and the Pussycats](/wiki/Josie_and_the_Pussycats_(film))*, which turned out to be a [box office failure](/wiki/Box_office_bomb), although it has since become a [cult classic](/wiki/Cult_film).

In 2000, she was the [cover girl](/wiki/Cover_girl) for the U.S. March/April issue of *[FHM](/wiki/FHM)*. She also starred in the [music video](/wiki/Music_video) for [New Found Glory](/wiki/New_Found_Glory)'s 2000 single "[Dressed to Kill](/wiki/New_Found_Glory_(album)#Track_listing)". In 2002, she was ranked No. 26 in *[Stuff](/wiki/Stuff_(magazine))* magazine's "102 Sexiest Women in the World". In 2003, she starred in the film *[11:14](/wiki/11:14)* as Cheri. She also appeared as a main cast member in the 2005 television [miniseries](/wiki/Miniseries) *[Into the West](/wiki/Into_the_West_(TV_miniseries))* produced by [Steven Spielberg](/wiki/Steven_Spielberg). In 2006, she appeared in the music video for [Daniel Powter](/wiki/Daniel_Powter)'s "[Love You Lately](/wiki/Love_You_Lately)".

In 2007, Cook was seen in the big screen adaptation of *[Nancy Drew](/wiki/Nancy_Drew_(2007_film))*. She played the female lead in the independent sports drama *[The Final Season](/wiki/The_Final_Season)*. She has appeared in numerous episodes of the [Seth Green](/wiki/Seth_Green) comedies *[Titan Maximum](/wiki/Titan_Maximum)* and *[Robot Chicken](/wiki/Robot_Chicken)*. In 2008, she guest-starred as [Abigail Lytar](/wiki/Abigail_Lytar) in two third-season episodes of the [USA Network](/wiki/USA_Network) series *[Psych](/wiki/Psych)*. She reprised the role in the [following season](/wiki/Psych_(season_4)).

In February 2010, Cook signed on to play the female lead role in [Fox TV](/wiki/Fox_Broadcasting_Company)'s comedy pilot *Nirvana*. She had a role in the Western horror film *[Vampire](/wiki/Vampire_(2011_film))*, the [English-language](/wiki/English-language) feature debut of [Japanese](/wiki/Japan) director [Iwai Shunji](/wiki/Iwai_Shunji). In 2012, Cook signed on to play the female lead role in the [TNT](/wiki/TNT_(U.S._TV_network)) [crime drama](/wiki/Crime_drama) series *[Perception](/wiki/Perception_(TV_series))* opposite [Eric McCormack](/wiki/Eric_McCormack). She starred in the independent film *Broken Kingdom*, which was directed by her husband [Daniel Gillies](/wiki/Daniel_Gillies#Personal_life). She also appeared in a *[Funny or Die](/wiki/Funny_or_Die)* sketch with [Chad Michael Murray](/wiki/Chad_Michael_Murray).

Cook in 2018

Cook starred in the [Hallmark Channel](/wiki/Hallmark_Channel) original film *Summer Love* in 2016. In the same year, she starred in another Hallmark film, *Autumn [In the Vineyard](/wiki/In_the_Vineyard)*, followed by its sequel *Summer in the Vineyard* in 2017. 2017 also saw Cook develop, star and executive produce the Hallmark Channel film *[Frozen in Love](/wiki/Frozen_in_Love)*, which was broadcast in January 2018 as part of the channel's 'Winterfest' season of programming.

Cook provided the voice for Chelsea Cunningham on the [Kids' WB](/wiki/Kids%27_WB) animated series *[Batman Beyond](/wiki/Batman_Beyond)* and in the animated film *[Batman Beyond: Return of the Joker](/wiki/Batman_Beyond:_Return_of_the_Joker)*. Cook voiced [Tifa Lockhart](/wiki/Tifa_Lockhart) in the [video games](/wiki/Video_game) *[Kingdom Hearts II](/wiki/Kingdom_Hearts_II)*, *[Dirge of Cerberus: Final Fantasy VII](/wiki/Dirge_of_Cerberus:_Final_Fantasy_VII)* and *[Dissidia 012 Final Fantasy](/wiki/Dissidia_012_Final_Fantasy)*, as well as the film *[Final Fantasy VII Advent Children](/wiki/Final_Fantasy_VII_Advent_Children)*. In 2011, she voiced the character of Jaesa Willsaam in the MMO game, *[Star Wars: The Old Republic](/wiki/Star_Wars:_The_Old_Republic)*. Cook's latest voice-over role is for the video game *[Yakuza](/wiki/Yakuza_(video_game))* in which she voices the role of Reina. In 2020 Cook appeared in the fifteenth season of *[Criminal Minds](/wiki/Criminal_Minds)* playing the role of Max in the final episodes of the series.

Cook owns her own production company, Ben's Sister Productions (in reference to her younger brother Ben Cook). She produced and starred in the film *[Love, Guaranteed](/wiki/Love,_Guaranteed)*, which debuted on [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) on September 3, 2020.

## Public service

Cook first gained national attention in 1997, when she was featured in an updated *[This Is Your Brain on Drugs](/wiki/This_Is_Your_Brain_on_Drugs)* [public service announcement](/wiki/Public_service_announcement) (PSA) television advertisement, in which she proceeds to destroy a kitchen with a [frying pan](/wiki/Frying_pan).

In 2011, she was selected by the [Obama administration](/wiki/Obama_administration) as a Champion of Change for Arts Education.

In June 2012, she began to award a small scholarship to students between ages 14 and 19. The scholarship helps pay for career classes, mentoring programs, and other school fees.

In 2017, Rachael Leigh Cook reprised her "This Is Your Brain on Drugs" role twenty years later for a PSA by the [Drug Policy Alliance](/wiki/Drug_Policy_Alliance) critiquing the [war on drugs](/wiki/War_on_drugs) and its alleged contribution to mass incarceration, [societal racism](/wiki/Societal_racism) and poverty. The ad was posted to [YouTube](/wiki/YouTube) on April 20, 2017, in recognition of [4/20](/wiki/420_(cannabis_culture)).

## Personal life

Cook married New Zealand-Canadian actor [Daniel Gillies](/wiki/Daniel_Gillies) on August 8, 2004, saying: "We got engaged after dating for maybe five months and we got married a couple of months after that". They have two children: a daughter born in September 2013 and a son born in April 2015. The couple separated in June 2019 and divorced in 2021.

As of July 2025, Cook has been dating American actor, [Brandon Routh](/wiki/Brandon_Routh).

Cook is a [vegetarian](/wiki/Vegetarianism).

# Patrick Sheane Duncan

## Infobox

- **Born:** 1947 (age 78–79)
- **Occupation:** Screenswriter Novelist
- **Nationality:** American

**Patrick Sheane Duncan** (born 1947) is an American writer, film producer and director.

A graduate of [Grand Valley State University](/wiki/Grand_Valley_State_University) in [Allendale Charter Township, Michigan](/wiki/Allendale_Charter_Township,_Michigan), Duncan's career has been influenced by his [Vietnam War](/wiki/Vietnam_War) experiences, which inspired the television miniseries *Vietnam War Story* (1987) and its sequel *Vietnam War Story: The Last Days* (1989) and the films *[84C MoPic](/wiki/84C_MoPic)* (1989) and *[Courage Under Fire](/wiki/Courage_Under_Fire)* (1996). Additional writing credits include *[A Home of Our Own](/wiki/A_Home_of_Our_Own)* (1993), *The Pornographer* (1994), *[Nick of Time](/wiki/Nick_of_Time_(film))* (1995), *[Mr. Holland's Opus](/wiki/Mr._Holland%27s_Opus)* (1995), and the television movies *A Painted House* (2003), *[Elvis](/wiki/Elvis_(miniseries))* (2005), and the *[Little Red Wagon](/wiki/Little_Red_Wagon)* (2012).

Duncan is a winner of the [CableACE Award](/wiki/CableACE_Award) for Writing for a Dramatic Series for *Vietnam War Story: The Last Days* and a [Christopher Award](/wiki/Christopher_Award) for *Mr. Holland's Opus*, which also garnered him a [Golden Globe](/wiki/Golden_Globe) nomination. He was nominated for the Grand Jury Prize at the [Sundance Film Festival](/wiki/Sundance_Film_Festival) for *84C MoPic* and *The Pornographer* and the [Independent Spirit Award](/wiki/Independent_Spirit_Award) for Best First Feature and Best Screenplay for *84C MoPic*. He received the [Vietnam Veterans of America](https://vva.org/) Excellence in the Arts Award at the organization's 1989 National Convention in Chicago.

Duncan's play, *Souls on Fire*, was produced by [Danny Glover](/wiki/Danny_Glover)'s theatre company, [Robey Theatre Company](/wiki/Robey_Theatre_Company) in Los Angeles.

In 2016, Duncan published the historical [horror](/wiki/Horror_fiction) novel *[Dracula vs. Hitler](/wiki/Dracula_vs._Hitler)*.

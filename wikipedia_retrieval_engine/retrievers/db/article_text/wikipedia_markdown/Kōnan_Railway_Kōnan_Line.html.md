The **Kōnan Line** (弘南線, *Kōnan-sen*) is a railway route operated by the Japanese [private railway](/wiki/Private_railway) operator [Kōnan Railway](/wiki/K%C5%8Dnan_Railway) in [Aomori Prefecture](/wiki/Aomori_Prefecture), from [Hirosaki Station](/wiki/Hirosaki_Station) in [Hirosaki](/wiki/Hirosaki,_Aomori) to [Kuroishi Station](/wiki/Kuroishi_Station_(Aomori)) in [Kuroishi](/wiki/Kuroishi,_Aomori).

## Rolling stock

A Konan 7000 series EMU at Kuroishi Station

Rolling stock on the line consists of former [Tokyu](/wiki/Tokyu_Corporation) 6000 and 7000 series [EMUs](/wiki/Electric_multiple_unit). Ex-[Nankai Railway](/wiki/Nankai_Railway) 1521 series 4-door EMUs were also previously used, but these were withdrawn in 2008 following the discontinuation of limited-stop "Rapid" services. An electric locomotive is also available for use on winter snow-clearing duties.

## History

The Kōnan Railway was founded on March 27, 1926, and began operations between Hirosaki and [Tsugaru-Onoe Station](/wiki/Tsugaru-Onoe_Station) on September 7, 1927. On July 1, 1948, the line was [electrified](/wiki/Railway_electrification_system) at 600 volts DC. The line was extended to Kōnan-Kuroishi Station (present-day Kuroishi Station) on July 1, 1950. Voltage on the line was raised to 750 volts on April 1, 1954, and to 1,500 volts on September 1, 1961.

Freight services ceased in 1984.

### Former connecting lines

- Kuroishi station - The 7 km (4.3 mi) line to Kawabe on the [Ou Main Line](/wiki/Ou_Main_Line) was opened in 1912 by the JGR, transferred to the Konan Railway Co. in 1984, the year that freight services ceased, and closed in 1998.

## Accidents

A train derailed at [Hiraka Station](/wiki/Hiraka_Station) on June 12, 2007, but no injuries were reported.

**Adam Jake Taggart** ([/ˈtæɡərt/](/wiki/Help:IPA/English) [*TAG-ərt*](/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key); born 2 June 1993) is an Australian [soccer](/wiki/Soccer) player who plays as a [striker](/wiki/Striker_(association_football)) for [A-League](/wiki/A-League_Men) club [Perth Glory](/wiki/Perth_Glory_FC), whom he [captains](/wiki/Captain_(association_football)), and the [Australia national team](/wiki/Australia_men%27s_national_soccer_team).

Taggart has won two golden boot awards in his playing career which is the A-League Golden Boot award, scoring 16 goals in 25 appearances for [Newcastle Jets](/wiki/Newcastle_Jets_FC) during the [2013–14 A-League](/wiki/2013%E2%80%9314_A-League) season and K League Golden Boot award, scoring 20 goals in 33 appearances for [Suwon Samsung Bluewings](/wiki/Suwon_Samsung_Bluewings) during the [2019 K League 1](/wiki/2019_K_League_1) season.

## Club career

### Perth Glory

Taggart made his senior debut in a 1–1 draw with [Melbourne Heart](/wiki/Melbourne_City_FC) in January 2011. He scored his first goal in a loss to [Gold Coast United](/wiki/Gold_Coast_United_FC), 75 minutes into the game.

### Newcastle Jets

On 2 March 2012, Taggart signed a two-year contract with [A-League](/wiki/A-League) club [Newcastle Jets](/wiki/Newcastle_Jets_FC). In November 2013, Taggart scored his first professional career hat-trick against [Melbourne Heart](/wiki/Melbourne_Heart) with all three goals scored from outside the 18 yard box. Newcastle would go on and win the match 3–1. After a strong start to the season, Adam had a dry patch with a lack of goals around Christmas, before scoring a brace against the Wanderers to salvage a 2–2 draw. He became a focal point of the Newcastle Jets attack, heading the lineup alongside [Emile Heskey](/wiki/Emile_Heskey), [Joel Griffiths](/wiki/Joel_Griffiths) and [Michael Bridges](/wiki/Michael_Bridges). In 2014, Taggart became the second Newcastle player to receive the golden boot of the A-League with 16 goals, after Joel Griffiths in 2008. He was also awarded the [A-League](/wiki/A-League) Young Player of the Season Award at the same ceremony.

### Fulham

On 24 June 2014, [Fulham](/wiki/Fulham_F.C.) signed Taggart from Newcastle Jets for an undisclosed fee on a three-year contract but injury delayed his involvement with the first team by a number of months.

#### Loan to Dundee United

On 1 September 2015, Taggart signed for [Scottish Premiership](/wiki/Scottish_Premiership) club [Dundee United](/wiki/Dundee_United_F.C.) on a loan deal until January 2016.

### Return to Perth Glory

On 26 January 2016, Taggart returned to [Perth Glory](/wiki/Perth_Glory_FC) again on a permanent deal. However, he was ineligible to play for Perth in the [2015–16 A-League](/wiki/2015%E2%80%9316_A-League) under [FIFA](/wiki/FIFA) regulations preventing players from registering for more than two clubs in a single season. In the first game of the [2016–17 season](/wiki/2016%E2%80%9317_Perth_Glory_FC_season), Taggart started in the number 11 role, scoring twice in the first half. After leading at half time 3–0, the Glory squandered the lead and the final result was a 3–3 draw.

### Brisbane Roar

On 1 May 2018, [Brisbane Roar](/wiki/Brisbane_Roar_FC) announced the signing of Taggart as his contract at Perth Glory ended. He signed a contract with Brisbane contracting his services to them for the next two years. On 14 February 2019, Brisbane Roar announced Taggart had come to terms with an Asian club for his transfer.

### Suwon Samsung Bluewings

On 18 February 2019, Taggart was sold to Korean giants, [Suwon Samsung Bluewings](/wiki/Suwon_Samsung_Bluewings) for $150,000. Taggart made his debut on 1 March 2019 in a 2–1 defeat against [Ulsan Hyundai](/wiki/Ulsan_Hyundai) where he came on as a substitute at half time scoring in the 63' minute. On 14 August 2019, he was named K League Player of the Month for July. Taggart was almost unplayable in July, scoring seven goals in just six league appearances to shoot to the top of the K-League scoring charts. On 17 August 2019, he scored his first K-League hat-trick against [Gangwon FC](/wiki/Gangwon_FC). Taggart would finish the [2019 K-League 1](/wiki/2019_K_League_1) season as the league top goal scorer in his debut season and also winning the [2019 Korean FA Cup](/wiki/2019_Korean_FA_Cup) with the club.

On 26 August 2020, Taggart scored his second hat-trick for the club against [FC Seoul](/wiki/FC_Seoul) in 3–1 win.

### Cerezo Osaka

On 20 December 2020, Taggart was transferred to [J1 League](/wiki/J1_League) club [Cerezo Osaka](/wiki/Cerezo_Osaka).

### Third spell at Perth Glory

On 15 December 2022, Perth Glory announced that Taggart would be returning to the club on a three-and-a-half-year deal.

On 11 October 2023, Taggart was named as co-captain along with [Mark Beevers](/wiki/Mark_Beevers) ahead of the [2023–24 A-League](/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_A-League_Men) season. His fine form throughout the season earned him a returned to the national team after a two years hiatus.

At the conclusion of the [2023–24 A-League](/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_A-League_Men) season, Taggart became the first player in the A-League to win the [golden boot](/wiki/Golden_Boot_Awards) whilst playing for a team who finished last.

On 22 March 2026, Taggart broke the club record for most goals in the A-League era, scoring his 60th goal for Perth in a 1–1 draw with Melbourne City.

## International career

Taggart playing for Australia in 2013.

Taggart represented [Australia](/wiki/Australia_men%27s_national_soccer_team) at [U-20](/wiki/Australia_national_under-20_association_football_team) level at the [2012 AFC U-19 Championship](/wiki/2012_AFC_U-19_Championship) in [United Arab Emirates](/wiki/United_Arab_Emirates) and at the [2013 FIFA U-20 World Cup](/wiki/2013_FIFA_U-20_World_Cup) in Turkey.

### Senior

Taggart made his debut for [Australia](/wiki/Australia_men%27s_national_soccer_team) in late 2012, in the second preliminary round of the [2013 EAFF East Asian Cup](/wiki/2013_EAFF_East_Asian_Cup) against [Hong Kong](/wiki/Hong_Kong_national_football_team), coming off the bench in a narrow win. He scored his first two international goals days later in a win over [Chinese Taipei](/wiki/Chinese_Taipei_national_football_team).

In 2013, Taggart played in the [2013 EAFF East Asian Cup](/wiki/2013_EAFF_East_Asian_Cup), scoring a goal in a loss to [China](/wiki/China_national_football_team).

Taggart was a part of the Socceroos squad for the [2014 FIFA World Cup](/wiki/2014_FIFA_World_Cup), and played in matches against [Netherlands](/wiki/Netherlands_national_football_team) and [Spain](/wiki/Spain_national_football_team).

In March 2024, Taggart returned to the national team after two years earning the called up with his stunning form for Perth Glory scoring 15 goals in 19 appearances in the [2023–24 A-League](/wiki/2023%E2%80%9324_A-League_Men) season.

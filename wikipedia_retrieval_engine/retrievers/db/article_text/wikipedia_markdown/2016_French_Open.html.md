The **2016 French Open** was a tennis tournament played on outdoor [clay courts](/wiki/Clay_court). It was the 115th edition of the [French Open](/wiki/French_Open) and the second Grand Slam event of the year. It took place at the [Stade Roland Garros](/wiki/Stade_Roland_Garros) from 22 May to 5 June and consisted of events for professional players in singles, doubles and mixed doubles play. Junior and wheelchair players also took part in singles and doubles events.

[Novak Djokovic](/wiki/Novak_Djokovic) won the [men's singles](/wiki/2016_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_singles) in the 2016 edition. [Stan Wawrinka](/wiki/Stan_Wawrinka) was the defending champion in men's singles, but he lost to [Andy Murray](/wiki/Andy_Murray) in the semifinals. [Serena Williams](/wiki/Serena_Williams) was the defending champion in the [women's singles](/wiki/2016_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_singles), but she lost to [Garbiñe Muguruza](/wiki/Garbi%C3%B1e_Muguruza) in the final.
[Roger Federer](/wiki/Roger_Federer) withdrew before the tournament to avoid "unnecessary [fitness] risk", making this tournament the first [Grand Slam](/wiki/Grand_Slam_(tennis)) he missed since the [1999 US Open](/wiki/1999_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_singles). Furthermore, nine-time champion [Rafael Nadal](/wiki/Rafael_Nadal) withdrew during the tournament due to injury, for the first time in his French Open career.

Novak Djokovic's victory at this tournament in his 20th Grand Slam final completed his [career Grand Slam](/wiki/Grand_Slam_(tennis)#Career_Grand_Slam) of all four major tournaments, the eighth man to do so in singles and the fifth since the start of the [Open Era](/wiki/History_of_tennis#Open_Era) (after Rod Laver, [Andre Agassi](/wiki/Andre_Agassi), [Roger Federer](/wiki/Roger_Federer), and [Rafael Nadal](/wiki/Rafael_Nadal)). Djokovic also achieved a [non-calendar year Grand Slam](/wiki/Grand_Slam_(tennis)#Non-calendar-year_Grand_Slam), becoming the first man since [Rod Laver](/wiki/Rod_Laver) in 1969 to hold all four major titles at once. The victory by Garbiñe Muguruza was her first Grand Slam win in her second Grand Slam final.

## Tournament

Court Philippe Chatrier, where the Finals of the French Open take place.

The 2016 French Open was the 115th edition of the French Open and was held at [Stade Roland Garros](/wiki/Stade_Roland_Garros) in Paris.

The tournament was run by the [International Tennis Federation](/wiki/International_Tennis_Federation) (ITF) and was part of the [2016 ATP World Tour](/wiki/2016_ATP_World_Tour) and the [2016 WTA Tour](/wiki/2016_WTA_Tour) calendars under the Grand Slam category. The tournament consisted of men's and women's singles and doubles draws as well as a mixed doubles event.

There were singles and doubles events for both boys and girls (players under 18), and singles and doubles events for men's and women's [wheelchair tennis](/wiki/Wheelchair_tennis) players as part of the UNIQLO tour under the Grand Slam category. The tournament was played on clay courts and took place over a series of 22 courts, including the three main showcourts, [Court Philippe Chatrier](/wiki/Stade_Roland_Garros#Court_Philippe_Chatrier), [Court Suzanne Lenglen](/wiki/Court_Suzanne_Lenglen) and [Court 1](/wiki/Stade_Roland_Garros#Court_1).

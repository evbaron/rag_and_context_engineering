The **Eggstock** is a [mountain](/wiki/Mountain) of the [Schwyzer Alps](/wiki/Schwyzer_Alps), located on the border between the [Swiss cantons](/wiki/Cantons_of_Switzerland) of [Schwyz](/wiki/Canton_of_Schwyz) and [Glarus](/wiki/Canton_of_Glarus), north of [Braunwald](/wiki/Braunwald,_Glarus). It lies on the range east of the [Bös Fulen](/wiki/B%C3%B6s_Fulen), between the valley of Bösbächi and the [cirque](/wiki/Cirque) of Braunwald and is composed of three summits: the *Hinterer Eggstock* (2,455 m [8,054 ft]), the *Mittlerer Eggstock* (2,436 m [7,992 ft]) and the *Vorderer Eggstock* (2,449 m [8,035 ft]).

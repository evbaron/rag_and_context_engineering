**1992** ([MCMXCII](/wiki/Roman_numerals)) was a [leap year starting on Wednesday](/wiki/Leap_year_starting_on_Wednesday) of the [Gregorian calendar](/wiki/Gregorian_calendar), the 1992nd year of the [Common Era](/wiki/Common_Era) (CE) and *[Anno Domini](/wiki/Anno_Domini)* (AD) designations, the 992nd year of the [2nd millennium](/wiki/2nd_millennium), the 92nd year of the [20th century](/wiki/20th_century), and the 3rd year of the [1990s](/wiki/1990s) decade.

1992 was designated as [International Space Year](/wiki/International_Space_Year) by the [United Nations](/wiki/United_Nations).

## Events

### January

- [January 1](/wiki/January_1) – [Boutros Boutros-Ghali](/wiki/Boutros_Boutros-Ghali) of [Egypt](/wiki/Egypt) replaces [Javier Pérez de Cuéllar](/wiki/Javier_P%C3%A9rez_de_Cu%C3%A9llar) of [Peru](/wiki/Peru) as [United Nations Secretary-General](/wiki/United_Nations_Secretary-General).
- [January 6](/wiki/January_6)
  - The [Nagorno-Karabakh Republic](/wiki/Republic_of_Artsakh) is proclaimed by the [Armenians](/wiki/Armenians) of [Nagorno-Karabakh](/wiki/Nagorno-Karabakh).
  - [1991–92 Georgian coup d'état](/wiki/1991%E2%80%9392_Georgian_coup_d%27%C3%A9tat): President of Georgia [Zviad Gamsakhurdia](/wiki/Zviad_Gamsakhurdia) flees the country as a result of the military coup.
- [January 7](/wiki/January_7) – [1992 European Community Monitor Mission helicopter downing](/wiki/1992_European_Community_Monitor_Mission_helicopter_downing): A Yugoslav Air Force [Mikoyan-Gurevich MiG-21](/wiki/Mikoyan-Gurevich_MiG-21) attacks two Italian Army [Agusta-Bell AB-206L LongRanger](/wiki/Bell_206) helicopters carrying observers from the [European Community Monitor Mission](/wiki/European_Community_Monitor_Mission). One crashes, killing five people on board. The other helicopter crash-lands, but its occupants survive.
- [January 9](/wiki/January_9)
  - [Bosnian Serbs](/wiki/Bosnian_Serbs) declare their [own republic](/wiki/Republika_Srpska_(1992%E2%80%9395)) within [Bosnia and Herzegovina](/wiki/Republic_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina), in protest at the decision by [Bosniaks](/wiki/Bosniaks) and [Bosnian Croats](/wiki/Bosnian_Croats) to seek recognition by the European Communities.
  - First confirmed detection of [exoplanets](/wiki/Exoplanet) with announcement of the discovery of several terrestrial-mass planets orbiting the pulsar [PSR B1257+12](/wiki/PSR_B1257%2B12), by radio astronomers [Aleksander Wolszczan](/wiki/Aleksander_Wolszczan) and [Dale Frail](/wiki/Dale_Frail) working in the United States.
- [January 15](/wiki/January_15) – The [Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia](/wiki/Socialist_Federal_Republic_of_Yugoslavia) begins to break up; [Slovenia](/wiki/Slovenia) and [Croatia](/wiki/Croatia) gain independence and international recognition in some Western countries.
- [January 16](/wiki/January_16) – [El Salvador](/wiki/El_Salvador) officials and rebel leaders sign the [Chapultepec Peace Accords](/wiki/Chapultepec_Peace_Accords) in Mexico City, ending the 12-year [Salvadoran Civil War](/wiki/Salvadoran_Civil_War) that claimed at least 75,000 lives.
- [January 19](/wiki/January_19)
  - In the [Bulgarian presidential election](/wiki/1992_Bulgarian_presidential_election), the first held by direct vote, [Zhelyu Zhelev](/wiki/Zhelyu_Zhelev), leader of the [Union of Democratic Forces](/wiki/Union_of_Democratic_Forces_(Bulgaria)), retains office.
  - [Paramount Leader](/wiki/Paramount_Leader) of China [Deng Xiaoping](/wiki/Deng_Xiaoping) speaks in Shenzhen during his southern tour, a move that would return China on its right-wing march towards [free market](/wiki/Free_market) economics.
- [January 20](/wiki/January_20) – [Air Inter Flight 148](/wiki/Air_Inter_Flight_148), an [Airbus A320-111](/wiki/Airbus_A320_family), crashes in the [Vosges Mountains](/wiki/Vosges_Mountains) near [Barr](/wiki/Barr,_Bas-Rhin), France, while circling to land at [Strasbourg](/wiki/Strasbourg), France, killing 87 of the 96 people on board. Facing tough competition from French high-speed [TGV](/wiki/TGV) trains, [Air Inter](/wiki/Air_Inter) had encouraged its pilots to fly at high speeds at low altitudes, and had not installed [ground proximity warning systems](/wiki/Ground_proximity_warning_system) on its airliners because such systems generated too many nuisance alarms during high-speed, low-altitude flight.
- [January 22](/wiki/January_22) – Rebel forces occupy [Zaire](/wiki/Zaire)'s national radio station in [Kinshasa](/wiki/Kinshasa) and broadcast a demand for the government's resignation.
- [January 24](/wiki/January_24) – China and [Israel](/wiki/Israel) establish diplomatic relations.
- [January 26](/wiki/January_26)
  - [Boris Yeltsin](/wiki/Boris_Yeltsin) announces that Russia will stop targeting cities of the United States and its allies with [nuclear weapons](/wiki/Nuclear_weapon). In return President [George H. W. Bush](/wiki/George_H._W._Bush) announces that the United States and its allies will stop targeting Russia and the remaining [communist](/wiki/Communism) states with nuclear weapons.
  - In [Mauritania](/wiki/Mauritania), security forces open fire on violent extremist opponents of President of Mauritania [Maaouya Ould Sid'Ahmed Taya](/wiki/Maaouya_Ould_Sid%27Ahmed_Taya), allegedly killing at least four people.
- [January 27](/wiki/January_27) – [First Nagorno-Karabakh War](/wiki/First_Nagorno-Karabakh_War): in the disputed territory of [Nagorno-Karabakh](/wiki/Nagorno-Karabakh), fighting between [Armenians](/wiki/Armenians) and [Azeris](/wiki/Azerbaijani_people) leaves at least 60 people dead.
- [January 30](/wiki/January_30) – [North Korea](/wiki/North_Korea) signs an accord with the [International Atomic Energy Agency](/wiki/International_Atomic_Energy_Agency) allowing for international inspections of [North Korea's nuclear power plants](/wiki/North_Korea_and_weapons_of_mass_destruction).

### February

- [February 1](/wiki/February_1) – President of the United States [George Bush](/wiki/George_H._W._Bush) meets with President of Russia [Boris Yeltsin](/wiki/Boris_Yeltsin) at [Camp David](/wiki/Camp_David), where they formally declare that the [Cold War](/wiki/Cold_War) is over.
- [February 3](/wiki/February_3) – South African State President [F.W. de Klerk](/wiki/F.W._de_Klerk) and [Nelson Mandela](/wiki/Nelson_Mandela), [African National Congress](/wiki/African_National_Congress) leader, are jointly awarded the [Felix Houphouet-Boigny Peace Prize](/wiki/Felix_Houphouet-Boigny_Peace_Prize) at the [UNESCO](/wiki/UNESCO) headquarters in Paris.
- [February 4](/wiki/February_4) – In [Venezuela](/wiki/Venezuela), [Hugo Chávez](/wiki/Hugo_Ch%C3%A1vez) leads [an unsuccessful coup attempt](/wiki/1992_Venezuelan_coup_d%27%C3%A9tat_attempts) against President of Venezuela [Carlos Andrés Pérez](/wiki/Carlos_Andr%C3%A9s_P%C3%A9rez).
- [February 6](/wiki/February_6) – The [Ruby Jubilee of Elizabeth II](/wiki/Ruby_Jubilee_of_Elizabeth_II) commemorates 40 years since the accession of [Elizabeth II](/wiki/Elizabeth_II) as [Queen of the United Kingdom](/wiki/Monarchy_of_the_United_Kingdom) and [other Commonwealth realms](/wiki/Commonwealth_realm).
- [February 7](/wiki/February_7) – The [Maastricht Treaty](/wiki/Maastricht_Treaty) is signed, founding the [European Union](/wiki/European_Union).
- [February 8](/wiki/February_8) – The opening ceremony for the [1992 Winter Olympics](/wiki/1992_Winter_Olympics) is held in [Albertville](/wiki/Albertville), France.
- [February 9](/wiki/February_9) – [Algerian Civil War](/wiki/Algerian_Civil_War): The government of [Algeria](/wiki/Algeria) declares a [state of emergency](/wiki/State_of_emergency) and begins a crackdown on the [Islamic Salvation Front](/wiki/Islamic_Salvation_Front).
- [February 14](/wiki/February_14) – [Ukraine](/wiki/Ukraine) and four other nations in the [Commonwealth of Independent States](/wiki/Commonwealth_of_Independent_States) reject Russia's proposal to maintain unified armed forces. Ukraine, [Moldova](/wiki/Moldova) and [Azerbaijan](/wiki/Azerbaijan) announce they will go ahead with plans to create their own military forces.
- [February 16](/wiki/February_16) – In [Lebanon](/wiki/Lebanon), [Israeli](/wiki/Israel_Defense_Forces) helicopter gunships assassinate [Abbas al-Musawi](/wiki/Abbas_al-Musawi), the leader of [Hezbollah](/wiki/Hezbollah), and his son, in retaliation for a February 14 raid that killed three Israeli soldiers.
- [February 18](/wiki/February_18) – [Iraq disarmament crisis](/wiki/Iraq_disarmament_crisis): The Executive Chairman of [UNSCOM](/wiki/UNSCOM) details [Iraq](/wiki/Ba%27athist_Iraq)'s refusal to abide by UN Security Council disarmament resolutions.
- [February 21](/wiki/February_21) – The [United Nations Security Council](/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council) approves [Resolution 743](/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council_Resolution_743) to send a [UNPROFOR](/wiki/UNPROFOR) peacekeeping force to [Yugoslavia](/wiki/Socialist_Federal_Republic_of_Yugoslavia).
- [February 25](/wiki/February_25)–[26](/wiki/February_26) – 613 [Azerbaijani](/wiki/Azerbaijani_people) civilians are [massacred in Khojaly](/wiki/Khojaly_Massacre).
- [February 26](/wiki/February_26) – The [Supreme Court of Ireland](/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Ireland) [rules](/wiki/X_Case) that a 14-year-old rape victim may travel to the United Kingdom to have an abortion.
- [February 28](/wiki/February_28) – Ownership of the port town of [Walvis Bay](/wiki/Walvis_Bay) is transferred from South Africa to [Namibia](/wiki/Namibia).

### March

- [March 1](/wiki/March_1) – The first victims of the Bosnian War are a Serb bridegroom's father and an Orthodox priest in [a Sarajevo shooting](/wiki/Sarajevo_wedding_attack). In the [Bosnian independence referendum](/wiki/1992_Bosnian_independence_referendum), held from February 29 to March 1 and boycotted by [Bosnian Serbs](/wiki/Bosnian_Serbs), the majority of the [Bosniak](/wiki/Bosniaks) and [Bosnian Croat](/wiki/Bosnian_Croat) communities have voted for [Bosnia-Herzegovina](/wiki/Bosnia-Herzegovina)'s independence.
- [March 2](/wiki/March_2) – In [Dubăsari](/wiki/Dub%C4%83sari), Moldova, escalating tensions turn into open hostilities and the beginning of the [Transnistria War](/wiki/Transnistria_War).
- [March 4](/wiki/March_4) – The Supreme Court of Algeria bans the [Islamic Salvation Front](/wiki/Islamic_Salvation_Front), which is poised to win control of the [Parliament of Algeria](/wiki/Parliament_of_Algeria) in runoff elections.
- [March 12](/wiki/March_12) – [Mauritius](/wiki/Mauritius) becomes a [republic](/wiki/Republic) while remaining a member of the [Commonwealth of Nations](/wiki/Commonwealth_of_Nations).
- [March 13](/wiki/March_13) – The 6.7 [Mw](/wiki/Seismic_magnitude_scales#Mw) [Erzincan earthquake](/wiki/1992_Erzincan_earthquake) affects eastern [Turkey](/wiki/Turkey) with a maximum [Mercalli intensity](/wiki/Mercalli_intensity_scale) of VIII (*Severe*), killing 498–652 and injuring around 2,000.
- [March 18](/wiki/March_18) – [White South Africans](/wiki/White_South_African) [vote in favour of political reforms](/wiki/1992_South_African_apartheid_referendum) which will end the [apartheid](/wiki/Apartheid) regime and create a power-sharing multi-racial government.
- [March 22](/wiki/March_22)
  - In [French regional elections](/wiki/1992_French_regional_elections), the conservative [Rally for the Republic](/wiki/Rally_for_the_Republic) and the centre-right [Union for French Democracy](/wiki/Union_for_French_Democracy) win in a landslide, capturing 20 of 22 metropolitan regional presidencies.
  - [STS-45](/wiki/STS-45): [Space Shuttle *Atlantis*](/wiki/Space_Shuttle_Atlantis) takes off from [Cape Canaveral](/wiki/Cape_Canaveral) carrying instruments designed to study [global warming](/wiki/Global_warming).
- [March 24](/wiki/March_24) – The [Treaty on Open Skies](/wiki/Treaty_on_Open_Skies) is signed in Helsinki, Finland, to establish a program of unarmed surveillance flights over the 34 member states. It went into effect on January 1, 2002.
- [March 25](/wiki/March_25) – The [International Atomic Energy Agency](/wiki/International_Atomic_Energy_Agency) orders [Iraq](/wiki/Iraq) to destroy an industrial complex at Al Atheer that is being used to manufacture nuclear weapons.
- [March 28](/wiki/March_28) - [The Shot (Duke-Kentucky)](/wiki/The_Shot_(Duke-Kentucky)): In what *[Sports Illustrated](/wiki/Sports_Illustrated)* deemed the greatest college basketball game of all time, [Christian Laettner](/wiki/Christian_Laettner) sinks an overtime buzzer-beater to advance Duke to their fifth straight Final Four.
- [March 31](/wiki/March_31) – The [Maintenance of Religious Harmony Act](/wiki/Maintenance_of_Religious_Harmony_Act) of [Singapore](/wiki/Singapore) comes into force.

### April

"April 1992" redirects here. For the Merzbow album, see [April 1992 (album)](/wiki/April_1992_(album)).

- [April 5](/wiki/April_5)
  - The Assembly of [Bosnia and Herzegovina](/wiki/Republic_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina) (without the presence of [Serb](/wiki/Serbs) political delegates) proclaims independence from the [Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia](/wiki/Socialist_Federal_Republic_of_Yugoslavia).
  - [Bosnian War](/wiki/Bosnian_War): Serb troops, following a mass rebellion of Serbs in [Bosnia and Herzegovina](/wiki/Republic_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina) against the Bosnian declaration of independence from [Yugoslavia](/wiki/Socialist_Federal_Republic_of_Yugoslavia), besiege the city of [Sarajevo](/wiki/Sarajevo).
  - President of Peru [Alberto Fujimori](/wiki/Alberto_Fujimori) issues [Decree Law 25418](/wiki/Decree_Law_25418), dissolving the [Congress of the Republic of Peru](/wiki/Congress_of_the_Republic_of_Peru), imposing censorship and having opposition politicians arrested, setting off the [1992 Peruvian constitutional crisis](/wiki/1992_Peruvian_constitutional_crisis).
- [April 6](/wiki/April_6) – The [Republic of Ilirida](/wiki/Republic_of_Ilirida) is proclaimed by [Albanian](/wiki/Albanians) Macedonian activists in [Struga](/wiki/Struga), [Republic of Macedonia](/wiki/Republic_of_Macedonia).
  - [Windows 3.1](/wiki/Windows_3.1) is released to manufacturing by [Microsoft](/wiki/Microsoft).
- [April 7](/wiki/April_7) – The United States recognizes the independence of [Slovenia](/wiki/Slovenia), [Croatia](/wiki/Croatia), and [Bosnia and Herzegovina](/wiki/Republic_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina). The [European Communities](/wiki/European_Communities) also recognizes [Bosnia and Herzegovina](/wiki/Republic_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina).
- [April 9](/wiki/April_9)
  - A jury in [Miami](/wiki/Miami), Florida, convicts former Panamanian ruler [Manuel Noriega](/wiki/Manuel_Noriega) of assisting [Colombia](/wiki/Colombia)'s cocaine cartel.
  - In the [United Kingdom general election](/wiki/1992_United_Kingdom_general_election) the [Conservative Party](/wiki/Conservative_Party_(UK)) led by Prime Minister of the United Kingdom [John Major](/wiki/John_Major) narrowly retains power.
- [April 10](/wiki/April_10)
  - [First Nagorno-Karabakh War](/wiki/First_Nagorno-Karabakh_War): [Maraga massacre](/wiki/Maraga_massacre) – At least 43 [Armenian](/wiki/Armenians) civilians are killed as their village of [Maraga, Azerbaijan](/wiki/Maraga,_Azerbaijan), is captured and destroyed by the [Azerbaijani Armed Forces](/wiki/Azerbaijani_Armed_Forces).
  - A [Provisional Irish Republican Army](/wiki/Provisional_Irish_Republican_Army) bomb [explodes](/wiki/Baltic_Exchange_bombing) at the [Baltic Exchange](/wiki/Baltic_Exchange) in the [City of London](/wiki/City_of_London); three are killed, 91 injured.
- [April 13](/wiki/April_13) – The 5.3 [Mw](/wiki/Seismic_magnitude_scales#Mw) [Roermond earthquake](/wiki/1992_Roermond_earthquake) affects the [Netherlands](/wiki/Netherlands), [Germany](/wiki/Germany) and [Belgium](/wiki/Belgium) with a maximum [Mercalli intensity](/wiki/Mercalli_intensity_scale) of VII (*Very strong*).
- [April 15](/wiki/April_15) – The [National Assembly of Vietnam](/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Vietnam) adopts the 1992 [Constitution of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam](/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Socialist_Republic_of_Vietnam).
- [April 16](/wiki/April_16) – President of Afghanistan [Mohammad Najibullah](/wiki/Mohammad_Najibullah) is ousted and detained by Muslim rebels moving towards [Kabul](/wiki/Kabul), setting the stage for the [civil war in Afghanistan (1992–96)](/wiki/Civil_war_in_Afghanistan_(1992%E2%80%9396)).
- [April 20](/wiki/April_20) – [The Freddie Mercury Tribute Concert](/wiki/The_Freddie_Mercury_Tribute_Concert), held at [Wembley Stadium](/wiki/Wembley_Stadium_(1923)), London, is televised live to over one billion people and raises millions of dollars for [AIDS](/wiki/AIDS) research.
- [April 21](/wiki/April_21) – The death of [Grand Duke Vladimir Kirillovich of Russia](/wiki/Grand_Duke_Vladimir_Kirillovich_of_Russia) results in a succession dispute between [Nicholas Romanov, Prince of Russia](/wiki/Nicholas_Romanov,_Prince_of_Russia) and Vladimir's daughter [Maria](/wiki/Maria_Vladimirovna,_Grand_Duchess_of_Russia) for the leadership of the Imperial Family of Russia.
- [April 22](/wiki/April_22) – Fuel leaking into a sewer causes [a series of explosions](/wiki/1992_Guadalajara_explosions) in [Guadalajara](/wiki/Guadalajara), Mexico; 215 are killed, 1,500 injured.
- [April 27](/wiki/April_27) – [Betty Boothroyd](/wiki/Betty_Boothroyd) becomes the first woman elected [Speaker of the House of Commons of the United Kingdom](/wiki/Speaker_of_the_House_of_Commons_(United_Kingdom)).
- [April 28](/wiki/April_28) – The two remaining constituent republics of the former [Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia](/wiki/Socialist_Federal_Republic_of_Yugoslavia) – [Serbia](/wiki/Republic_of_Serbia_(1992%E2%80%932006)) and [Montenegro](/wiki/Republic_of_Montenegro_(1992%E2%80%932006)) – form a new state, named the [Federal Republic of Yugoslavia](/wiki/Serbia_and_Montenegro) (which in [2003](/wiki/2003) becomes [Serbia and Montenegro](/wiki/Serbia_and_Montenegro)), bringing to an end the official state union of Serbs, Croats, Slovenes, Montenegrins, Bosniaks and Macedonians that has existed since [1918](/wiki/1918) (with the exception of a brief occupation period during [World War II](/wiki/World_War_II)).
- [April 29](/wiki/April_29)
  - [Los Angeles riots](/wiki/1992_Los_Angeles_riots): The acquittal of four policemen in the [Rodney King](/wiki/Rodney_King) beating criminal trial triggers massive rioting in Los Angeles. The riots will last for six days resulting in 63 deaths and over $1 billion in damages before order is restored by the military.
  - In [Sierra Leone](/wiki/Sierra_Leone), a group of young soldiers launch a military coup that sends president [Joseph Saidu Momoh](/wiki/Joseph_Saidu_Momoh) into exile in [Guinea](/wiki/Guinea), and the National Provisional Ruling Council (NPRC) is established with 25-year-old Captain [Valentine Strasser](/wiki/Valentine_Strasser) as its chairman and Head of State of the country.
- [April 30](/wiki/April_30) – [Brčko bridge massacre](/wiki/Br%C4%8Dko_bridge_massacre): around 100 Croat and Bosniak civilians are blown up while crossing the bridge across the [Sava](/wiki/Sava) in [Brčko](/wiki/Br%C4%8Dko), [Bosnia and Herzegovina](/wiki/Bosnia_and_Herzegovina).

### May

- [May 1](/wiki/May_1) – [Lithuania](/wiki/Lithuania) introduces a new temporary currency, the [talonas](/wiki/Talonas).
- [May 5](/wiki/May_5)
  - Russian leaders in [Crimea](/wiki/Crimea) declare their separation from [Ukraine](/wiki/Ukraine) as a new republic. They withdraw the secession on [May 10](/wiki/May_10).
  - [Armand Césari Stadium disaster](/wiki/Armand_C%C3%A9sari_Stadium_disaster) in [Bastia](/wiki/Bastia) on [Corsica](/wiki/Corsica): 18 people are killed and 2,300 are injured when one of the terraces collapses before a football match between [SC Bastia](/wiki/SC_Bastia) and [Olympique de Marseille](/wiki/Olympique_de_Marseille).
- [May 7](/wiki/May_7)
  - *[STS-49](/wiki/STS-49)*: [Space Shuttle *Endeavour*](/wiki/Space_Shuttle_Endeavour) makes its maiden flight, as a replacement for [Space Shuttle *Challenger*](/wiki/Space_Shuttle_Challenger).
  - In the [Sydney River McDonald's murders](/wiki/Sydney_River_McDonald%27s_murders) in [Nova Scotia](/wiki/Nova_Scotia), Canada, three McDonald's employees are killed and a fourth is left permanently disabled during a botched robbery.
- [May 9](/wiki/May_9)
  - The [United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change](/wiki/United_Nations_Framework_Convention_on_Climate_Change) is adopted in New York.
  - The [Westray Mine](/wiki/Westray_Mine) in Plymouth, Nova Scotia, Canada, explodes, killing all 26 miners working the night shift.
- [May 10](/wiki/May_10) – [Sweden](/wiki/Sweden_men%27s_national_ice_hockey_team) wins the [Ice Hockey World Championships](/wiki/Ice_Hockey_World_Championships) in [Czechoslovakia](/wiki/Czechoslovakia) defeating [Finland](/wiki/Finland_men%27s_national_ice_hockey_team), 5–2, in the final game in [Prague](/wiki/Prague).
- [May 13](/wiki/May_13) – [Falun Gong](/wiki/Falun_Gong) is introduced by [Li Hongzhi](/wiki/Li_Hongzhi) in China.
- [May 15](/wiki/May_15) – The [Collective Security Treaty Organization](/wiki/Collective_Security_Treaty_Organization) is established by six post-Soviet states belonging to the [Commonwealth of Independent States](/wiki/Commonwealth_of_Independent_States) (effective April 20, 1994).
- [May 16](/wiki/May_16)–[17](/wiki/May_17) – [Bosnian War](/wiki/Bosnian_War): [U.N. peacekeepers](/wiki/United_Nations_peacekeeping) withdraw from [Sarajevo](/wiki/Sarajevo).
- [May 17](/wiki/May_17) – Protests begin in [Bangkok](/wiki/Bangkok), [Thailand](/wiki/Thailand), against the government of General [Suchinda Kraprayoon](/wiki/Suchinda_Kraprayoon), sparking a [bloody crackdown](/wiki/Black_May_(1992)).
- [May 23](/wiki/May_23) – [Capaci bombing](/wiki/Capaci_bombing): A [Mafia](/wiki/Sicilian_Mafia) bomb on the autostrada in Sicily kills five people, including Italian anti-Mafia judge [Giovanni Falcone](/wiki/Giovanni_Falcone).
- [May 24](/wiki/May_24)
  - In [Thailand](/wiki/Thailand), [Suchinda Kraprayoon](/wiki/Suchinda_Kraprayoon) agrees to resign.
  - [Parliamentary election](/wiki/1992_Burkinab%C3%A9_parliamentary_election) held in [Burkina Faso](/wiki/Burkina_Faso), for the first time since 1978.
- [May 30](/wiki/May_30) – [United Nations Security Council Resolution 757](/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council_Resolution_757) imposes economic [sanctions on Yugoslavia](/wiki/Sanctions_on_Yugoslavia) in an effort to end its attacks on Bosnia and Herzegovina.

### June

- [June 2](/wiki/June_2) – In a [national referendum](/wiki/1992_Danish_Maastricht_Treaty_referendum) Denmark rejects the [Maastricht Treaty](/wiki/Maastricht_Treaty) by a narrow margin.
- [June 3](/wiki/June_3)–[14](/wiki/June_14) – The [Earth Summit](/wiki/Earth_Summit) is held in [Rio de Janeiro](/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro).
- [June 8](/wiki/June_8) – The first [World Oceans Day](/wiki/World_Oceans_Day) is celebrated, coinciding with the [Earth Summit](/wiki/Earth_Summit) held in [Rio de Janeiro](/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro), [Brazil](/wiki/Brazil).
- [June 10](/wiki/June_10)–[26](/wiki/June_26) – [Sweden](/wiki/Sweden) hosts the [UEFA Euro 1992](/wiki/UEFA_Euro_1992) football tournament, which is won by [Denmark](/wiki/Denmark_national_football_team).
- [June 16](/wiki/June_16) – A "Joint Understanding" agreement on arms reduction is signed by U.S. president [George H. W. Bush](/wiki/George_H._W._Bush) and Russian president [Boris Yeltsin](/wiki/Boris_Yeltsin) (this is later codified in [START II](/wiki/START_II)).
- [June 17](/wiki/June_17)
  - Two German relief workers held since 1989, Thomas Kemptner and Heinrich Struebig, are handed over to the German authorities after their release; they are the last Western hostages in Lebanon.
  - Violence breaks out between the [African National Congress](/wiki/African_National_Congress) and the [Inkatha Freedom Party](/wiki/Inkatha_Freedom_Party) in [Boipatong](/wiki/Boipatong,_Gauteng), South Africa, [leaving 45 dead](/wiki/Boipatong_massacre).
- [June 18](/wiki/June_18) – [Ireland](/wiki/Republic_of_Ireland) votes for the [Eleventh Amendment of the Constitution of Ireland](/wiki/Eleventh_Amendment_of_the_Constitution_of_Ireland) to accept the [Maastricht Treaty](/wiki/Maastricht_Treaty) with a popular vote of over 69%.
- [June 20](/wiki/June_20)
  - [Estonia](/wiki/Estonia) adopts the [kroon](/wiki/Estonian_kroon) as currency, becoming the first former Soviet Republic to replace the [Soviet rouble](/wiki/Soviet_rouble).
  - In [Paraguay](/wiki/Paraguay) the National Constituent Assembly approves the new [Constitution of Paraguay](/wiki/Constitution_of_Paraguay).
- [June 21](/wiki/June_21) – [Nelson Mandela](/wiki/Nelson_Mandela) announces that the [African National Congress](/wiki/African_National_Congress) will halt negotiations with the government of South Africa following the [Boipatong massacre](/wiki/Boipatong_massacre) of June 17.
- [June 23](/wiki/June_23) – The [Israeli legislative election](/wiki/1992_Israeli_legislative_election) is won by the [Israeli Labor Party](/wiki/Israeli_Labor_Party) under the leadership of [Yitzhak Rabin](/wiki/Yitzhak_Rabin), ousting a [Likud](/wiki/Likud) government.
- [June 25](/wiki/June_25) – The [Black Sea Economic Cooperation](/wiki/Black_Sea_Economic_Cooperation) (BSEC) is founded.
- [June 26](/wiki/June_26) – [Denmark](/wiki/Denmark_national_football_team) beats [Germany](/wiki/Germany_national_football_team) 2–0 in [the final](/wiki/UEFA_Euro_1992_Final) to win the [1992 UEFA European Football Championship](/wiki/1992_UEFA_European_Football_Championship) at [Ullevi](/wiki/Ullevi) Stadium in [Gothenburg](/wiki/Gothenburg), Sweden.
- [June 28](/wiki/June_28) – [Estonia](/wiki/Estonia) holds a referendum on its [constitution](/wiki/Constitution_of_Estonia), which will come into effect on July 3.

### July

- [July 6](/wiki/July_6)–[8](/wiki/July_8) – The [18th G7 summit](/wiki/18th_G7_summit) is held in [Munich](/wiki/Munich).
- [July 6](/wiki/July_6)–[29](/wiki/July_29) – [Iraq disarmament crisis](/wiki/Iraq_disarmament_crisis): [Iraq](/wiki/Ba%27athist_Iraq) refuses a UN inspection team access to the Iraqi Ministry of Agriculture. UNSCOM claims that it has reliable information that the site contains archives related to illegal weapons activities. UN inspectors stage a 17-day "sit-in" outside of the building, but leave when their safety is threatened by Iraqi soldiers.
- [July 10](/wiki/July_10)
  - In [Miami](/wiki/Miami), former [Panamanian](/wiki/Panama) dictator [Manuel Noriega](/wiki/Manuel_Noriega) is sentenced to 40 years in prison for participating in the [illegal drug trade](/wiki/Illegal_drug_trade) and [racketeering](/wiki/Racketeering).
  - The [*Giotto* spacecraft](/wiki/Giotto_(spacecraft)) flies past Comet [26P/Grigg–Skjellerup](/wiki/26P/Grigg%E2%80%93Skjellerup), gathering measurements about the comet.
- [July 13](/wiki/July_13) – [Yitzhak Rabin](/wiki/Yitzhak_Rabin) becomes prime minister of [Israel](/wiki/Israel).
- [July 16](/wiki/July_16) – At the [1992 Democratic National Convention](/wiki/1992_Democratic_National_Convention), [Arkansas](/wiki/Arkansas) Governor Bill Clinton accepts his party's presidential nomination on behalf of the "forgotten middle class".
- [July 17](/wiki/July_17) – The Slovak National Council [declares Slovakia an independent country](/wiki/Slovak_National_Council%27s_Declaration_of_Independence_of_the_Slovak_Nation), signaling the [breakup of Czechoslovakia](/wiki/Dissolution_of_Czechoslovakia).
- [July 19](/wiki/July_19)
  - [Via D'Amelio bombing](/wiki/Via_D%27Amelio_bombing): A car bomb placed by the [Sicilian Mafia](/wiki/Sicilian_Mafia) (with the collaboration of Italian intelligence) kills judge [Paolo Borsellino](/wiki/Paolo_Borsellino) and five members of his police escort.
  - The [Cabinet of Israel](/wiki/Cabinet_of_Israel) approves a freeze on new [Israeli settlement](/wiki/Israeli_settlement) in the occupied territories, a move expected to reinvigorate the Middle East Peace Process.
- [July 20](/wiki/July_20) – [Václav Havel](/wiki/V%C3%A1clav_Havel) resigns as president of [Czechoslovakia](/wiki/Czech_and_Slovak_Federative_Republic).
- [July 21](/wiki/July_21) – The [Transnistria War](/wiki/Transnistria_War) ends with a ceasefire.
- [July 22](/wiki/July_22) – Near [Medellín](/wiki/Medell%C3%ADn), [Colombian](/wiki/Colombia) drug lord [Pablo Escobar](/wiki/Pablo_Escobar) escapes from his luxury prison, fearing extradition to the United States.
- [July 23](/wiki/July_23) – [Abkhazia](/wiki/Abkhazia) declares independence from [Georgia](/wiki/Georgia_(country)).
- [July 25](/wiki/July_25)–[August 9](/wiki/August_9) – The [1992 Summer Olympics](/wiki/1992_Summer_Olympics) are held in [Barcelona](/wiki/Barcelona), [Catalonia](/wiki/Catalonia), Spain.
- [July 26](/wiki/July_26) – [Iraq](/wiki/Ba%27athist_Iraq) agrees to allow UN weapons inspectors to search the Iraqi Agricultural Ministry building in Baghdad. When inspectors arrive on July 28 and 29, they find nothing and voice suspicions that Iraqi records have been removed.
- [July 31](/wiki/July_31)
  - [Georgia](/wiki/Georgia_(country)) becomes the 179th member of the [United Nations](/wiki/United_Nations) after seceding from the [Soviet Union](/wiki/Soviet_Union) the previous year.
  - [Thai Airways International Flight 311](/wiki/Thai_Airways_International_Flight_311), an [Airbus A310-300](/wiki/Airbus_A310-300), crashes into a mountain north of [Kathmandu](/wiki/Kathmandu), [Nepal](/wiki/Nepal) killing all 113 people on board.
  - [China General Aviation Flight 7552](/wiki/China_General_Aviation_Flight_7552) bound for [Xiamen](/wiki/Xiamen) crashes soon after taking off from [Nanjing Dajiaochang Airport](/wiki/Nanjing_Dajiaochang_Airport), killing 108 of the 116 people on board.

### August

- [August 3](/wiki/August_3)–[4](/wiki/August_4) – Millions of black South Africans participate in a [general strike](/wiki/General_strike) called by the [African National Congress](/wiki/African_National_Congress) to protest the lack of progress in negotiations with the government of State President of South Africa [F. W. de Klerk](/wiki/F._W._de_Klerk).
- [August 12](/wiki/August_12) – Canada, Mexico and the United States announce that a deal has been reached on the [North American Free Trade Agreement](/wiki/North_American_Free_Trade_Agreement); this will be formally signed on December 17.
- [August 18](/wiki/August_18) – Prime Minister of the United Kingdom [John Major](/wiki/John_Major) announces the creation of [Iraqi no-fly zones](/wiki/Iraqi_no-fly_zones) (→ [Operation Southern Watch](/wiki/Operation_Southern_Watch)).
- [August 24](/wiki/August_24)
  - [Concordia University massacre](/wiki/Concordia_University_massacre): [Valery Fabrikant](/wiki/Valery_Fabrikant) murders four colleagues and seriously wounds another in a shooting at [Concordia University](/wiki/Concordia_University), in [Montreal](/wiki/Montreal), [Quebec](/wiki/Quebec).
  - [China](/wiki/China) and [South Korea](/wiki/South_Korea) establish [diplomatic relations](/wiki/China%E2%80%93South_Korea_relations).
- [August 24](/wiki/August_24)–[28](/wiki/August_28) – [Hurricane Andrew](/wiki/Hurricane_Andrew) hits south [Florida](/wiki/Florida) and [Louisiana](/wiki/Louisiana) and dissipates over the Tennessee valley when it merges with a storm system; 23 are killed.
- [August 29](/wiki/August_29) – In [Rostock](/wiki/Rostock), Germany, tens of thousands rally to protest [neo-Nazi](/wiki/Neo-Nazism) attacks on refugees and immigrants begun on August 22.

### September

- [September 1](/wiki/September_1) – In Beijing, police arrest [Shen Tong](/wiki/Shen_Tong) for his role in organizing the [Tiananmen Square protests of 1989](/wiki/Tiananmen_Square_protests_of_1989).
- [September 2](/wiki/September_2) – The 7.7 [Mw](/wiki/Seismic_magnitude_scales#Mw) [Nicaragua earthquake](/wiki/1992_Nicaragua_earthquake) affects the west coast of Nicaragua. With a Ms –Mw  disparity of half a unit, this [tsunami earthquake](/wiki/Tsunami_earthquake) triggers a tsunami that causes most of the damage and casualties, with at least 116 killed. Average runup heights are 3–8 meters (9.8–26.2 ft).
- [September 7](/wiki/September_7)
  - In [Ciskei](/wiki/Ciskei), members of the [Ciskei Defence Force](/wiki/Ciskei_Defence_Force) loyal to dictator [Oupa Gqozo](/wiki/Oupa_Gqozo) open fire into a crowd of anti-Gqozo protestors organized by the [African National Congress](/wiki/African_National_Congress), killing at least 28 people and wounding nearly 200.
  - President of Tajikistan [Rahmon Nabiyev](/wiki/Rahmon_Nabiyev) is forced to resign following weeks of [clan and religious warfare](/wiki/Tajikistani_Civil_War) that have left nearly 2,000 people dead.
- [September 12](/wiki/September_12) – In [Peru](/wiki/Peru), police arrest [Abimael Guzmán](/wiki/Abimael_Guzm%C3%A1n), the leader of the [Shining Path](/wiki/Shining_Path) guerilla movement, who has evaded capture for 12 years.
- [September 16](/wiki/September_16) – [Black Wednesday](/wiki/Black_Wednesday): The [pound sterling](/wiki/Pound_sterling) and the [Italian lira](/wiki/Lira) are forced out of the [European Exchange Rate Mechanism](/wiki/European_Exchange_Rate_Mechanism).
- [September 20](/wiki/September_20) – French voters narrowly approve the [Maastricht Treaty](/wiki/Maastricht_Treaty) in the [French Maastricht Treaty referendum](/wiki/1992_French_Maastricht_Treaty_referendum).
- [September 21](/wiki/September_21) – Mexico establishes diplomatic relations with [Vatican City](/wiki/Vatican_City), ending a break that has lasted over 130 years.
- [September 28](/wiki/September_28)
  - Law enforcement officials in the United States, [Colombia](/wiki/Colombia) and Italy announce that they have arrested more than 165 people on [money laundering](/wiki/Money_laundering) charges related to [cocaine](/wiki/Cocaine) trafficking.
  - [Pakistan International Airlines Flight 268](/wiki/Pakistan_International_Airlines_Flight_268) crashes into a mountain while on approach to [Tribhuvan International Airport](/wiki/Tribhuvan_International_Airport) in [Kathmandu](/wiki/Kathmandu), Nepal. All 167 occupants on board were killed. The crash of PIA Flight 268 occurred just 2 months after Thai Airways International Flight 311 crashed into a mountain near Kathmandu under similar circumstances, killing all 113 occupants on board.
- [September 29](/wiki/September_29) – The [Chamber of Deputies of Brazil](/wiki/Chamber_of_Deputies_of_Brazil) votes to impeach President of Brazil [Fernando Collor](/wiki/Fernando_Collor_de_Mello), the country's first democratically elected leader in 29 years. Vice President [Itamar Franco](/wiki/Itamar_Franco) becomes acting president.

### October

- [October 2](/wiki/October_2) – A riot breaks out in the [Carandiru Penitentiary](/wiki/Carandiru_Penitentiary) in [São Paulo](/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Paulo), [Brazil](/wiki/Brazil), resulting in the [Carandiru massacre](/wiki/Carandiru_massacre).
- [October 3](/wiki/October_3) – After performing a song protesting against alleged [Catholic Church child sexual abuse](/wiki/Catholic_Church_sexual_abuse_cases), Irish singer-songwriter [Sinéad O'Connor](/wiki/Sin%C3%A9ad_O%27Connor) rips up a photograph of [Pope John Paul II](/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II) on the US television programme *[Saturday Night Live](/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live)*, causing [huge controversy](/wiki/Sin%C3%A9ad_O%27Connor_on_Saturday_Night_Live).
- [October 4](/wiki/October_4)
  - The government of [Mozambique](/wiki/Mozambique) signs the [Rome General Peace Accords](/wiki/Rome_General_Peace_Accords) with leaders of [RENAMO](/wiki/RENAMO), ending the 16-year-old [Mozambican Civil War](/wiki/Mozambican_Civil_War).
  - Israeli cargo plane [El Al Flight 1862](/wiki/El_Al_Flight_1862) crashes into residential buildings in Amsterdam's [Bijlmermeer](/wiki/Bijlmermeer), Netherlands, after taking off from [Schiphol Airport](/wiki/Amsterdam_Airport_Schiphol) and losing two engines, killing all 4 people on board and 39 on the ground.
- [October 6](/wiki/October_6) – [Lennart Meri](/wiki/Lennart_Meri) becomes the first [President of Estonia](/wiki/President_of_Estonia) after regaining independence. The [Estonian Government in Exile](/wiki/Estonian_Government_in_Exile) resigns on the next day.
- [October 7](/wiki/October_7) – In [Peru](/wiki/History_of_Peru_(1980%E2%80%932000)), [Shining Path](/wiki/Shining_Path) leader [Abimael Guzmán](/wiki/Abimael_Guzm%C3%A1n) is convicted of treason and sentenced to life in prison.
- [October 11](/wiki/October_11) – The *[Catechism of the Catholic Church](/wiki/Catechism_of_the_Catholic_Church)* is promulgated by [Pope John Paul II](/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II) with his [apostolic constitution](/wiki/Apostolic_constitution), *[Fidei depositum](/wiki/Fidei_depositum)*.
- [October 12](/wiki/October_12)
  - In the [Dominican Republic](/wiki/Dominican_Republic), [Pope John Paul II](/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II) celebrates the 500th anniversary of the meeting of two cultures.
  - The 5.8 [mb](/wiki/Seismic_magnitude_scales#mb) [Cairo earthquake](/wiki/1992_Cairo_earthquake) affects the city with a maximum Mercalli intensity of VIII (*Severe*), leaving 545 dead and 6,512 injured.
- [October 19](/wiki/October_19) – The [Chinese Communist Party](/wiki/Chinese_Communist_Party) promotes several market-oriented reformers to the [Politburo Standing Committee of the Chinese Communist Party](/wiki/Politburo_Standing_Committee_of_the_Chinese_Communist_Party), signaling a defeat for hard-line ideologues.
- [October 20](/wiki/October_20) – The last [Yugoslav Army](/wiki/Military_of_Serbia_and_Montenegro) troops leave [Croatia](/wiki/Croatia).
- [October 21](/wiki/October_21) – 150,000 coal miners march in London to protest government plans to close coal mines and reduce the number of miners.
- [October 23](/wiki/October_23) – Emperor of Japan [Akihito](/wiki/Akihito) begins the first imperial visit to China, telling a Beijing audience he feels deep sorrow for the suffering of the Chinese people during World War II.
- [October 25](/wiki/October_25) – [Lithuania](/wiki/Lithuania) holds [a referendum](/wiki/Referendums_in_Lithuania) on [its first constitution](/wiki/Constitution_of_Lithuania) after declaring independence from the [Soviet Union](/wiki/Soviet_Union) in 1990.
- [October 26](/wiki/October_26) – In a [national referendum](/wiki/Referendums_in_Canada), voters in Canada reject the [Charlottetown Accord](/wiki/Charlottetown_Accord).
- [October 28](/wiki/October_28) – [Hans-Adam II, Prince of Liechtenstein](/wiki/Hans-Adam_II,_Prince_of_Liechtenstein) threatens to dissolve the [Landtag of Liechtenstein](/wiki/Landtag_of_Liechtenstein) and dismiss the government over disagreements regarding a referendum on Liechtenstein's accession to the [European Economic Area](/wiki/European_Economic_Area).
- [October 31](/wiki/October_31) – [Pope John Paul II](/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II) issues an apology and lifts the [1633](/wiki/1633) edict of the [Inquisition](/wiki/Inquisition) against [Galileo Galilei](/wiki/Galileo_Galilei).

### November

- [November 3](/wiki/November_3) – In the [1992 United States presidential election](/wiki/1992_United_States_presidential_election), Democratic [Arkansas](/wiki/Arkansas) governor [Bill Clinton](/wiki/Bill_Clinton) defeats Republican president [George H. W. Bush](/wiki/George_H._W._Bush) and Independent [Ross Perot](/wiki/Ross_Perot).
- [November 8](/wiki/November_8) – More than 350,000 people rally in Berlin to protest right-wing violence against immigrants; stones and eggs are thrown at President of Germany [Richard von Weizsäcker](/wiki/Richard_von_Weizs%C3%A4cker) and Chancellor of Germany [Helmut Kohl](/wiki/Helmut_Kohl).
- [November 11](/wiki/November_11) – The [Church of England](/wiki/Church_of_England) votes to allow women to become priests.
- [November 13](/wiki/November_13)
  - The [government of Peru](/wiki/Government_of_Peru) announces it has arrested a small group of army officers who were plotting the assassination of President [Alberto Fujimori](/wiki/Alberto_Fujimori).
  - A report by the [World Meteorological Organization](/wiki/World_Meteorological_Organization) reports an unprecedented level of [ozone depletion](/wiki/Ozone_depletion) in both the Arctic and Antarctic.
- [November 14](/wiki/November_14) – In poor conditions caused by [Cyclone Forrest](/wiki/Cyclone_Forrest), [Vietnam Airlines Flight 474](/wiki/Vietnam_Airlines_Flight_474) crashes near [Nha Trang](/wiki/Nha_Trang), killing 30.
- [November 15](/wiki/November_15) – The [Lithuanian parliamentary election](/wiki/1992_Lithuanian_parliamentary_election) sees the Communists of the [Democratic Labour Party of Lithuania](/wiki/Democratic_Labour_Party_of_Lithuania), led by [Algirdas Brazauskas](/wiki/Algirdas_Brazauskas), return to power.
- [November 18](/wiki/November_18) – Russian president [Boris Yeltsin](/wiki/Boris_Yeltsin) releases the flight data recorder (FDR) and cockpit voice recorder (CVR) of [Korean Air Flight 007](/wiki/Korean_Air_Flight_007), which was shot down by the Soviets in [1983](/wiki/1983).
- [November 24](/wiki/November_24) – In China, [China Southern Airlines Flight 3943](/wiki/China_Southern_Airlines_Flight_3943), a [China Southern Airlines](/wiki/China_Southern_Airlines) domestic flight, crashes, killing all 141 people on board.
- [November 25](/wiki/November_25)
  - The [Czechoslovak](/wiki/Czech_and_Slovak_Federative_Republic) Federal Assembly votes to split the country into two separate states of the Czech Republic and [Slovakia](/wiki/Slovakia), to take effect on January 1, 1993.
  - In [a national referendum](/wiki/November_1992_Irish_constitutional_referendums) related to [abortion](/wiki/Abortion), voters in Ireland reject the proposed [Twelfth Amendment of the Constitution Bill 1992](/wiki/Twelfth_Amendment_of_the_Constitution_Bill_1992) but approve the [Thirteenth Amendment of the Constitution of Ireland](/wiki/Thirteenth_Amendment_of_the_Constitution_of_Ireland) and the [Fourteenth Amendment of the Constitution of Ireland](/wiki/Fourteenth_Amendment_of_the_Constitution_of_Ireland).
- [November 27](/wiki/November_27) – The [government of Venezuela](/wiki/Government_of_Venezuela) puts down [a coup attempt](/wiki/November_1992_Venezuelan_coup_d%27%C3%A9tat_attempt) by a group of [Air Force](/wiki/Bolivarian_Military_Aviation) officers who have bombed the presidential palace.

### December

- [December 1](/wiki/December_1) – [South Korea](/wiki/South_Korea) and [South Africa](/wiki/South_Africa) reestablish diplomatic relations. [South Korea](/wiki/South_Korea) previously had diplomatic relations with South Africa from 1961 to 1978, when they were severed by the former due to the latter's policy of [apartheid](/wiki/Apartheid).
- [December 3](/wiki/December_3) – [UN Security Council Resolution 794](/wiki/UN_Security_Council_Resolution_794) is unanimously passed, approving a coalition of [United Nations](/wiki/United_Nations) peacekeepers led by the United States to form [UNITAF](/wiki/UNITAF), tasked with ensuring that humanitarian aid gets distributed and establishing peace in [Somalia](/wiki/Somalia).
- [December 4](/wiki/December_4) – US President George Bush announces the deployment of US troops to Africa as part of the United Task Force (UNITAF); UNITAF troops land at Mogadishu on 9 December.
- [December 6](/wiki/December_6) – [Demolition of Babri Masjid](/wiki/Demolition_of_Babri_Masjid): Extremist Hindu activists in India demolish [Babri Masjid](/wiki/Babri_Masjid) – a 16th-century [mosque](/wiki/Mosque) in [Ayodhya](/wiki/Ayodhya) which has been used as a temple since [1949](/wiki/1949) – leading to widespread communal violence, including the [Bombay riots](/wiki/Bombay_riots), in all killing over 1,500 people.
- [December 12](/wiki/December_12) – The 7.8 [Mw](/wiki/Seismic_magnitude_scales#Mw) [Flores earthquake](/wiki/1992_Flores_earthquake_and_tsunami) affects the [Lesser Sunda Islands](/wiki/Lesser_Sunda_Islands) in [Indonesia](/wiki/Indonesia) with a maximum Mercalli intensity of VIII (*Severe*) leaving at least 2,500 dead. A destructive tsunami with wave heights of 25 m (82 ft) follows.
- [December 16](/wiki/December_16) – The [Czech National Council](/wiki/Czech_National_Council) adopts the [Constitution of the Czech Republic](/wiki/Constitution_of_the_Czech_Republic).
- [December 18](/wiki/December_18) – The [South Korean presidential election](/wiki/1992_South_Korean_presidential_election) is won by [Kim Young-sam](/wiki/Kim_Young-sam), the first non-military candidate elected since 1961.
- [December 21](/wiki/December_21) – In [Yugoslavia](/wiki/Serbia_and_Montenegro), President of Serbia [Slobodan Milošević](/wiki/Slobodan_Milo%C5%A1evi%C4%87) defeats [Milan Panić](/wiki/Milan_Pani%C4%87) in the [Serbian presidential election](/wiki/1992_Serbian_general_election).
- [December 22](/wiki/December_22) – The [Archives of Terror](/wiki/Archives_of_Terror) are discovered by [Martín Almada](/wiki/Mart%C3%ADn_Almada) in Asunción, detailing the fates of thousands of Latin Americans who have been secretly kidnapped, tortured and killed by the security services of Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Paraguay and Uruguay in [Operation Condor](/wiki/Operation_Condor).
- [December 28](/wiki/December_28) – Actress [Daniella Perez](/wiki/Daniella_Perez) is murdered in [Rio de Janeiro](/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro), after being stabbed 18 times with scissors by actor Guilherme de Pádua and his then-wife Paula Thomaz. The crime shocked [Brazil](/wiki/Brazil), due to both the victim and the murderer being romantic partners in the telenovela [*De Corpo e Alma*](/wiki/De_Corpo_e_Alma_(TV_series)), which aired on [TV Globo](/wiki/TV_Globo).
- [December 29](/wiki/December_29) – [Brazil's president](/wiki/List_of_presidents_of_Brazil) [Fernando Collor de Mello](/wiki/Fernando_Collor_de_Mello) is found guilty on charges that he stole more than $32 million from the government, preventing him from holding any elected office for eight years. Collor resigns the presidency hours before the sentence to be passed by the [Supreme Federal Court](/wiki/Supreme_Federal_Court).

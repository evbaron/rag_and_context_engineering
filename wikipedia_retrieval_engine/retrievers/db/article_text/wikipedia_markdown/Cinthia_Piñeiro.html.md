**Cinthia Josefina Piñeiro Torres** (born February 4, 1986, in [Santo Domingo](/wiki/Santo_Domingo)) is a [volleyball](/wiki/Volleyball) and [beach volleyball](/wiki/Beach_volleyball) player from [Dominican Republic](/wiki/Dominican_Republic), who participated in the [NORCECA Beach Volleyball Circuit 2007](/wiki/2007_NORCECA_Beach_Volleyball_Circuit_(Santo_Domingo)) with [Ysaires Restituyo](/wiki/Ysaires_Restituyo) finishing in 9th place.

She won the gold medal of the Dominican Republic National Championship 2006, partnering [Rosalin Angeles](/wiki/Rosalin_Angeles).

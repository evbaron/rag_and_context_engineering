**Tomáš Pekhart** (born 26 May 1989) is a Czech professional [footballer](/wiki/Association_football) who plays as a [forward](/wiki/Forward_(association_football)) for [Czech First League](/wiki/Czech_First_League) club [Dukla Prague](/wiki/FK_Dukla_Prague). He represented the Czech Republic at both junior and senior level.

## Club career

### Early career

Born in [Sušice](/wiki/Su%C5%A1ice), [Czechoslovakia](/wiki/Czechoslovakia), he began his youth career with his hometown club TJ Sušice and also played for TJ Klatovy before joining [Slavia Prague](/wiki/SK_Slavia_Prague) in 2003.

Pekhart joined the [Tottenham Hotspur](/wiki/Tottenham_Hotspur_F.C.) youth academy during the summer of 2006.

#### Loan to Southampton

In August 2008, Pekhart joined [Championship](/wiki/Football_League_Championship) side [Southampton](/wiki/Southampton_F.C.) on loan until January 2009. He made his first league appearance as a substitute on 14 September, away against [Queens Park Rangers](/wiki/Queens_Park_Rangers_F.C.) which Southampton lost 4–1. He scored his only league goal for Southampton in a 2–2 home draw against [Ipswich](/wiki/Ipswich_Town_F.C.).

#### Loan to Slavia Prague

He returned to Spurs in January 2009 and on transfer deadline day Pekhart returned to his old club Slavia Prague on a year-long loan until January 2010. On 15 March 2009, Pekhart scored a late goal for Slavia in a 2–1 victory over [FK Jablonec](/wiki/FK_Baumit_Jablonec). Pekhart was rewarded for his first goal with the club and was handed a start on 22 March, scoring the lone goal in the 1–0 victory over [FC Zlín](/wiki/FC_Zl%C3%ADn).

### FK Jablonec

On 12 January 2010, [FK Jablonec](/wiki/FK_Baumit_Jablonec) confirmed the signing of Pekhart on a three-and-a-half-year deal for an undisclosed fee. On 20 March 2010, Pekhart scored his first goal for Jablonec, a late winner in a 2–1 victory over [Příbram](/wiki/FK_Viagem_P%C5%99%C3%ADbram). On 2 May 2010, Pekhart scored two goals in a 4–0 victory over [Bohemians 1905](/wiki/Bohemians_1905). On 8 May 2010, Pekhart scored the opening goal in a 3–0 victory over his former club [Slavia Prague](/wiki/Slavia_Prague). Pekhart continued his fine form during the 2010–11 season, scoring 11 goals in 15 league matches. However, after just half of the [2010–11 Czech First League](/wiki/2010%E2%80%9311_Czech_First_League) season, he moved on loan to fellow [Czech First League](/wiki/Czech_First_League) side [Sparta Prague](/wiki/AC_Sparta_Prague) as part of a deal with [1. FC Nürnberg](/wiki/1._FC_N%C3%BCrnberg), whom he joined on 1 July 2011.

#### Loan to Sparta Prague

Pekhart moved on loan for the second half of the 2010–11 season to [Sparta Prague](/wiki/AC_Sparta_Prague). On 19 March 2011, Pekhart scored his first two goals for Sparta in a 2-0 victory over [FC Zbrojovka Brno](/wiki/FC_Zbrojovka_Brno). In nine league games for Sparta, Pekhart scored seven goals. He ended the 2010-11 season scoring 18 goals in 24 matches, one goal behind former teammate [David Lafata](/wiki/David_Lafata) in the [Czech First League](/wiki/Czech_First_League) scoring table.

### Nürnberg

On 6 August 2011, Pekhart scored in his debut in the [Bundesliga](/wiki/Bundesliga), helping [1. FC Nürnberg](/wiki/1._FC_N%C3%BCrnberg) to a 1–0 victory over [Hertha Berlin](/wiki/Hertha_Berlin) on the opening day of Germany’s football season. On 1 October 2011, Pekhart scored the equalizer in a 3–3 draw with [Mainz](/wiki/1._FSV_Mainz_05). He ended his first season in Germany with 10 goals in 34 matches.

### FC Ingolstadt 04

On 28 August 2014, Pekhart signed a three-year deal with the German second league side [FC Ingolstadt 04](/wiki/FC_Ingolstadt_04). He made his debut for the club on 31 August, appearing as a second half substitute in a 0–3 victory over [SV Sandhausen](/wiki/SV_Sandhausen). While with Ingolstadt Pekhart was seldom used.

### AEK Athens

Pekhart moved to [AEK Athens](/wiki/AEK_Athens_F.C.) on 1 February 2016. On 4 February 2016, he made his debut with the club, in an away 1–1 draw against [Iraklis](/wiki/Iraklis_F.C._(Thessaloniki)) at [Kaftanzoglio Stadium](/wiki/Kaftanzoglio_Stadium). On 20 February 2016, Pekhart scored his first goal for AEK in a 1–1 draw with Iraklis. On 10 April 2016, Pekhart scored two goals in a 2–1 victory over [Panionios F.C.](/wiki/Panionios_F.C.) During the 2016–17 season, Pekhart was AEK's top scorer, scoring 12 goals in 31 matches.

### Hapoel Be'er Sheva

On 21 July 2017, Pekhart signed with [Hapoel Be'er Sheva](/wiki/Hapoel_Be%27er_Sheva_F.C.) for three years. On 10 August 2017, Pekhart scored his first goal for Hapoel in a 4–2 victory over [Bnei Yehuda Tel Aviv](/wiki/Bnei_Yehuda_Tel_Aviv) in the [2017 Israel Super Cup](/wiki/2017_Israel_Super_Cup), claiming his first title with the Israeli side. On 19 August 2017, Pekhart scored his first goal in the Israeli league in a 1–1 draw with [Maccabi Netanya](/wiki/Maccabi_Netanya). He ended the [2017–18](/wiki/2017%E2%80%9318_Israeli_Premier_League) season scoring 10 goals in 28 matches, helping Hapoel win its fifth league title.

### Las Palmas

On 13 August of the following year, he agreed to a two-year deal with [Segunda División](/wiki/Segunda_Divisi%C3%B3n) side [UD Las Palmas](/wiki/UD_Las_Palmas). Pekhart scored his first goal for Las Palmas on 18 November 2018 in a 4–1 loss to [Cádiz CF](/wiki/C%C3%A1diz_CF). On 27 September 2019, he scored his club's third goal in a 3–2 victory over [Albacete Balompié](/wiki/Albacete_Balompi%C3%A9). With the trio of Pekhart, [Pedri](/wiki/Pedri), and [Jonathan Viera](/wiki/Jonathan_Viera) Las Palmas went on an unbeaten run to move up the Segunda Division standings. On 5 October 2019, [Pedri](/wiki/Pedri) and Pekhart goals helped the island side to a 0–2 victory over [CD Lugo](/wiki/CD_Lugo). On 13 October 2019, Las Palmas won their fourth straight match, defeating [Deportivo La Coruña](/wiki/Deportivo_La_Coru%C3%B1a) 3–0 with two goals by Pekhart and one by [Jonathan Viera](/wiki/Jonathan_Viera).

### Legia Warsaw

On 10 February 2020, Pekhart agreed to a two-and-a-half-year deal with [Ekstraklasa](/wiki/Ekstraklasa) side [Legia Warsaw](/wiki/Legia_Warsaw). On 22 February 2020, Pekhart made his debut for Legia, scoring his club's fourth goal in a 4–0 victory over [Jagiellonia Białystok](/wiki/Jagiellonia_Bia%C5%82ystok). On 7 June 2020, Pekhart scored two goals to help Legia to a 1–3 victory over [Wisła Kraków](/wiki/Wis%C5%82a_Krak%C3%B3w). He ended his first season in Poland appearing in 11 league matches scoring 5 goals, helping Legia to a league title.

On 21 March 2021, Pekhart scored four goals in a 4–0 victory over [Zagłębie Lubin](/wiki/Zag%C5%82%C4%99bie_Lubin). During the [2020–21](/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Legia_Warsaw_season) season, he was the leading scorer in [Ekstraklasa](/wiki/Ekstraklasa), scoring 22 goals as Legia won the league title again. On 14 July 2021, Pekhard scored the second goal in a 2–0 victory in the second leg of the first round of [UEFA Champions League](/wiki/UEFA_Champions_League) qualifying against [FK Bodø/Glimt](/wiki/FK_Bod%C3%B8/Glimt) as the Polish champions advanced to the next round. Pekhart ended the [2021–22 season](/wiki/2021%E2%80%9322_Legia_Warsaw_season) in Poland appearing in 45 matches and scoring 10 goals, in a season in which Legia finished 10th in the league standings. After rejecting offers to renew his contract by Legia, Pekhart left the Polish club on 30 June 2022.

### Gaziantep

On 10 August 2022, Pekhart joined [Gaziantep](/wiki/Gaziantep_F.K.) in Turkey on a two-year contract.

### Return to Legia Warsaw

On 30 January 2023, Pekhart agreed to a one-and-a-half-year deal with Ekstraklasa side Legia Warsaw.

On 23 May 2025, Legia announced they would not extend their deal with Pekhart for the following season.

### FK Dukla Prague

On 4 January 2026, Pekhart signed a contract with [Dukla Prague](/wiki/FK_Dukla_Prague).

## International career

Pekhart has represented his country at youth international level. Pekhart played and scored in the final of the [2006 UEFA Under-17 Championship](/wiki/2006_UEFA_European_Under-17_Football_Championship) in [Luxembourg](/wiki/Luxembourg) for his country against Russia.

In 2007, he represented his country in the [FIFA Under-20 World Cup](/wiki/2007_FIFA_U-20_World_Cup) in Canada as the youngest player in the squad having just turned 18. He played in all seven matches of the tournament, including the final against [Argentina](/wiki/Argentina_national_football_team) in which the Czechs finished runners up after [Mauro Zárate](/wiki/Mauro_Z%C3%A1rate) scored the winning goal four minutes from full-time for Argentina.

He made his debut for the [Czech Republic](/wiki/Czech_Republic_national_football_team) on 22 May 2010 in a match against Turkey.

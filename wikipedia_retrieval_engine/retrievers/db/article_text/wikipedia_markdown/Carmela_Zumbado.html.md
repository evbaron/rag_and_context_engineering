# Carmela Zumbado

**Carmela Zumbado** (born February 27, 1991) is an American actress. Her breakthrough role was as [Delilah Alves](/wiki/Delilah_Alves) in the [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) [thriller](/wiki/Thriller_film) series [*You*](/wiki/You_(TV_series)) (2019). She subsequently earned praise for playing Ximena Arista in the [drama](/wiki/Drama) film *[The Wall of Mexico](/wiki/The_Wall_of_Mexico)* (2019), and has additionally played Jeny B in the [action](/wiki/Action_film) film *[Need for Speed](/wiki/Need_for_Speed_(film))* (2014) and Denise Martinez on the [musical series](/wiki/Music_genre) *[Crazy Ex-Girlfriend](/wiki/Crazy_Ex-Girlfriend)* (2019). She appeared on season nine of [*Chicago P.D.*](/wiki/Chicago_P.D._(TV_series)) in a recurring role.

## Early life

Zumbado is a second-generation Cuban-American and was born and raised in [Miami, Florida](/wiki/Miami,_Florida). She is the daughter of Emmy Award-winning photojournalist Tony Zumbado and Lilliam Zumbado.
She is also the oldest of three siblings and has younger sisters named [Gigi](/wiki/Gigi_Zumbado) and [Marisela](/wiki/Marisela_Zumbado), who are also actresses.

## Career

Zumbado began acting in 2012, making her debut with two appearances on the television [crime](/wiki/Crime) series *[America’s Most Wanted](/wiki/America%E2%80%99s_Most_Wanted)*, appearing first as Karen Martinez, then as Teresa Martinez. In the same year, she appeared in the television film *[Freestyle Love Supreme](/wiki/Freestyle_Love_Supreme)* as Rachel, a film which was later adapted onto Broadway. Zumbado continued to make appearances on shows such as [*Necessary Roughness*](/wiki/Necessary_Roughness_(TV_series)) and *[Graceland](/wiki/Graceland_(TV_series))* in 2013, and in the same year made a [cameo appearance](/wiki/Cameo_appearance) in the film *[Identity Thief](/wiki/Identity_Thief)*, as a salesperson at a salon.

In 2014, she made her film debut as Jeny B in the action-thriller film [*Need for Speed*](/wiki/Need_for_Speed_(film)). The film was a commercial success and critical failure. She made appearances on various television shows that same year, including a cameo appearance in the [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) series [*Bloodline*](/wiki/Bloodline_(TV_series)). Zumbado had a recurring role as Windi Stewart in the crime series *[NCIS: New Orleans](/wiki/NCIS:_New_Orleans)* and appeared as Susan in the crime series *[NCIS: Los Angeles](/wiki/NCIS:_Los_Angeles)*. In 2016, she had a guest role as Denise Martinez on the [comedic](/wiki/Comedy) television series *[Crazy Ex-Girlfriend](/wiki/Crazy_Ex-Girlfriend)*, which she later reprised in 2019.

In 2019, she starred as Ximena Arista in the [drama](/wiki/Drama_Film) film *[The Wall of Mexico](/wiki/The_Wall_of_Mexico)*, which was favorably received. In the same year, it was announced that she was cast in the role of [Delilah Alves](/wiki/Delilah_Alves) in the second season of the Netflix [thriller](/wiki/Thriller_(genre)) series [*You*](/wiki/You_(TV_series)). Zumbado had a guest role as Paloma Ball on the final season of the television series [*The Magicians*](/wiki/The_Magicians_(American_TV_series)), and played Gwen, the ex-wife of character [Freddie Benson](/wiki/Freddie_Benson_(iCarly_Character)) on the [sitcom](/wiki/Sitcom) reboot [*iCarly*](/wiki/ICarly_(2021_TV_series)). In 2023, she appeared as part of the main cast in season two of *[Power Book IV: Force](/wiki/Power_Book_IV:_Force)* as Mireya Garcia.

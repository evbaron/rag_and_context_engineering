**Modern Records** was a record label founded in 1980 by [Stevie Nicks](/wiki/Stevie_Nicks), Danny Goldberg, and Paul Fishkin. Its logo clearly stated the founding year to avoid confusion with the earlier [Modern Records](/wiki/Modern_Records). The label had a distribution deal with [Atlantic Records](/wiki/Atlantic_Records) in the United States (also had international distribution with WEA and EMI) and Nicks was the biggest artist on the label, with other artists such as Joey Wilson, [Venice](/wiki/Venice_(band)), [Sandy Stewart](/wiki/Sandy_Stewart_(musician)), [Poe](/wiki/Poe_(singer)), and [Natalie Cole](/wiki/Natalie_Cole) also having records released on the label. Modern Records folded in 1999. The record label was born out of necessity due to Warner Brothers refusing to sign Nicks as a solo act.

**Sammy Shore** (February 7, 1927 – May 18, 2019) was an American actor, stand-up comedian and co-founder of [The Comedy Store](/wiki/The_Comedy_Store).

## Early life

Sammy Shore was born in 1927 in [New York City](/wiki/New_York_City) at the start of the [Great Depression in the United States](/wiki/Great_Depression_in_the_United_States). He grew up in a traditional household to Jewish parents.

## Career

Shore began his career doing stand-up comedy with [Shecky Greene](/wiki/Shecky_Greene) in the [Catskill Mountains](/wiki/Catskill_Mountains). He was chosen, in 1969, to [open](/wiki/Opening_act) for [Elvis Presley](/wiki/Elvis_Presley)’s comeback performance at the [International Hotel](/wiki/Westgate_Las_Vegas), [Las Vegas](/wiki/Las_Vegas). He then opened for Elvis’ road shows until 1972. Shore continued to perform comedy, opening for [Tony Orlando](/wiki/Tony_Orlando), [Tony Bennett](/wiki/Tony_Bennett), [Barbra Streisand](/wiki/Barbra_Streisand), [Sammy Davis Jr](/wiki/Sammy_Davis_Jr.). and others. Shore served in the [U.S. Navy](/wiki/United_States_Navy) during [World War II](/wiki/World_War_II).

On April 7, 1972, he founded the Comedy Store with his writing partner Rudy De Luca. In 1974, his ex-wife, [Mitzi](/wiki/Mitzi_Shore), became the owner of the club. The club launched the careers of many comedians including [Rodney Dangerfield](/wiki/Rodney_Dangerfield), [David Letterman](/wiki/David_Letterman), [Eddie Murphy](/wiki/Eddie_Murphy), [Robin Williams](/wiki/Robin_Williams), [Jim Carrey](/wiki/Jim_Carrey), and [Joe Rogan](/wiki/Joe_Rogan), also including his own son, Pauly.

Shore appeared in many films including *[The Bellboy](/wiki/The_Bellboy)* with [Jerry Lewis](/wiki/Jerry_Lewis) and both *[Life Stinks](/wiki/Life_Stinks)* and *[History of the World Part 1](/wiki/History_of_the_World,_Part_I)* from [Mel Brooks](/wiki/Mel_Brooks). He appeared on *[The Ed Sullivan Show](/wiki/The_Ed_Sullivan_Show)* and was on *[Sanford and Son](/wiki/Sanford_and_Son).*

Shore was voted Best Comedy Act in [Atlantic City, New Jersey](/wiki/Atlantic_City,_New_Jersey), and the [Los Angeles Board of Supervisors](/wiki/Los_Angeles_County_Board_of_Supervisors) named a day after him on July 24, 1990. He appeared at [Harrah's Casinos](/wiki/List_of_Caesars_Entertainment_properties) and hotels more than any other entertainer.

## Personal life

Shore was married to Mitzi for 24 years, and was the father of four, including actor and comedian [Pauly Shore](/wiki/Pauly_Shore). Shore married Suzanne Dennie in 1990. She was [Miss Alabama](/wiki/Miss_Alabama) in 1970. They remained together until his death in [Las Vegas, Nevada](/wiki/Las_Vegas,_Nevada) of natural causes at the age of 92.

**Katharina Molitor** (born 8 November 1983) is a German sportswoman who competes as a [javelin thrower](/wiki/Javelin_throw) and [volleyball](/wiki/Volleyball) player. As a javelin thrower, she is a World Champion, having won gold in 2015, and her personal best throw is 67.69 [m](/wiki/Metre). As a volleyball player, she represents Bayer Leverkusen in the *Erste Volleyball-Bundesliga*, the highest tier of German volleyball.

She is a two-time Olympian, having competed in the javelin throw for Germany at the [2008](/wiki/2008_Summer_Olympics) and [2012 Summer Olympics](/wiki/2012_Summer_Olympics). She [finished eighth](/wiki/Athletics_at_the_2008_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_javelin_throw) in 2008, and [improved to sixth](/wiki/Athletics_at_the_2012_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_javelin_throw) in 2012. She won gold at the [2010 German Championships](/wiki/2010_German_Athletics_Championships) and has since competed in every European and World Championships. Her best result is [first place](/wiki/2015_World_Championships_in_Athletics_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_javelin_throw) at the [2015 World Championships](/wiki/2015_World_Championships_in_Athletics).

The **2013–14 Portland Trail Blazers season** was the franchise's 44th season in the [National Basketball Association](/wiki/National_Basketball_Association) (NBA).

Prior to this season, the Rose Garden was renamed as the [Moda Center](/wiki/Moda_Center). The season saw the [Blazers](/wiki/Portland_Trail_Blazers) improve on their mediocre 2012–13 output, finishing with a 54–28 record, and finishing 5th in the West. After a three-year hiatus, the Blazers returned to the playoffs, facing the [Houston Rockets](/wiki/2013%E2%80%9314_Houston_Rockets_season), in the first round, their first meeting since [2009](/wiki/2009_NBA_Playoffs). The Blazers defeated the Rockets in six games, thanks to [Damian Lillard](/wiki/Damian_Lillard)'s series-clinching three pointer in Game 6 at home, winning their first playoff series since [2000](/wiki/1999-2000_Portland_Trail_Blazers_season). However, their season ended in the next round with a defeat to the eventual NBA champion [San Antonio Spurs](/wiki/2013%E2%80%9314_San_Antonio_Spurs_season) in five games.

## Key dates

- **July 1**: 2013 NBA free agency began.

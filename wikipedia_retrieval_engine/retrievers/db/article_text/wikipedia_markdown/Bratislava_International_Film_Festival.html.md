# Bratislava International Film Festival

## Infobox

- **Location:** Bratislava, Slovakia
- **Established:** 1999

The **Bratislava International Film Festival** (also known as **Bratislava IFF**) is an international [film festival](/wiki/Film_festival) held annually in [Bratislava](/wiki/Bratislava), [Slovakia](/wiki/Slovakia). It was established in 1999.

Apart from the international competition programme, it also regularly features renowned authors' film retrospectives, a European film program, [independent film](/wiki/Independent_film) programmes and various theme programmes.

## Awards

Awards are presented in the following categories:

- **Grand Prix** for best film in the international competition
- **Best Director**
- **Best Actress**
- **Best Actor**
- **[FIPRESCI](/wiki/FIPRESCI) Award** for best film, as determined by a jury of film critics

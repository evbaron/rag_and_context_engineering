**Lanee "Carrie" Butler-Beashel** (born June 3, 1970, in [Manhasset, New York](/wiki/Manhasset,_New_York)) is an American [windsurfer](/wiki/Windsurfer). She competed at four Olympics from 1992 to 2004. Her best position was fourth in 2000.

She is married to America's Cup sailor [Adam Beashel](/wiki/Adam_Beashel). Her brother in law is six-time Olympian [Colin Beashel](/wiki/Colin_Beashel). She and Adam have two sons, born in 2005 and 2008.

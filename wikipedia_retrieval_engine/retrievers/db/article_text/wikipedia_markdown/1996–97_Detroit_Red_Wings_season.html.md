The **[1996–97](/wiki/1996%E2%80%9397_NHL_season) [Detroit Red Wings](/wiki/Detroit_Red_Wings) season** was the [71st](/wiki/Detroit_Red_Wings_seasons) [National Hockey League](/wiki/National_Hockey_League) [season](/wiki/Season_(sports)) in [Detroit](/wiki/Detroit), [Michigan](/wiki/Michigan). The highlight of the Red Wings season was winning the [Stanley Cup](/wiki/1997_Stanley_Cup_Finals), their first since [1955](/wiki/1954%E2%80%9355_NHL_season).

## Off-season

On July 23, 1996, Detroit Red Wings President [Bill Evo](/wiki/Bill_Evo) resigned his position after serving just ten months at the team's helm. The Nickname "Hockeytown" was coined to launch the start of a five-year marketing campaign.

## Regular season

The "HOCKEYTOWN" logo, a Red Wings logo overlapped with the term "HOCKEYTOWN," was shown over center ice starting this season and was still there as of 2022.

A season highlight was [Sergei Fedorov](/wiki/Sergei_Fedorov)'s five-goal performance on December 26, 1996, in a game against the [Washington Capitals](/wiki/Washington_Capitals). The Red Wings won 5–4 in overtime. Fedorov's fifth goal of the game came at 2:39 of the overtime period.

On February 8, 1997, coach [Scotty Bowman](/wiki/Scotty_Bowman) achieved his 1000th victory as an NHL head coach against his previous team, the [Pittsburgh Penguins](/wiki/Pittsburgh_Penguins).

On March 26, 1997, the [Red Wings–Avalanche brawl](/wiki/Detroit_Red_Wings%E2%80%93Colorado_Avalanche_brawl) continued to fuel the rivalry between the teams. Detroit won that game 6–5 in overtime.

## Playoffs

Main article: [1997 Stanley Cup playoffs](/wiki/1997_Stanley_Cup_playoffs)

The Red Wings won the [1997 Stanley Cup Finals](/wiki/1997_Stanley_Cup_Finals), their first [Stanley Cup](/wiki/Stanley_Cup) since the [1954–55 NHL season](/wiki/1954%E2%80%9355_NHL_season).

Six days after winning the Stanley Cup, tragedy struck when defenseman [Vladimir Konstantinov](/wiki/Vladimir_Konstantinov), defenseman [Viacheslav Fetisov](/wiki/Viacheslav_Fetisov), and massage therapist Sergei Mnatsakanov were involved in a limousine accident. The driver, who later said he fell asleep, had a suspended license for previous drunk driving convictions.

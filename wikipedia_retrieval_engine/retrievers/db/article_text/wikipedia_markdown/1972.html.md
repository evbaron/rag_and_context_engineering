This article is about the year 1972. For other uses, see [1972 (disambiguation)](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_(disambiguation)).

From top to bottom, left to right: the [Watergate scandal](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watergate_scandal) begins with the DNC break-in, triggering a major U.S. political crisis; President [Richard Nixon](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Nixon) makes a historic [visit to China](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_visit_by_Richard_Nixon_to_China); the [1972 Summer Olympics](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Summer_Olympics) are marred by the [Munich massacre](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich_massacre); [Apollo 17](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_17) becomes the final Apollo Moon landing; [Bloody Sunday](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloody_Sunday_(1972)) intensifies the Troubles in Northern Ireland; the [1972 Iran blizzard](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Iran_blizzard) kills thousands; the [1972 Black Hills flood](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Black_Hills_flood) kills 238 in [Rapid City, South Dakota](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapid_City,_South_Dakota); [Uruguayan Air Force Flight 571](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uruguayan_Air_Force_Flight_571) crashes in the Andes, leading to a dramatic survival ordeal; and the [1972 Nicaragua earthquake](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Nicaragua_earthquake) devastates Managua.

**1972** ([MCMLXXII](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_numerals)) was a [leap year starting on Saturday](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leap_year_starting_on_Saturday) of the [Gregorian calendar](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorian_calendar), the 1972nd year of the [Common Era](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Era) (CE) and *[Anno Domini](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anno_Domini)* (AD) designations, the 972nd year of the [2nd millennium](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_millennium), the 72nd year of the [20th century](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20th_century), and the 3rd year of the [1970s](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1970s) decade.

Within the context of [Coordinated Universal Time](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time) (UTC) it was the longest year ever, as two [leap seconds](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leap_second) were added during this 366-day year, an event which has not since been repeated. (If its start and end are defined using [mean solar time](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_time) [the legal time scale], its duration was 31622401.141 seconds of [Terrestrial Time](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrestrial_Time) (or [Ephemeris Time](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ephemeris_Time)), which is slightly shorter than [1908](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1908_in_science#Astronomy)).

## Events

### January

Main article: [January 1972](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_1972)

- [January 1](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_1) – [Kurt Waldheim](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Waldheim) becomes [Secretary-General of the United Nations](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretary-General_of_the_United_Nations).
- [January 4](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_4) – The first scientific hand-held calculator ([HP-35](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HP-35)) is introduced (price $395).
- [January 7](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_7) – [Iberia Airlines Flight 602](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iberia_Airlines_Flight_602) crashes into a 462-meter peak on the island of [Ibiza](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibiza); 104 are killed.
- [January 9](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_9) – The [RMS *Queen Elizabeth*](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RMS_Queen_Elizabeth) catches fire and sinks in Hong Kong's Victoria harbor while undergoing conversion to a floating university.
- [January 10](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_10) – Independence leader [Sheikh Mujibur Rahman](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheikh_Mujibur_Rahman) returns to [Bangladesh](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh) after spending over nine months in prison in Pakistan.
- [January 11](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_11) – Sheikh Mujibur Rahman declares a new constitutional government in Bangladesh, with himself as president.
- [January 12](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_12) – In a 10-hour siege, a cell of 4 left-wing insurgents hold off a task force of 2500 army soldiers and police agents in [Santo Domingo](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santo_Domingo), [Dominican Republic](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominican_Republic): eight members of the security forces and the entire insurgent cell are killed in the course of the siege.
- [January 13](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_13) – Prime Minister of [Ghana](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghana) [Kofi Abrefa Busia](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kofi_Abrefa_Busia) is overthrown in a military coup by Colonel [Ignatius Kutu Acheampong](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ignatius_Kutu_Acheampong).
- [January 14](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_14) – Queen [Margrethe II](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margrethe_II) of Denmark succeeds her father, King [Frederik IX](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederik_IX), on the throne of Denmark, the first queen regnant of Denmark since 1412 and the first Danish monarch not named Frederick or Christian since 1513.
- [January 18](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_18) – Members of the [Mukti Bahini](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mukti_Bahini) lay down their arms to the government of the newly independent [Bangladesh](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh), 33 days after winning the war against the occupying [Pakistan Army](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan_Army).
- [January 19](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_19) – The [Libertarian](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libertarian) enclave [Minerva](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Minerva) on a platform in the South Pacific, sponsored by the [Phoenix Foundation](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenix_Foundation), declares independence. Soon neighboring [Tonga](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonga) annexes the area and dismantles the platform.
- [January 20](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_20) – President [Zulfikar Ali Bhutto](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zulfikar_Ali_Bhutto) announces that Pakistan will immediately begin a [nuclear weapons](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_weapon) program.
- [January 21](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_21)
  - A New Delhi [bootlegger](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moonshine) sells [wood alcohol](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wood_alcohol) to a wedding party; 100 people die.
  - [Tripura](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripura), part of the former independent [Twipra Kingdom](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twipra_Kingdom), becomes a full [state of India](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_India).
- [January 24](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_24) – Japanese soldier [Shoichi Yokoi](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoichi_Yokoi) is discovered in [Guam](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guam); he has spent 28 years in the jungle, having failed to surrender after [World War II](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II).
- [January 26](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_26)
  - [Yugoslavian](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Federal_Republic_of_Yugoslavia) air stewardess [Vesna Vulović](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vesna_Vulovi%C4%87) is the only survivor when her plane crashes in [Czechoslovakia](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovakia). She survives after falling 10,160 meters (33,330 feet) in the tail section of the aircraft.
  - The [Aboriginal Tent Embassy](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aboriginal_Tent_Embassy) is set up on the lawn of [Parliament House](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_House,_Canberra) in [Canberra](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canberra).
- [January 30](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_30)
  - [Bloody Sunday](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloody_Sunday_(1972)): The British Army kills 14 unarmed [nationalist](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_nationalism) civil rights marchers in [Derry](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derry), Northern Ireland.
  - [Pakistan](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan) withdraws from the [Commonwealth of Nations](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_of_Nations).
- [January 31](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_31) – King [Birendra](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birendra) succeeds his father as King of [Nepal](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal).

### February

Main article: [February 1972](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_1972)

- [February 2](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_2)
  - A bomb explodes at the British Yacht Club in [West Berlin](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Berlin), killing Irwin Beelitz, a German boat builder. The West German militant group [June 2 Movement](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_2_Movement) claims responsibility, announcing its support of the [Provisional Irish Republican Army](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Irish_Republican_Army).
  - Anti-British riots take place throughout Ireland. The [British Embassy in Dublin](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Embassy_in_Dublin) is [burned to the ground](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burning_of_the_British_Embassy_in_Dublin), as are several British-owned businesses.
- [February 3](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_3)–[13](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_13) – The [1972 Winter Olympics](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Winter_Olympics) are held in [Sapporo](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapporo), Japan.
- [February 4](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_4) – *[Mariner 9](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariner_9)* sends pictures as it orbits [Mars](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mars).
- [February 15](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_15) – President of [Ecuador](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuador) [José María Velasco Ibarra](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Mar%C3%ADa_Velasco_Ibarra) is deposed for the fourth time.
- [February 17](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_17) – [Volkswagen Beetle](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volkswagen_Beetle) sales exceed those of the [Ford Model T](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Model_T) when the 15,007,034th Beetle is produced.
- [February 19](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_19) – [Asama-Sansō incident](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asama-Sans%C5%8D_incident): Five [United Red Army](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Red_Army) members break into a lodge below [Mount Asama](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Asama) in Japan, taking the wife of the lodgekeeper hostage.
- [February 21](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_21) – The [Soviet](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet) uncrewed [spaceship](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spacecraft) *[Luna 20](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luna_20)* lands on the [Moon](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moon).
- [February 21](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_21)–[28](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_28) – U.S. President [Richard Nixon](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Nixon) makes an unprecedented [8-day visit](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Nixon_visit_to_China) to the People's Republic of China and meets with [Mao Zedong](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mao_Zedong).
- [February 22](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_22)
  - [The Troubles](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Troubles): [1972 Aldershot bombing](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Aldershot_bombing) – A car bomb planted by the [Official Irish Republican Army](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_Irish_Republican_Army) kills seven people outside a British military base in [Aldershot](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aldershot), England.
  - [Lufthansa Flight 649](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lufthansa_Flight_649) is hijacked and taken to [Aden](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aden). Passengers are released the following day after a ransom of 5 million US dollars is agreed.
- [February 23](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_23) – US activist [Angela Davis](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angela_Davis) is released from jail. Rodger McAfee, a farmer from [Caruthers, California](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caruthers,_California), helps her make bail.
- [February 26](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_26) – *[Luna 20](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luna_20)* comes back to Earth with 55 grams (1.9 ounces) of lunar soil.
- [February 28](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_28) – The Asama-Sansō incident ends in a standoff between 5 members of the Japanese United Red Army and the authorities, in which two policemen are killed and 12 injured.

### March

Main article: [March 1972](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_1972)

- [March 1](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_1) – [Juan María Bordaberry](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_Mar%C3%ADa_Bordaberry) is sworn in as [President of Uruguay](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Uruguay) amid accusations of electoral fraud.
- [March 2](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_2)
  - The [Club of Rome](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_of_Rome) presents the research results leading to its report *[The Limits to Growth](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Limits_to_Growth)*, published later in the month.
  - The *[Pioneer 10](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pioneer_10)* spacecraft is launched from [Cape Kennedy](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Kennedy), to be the first man-made spacecraft to leave the [Solar System](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_System).
  - [Jean-Bédel Bokassa](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-B%C3%A9del_Bokassa) becomes President of the [Central African Republic](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_African_Republic).
- [March 4](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_4)
  - [Libya](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_Arab_Republic) and the [Soviet Union](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) sign a cooperation treaty.
  - The [Organisation of the Islamic Conference](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organisation_of_the_Islamic_Conference) Charter is signed (effective February 28, 1973).
- [March 19](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_19) – India and [Bangladesh](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh) sign the [Indo-Bangladeshi Treaty of Friendship, Cooperation and Peace](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Bangladeshi_Treaty_of_Friendship,_Cooperation_and_Peace).
- [March 22](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_22)
  - The [92nd U.S. Congress](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/92nd_U.S._Congress) votes to send the proposed [Equal Rights Amendment](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equal_Rights_Amendment) to the states for ratification.
  - *[Eisenstadt v. Baird](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eisenstadt_v._Baird)*: The Supreme Court of the U.S. rules that unmarried people have the right to access contraception on the same basis as married couples
- [March 25](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_25) – "[Après toi](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apr%C3%A8s_toi)" sung by [Vicky Leandros](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicky_Leandros) (music by Klaus Munro & Mario Panas, lyric by Klaus Munro & Yves Dessca) wins the [Eurovision Song Contest 1972](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurovision_Song_Contest_1972) (staged in Edinburgh) for [Luxembourg](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxembourg).
- [March 26](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_26) – An [avalanche](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avalanche) on [Mount Fuji](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Fuji) in Japan kills 19 climbers.
- [March 27](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_27)
  - The [First Sudanese Civil War](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Sudanese_Civil_War) ends.
  - The Soviet Union launches [Venera 8](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venera_8), which will make the first soft landing on Venus.
- [March 30](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_30) – [Vietnam War](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_War): The [Easter Offensive](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Easter_Offensive) begins after [North Vietnamese](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Vietnam) forces cross into the Demilitarized Zone (DMZ) of [South Vietnam](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Vietnam)

### April

Main article: [April 1972](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_1972)

- [April 4](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_4) – The U.S. formally recognizes [Bangladesh](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh).
- [April 10](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_10)
  - The U.S. and the Soviet Union join some 70 nations in signing the [Biological Weapons Convention](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_Weapons_Convention), an agreement to ban [biological warfare](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_warfare).
  - [Tombs](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yinqueshan_Han_Tombs) containing [bamboo slips](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yinqueshan_Han_Slips), among them [Sun Tzu](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Tzu)'s *[Art of War](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_of_War)* and [Sun Bin](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Bin)'s lost [military treatise](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Bin%27s_Art_of_War), are accidentally discovered by construction workers in [Shandong](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong).
  - The 6.7 Mw  [Qir earthquake](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Qir_earthquake) shakes southern Iran with a maximum Mercalli intensity of IX (Violent), killing 5,374 people in the province of [Fars](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fars_province).
  - The [44th Annual Academy Awards](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/44th_Annual_Academy_Awards) are held at the [Dorothy Chandler Pavilion](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorothy_Chandler_Pavilion) in Los Angeles.
- [April 13](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_13) – The [Universal Postal Union](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Postal_Union) decides to recognize the People's Republic of China as the only legitimate Chinese representative, effectively expelling the [Republic of China](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_China) administering [Taiwan](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan).
- [April 16](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_16)
  - [Apollo 16](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_16) ([John Young](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Young_(astronaut)), [Ken Mattingly](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Mattingly), [Charlie Duke](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Duke)) is launched. During the mission, the astronauts, driving the [Lunar Roving Vehicle](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunar_Roving_Vehicle), achieve a [lunar rover](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunar_rover) speed record of 17 km/h.
  - [Vietnam War](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_War): Nguyen Hue Offensive – Prompted by the [North Vietnamese](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Vietnam) offensive, the United States resumes bombing of [Hanoi](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanoi) and [Haiphong](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haiphong).
- [April 26](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_26) – The [Lockheed L-1011 TriStar](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_L-1011_TriStar) enters service with [Eastern Airlines](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Airlines).
- [April 27](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_27)
  - *[Ikiza](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikiza)*: [Burundi](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burundi) government forces begin a 4-month genocide against the [Hutu](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hutu) people, killing 100,000–300,000.
  - A no-confidence vote against [German Chancellor](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chancellor_of_Germany_(Federal_Republic)) [Willy Brandt](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willy_Brandt) fails under obscure circumstances; years later it turned out that at least one opposition MP was paid by the East German [Stasi](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stasi) for not supporting the vote.
- [April 29](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_29) – The fourth anniversary of the Broadway musical *[Hair](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hair_(musical))* is celebrated with a free concert at a Central Park bandshell, followed by dinner at the Four Seasons. There, 13 [Black Panther](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Panther_Party) protesters and the show's co-author, [Jim Rado](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Rado), are arrested for disturbing the peace and for using marijuana.

### May

Main article: [May 1972](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_1972)

- [May 2](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_2) – Fire at the [Sunshine Mine](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunshine_Mine), a [silver](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver) mine in [Idaho](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idaho), kills 91.
- [May 5](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_5) – An [Alitalia](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alitalia) [DC-8](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DC-8) [crashes](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alitalia_Flight_112) west of [Palermo](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palermo), [Sicily](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sicily); 115 die.
- [May 7](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_7) – [General elections](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Italian_general_election) are held in Italy.
- [May 10](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_10) – [Operation Linebacker](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Linebacker) and [Operation Custom Tailor](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Custom_Tailor) begin with large-scale bombing operations against North Vietnam by tactical fighter aircraft.
- [May 13](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_13) – A [fire in a nightclub atop the Sennichi department store](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sennichi_Department_Store_Building_fire) in [Osaka](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka), Japan, kills 115.
- [May 21](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_21) – In [St. Peter's Basilica](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Peter%27s_Basilica) ([Vatican City](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vatican_City)), [Laszlo Toth](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laszlo_Toth) attacks [Michelangelo](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelangelo)'s *[Pietà](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piet%C3%A0_(Michelangelo))* statue with a [geologist's hammer](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geologist%27s_hammer), shouting that he is [Jesus Christ](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesus_Christ).
- [May 22](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_22)
  - The [Dominion of Ceylon](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_of_Ceylon) becomes the republic of [Sri Lanka](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka) under prime minister [Sirimavo Bandaranaike](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirimavo_Bandaranaike), when its new [constitution](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution) is ratified.
  - [Ferit Melen](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferit_Melen) forms the new (interim) government of [Turkey](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey) (35th government)
- [May 23](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_23) – The Tamil United Front (later known as [Tamil United Liberation Front](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_United_Liberation_Front)), a pro-[Tamil](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamils) organization, is founded in Sri Lanka.
- [May 26](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_26)
  - [Richard Nixon](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Nixon) and [Leonid Brezhnev](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonid_Brezhnev) sign the [SALT I treaty](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SALT_I_treaty) in Moscow, as well as the [Anti-Ballistic Missile Treaty](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Ballistic_Missile_Treaty) and other agreements.
  - [Willandra National Park](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willandra_National_Park) is established in Australia.
- [May 27](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_27) – [Mark Donohue](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Donohue) wins the [Indianapolis 500](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Indianapolis_500) in a [Penske Racing](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penske_Racing) [McLaren](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McLaren)–[Offenhauser](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Offenhauser).
- [May 30](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_30)
  - [Lod Airport massacre](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lod_Airport_massacre): Three [Japanese Red Army](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_Red_Army) members operating on behalf of the [Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine – External Operations](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_Front_for_the_Liberation_of_Palestine_%E2%80%93_External_Operations) kill 26 and injure 80 people at [Lod Airport](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lod_Airport), [Israel](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel).

- [December 2](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/December_2) – [1972 Australian federal election](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Australian_federal_election): The [Labor Party](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Labor_Party) led by [Gough Whitlam](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gough_Whitlam) defeats the [Liberal](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_Party_of_Australia)/[Country](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Party_of_Australia) [Coalition](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coalition_(Australia)) [government](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McMahon_government) led by [Prime Minister](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Australia) [William McMahon](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_McMahon). Whitlam will be sworn in on [December 5](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/December_5); his first action using executive power is to withdraw all Australian personnel from the [Vietnam War](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_War). McMahon resigns from the Liberal leadership almost immediately; he will be [replaced](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Liberal_Party_of_Australia_leadership_election) by outgoing [Treasurer](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasurer_of_Australia) [Billy Snedden](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Snedden).

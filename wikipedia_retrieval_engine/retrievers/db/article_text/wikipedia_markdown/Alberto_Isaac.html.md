# Alberto Isaac

**Alberto Isaac** (18 March 1923 – 9 January 1998) was a Mexican freestyle [swimmer](/wiki/Swimming_(sport)) and later a film director and screenwriter. He competed in the [1948 Summer Olympics](/wiki/1948_Summer_Olympics) and the [1952 Summer Olympics](/wiki/1952_Summer_Olympics).

In 1969, he directed the documentary film *[The Olympics in Mexico](/wiki/The_Olympics_in_Mexico)* which was nominated for an [Academy Award](/wiki/Academy_Award) for [Best Documentary Feature](/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Documentary_Feature). In 1980 he was a member of the jury at the [30th Berlin International Film Festival](/wiki/30th_Berlin_International_Film_Festival). In 1987, he was a member of the jury at the [15th Moscow International Film Festival](/wiki/15th_Moscow_International_Film_Festival).

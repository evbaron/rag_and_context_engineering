**Nouhaila Benzina** ([Arabic](/wiki/Arabic_language): نهيلة بنزينة; born 11 May 1998) is a Moroccan professional [footballer](/wiki/Association_football) who plays as a [defender](/wiki/Defender_(association_football)) for [AS FAR](/wiki/AS_FAR_(women)) and the [Morocco women's national team](/wiki/Morocco_women%27s_national_football_team).

In July 2023, she made history as the first female player to ever wear an Islamic headscarf (a [hijab](/wiki/Hijab)) at the senior-level [FIFA Women's World Cup](/wiki/FIFA_Women%27s_World_Cup). She has been considered a [role model](/wiki/Role_model) for Muslim female footballers.

## Club career

Benzina has played for [AS FAR](/wiki/AS_FAR_(football_club)) in Morocco.

## International career

Benzina has capped for Morocco at under-20 and senior levels.

Benzina was included in the Moroccan national team squad for the [2023 FIFA Women's World Cup](/wiki/2023_FIFA_Women%27s_World_Cup), which marked the debut World Cup appearance for both Benzina and the Moroccan national team. Morocco's first Women's World Cup match was on 24 July against [Germany](/wiki/Germany_women%27s_national_football_team), where Benzina made headlines around the world for being the first player on the team roster to wear a [hijab](/wiki/Hijab) at a World Cup match, though she did not play in the match. On 30 July she became the first player to play in a World Cup match wearing a hijab in her country's 1–0 win against [South Korea](/wiki/South_Korea_women%27s_national_football_team). French press, namely *[L'Équipe](/wiki/L%27%C3%89quipe)*, made [Islamophobic](/wiki/Islamophobia) comments directed at her after the match.

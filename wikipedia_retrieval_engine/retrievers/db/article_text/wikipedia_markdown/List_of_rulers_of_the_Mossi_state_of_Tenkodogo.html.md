# List of rulers of the Mossi state of Tenkodogo

This is a **list of traditional rulers of the [Mossi](/wiki/Mossi_people) kingdom of [Tenkodogo](/wiki/Tenkodogo)**, which today is part of [Burkina Faso](/wiki/Burkina_Faso). Tenkodogo today is now the seventh largest town and is the seat of the regional government of [Boulgou Province](/wiki/Boulgou_Province). The traditional ruler is known as Tenkodogo-*naaba*.

**Naaba** = *Ruler*

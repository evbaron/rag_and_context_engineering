# Moxostoma

"Redhorse" redirects here. For other uses, see [Red Horse (disambiguation)](/wiki/Red_Horse_(disambiguation)).

***Moxostoma***, the **redhorses** or **jumprocks**, is a genus of North American [ray-finned fish](/wiki/Ray-finned_fish) in the family [Catostomidae](/wiki/Catostomidae). Redhorses are variable in size, geographic location, and other ecological traits such as spawning substrate. Several redhorses are long-lived (lifespans greater than 20 years), much like many other [catostomid](/wiki/Catostomidae) species. The [silver redhorse](/wiki/Silver_redhorse) is the longest-lived redhorse known by nearly a decade, with ages exceeding 40 years. Redhorses are broadly of conservation concern, as these long-lived species are highly intolerant to environmental pollution, [habitat fragmentation](/wiki/Habitat_fragmentation), and are currently subject to unregulated 21st century sport [bowfishing](/wiki/Bowfishing) which is removing and wantonly wasting several of these species by the ton.

## Species

The following 26 species are recognized in this genus:

- *[Moxostoma albidum](/wiki/Moxostoma_albidum)* ([Girard](/wiki/Charles_Fr%C3%A9d%C3%A9ric_Girard), 1856) (Longlip jumprock)
- *[Moxostoma anisurum](/wiki/Moxostoma_anisurum)* ([Rafinesque](/wiki/Constantine_Samuel_Rafinesque), 1820) (Silver redhorse)
- *Moxostoma antelunare* Akin, Jenkins & Armbruster, 2025
- *[Moxostoma ariommum](/wiki/Moxostoma_ariommum)* [C. R. Robins](/wiki/Charles_Richard_Robins) & Raney, 1956 (Bigeye jumprock)
- *[Moxostoma austrinum](/wiki/Moxostoma_austrinum)* [T. H. Bean](/wiki/Tarleton_Hoffman_Bean), 1880 (Mexican redhorse)
- *[Moxostoma breviceps](/wiki/Moxostoma_breviceps)* ([Cope](/wiki/Edward_Drinker_Cope), 1870) (Smallmouth redhorse)
- *[Moxostoma carinatum](/wiki/Moxostoma_carinatum)* (Cope, 1870) (River redhorse)
- *Moxostoma carolina* Hogue, Tracy, Hughes & Jenkins, 2026 (Carolina redhorse)
- *[Moxostoma cervinum](/wiki/Moxostoma_cervinum)* ([Cope, 1868) (Blacktip jumprock)
- *[Moxostoma collapsum](/wiki/Moxostoma_collapsum)* (Cope, 1870) (Notchlip redhorse)
- *[Moxostoma congestum](/wiki/Moxostoma_congestum)* ([S. F. Baird](/wiki/Spencer_Fullerton_Baird) & Girard, 1854) (Gray redhorse)
- *[Moxostoma duquesni](/wiki/Moxostoma_duquesni)* ([Lesueur](/wiki/Charles_Alexandre_Lesueur), 1817) (Black redhorse)
- *[Moxostoma erythrurum](/wiki/Moxostoma_erythrurum)* ([Rafinesque](/wiki/Constantine_Samuel_Rafinesque), 1818) (Golden redhorse)
- *[Moxostoma hubbsi](/wiki/Moxostoma_hubbsi)* V. Legendre, 1952 (Copper redhorse)
- †*[Moxostoma lacerum](/wiki/Moxostoma_lacerum)* ([D. S. Jordan](/wiki/David_Starr_Jordan) & Brayton, 1877) (Harelip sucker)
- *[Moxostoma lachneri](/wiki/Moxostoma_lachneri)* C. R. Robins & Raney, 1956 (Greater jumprock)
- *[Moxostoma macrolepidotum](/wiki/Moxostoma_macrolepidotum)* (Lesueur, 1817) (Shorthead redhorse)

- *[Moxostoma pappillosum](/wiki/Moxostoma_pappillosum)* (Cope, 1870) (V-lip redhorse)
- *[Moxostoma pisolabrum](/wiki/Moxostoma_pisolabrum)* Trautman & [R. G. Martin](/wiki/Robert_G._Martin), 1951 (Pealip redhorse)
- *[Moxostoma poecilurum](/wiki/Moxostoma_poecilurum)* (D. S. Jordan, 1877) (Blacktail redhorse)
- *[Moxostoma robustum](/wiki/Moxostoma_robustum)* (Cope, 1870) (Robust redhorse)
- *[Moxostoma rupiscartes](/wiki/Moxostoma_rupiscartes)* D. S. Jordan & [O. P. Jenkins](/wiki/Oliver_Peebles_Jenkins), 1889 (Striped jumprock)
- *[Moxostoma ugidatli](/wiki/Moxostoma_ugidatli)* Jenkins, Favrot, Freeman, Albanese & Ambruster, 2025 (Sicklefin redhorse)
- *[Moxostoma valenciennesi](/wiki/Moxostoma_valenciennesi)* D. S. Jordan, 1885 (Greater redhorse)

Additionally, two undescribed species are thought to belong to this genus:

- *Moxostoma sp. 1 "Apalachicola"* Undescribed,  (Apalachicola Redhorse)
- *Moxostoma sp. 4 "Brassy"* Undescribed,  (Brassy Jumprock)

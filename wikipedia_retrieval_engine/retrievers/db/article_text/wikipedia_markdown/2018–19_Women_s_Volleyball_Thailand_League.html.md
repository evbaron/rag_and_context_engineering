# 2018–19 Women's Volleyball Thailand League

## Infobox

- **Dates:** 27 October 2018 – 17 March 2019
- **Teams:** 8
- **Venue(s):** 7 (in 5 host cities)
- **Third place:** 3BB Nakornnont
- **Fourth place:** Thai–Denmark Khonkaen Star
- **MVP:** Onuma Sittirak
- **Matches played:** 56 (Regular seasons) 4 (Final series)

The **2018–19 Women's Volleyball Thailand League** is the 14th season of the [Women's Volleyball Thailand League](/wiki/Women%27s_Volleyball_Thailand_League), the top Thai professional league for women's [volleyball](/wiki/Volleyball) clubs, since its establishment in 2005, also known as **CP Women's Volleyball Thailand League** due to the sponsorship deal with [Charoen Pokphand](/wiki/Charoen_Pokphand). A total of 8 teams will compete in the league. The season will begin on 27 October 2018, and is scheduled to conclude in 2019. This season will be organized by the [Thailand Volleyball Association](/wiki/Thailand_Volleyball_Association) (TVA) instead Thailand Volleyball Co., Ltd. The season started.

[Supreme Chonburi](/wiki/Supreme_Volleyball_Club) are the defending champions, also the reigning Asian club champions, while the 2018 Pro Challenge champion will has entered as the promoted team from the 2018 Volleyball Pro Challenge.

## Format

### Regular season

- **First leg:** single [round-robin](/wiki/Round-robin_tournament).
- **Second leg:** single [round-robin](/wiki/Round-robin_tournament).

#### Regular season standing procedure

1. Number of matches won
2. Match points
3. Sets ratio
4. Points ratio
5. Result of the last match between the tied teams

Match won 3–0 or 3–1: 3 match points for the winner, 0 match points for the loser
Match won 3–2: 2 match points for the winner, 1 match point for the loser

# List of Xena: Warrior Princess episodes

*Xena: Warrior Princess* complete collection on [DVD](/wiki/DVD).

*[Xena: Warrior Princess](/wiki/Xena:_Warrior_Princess)* is an American [television series](/wiki/Television_series) that was created by [Robert Tapert](/wiki/Robert_Tapert) and John Schulian. *Xena* is a [historical fantasy](/wiki/Historical_fantasy) set primarily in [ancient Greece](/wiki/Ancient_Greece), although it has a flexible time setting and occasionally features [Oriental](/wiki/Oriental), [Egyptian](/wiki/Ancient_Egypt) and [Medieval](/wiki/Middle_Ages) elements. The flexible fantasy framework of the show accommodates a considerable range of theatrical styles, from high [melodrama](/wiki/Melodrama) to [slapstick](/wiki/Slapstick) comedy, from whimsical and [musical](/wiki/Music) to all-out [action](/wiki/Action_film) and [adventure](/wiki/Adventure_film). While the show is typically set in ancient times, its themes are essentially modern and it investigates the ideas of taking responsibility for past misdeeds, the value of human life, personal liberty and sacrifice, and friendship. The show often addresses ethical dilemmas, such as the morality of [pacifism](/wiki/Pacifism); however, the storylines rarely seek to provide unequivocal solutions.

The character of Xena debuted on March 13, 1995, on the TV series *[Hercules: The Legendary Journeys](/wiki/Hercules:_The_Legendary_Journeys)*. Three episodes featuring Xena, collectively called *The Xena Trilogy*, initially aired as part of the first season of *[Hercules: The Legendary Journeys](/wiki/Hercules:_The_Legendary_Journeys)*. Xena's own series began on September 4, 1995. The series ran for six seasons and 134 episodes until its final episode aired on June 23, 2001. The series won an [Emmy Award](/wiki/Emmy_Awards) in 2001, and was ranked in #10 in TV Guide's Top 25 Cult TV Shows of All Time.

### Introductory episodes (1995)

The program and its namesake character are introduced during the first season of *[Hercules: The Legendary Journeys](/wiki/Hercules:_The_Legendary_Journeys)*. The *Hercules: The Legendary Journeys* episodes, "[The Warrior Princess](/wiki/List_of_Hercules:_The_Legendary_Journeys_episodes#ep9)", "[The Gauntlet](/wiki/List_of_Hercules:_The_Legendary_Journeys_episodes#ep12)" and "[Unchained Heart](/wiki/List_of_Hercules:_The_Legendary_Journeys_episodes#ep13)", served as [pilot episodes](/wiki/Television_pilot) for the show. A director's cut of the episodes were later released as a separate VHS set and DVD as *Hercules: The Xena Trilogy*

# List of New Zealand Test cricket records

[Richard Hadlee](/wiki/Richard_Hadlee) holds the record for the most Test [wickets](/wiki/Wicket#Dismissal_of_a_batter) (431) and the most [five-wicket hauls](/wiki/Five-wicket_haul) (36) for New Zealand.

**[Test cricket](/wiki/Test_cricket)** is the oldest form of [cricket](/wiki/Cricket) played at [international level](/wiki/International_cricket). A Test match is scheduled to take place over a period of five days, and is played by teams representing [full member](/wiki/List_of_International_Cricket_Council_members#Full_members) nations of the [International Cricket Council](/wiki/International_Cricket_Council) (ICC).

The following is a list of records by the [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand_national_cricket_team) [Test cricket](/wiki/Test_cricket) team. It is based on the [List of Test cricket records](/wiki/List_of_Test_cricket_records), but concentrates solely on records dealing with the New Zealand Test cricket team, and any [cricketers](/wiki/List_of_New_Zealand_Test_cricketers) who have played for that team.

New Zealand took part in their first recognised Test cricket match against [England](/wiki/England_cricket_team) in the 1929/30 season, and the records listed here date from that time.

New Zealand won the inaugural ICC World Test Championship, beating India in the final by 8 wickets. They are ranked number two in Tests, number one in ODIs and number four in T20Is.

### Result records

A Test match is [won](/wiki/Result_(cricket)) when one side has scored more [runs](/wiki/Run_(cricket)) than the total runs scored by the opposing side during their two [innings](/wiki/Innings). If both sides have completed both their allocated innings and the side that fielded last has the higher aggregate of runs, it is known as a win by runs. This indicates the number of runs that they had scored more than the opposing side. If one side scores more runs in a single innings than the total runs scored by the other side in both their innings, it is known as a win by innings and runs. If the side batting last wins the match, it is known as a win by [wickets](/wiki/Wicket#Dismissing_a_batsman), indicating the number of wickets that were still to fall.

In the second innings, New Zealand scored 483 runs to set a target of 257 runs to win for England, which they fell one run short of.

### Highest partnerships by wicket

In cricket, two batsmen are always present at the [crease](/wiki/Crease_(cricket)) batting together in a partnership. This partnership will continue until one of them is [dismissed](/wiki/Dismissal_(cricket)), [retires](/wiki/Substitute_(cricket)#Retired_hurt_(or_not_out)) or the innings comes to a close.

A wicket partnership describes the number of runs scored before each wicket falls. The first wicket partnership is between the opening batsmen and continues until the first wicket falls. The second wicket partnership then commences between the [not out](/wiki/Not_out) batsman and the number three batsman. This partnership continues until the second wicket falls. The third wicket partnership then commences between the not out batsman and the new batsman. This continues down to the tenth wicket partnership. When the tenth wicket has fallen, there is no batsman left to partner so the innings is closed.

## Bowling records

### Most career wickets

A [bowler](/wiki/Bowling_(cricket)) takes the [wicket](/wiki/Wicket#dismissing_a_batsman) of a batsman when the form of dismissal is [bowled](/wiki/Bowled), [caught](/wiki/Caught), [leg before wicket](/wiki/Leg_before_wicket), [stumped](/wiki/Stumped) or [hit wicket](/wiki/Hit_wicket). If the batsman is dismissed by [run out](/wiki/Run_out), [obstructing the field](/wiki/Obstructing_the_field), [handling the ball](/wiki/Handled_the_ball), [hitting the ball twice](/wiki/Hit_the_ball_twice) or [timed out](/wiki/Timed_out) the bowler does not receive credit.

[Shane Warne](/wiki/Shane_Warne) held the record for the most Test wickets with 708 until December 2007 when [Sri Lankan](/wiki/Sri_Lanka_national_cricket_team) bowler [Muttiah Muralitharan](/wiki/Muttiah_Muralitharan) passed Warne's milestone. Muralitharan, who continued to play until 2010, finished with 800 wickets to his name. [James Anderson](/wiki/James_Anderson_(cricketer)) of England is third on the list with 704 wickets, which is the record for most wickets by a [fast bowler](/wiki/Fast_bowling) in Test cricket. New Zealand's [Richard Hadlee](/wiki/Richard_Hadlee) is the highest ranked New Zealand bowler taking 431 wickets.

### Best figures in a match

A bowler's bowling figures in a match is the sum of the wickets taken and the runs conceded over both innings.

### Best career economy rate

### Worst figures in a match

### Hat-trick

In [cricket](/wiki/Cricket), a **hat-trick** occurs when a bowler [takes three wickets](/wiki/Dismissal_(cricket)) with consecutive [deliveries](/wiki/Delivery_(cricket)). The deliveries may be interrupted by an [over](/wiki/Over_(cricket)) bowled by another bowler from the other end of the [pitch](/wiki/Cricket_pitch) or the other team's [innings](/wiki/Innings), but must be three consecutive deliveries by the individual bowler in the same match. Only [wickets](/wiki/Wickets#Dismissing_a_batsman) attributed to the bowler count towards a hat-trick; run outs do not count.
In [Test cricket](/wiki/Test_cricket) history there have been just [44 hat-tricks](/wiki/List_of_Test_cricket_hat-tricks), the first achieved by [Fred Spofforth](/wiki/Fred_Spofforth) for [Australia](/wiki/Australia_national_cricket_team) against [England](/wiki/England_cricket_team) in 1879. In 1912, Australian [Jimmy Matthews](/wiki/Jimmy_Matthews) achieved the feat twice in one game against [South Africa](/wiki/South_Africa_national_cricket_team). The only other players to achieve two hat-tricks are Australia's [Hugh Trumble](/wiki/Hugh_Trumble), against England in 1902 and 1904, [Pakistan](/wiki/Pakistan_national_cricket_team)'s [Wasim Akram](/wiki/Wasim_Akram), in separate games against [Sri Lanka](/wiki/Sri_Lanka_national_cricket_team) in 1999, and England's [Stuart Broad](/wiki/Stuart_Broad).

## Wicket-keeping records

The [wicket-keeper](/wiki/Wicket-keeper) is a specialist [fielder](/wiki/Fielding_(cricket)) who stands behind the [stumps](/wiki/Stump_(cricket)) being guarded by the batsman on [strike](/wiki/Strike_(cricket)) and is the only member of the fielding side allowed to wear gloves and leg pads.

### Most career dismissals

A wicket-keeper can be credited with the dismissal of a batsman in two ways, [caught](/wiki/Caught) or [stumped](/wiki/Stumped). A fair catch is taken when the [ball](/wiki/Cricket_ball) is caught fully within the field of play without it bouncing after the ball has touched the striker's [bat](/wiki/Cricket_bat) or glove holding the bat, while a stumping occurs when the wicket-keeper puts down the wicket while the batsman is out of his [ground](/wiki/Crease_(cricket)) and not attempting a run.
New Zealand's [BJ Watling](/wiki/BJ_Watling) is the highest New Zealander in taking most dismissals in Test cricket as a designated wicket-keeper.

## Fielding records

### Most career catches

[Caught](/wiki/Caught) is one of the nine methods a batsman can be [dismissed](/wiki/Dismissal_(cricket)) in cricket. The majority of catches are caught in the [slips](/wiki/Slip_(cricket)), located behind the batsman, next to the wicket-keeper, on the [off side](/wiki/Off_side) of the field. Most slip fielders are [top order](/wiki/Batting_order_(cricket)) batsmen.

India's [Rahul Dravid](/wiki/Rahul_Dravid) holds the record for the most catches in Test cricket by a non-wicket-keeper with 210, followed by [Mahela Jayawardene](/wiki/Mahela_Jayawardene) of Sri Lanka on 205 and South African [Jacques Kallis](/wiki/Jacques_Kallis) with 200. [Stephen Fleming](/wiki/Stephen_Fleming) is the highest ranked New Zealander in seventh, securing 171 catches in his Test career.

An [umpire](/wiki/Umpire_(cricket)) in cricket is a person who officiates the match according to the *[Laws of Cricket](/wiki/Laws_of_Cricket)*. Two umpires adjudicate the match on the field, whilst a [third umpire](/wiki/Third_umpire) has access to video replays, and a [fourth umpire](/wiki/Fourth_umpire) looks after the match balls and other duties. The records below are only for on-field umpires.

[Aleem Dar](/wiki/Aleem_Dar) of Pakistan holds the record for the most Test matches umpired with 130. They are followed by South Africa's [Rudi Koertzen](/wiki/Rudi_Koertzen) who officiated in 108.

# Colin Beashel

**Colin Kenneth Beashel** (born 21 November 1959) is an Australian sailor who crewed on the winning America's Cup team [Australia II](/wiki/Australia_II) in 1983 and competed at six Olympics between 1984 and 2004, winning bronze in 1996. He became, jointly with Brazilian [Torben Grael](/wiki/Torben_Grael), the eighth sailor to compete at [six Olympics](/wiki/List_of_athletes_with_the_most_appearances_at_Olympic_Games). He helmed Australia Challenge at the [1992 Louis Vuitton Cup](/wiki/1992_Louis_Vuitton_Cup).

Born in [Sydney](/wiki/Sydney), Beashel comes from a sailing family. His father Ken is a local sailing legend. His brother [Adam](/wiki/Adam_Beashel) was a sailor for [Team New Zealand](/wiki/Team_New_Zealand) in the [America's Cup](/wiki/America%27s_Cup) in [2003](/wiki/2003_America%27s_Cup), [2007](/wiki/2007_America%27s_Cup) and 2013. Adam's wife [Lanee Butler](/wiki/Lanee_Butler) sailed at four Olympics.

Beashel competed at the Olympics in the two-person keelboat, with [Richard Coxon](/wiki/Richard_Coxon) in 1984, [Gregory Torpy](/wiki/Gregory_Torpy) in 1988, and [David Giles](/wiki/David_Giles_(sailor)) from 1992 to 2004. He and Giles also won the World Championships in 1998 in the Star class. He now runs the family boat shop in [Elvina Bay](/wiki/Elvina_Bay), [Pittwater](/wiki/Pittwater).

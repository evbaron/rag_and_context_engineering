**Franco Sebastián Cristaldo** (born 15 August 1996) is an Argentine professional [footballer](/wiki/Association_football) who plays mainly as an [attacking midfielder](/wiki/Attacking_midfielder) for [Talleres](/wiki/Talleres_de_C%C3%B3rdoba).

## Club career

Born in [Morón, Buenos Aires](/wiki/Mor%C3%B3n,_Buenos_Aires), Cristaldo was a [Boca Juniors](/wiki/Boca_Juniors) youth graduate. On 16 November 2014, he made his first team debut, coming on as a second-half [substitute](/wiki/Substitute_(association_football)) for [Luciano Acosta](/wiki/Luciano_Acosta) in a 1–1 away draw against [Arsenal de Sarandí](/wiki/Arsenal_de_Sarand%C3%AD).

Cristaldo was definitely promoted to the main squad in January 2015 by manager [Rodolfo Arruabarrena](/wiki/Rodolfo_Arruabarrena). He scored his first professional goal on 29 March, netting the last in a 3–0 home win against [Estudiantes de la Plata](/wiki/Estudiantes_de_la_Plata).

On 11 January 2016, Cristaldo joined [Elche CF](/wiki/Elche_CF) in [Segunda División](/wiki/Segunda_Divisi%C3%B3n), on loan until the end of [the season](/wiki/2015%E2%80%9316_Segunda_Divisi%C3%B3n).

**Joël Thierry Retornaz** (born 30 September 1983 in [Chêne-Bougeries](/wiki/Ch%C3%AAne-Bougeries), [Genève](/wiki/Gen%C3%A8ve), [Switzerland](/wiki/Switzerland)) is an [Italian](/wiki/Italians) [curler](/wiki/Curling) from [Cembra](/wiki/Cembra). He was the skip of the Italian men's Olympic curling team in 2006, 2018, 2022, and 2026.

## Career

Retornaz gained sudden renown in [Italy](/wiki/Italy) during the [2006](/wiki/Curling_at_the_2006_Winter_Olympics) [Winter Olympics](/wiki/2006_Winter_Olympics). Although Italy has little curling tradition, and the sport was practiced only by a few hundred amateurs, Retornaz led the semi-professional Italian team to several unexpected victories over strong teams, including [Canada](/wiki/Canada). This breakthrough inspired a sudden national curiosity for [curling](/wiki/Curling), previously almost unknown in Italy.

Retornaz returned to the [Olympics in 2018](/wiki/Curling_at_the_2018_Winter_Olympics), skipping the Italians again while throwing third rocks. The team finished 9th with a 3-6 record. The team finished 3–6 again at the [2022 Olympics](/wiki/Curling_at_the_2022_Winter_Olympics), placing 9th again.

Retornaz has represented Italy in eleven [World Curling Championships](/wiki/World_Curling_Championships), in 2005, 2010, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025 skipping the team in each event except 2005. They have twice won bronze medals at the [2022](/wiki/2022_World_Men%27s_Curling_Championship) and [2024 World Men's Curling Championship](/wiki/2024_World_Men%27s_Curling_Championship), Italy's first medals at the curling worlds of either gender.

Retornaz has competed for Italy in 16 [European Curling Championships](/wiki/European_Curling_Championships) (as of 2024), finally winning a bronze medal in his 11th try in 2018, [skipping](/wiki/Skip_(curling)) the rink of [Amos Mosaner](/wiki/Amos_Mosaner), [Sebastiano Arman](/wiki/Sebastiano_Arman), [Simone Gonin](/wiki/Simone_Gonin) and [Fabio Ribotta](/wiki/Fabio_Ribotta). Retornaz won a second bronze in 2021 and a third in 2022.

## Personal life

In addition to curling, Retornaz breeds and races [quarter horses](/wiki/American_Quarter_Horse). He is employed as a businessman. He is currently married and lives in [Lugano](/wiki/Lugano), [Switzerland](/wiki/Switzerland). Retornaz speaks five languages, his first language is Italian, which was spoken to him by his mother, his father is from Switzerland and from whom he learned French when he was raised in [Geneva](/wiki/Geneva), he also speaks English, German and Chinese, which he learned in school.

## Grand Slam record

Retornaz won Italy's first Grand Slam championship at the [2022 Masters](/wiki/2022_Masters_(curling)).

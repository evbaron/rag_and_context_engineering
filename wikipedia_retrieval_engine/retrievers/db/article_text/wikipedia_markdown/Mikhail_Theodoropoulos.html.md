# Mikhail Theodoropoulos

## Infobox

- **Nationality:** Greek
- **Born:** 1933 (age 92–93) Stamata, Greece
- **Sport:** Wrestling

**Mikhail Theodoropoulos** (born 1933) is a Greek [wrestler](/wiki/Amateur_wrestling). He competed in the [men's Greco-Roman bantamweight](/wiki/Wrestling_at_the_1960_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_Greco-Roman_bantamweight) at the [1960 Summer Olympics](/wiki/1960_Summer_Olympics).

**Nick Hardt** (born 20 September 2000) is a [Dominican](/wiki/Dominican_Republic) professional [tennis](/wiki/Tennis) player.
He has a career high [ATP](/wiki/Association_of_Tennis_Professionals) singles ranking of world No. 180 achieved on 29 July 2024 and a doubles ranking of No. 282 achieved on 24 August 2023. He is currently the No. 1 Dominican player.

Hardt represents the [Dominican Republic](/wiki/Dominican_Republic_Davis_Cup_team) in the [Davis Cup](/wiki/Davis_Cup).

## Junior career

Hardt was ranked No. 16 in the world junior rankings by the [ITF](/wiki/International_Tennis_Federation) on 5 March 2018.

In 2016, he won the XXV Copa Merengue tournament in [Santo Domingo](/wiki/Santo_Domingo), [Dominican Republic](/wiki/Dominican_Republic), becoming the youngest Dominican to win the title at 15 years of age.

In 2017, he reached the quarterfinals of the [Eddie Herr Junior Championships](https://www.itftennis.com/en/tournament/eddie-herr-itf/usa/2017/j-g1-usa-03a-2017/draws-and-results/) ITF tournament where he lost to eventual champion, Bulgarian [Adrian Andreev](/wiki/Adrian_Andreev).

In 2018, he reached the quarterfinals of the [2018 French Open Junior Championships](/wiki/2018_French_Open_%E2%80%93_Boys%27_singles) and the third round in the [2018 U.S. Open Junior Championships](/wiki/2018_US_Open_%E2%80%93_Boys%27_singles). Hardt also reached the finals of the 2018 [Porto Alegre Junior Championships](/wiki/Porto_Alegre_Junior_Championships) losing to the No. 1 ranked junior in the world [Sebastián Báez](/wiki/Sebasti%C3%A1n_B%C3%A1ez) in the finals.

## Professional career

### 2017: Davis Cup debut

Hardt was first nominated to the team for the [2017 Davis Cup](/wiki/2017_Davis_Cup) and faced [Chilean](/wiki/Chile_Davis_Cup_team) tennis player [Nicolás Jarry](/wiki/Nicol%C3%A1s_Jarry) in his first match.

### 2022: Maiden Challenger final, top 205 debut

He reached his maiden Challenger final at the [2022 JC Ferrero Challenger Open](/wiki/2022_JC_Ferrero_Challenger_Open). As a result he moved more than 35 positions up to a new career-high in the top 250 on 10 October 2022. Two months later, he reached a career-high of No. 201 on 22 December 2022.

### 2024: Maiden Challenger title, ATP debut and first win, top 200

Ranked No. 301, he won his maiden Challenger title as an alternate at the [Sánchez-Casal Mapfre Cup](/wiki/S%C3%A1nchez-Casal_Mapfre_Cup) in Barcelona defeating wildcard and second seed [Bernabe Zapata Miralles](/wiki/Bernabe_Zapata_Miralles). He was only the second Challenger champion from the Dominican Republic. As a result he raised 70 positions up in the rankings to return to the top 230.

Ranked No. 229, he qualified for the ATP 500 tournament at the [Barcelona Open](/wiki/2024_Barcelona_Open_Banc_Sabadell_-_Singles) making his ATP debut, having entered the qualifying competition as an alternate. There, he earned his first ATP Tour win, defeating wildcard [Martin Landaluce](/wiki/Martin_Landaluce) in three sets, becoming the first [Dominican](/wiki/Dominican_Republic) player to win an ATP tour-level match since [Víctor Estrella Burgos](/wiki/V%C3%ADctor_Estrella_Burgos) in [2018 Quito](/wiki/2018_Ecuador_Open_Quito_%E2%80%93_Singles). As a result of his win, he moved 25 positions up close to his career-high. He lost to 13th seed [Tomás Martín Etcheverry](/wiki/Tom%C3%A1s_Mart%C3%ADn_Etcheverry) in the second round.

On 20 May 2024, he made into the top 200 in the singles rankings after reaching the semifinals at the [2024 Internazionali di Tennis Francavilla al Mare](/wiki/2024_Internazionali_di_Tennis_Francavilla_al_Mare) Challenger in Italy.

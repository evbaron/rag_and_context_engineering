# Treaty of Resht

The **Treaty of Resht** was signed between the [Russian Empire](/wiki/Russian_Empire) and [Safavid Empire](/wiki/Safavid_Iran) in [Rasht](/wiki/Rasht) on 21 January 1732. According to this treaty Russia waived its claim to any territory south of the [Kura River](/wiki/Kura_(Caspian_Sea)). This included return of the provinces of [Gilan](/wiki/Gilan_Province), [Mazandaran](/wiki/Mazandaran_Province), and [Astarabad](/wiki/Astarabad), [conquered](/wiki/Russo-Persian_War_(1722%E2%80%9323)) by [Peter I](/wiki/Peter_the_Great) in the early 1720s. The Iranian cities of [Derbent](/wiki/Derbent), [Tarki](/wiki/Tarki), [Ganja](/wiki/Ganja,_Azerbaijan), etc. north of the Kura river would be returned three years later. In return, the Iranians, now *de facto* ruled by the militarily successful [Nader](/wiki/Nader_Shah), granted trade privileges to Russian merchants and promised to restore the Georgian king [Vakhtang VI](/wiki/Vakhtang_VI), then residing in exile in Russia, on the throne of [Kartli](/wiki/Kingdom_of_Kartli_(1484%E2%80%931762)) as soon as the [Ottoman](/wiki/Ottoman_Empire) troops could be expelled from that country. The provisions were confirmed by the 1735 [Treaty of Ganja](/wiki/Treaty_of_Ganja), according which treaty all the regions north of the Kura river were returned as well.

**Salthill and Monkstown railway station** ([Irish](/wiki/Irish_language): *Stáisiún Chnoc an tSalainn agus Baile na Manach*) serves the areas of Salthill (on the coast) and [Monkstown](/wiki/Monkstown,_Dublin) (just inland) in [Dublin](/wiki/Dublin), Ireland.

It is on the [DART](/wiki/Dublin_Area_Rapid_Transit) line between the stations of [Seapoint](/wiki/Seapoint_railway_station) and [Dún Laoghaire](/wiki/D%C3%BAn_Laoghaire_railway_station). The station has a car park, information office, automated ticket and vending machines. The information office is open between 05:45–00:15, Monday to Sunday.

## History

The original Salthill station opened in May 1837. It was built by the [Dublin and Kingstown Railway](/wiki/Dublin_and_Kingstown_Railway). It closed in 1960 and was electrified and reopened in 1984 with the arrival of [DART](/wiki/Dublin_Area_Rapid_Transit) services.

## Services

The station is not staffed, it features no toilets and there is no Wi-Fi. There is also no enclosed waiting room, but there are multiple passenger shelters on both sides of the track. There are 2 ticket machines within the station, and a night gate is opened during the evening. There used to be a vending machine within the building however it has since been removed.

With the exception of the DART, there is no direct public transport to or from the station. The nearest bus stops are in [Monkstown](/wiki/Monkstown,_Dublin) village, located less than 300 m from the station, which are served by the following:

[Dublin Bus](/wiki/Dublin_Bus) Routes:

- 7 / 7A from [Mountjoy Square](/wiki/Mountjoy_Square) to [Bride's Glen](/wiki/Cherrywood,_Dublin) / [Loughlinstown](/wiki/Loughlinstown). Route 7 provides a connection to the [Luas Green Line](/wiki/Green_Line_(Luas)) terminus at [Bride's Glen](/wiki/Brides_Glen_Luas_stop)
- 7N Nitelink from [Dublin](/wiki/Dublin) city centre to [Shankill](/wiki/Shankill,_Dublin), via [Blackrock](/wiki/Blackrock,_Dublin) (Fri & Sat only)

[Go-Ahead Ireland](/wiki/Go-Ahead_Ireland) Routes:

- S8 from [Citywest](/wiki/Citywest) to [Dún Laoghaire](/wiki/D%C3%BAn_Laoghaire), via [The Square](/wiki/The_Square_Tallaght) and [Sandyford Business District](/wiki/Sandyford#Sandyford_Business_District)

Private Operators

- [Aircoach](/wiki/Aircoach) route 703 from [Killiney](/wiki/Killiney) to [Dublin Airport](/wiki/Dublin_Airport), via Monkstown

There is also a [taxi rank](/wiki/Taxi_rank) in Monkstown village and a large car park adjacent to the station.

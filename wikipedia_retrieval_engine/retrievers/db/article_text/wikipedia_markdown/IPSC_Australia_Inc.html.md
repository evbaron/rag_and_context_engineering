# IPSC Australia Inc

## Infobox

- **Regional Director:** Gareth Graham [ 1 ]
- **Parent organization:** International Practical Shooting Confederation
- **Website:** ipsc.org.au

**IPSC Australia Inc** is the [Australian](/wiki/Australia) association for [practical shooting](/wiki/Practical_shooting) under the [International Practical Shooting Confederation](/wiki/International_Practical_Shooting_Confederation). It consists of seven sections: [Victoria](/wiki/Victoria_(Australia)) and [Tasmania](/wiki/Tasmania), [South Australia](/wiki/South_Australia), [New South Wales](/wiki/New_South_Wales), [Australian Capital Territory](/wiki/Australian_Capital_Territory), [Queensland](/wiki/Queensland), [Northern Territory](/wiki/Northern_Territory) and [Western Australia](/wiki/Western_Australia).

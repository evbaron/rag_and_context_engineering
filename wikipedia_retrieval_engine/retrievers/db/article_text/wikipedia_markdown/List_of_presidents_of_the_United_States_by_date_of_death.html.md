# List of presidents of the United States by date of death

The following is a list of **presidents of the United States by date of death**, plus additional lists of presidential death related statistics. Of the 45 people who have served as [President of the United States](/wiki/President_of_the_United_States) since the office came into existence in 1789, 40 have died – eight of them while [in office](/wiki/Incumbent).

The [oldest president](/wiki/List_of_presidents_of_the_United_States_by_age) at the time of death was [Jimmy Carter](/wiki/Jimmy_Carter), who died at 100 years, 89 days. [John F. Kennedy](/wiki/John_F._Kennedy), [assassinated](/wiki/Assassination_of_John_F._Kennedy) at the age of 46 years, 177 days, was the youngest to have died in office; the youngest to have [died by natural causes](/wiki/Death_by_natural_causes) was [James K. Polk](/wiki/James_K._Polk), who died of [cholera](/wiki/Cholera) at the age of 53 years, 225 days.

## Died same day, date, year, age

### Same day

- July 4, 1826: [Thomas Jefferson](/wiki/Thomas_Jefferson) at 12:50 p.m., and [John Adams](/wiki/John_Adams) at 6:20 p.m.

### Same date

- March 8: [Millard Fillmore](/wiki/Millard_Fillmore) in 1874 and [William Howard Taft](/wiki/William_Howard_Taft) in 1930
- July 4: [John Adams](/wiki/John_Adams) and [Thomas Jefferson](/wiki/Thomas_Jefferson) in 1826, and [James Monroe](/wiki/James_Monroe) in 1831
- December 26: [Harry S. Truman](/wiki/Harry_S._Truman) in 1972 and [Gerald Ford](/wiki/Gerald_Ford) in 2006

### Same calendar year

- 1826: [Thomas Jefferson](/wiki/Thomas_Jefferson) and [John Adams](/wiki/John_Adams), both on July 4
- 1862: [John Tyler](/wiki/John_Tyler) and [Martin Van Buren](/wiki/Martin_Van_Buren), on January 18 and July 24, respectively
- 1901: [Benjamin Harrison](/wiki/Benjamin_Harrison) and [William McKinley](/wiki/William_McKinley), on March 13 and September 14, respectively

### Same age (rounded down to nearest year)

- 93: [Gerald Ford](/wiki/Gerald_Ford) and [Ronald Reagan](/wiki/Ronald_Reagan)
- 90: [John Adams](/wiki/John_Adams) and [Herbert Hoover](/wiki/Herbert_Hoover)
- 78: [Andrew Jackson](/wiki/Andrew_Jackson) and [Dwight D. Eisenhower](/wiki/Dwight_D._Eisenhower)
- 71: [John Tyler](/wiki/John_Tyler) and [Grover Cleveland](/wiki/Grover_Cleveland)
- 67: [George Washington](/wiki/George_Washington), [Benjamin Harrison](/wiki/Benjamin_Harrison), and [Woodrow Wilson](/wiki/Woodrow_Wilson)
- 64: [Franklin Pierce](/wiki/Franklin_Pierce) and [Lyndon B. Johnson](/wiki/Lyndon_B._Johnson)
- 63: [Ulysses S. Grant](/wiki/Ulysses_S._Grant) and [Franklin D. Roosevelt](/wiki/Franklin_D._Roosevelt)
- 60: [Theodore Roosevelt](/wiki/Theodore_Roosevelt) and [Calvin Coolidge](/wiki/Calvin_Coolidge)
- 57: [Chester A. Arthur](/wiki/Chester_A._Arthur) and [Warren G. Harding](/wiki/Warren_G._Harding)

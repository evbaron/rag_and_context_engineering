**August 16** is the 228th day of the year (229th in [leap years](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leap_year)) in the [Gregorian calendar](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorian_calendar); 137 days remain until the end of the year.

## Events

### Pre-1600

- [1 BC](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1_BC) – [Wang Mang](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Mang) consolidates his power in China and is declared marshal of state. [Emperor Ai of Han](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_Ai_of_Han), who died the previous day, had no heirs.
- [942](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/942) – Start of the four-day [Battle of al-Mada'in](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_al-Mada%27in), between the [Hamdanids](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamdanids) of [Mosul](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosul) and the Baridis of [Basra](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basra) over control of the [Abbasid](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbasid) capital, [Baghdad](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baghdad).
- [963](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/963) – [Nikephoros II Phokas](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikephoros_II_Phokas) is crowned emperor of the [Byzantine Empire](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byzantine_Empire).
- [1328](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1328) – The [House of Gonzaga](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Gonzaga) seizes power in the [Duchy of Mantua](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duchy_of_Mantua), and will rule until 1708.
- [1513](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1513) – [Battle of the Spurs](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Spurs) (Battle of Guinegate): King [Henry VIII](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_VIII) of England and his [Imperial allies](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Roman_Empire) defeat French Forces who are then forced to retreat.
- [1550](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1550) – [Rabbi Moses Isserles](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabbi_Moses_Isserles) issues his ruling in the [Bragadin-Giustiniani dispute](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bragadin-Giustiniani_dispute), one of the earliest instances of a [copyright](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copyright) suit over any book.
- [1570](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1570) – The [Principality of Transylvania](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principality_of_Transylvania_(1570-1711)) is established after [John II Zápolya](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_II_Z%C3%A1polya) renounces his claim as [King of Hungary](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_Hungary) in the [Treaty of Speyer](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Speyer_(1570)).

### 1601–1900

- [1652](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1652) – [Battle of Plymouth](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Plymouth_(1652)): Inconclusive naval action between the fleets of [Michiel de Ruyter](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michiel_de_Ruyter) and [George Ayscue](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Ayscue) in the [First Anglo-Dutch War](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Anglo-Dutch_War).
- [1777](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1777) – [American Revolutionary War](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Revolutionary_War): The Americans led by General [John Stark](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Stark) rout British and [Brunswick](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duchy_of_Brunswick) troops under [Friedrich Baum](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Baum) at the [Battle of Bennington](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Bennington) in [Walloomsac, New York](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walloomsac,_New_York).
- [1780](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1780) – American Revolutionary War: [Battle of Camden](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Camden): The British defeat the Americans near [Camden, South Carolina](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camden,_South_Carolina).
- [1792](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1792) – [Maximilien Robespierre](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximilien_Robespierre) presents the petition of the [Commune of Paris](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Commune_(French_Revolution)) to the [Legislative Assembly](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_Assembly_(France)), which demanded the formation of a [revolutionary tribunal](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolutionary_Tribunal).
- [1793](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1793) – [French Revolution](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Revolution): A [levée en masse](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lev%C3%A9e_en_masse) is decreed by the [National Convention](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Convention).
- [1812](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1812) – [War of 1812](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_1812): American General [William Hull](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Hull) surrenders [Fort Detroit](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Shelby_(Michigan)) [without a fight](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Detroit) to the British Army.
- [1819](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1819) – [Peterloo Massacre](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peterloo_Massacre): Seventeen people die and over 600 are injured in [cavalry](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cavalry) charges at a public meeting at St. Peter's Field, [Manchester](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester), England.
- [1837](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1837) – Three African soldiers in the [1st West India Regiment](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1st_West_India_Regiment) – [Daaga](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daaga), Edward Coffin and Maurice Ogston – were executed for their role in the [St. Joseph Mutiny](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Joseph_Mutiny) in the [British colony of Trinidad](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colony_of_Trinidad).
- [1841](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1841) – [U.S. President](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_United_States) [John Tyler](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Tyler) vetoes a bill which called for the re-establishment of the [Second Bank of the United States](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Bank_of_the_United_States). Enraged [Whig Party](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whig_Party_(United_States)) members riot outside the [White House](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House) in the most violent demonstration on White House grounds in U.S. history.
- [1844](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1844) – Governor-general of the Philippines [Narciso Claveria](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narciso_Claver%C3%ADa_y_Zald%C3%BAa), signs a decree to reform the country's calendar by skipping Tuesday, [December 31](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/December_31), as a solution to anomalies that had existed since [Ferdinand Magellan](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_Magellan)'s arrival in [1521](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1521).
- [1858](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1858) – U.S. President [James Buchanan](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Buchanan) inaugurates the new [transatlantic telegraph cable](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transatlantic_telegraph_cable) by exchanging greetings with [Queen Victoria](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Victoria) of the United Kingdom. However, a weak signal forces a shutdown of the service in a few weeks.
- [1859](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1859) – The [Grand Duchy of Tuscany](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Duchy_of_Tuscany) formally deposes the exiled [House of Lorraine](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Lorraine).
- [1863](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1863) – The [Dominican Restoration War](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominican_Restoration_War) begins when [Gregorio Luperón](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorio_Luper%C3%B3n) raises the [Dominican flag](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_the_Dominican_Republic) in [Santo Domingo](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santo_Domingo) after Spain had recolonized the country.
- [1869](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1869) – [Battle of Acosta Ñu](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Acosta_%C3%91u): A [Paraguayan](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paraguay) battalion largely made up of children is massacred by the [Brazilian Army](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Army) during the [Paraguayan War](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paraguayan_War).
- [1870](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1870) – [Franco-Prussian War](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franco-Prussian_War): The [Battle of Mars-la-Tour](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Mars-la-Tour) is fought, resulting in a [Prussian](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Prussia) victory.
- [1876](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1876) – [Richard Wagner](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Wagner)'s *[Siegfried](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siegfried_(opera))*, the penultimate opera in his *[Ring](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Der_Ring_des_Nibelungen)* cycle, is premiered at the [Bayreuth Festspielhaus](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayreuth_Festspielhaus).
- [1891](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1891) – The [Basilica of San Sebastian, Manila](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basilica_of_San_Sebastian,_Manila), the first all-steel church in Asia, is officially inaugurated and blessed.
- [1896](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1896) – [Skookum Jim Mason](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keish), [George Carmack](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Carmack) and [Dawson Charlie](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dawson_Charlie) discover gold in a tributary of the [Klondike River](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klondike_River) in Canada, setting off the [Klondike Gold Rush](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klondike_Gold_Rush).
- [1900](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1900) – The [Battle of Elands River](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Elands_River_(1900)) during the Second Boer War ends after a 13-day siege is lifted by the British. The battle had begun when a force of between 2,000 and 3,000 Boers had surrounded a force of 500 Australians, Rhodesians, Canadians and British soldiers at a supply dump at Brakfontein Drift.

### 1901–present

- [1906](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1906) – The 8.2 [Mw](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seismic_magnitude_scales#Mw) [Valparaíso earthquake](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1906_Valpara%C3%ADso_earthquake) hits central Chile, killing 3,882 people.
- [1913](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1913) – Tōhoku Imperial University of Japan (modern day [Tohoku University](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tohoku_University)) becomes the first university in Japan to admit female students.
- 1913 – Completion of the [Royal Navy](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Navy) [battlecruiser](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battlecruiser) [HMS *Queen Mary*](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Queen_Mary).
- [1916](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1916) – The [Migratory Bird Treaty](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Migratory_Bird_Treaty) between Canada and the United States is signed.
- [1918](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1918) – The [Battle of Lake Baikal](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Lake_Baikal) was fought between the [Czechoslovak Legion](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovak_Legion) and the [Red Army](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Army).
- [1920](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1920) – [Ray Chapman](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Chapman) of the [Cleveland Indians](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleveland_Indians) is hit on the head by a [fastball](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fastball) thrown by [Carl Mays](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Mays) of the [New York Yankees](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Yankees). Next day, Chapman will become the second player to die from injuries sustained in a [Major League Baseball](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Major_League_Baseball) game.
- 1920 – The congress of the [Communist Party of Bukhara](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_Bukhara) opens. The congress would call for armed revolution.
- 1920 – [Polish–Soviet War](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish%E2%80%93Soviet_War): The [Battle of Radzymin](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Radzymin_(1920)) concludes; the Soviet [Red Army](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Army) is forced to turn away from [Warsaw](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw).
- [1923](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1923) – The United Kingdom gives the name "[Ross Dependency](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ross_Dependency)" to part of its claimed Antarctic territory and makes the [Governor-General](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor-General_of_New_Zealand) of the [Dominion of New Zealand](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_of_New_Zealand) its administrator.
- [1927](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1927) – The [Dole Air Race](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dole_Air_Race) begins from [Oakland, California](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland,_California), to [Honolulu, Hawaii](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honolulu), during which six out of the eight participating planes crash or disappear.
- [1929](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1929) – The [1929 Palestine riots](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1929_Palestine_riots) break out in [Mandatory Palestine](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandatory_Palestine) between [Palestinian Arabs](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinians) and Jews and continue until the end of the month. In total, 133 Jews and 116 Arabs are killed.
- [1930](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1930) – The first color sound [cartoon](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartoon), *[Fiddlesticks](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiddlesticks_(1930_film))*, is released by [Ub Iwerks](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ub_Iwerks).
- 1930 – The first [British Empire Games](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_Games) are opened in [Hamilton, Ontario](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton,_Ontario), by the [Governor General of Canada](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_General_of_Canada), the [Viscount Willingdon](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freeman_Freeman-Thomas,_1st_Marquess_of_Willingdon).
- [1933](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1933) – [Christie Pits riot](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christie_Pits_riot) takes place in [Toronto](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto), [Ontario](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontario).
- [1942](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1942) – [World War II](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II): [US Navy](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Navy) [L-class blimp](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L-class_blimp) [L-8](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L-8) drifts in from the Pacific and eventually crashes in [Daly City, California](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daly_City,_California). The two-man crew cannot be found.
- [1943](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1943) – World War II: 317 Greek civilians are [murdered](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massacre_of_Kommeno) by soldiers of the German [1st Mountain Division](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1st_Mountain_Division_(Wehrmacht)) in the village of [Kommeno](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kommeno), Greece.
- [1944](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1944) – First flight of a jet with [forward-swept wings](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forward-swept_wing), the [Junkers Ju 287](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junkers_Ju_287).
- [1945](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1945) – The National Representatives' Congress, the precursor of the current [National Assembly of Vietnam](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assembly_of_Vietnam), convenes in [Sơn Dương](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C6%A1n_D%C6%B0%C6%A1ng).
- [1946](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1946) – [Mass riots](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_Action_Day) in [Kolkata](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolkata) begin; more than 4,000 people would be killed in 72 hours.
- 1946 – The [All Hyderabad Trade Union Congress](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Hyderabad_Trade_Union_Congress) is founded in [Secunderabad](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secunderabad).
- [1954](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1954) – The first issue of *[Sports Illustrated](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_Illustrated)* is published.
- [1960](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1960) – [Cyprus](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyprus) gains its independence from the United Kingdom.
- 1960 – [Joseph Kittinger](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Kittinger) parachutes from a balloon over [New Mexico](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Mexico), United States, at 102,800 feet (31,300 m), setting three records that held until 2012: High-altitude jump, [free fall](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_fall), and highest speed by a human without an aircraft.
- [1964](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964) – [Vietnam War](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_War): A *[coup d'état](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coup_d%27%C3%A9tat)* replaces [Dương Văn Minh](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C6%B0%C6%A1ng_V%C4%83n_Minh) with General [Nguyễn Khánh](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_Kh%C3%A1nh) as President of [South Vietnam](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Vietnam). A new [constitution](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution) is established with aid from the [U.S. Embassy](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_diplomatic_missions_of_the_United_States).
- [1966](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1966) – Vietnam War: The [House Un-American Activities Committee](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_Un-American_Activities_Committee) begins investigations of Americans who have aided the [Viet Cong](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viet_Cong). The committee intends to introduce legislation making these activities illegal. Anti-war demonstrators disrupt the meeting and 50 people are arrested.
- [1972](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972) – In an unsuccessful [coup d'état](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coup_d%27%C3%A9tat) [attempt](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Moroccan_coup_attempt), the [Royal Moroccan Air Force](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Moroccan_Air_Force) fires upon [Hassan II of Morocco](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassan_II_of_Morocco)'s plane while he is traveling back to [Rabat](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabat).
- [1975](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1975) – Australian Prime Minister [Gough Whitlam](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gough_Whitlam) symbolically hands over land to the [Gurindji people](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gurindji_people) after the eight-year [Wave Hill walk-off](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave_Hill_walk-off), a landmark event in the history of [Indigenous land rights in Australia](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_land_rights_in_Australia), commemorated in a 1991 song by [Paul Kelly](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Kelly_(Australian_musician)) and an annual celebration.
- [1986](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986) – A [Sudan Airways](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudan_Airways) [Fokker F27 Friendship](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fokker_F27_Friendship) is [shot down](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_Sudan_Airways_Fokker_F-27_shootdown) near [Malakal](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malakal) in present day South Sudan, killing 60.
- [1987](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987) – [Northwest Airlines Flight 255](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwest_Airlines_Flight_255), a [McDonnell Douglas MD-82](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McDonnell_Douglas_MD-80#MD-82), crashes after takeoff in [Detroit](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit), [Michigan](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan), killing 154 of the 155 on board, plus two people on the ground.
- [1989](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989) – A [solar particle event](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_particle_event) affects computers at the [Toronto Stock Exchange](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto_Stock_Exchange), forcing a halt to trading.
- [1991](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991) – [Indian Airlines Flight 257](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Airlines_Flight_257), a [Boeing 737-200](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_737), crashes during approach to [Imphal Airport](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imphal_Airport), killing all 69 people on board.
- [2005](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005) – [West Caribbean Airways Flight 708](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Caribbean_Airways_Flight_708), a [McDonnell Douglas MD-82](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McDonnell_Douglas_MD-80#MD-82), crashes in [Machiques](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machiques), Venezuela, killing all 160 people on board.
- [2008](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008) – The [Trump International Hotel and Tower](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_International_Hotel_and_Tower_(Chicago)) in Chicago is topped off at 1,389 feet (423 m), at the time becoming the world's highest [residence](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwelling) above ground-level.
- [2010](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010) – [AIRES Flight 8250](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AIRES_Flight_8250) crashes at [Gustavo Rojas Pinilla International Airport](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustavo_Rojas_Pinilla_International_Airport) in [San Andrés, San Andrés y Providencia](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Andr%C3%A9s,_San_Andr%C3%A9s_y_Providencia), Colombia, killing two people.
- [2012](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012) – [South African police](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_Police_Service) fatally shoot 34 miners and wound 78 more during an [industrial dispute](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marikana_miners%27_strike) at Marikana near [Rustenburg](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rustenburg).
- [2013](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013) – The ferry [*St. Thomas Aquinas*](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MV_St._Thomas_Aquinas) collides with a cargo ship and sinks at [Cebu](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebu), Philippines, killing 61 people with 59 others missing.
- [2015](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015) – More than 96 people are killed and hundreds injured following a [series of air-raids](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Douma_market_massacre) by the [Syrian Arab Air Force](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_Arab_Air_Force) on the [rebel](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syrian_opposition_to_Bashar_al-Assad)-held market town of [Douma](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douma,_Syria).
- 2015 – [Trigana Air Flight 267](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trigana_Air_Flight_267), an [ATR 42](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATR_42), crashes in [Oksibil](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oksibil), [Bintang Mountains Regency](//en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bintang_Mountains_Regency), killing all 54 people on board.

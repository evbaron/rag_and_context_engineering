# Stephan von Breuning (entomologist)

## Infobox

- **Born:** 21 November 1894 Vienna, Austria-Hungary
- **Died:** 11 March 1983 (1983-03-11) (aged 88) Paris, France
- **Known for:** Études sur les Lamiaires
- **Fields:** Entomology

Not to be confused with [Stephan von Breuning (librettist)](/wiki/Stephan_von_Breuning_(librettist)).

**Stephan von Breuning** (21 November 1894 – 11 March 1983) was an Austrian [entomologist](/wiki/Entomologist) who specialised in the study of beetles ([coleopterology](/wiki/Coleopterology)), particularly within the *Carabus*, *Calosoma* and *Ceroglossus* (all of them are genera of the [Carabidae](/wiki/Carabidae) family, also known as [ground beetles](/wiki/Ground_beetles)) and the longhorn family ([Cerambycidae](/wiki/Cerambycidae)).

## Career

As an amateur working on the rich collections of the [Muséum national d'Histoire naturelle](/wiki/Mus%C3%A9um_national_d%27Histoire_naturelle) in [Paris](/wiki/Paris), France, von Breuning described 7,894 [taxa](/wiki/Taxon) of Cerambycidae.

## Works

The complete list of von Breuning's entomological works is published in the *[Bulletin de la Société Sciences Nat](/wiki/Bulletin_de_la_Soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9_Sciences_Nat)*, number 41. Among his most famous works there are the *Monographie Der Gattung Carabus*, published between 1932 and 1934 by Troppau, Schlesien, Cechoslovak Republik, and *Études sur les Lamiaires*, published in *[Novitates Entomologicae](/wiki/Novitates_Entomologicae)*, 1934–1946.

## Personal life

Von Breuning and his wife lived in a small studio at the top of an old building on rue Durantin, Paris [18ème](/wiki/18th_arrondissement_of_Paris).

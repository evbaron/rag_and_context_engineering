# 1980 NFL draft

The **1980 NFL draft** was the procedure by which [National Football League](/wiki/National_Football_League) teams selected amateur [college football](/wiki/College_football) players. It is officially known as the NFL Annual Player Selection Meeting. The [draft](/wiki/NFL_draft) was held April 29–30, [1980](/wiki/1980_NFL_season), at the [New York Sheraton Hotel](/wiki/Park_Central_Hotel) in [New York City](/wiki/New_York_City), New York. The league also held a [supplemental draft](/wiki/NFL_draft#Supplemental_draft) after the regular draft and before the regular season. With the [first overall pick](/wiki/List_of_first_overall_National_Football_League_Draft_picks) of the draft, the [Detroit Lions](/wiki/Detroit_Lions) selected running back [Billy Sims](/wiki/Billy_Sims).

This draft is notable as the first that the nascent [ESPN](/wiki/ESPN) network (which had first gone on the air seven months earlier) aired in its entirety, and the first to be televised.

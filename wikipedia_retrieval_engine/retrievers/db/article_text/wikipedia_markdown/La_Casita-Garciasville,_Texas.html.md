**La Casita-Garciasville** is a former [census-designated place](/wiki/Census-designated_place) (CDP) in [Starr County](/wiki/Starr_County,_Texas), [Texas](/wiki/Texas), United States. The population was 2,177 at the [2000 census](/wiki/2000_United_States_census).

## Geography

La Casita-Garciasville is located at [26°19′34″N 98°42′25″W / 26.32611°N 98.70694°W / 26.32611; -98.70694](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=La_Casita-Garciasville,_Texas&params=26_19_34_N_98_42_25_W_type:city) (26.326127, -98.707054).

According to the [United States Census Bureau](/wiki/United_States_Census_Bureau), the CDP has a total area of 4.4 square miles (11.3 km2), of which 4.3 square miles (11.2 km2) is land and 0.04 square mile (0.1 km2) (0.68%) is water.

La Casita-Garciasville first appeared as a [census designated place](/wiki/Census_designated_place) in the [1990 U.S. census](/wiki/1990_U.S._census). Prior to the 2010 census, the CDP was deleted and parts were taken to form [Amada Acres](/wiki/Amada_Acres,_Texas), [Chaparrito](/wiki/Chaparrito,_Texas), [La Casita](/wiki/La_Casita,_Texas), [Loma Linda East](/wiki/Loma_Linda_East,_Starr_County,_Texas), [Manuel Garcia](/wiki/Manuel_Garcia,_Texas), [Manuel Garcia II](/wiki/Manuel_Garcia_II,_Texas), [Olivia Lopez de Gutierrez](/wiki/Olivia_Lopez_de_Gutierrez,_Texas), [Ramirez-Perez](/wiki/Ramirez-Perez,_Texas) and [Victoria Vera](/wiki/Victoria_Vera,_Texas) CDPs.

As of the [census](/wiki/Census) of 2000, there were 2,177 people, 562 households, and 501 families residing in the CDP. The population density was 501.0 inhabitants per square mile (193.4/km2). There were 668 housing units at an average density of 153.7 per square mile (59.3/km2). The racial makeup of the CDP was 87.74% [White](/wiki/White_(U.S._Census)), 0.05% [Native American](/wiki/Native_American_(U.S._Census)), 10.52% from [other races](/wiki/Race_(United_States_Census)), and 1.70% from two or more races. [Hispanic](/wiki/Hispanic_(U.S._Census)) or [Latino](/wiki/Latino_(U.S._Census)) of any race were 99.13% of the population.

There were 562 households, out of which 56.0% had children under the age of 18 living with them, 69.0% were [married couples](/wiki/Marriage) living together, 15.8% had a female householder with no husband present, and 10.7% were non-families. 10.5% of all households were made up of individuals, and 4.8% had someone living alone who was 65 years of age or older. The average household size was 3.87 and the average family size was 4.19.

In the CDP, the population was spread out, with 39.0% under the age of 18, 11.1% from 18 to 24, 27.4% from 25 to 44, 15.0% from 45 to 64, and 7.5% who were 65 years of age or older. The median age was 25 years. For every 100 females, there were 104.4 males. For every 100 females age 18 and over, there were 94.7 males.

The median income for a household in the CDP was $15,921, and the median income for a family was $17,149. Males had a median income of $14,948 versus $11,442 for females. The [per capita income](/wiki/Per_capita_income) for the CDP was $5,507. About 44.9% of families and 53.0% of the population were below the [poverty line](/wiki/Poverty_line), including 64.2% of those under age 18 and 32.7% of those age 65 or over.

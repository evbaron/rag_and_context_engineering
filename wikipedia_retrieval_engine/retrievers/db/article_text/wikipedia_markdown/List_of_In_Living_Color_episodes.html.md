## Episodes

This list is considered far from complete. Some sketches may have been omitted due to lack of evidence. Sources of the sketch titles shown below come from the DVD collection and the televised version shown on [Fox](/wiki/Fox_Broadcasting_Company), [FX](/wiki/FX_(TV_channel)), [FXX](/wiki/FXX), [BET](/wiki/BET), [Centric](/wiki/BET_Her), [Aspire](/wiki/Aspire_(TV_network)), and [Fusion TV](/wiki/Fusion_TV).

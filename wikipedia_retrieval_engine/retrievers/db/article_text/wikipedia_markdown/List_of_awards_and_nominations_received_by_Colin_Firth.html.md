The following article is a **List of awards and nominations received by Colin Firth**.

**[Colin Firth](/wiki/Colin_Firth)** is an English actor known for his roles in film, television, and theatre. Over his career has received an [Academy Award](/wiki/Academy_Award), two [BAFTA Awards](/wiki/BAFTA_Awards), a [Golden Globe Award](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award) and three [Screen Actors Guild Awards](/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Awards) as well as nominations for two [Primetime Emmy Awards](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Awards).

Firth received the [Academy Award for Best Actor](/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actor) for his portrayal as [King George VI](/wiki/King_George_VI) in [Tom Hooper](/wiki/Tom_Hooper)'s historical drama *[The King's Speech](/wiki/The_King%27s_Speech)* (2010). The performance also earned him the [BAFTA Award for Best Actor in a Leading Role](/wiki/BAFTA_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Leading_Role), the [Golden Globe Award for Best Actor in a Motion Picture – Drama](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Motion_Picture_%E2%80%93_Drama), and the [Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Actor in a Leading Role](/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Male_Actor_in_a_Leading_Role). He was previously Oscar-nominated for playing a [gay](/wiki/Gay) man who lost a long-time partner in the romantic drama *[A Single Man](/wiki/A_Single_Man)* (2009), which also earned him the [BAFTA Award](/wiki/BAFTA_Award_for_Best_Actor) and the [Volpi Cup for Best Actor](/wiki/Volpi_Cup_for_Best_Actor).

He also received three [British Academy Film Award](/wiki/British_Academy_Film_Award) nominations for his performances in the romantic comedy *[Bridget Jones's Diary](/wiki/Bridget_Jones%27s_Diary)* (2000), the romantic drama *[A Single Man](/wiki/A_Single_Man)* (2009), and the historical drama *The King's Speech* (2010), winning for the later two. He received [Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Cast in a Motion Picture](/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Cast_in_a_Motion_Picture) nominations for the romantic epic drama *[The English Patient](/wiki/The_English_Patient_(film))* (1996), and the romantic period comedy-drama *[Shakespeare in Love](/wiki/Shakespeare_in_Love)* (1998), before winning for the historical drama *The King's Speech* (2010).

For his work on television, he received two [British Academy Television Award](/wiki/British_Academy_Television_Award) nominations for his roles in the [BBC](/wiki/BBC) projects *[Tumbledown](/wiki/Tumbledown)* (1989), and *[Pride and Prejudice](/wiki/Pride_and_Prejudice_(1995_TV_series))* (1996) as well as two [Primetime Emmy Award](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award) nominations for [Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Limited Series or Movie](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Supporting_Actor_in_a_Limited_or_Anthology_Series_or_Movie) for his role as Dr. [Wilhelm Stuckart](/wiki/Wilhelm_Stuckart) in the [BBC](/wiki/BBC) / [HBO](/wiki/HBO) film *[Conspiracy](/wiki/Conspiracy_(2001_film))* (2001), and [Outstanding Lead Actor in a Limited Series or Movie](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Lead_Actor_in_a_Limited_or_Anthology_Series_or_Movie) for playing [Michael Peterson](/wiki/Michael_Peterson_trial) the [HBO](/wiki/HBO) limited series *[The Staircase](/wiki/The_Staircase_(American_miniseries))* (2022). For the later he also received a [Golden Globe Award](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award) nomination.

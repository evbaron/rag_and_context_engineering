# Jack Purvis (actor)

**Jack Purvis** (13 July 1937 – 21 November 1997) was a British actor. Purvis had [dwarfism](/wiki/Dwarfism), and at 4 feet 2 inches (1.27 m) was mainly cast in roles requiring actors of short stature. He appeared in three of filmmaker [Terry Gilliam](/wiki/Terry_Gilliam)'s early fantasy films, with significant roles in *[Time Bandits](/wiki/Time_Bandits)* and *[The Adventures of Baron Munchausen](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Baron_Munchausen)* as well as a role in each film of the [original trilogy](/wiki/Star_Wars_original_trilogy) in filmmaker [George Lucas](/wiki/George_Lucas)'s *[Star Wars](/wiki/Star_Wars)*.

## Career

For twenty years until 1991, Purvis performed as part of a musical comedy double-act with [Kenny Baker](/wiki/Kenny_Baker_(English_actor)), billed as the "Mini-Tones" (Baker was also a dwarf). Purvis played the trumpet to Baker's mouth-organ and vibraphone.
The duo would attend auditions for *[Star Wars](/wiki/Star_Wars)* in 1976, where Baker was instantly cast as [R2-D2](/wiki/R2-D2). At that time the duo had reached the final on the talent show *[Opportunity Knocks](/wiki/Opportunity_Knocks_(UK_TV_series))*, and were reluctant to put the Mini-Tones work on hold. After some negotiation, Purvis was also hired to appear in the film, and would go on to play a different alien creature in each film of the original trilogy.

In 1991, Purvis became [quadriplegic](/wiki/Quadriplegic) after his neck was broken in a car repair accident. This and the 1990 death of [David Rappaport](/wiki/David_Rappaport) led to [Terry Gilliam](/wiki/Terry_Gilliam)'s indefinitely shelving an intended sequel to *Time Bandits*, where both actors played titular roles.

Purvis died in November 1997 at the age of 60.

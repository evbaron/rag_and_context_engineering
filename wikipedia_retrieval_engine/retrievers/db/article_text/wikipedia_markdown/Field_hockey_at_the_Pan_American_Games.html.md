# Field hockey at the Pan American Games

## Infobox

- **Sport:** Field hockey
- **Founded:** 1967; 59 years ago ( 1967 )
- **First season:** 1967
- **No. of teams:** 8
- **Confederation:** PAHF ( Americas )
- **Most recent champions:** M: Argentina (11th title) W: Argentina (8th title)
- **Most titles:** M: Argentina (11 titles) W: Argentina (8 titles)
- **Qualification:** Men's Pan American Cup Women's Pan American Cup CAC Games South American Games

The winner of the field hockey event of the [Pan American Games](/wiki/Pan_American_Games) qualifies for the [Summer Olympics](/wiki/Summer_Olympics) in the following year.

Women's field hockey was introduced at the [1987 edition](/wiki/1987_Pan_American_Games), and has served as the Olympics qualifying event since 1999.

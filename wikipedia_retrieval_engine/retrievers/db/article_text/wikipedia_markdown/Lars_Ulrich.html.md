**Lars Ulrich** ([/ˈʊlrɪk/](/wiki/Help:IPA/English) [ⓘ](/wiki/File:En-us-Ulrich.oga); Danish: [[ˈlɑːs ˈulˀʁek]](/wiki/Help:IPA/Danish); born 26 December 1963) is a Danish musician who is the drummer and a founding member of American [heavy metal](/wiki/Heavy_metal_music) band [Metallica](/wiki/Metallica). Along with [James Hetfield](/wiki/James_Hetfield), Ulrich has songwriting credits on almost all of the band's songs, and the two of them are the only remaining original members of the band.

The son and grandson respectively of [tennis](/wiki/Tennis) players [Torben](/wiki/Torben_Ulrich) and [Einer Ulrich](/wiki/Einer_Ulrich), he played tennis in his youth and moved to [Los Angeles](/wiki/Los_Angeles) at age 16 to train professionally. However, rather than playing tennis, Ulrich began playing drums. After publishing an advertisement in *[The Recycler](/wiki/The_Recycler)*, Ulrich met Hetfield and formed Metallica.

## Early life

Ulrich in a press photo with Metallica in 1983

Ulrich was born on 26 December 1963 into an upper-middle-class family in [Gentofte](/wiki/Gentofte_Municipality), Denmark; the son of Lone (*née* Sylvester-Hvid) and tennis player [Torben Ulrich](/wiki/Torben_Ulrich). The family lived at [Lundevangsvej 12](/wiki/Lundevangsvej_12) in [Hellerup](/wiki/Hellerup). His paternal grandfather was tennis player [Einer Ulrich](/wiki/Einer_Ulrich). His paternal grandmother, Ulla Meyer, was from a Jewish family; as a result, Ulrich's grandfather was persecuted by the [Nazis](/wiki/Nazis) during [World War II](/wiki/World_War_II). Saxophonist [Dexter Gordon](/wiki/Dexter_Gordon) was Ulrich's godfather, and he is a childhood friend of musician [Neneh Cherry](/wiki/Neneh_Cherry).

In February 1973, Ulrich's father obtained passes for five of his friends to a [Deep Purple](/wiki/Deep_Purple) concert held in the same [Copenhagen](/wiki/Copenhagen) stadium as one of his tennis tournaments. When one of the friends could not go, they gave their ticket to the nine-year-old Lars, who was mesmerized by the performance and bought the band's album *[Fireball](/wiki/Fireball_(album))* the next day. The concert and album had a considerable impact on Ulrich, inspiring the start of his music career.

As a result of his newfound interest in music, he received his first drum kit, a [Ludwig](/wiki/Ludwig-Musser), from his grandmother around the age of 12 or 13. Ulrich originally intended to follow in his father's footsteps and play tennis, and he moved to [Newport Beach, California](/wiki/Newport_Beach,_California), in the summer of 1980. Despite being ranked in the top ten tennis players of his age group in Denmark, Ulrich failed to make it into the seven man [Corona del Mar High School](/wiki/Corona_del_Mar_High_School) tennis team, contributing to his decision to focus on music.

In the documentary *[Anvil! The Story of Anvil](/wiki/Anvil!_The_Story_of_Anvil)*, Ulrich said that attending a [Y&T](/wiki/Y%26T) show was his defining moment in wanting to become a musician. In 1981, he discovered British heavy metal band [Diamond Head](/wiki/Diamond_Head_(English_band)). He was excited about the band's style of music after purchasing their album *[Lightning to the Nations](/wiki/Lightning_to_the_Nations)*, and traveled from [San Francisco](/wiki/San_Francisco) to [London](/wiki/London) to see the band perform at the [Woolwich](/wiki/Woolwich) Odeon. Ulrich remains a fan of Diamond Head and would later mix their album *[The Best of Diamond Head](/wiki/The_Best_of_Diamond_Head)*. Upon returning to the U.S., Ulrich placed an advert in a local newspaper looking for musicians to start a band with him. [James Hetfield](/wiki/James_Hetfield) responded, and Metallica was formed.

## Career

### Metallica

Ulrich performing with Metallica in 2009

Later in 1981, Ulrich met [James Hetfield](/wiki/James_Hetfield) in [Downey, California](/wiki/Downey,_California), and they formed the [heavy metal](/wiki/Heavy_metal_music) band [Metallica](/wiki/Metallica) upon Ulrich securing a spot in a compilation album of local metal bands named *Metal Massacre*. He got the band name from a friend, Ron Quintana, who was brainstorming names for a heavy metal fanzine he was creating, with Metallica and Metal Mania being among the options; Ulrich encouraged him to choose Metal Mania, and used the name Metallica for himself.

He became known as a pioneer of fast thrash [drum beats](/wiki/Drum_beat), featured on many of Metallica's early songs, such as "Metal Militia" from *[Kill 'Em All](/wiki/Kill_%27Em_All)*, "Fight Fire with Fire" from *[Ride the Lightning](/wiki/Ride_the_Lightning)*, "Battery" and "Damage Inc." from *[Master of Puppets](/wiki/Master_of_Puppets)* and "Dyers Eve" from *[...And Justice for All](/wiki/...And_Justice_for_All_(album))*. He has since been considerably influential due to both the popularity of his band, as well as his drum techniques, such as the [double bass drum](/wiki/Double_bass_drum) in the song "[One](/wiki/One_(Metallica_song))" (*[...And Justice for All](/wiki/...And_Justice_for_All_(album))*) and "Dyers Eve". Since the release of *[Metallica](/wiki/Metallica_(album))*, Ulrich adopted a less focused and simplified style of drumming, and reduced his kit from a 9-piece to a 7-piece.

Between 1998 and 2002, Ulrich tried running a record label, the Music Company, which was a joint venture with Metallica accountant Tim Duffy. It failed to catch on and folded in 2002. His voice can be heard at the beginning of "Leper Messiah" and he also counts to four in his native [Danish](/wiki/Danish_language) in the "[St. Anger](/wiki/St._Anger_(song))" music video.

### Napster lawsuit

Main article: [Metallica v. Napster, Inc.](/wiki/Metallica_v._Napster,_Inc.)

Ulrich performing with Metallica in 2012

In April 2000, Ulrich became a vocal opponent of [Napster](/wiki/Napster) and [file sharing](/wiki/File_sharing) as [Metallica](/wiki/Metallica) filed a lawsuit against the company for [copyright infringement](/wiki/Copyright_infringement) and [racketeering](/wiki/Racket_(crime)). In July 2000, he testified before the [Senate Judiciary Committee](/wiki/United_States_Senate_Committee_on_the_Judiciary) after Metallica's entire catalogue, including the then-unfinished track "[I Disappear](/wiki/I_Disappear)" was found to be freely available for download on the service. The case was settled out-of-court, resulting in more than 300,000 Napster users being banned from the service.

## Other activities

Ulrich at the 2013 [San Diego Comic-Con](/wiki/San_Diego_Comic-Con)

Ulrich has been an activist in support of expanding access to health care for U.S. citizens, with his family working with the Haight Ashbury Free Clinic of San Francisco, California. He raised $32,000 for the group during a celebrity edition of the game show *[Who Wants to Be a Millionaire?](/wiki/Who_Wants_to_Be_a_Millionaire%3F)*.

Ulrich made his acting debut in the [HBO](/wiki/HBO) original film *[Hemingway & Gellhorn](/wiki/Hemingway_%26_Gellhorn)*, which began filming in March 2011 and was released on 28 May 2012. He made a brief cameo appearance as himself in the film *[Get Him to the Greek](/wiki/Get_Him_to_the_Greek)*, as the partner of the character Jackie Q. In 2012, Ulrich was the focus of the documentary film *Mission to Lars*. The film by Kate and Will Spicer concerns their journey with their brother Tom, who lives in a care home in [Devon](/wiki/Devon), England and who has [Fragile X syndrome](/wiki/Fragile_X_syndrome), to try to meet Ulrich at one of Metallica's 2009 gigs in California.

Having led a campaign for several years to get his longtime favorite band, [Deep Purple](/wiki/Deep_Purple), inducted into the [Rock and Roll Hall of Fame](/wiki/Rock_and_Roll_Hall_of_Fame), Ulrich delivered the induction speech when the band went into the Hall in 2016. In 2017, Ulrich began hosting a show on [Apple Music](/wiki/Apple_Music) called *It's Electric*, which has featured conversations with [Joan Jett](/wiki/Joan_Jett), [Noel Gallagher](/wiki/Noel_Gallagher), [Dave Grohl](/wiki/Dave_Grohl) and [Jerry Cantrell](/wiki/Jerry_Cantrell).

## Artistry

### Playing style

Ulrich performing with Metallica in 2025

Ulrich's drumming style has changed throughout his career. During the 1980s, he was known for his fast, aggressive thrash beats. Unlike most drummers, Ulrich does not have a [ride cymbal](/wiki/Ride_cymbal) in his kit and instead favors the [China cymbal](/wiki/China_cymbal). He said that he does not like the "ding" sound of the former and prefers the loudness of the latter. Although, during some live performances on the [M72 World Tour](/wiki/M72_World_Tour) (particularly for "[Until It Sleeps](/wiki/Until_It_Sleeps)"), his drum tech places a ride cymbal next to his China cymbal, and removes it before the next song.

Ulrich consciously simplified his style in the 1990s to support the hard rock-oriented songwriting of Metallica's albums during that period. He restored some of his earlier thrash metal "signature" techniques for the band's 2008 album *[Death Magnetic](/wiki/Death_Magnetic)*, as well as their 2016 album *[Hardwired... to Self-Destruct](/wiki/Hardwired..._to_Self-Destruct)*, which both albums saw a more aggressive and experimental drumming style from Ulrich.

### Equipment

One of Ulrich's signature [Tama](/wiki/Tama_Drums) drum kits

Ulrich endorses [Tama](/wiki/Tama_Drums) drums and hardware. He plays a Starclassic Maple series drum kit, with 10" and 12" rack toms, two 16" floor toms and two 22" bass drums. From 2008 to 2016 he used the kit in orange, and the [WorldWired Tour](/wiki/WorldWired_Tour) featured the similar kit with a Deeper Purple finish. During the [M72 World Tour](/wiki/M72_World_Tour), the kit is bright yellow with black hardware. With Tama, Ulrich has designed his signature 14×6.5" steel [snare drum](/wiki/Snare_drum), model LU1465. Ulrich also endorses [Zildjian](/wiki/Avedis_Zildjian_Company) cymbals, [Remo](/wiki/Remo) drumheads, and Ahead drumsticks.

## Personal life

Ulrich in 2008

Ulrich's first marriage was in 1988 to Debbie Jones, a British woman he met on tour, but they divorced in 1990 during the recordings of the *Black* album. His second marriage was to Skylar Satenstein, an emergency medicine physician, from 1997 to 2004. They had two sons before divorcing. Following his divorce from Satenstein, Ulrich dated Danish actress [Connie Nielsen](/wiki/Connie_Nielsen) from 2004 to 2012. They had one child together. He married American fashion model [Jessica Miller](/wiki/Jessica_Miller_(model)) in 2015. Ulrich's sons Myles and Layne play in a band named Taipei Houston, which released its debut album in 2022.

Ulrich resides in [San Francisco](/wiki/San_Francisco), [California](/wiki/California), but only has [Danish citizenship](/wiki/Danish_nationality_law). Ulrich is a friend of [Noel Gallagher](/wiki/Noel_Gallagher) and his band [Oasis](/wiki/Oasis_(band)) and claimed Gallagher was his inspiration to give up [cocaine](/wiki/Cocaine) in the 2000s. He also has a passion for fine art. In 2002, he sold a painting from his own collection, *Self Portrait* by [Jean-Michel Basquiat](/wiki/Jean-Michel_Basquiat), 1982. With a pre-sale price estimate of $5 million, it ultimately sold for triple that at Christie's Auction House. In 2009, Ulrich revealed he suffered from [tinnitus](/wiki/Tinnitus) due to many years of touring without the use of any auditory protection. To unwind after some gigs, Ulrich listens to jazz.

## Awards and honors

Ulrich at the 2024 [Gershwin Prize](/wiki/Gershwin_Prize) ceremony

When Ulrich and other current and former Metallica members were [inducted](/wiki/List_of_Rock_and_Roll_Hall_of_Fame_inductees) into the [Rock and Roll Hall of Fame](/wiki/Rock_and_Roll_Hall_of_Fame) in 2009, Ulrich was the first [Dane](/wiki/Danish_people) to receive the honor.

Ulrich was knighted in his native country of Denmark. He was awarded the Knight's Cross of the [Order of the Dannebrog](/wiki/Order_of_the_Dannebrog) on 26 May 2017 by [Margrethe II](/wiki/Margrethe_II).

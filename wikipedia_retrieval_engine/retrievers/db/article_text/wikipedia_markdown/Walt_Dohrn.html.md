**Walter Dohrn** (born December 5, 1970) is an American writer, director, animator, musician, and voice actor. He performed the voice of [Rumpelstiltskin](/wiki/List_of_Shrek_characters#Rumpelstiltskin) in *[Shrek Forever After](/wiki/Shrek_Forever_After)* (2010), as well as various characters in *[Shrek the Third](/wiki/Shrek_the_Third)* (2007). He co-directed the film *[Trolls](/wiki/Trolls_(film))* (2016) and directed its sequels, and voiced various characters in the franchise. With [Conrad Vernon](/wiki/Conrad_Vernon), he is co-directing *[Shrek 5](/wiki/Shrek_5)*, set for release in 2027.

Dohrn has worked as writer, director, and storyboard director in [season 2](/wiki/SpongeBob_SquarePants_season_2) of *[SpongeBob SquarePants](/wiki/SpongeBob_SquarePants)*, and he also worked on 2 episodes from [season 3](/wiki/SpongeBob_SquarePants_season_3). In 2020, Dohrn signed a deal with [DreamWorks Animation](/wiki/DreamWorks_Animation).

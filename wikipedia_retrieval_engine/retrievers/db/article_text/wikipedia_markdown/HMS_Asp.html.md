# HMS Asp

This article is about ships of the Royal Navy. For the snake, see [Asp (reptile)](/wiki/Asp_(reptile)).

Five vessels of Britain's [Royal Navy](/wiki/Royal_Navy) have borne the name **HMS *Asp***, named after the [Asp](/wiki/Asp_(reptile)), which in antiquity referred to any one of several venomous snake species found in the Nile region.

- [HMS *Asp* (1797)](/wiki/HMS_Asp_(1797)) was an [*Acute*-class gunbrig](/wiki/Acute-class_gunbrig) (ex-*GB No.5*), disposed of in 1803.
- [HMS *Asp* (1808)](/wiki/French_brig_Serpent_(1807)) was the French Navy's corvette *Serpent*, under the command of *Lieutenant de vaisseau* Paul de Lamanon, when [HMS *Acasta*](/wiki/HMS_Acasta_(1797)) captured her in 1808 off [La Guaira](/wiki/La_Guaira), Venezuela. Rear-Admiral the Honourable Sir [Alexander Cochrane](/wiki/Alexander_Cochrane) provisionally named her *Pert*, but as there was already a brig [HMS *Pert*](/wiki/HMS_Pert_(1804)), the Admiralty named her HMS *Asp*. The Royal Navy commissioned her as 16-gun sloop and disposed of her in 1814. She then made four voyages as a whaler, and wrecked in December 1828 on the fifth voyage.
- HMS *Asp* (1826) was a cutter that the Royal Navy purchased in 1826 and sold in 1829.
- HMS *Asp* (1837) was a paddle steamer [packet](/wiki/Packet_ship) of 112 tons that the Admiralty acquired from the [Post Office](/wiki/Post_Office_Packet_Service) in 1837 and disposed of in 1881.
- HMS *Asp* (1890) was a tug in service 1891-1947

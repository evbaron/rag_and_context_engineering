# Daisaku Kimura

## Infobox

- **Born:** ( 1939-07-13 ) July 13, 1939 (age 86) Tokyo-Fu, Japan
- **Occupations:** Film director, cinematographer

**Daisaku Kimura** (木村 大作, *Kimura Daisaku*) is a Japanese film director and cinematographer.

## Overviews

Kimura received the certificate of the [Person of Cultural Merit](/wiki/Person_of_Cultural_Merit) from Hinako Yokota (November 4, 2020)

He won the award for best director at the 33rd [Japan Academy Prize](/wiki/Japan_Academy_Prize_(film)) for *[Mt. Tsurugidake](/wiki/Mt._Tsurugidake)*.

## Filmography

- *[Submersion of Japan](/wiki/Submersion_of_Japan)* (1973, cinematographer)
- *[Mt. Tsurugidake](/wiki/Mt._Tsurugidake)* (2009, director)
- *[Climbing to Spring](/wiki/Climbing_to_Spring)* (2014, director)
- *Samurai's Promise* (2018, director)
- *Hara o Kukutte* (2027, director)

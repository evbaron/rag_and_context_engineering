# List of Major League Baseball career hits leaders

Pete Rose is the all-time MLB hits leader with 4,256 hits.

Listed are all [Major League Baseball](/wiki/Major_League_Baseball) players who have reached the 2,000 [hit](/wiki/Hit_(baseball)) milestone during their career in MLB. [Pete Rose](/wiki/Pete_Rose) holds the Major League record for most career hits, with 4,256. Rose and [Ty Cobb](/wiki/Ty_Cobb), second most, are the only players with 4,000 or more career hits. [Cap Anson](/wiki/Cap_Anson) was the first player to achieve [2,000 hits](/wiki/List_of_Major_League_Baseball_progressive_career_hits_leaders), doing so in the [1889 season](/wiki/1880_Major_League_Baseball_season). [Nap Lajoie](/wiki/Nap_Lajoie) was the first player to accumulate 2,000 hits in the American League, doing so in the [1912 season](/wiki/1912_Major_League_Baseball_season).

For many years, [George Davis](/wiki/George_Davis_(baseball)) was considered the first [switch hitter](/wiki/Switch_hitter) to collect 2,000 hits, achieving that total during the [1902 season](/wiki/1902_Major_League_Baseball_season). However, recent research in the 2010s has revealed that 19th century hitter [Roger Connor](/wiki/Roger_Connor) also batted right-handed at times in his career; he reached 2,000 hits in the 1893 season.

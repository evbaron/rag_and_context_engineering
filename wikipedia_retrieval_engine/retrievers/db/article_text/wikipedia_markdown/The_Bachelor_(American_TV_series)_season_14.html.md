*The Bachelor: On the Wings of Love* is the **fourteenth season** of ABC reality television series *[The Bachelor](/wiki/The_Bachelor_(American_TV_series))*. The season premiere aired on January 4, 2010. The show features 31-year-old [Jake Pavelka](/wiki/Jake_Pavelka), a pilot from [Dallas](/wiki/Dallas), [Texas](/wiki/Texas), courting 25 women.

Pavelka finished in seventh place on [season 5](/wiki/The_Bachelorette_(American_TV_series)_season_5) of *[The Bachelorette](/wiki/The_Bachelorette_(American_TV_series))* featuring [Jillian Harris](/wiki/Jillian_Harris). It is the first season of *The Bachelor* to be broadcast in [high definition](/wiki/High-definition_television). The season concluded on March 1, 2010, with Pavelka choosing to propose to 23-year-old marketing rep Vienna Girardi. The couple ended their engagement in June 2010.

### Future appearances

#### *Dancing with the Stars*

Jake Pavelka competed in the [tenth season](/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_(American_season_10)) of *[Dancing with the Stars](/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_(American_TV_series))*. He partnered with [Chelsie Hightower](/wiki/Chelsie_Hightower) and finished in 7th place.

#### *The Bachelorette*

[Ali Fedotowsky](/wiki/Ali_Fedotowsky) was chosen as the bachelorette for the [sixth season](/wiki/The_Bachelorette_(season_6)) of *[The Bachelorette](/wiki/The_Bachelorette_(American_TV_series))*.

#### *Bachelor Pad*

Runner-up [Tenley Molzahn](/wiki/Tenley_Molzahn), along with contestants [Gia Allemand](/wiki/Gia_Allemand), Ashley Elmore, Elizabeth Kitt, Michelle Kujawa and Jessie Sulidis, returned for the inaugural season of *[Bachelor Pad](/wiki/Bachelor_Pad#Season_1_(2010))*. Michelle was eliminated in week 1, Jessie in week 2, Gia in week 3, and Ashley in week 5. Elizabeth and her partner, Jesse Kovacs, were eliminated at the beginning of week 6, finishing in 3rd place. Tenley and her partner, Kiptyn Locke, were eliminated at the end of week 6, finishing as the runners-up.

Jake, Gia, Vienna Girardi, and Ella Nolan, returned for the second season of *Bachelor Pad*. Gia quit in week 2, and Jake was eliminated in week 3. Ella and her partner, Kirk DeWindt, were eliminated at the beginning of week 6, finishing in 4th place. Vienna and her partner, Kasey Kahl, were eliminated at the end of week 6, finishing in 3rd place.

#### *Bachelor in Paradise*

**[Season 1](/wiki/Bachelor_in_Paradise_(American_season_1))**

Michelle returned for the inaugural season of *[Bachelor in Paradise](/wiki/Bachelor_in_Paradise_(American_TV_series))*. She quit in week 1.

**[Season 2](/wiki/Bachelor_in_Paradise_(American_season_2))**

Tenley returned for the second season of *Paradise*. She broke up with Joshua Albers in week 6.

#### Other appearances

Outside of the *Bachelor Nation* franchise, Tenley and Michelle appeared as contestants in the *Bachelors vs. Bachelorettes* special on the [season 7](/wiki/Wipeout_(season_7)) of *[Wipeout](/wiki/Wipeout_(2008_game_show))*.

## Post-show

### After the Final Rose

This is the special episode that airs after every finale. In season 14's segment, Jake told Chris Harrison about *Bachelor Pad*, Ali was chosen as the next bachelorette in the [sixth season of *The Bachelorette*](/wiki/The_Bachelorette_(season_6)). Jake and Vienna were seen in a public appearance as a couple. [Jeffrey Osborne](/wiki/Jeffrey_Osborne) sang "On the Wings of Love" at the closing of the show.

In June 2010, several months after their engagement, it was announced that Jake and Vienna Girardi had split.

### Deaths

#### Gia Allemand

On August 14, 2013, third-place contestant Gia Allemand died after a reported suicide attempt. Several contestants and Pavelka expressed their condolences via Twitter, with Pavelka saying, "I am in complete shock and devastated to hear the news about Gia. She was one of the sweetest people I have ever known. And a very dear friend. My heart goes out to her family during this very difficult time. We have lost an angel today. I miss you Gia...".

#### Alexa McAllister

On 16 February 2016, Alexa (Lex) McAllister died after an apparent suicide attempt in [Columbus, Ohio](/wiki/Columbus,_Ohio), as reported by TMZ. Per E! News, the local police said that on 13 February, they'd received a call in which it was claimed that McAllister had overdosed on prescription drugs. While McAllister was in a stable condition on her way to [Grant Medical Center](/wiki/Grant_Medical_Center), E! News got access to a police report, which stated that she had taken "a lot" of pills. Her family took her off life support after her health began to deteriorate. McAllister was 31. Pavelka tweeted his condolences on Twitter, "I'm so sad to hear about Alexa. Such a beautiful girl. My heart breaks for her family. Covering them in prayer during this rough time".

## Jake Pavelka

Main article: [Jake Pavelka](/wiki/Jake_Pavelka)

Jake Pavelka grew up in [Denton, Texas](/wiki/Denton,_Texas), and attended [University of North Texas](/wiki/University_of_North_Texas) and [Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University](/wiki/Embry-Riddle_Aeronautical_University). In his free time, he takes dancing lessons, woodworking, and has fun flying acrobatic planes and he is also a former child actor. Pavelka is an airline pilot, and took his first flying lessons at 12 years old. He also became a certified flight instructor when he was 23 years old.

Pavelka became a member of an ABC reality show for the third time when he appeared as a celebrity contestant on the [tenth season](/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_(American_TV_series)_season_10) of *[Dancing with the Stars](/wiki/Dancing_with_the_Stars_(American_TV_series))*. Partnered with [Chelsie Hightower](/wiki/Chelsie_Hightower), he was the fifth competitor eliminated on April 27, 2010.

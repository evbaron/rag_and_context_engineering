**Ana Ligia Fabian Hernández** (born November 7, 1988) is a [volleyball](/wiki/Volleyball) player from the [Dominican Republic](/wiki/Dominican_Republic), who participated in the [2007 Junior World Championship](/wiki/Women%27s_U20_Volleyball_World_Championship) in [Thailand](/wiki/Thailand). She competed as wing spiker, wearing the #4 jersey. At the [2006 U-20 NORCECA Women´s Junior Continental Championship](/wiki/NORCECA_Women%C2%B4s_Junior_Continental_Championship_U-20), she took with her team the silver medal.

She participated with Ingrid Carmona at the [NORCECA Beach Volleyball Circuit](/wiki/NORCECA_Beach_Volleyball_Circuit) in [2007](/wiki/2007_NORCECA_Beach_Volleyball_Circuit_(Santo_Domingo)) and [2009](/wiki/NORCECA_Beach_Volleyball_Circuit_2009) with [Ysaires Restituyo](/wiki/Ysaires_Restituyo), finishing in 11th and 9th position.

# Alvania belgica

***Alvania belgica*** is an extinct [species](/wiki/Species) of minute [sea snail](/wiki/Sea_snail), a [marine](/wiki/Marine_(ocean)) [gastropod](/wiki/Gastropod) [mollusc](/wiki/Mollusc) or [micromollusk](/wiki/Micromollusk) in the [family](/wiki/Family_(biology)) [Rissoidae](/wiki/Rissoidae).

## Distribution

Fossils of this species were found in [Miocene](/wiki/Miocene) strata in [Belgium](/wiki/Belgium) (age range: 15.97 to 11.608 Ma)

## References

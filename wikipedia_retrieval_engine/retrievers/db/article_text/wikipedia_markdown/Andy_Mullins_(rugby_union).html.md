**Andrew Richard Mullins** (born 1964) is an English former rugby union player. Mullins represented [Harlequin FC](/wiki/Harlequin_FC) and won a single cap for [England](/wiki/England_national_rugby_union_team) in 1989.

## Early life

Andy Mullins was born on 12 December 1964 in Eltham. He was educated at [Dulwich College](/wiki/Dulwich_College) and [Durham University](/wiki/Durham_University), where he was a member of [Hatfield College](/wiki/Hatfield_College,_Durham). He spent a year between school and university in the [British Army](/wiki/British_Army).

## Rugby union career

Mullins was selected for [England B](/wiki/England_Saxons) against [Australia](/wiki/Australia_national_rugby_union_team) in 1988, part of the latter teams [European tour](/wiki/1988_Australia_rugby_union_tour_of_England,_Scotland_and_Italy). He made his international debut on 4 November 1989 at [Twickenham](/wiki/Twickenham_stadium) in the [England](/wiki/England_national_rugby_union_team) vs [Fiji](/wiki/Fiji_national_rugby_union_team) match. He was on the winning side.

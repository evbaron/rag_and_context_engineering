# Lists of earthquakes

Earthquakes (6.0+ [Mw](/wiki/Seismic_magnitude_scales#Mw)) between 1900 and 2017

[Earthquakes](/wiki/Earthquake) are caused by movements within the [Earth's crust](/wiki/Earth%27s_crust) and uppermost [mantle](/wiki/Earth%27s_mantle). They range from weak events detectable only by [seismometers](/wiki/Seismometer), to sudden and violent events lasting many minutes which have caused some of the biggest disasters in [human history](/wiki/Human_history). Below, there are earthquakes listed by period, region or country, year, [magnitude](/wiki/Moment_magnitude_scale), cost, fatalities, and number of scientific studies.

## Deadliest earthquakes

See also: [List of deadliest 20th-century earthquakes per year](/wiki/Lists_of_20th-century_earthquakes#List_of_deadliest_earthquakes) and [List of deadliest 21st-century earthquakes per year](/wiki/Lists_of_21st-century_earthquakes#Deadliest_earthquakes_by_year)

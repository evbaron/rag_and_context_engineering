**Park Ji-min** ([Korean](/wiki/Korean_language): 박지민; born October 13, 1995), known [mononymously](/wiki/Mononym) as **Jimin**, is a South Korean singer, songwriter, and dancer. In 2013, he made his debut as a member of the South Korean boy band [BTS](/wiki/BTS), under the record label [Big Hit Entertainment](/wiki/Big_Hit_Music). Jimin has released three solo tracks under BTS' name—"Lie" in 2016, "[Serendipity](/wiki/Serendipity_(BTS_song))" in 2017, and "Filter" in 2020—all of which have charted on South Korea's [Gaon Digital Chart](/wiki/Gaon_Digital_Chart).

He released his first credited solo song, the digital track "Promise", which he co-wrote, in 2018 and recorded the duet "[With You](/wiki/With_You_(Jimin_song))" with [Ha Sung-woon](/wiki/Ha_Sung-woon) for the soundtrack for the [TvN](/wiki/TvN_(South_Korean_TV_channel)) drama *[Our Blues](/wiki/Our_Blues)* in 2022. Jimin released his debut solo album, *[Face](/wiki/Face_(Jimin_album))* in 2023, which debuted at number one in South Korea and Japan and number two on the US [*Billboard* 200](/wiki/Billboard_200), making him the highest-charting Korean solo artist on the latter chart. The album's single "[Like Crazy](/wiki/Like_Crazy_(song))" became the first song by a Korean solo artist to debut at number one on the US [*Billboard* Hot 100](/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100) in history. Jimin also became the first Korean solo artist to top the *Billboard* [Artist 100](/wiki/Artist_100) chart. In 2024, he released his second album, *[Muse](/wiki/Muse_(Jimin_album))*, and its lead single "[Who](/wiki/Who_(song))", which debuted at number one on the [*Billboard* Global 200](/wiki/Billboard_Global_200).

## Early life and education

Park Ji-min was born on October 13, 1995, in [Geumjeong District](/wiki/Geumjeong_District), [Busan](/wiki/Busan), South Korea. He is of the [Milyang Park clan](/wiki/Milyang_Park_clan). His immediate family includes his mother, father, and younger brother.

When he was a child, he attended Busan's Hodong Elementary School and Yonsan Middle School. In middle school, he attended Just Dance Academy, where he learned [popping](/wiki/Popping) and [locking](/wiki/Locking_(dance)). Prior to becoming a trainee, Jimin studied [contemporary dance](/wiki/Contemporary_dance) at Busan High School of Arts and was a top student in the [modern dance](/wiki/Modern_dance) department. A teacher suggested that he join an entertainment company, leading him to [Big Hit Entertainment](/wiki/Big_Hit_Music). Once he passed the auditions in 2012, he transferred to [Korean Arts High School](/wiki/Korean_Arts_High_School), graduating in 2014.

Jimin graduated from Global Cyber University in August 2020 with a major in Broadcasting and Entertainment. He was awarded the President's Award, the school's highest honor. As of 2021, he is enrolled at [Hanyang Cyber University](/wiki/Hanyang_Cyber_University), pursuing a [Master of Business Administration](/wiki/Master_of_Business_Administration) in Advertising and Media.

## Career

### 2013–present: BTS

Main article: [BTS](/wiki/BTS)

Jimin performing in September 2013

Jimin was the last Big Hit trainee to be added to the lineup that would eventually become [BTS](/wiki/BTS). Of the band's seven members, he spent the shortest period of time—six months—as a trainee prior to debuting. On June 13, 2013, he debuted as a [vocalist](/wiki/Singing) and [dancer](/wiki/Dance) in BTS with the song "No More Dream" from their first single *[2 Cool 4 Skool](/wiki/2_Cool_4_Skool)*. Under the band's name, he released three solo songs: "Lie", "[Serendipity](/wiki/Serendipity_(BTS_song))", and "Filter". "Lie" was released in 2016, as part of the band's second Korean studio album, *[Wings](/wiki/Wings_(BTS_album))*. It was described as stunning and dramatic, conveying dark undertones and emotions in line with the overall concept of the album. In contrast, "[Serendipity](/wiki/Serendipity_(BTS_song))", from the band's *[Love Yourself: Her](/wiki/Love_Yourself:_Her)* (2017) [extended play](/wiki/Extended_play) (EP), was soft and sensual, expressing the joy, conviction, and curiosity of love. "Filter", a track from BTS' 2020 studio album *[Map of the Soul: 7](/wiki/Map_of_the_Soul:_7)*, had a [Latin pop](/wiki/Latin_pop) flair, with lyrics reflecting the different sides of Jimin that he shows to the world and those around him. Jimin additionally co-wrote and co-produced another track included on that album, "Friends", which was later featured in the [Marvel Studios](/wiki/Marvel_Studios) film *[Eternals](/wiki/Eternals_(film))*.

"Serendipity" and "Lie" both surpassed fifty million streams on [Spotify](/wiki/Spotify) in 2018, followed shortly thereafter by the former's full length version from BTS' *[Love Yourself: Answer](/wiki/Love_Yourself:_Answer)* (2018) compilation album, which achieved the milestone in early 2019. Jimin became the first Korean artist to have three solo tracks accumulate over 50 million streams each, surpassing [Psy](/wiki/Psy), who previously crossed the 50 million streams mark twice with "[Gangnam Style](/wiki/Gangnam_Style)" (2012) and "[Gentleman](/wiki/Gentleman_(Psy_song))" (2013). Both songs were also the only solos by a BTS member included in the [Official Charts Company](/wiki/Official_Charts_Company)'s (OCC) list of the top 20 most streamed BTS songs in the United Kingdom as of October 2018, ranking at number 17 and 19 respectively. In April 2019, the list was expanded to reflect the top 40, and both tracks were the highest ranked solos included that year, at numbers 18 and 20 respectively.

In May 2019, Jimin became the first BTS member to have a solo music video achieve 100 million views on [YouTube](/wiki/YouTube) when "Serendipity" surpassed the milestone. He remained the only BTS member with multiple solo songs in the January 2020 update of the OCC's top 40 list: "Lie" and "Serendipity" were the second and third most-streamed solos, at numbers 24 and 29 respectively, with the full-length version of the latter at number 38. The following month, Jimin earned his first entry on the [*Billboard* Hot 100](/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100) with "Filter", which debuted at number 87 on the chart, as well his first number one on the World Digital Song Sales chart. The song set the record for the biggest streaming debut among all Korean songs on Spotify, accruing over 2.2 million streams in its first 24 hours of release, and went on to become the fastest Korean solo in the platform's history to surpass 20–60 million streams. It was also the only solo album track by BTS to receive a Song of the Year nomination at the [Gaon Chart Music Awards](/wiki/Gaon_Chart_Music_Awards). In March 2021, "Filter" became the 15th BTS song to spend a full year on *Billboard*'s [World Digital Song Sales](/wiki/World_Digital_Song_Sales) chart. It was the longest-charting Korean song released in 2020 on the ranking, having spent 80 weeks on the chart as of the issue dated October 9, 2021.

Jimin was awarded the fifth-class Hwagwan [Order of Cultural Merit](/wiki/Order_of_Cultural_Merit_(South_Korea)) in 2018, alongside his bandmates, by the [President of South Korea](/wiki/President_of_South_Korea), [Moon Jae-in](/wiki/Moon_Jae-in), for their contributions to the promotion of Korean culture. Three years later, Moon appointed him [Special Presidential Envoy for Future Generations and Culture](/wiki/Office_of_the_President_(South_Korea)), alongside his bandmates, to help "lead the global agenda for future generations" and "expand South Korea's diplomatic efforts and global standing" in the international community.

### 2014–present: Solo work

Jimin on the set of the "[Boy with Luv](/wiki/Boy_with_Luv)" music video, March 2019.

In 2014, Jimin collaborated with bandmate [Jung Kook](/wiki/Jung_Kook) on a song called "Christmas Day", a Korean rendition of [Justin Bieber](/wiki/Justin_Bieber)'s "[Mistletoe](/wiki/Mistletoe_(Justin_Bieber_song))"; Jimin wrote the Korean lyrics himself. The two collaborated again in 2017, on a cover of [Charlie Puth](/wiki/Charlie_Puth)'s "[We Don't Talk Anymore](/wiki/We_Don%27t_Talk_Anymore_(Charlie_Puth_song))" featuring [Selena Gomez](/wiki/Selena_Gomez). Jung Kook previously released a solo version of the song in February, and the two prepared the duet as a special gift to the band's fandom, releasing it on June 2, during BTS' fourth anniversary celebrations. *Teen Vogue* wrote that "adding Jimin's voice to the mix makes the rendition all the more lovely", while the Canadian outlet *[Flare](/wiki/Flare_(magazine))* magazine stated that "it honestly might be better than the original". *Elite Daily* described the cover as "nothing short of flawless".

Jimin appeared on several variety shows such as *[Hello Counselor](/wiki/Hello_Counselor)*, *[Please Take Care of My Refrigerator](/wiki/Please_Take_Care_of_My_Refrigerator)*, and *God's Workplace* in 2016. He also served as a special MC on [domestic television music programs](/wiki/South_Korean_music_programs) such as *[Show! Music Core](/wiki/Show!_Music_Core)* and *[M Countdown](/wiki/M_Countdown)*. In December 2016, he participated in a dance duet at the [KBS Song Festival](/wiki/KBS_Song_Festival) with [Taemin](/wiki/Lee_Tae-min) from [Shinee](/wiki/Shinee).

On December 30, 2018, Jimin released "Promise", his first credited song as a solo artist, for free on BTS' [SoundCloud](/wiki/SoundCloud) page. It earned the biggest 24-hour debut in SoundCloud history with 8.5 million streams, surpassing previous record holder [Drake](/wiki/Drake_(musician))'s "Duppy Freestyle" which received 4.9 million streams, and would go on to become the most streamed song on the platform by December 2021, with over 300 million streams. Described by [*Billboard*](/wiki/Billboard_(magazine)) as a "mellow [pop ballad](/wiki/Pop_ballad)", the song was composed by Jimin and Big Hit producer Slow Rabbit, who also produced the track, and features lyrics written by Jimin and bandmate [RM](/wiki/RM_(rapper)). Jimin released his second solo song "Christmas Love", about his childhood memories of the holidays, on December 24, 2020. In 2022, he contributed the single "With You", a duet with [Ha Sung-woon](/wiki/Ha_Sung-woon), to the soundtrack of the [TvN](/wiki/TvN_(South_Korean_TV_channel)) drama *[Our Blues](/wiki/Our_Blues)*; the single was released on April 24.

In January 2023, Jimin co-wrote and featured on the single "[Vibe](/wiki/Vibe_(Taeyang_song))" by [Taeyang](/wiki/Taeyang). His first official solo project since BTS announced an increased focus on individual music endeavors a year prior, the single earned Jimin his debut entry on the [*Billboard* Hot 100](/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100) as a solo artist, at number 76. He was announced as a [global ambassador](/wiki/Brand_ambassador) for [Dior](/wiki/Dior) later that month, and for [Tiffany & Co.](/wiki/Tiffany_%26_Co.) in March. "Promise" and "Christmas Love" were made available on streaming platforms worldwide as official singles under Jimin's name on March 6. His debut solo album *[Face](/wiki/Face_(Jimin_album))* was released on March 24. It set several chart and sales records, debuting at number one in South Korea and Japan, and number two in the US, making Jimin the highest-charting Korean solo artist of all time on the [*Billboard* 200](/wiki/Billboard_200). The album's two singles, "[Set Me Free Pt. 2](/wiki/Set_Me_Free_Pt._2)" and "[Like Crazy](/wiki/Like_Crazy_(song))", released on March 17 and 24 respectively, both entered the Hot 100: the former debuted at number 30, while the latter debuted at number one, making Jimin the first South Korean solo artist in history to top the chart. The album's release led Jimin to become the first Korean solo artist to reach number one on the *Billboard* [Artist 100](/wiki/Artist_100). In May, he appeared as a featured artist, alongside [Jvke](/wiki/Jvke) and [Muni Long](/wiki/Muni_Long), on the single "[Angel Pt. 1](/wiki/Angel_Pt._1)" by [Kodak Black](/wiki/Kodak_Black) and [NLE Choppa](/wiki/NLE_Choppa), as part of the soundtrack for the film *[Fast X](/wiki/Fast_X)*. The digital single "[Closer Than This](/wiki/Closer_Than_This)" was released on December 22, shortly after Jimin [enlisted](/wiki/Conscription_in_South_Korea) in the [South Korean Army](/wiki/Republic_of_Korea_Army). Originally recorded during the production of *Face*, it is a song dedicated to the BTS fandom in gratitude for their support.

Big Hit announced an eight-episode travel reality series, *[Are You Sure?!](/wiki/Are_You_Sure%3F_(Disney%2B_series))*, featuring Jimin and [Jung Kook](/wiki/Jung_Kook) on July 2, 2024. Available for [streaming](/wiki/Streaming_media) exclusively on [Disney+](/wiki/Disney%2B), the premiere episode aired August 8, with subsequent episodes following weekly through September 19. The label released Jimin's second solo album, *[Muse](/wiki/Muse_(Jimin_album))*, on July 19, while he was carrying out his mandatory military service. It was supported by the singles "[Smeraldo Garden Marching Band](/wiki/Smeraldo_Garden_Marching_Band)" and "[Who](/wiki/Who_(song))". "Who" became Jimin's first song to debut atop both of *Billboard*'s [global charts](/wiki/Billboard_Global_200) and [the longest-charting song by a Korean artist](/wiki/List_of_K-pop_songs_on_the_Billboard_charts#Billboard_Hot_100_(Complete)) on the [*Billboard* Hot 100](/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100) after spending 33 weeks on the chart, surpassing [BTS](/wiki/BTS)' "[Dynamite](/wiki/Dynamite_(BTS_song))".

## Artistry

Jimin performing "[Blood Sweat & Tears](/wiki/Blood_Sweat_%26_Tears_(BTS_song))" at the 2016 [Melon Music Awards](/wiki/Melon_Music_Awards).

Jimin's vocals have been described as delicate and sweet. He is regarded as an exceptional dancer among the members of BTS and in K-pop in general, often praised for his "smooth and elegant movements" and charm on stage. In the documentary *[Burn the Stage](/wiki/Burn_the_Stage:_The_Movie#Adaptation)* released in 2018, Jimin addressed his [perfectionism](/wiki/Perfectionism_(psychology)). In 2023, Jimin stated that as a pop artist, he "aims to excel at both dancing and singing and perform a diverse range of musical genres and themes" while "taking a healthy and sustainable approach to do so".

He has cited singer [Rain](/wiki/Rain_(entertainer)) as an inspiration and one of the reasons why he wanted to become a singer and performer.

## Impact and influence

In 2016, Jimin was ranked as the 14th most popular idol in an annual survey conducted by [Gallup Korea](/wiki/Gallup_Korea). He subsequently ranked seventh in 2017, and then consecutively ranked first in 2018 and 2019, as the only idol to top the survey for two consecutive years. In 2018, Jimin was the ninth most-tweeted about celebrity and the eighth most-tweeted about musician globally. He was named the 17th best boy band member in history by *[The Guardian](/wiki/The_Guardian)*. From January to May 2018, Jimin won the monthly "Top K-Pop Artist–Individual" award for *Peeper x Billboard*, a collaboration between the *Peeper* social media app and [*Billboard* Korea](/wiki/Billboard_(magazine)) that collects fan votes for their favorite K-pop artists. The prize was a donation to [UNICEF](/wiki/UNICEF) in his name.

The Cultural Conservation Society awarded Jimin a plaque of appreciation in 2019, for performing [buchaechum](/wiki/Buchaechum), a traditional Korean fan dance, during the [2018 Melon Music Awards](/wiki/2018_Melon_Music_Awards) and helping spread the dance outside of Korea. In October 2021, he became the first idol to spend 34 consecutive months atop the brand reputation ranking for individual boy group idols and the only one to top the overall ranking for three consecutive years.

Jimin is often cited as a role model by various idols in the [K-pop](/wiki/K-pop) industry, many of whom try to emulate his style of dance, mannerisms, and stage presence; media outlets have called him the "Idol of Idols", "Idol's Bible", and "Rookies' Bible". Artists who have cited him as an influence include Arthur of [Kingdom](/wiki/Kingdom_(group)), Bic of [MCND](/wiki/MCND), Kim Si-hun of [BDC](/wiki/BDC_(group)), Woochul of [Newkidd](/wiki/Newkidd), [Hyunjin](/wiki/Hyunjin_(Stray_Kids_singer)) of [Stray Kids](/wiki/Stray_Kids), Wooyoung of [Ateez](/wiki/Ateez), Lim Se-jun of [Victon](/wiki/Victon), Huening Kai and [Beomgyu](/wiki/Beomgyu) of [Tomorrow X Together](/wiki/Tomorrow_X_Together), and [Ni-Ki](/wiki/Ni-Ki) and Jay of [Enhypen](/wiki/Enhypen). British internet personality [Oli London](/wiki/Oli_London) underwent 15 cosmetic surgeries by 2020 to resemble Jimin.

Jimin has also been acknowledged beyond the [K-pop](/wiki/K-pop) industry. In 2019, Award-winning director and photographer [Gus Van Sant](/wiki/Gus_Van_Sant) named him as the artist he wished to work with.
In 2021, Olympic figure skater [Yuzuru Hanyu](/wiki/Yuzuru_Hanyu) shared being a [BTS](/wiki/BTS) fan and mentioned Jimin as a source of inspiration for his dance movements on the rink. In 2023, [Ryan Gosling](/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) gifted Jimin the guitar his character Ken used in the movie *[Barbie](/wiki/Barbie_(film))*, as Jimin's "[Permission to Dance](/wiki/Permission_to_Dance)" music video outfit served as inspiration for one of the outfits Gosling wore for the role.

*Forbes* Korea included Jimin on the [*Forbes* Korea Power Celebrity 40](/wiki/Forbes_Korea_Power_Celebrity_40) for 2024, at number seven, making him the highest ranking K-pop soloist and second-highest ranking soloist on the list. In 2025, Jimin made it to the [Madame Tussauds](/wiki/Madame_Tussauds) Hot 100 list, recognizing him as a [K-pop star](/wiki/Korean_idol).

## Philanthropy

### Education

Jimin is a supporter of education and the arts. From 2016 to 2018, he covered uniform expenses of students at his alma mater, Busan Hodong Elementary School. After the school's closing was announced, he donated summer and winter middle school uniforms to the final graduates and gifted autographed albums to the entire student body. In early 2019, Jimin donated [₩](/wiki/South_Korean_won)100 million (equivalent to ₩117.23 million or [US$](/wiki/USD)82,454.1 in 2025) to the Busan Department of Education to help support students from low income families. Of the total, [₩](/wiki/South_Korean_won)30 million (equivalent to ₩35.17 million or [US$](/wiki/USD)24,736.23 in 2025) went to his alma mater, Busan Arts High School. In July 2020, he donated [₩](/wiki/South_Korean_won)100 million (equivalent to ₩116.61 million or [US$](/wiki/USD)82,013.45 in 2025) to the Jeonnam Future Education Foundation to fund the creation of a scholarship fund for talented students from [South Jeolla Province](/wiki/South_Jeolla_Province) who were struggling financially.

In September 2022, Jimin made a donation to the Gangwon Provincial Office of Education. In March 2023, he donated [₩](/wiki/South_Korean_won)100 million ([US$](/wiki/USD)87,416.41) to the North Chungcheong Provincial Office of Education to fund books and reading education programs for students. In May 2024, he made a donation of [₩](/wiki/South_Korean_won)100 million ([US$](/wiki/USD)87,416.41) to the South Gyeongsang Provincial Office of Education to create a scholarship for underprivileged students. In September 2024, Jimin donated [₩](/wiki/South_Korean_won)100 million ([US$](/wiki/USD)87,416.41) to fund scholarships, along with living and medical expenses for Korean soldiers. The donation serves to support families of soldiers who have struggled, suffered a loss, or passed. Following his donation, Jimin was appointed to the honor society of the [Community Chest of Korea](/wiki/Community_Chest_of_Korea)'s [Chungnam](/wiki/South_Chungcheong_Province) branch. In July 2025, he donated [₩](/wiki/South_Korean_won)100 million ([US$](/wiki/USD)87,416.41) to the Jeonbuk Office of Education in the North Jeolla Province. This marked his 6th donation since 2019 to Provincial Offices of Education for the creation of scholarships for deprived students.

### Other philanthropy

In July 2021, Jimin donated [₩](/wiki/South_Korean_won)100 million (equivalent to ₩113.76 million or [US$](/wiki/USD)80,014.42 in 2025) to [Rotary International](/wiki/Rotary_International) to help polio patients. As with previous donations, the benefaction was made privately but became public in September, when the Go-seong Rotary Club displayed a banner thanking him for the donation. He was announced as a member of the Green Noble Club, a group of major donors to the Green Umbrella Children's Foundation who have contributed ₩100 million or more, on October 12, 2021. In February 2023, he donated [₩](/wiki/South_Korean_won)100 million ([US$](/wiki/USD)87,416.41) through the Korean UNICEF Committee toward emergency relief for children affected by the [Turkey–Syria earthquake](/wiki/2023_Turkey%E2%80%93Syria_earthquake).

In August 2023, Jimin participated in the "Hometown Love Donation System", a program allowing non-residents to donate to support welfare in [Nam District, Busan](/wiki/Nam_District,_Busan). He donated the maximum allowed donation amount of [₩](/wiki/South_Korean_won)5 million ([US$](/wiki/USD)4,370.82) and proceeded to re-donate his campaign's donor return gifts to people in need in the region. In February 2024, Jimin was inducted in the Nam-gu Hometown Love Donation System Donor Hall of Fame for his contribution to the district welfare programs.

In December 2024, Jimin donated [₩](/wiki/South_Korean_won)30 million ([US$](/wiki/USD)26,224.92) to the Busan Habitat Challenge, a project aiming to improve the living conditions of the elderly in the city. In March 2025, he donated [₩](/wiki/South_Korean_won)100 million ([US$](/wiki/USD)87,416.41) to support firefighters following the [wildfires](/wiki/2025_South_Korea_wildfires) in the [Gyeongbuk region](/wiki/Gyeongbuk).

## Personal life

Since 2018, Jimin has lived in [Hannam-dong](/wiki/Hannam-dong), Seoul, South Korea, with his bandmates. In 2021, he purchased a property in the area worth [₩](/wiki/South_Korean_won)5.9 [billion](/wiki/1,000,000,000) (equivalent to ₩6.71 [billion](/wiki/1,000,000,000) or [US$](/wiki/USD)4.72 million in 2025).

### Health

In 2017, during BTS' [Wings Tour](/wiki/The_Wings_Tour), Jimin was unable to participate in the show in [Macau](/wiki/Macau) due to neck and shoulder cramping. The following year, while in [London](/wiki/London) for the [Love Yourself World Tour](/wiki/Love_Yourself_World_Tour), he withdrew from the band's scheduled performance on *[The Graham Norton Show](/wiki/The_Graham_Norton_Show)* because of "severe muscle pain in his neck and back", according to a statement released by [Big Hit Entertainment](/wiki/Big_Hit_Music).

In 2022, Jimin was hospitalized after having abdominal pain and a mild sore throat. He underwent [appendicitis](/wiki/Appendicitis) surgery on January 31 and received a positive [COVID-19](/wiki/COVID-19) diagnosis.

### Military service

On November 22, 2023, Big Hit announced through [Weverse](/wiki/Weverse) that Jimin, along with bandmates [RM](/wiki/RM_(musician)), [V](/wiki/V_(singer)), and [Jung Kook](/wiki/Jung_Kook), had started the enlistment process to carry out his [mandatory military service](/wiki/Conscription_in_South_Korea). He enlisted as an active duty soldier on December 12 at the Army 5th Division Recruit Training Center in [Yeoncheon](/wiki/Yeoncheon) with Jung Kook using the buddy system. He completed his five weeks of basic training in January 2024, placing first as an outstanding trainee and received the Best trainee commendation award from the Division Commander, taking an oath as the representative of all trainees in his unit. He was then appointed to an Artillery Unit in the [5th Infantry Division](/wiki/5th_Infantry_Division_(South_Korea)). In June, Jimin received an early promotion and was selected as a Special Class Warrior for his excellent military performance. He was formally discharged on June 11, 2025.

**Neon Trees** are an American [rock](/wiki/Rock_music) band founded in [Provo, Utah](/wiki/Provo,_Utah). The band received nationwide exposure in late 2008 when they opened several North American tour dates for the band [the Killers](/wiki/The_Killers). Not long after, the band was signed by [Mercury Records](/wiki/Mercury_Records). Their first single, "[Animal](/wiki/Animal_(Neon_Trees_song))", climbed to No. 13 on the [*Billboard* Hot 100](/wiki/Billboard_Hot_100) and No. 1 on the [Alternative Songs](/wiki/Alternative_Songs) chart.

Since their formation, Neon Trees has released five studio albums: *[Habits](/wiki/Habits_(album))* (2010), *[Picture Show](/wiki/Picture_Show_(album))* (2012), *[Pop Psychology](/wiki/Pop_Psychology_(album))* (2014), *[I Can Feel You Forgetting Me](/wiki/I_Can_Feel_You_Forgetting_Me)* (2020), and *[Sink Your Teeth](/wiki/Sink_Your_Teeth)* (2024). The band has also released eight EPs (two exclusive to [iTunes](/wiki/ITunes)) and fourteen singles.

## History

### Early years (2005–2010)

Formed initially by childhood friends [Tyler Glenn](/wiki/Tyler_Glenn) ([vocals](/wiki/Lead_vocals), [keyboards](/wiki/Keyboard_instrument)) and Chris Allen ([guitar](/wiki/Guitar), [vocals](/wiki/Backing_vocals)) who both grew up in [Murrieta, California](/wiki/Murrieta,_California), the band made its first home in Provo after Glenn and Allen moved there from Southern California, eventually adding Branden Campbell ([bass guitar](/wiki/Bass_guitar), vocals) and [Elaine Bradley](/wiki/Elaine_Bradley) ([drums](/wiki/Drumkit), [percussion](/wiki/Percussion), vocals) to the lineup. David Charles plays with the band as touring guitarist.

They first started playing under the name Neon Trees in late 2005 with five original members, Tyler Glenn and Chris Allen from California, and three veterans of the Utah music scene, bass player Mike Liechty, drummer Jason Gibbons and keyboardist Nathan Evans. The band's name originates from the lighted trees on the [In-N-Out Burger](/wiki/In-N-Out_Burger) signs, which inspired Tyler Glenn. In a strange coincidence, it was later learned that Branden Campbell's father, Steve Campbell, installed the exact Neon Trees in the In-N-Out that inspired Glenn while working for a sign company out of Las Vegas.

They released an EP, *Becoming Different People*, in 2006 during the time of their first tour through California. The EP featured promising songs like "Phones", "Sister Stereo", "Up Against the Glass". They also recorded other unreleased tracks during the "Treehouse Sessions" in 2006.

The band's lineup soon narrowed down to four members with Branden Campbell and Elaine Bradley joining the group on bass and drums, respectively. Touring musician Bruce Rubenson on keyboards and drums joined in. Shortly thereafter, [The Killers](/wiki/The_Killers)' drummer, [Ronnie Vannucci Jr.](/wiki/Ronnie_Vannucci_Jr.), went to watch the band play at a small club in Las Vegas as he had been in a Ska band called "Attaboy Skip" with Neon Trees bassist Branden Campbell prior to joining The Killers. Ronnie was impressed, and as a result they were chosen to open for The Killers for a string of dates on their 2008 North American tour. Vannucci later helped the band sign to major label [Mercury Records](/wiki/Mercury_Records), which was announced in late 2008. Additionally, the group was voted Band of the Year in 2009 by *[City Weekly](/wiki/City_Weekly)*, a popular Salt Lake City publication.

### 2010–11: *Habits* and commercial breakthrough

Main article: [Habits](/wiki/Habits_(album))

Neon Trees at Music Midtown 2012

Although they had several prior independent releases, the band released its debut album on Mercury/[Island Def Jam](/wiki/The_Island_Def_Jam_Music_Group), [*Habits*](/wiki/Habits_(album)), on March 16, 2010. "Animal" was chosen as the lead single and was shortly thereafter featured as the Free Single of the Week on [iTunes](/wiki/ITunes) in March 2010. They performed the song on *Jimmy Kimmel Live!* on March 23, 2010, *The Tonight Show with Jay Leno* on May 14, 2010, *Lopez Tonight* on October 18, 2010, *Live! with Regis & Kelly* on November 4, 2010, and TBS' *Conan* on November 24, 2010. They performed a second single *1983* on *[Late Night with Jimmy Fallon](/wiki/Late_Night_with_Jimmy_Fallon)* on January 13, 2011 and another stint on *[The Tonight Show with Jay Leno](/wiki/The_Tonight_Show_with_Jay_Leno)* on Jan 18, 2011. They performed third single "Your Surrender" on *Late Show with David Letterman* on May 5, 2011 and a third appearance on *The Tonight Show with Jay Leno* on Jun 10, 2011. [Mark Hoppus](/wiki/Mark_Hoppus) of [Blink-182](/wiki/Blink-182) selected "Animal" as his *Spin* magazine Pick of the Week. The song has also been featured on a commercial for Las Vegas. The group has also released a digital EP of four remixes of the song, one of which by Tyler Glenn.

In 2010, the band covered [Justin Bieber](/wiki/Justin_Bieber)'s song "[Baby](/wiki/Baby_(Justin_Bieber_song))" as well as [Ben E. King](/wiki/Ben_E._King)'s "[Stand By Me](/wiki/Stand_by_Me_(Ben_E._King_song))". Throughout early 2010 the band, along with [Mutemath](/wiki/Mutemath) and [Street Drum Corps](/wiki/Street_Drum_Corps), opened for the [Thirty Seconds to Mars](/wiki/Thirty_Seconds_to_Mars) tour [Into the Wild](/wiki/Into_the_Wild_Tour). Subsequently, they headlined the Bang the Gong Tour with [Paper Tongues](/wiki/Paper_Tongues), [MeTalkPretty](/wiki/MeTalkPretty), and [Civil Twilight](/wiki/Civil_Twilight_(band)) in mid-2010, performed a set to 7,000 at [Lollapalooza](/wiki/Lollapalooza), and went on tour with Thirty Seconds to Mars in the last half of the year.

### 2011–13: *Picture Show*

Main article: [Picture Show](/wiki/Picture_Show_(album))

On December 7, 2011, an article published by *[Rolling Stone](/wiki/Rolling_Stone)*'s website streamed the band's new song "[Everybody Talks](/wiki/Everybody_Talks)". On February 10, 2012, a video was posted on their official YouTube page, titled Neon Trees – Picture Show Teaser One / The New Album April 17, advertising their new album is to be released on April 17, 2012. In March 2012, Neon Trees and their single "Everybody Talks" were featured in a commercial for the 2012 [Buick Verano](/wiki/Buick_Verano_(North_America)). The single was covered in episode five of season four of *Glee*.

Neon Trees' second studio album *[Picture Show](/wiki/Picture_Show_(album))* was released on April 17, 2012. On August 29, 2012, Neon Trees performed "Everybody Talks" on the *[America's Got Talent](/wiki/America%27s_Got_Talent)* live results show. In early fall 2012, the band toured with [The Offspring](/wiki/The_Offspring) and [Dead Sara](/wiki/Dead_Sara). In 2013 they joined [Maroon 5](/wiki/Maroon_5) in their world tour alongside [Owl City](/wiki/Owl_City). In November/December 2013 they were a support act for [Taylor Swift](/wiki/Taylor_Swift)'s three concerts in New Zealand and four concerts in Australia. The band was featured on NBC's coverage of the 2012 Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade. They performed an abbreviated version of their single "Everybody Talks". The second single from the album is the track "[Lessons in Love (All Day, All Night)](/wiki/Lessons_in_Love_(All_Day,_All_Night))" with the collaboration of American record producer and D.J. [Kaskade](/wiki/Kaskade).

### 2014–2017: *Pop Psychology*

Main article: [Pop Psychology](/wiki/Pop_Psychology_(album))

Neon Trees' third studio album, *[Pop Psychology](/wiki/Pop_Psychology_(album))*, was released on April 22, 2014. The first single from *Pop Psychology*, titled "[Sleeping with a Friend](/wiki/Sleeping_with_a_Friend)", was released for digital download. The band performed the single on *[The Tonight Show with Jay Leno](/wiki/The_Tonight_Show_with_Jay_Leno)* on January 22, 2014.

"Sleeping with a Friend" was solicited to U.S. [Contemporary hit radio](/wiki/Contemporary_hit_radio) by both Mercury and [Island Records](/wiki/Island_Records) on February 25, 2014. Neon Trees later released four more singles following "Sleeping with a Friend". The singles are titled "I Love You (But I Hate Your Friends)", "Voices In the Halls", and "First Things First". "First Things First" is the end credit song for the 2016 Netflix Original series *Dirk Gently's Holistic Detective Agency*.

In 2015, the group announced the "An Intimate Night With Neon Trees" Tour. The tour ran from June 6, 2015, to July 26, 2015, finishing in [Boston, Massachusetts](/wiki/Boston,_Massachusetts), at the House Of Blues Boston. Opening acts included [Alex Winston](/wiki/Alex_Winston), Yes You Are, Coin, and [Fictionist](/wiki/Fictionist).

On May 5, 2015, the band released a non-album single entitled "Songs I Can't Listen To" and an accompanying music video on June 3, 2015, featuring [Dustin Lance Black](/wiki/Dustin_Lance_Black).

On August 4, 2017, the band premiered a new single "Feel Good".

### 2019–2023: *I Can Feel You Forgetting Me*

Main article: [I Can Feel You Forgetting Me](/wiki/I_Can_Feel_You_Forgetting_Me)

Neon Trees released "Used to Like", the lead single from their fourth album, on November 13, 2019. The second single, "New Best Friend" was released on May 20, 2020. On July 10, 2020, the band released the third single called "Mess Me Up". The fourth and final single, "Nights", was released on July 22, 2020. The album, *[I Can Feel You Forgetting Me](/wiki/I_Can_Feel_You_Forgetting_Me)*, was released on July 24, 2020. In December 2021, they released a new single and video, "Holiday Rock".

### 2023–present: *Sink Your Teeth*

Main article: [Sink Your Teeth](/wiki/Sink_Your_Teeth)

*Sink Your Teeth*, the fifth studio album by Neon Trees, was released on September 20, 2024. There are 12 songs on the album. On June 30, 2023, the first and lead single of the album "Favorite Daze" was released. The second single, "Losing My Head", was released on September 15, 2023. Three more singles were released in early 2024 before the album; "Bad Dreams" (May 31), "El Diablo" (July 19), and "Cruel Intentions" (August 28)

## Musical style

Neon Trees' musical style has been described as [pop rock](/wiki/Pop_rock), [new wave](/wiki/New_wave_music), [synth-pop](/wiki/Synth-pop), [indie rock](/wiki/Indie_rock), [alternative rock](/wiki/Alternative_rock), [pop](/wiki/Pop_music), and [electropop](/wiki/Electropop). When describing the band's sound, Heather Phares of [AllMusic](/wiki/AllMusic) wrote "Neon Trees blend new wave, [dance](/wiki/Dance_music), and indie into smart, catchy pop." Sputnikmusic described the band's debut studio album *[Habits](/wiki/Habits_(album))* as a "refreshing blast of timeless [rock](/wiki/Rock_music) energy and spirit that wouldn’t sound out of place at any point from ‘60s [garage-rock](/wiki/Garage_rock) to 2010 [dance rock](/wiki/Dance-rock)."

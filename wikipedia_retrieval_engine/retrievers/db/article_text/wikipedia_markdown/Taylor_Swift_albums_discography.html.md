The American singer-songwriter [Taylor Swift](/wiki/Taylor_Swift) has released 12 original albums, 4 [re-recorded albums](/wiki/Re-recording_(music)), 5 [extended plays (EPs)](/wiki/Extended_play), and 4 [live albums](/wiki/Live_album). In the United States, as of May 2025, she had sold 116.7 million [album-equivalent units](/wiki/Album-equivalent_unit), coming from 54 million pure sales and 70.7 billion [streams](/wiki/Streaming_media); the [Recording Industry Association of America](/wiki/Recording_Industry_Association_of_America) (RIAA) as of November 2025 [certified](/wiki/RIAA_certification) 110 million album units, making her the [highest-certified female album artist](/wiki/List_of_highest-certified_music_artists_in_the_United_States). On the [*Billboard* 200](/wiki/Billboard_200) chart, she has accumulated 15 albums and 98 total weeks at number one, more than any other solo act.

Swift released six albums with [Big Machine Records](/wiki/Big_Machine_Records). Her debut album, [*Taylor Swift*](/wiki/Taylor_Swift_(album)) (2006), spent more weeks on the *Billboard* 200 than any other album in the 2000s decade, and her second, *[Fearless](/wiki/Fearless_(Taylor_Swift_album))* (2008), was the only album from the 2000s decade to spend one year in the top 10 of the *Billboard* 200. *Fearless* was Swift's first album to top the charts in Australia, Canada, and New Zealand. Her next albums—*[Speak Now](/wiki/Speak_Now)* (2010), *[Red](/wiki/Red_(Taylor_Swift_album))* (2012), *[1989](/wiki/1989_(album))* (2014), and [*Reputation*](/wiki/Reputation_(album)) (2017)—all reached number one on the *Billboard* 200, and each of them sold over one million US first-week copies. All four albums reached number one in Australia, Canada, and New Zealand; and the latter three all reached number one in the United Kingdom.

Under a recording contract with [Republic Records](/wiki/Republic_Records) signed in 2018, Swift has released six original albums—*[Lover](/wiki/Lover_(album))* (2019), [*Folklore*](/wiki/Folklore_(Taylor_Swift_album)) (2020), *[Evermore](/wiki/Evermore)* (2020), *[Midnights](/wiki/Midnights)* (2022), *[The Tortured Poets Department](/wiki/The_Tortured_Poets_Department)* (2024), and *[The Life of a Showgirl](/wiki/The_Life_of_a_Showgirl)* (2025)—all of them peaked atop the *Billboard* 200. *Lover* was Swift's first to reach number one in Spain and Sweden. *Midnights*, *The Tortured Poets Department*, and *The Life of a Showgirl* each sold one million first-week copies in the United States, and they reached number one in Australia, Canada, Denmark, Ireland, New Zealand, Spain, Norway, and the United Kingdom. In the United States, *The Tortured Poets Department* was Swift's first album to open with two million units first-week, and *The Life of a Showgirl* opened with four million units, becoming the [fastest-selling album in modern American history](/wiki/List_of_fastest-selling_albums#United_States).

Swift's departure from Big Machine resulted in a [dispute over the ownership of her first six albums](/wiki/Taylor_Swift_masters_dispute) in 2019, which resulted in her re-recording four albums—*[Fearless (Taylor's Version)](/wiki/Fearless_(Taylor%27s_Version))* (2021), *[Red (Taylor's Version)](/wiki/Red_(Taylor%27s_Version))* (2021), *[Speak Now (Taylor's Version)](/wiki/Speak_Now_(Taylor%27s_Version))* (2023), and *[1989 (Taylor's Version)](/wiki/1989_(Taylor%27s_Version))* (2023)—all of which reached number one in Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, and the United States. *Fearless (Taylor's Version)* was the first re-recorded album to top the *Billboard* 200, and *1989 (Taylor's Version)* sold over a million US first-week copies. Swift holds the record for the [most annual best-selling albums in the United States](/wiki/List_of_best-selling_albums_by_year_in_the_United_States)—nine times: *Fearless* in 2009, *1989* in 2014, *Reputation* in 2017, *Lover* in 2019, *Folklore* in 2020, *Midnights* in 2022, *1989 (Taylor's Version)* in 2023, *The Tortured Poets Department* in 2024, and *The Life of a Showgirl* in 2025.

# Basibasy mine

## Infobox

- **Location:** Basibasy
- **Country:** Madagascar
- **Coordinates:** 22°9′S 43°36′E / 22.150°S 43.600°E / -22.150; 43.600
- **Products:** Titanium

The **Basibasy mine** is one of the largest [titanium](/wiki/Titanium) mines in [Madagascar](/wiki/Madagascar). The mine is located in [Basibasy](/wiki/Basibasy), [Atsimo-Andrefana](/wiki/Atsimo-Andrefana). The mine has reserves amounting to 446 million tonnes of ore grading 5.5% [titanium](/wiki/Titanium).

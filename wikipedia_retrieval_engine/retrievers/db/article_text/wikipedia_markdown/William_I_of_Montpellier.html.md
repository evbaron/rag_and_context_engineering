# William I of Montpellier

## Infobox

- **Tenure:** 985–1025
- **Successor:** William II of Montpellier
- **House:** Guilhem
- **Religion:** Roman Catholicism

**William I** (or in [Occitan](/wiki/Occitan_language): **Guilhem I**) was the founder of a dynasty which bears his name, the Guilhems, [Lords of Montpellier](/wiki/Lords_of_Montpellier). He received his [fief](/wiki/Fief) (a [manor](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Manor)) of *Monspestularius* ([Montpellier](/wiki/Montpellier)) on 26 November 985 from Bernard, Count of Mauguio, with the permission of Ricuin, Bishop of Maguelone.

Without descendants after his death, his nephew Guilhem II of Montpellier succeeded him.

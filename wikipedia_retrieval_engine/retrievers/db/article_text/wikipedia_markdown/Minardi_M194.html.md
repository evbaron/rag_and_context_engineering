The **Minardi M194** was a [Formula One](/wiki/Formula_One) car designed by [Aldo Costa](/wiki/Aldo_Costa) and [Gustav Brunner](/wiki/Gustav_Brunner) and built by [Minardi](/wiki/Minardi) for the [1994 season](/wiki/1994_Formula_One_season). It was introduced at that year's [Canadian Grand Prix](/wiki/1994_Canadian_Grand_Prix) to replace the [Minardi M193](/wiki/Minardi_M193)B. The car was again powered by the [Ford](/wiki/Cosworth#Other_Formula_One_engines) HBD [V8 engine](/wiki/V8_engine).

Minardi team drivers during 1994 were [Pierluigi Martini](/wiki/Pierluigi_Martini) and former Grand Prix winner [Michele Alboreto](/wiki/Michele_Alboreto). The team's test driver was [Luca Badoer](/wiki/Luca_Badoer).

The M194 would only score two points during its 11 races, both when Martini finished in 5th place at the car's second race, the [French Grand Prix](/wiki/1994_French_Grand_Prix). The team in fact scored more points in 1994 (3 points) using the M193B than with the M194. The combined 5 point total saw Minardi only finish tenth in the Constructors' Championship.

The M194 was replaced for the [1995](/wiki/1995_Formula_One_World_Championship) season by the [Minardi M195](/wiki/Minardi_M195). Alboreto retired and was replaced by test driver Badoer.

## Livery

The M194 had a blue, orange and white livery, with main sponsorship from [Lucchini](/wiki/Lucchini_RS) and Beta Tools.

During the [British Grand Prix](/wiki/1994_British_Grand_Prix), the car ran with the words "Italia IN - Ireland OUT", in response to the elimination of [Ireland](/wiki/Republic_of_Ireland_national_football_team) from the [1994 FIFA World Cup](/wiki/1994_FIFA_World_Cup) and the provocation from [Eddie Jordan](/wiki/Eddie_Jordan) in the previous race.

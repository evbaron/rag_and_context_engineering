Not to be confused with *[Green Lantern: New Guardians](/wiki/Green_Lantern:_New_Guardians)*.

The **New Guardians** are a [DC Comics](/wiki/DC_Comics) [superhero team](/wiki/Superhero_team) featured in the short-lived eponymous series *The New Guardians*. It was a spin-off from the *[Millennium](/wiki/Millennium_(comics))* event and ran for twelve issues, from 1988 through 1989, before being canceled. The characters first appeared in *Millennium* #1, (January 1988), written by [Steve Engelhart](/wiki/Steve_Engelhart) and drawn by [Joe Staton](/wiki/Joe_Staton). The series has since gained infamy among comic fans due to its poor writing and second issue, which features "[Snowflame](/wiki/Snowflame)", a [supervillain](/wiki/Supervillain) who gained powers from the use of cocaine.

## History

The New Guardians, who were also initially known as the Chosen, were a group of people selected by a [Guardian of the Universe](/wiki/Guardians_of_the_Universe) named **Herupa Hando Hu** and a [Zamaron](/wiki/Zamaron) named **Nadia Safir** in the [DC Comics](/wiki/DC_Comics) crossover *[Millennium](/wiki/Millennium_(comics))*. These "Chosen" were then given powers by their selectors.

Members of the Chosen were selected from several [nationalities](/wiki/Nationalities) to form a superhero group representative of the entire [human race](/wiki/Human).

### Original members

- **[Betty Clawman](/wiki/Betty_Clawman)** - Formerly an Australian woman, now a disembodied cosmic force with ill-defined abilities, residing in the aboriginal [Dreamtime](/wiki/Dreamtime).
- **[Extraño](/wiki/Extra%C3%B1o)** - Peruvian male, the team's resident magician was also one of the first obviously homosexual characters in comics (due to the publication standards of the time, the character's homosexuality could only be implied), and was one of the first to reveal that he was [HIV](/wiki/HIV)-positive.
- **[Floronic Man](/wiki/Floronic_Man)** - Formerly American male, who became a plant-human creature with a variety of nature-related powers. The character has since been seen in the pages of the *[Son of Vulcan](/wiki/Vulcan_(DC_Comics))* mini-series where he returned to acting as a villain.
- **[Gloss](/wiki/Gloss_(character))** - A Chinese woman who channels the mystic [Dragon Lines of the Earth](/wiki/Feng_shui), and thus sports an array of Earth-related powers. She later joined the [Global Guardians](/wiki/Global_Guardians), where she remained a member until she was killed by [Prometheus](/wiki/Prometheus_(DC_Comics)).
- **[Harbinger](/wiki/Harbinger_(DC_Comics))** - Agent of the [Monitor](/wiki/Monitor_(Mar_Novu)) in *[Crisis on Infinite Earths](/wiki/Crisis_on_Infinite_Earths)*, she possessed the powers of flight, self-replication and energy blasts. Keeper of the histories of the [multiverse](/wiki/Multiverse_(DC_Comics)), Harbinger died in the pages of *[Superman/Batman](/wiki/Superman/Batman)*. [Donna Troy](/wiki/Donna_Troy) has since taken up her mantle.
- **[Jet](/wiki/Jet_(DC_Comics))** - An English girl who could manipulate electromagnetic fields to a variety of effects. She was infected with HIV after being bitten by The Hemogoblin, a vampire with [AIDS](/wiki/AIDS). After apparently giving her life to fend off an alien [invasion](/wiki/Invasion!_(DC_Comics)), Jet is now alive again and leading the Global Guardians.
- **Ram** - Formerly a Japanese man, now a being of silicon and electronics, Ram was physically durable and also had the ability to communicate with electrical equipment over great distances (such as satellites in orbit). He was reportedly killed during a battle in [Roulette](/wiki/Roulette_(DC_Comics))'s casino.
- **[Thomas Kalmaku](/wiki/Thomas_Kalmaku)** - An [Inuk](/wiki/Inuk) friend of [Hal Jordan](/wiki/Hal_Jordan)'s, Tom originally turned down the Guardian's offer of advancement, but later developed the super power to "bring out the best in people". He subsequently left the team and has shown no further signs of possessing such powers.

## Green Lantern: New Guardians

Main article: [Green Lantern: New Guardians](/wiki/Green_Lantern:_New_Guardians)

*Green Lantern: New Guardians* is an [American comic book](/wiki/American_comic_book) [series](/wiki/Ongoing_series) written by [Tony Bedard](/wiki/Tony_Bedard) with art by Tyler Kirkham and Batt and published by [DC Comics](/wiki/DC_Comics).

The team is made up of a representative of each of the Corps that tap into a particular portion of the emotional spectrum; its members are [Kyle Rayner](/wiki/Kyle_Rayner) ([Green Lantern Corps](/wiki/Green_Lantern_Corps)), [Arkillo](/wiki/Arkillo) ([Sinestro Corps](/wiki/Sinestro_Corps)), [Bleez](/wiki/Bleez) ([Red Lantern Corps](/wiki/Red_Lantern_Corps)), Glomulus ([Orange Lantern Corps](/wiki/Orange_Lantern_Corps)), Munk ([Indigo Tribe](/wiki/Indigo_Tribe)), [Saint Walker](/wiki/Saint_Walker) ([Blue Lantern Corps](/wiki/Blue_Lantern_Corps)), and [Fatality](/wiki/Fatality_(character)) ([Star Sapphires](/wiki/Star_Sapphire_(comics))).

An earlier version of the team, consisting of [Hal Jordan](/wiki/Hal_Jordan), [Carol Ferris](/wiki/Carol_Ferris), Sinestro, [Atrocitus](/wiki/Atrocitus), [Larfleeze](/wiki/Larfleeze), Saint Walker, and [Indigo-1](/wiki/Indigo_Tribe), was seen during the *[Brightest Day](/wiki/Brightest_Day)* story arc in *Green Lantern*.

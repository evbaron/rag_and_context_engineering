# List of United States cities by population

"List of largest cities in the United States" redirects here. For a list of largest cities by area, see [List of United States cities by area](/wiki/List_of_United_States_cities_by_area).

This is a list of the most populous [municipal corporations](/wiki/Municipal_corporation) of the [United States](/wiki/United_States). As defined by the [United States Census Bureau](/wiki/United_States_Census_Bureau), an [incorporated place](/wiki/Place_(United_States_Census_Bureau)#Incorporated_place) includes [cities](/wiki/City), [towns](/wiki/Town), [villages](/wiki/Village_(United_States)), [boroughs](/wiki/Borough_(United_States)), and [municipalities](/wiki/Municipality). A few exceptional [census-designated places](/wiki/Census-designated_place) (CDPs) are also included in the Census Bureau's listing of incorporated places. [Consolidated city-counties](/wiki/Consolidated_city-county) represent a distinct type of government that includes the entire population of a county, or county equivalent. Some consolidated city-counties, however, include multiple incorporated places. This list presents only the portion of such consolidated city-counties that are not a part of another incorporated place.

This list refers only to the population of individual municipalities within their [defined limits](/wiki/City_limits); the populations of other municipalities considered [suburbs](/wiki/Suburb) of a central city are listed separately, and unincorporated areas within [urban agglomerations](/wiki/Urban_area) are not included. Therefore, a different ranking is evident when considering [U.S. urban areas](/wiki/List_of_United_States_urban_areas) or [metropolitan areas](/wiki/Metropolitan_statistical_area).

## Census-designated places

The following table lists U.S. [census-designated places](/wiki/Census-designated_place) (CDPs) with populations of at least 100,000 according to the 2020 census. A CDP is a [concentration of population](/wiki/Place_(United_States_Census_Bureau)) identified by the United States Census Bureau for statistical purposes. CDPs are delineated for each decennial census as the statistical counterparts of [incorporated places](/wiki/Incorporated_place) such as [cities](/wiki/City), [towns](/wiki/Town) and [villages](/wiki/Village_(United_States)). CDPs are populated areas that lack separate municipal government, but which otherwise physically resemble incorporated places. Unlike the incorporated cities in the main list, the US Census Bureau does not release annual population estimates for CDPs.

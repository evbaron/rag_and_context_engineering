**James Kent Trotter** (born 29 July 1999) is a Japanese tennis player.
He has a career high [ATP](/wiki/Association_of_Tennis_Professionals) singles ranking of world No. 170 achieved on 21 April 2025 and a doubles ranking of No. 142 achieved on 9 September 2024.

Trotter has won two singles and six [ATP Challenger](/wiki/ATP_Challenger_Tour) doubles titles.

## Professional career

He won the Challenger doubles titles at the [2023 Columbus Challenger](/wiki/2023_Columbus_Challenger) with [Robert Cash](/wiki/Robert_Cash), at the [2024 Cleveland Open](/wiki/2024_Cleveland_Open) and in [Cherbourg](/wiki/2024_Challenger_La_Manche) with [George Goldhoff](/wiki/George_Goldhoff), at the [2024 Tyler Tennis Championships](/wiki/2024_Tyler_Tennis_Championships) and again in [Columbus](/wiki/2024_Columbus_Challenger) with [Hans Hach Verdugo](/wiki/Hans_Hach_Verdugo).
At the same tournament in [Tyler](/wiki/2024_Tyler_Tennis_Championships_%E2%80%93_Singles), Texas, he also won his first ATP Challenger singles title defeating [Brandon Holt](/wiki/Brandon_Holt) in the final.

## College career

Trotter played college [tennis](/wiki/Ohio_State_Buckeyes_men%27s_tennis) at [Ohio State](/wiki/Ohio_State_University), where he won the NCAA Division I tennis men's doubles championship in 2023.

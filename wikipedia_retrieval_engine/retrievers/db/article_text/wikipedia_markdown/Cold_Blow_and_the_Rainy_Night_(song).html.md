# Cold Blow and the Rainy Night (song)

## Infobox

- **Genre:** English folk song

"**Cold Blow and a Rainy Night"** (also known as "**Cold Haily Windy Night", Let Me In This Ae Nicht"**, or **"The Laird o’ Windy Wa's**") is an English folk song which has been recorded by numerous musicians and musical groups, including James Bowie (Blind Jimmie), [Jeannie Robertson](/wiki/Jeannie_Robertson), [Steeleye Span](/wiki/Steeleye_Span), [Martin Carthy](/wiki/Martin_Carthy), [Planxty](/wiki/Planxty), and the Exiles.

It is a typical "night-visiting song".

The song has been documented through [EFDSS](/wiki/EFDSS) and Mainly Norfolk. The song has been recorded by [Jon Boden](/wiki/Jon_Boden) and [Oli Steadman](/wiki/Oli_Steadman) for inclusion in their respective lists of daily folk songs "[A Folk Song A Day](/wiki/A_Folk_Song_A_Day)" and "365 Days Of Folk".

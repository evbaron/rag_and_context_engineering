**Lake Monona** ([/mɪˈnoʊnə/](/wiki/Help:IPA/English) [*mih-NOH-nə*](/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key)) is a [freshwater](/wiki/Freshwater) drainage [lake](/wiki/Lake) in [Dane County, Wisconsin](/wiki/Dane_County,_Wisconsin), surrounded on three sides by the city of [Madison, Wisconsin](/wiki/Madison,_Wisconsin), and on the south east side by the city of [Monona, Wisconsin](/wiki/Monona,_Wisconsin). It is the second-largest of a chain of four lakes along the [Yahara River](/wiki/Yahara_River) (also including [Mendota](/wiki/Lake_Mendota), [Kegonsa](/wiki/Lake_Kegonsa), and [Waubesa](/wiki/Lake_Waubesa)) in the area and forms the south shore of the [isthmus](/wiki/Madison_Isthmus) that forms downtown Madison. The name 'Monona' is a word believed to mean 'beautiful', although the lake was originally named by the [Ho-Chunk (Winnebago)](/wiki/Ho-Chunk) 'Tchee-ho-bo-kee-xa-te-la' or 'Teepee Lake'.

## Description

View of Lake Monona from Monona Terrace

Lake Monona rests at [43°4′9″N 89°21′34″W / 43.06917°N 89.35944°W / 43.06917; -89.35944](https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Lake_Monona&params=43_4_9_N_89_21_34_W_region:US_type:waterbody). It measures 3,274 [acres](/wiki/Acre) (13.25 km2), has a mean depth of 27 ft (8.2 m) and a maximum depth of 74 ft (23 m). Its volume is approximately 28 billion US gal (110 million m3) and it has 13 miles (21 km) of shoreline, about 40% of which is publicly owned. The elevation of the lake is 845', regulated by [locks](/wiki/Lock_(water_navigation)) at the mouth of the Yahara River at Lake Mendota. Monona is fed by three [tributaries](/wiki/Tributary): the Yahara River (from [Lake Mendota](/wiki/Lake_Mendota)), Starkweather Creek, and [Wingra Creek](/wiki/Wingra_Creek). Lake Monona is typically frozen for 107 days a year, give or take 10 days depending on the season. Access to the lake is by boat ramp.

Monona is home to many species of fish and is a popular lake for fishing. Sport fish species include [bluegill](/wiki/Bluegill), [lake sturgeon](/wiki/Lake_sturgeon), [largemouth bass](/wiki/Largemouth_bass), [smallmouth bass](/wiki/Smallmouth_bass), [muskellunge](/wiki/Muskellunge) (muskie), [northern pike](/wiki/Northern_pike), and [walleye](/wiki/Walleye).

Twenty-six-year-old soul singer [Otis Redding](/wiki/Otis_Redding) died when his plane crashed in Lake Monona on December 10, 1967, during a storm en route to a concert in Madison. The pilot, who was Redding's manager, and four out of the five members of the [Bar-Kays](/wiki/Bar-Kays) (then Otis's backup band) who were on the plane, also died, with the sole survivor being trumpeter [Ben Cauley](/wiki/Ben_Cauley).

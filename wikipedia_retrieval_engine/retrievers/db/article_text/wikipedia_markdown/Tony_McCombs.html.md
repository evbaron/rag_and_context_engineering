**Tony McCombs** (born August 24, 1974) is an American former professional [football](/wiki/American_football) player who was a [linebacker](/wiki/Linebacker) in the [National Football League](/wiki/National_Football_League) (NFL). He played [college football](/wiki/College_football) for the [Eastern Kentucky Colonels](/wiki/Eastern_Kentucky_Colonels_football) and was selected in the sixth round of the [1997 NFL draft](/wiki/1997_NFL_draft). He played for the [Arizona Cardinals](/wiki/Arizona_Cardinals) from 1997 to 1998.

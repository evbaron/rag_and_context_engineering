**Matthew Steven LeBlanc** ([/ləˈblɑːŋk/](/wiki/Help:IPA/English) [*lə-BLAHNK*](/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key); born July 25, 1967) is an American actor. He gained global recognition with his portrayal of [Joey Tribbiani](/wiki/Joey_Tribbiani) in the [NBC](/wiki/NBC) sitcom *[Friends](/wiki/Friends)* (1994–2004), and in its [spin-off](/wiki/Spin-off_(media)) series *[Joey](/wiki/Joey_(TV_series))* (2004–2006). For his work on *Friends*, LeBlanc received three nominations at the [Primetime Emmy Awards](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Awards). He has also starred as a fictionalized version of himself in *[Episodes](/wiki/Episodes_(TV_series))* (2011–2017), for which he won a [Golden Globe Award](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award) and received four additional Emmy Award nominations. He co-hosted [*Top Gear*](/wiki/Top_Gear_(2002_TV_series)) from 2016 to 2019. From 2016 to 2020, he played patriarch Adam Burns in the CBS sitcom *[Man with a Plan](/wiki/Man_with_a_Plan_(TV_series))*.

## Early life

Matthew Steven LeBlanc was born at [Newton-Wellesley Hospital](/wiki/Newton-Wellesley_Hospital) in [Newton, Massachusetts](/wiki/Newton,_Massachusetts). His mother, Patricia ([née](/wiki/Birth_name#Maiden_and_married_names) Di Cillo), was an office manager; his father, Paul LeBlanc, was a mechanic and a veteran of the [Vietnam War](/wiki/Vietnam_War). He has a brother named Justin LeBlanc. His father is of French-Canadian descent and his mother is of Italian ancestry, the daughter of immigrants from [Arce, Lazio](/wiki/Arce,_Lazio). He attended [Newton North High School](/wiki/Newton_North_High_School), where he graduated in the same year as future comedian [Louis C.K.](/wiki/Louis_C.K.) After high school he attended college at [Wentworth Institute of Technology](/wiki/Wentworth_Institute_of_Technology) in Boston. He dropped out shortly after starting his second semester.

LeBlanc moved to New York at the age of 17 to pursue a career in modeling, but he was told he was too short to be in the industry. His acting career began after a woman invited him to accompany her to an audition, where he ended up getting signed by her manager. Although he had booked commercials and television and film roles before *Friends*, he was reportedly down to his last eleven dollars before landing the role of Joey Tribbiani.

## Career

### 1987–1994: Early career

LeBlanc at the [47th Primetime Emmy Awards](/wiki/47th_Primetime_Emmy_Awards), 1995

LeBlanc first appeared in a 1987 [Heinz Tomato Ketchup](/wiki/Heinz_Tomato_Ketchup) commercial. In 1988, he starred in the television drama *[TV 101](/wiki/TV_101)* for one season. In 1991, he had a recurring role on the hit [Fox](/wiki/Fox_(network)) sitcom *[Married... with Children](/wiki/Married..._with_Children)*. He played Vinnie Verducci, a family friend of protagonist [Al Bundy](/wiki/Al_Bundy) ([Ed O'Neill](/wiki/Ed_O%27Neill)) who briefly dates his daughter, [Kelly](/wiki/Kelly_Bundy) ([Christina Applegate](/wiki/Christina_Applegate)). He also guest-starred in the first season of *[Red Shoe Diaries](/wiki/Red_Shoe_Diaries)*. LeBlanc starred in two short-lived *Married... with Children* spin-offs: *[Top of the Heap](/wiki/Top_of_the_Heap)* (1991) and *[Vinnie & Bobby](/wiki/Vinnie_%26_Bobby)* (1992).

He appeared in two [Bon Jovi](/wiki/Bon_Jovi) music videos: "[Miracle](/wiki/Miracle_(Jon_Bon_Jovi_song))", from the *[Young Guns II](/wiki/Young_Guns_II)* soundtrack in 1990, and "[Say It Isn't So](/wiki/Say_It_Isn%27t_So_(Bon_Jovi_song))" in 2000. He also appeared in videos for [Alanis Morissette](/wiki/Alanis_Morissette)'s single "[Walk Away](/wiki/Walk_Away_(Alanis_Morissette_song))", [Tom Petty & The Heartbreakers](/wiki/Tom_Petty_%26_The_Heartbreakers)'s "[Into The Great Wide Open](/wiki/Into_The_Great_Wide_Open)" and [Bob Seger](/wiki/Bob_Seger)'s "[Night Moves](/wiki/Night_Moves_(Bob_Seger_song))".

### 1994–2006: *Friends*, *Joey* and film roles

LeBlanc found success as the dimwitted but lovable [Joey Tribbiani](/wiki/Joey_Tribbiani) on *[Friends](/wiki/Friends)*; he played this character for 12 years—10 seasons of *Friends* and two seasons of [*Joey*](/wiki/Joey_(TV_series)). *Friends* was wildly successful, and LeBlanc (along with co-stars [Jennifer Aniston](/wiki/Jennifer_Aniston), [Courteney Cox](/wiki/Courteney_Cox), [Lisa Kudrow](/wiki/Lisa_Kudrow), [Matthew Perry](/wiki/Matthew_Perry), and [David Schwimmer](/wiki/David_Schwimmer)) gained wide recognition among viewers. This ensemble situation comedy became a major hit for NBC, airing on Thursday nights for ten years.

For his performance, LeBlanc received three [Primetime Emmy Award](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award) nominations, three [Golden Globe](/wiki/Golden_Globe) award nominations, and one [Screen Actors Guild Award](/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild_Award) nomination. During this time he also appeared in the films *[Lookin' Italian](/wiki/Lookin%27_Italian)* (1994), *[Ed](/wiki/Ed_(film))* (1996), *[Lost in Space](/wiki/Lost_in_Space_(film))* (1998), *[Charlie's Angels](/wiki/Charlie%27s_Angels_(2000_film))* (2000), and its sequel, *[Charlie's Angels: Full Throttle](/wiki/Charlie%27s_Angels:_Full_Throttle)* (2003).

Following the cancellation of *Joey*, LeBlanc announced that he would be taking a one-year hiatus from acting on television, which eventually turned into five years.

### 2011–present: Career revival

LeBlanc at the Golden Globes with [Meryl Streep](/wiki/Meryl_Streep), 2012

From 2011, LeBlanc appeared as a fictionalized version of himself in [*Episodes*](/wiki/Episodes_(TV_series)), a television series about an American remake of a fictional British television series. The series is written by *Friends* co-creator [David Crane](/wiki/David_Crane_(producer)) and his partner [Jeffrey Klarik](/wiki/Jeffrey_Klarik). The role was nominated for a [Primetime Emmy Award](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Awards) four times and, at the [69th Golden Globe Awards](/wiki/69th_Golden_Globe_Awards) in 2012, LeBlanc was awarded [Best Actor – Television Series Musical or Comedy](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Actor_%E2%80%93_Television_Series_Musical_or_Comedy) of 2011.

In February 2012, LeBlanc appeared in the second episode of the eighteenth series of [*Top Gear*](/wiki/Top_Gear_(2002_TV_series)), where he set the fastest lap time in the "[Star in a Reasonably Priced Car](/wiki/Reasonably_Priced_Car)" segment in a [Kia Cee'd](/wiki/Kia_Ceed#First_generation_(ED;_2006)). Lapping at 1:42.1, he beat the show's previous record-holder, [Rowan Atkinson](/wiki/Rowan_Atkinson), by 0.1 seconds. He later appeared in the fourth episode of the nineteenth series to race the [new Kia Cee'd](/wiki/Kia_Ceed#Second_generation_(JD;_2012)), with which he beat his previous time.

In February 2016, the [BBC](/wiki/BBC_America) announced LeBlanc had signed on to become one of the new *Top Gear* hosts, signing a two-year deal later that year. In May 2018, he announced that he was leaving the series in order to spend more time with his family and friends in the US. He and [Rory Reid](/wiki/Rory_Reid_(journalist)) were replaced by new co-hosts [Paddy McGuinness](/wiki/Paddy_McGuinness) and [Andrew Flintoff](/wiki/Andrew_Flintoff) for the twenty-seventh series in June 2019.

LeBlanc played the lead role in the [CBS](/wiki/CBS) sitcom *[Man with a Plan](/wiki/Man_with_a_Plan_(TV_series))*, which began airing in 2016 until it was cancelled in 2020.

## Personal life

LeBlanc has an extensive interest in cars, and worked as a carpenter before acting. In the mid-1990s, he dated actress [Kate Hudson](/wiki/Kate_Hudson). He married Melissa McKnight, a British-born American model and a divorced single mother of two children, in May 2003. They had been introduced to each other in 1997 by McKnight's friend, Kelly Phillips (wife of actor [Lou Diamond Phillips](/wiki/Lou_Diamond_Phillips)). LeBlanc proposed to her a year later. Their daughter, born in 2004, began suffering seizures at eight months old. By the time she was two years old, the condition, thought to be due to cortical [dysplasia](/wiki/Dysplasia), had mostly subsided. LeBlanc and McKnight divorced in October 2006, citing [irreconcilable differences](/wiki/Irreconcilable_differences).

LeBlanc met actress [Andrea Anders](/wiki/Andrea_Anders) in 2004, while she was co-starring as his friend and eventual love interest on *[Joey](/wiki/Joey_(sitcom))*. The pair eventually embarked on a relationship, which was confirmed in 2006 following LeBlanc's divorce from his wife Melissa McKnight. After over eight years as a couple, LeBlanc announced at the start of 2015 that he and Anders had separated several months earlier.

LeBlanc started getting gray hair around the second season of *Friends* and dyed his hair throughout the course of the series. Once the spin-off series *Joey* ended, he stopped dyeing his hair.

LeBlanc primarily resides in [Pacific Palisades, Los Angeles](/wiki/Pacific_Palisades,_Los_Angeles).

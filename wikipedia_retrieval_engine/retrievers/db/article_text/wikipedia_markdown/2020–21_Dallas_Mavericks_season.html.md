The **2020–21 Dallas Mavericks season** was the 41st season of the franchise in the [National Basketball Association](/wiki/National_Basketball_Association) (NBA). The Mavericks clinched the Southwest division for the first time since the [2009-10 season](/wiki/2009%E2%80%9310_Dallas_Mavericks_season) following a 110–90 victory over the [Cleveland Cavaliers](/wiki/2020-21_Cleveland_Cavaliers_season) on May 7, 2021. However, the Mavericks lost in the first round to the [Los Angeles Clippers](/wiki/2020-21_Los_Angeles_Clippers_season) for the second consecutive season in seven games.

Following the season on June 17, 2021, long time head coach [Rick Carlisle](/wiki/Rick_Carlisle) resigned from his position as head coach after 13 seasons and leading the team to a championship in [2011](/wiki/2011_NBA_Finals).

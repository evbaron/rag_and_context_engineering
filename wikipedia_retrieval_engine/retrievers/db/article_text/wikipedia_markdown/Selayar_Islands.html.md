# Selayar Islands

**Selayar** or **Saleyer** (Indonesian: *Kabupaten Selayar*, Dutch: *Saleijer*), is an [archipelago](/wiki/Archipelago) of [South Sulawesi](/wiki/South_Sulawesi) province, [Indonesia](/wiki/Indonesia). It lies in the [Flores Sea](/wiki/Flores_Sea), between [Sulawesi](/wiki/Sulawesi) and [Flores](/wiki/Flores), around 150 km southeast of the major city of [Makassar](/wiki/Makassar). *Kabupaten Selayar* is the [Regency](/wiki/Selayar_Islands_Regency), covering 1,357 km2 with a population of about 139,145 in mid 2022. There are 73 islands, the main one being [Selayar Island](/wiki/Selayar_Island). To the east lies *Pulau* (Island) Kalaotoa and *Pulau* Karompa Lompo (in [Sulawesi Tenggara](/wiki/Sulawesi_Tenggara) province), and to the west lies *Kepulauan Sabalana* (the [Sabalana Islands](/wiki/Sabalana_Islands)). It is a [biodiverse](/wiki/Biodiverse) [diving](/wiki/Underwater_diving) site.

The [Selayar Strait](/wiki/Selayar_Strait) (*Selat Selayar*) is more than 100 [fathoms](/wiki/Fathoms) deep and, with a strong current, is dangerous for native ships to navigate. The [strata](/wiki/Stratum) of Selayar Island are all [sedimentary](/wiki/Sedimentary) rocks: coraline [limestone](/wiki/Limestone), occasionally [sandstone](/wiki/Sandstone); everywhere, except in the north and north-west, covered by a fertile soil. The [watershed](/wiki/Drainage_divide) is a chain running throughout the island from N. to S., reaching in Bontona Haru 5840 ft.,\ and sloping steeply to the east coast.

## Demographics

Main article: Selayar people

The Selayar Islands population, mainly a mixture of Selayar, [Makassar](/wiki/Makassar_people), [Bugis](/wiki/Bugis_people), [Bajau](/wiki/Bajau_people) peoples, and the migrants of [Luwu](/wiki/Luwu) and [Butonese](/wiki/Butonese_people) peoples, is estimated in mid 2021 at 89,147 on the main island and 48,827 on the islands to the southeast of it. They speak the several [Makassaric languages](/wiki/Makassaric_languages) (mainly [Selayar](/wiki/Selayar_language) and [Makassar](/wiki/Makassar_language)) and are for the most part nominally [Muslims](/wiki/Muslim) (though many non-Muslim customs survive) and support themselves by agriculture, fishing, seafaring, trade, the preparation of salt (on the south coast) and weaving. Raw and prepared cotton, tobacco, [trepang](/wiki/Holothuroidea), tortoise-shell, coconuts and coconut oil, and salt are exported. There is frequent movement between the area and other parts of [Sulawesi](/wiki/Sulawesi) as well as to other parts Indonesia. For that reason, and also because of its excellent horses and numerous [water buffaloes](/wiki/Domestic_buffalo), the Salayar Islands are often compared with the [Kangean Islands](/wiki/Kangean_Islands) in [Madura](/wiki/Madura), being of the same importance to Sulawesi as is Madura to [Java](/wiki/Java_(island)).

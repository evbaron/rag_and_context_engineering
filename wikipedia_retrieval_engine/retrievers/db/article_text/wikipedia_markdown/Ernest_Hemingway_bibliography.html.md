# Ernest Hemingway bibliography

Hemingway writing in [Kenya](/wiki/Kenya_Colony), 1953

[Ernest Hemingway](/wiki/Ernest_Hemingway) (1899–1961) was an American novelist, short-story writer, journalist, and sportsman. His economical and understated style—which he termed the [iceberg theory](/wiki/Iceberg_theory)—had a strong influence on 20th-century fiction. Many of his works are considered classics of [American literature](/wiki/American_literature).

Hemingway produced most of his work between the mid-1920s and the mid-1950s, and he was awarded the [1954 Nobel Prize in Literature](/wiki/1954_Nobel_Prize_in_Literature). He published seven novels, six short-story collections, and two nonfiction works.

Three of his novels, four short-story collections, and three nonfiction works were published posthumously.

**Neil Christie Newbon** (born 14 August 1978) is an English actor known for voicing Elijah Kamski and Gavin Reed in *[Detroit: Become Human](/wiki/Detroit:_Become_Human)*, Nicholai Ginovaef and Karl Heisenberg in *[Resident Evil 3](/wiki/Resident_Evil_3_(2020_video_game))* (2020) and *[Resident Evil Village](/wiki/Resident_Evil_Village)* respectively, Zeon in *[Xenoblade Chronicles 3](/wiki/Xenoblade_Chronicles_3)* and [Astarion](/wiki/Astarion) in *[Baldur's Gate 3](/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_3)*, for which he won the [Game Award for Best Performance](/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2023).

## Early life

Neil Christie Newbon was born in [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham) on 14 August 1978, the son of sports presenter [Gary Newbon](/wiki/Gary_Newbon). He is of Scottish descent on his mother's side and Belarusian descent on his father's side. His sister Claire is a journalist, while his twin brother Laurence is a cameraman. He played football as a teenager, usually as a [goalkeeper](/wiki/Goalkeeper_(association_football)).

## Career

In 1998, Newbon appeared in the [BBC Two](/wiki/BBC_Two) comedy series *[Goodness Gracious Me](/wiki/Goodness_Gracious_Me_(TV_series))* as James ('Jamms'), a waiter in the show's notable "Going for an English" sketch. He has appeared in numerous films. He voiced a tank driver in *[Jackboots on Whitehall](/wiki/Jackboots_on_Whitehall)*. He starred in the music videos for the Now Voyager Mix of "[You Got the Love](/wiki/You_Got_the_Love#Music_video)" by [The Source](/wiki/The_Source_(musician)) and [Candi Staton](/wiki/Candi_Staton) in 2006 and "[Kingdom of Rust](/wiki/Kingdom_of_Rust_(song))" by [Doves](/wiki/Doves_(band)) in 2009. In 2012, he joined the cast of the [Channel 4](/wiki/Channel_4) soap opera *[Hollyoaks](/wiki/Hollyoaks)* as [Simon Walker](/wiki/Simon_Walker_(Hollyoaks)).

Since 2012, Newbon has provided the voice and [motion capture](/wiki/Motion_capture_acting) for numerous video game characters, including Elijah Kamski and Gavin Reed in *[Detroit: Become Human](/wiki/Detroit:_Become_Human)*, Nicholai Ginovaef in *[Resident Evil 3](/wiki/Resident_Evil_3_(2020_video_game))*, Karl Heisenberg in *[Resident Evil Village](/wiki/Resident_Evil_Village)* and [Astarion](/wiki/Astarion) in *[Baldur's Gate 3](/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_3)*. For his performance as Astarion, he won Best Performance at the [2023 Game Awards](/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2023) as well as the [Golden Joystick](/wiki/Golden_Joystick_Awards) for Best Supporting Performer.

Newbon is a co-founder of the training facility Performance Captured Academy, which offers courses on motion capture for aspiring actors.

## Personal life

Newbon married film director China Moo-Young in October 2012, and they had one daughter together before divorcing in 2015. He confirmed that he was in a relationship with his Performance Captured Academy co-founder Saleta Losada in 2018, and they were married in June 2023.

Newbon has trained in various martial arts for over 16 years, including [kung fu](/wiki/Kung_fu) (in the styles [Crane](/wiki/Fujian_White_Crane), [Shaolin](/wiki/Shaolin_kung_fu), [Snake](/wiki/Snake_kung_fu), and [Tiger](/wiki/Fu_Jow_Pai)), [kickboxing](/wiki/Kickboxing), [judo](/wiki/Judo), and [Wadō-ryū karate](/wiki/Wad%C5%8D-ry%C5%AB).

**Jordan William Hunter Luck** [MNZM](/wiki/Member_of_the_New_Zealand_Order_of_Merit) (born 15 October 1961) is the former lead singer and songwriter of the New Zealand rock band [the Exponents](/wiki/The_Exponents). He was born in [Vanderhoof](/wiki/Vanderhoof,_British_Columbia) in the province of [British Columbia](/wiki/British_Columbia), Canada. His family moved to [Tokarahi](/wiki/Tokarahi) (near [Oamaru](/wiki/Oamaru)) and later moved to [Geraldine](/wiki/Geraldine,_New_Zealand) where he grew up. He attended [University of Canterbury](/wiki/University_of_Canterbury) and [College House](/wiki/College_House_(University_of_Canterbury)). He is now in a band called The Jordan Luck Band.

At the 2007 [APRA Silver Scroll](/wiki/APRA_Silver_Scroll) Awards on 18 September, Luck was named as the first inductee to the [New Zealand Music Hall of Fame](/wiki/New_Zealand_Music_Hall_of_Fame). He was appointed a [Member of the New Zealand Order of Merit](/wiki/New_Zealand_Order_of_Merit) for services to music in the [2012 Queen's Birthday and Diamond Jubilee Honours](/wiki/2012_Birthday_Honours_(New_Zealand)).

In 2019, Luck would cover Al Park's "I Walked Away" for the covers collection *Better Already - The Songs Of Al Park*. Park, a singer-songwriter sometimes credited as the father figure for the 'Lyttelton Sound' and the first guy to bring punk music to [Otautahi](/wiki/Christchurch), had featured in the video for "[Victoria](/wiki/Victoria_(Dance_Exponents_song))", a top ten hit in 1982 for Luck and his band The Dance Exponents.

Also in 2019, Luck would tour New Zealand with The Jordan Luck Band, starting off at Peach & Porker in [Te Awamutu](/wiki/Te_Awamutu) on 23 February 2019 and ending the tour in Christchurch on 22 June 2019.

# Billboard year-end top 30 singles of 1954

[Kitty Kallen](/wiki/Kitty_Kallen) had two songs in the year-end top 30, including "[Little Things Mean a Lot](/wiki/Little_Things_Mean_a_Lot)", the number one song of 1954.

This is a list of *[Billboard](/wiki/Billboard_(magazine))* magazine's Top 30 popular songs of [1954](/wiki/1954_in_music) according to retail sales.

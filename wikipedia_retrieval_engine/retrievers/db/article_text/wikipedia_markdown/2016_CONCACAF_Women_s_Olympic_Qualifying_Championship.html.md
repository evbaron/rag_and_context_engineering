The **2016 CONCACAF Women's Olympic Qualifying Championship** was the 4th edition of the [CONCACAF Women's Olympic Qualifying Tournament](/wiki/CONCACAF_Women%27s_Olympic_Qualifying_Tournament), the quadrennial international [football](/wiki/Association_football) tournament organized by [CONCACAF](/wiki/CONCACAF) to determine which women's national teams from the North, Central American and Caribbean region qualify for the [Olympic football tournament](/wiki/Football_at_the_Summer_Olympics). CONCACAF announced on 12 August 2015 that the [United States](/wiki/United_States) would host the tournament between 10–21 February 2016 in [Houston](/wiki/Houston) and [Frisco](/wiki/Frisco,_Texas), [Texas](/wiki/Texas). A total of eight teams played in the tournament.

The top two teams of the tournament qualified for the [2016 Summer Olympics women's football tournament](/wiki/Football_at_the_2016_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Women%27s_tournament) in Brazil as the CONCACAF representatives.

The [United States](/wiki/United_States_women%27s_national_soccer_team) won the tournament with a 2–0 final win over [Canada](/wiki/Canada_women%27s_national_soccer_team). Both teams qualified for the Olympics, their sixth and third in a row respectively.

## Qualification

Further information: [2016 CONCACAF Women's Olympic Qualifying Championship qualification](/wiki/2016_CONCACAF_Women%27s_Olympic_Qualifying_Championship_qualification)

The eight berths were allocated to the three regional zones as follows:

- Three teams from the [North American Zone (NAFU)](/wiki/North_American_Football_Union), i.e., [Canada](/wiki/Canada_women%27s_national_soccer_team), [Mexico](/wiki/Mexico_women%27s_national_football_team) and the hosts [United States](/wiki/United_States_women%27s_national_soccer_team), who all qualified automatically due to being the only teams in the zone
- Two teams from the [Central American Zone (UNCAF)](/wiki/Central_American_Football_Union)
- Three teams from the [Caribbean Zone (CFU)](/wiki/Caribbean_Football_Union)

Regional qualification tournaments were held to determine the five teams joining Canada, Mexico, and the United States at the final tournament.

## Draw

The draw for the tournament took place on 23 November 2015 at 10:00 [EST](/wiki/Eastern_Time_Zone) ([UTC−5](/wiki/UTC%E2%88%9205:00)) at the InterContinental Doral in [Doral, Florida](/wiki/Doral,_Florida). The draw was conducted by [Cat Whitehill](/wiki/Cat_Whitehill) and [Tiffany Roberts](/wiki/Tiffany_Roberts).

The eight teams were drawn into two groups of four teams. Tournament host, defending CONCACAF Olympic Qualifying Championship champion and 2012 Olympic gold medalist United States were seeded in Group A.

Each team could register a maximum of 20 players (two of whom must be goalkeepers).

## Group stage

The top two teams of each group advanced to the semi-finals. The teams were ranked according to [points](/wiki/Three_points_for_a_win) (3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, 0 points for a loss). If tied on points, tiebreakers would be applied in the following order:

1. Goal difference in all group matches;
2. Greatest number of goals scored in all group matches;
3. Greatest number of points obtained in the group matches between the teams concerned;
4. Goal difference resulting from the group matches between the teams concerned;
5. Greater number of goals scored in all group matches between the teams concerned;
6. Drawing of lots.

All times were local, [CST](/wiki/Central_Time_Zone) ([UTC−6](/wiki/UTC%E2%88%9206:00)).

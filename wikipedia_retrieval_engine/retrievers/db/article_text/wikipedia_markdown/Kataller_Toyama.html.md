**Kataller Toyama** (カターレ富山, *Katāre Toyama*) is a [football](/wiki/Football_(soccer)) club based in [Toyama](/wiki/Toyama_(city)), Capital of [Toyama Prefecture](/wiki/Toyama_Prefecture). The club currently play in [J2 League](/wiki/J2_League) after promotion from J3 in 2024, Japanese second tier of professional football.

## History

The idea of a merged club had been discussed by the Toyama Prefectural Football Association as early as 2005, but discussions had come to nothing at the time.

On 10 September 2007, [YKK](/wiki/YKK_Group) (owner of YKK AP SC) and [Hokuriku Electric Power Company](/wiki/Hokuriku_Electric_Power_Company) (owner of ALO's Hokuriku), agreed with merging their clubs to aim promotion to the [J.League](/wiki/J.League) in response of eager request by the TPFA. According to Tulip TV, local broadcasting company, over twenty companies informally promised to invest in the new club. In the media briefing, the governor of Toyama Prefecture also participated.

TPFA has founded an organisation named "Civic Football Club Team of Toyama Prefecture (富山県民サッカークラブチーム)" with two major economic organisation and representatives of Hokuriku Electric Power Company and YKK. The Japan Football League confirmed that the merged club would compete in the JFL from the 2008 season.

They applied for [J.League Associate Membership](/wiki/J.League_100_Year_Plan_club_status) in January 2008, then their application was accepted at the board meeting of [J.League](/wiki/J.League) on 19 February 2008. On 23 November 2008, they secured qualification for promotion to the [J2 League](/wiki/J2_League) and on 1 December 2008, promotion was made official by J.League.

In [2014](/wiki/2014_J._League_Division_2), after a six-year stint at the J2, Kataller Toyama was relegated to the J3 ahead of the 2015 season after a J2 bottom-place finish.

On 7 December 2024, Kataller Toyama secure promotion to [J2 League](/wiki/J2_League) after draw 2-2 against [Matsumoto Yamaga](/wiki/Matsumoto_Yamaga_FC) and ended 10 years in third tier.

## Name and crest

The word "kataller" is a [portmanteau](/wiki/Portmanteau) of the phrase *katare* (勝たれ) which in [Toyama dialect](/wiki/Hokuriku_dialect) means "to win", and the [French](/wiki/French_language) *aller*, "to go". The phrase is also intended to be a pun of [Italian](/wiki/Italian_language) *cantare*, "to sing", and of native Japanese *katare* (語れ), "to talk" (written with a different [kanji](/wiki/Kanji) character).

The crest is shaped in the form of a [tulip](/wiki/Tulip), the official [Toyama Prefecture](/wiki/Toyama_Prefecture) flower.

The **1911 Carlisle Indians football team** represented the [Carlisle Indian Industrial School](/wiki/Carlisle_Indian_Industrial_School) as an independent during the [1911 college football season](/wiki/1911_college_football_season). Led by tenth-year head coach [Pop Warner](/wiki/Pop_Warner), the Indians compiled a record of 11–1 and outscored opponents 298 to 49. The season included one of the greatest upsets in [college football](/wiki/College_football) history. Against Harvard, [Jim Thorpe](/wiki/Jim_Thorpe) scored all of the Indians' points in a shocking upset over the period powerhouse, 18–15. The only loss for Carlisle came at the hands of [Syracuse](/wiki/Syracuse_Orange_football) the following week, 12–11. [Walter Camp](/wiki/Walter_Camp) selected Thorpe first-team All-American. One source claims Thorpe was "recognized as the greatest player of the year and a man whose kicking is likely to revolutionize the game." [College Football Hall of Fame](/wiki/College_Football_Hall_of_Fame) members on the team include Thorpe, [Gus Welch](/wiki/Gus_Welch), and [William "Lone Star" Dietz](/wiki/William_Henry_Dietz).

# List of films featuring eclipses

There is a body of films featuring [stellar eclipses](/wiki/Solar_eclipse) and [eclipses of natural satellites](/wiki/Lunar_eclipse). Compared to other astronomical events featured in films, such as [full moons](/wiki/Full_moon) and [asteroid](/wiki/Asteroid) strikes, solar eclipses are less commonly seen. When they have featured in films, they often drive the plot and have a portentous presence. [NPR](/wiki/NPR)'s [Glen Weldon](/wiki/Glen_Weldon) said that films use eclipses "to signal to audiences that the normal rules have temporarily lifted, and things are about to get weird". The first film to feature a solar eclipse was the 1907 silent film *[The Eclipse, or the Courtship of the Sun and Moon](/wiki/The_Eclipse,_or_the_Courtship_of_the_Sun_and_Moon)* that featured a solar eclipse as a fantastical consummation between the Sun and the Moon. Eclipses have been seen as bad omens throughout history, so filmmakers leverage that belief "as visual cues or key plot points", according to *[The Oregonian](/wiki/The_Oregonian)*'s Amy Wang. The most accurate depiction of a solar eclipse in film is seen in the 1961 religious epic film *[Barabbas](/wiki/Barabbas_(1961_film))* due to the filming of an actual solar eclipse during its crucifixion scene (see [solar eclipse of February 15, 1961](/wiki/Solar_eclipse_of_February_15,_1961)).

| *[2001: A Space Odyssey](/wiki/2001:_A_Space_Odyssey_(film))* | 1968 | The American science fiction film's opening sequence shows an eclipse of the Sun by both the Earth and the Moon. The three bodies and the camera (which is farther out in space) are aligned; the sequence starts with the Sun fully eclipsed to the camera, and shows the eclipse ending and the Sun becoming visible past the Earth and Moon. |
| *Almanach 1999-2000* | 1999 | The Canadian documentary film explores [divination](/wiki/Divination) in a humorous way and includes eclipses as part of its subject matter. |

| *[Apocalypto](/wiki/Apocalypto)* | 2006 | The Yucatec Mayan-language action-adventure film, set in [pre-Columbian](/wiki/Pre-Columbian) [Yucatan](/wiki/Yucatan) and [Guatemala](/wiki/Guatemala) around the year 1511, Mayan leaders take advantage of a solar eclipse as a sign of an angry god whose blood thirst is satisfied by the leaders. The moon is shown as being near full, rather than as a new moon, on the night after the eclipse. |
| *[Avatar: The Way of Water](/wiki/Avatar:_The_Way_of_Water)* | 2022 | An eclipse occurs during a pivotal moment in the final battle of the film. |
| *[Barabbas](/wiki/Barabbas_(1961_film))* | 1961 | In the religious epic film about [Barabbas](/wiki/Barabbas), a solar eclipse takes place during a crucifixion scene. A real eclipse was filmed for the scene (see [solar eclipse of February 15, 1961](/wiki/Solar_eclipse_of_February_15,_1961)). |
| *[Bloody Birthday](/wiki/Bloody_Birthday)* | 1981 | In the American horror film's opening, three children are born during a solar eclipse, which leads them to grow up with violent personalities. |
| *[A Connecticut Yankee in King Arthur's Court](/wiki/A_Connecticut_Yankee_in_King_Arthur%27s_Court_(1949_film))* | 1949 | The American film, based on [Mark Twain](/wiki/Mark_Twain)'s 1889 novel *[A Connecticut Yankee in King Arthur's Court](/wiki/A_Connecticut_Yankee_in_King_Arthur%27s_Court)*, features an American protagonist from 1912 who wakes up in Arthurian Britain in AD 528 and uses his knowledge of an upcoming solar eclipse to his advantage. |

| *[Dolores Claiborne](/wiki/Dolores_Claiborne_(film))* | 1995 | In the American psychological thriller film, a housekeeper is sent home by her boss to witness [a solar eclipse](/wiki/Solar_eclipse_of_July_20,_1963). In the midst of the eclipse, she confronts her abusive and alcoholic husband. |
| *[Dragonslayer](/wiki/Dragonslayer_(1981_film))* | 1981 | The American fantasy film features the climactic battle with a dragon during a solar eclipse. |
| *[Dune: Part Two](/wiki/Dune:_Part_Two)* | 2024 | In the science fiction film's opening scene, a solar eclipse occurs during the [Fremen](/wiki/Fremen)'s ambush of hostile [Sardaukar](/wiki/Sardaukar) troops. |
| *[Eclipse](/wiki/Eclipse_(1994_film))* | 1994 | The Canadian drama film features a kid who films a documentary about an upcoming solar eclipse. |
| *[The Eclipse, or the Courtship of the Sun and Moon](/wiki/The_Eclipse,_or_the_Courtship_of_the_Sun_and_Moon)* | 1907 | The French silent film shows a solar eclipse resulting from a consummation between the Sun and the Moon. |
| *[Fantasia](/wiki/Fantasia_(1940_film))* | 1940 | In one of the segments of the American animated film, a solar eclipse takes place after dinosaurs go extinct, and the landscape is changed into a sea. |
| *[Farinelli](/wiki/Farinelli_(film))* | 1994 | The biographical drama film, set in 18th-century Europe, features a scene of singing during a solar eclipse. |
| *[From Dusk Till Dawn 2: Texas Blood Money](/wiki/From_Dusk_Till_Dawn_2:_Texas_Blood_Money)* | 1999 | [Vampires](/wiki/Vampire) in a criminal gang raid a bank and attack people they find one night. Dawn appears to defeat them, but a solar eclipse turns the tide turns back in their favor. |
| *[Gerald's Game](/wiki/Gerald%27s_Game_(film))* | 2017 | The American psychological horror film, based on [Stephen King](/wiki/Stephen_King)'s novel *[Gerald's Game](/wiki/Gerald%27s_Game)*, features a solar eclipse in a memory flashback in which the female main character, a preteen at the time, is sexually assaulted. |
| *[Hellboy](/wiki/Hellboy_(2004_film))* | 2004 | In the American superhero film, [Hellboy](/wiki/Hellboy) has to open a gate to hell during a lunar eclipse. |
| *[The House of the Devil](/wiki/The_House_of_the_Devil_(2009_film))* | 2009 | The American horror film, set in the 1980s, features a small-town university student who is hired to stay at a house in the middle of nowhere to take care of an old woman on the night of a full lunar eclipse. |
| *[Judy Berlin](/wiki/Judy_Berlin)* | 1999 | In the American drama film, the town of [Babylon, New York](/wiki/Babylon,_New_York) experiences an hours-long solar eclipse, and it affects the world of the characters who wander the streets in memory and in search of meaning to their suburban lives. |
| *[The Kid Who Would Be King](/wiki/The_Kid_Who_Would_Be_King)* | 2019 | The English-language fantasy film features a solar eclipse that fully empowers the wicked sorceress Morgana. |
| *[King Solomon's Mines](/wiki/King_Solomon%27s_Mines_(1937_film))* | 1937 | The British adventure film features in its climax one of the protagonists predicting a solar eclipse to their advantage. |
| *[A Knight in Camelot](/wiki/A_Knight_in_Camelot)* | 1998 | The American television film features a scientist who is zapped back to the time of [King Arthur](/wiki/King_Arthur), and she predicts a solar eclipse to avoid being burned at the stake. |
| *[Ladyhawke](/wiki/Ladyhawke_(film))* | 1985 | The ending of this American medieval fantasy film, set in 13th-century Europe, features a solar eclipse foretold by a divine riddle. It brings an end to an infernal curse. |

| *[Lara Croft: Tomb Raider](/wiki/Lara_Croft:_Tomb_Raider)* | 2001 | The English-language action-adventure film features a solar eclipse as part of a rare planetary alignment that allows access to a relic that can control space and time. |
| *[Little Shop of Horrors](/wiki/Little_Shop_of_Horrors_(film))* | 1986 | In the American horror comedy film, the monstrous houseplant Audrey II appears during a solar eclipse. |

Professor Lisa Yaszek said of the racist depiction, "Apparently somehow these black Americans don't know what an eclipse is, so they have that very stereotypical reaction of confusion and fear, which causes chaos for the boys... the way it plays out is so stereotypical and really horrible." |
| *[Perfect Strangers](/wiki/Perfect_Strangers_(2017_film))* | 2017 | The Spanish dark comedy film features a dinner party that takes place on the night of a lunar eclipse. |
| *[Pharaoh](/wiki/Pharaoh_(film))* | 1966 | In the Polish production, with the story set in Ancient Egypt, Egyptian priests use the occurrence of a solar eclipse to subdue the crowds. |

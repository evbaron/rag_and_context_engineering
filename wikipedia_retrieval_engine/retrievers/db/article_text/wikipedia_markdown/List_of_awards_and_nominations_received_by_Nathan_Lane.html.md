This article is a **List of awards and nominations received by Nathan Lane**.

**[Nathan Lane](/wiki/Nathan_Lane)** is an American actor and comedian known for his roles on stage and screen. He has received several awards including three [Tony Awards](/wiki/Tony_Awards), seven [Drama Desk Awards](/wiki/Drama_Desk_Awards), which includes the [Hal Prince](/wiki/Hal_Prince) Lifetime Achievement Award, three [Emmy Awards](/wiki/Emmy_Awards), and an [Actor Awards](/wiki/Actor_Awards) as well as nominations for two [Golden Globe Awards](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Awards).

Lane has received six [Tony Award](/wiki/Tony_Award) nominations receiving three wins for his performances as [Pseudolus](/wiki/Pseudolus) in the [Stephen Sondheim](/wiki/Stephen_Sondheim) musical comedy *[A Funny Thing Happened on the Way to the Forum](/wiki/A_Funny_Thing_Happened_on_the_Way_to_the_Forum)* (1996), Max Bialystock in the [Mel Brooks](/wiki/Mel_Brooks) satirical musical *[The Producers](/wiki/The_Producers_(musical))* (2001), and [Roy Cohn](/wiki/Roy_Cohn) in [Tony Kushner](/wiki/Tony_Kushner) epic play *[Angels in America](/wiki/Angels_in_America)* (2018). Lane has received eleven [Drama Desk Award](/wiki/Drama_Desk_Award) nominations receiving seven wins, which includes the Harold S. Prince Award for Lifetime Achievement, as well as two [Obies](/wiki/Obies), the [Lucille Lortel Award](/wiki/Lucille_Lortel_Award) and six [Outer Critics Circle Awards](/wiki/Outer_Critics_Circle_Awards). He also won the [Laurence Olivier Award](/wiki/Laurence_Olivier_Award) for his performance in the [West End](/wiki/West_End_theatre) production in *The Producers*.

For his film work he has received two [Golden Globe Award](/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award) nominations for his performances in [Mike Nichols](/wiki/Mike_Nichols)' *[The Birdcage](/wiki/The_Birdcage)* (1996) and the film adaptation of the [Mel Brooks](/wiki/Mel_Brooks) [Broadway](/wiki/Broadway_(theatre)) [musical](/wiki/The_Producers_(musical)) *[The Producers](/wiki/The_Producers_(2005_film))*. He received the [Actor Award for Outstanding Performance by a Cast in a Motion Picture](/wiki/Actor_Award_for_Outstanding_Performance_by_a_Cast_in_a_Motion_Picture) for *[The Birdcage](/wiki/The_Birdcage)* (1996) and the [National Board of Review](/wiki/National_Board_of_Review) Award, winning for Outstanding Ensemble for *[Nicholas Nickleby](/wiki/Nicholas_Nickleby)*.

Lane has also received various awards for his work on television including a [Primetime Emmy Award](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award), and a record breaking eight nominations in the guest actor category. He won the [Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Guest Actor in a Comedy Series](/wiki/Primetime_Emmy_Award_for_Outstanding_Guest_Actor_in_a_Comedy_Series) for playing Teddy Dimas in the [Hulu](/wiki/Hulu) mystery-comedy series *[Only Murders in the Building](/wiki/Only_Murders_in_the_Building)* in 2022. He was Emmy-nominated for playing a thief in *[Frasier](/wiki/Frasier)* (1995), a former lover in *[Mad About You](/wiki/Mad_About_You)* (1998), and Pepper Saltzman in *[Modern Family](/wiki/Modern_Family)* (2011-2014). He also received two [Daytime Emmy Awards](/wiki/Daytime_Emmy_Awards) for voicing [Timon](/wiki/Timon_and_Pumbaa) in *[Timon & Pumbaa](/wiki/Timon_%26_Pumbaa_(TV_series))* (1996) and [Spot Helperman / Scott Leadready II](/wiki/Teacher%27s_Pet_(TV_series)#Spot_Helperman) in *[Teacher's Pet](/wiki/Teacher%27s_Pet_(TV_series))* (2001).

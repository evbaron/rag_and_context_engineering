**Joel Edward McHale** (born November 20, 1971) is an American actor, comedian, and television presenter. He is best known for hosting *[The Soup](/wiki/The_Soup)* (2004–2015) and his role as [Jeffrey "Jeff" Winger](/wiki/Jeff_Winger) on the [NBC](/wiki/NBC) sitcom *[Community](/wiki/Community_(TV_series))* (2009–2015). He has performed in the films *[Spider-Man 2](/wiki/Spider-Man_2)* (2004), *[Spy Kids: All the Time in the World](/wiki/Spy_Kids:_All_the_Time_in_the_World)* (2011), *[Ted](/wiki/Ted_(film))* (2012), and *[The Happytime Murders](/wiki/The_Happytime_Murders)* (2018).

McHale also starred in the short-lived [CBS](/wiki/CBS) sitcom *[The Great Indoors](/wiki/The_Great_Indoors_(TV_series))* (2016–2017), hosted a reboot of *[Card Sharks](/wiki/Card_Sharks)* (2019–2021), and portrayed the superhero [Starman](/wiki/Sylvester_Pemberton) on the show *[Stargirl](/wiki/Stargirl_(TV_series))* (2020–2022). In 2020, he hosted a special aftershow interviewing key subjects from the [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) documentary series *[Tiger King](/wiki/Tiger_King)* and voiced [Johnny Cage](/wiki/Johnny_Cage) in the direct-to-video martial arts film *[Mortal Kombat Legends: Scorpion's Revenge](/wiki/Mortal_Kombat_Legends:_Scorpion%27s_Revenge)*, a role he reprised in its sequel, *[Mortal Kombat Legends: Battle of the Realms](/wiki/Mortal_Kombat_Legends:_Battle_of_the_Realms)* (2021). He also voices X-PO in *[Lego Dimensions](/wiki/Lego_Dimensions)* (2015–2017) and The Scientist in *[Fortnite](/wiki/Fortnite)* (2021–present). He has a lead role in the Fox comedy series *[Animal Control](/wiki/Animal_Control_(TV_series))* (2023–present), and recurs as abusive chef David Fields on the [FX on Hulu](/wiki/FX_on_Hulu) [comedy-drama](/wiki/Comedy-drama) series *[The Bear](/wiki/The_Bear_(TV_series))* (2022–2024).

## Early life

McHale was born in [Rome](/wiki/Rome), Italy, on November 20, 1971, the son of Jack McHale, who worked as the [dean of students](/wiki/Dean_of_students) at [Loyola University Chicago](/wiki/Loyola_University_Chicago)'s Rome Center, and his wife Laurie. His father is American and is from [Chicago](/wiki/Chicago), while his mother is Canadian and a native of [Vancouver](/wiki/Vancouver). McHale is of Irish and Norwegian descent, and was raised Catholic. He grew up on [Mercer Island, Washington](/wiki/Mercer_Island,_Washington), a suburb of [Seattle](/wiki/Seattle). He also briefly lived in [Haddonfield, New Jersey](/wiki/Haddonfield,_New_Jersey), before returning to Mercer Island. He attended [Mercer Island High School](/wiki/Mercer_Island_High_School). He received a [bachelor's degree](/wiki/Bachelor%27s_degree) in history from the [University of Washington](/wiki/University_of_Washington) in 1995, and was briefly in the [Theta Chi](/wiki/Theta_Chi) fraternity but left it because he "couldn't stand it."

McHale was recruited to be on the University of Washington's [rowing](/wiki/Rowing_(sport)) team but later joined its [football](/wiki/Washington_Huskies_football) team. He was a [walk-on](/wiki/Walk-on_(sports)) as a [tight end](/wiki/Tight_end). He spent two years with the team but did not play in any games. He was on the team that played in the [1993 Rose Bowl](/wiki/1993_Rose_Bowl). He was part of the *[Almost Live!](/wiki/Almost_Live!)* cast, a local [sketch comedy](/wiki/Sketch_comedy) television show produced by [Seattle](/wiki/Seattle)'s [KING-TV](/wiki/KING-TV). From 1993 to 1997, he was a member of the [improv comedy](/wiki/Improv_comedy) group at [Unexpected Productions](/wiki/Unexpected_Productions), participating in *Theatersports!* at the Market Theater located in [Pike Place Market](/wiki/Pike_Place_Market) in downtown Seattle. He received an [MFA](/wiki/Master_of_Fine_Arts) from the Professional Actors Training Program at the University of Washington.

## Career

### Hosting

In 2004, McHale began hosting *[The Soup](/wiki/The_Soup)*, a satirical weekly television show on the [E!](/wiki/E!) television network. Throughout the show, he takes the audience through the oddities and ridiculous happenings of the week in television. He frequently appears as a co-host on *[Loveline](/wiki/Loveline)*. He has been a judge on *[Iron Chef America](/wiki/Iron_Chef_America)*. McHale made a guest appearance on the finale of *[Last Comic Standing](/wiki/Last_Comic_Standing)*'s sixth season, when he recapped the show's events in his typical format of jokes made popular on *The Soup*. He made an appearance as a guest judge on *[RuPaul's Drag Race](/wiki/RuPaul%27s_Drag_Race)* season 11, episode 4, "[Trump: The Rusical](/wiki/Trump:_The_Rusical)".

McHale was the host at the 2014 [White House Correspondents' Association](/wiki/White_House_Correspondents%27_Association) annual dinner. He hosted the 2011 [Independent Spirit Awards](/wiki/Independent_Spirit_Awards) and the [2015 ESPY Awards](/wiki/2015_ESPY_Awards). In 2016, he appeared as an occasional co-host alongside [Kelly Ripa](/wiki/Kelly_Ripa) in the [ABC](/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company) morning show *[Live with Kelly](/wiki/Live_with_Kelly_and_Ryan)*. McHale hosted the [43rd People's Choice Awards](/wiki/43rd_People%27s_Choice_Awards) on January 18, 2017, and the [2017 Webby Awards](/wiki/2017_Webby_Awards) on May 15, 2017. McHale hosted video game developer and publisher [Ubisoft](/wiki/Ubisoft)'s press conference at [E3 2010](/wiki/E3_2010).

In January 2018, it was announced that he would be receiving his own talk show on [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) in February. *[The Joel McHale Show with Joel McHale](/wiki/The_Joel_McHale_Show_with_Joel_McHale)* combined celebrity guests, pre-taped sketches and video clips in a half-hour series that focused on pop culture and news from around the world. On August 17, 2018, it was announced that Netflix had canceled the show after 19 episodes, reportedly due to low viewership.

On April 8, 2019, TVLine reported that McHale would host a revival of the game show *[Card Sharks](/wiki/Card_Sharks)* for [ABC](/wiki/American_Broadcasting_Company). The revival would premiere on June 12 of that year, running for two seasons and 21 episodes until July 7, 2021. The iteration was canceled in April 2022.

Beginning March 27, 2020, McHale co-hosts *The Darkest Timeline* podcast with former *Community* co-star [Ken Jeong](/wiki/Ken_Jeong); the podcast was created in response to the ongoing [COVID-19 pandemic](/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic). Also in 2020, he hosted an after-show special of the [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) documentary series *[Tiger King](/wiki/Tiger_King)*. With Jeong, they became the new hosts of [Fox](/wiki/Fox_Broadcasting_Company)'s [New Year's Eve](/wiki/New_Year%27s_Eve) special *[New Year's Eve Toast & Roast](/wiki/Fox_New_Year%27s_Eve_specials)*. The second edition was canceled due to the quickly rising cases of [Omicron variant](/wiki/SARS-CoV-2_Omicron_variant) in the [COVID-19 pandemic](/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic).

In 2021, McHale began hosting *[Crime Scene Kitchen](/wiki/Crime_Scene_Kitchen)*, also on Fox. In 2023, McHale hosted the [E!](/wiki/E!) reality competition series *[House of Villains](/wiki/House_of_Villains)*.

He currently is the host of the 1% Club.

### Acting

McHale at the 2014 [San Diego Comic-Con](/wiki/San_Diego_Comic-Con)

After earning his master's degree in acting, McHale moved to Los Angeles and landed small roles in *[Diagnosis: Murder](/wiki/Diagnosis:_Murder)*, *[CSI: Miami](/wiki/CSI:_Miami)*, and *[Will & Grace](/wiki/Will_%26_Grace)*. He played a TV reporter in the biopic *[Lords of Dogtown](/wiki/Lords_of_Dogtown)*. He guest-starred as a cast member on the [NBC](/wiki/NBC) improv comedy show *[Thank God You're Here](/wiki/Thank_God_You%27re_Here_(American_TV_series))* during the pilot episode and had a guest role on an episode of *[Pushing Daisies](/wiki/Pushing_Daisies)*. McHale appeared in *[Spider-Man 2](/wiki/Spider-Man_2)* in a small role as Mr. Jacks, a bank manager.

McHale has a long association with [Circle X Theatre](/wiki/Circle_X_Theatre), where he has done several plays. McHale was a weekly guest on *[The Adam Carolla Show](/wiki/The_Adam_Carolla_Show_(radio_program))* and *[Mickey and Amelia](/wiki/Mickey_and_Amelia)*, and occasional guest on *[Opie and Anthony](/wiki/Opie_and_Anthony)*. He occasionally appeared on *[Countdown with Keith Olbermann](/wiki/Countdown_with_Keith_Olbermann)* in a humor sequence closing the show.

He was involved in the American version of the British TV show *[The IT Crowd](/wiki/The_IT_Crowd)*.

McHale starred in the sitcom *[Community](/wiki/Community_(TV_series))*, which premiered in fall 2009, and continued to host *[The Soup](/wiki/The_Soup)*. McHale made an appearance on the first episode of the fifth season of *[Tosh.0](/wiki/Tosh.0)*. He played Rex in *[Ted](/wiki/Ted_(film))* (2012) and had a short guest stint on the FX show *[Sons of Anarchy](/wiki/Sons_of_Anarchy)*. He played a police officer in the supernatural horror film *[Deliver Us from Evil](/wiki/Deliver_Us_from_Evil_(2014_film))*. McHale appeared in 3 episodes between the tenth and eleventh seasons of *[The X-Files](/wiki/The_X-Files)* in 2016 and 2018, respectively.

McHale played the lead role in the [CBS](/wiki/CBS) sitcom *[The Great Indoors](/wiki/The_Great_Indoors_(TV_series))*, which began airing in the fall of 2016. The series was cancelled on May 13, 2017, due to low ratings.

In 2018, McHale was cast in the recurring role of Chris on the second season of the [Netflix](/wiki/Netflix) horror-comedy series *[Santa Clarita Diet](/wiki/Santa_Clarita_Diet)*.

In December 2018, it was announced that McHale was cast as [Sylvester Pemberton](/wiki/Sylvester_Pemberton), the Golden Age Starman in the [DC Universe](/wiki/DC_Universe_(streaming_service)) and [The CW](/wiki/The_CW) series *[Stargirl](/wiki/Stargirl_(TV_series))*. After sporadic appearances in its first two seasons, he was upgraded to series regular for the third.

McHale has been a regular guest panelist on *[The Masked Singer](/wiki/The_Masked_Singer_(American_TV_series))*. He first guested in episode four of [the first season](/wiki/The_Masked_Singer_(American_season_1)), then in two episodes of [season 2](/wiki/The_Masked_Singer_(American_season_2)) and episode 8 of [season 3](/wiki/The_Masked_Singer_(American_season_3)). In [season 4](/wiki/The_Masked_Singer_(American_season_4)), he was a guest panelist in episodes 3 and 4, on his first appearance performing [Robin Thicke](/wiki/Robin_Thicke)'s "[Blurred Lines](/wiki/Blurred_Lines)" as the "Robin" (wearing a cut-out face of Robin Thicke). He went on to be a guest panelist in the third episode of [season 5](/wiki/The_Masked_Singer_(American_season_5)) and episode 7 of [season 6](/wiki/The_Masked_Singer_(American_season_6)). He was absent in [season 7](/wiki/The_Masked_Singer_(American_season_7)). He later served as a guest panelist alongside the late [Leslie Jordan](/wiki/Leslie_Jordan) on episode 7 of [season 8](/wiki/The_Masked_Singer_(American_season_8)).

In April 2025, he is set with [Paula Patton](/wiki/Paula_Patton) for musical indie *Reimagined*.

## Personal life

McHale married Sarah Williams in July 1996. They have two sons. The family resides in [Los Angeles, California](/wiki/Los_Angeles,_California), currently in [Studio City](/wiki/Studio_City,_Los_Angeles) and previously in [Los Feliz](/wiki/Los_Feliz,_Los_Angeles).

In a 2018 interview on [Dax Shepard](/wiki/Dax_Shepard)'s podcast *[Armchair Expert](/wiki/Armchair_Expert)*, McHale revealed he was [dyslexic](/wiki/Dyslexia), having discovered this when his two sons were diagnosed.

### Religious beliefs and political views

He attends a [Presbyterian church](/wiki/Presbyterianism) and has been supportive of the [LGBT community](/wiki/LGBT_community). While performing at the [Durham Performing Arts Center](/wiki/Durham_Performing_Arts_Center) in North Carolina, he wore a handmade [gay-rights](/wiki/LGBT_social_movements) shirt, talked about the [bathroom law](/wiki/Public_Facilities_Privacy_%26_Security_Act) passed weeks prior, and said he was donating all proceeds to the LGBTQ Center of Durham and would not perform in the state again until the law was overturned.

McHale, along with several of his *Community* castmates, made a campaign video in support of [Joe Biden](/wiki/Joe_Biden) in October 2020 titled "Human Beings for Biden."

## Philanthropy

In 2021, McHale has shown his support for the [Children's Tumor Foundation](/wiki/Children%27s_Tumor_Foundation), an organization dedicated to raising awareness for and treating [neurofibromatosis](/wiki/Neurofibromatosis). Some other charities and foundations that McHale has supported include [DoSomething.org](/wiki/DoSomething.org), [Elton John AIDS Foundation](/wiki/Elton_John_AIDS_Foundation), Friar's Charitable Foundation, [Jonsson Cancer Center Foundation](/wiki/Jonsson_Cancer_Center_Foundation), [Make-A-Wish Foundation](/wiki/Make-A-Wish_Foundation), [Motion Picture & Television Fund](/wiki/Motion_Picture_%26_Television_Fund), [Noreen Fraser Foundation](/wiki/Noreen_Fraser_Foundation), [Screen Actors Guild](/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild), The FEED Foundation, [The Trevor Project](/wiki/The_Trevor_Project), and [UNICEF](/wiki/UNICEF).

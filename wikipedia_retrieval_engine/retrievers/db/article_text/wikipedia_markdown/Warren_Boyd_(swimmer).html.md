# Warren Boyd (swimmer)

## Infobox

- **Born:** ( 1926-12-23 ) 23 December 1926 Sydney, Australia
- **Died:** 17 December 2018 (2018-12-17) (aged 91) [ 1 ]
- **Sport:** Swimming
- **Strokes:** freestyle
- **Club:** Manly Swimming Club

**Warren Boyd** (23 December 1926 – 17 December 2018) was an Australian [swimmer](/wiki/Swimming_(sport)). He competed in the [men's 100 metre freestyle](/wiki/Swimming_at_the_1948_Summer_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_100_metre_freestyle) at the [1948 Summer Olympics](/wiki/1948_Summer_Olympics).

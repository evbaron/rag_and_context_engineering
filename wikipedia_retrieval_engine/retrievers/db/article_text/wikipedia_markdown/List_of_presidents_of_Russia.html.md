# List of presidents of Russia

For Russian heads of state prior to introduction of presidency, see [list of heads of state of Russia](/wiki/List_of_heads_of_state_of_Russia).

The office of the [president of Russia](/wiki/President_of_Russia) is the highest authority in the [Russian Federation](/wiki/Russian_Federation). The holder is the federation's head of state and has formal presidency over the [State Council](/wiki/State_Council_(Russia)) as well as being the [commander in chief](/wiki/Commander_in_chief) of the [Russian Armed Forces](/wiki/Russian_Armed_Forces). The office was [introduced in 1918](/wiki/Decree_on_the_system_of_government_of_Russia_(1918)) after the [February Revolution](/wiki/February_Revolution) with the current office emerging after a [referendum](/wiki/1991_Russian_presidential_referendum) of 1991. During the Soviet period of history, [Russia](/wiki/Russian_Soviet_Federative_Socialist_Republic) was *[de jure](/wiki/De_jure)* headed by collective bodies such as the [All-Russian Central Executive Committee](/wiki/All-Russian_Central_Executive_Committee) and the [Presidium of the Supreme Soviet](/wiki/Presidium_of_the_Supreme_Soviet_of_the_Russian_Soviet_Federative_Socialist_Republic), since the [Soviet theory of government](/wiki/Soviet_(council)#Within_the_Soviet_Union) denied the very necessity of the presidential office. The office of the [President of the Soviet Union](/wiki/President_of_the_Soviet_Union) was introduced in 1990 during [Mikhail Gorbachev](/wiki/Mikhail_Gorbachev)'s [unsuccessful reforms](/wiki/Perestroika) of the Soviet Union's [one-party](/wiki/One-party_state) [communist state](/wiki/Communist_state). Gorbachev became first and last president of the Union. His tenure was marked by the [legal and political confrontation](/wiki/War_of_Laws) with Russia and other republics of the USSR which eventually led to their full independence in late 1991.

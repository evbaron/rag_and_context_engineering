# List of micronations

[Micronations](/wiki/Micronation), sometimes also referred to as *model countries* and *new country projects*, are small, [self-proclaimed](/wiki/Self-proclaimed) entities that assert their sovereignty as independent states but which are not acknowledged as such by any of the [recognised sovereign states](/wiki/List_of_sovereign_states), or by any supranational organization. They should not be confused with [microstates](/wiki/Microstate), which are recognised independent states of a small size, nor should they be confused with [unrecognised states](/wiki/List_of_states_with_limited_recognition), which are of more geopolitical significance because they exercise clear control of actual territory to the exclusion of widely recognized countries.

Motivations for the creation of micronations include theoretical experimentation, political protest, artistic expression, personal entertainment and the conduct of criminal activity.

The following is a list of notable micronations.

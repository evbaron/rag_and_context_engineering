**Marion Couthouy Smith** (1853–1931) was a poet from the United States. She published three books of poetry between 1906 and 1918 and individual poems through the *[Harper's Magazine](/wiki/Harper%27s_Magazine)*, *[Century Magazine](/wiki/Century_Magazine)*, *[Atlantic Monthly](/wiki/Atlantic_Monthly)*, and *[The New England Magazine](/wiki/The_New_England_Magazine)*.

## Biography

Marion Couthouy Smith was born in Philadelphia on October 22, 1853 the daughter of Henry Pratt and Maria Couthouy Williams. She graduated 1871 from Miss A. M. Anable's school in Philadelphia.

She died in New York City on November 19, 1931.

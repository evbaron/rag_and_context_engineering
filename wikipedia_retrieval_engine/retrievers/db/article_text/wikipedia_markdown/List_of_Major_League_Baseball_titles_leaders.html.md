# List of Major League Baseball titles leaders

At the end of each [Major League Baseball](/wiki/Major_League_Baseball) season, the league leaders of various statistical categories are announced. Leading either the American League or the National League in a particular category is referred to as a *title*.

The following lists describe which players hold the most titles in a career for a particular category. Listed are players with four or more titles in a category. Active players are ***highlighted***.

# Gymnastics at the 1992 Summer Olympics – Women's artistic team all-around

## Infobox

- **Venue:** Palau Sant Jordi
- **Date:** 28 July 1992
- **Competitors:** 72 from 12 nations

Main article: [Gymnastics at the 1992 Summer Olympics](/wiki/Gymnastics_at_the_1992_Summer_Olympics)

These are the results of the women's artistic team all-around competition, one of six events for female competitors in [artistic gymnastics](/wiki/Artistic_gymnastics) at the [1992 Summer Olympics](/wiki/1992_Summer_Olympics) in [Barcelona](/wiki/Barcelona). The compulsory and optional rounds took place on July 28 at the [Palau Sant Jordi](/wiki/Palau_Sant_Jordi).

## Qualification

The top 12 teams at the [1991 World Artistic Gymnastics Championships](/wiki/1991_World_Artistic_Gymnastics_Championships) earned places in the team all-around competition.

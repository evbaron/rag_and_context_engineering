# Ysaires Restituyo

## Infobox

**Ysaires Restituyo** is a female [beach volleyball](/wiki/Beach_volleyball) player from [Dominican Republic](/wiki/Dominican_Republic), who played in the [NORCECA Beach Volleyball Circuit](/wiki/NORCECA_Beach_Volleyball_Circuit) [2007](/wiki/2007_NORCECA_Beach_Volleyball_Circuit_(Santo_Domingo)) with [Cinthia Piñeiro](/wiki/Cinthia_Pi%C3%B1eiro), [2008](/wiki/2008_NORCECA_Beach_Volleyball_Circuit_(Santo_Domingo)), with Ingrid Carmona and [2009](/wiki/2009_NORCECA_Beach_Volleyball_Circuit_(Boca_Chica)) with [Ana Ligia Fabian](/wiki/Ana_Ligia_Fabian); at [Santo Domingo](/wiki/Santo_Domingo), [Dominican Republic](/wiki/Dominican_Republic).

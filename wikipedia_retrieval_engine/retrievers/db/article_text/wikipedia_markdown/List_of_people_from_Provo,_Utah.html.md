# List of people from Provo, Utah

This is a **list of notable people from [Provo, Utah](/wiki/Provo,_Utah)**. This list includes notable individuals born and raised in Provo, those who currently live in Provo, and those who lived for a significant period in Provo.

- [Robbie Bosco](/wiki/Robbie_Bosco) (b. 1963), former BYU football player
- [Paul D. Boyer](/wiki/Paul_D._Boyer) (1918–2018), recipient of 1997 Nobel Prize in Chemistry
